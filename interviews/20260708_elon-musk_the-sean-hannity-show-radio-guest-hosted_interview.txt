Elon Musk joined guest host Texas Governor Greg Abbott on the Sean Hannity Show for a call-in segment focused almost entirely on SpaceX — its current operations, Starlink, moon and Mars ambitions, space-based data centers, and employee stock ownership — with a closing exchange about Texas. The most newsworthy items were Musk's stated timelines: potentially landing astronauts on the moon within two to three years, sending the first people to Mars in about five years, and launching the company's "first AI satellites" next year, scaling to "large scale" data centers in space within about two years.

## SpaceX's Current Operations
1. **Starlink as core business:** Musk described SpaceX's satellite launches as primarily serving the Starlink internet system, which he said provides "global high-bandwidth internet connectivity" reaching anywhere from Texas to the poles or the middle of the ocean, calling it "the only such system that exists in the world."
2. **NASA astronaut and cargo transport:** Musk said SpaceX's Dragon spacecraft is the only NASA-qualified vehicle for transporting astronauts to and from the International Space Station besides Russia's Soyuz, and that SpaceX also resupplies the station with cargo and scientific experiments, carrying both US and international astronauts.
3. **Launch market share:** Musk stated SpaceX delivers "about 85% of all the payloads to orbit of Earth," with China accounting for roughly 8% of the remainder and the rest of the world (including the rest of the US) making up about 7% — meaning SpaceX transports "almost 10 times as many satellites to orbit as the rest of the world combined."

## Moon and Mars Ambitions
1. **Moon base within 5 years, city within 10:** Musk said he hopes SpaceX has landed its first people on the moon "in less than five years," with a possibility of putting the first astronauts there in two to three years "if things go well," expanding to "thousands of people on the moon" within roughly 10 years.
2. **Vision of a lunar metropolis:** He described wanting a "full-blown self-sustaining city on the moon — like an actual metropolis," open eventually to any member of the public who wants to go, whether for a vacation or a permanent move, framing the effort as making "science fiction not fiction."
3. **Starship's scale:** Musk said the Starship system is "designed to carry ultimately tens of thousands of tons to the moon."
4. **Mars timeline:** Musk explained Mars launch windows occur only once every 26 months and the trip takes about six months (versus a few days to the moon), but said "if things go well, we can probably send the first people to Mars in about five years," with the cadence increasing every two years thereafter, and hopefully "thousands of people" sent to Mars within a "10-or-12-year timeframe."
5. **Overarching mission:** Musk framed SpaceX's purpose as expanding "life and consciousness as we know it beyond Earth... to the rest of the solar system, and eventually to other star systems."

## Space-Based Data Centers
1. **Compute expansion in space:** Musk said the way to expand computing capacity without consuming Earth's land is to build data centers in space, noting Earth receives "about half a billionth of the sun's energy."
2. **Timeline:** He said SpaceX will "probably be launching our first AI satellites next year," scaling to large-scale deployment "in about two years."

## Employee Stock Ownership
1. **Broad-based equity philosophy:** Musk said his philosophy "from day one" has been that everyone at the company should receive stock, citing a welder who started around 2015 at about $28/hour as one of "several thousand people" on the production line whose stock, if they joined early, is "probably worth over a million dollars at this point."

## Texas Ties
1. **Texas flag emoji commitment:** Asked by Abbott for a Texas flag emoji on X, Musk said, "Sure, I will ask the team to make a Texas flag emoji," speaking from the Giga Texas factory, which he noted has a large Texas flag and American flag outside.
