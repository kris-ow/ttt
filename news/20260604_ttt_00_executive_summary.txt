## Robotaxi
- **Austin geofence more than doubles.** Tesla expanded the unsupervised robotaxi operating area in Austin to more than double its prior coverage, per Sawyer Merritt. Broader coverage directly increases potential utilization and revenue per vehicle, and accelerates the pathway to converting additional supervised vehicles to driverless status.
- **Unsupervised robotaxis now operating on Austin highways.** Per the Cern Basher podcast, driverless Cybercabs are running on highway segments within the geofenced zone — not just surface streets — a notable step given Tesla's historical caution around high-speed unsupervised operation.
- **"Robotaxi the way it was intended."** Cern Basher cited a post from Tesla's Ashok declaring that unsupervised service in greater Austin is now operating "in earnest." Both Cern Basher and Phil Beisel described the moment as a pivot point, with Beisel characterizing the safety case as "largely checked" and the remaining challenges as operational — pickup/dropoff precision, dynamic routing, and in-vehicle communication.
- **Grok voice integration unlikely on current AI4 hardware.** Phil Beisel gives only 20% odds that full Grok voice integration (voice recognition through text-to-speech, all cloud-dependent today) is achievable on AI4, arguing the inference load cannot borrow FSD compute cycles without degrading safety performance. His expectation is that AI5 will be required for a complete in-vehicle experience.

## Autonomous Driving
- **FSD V14.3.3 now rolling out.** The update focuses on driver monitoring system improvements: better eye-gaze tracking, enhanced eyewear handling, and higher accuracy in variable lighting. Sawyer Merritt confirmed it was downloading on his Model Y. Incremental, but directly relevant to Tesla's supervised-to-unsupervised regulatory pathway.
- **Lemonade FSD insurance discount — up to 50% in Indiana.** Lemonade launched an "Autonomous Car" program offering Tesla drivers up to a 50% premium reduction when using FSD (Supervised), citing FSD as "twice as safe." This is a meaningful commercial actuarial validation of Tesla's safety narrative and, if it expands to other states, could materially reduce per-mile insurance costs for FSD-equipped vehicles.

## Electric Vehicles
- **Germany +322% YoY in May.** Tesla registered 5,111 vehicles in Germany in May 2026 per the Federal Motor Transport Authority — a standout result in a difficult market. Sales surpassed all prior years except 2023, per Herbert (Brighter).
- **Japan +182% YoY in May; now #3 luxury brand.** Tesla registered 1,996 vehicles in Japan in May 2026, pushing past Audi to the #3 luxury brand position for the month. YTD Japan registrations stand at 8,194 (+157% YoY), on pace for a record year.
- **China wholesale +39% YoY in May — highest of 2026.** Per analyst Roland Percher's data cited by Herbert, May was China's strongest month of the year so far. Australia added a milestone: Model Y was the #1 bestselling vehicle of any kind in May, with Tesla hitting a record 20% EV market share.
- **Used Cybertruck launched June 1; sold out near-immediately.** Tesla began selling used Foundation Series Cybertrucks directly from inventory at $66,200 starting price, with $2,500 nationwide shipping. Units sold out almost immediately, reinforcing the thesis that price — not product — has been the primary barrier to adoption. A separately launched lower-priced new trim also cleared within roughly a week to ten days.
- **Quebec heat pump class action up to $400M.** A Quebec Tesla owner filed a class action request covering all Quebec owners of heat pump-equipped Model S (2021+), Model 3 (2021+), Model X (2021+), Model Y (2020+), and Cybertruck (2023+). The suit alleges hidden defect under provincial consumer law, seeks repair reimbursements and punitive damages, and follows a NHTSA recall and a Transport Canada investigation that received over 170 Canadian complaints.

## Energy
- **SpaceX cumulative Megapack purchases reach ~$699M.** SpaceX disclosed a $269M Megapack purchase in April 2026. Combined with $430M in purchases by SpaceX/xAI in the prior year, the known cumulative total is approximately $699M — a meaningful and growing related-party revenue stream for Tesla Energy.

## Market & Competition
- **SpaceX S-1A opens acquisition door; Tesla the named candidate.** The amended SpaceX S-1A filing includes explicit language authorizing share issuance for future acquisitions. Multiple commentators (Bhakdi, Merz, brighterwithherbert) flag this as deliberate signal, not boilerplate, with Tesla as the primary cited target. SpaceX has authorized 36 billion shares against approximately 12.86 billion currently outstanding — roughly 3x the company — which Bhakdi frames as far exceeding routine incentive needs.
- **Merger debate: Bhakdi vs. Merz on timing and structure.** Both guests broadly accept a merger is being planned but disagree on mechanics. Merz's window: announcement as early as late June 2026 and no later than August 15 (driven by Musk's ~$7B option exercise deadline at a $23 strike price). Both converge on a preferred structure of a one-to-one market cap offer with a guaranteed floor/ratchet to ensure deal certainty. Merz estimates combined-entity trading under a single ticker by approximately Q2 2027.
- **SpaceX IPO officially announced at $1.75T valuation.** SpaceX set IPO terms at $135/share for approximately 555.55 million shares, targeting $74.4B in proceeds (up to ~$85.7B with overallotment). Merz and others flag three rapid index inclusions (MSCI, FTSE, NASDAQ) within the first 15 trading days and a potential S&P 500 inclusion around mid-December 2026 — estimated to drive over $100B in passive purchases — as structural price support engineered around the merger timeline.
