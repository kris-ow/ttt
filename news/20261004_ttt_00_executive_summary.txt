## Robotaxi
- Cybercab fleet scaling faster than last year's robotaxi launch: launched ~Sept 3-4 with 45 vehicles; one month later a tracker shows 55 confirmed with an estimated true count near 80, per jobhakdi — roughly 4x last year's pace of 10-20 Model Y units in the first 30 days. Separately, Sawyer Merritt claims registered Cybercabs have "nearly quadrupled" with no specific count given and no major issues since rides began a month ago.
- Robotaxi app expanded to much of the EU (France, UK, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Ireland, Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Austria, Poland) on October 1, with Singapore and Malaysia reported to follow October 4 — though a commentator cautioned this may just enable US app access for travelers rather than signal local deployment.
- Cybercab slated to appear at "theXtakeover" event in Austin next week.

## Autonomous Driving
- FSD approved in roughly eight, mostly smaller, EU countries (Netherlands, Czech Republic, Slovenia, Lithuania, Estonia, Denmark, Belgium), with Germany, France, UK and Poland still pending, per Tesla Jigsaw on the futureaza podcast. The Dutch regulator RDW reportedly approved FSD around April after ~18 months of testing and ~100 million miles of data.
- France's transport minister reportedly blocked approval over FSD vehicles speeding in 30 km/h zones; the guest cited data claiming human drivers speed in such zones ~82% of the time versus ~50% for Tesla on FSD, and said Tesla supplied data and test vehicles to the minister.
- UK restricts Autopilot/FSD lane-change maneuvers, requiring eye-tracking confirmation and steering-wheel contact, which the guest called a safety downgrade versus the US experience; he estimated UK approval is possible within 12 months, with EU-wide approval (via single-state extension) targeted for January 2027.

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus V3 production estimated by jobhakdi at 5,000+ units deployed internally by October/November, building toward a cited year-end target around 1,000/week — the host's own estimate, citing unspecified "multiple vendors across China."
- AI5 chip memory reportedly cut to 72GB per chip per an Elon Musk tweet referenced by jobhakdi, interpreted as Tesla racing to scale AI5 production for Optimus and robotaxi through 2028-2029.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Flight 14 (this past Monday) completed multiple Earth orbits, deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites, and executed a simulated water landing, per jobhakdi, who called it proof of heat-shield and orbital-delivery viability with full reusability expected in 2027.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- Falcon 9 launch cost estimated at roughly $2,700/kg, a ~95% reduction from the Space Shuttle era's ~$54,000/kg (1981-2011), framed as the shift that opened orbital access beyond government customers.
