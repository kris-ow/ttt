## Brief

- Tesla Q2 revenue $28.24B tops $100B TTM, EPS misses badly
- Free cash flow -$1.1B, first cash-burning quarter since early 2024
- Robotaxi hits 380,000 unsupervised miles across six cities, zero incidents
- Robotaxi launches in Tampa and Orlando, now seven metro areas
- France delays FSD approval, wants EU-wide sign-off first
- Electrek: Tesla driver-assist crashes hit record 207 in May 2026

## Financials
- Revenue of $28.24B (+26% YoY) beat consensus (~$26.4B), but non-GAAP EPS of $0.33 (GAAP diluted $0.32) badly missed the ~$0.53 Street estimate, down 18% YoY. Trailing-twelve-month revenue topped $100 billion for the first time.
- Operating margin collapsed to 1.4% from 4.1% a year ago as opex jumped 47% to $4.35B; automotive gross margin ex-credits fell sequentially from 19.2% to 16.3%, which CFO Vaibhav Taneja attributed mostly to a non-repeating ~$230M warranty/tariff benefit from Q1 rather than underlying deterioration.
- Capex more than doubled sequentially to ~$5.7–5.8B, driving free cash flow to -$1.1B — smaller than the Street's feared -$3–5B miss. Tesla reaffirmed full-year capex guidance above $25B, growing for 2–3 more years, and disclosed new debt facilities of up to $30B.
- Net income of $1.11B (-5% YoY) was aided by a $1B mark-to-market gain on Tesla's SpaceX stake, partly offset by ~$300M FX losses and ~$100M Bitcoin losses; one panel estimate pegs EPS near $0.10 without the SpaceX gain.
- Regulatory credit revenue collapsed to $146M, down 67% YoY and down from $380M in Q1 — a trend not expected to reverse given the expired $7,500 federal EV credit and changed fuel-economy penalty rules.
- Energy gross margin fell sharply from 39.5% to 20.4% on a ~$240M warranty true-up for legacy cell issues; management expects normalization to the mid-to-low 20% range. Services margin hit an all-time high of 14.1%.

## Robotaxi
- Ashok Elluswamy disclosed 380,000+ unsupervised robotaxi miles across six cities in two states with zero notable safety incidents, and said unsupervised miles are growing over 10% week-over-week, a rate management expects to continue through year-end.
- Tesla launched unsupervised Model Y robotaxi service in Tampa and Orlando, bringing total active areas to seven; vehicle counts in the new markets were not disclosed.
- Electrek's own quarter-over-quarter analysis of Tesla's cumulative mileage chart (2.4M+ paid miles) shows paid-mile growth flatlined at ~900,000 in both Q1 and Q2 2026, breaking the prior acceleration trend; the outlet also cites its own estimate of ~21 active unsupervised vehicles across Austin, Dallas, Tampa and Orlando, down from an Austin peak of ~25 in late April.
- Cybercab began production at Giga Texas with employee rides and public-road engineering test drives underway; management said manufacturing targets are being aligned to unsupervised-mile growth, but gave no public deployment date. Musk said Tesla will stay fully vertically integrated rather than use ride-share partnerships, calling reliability — not demand — the binding constraint.
- Cantor Fitzgerald reiterated overweight with a $510 price target citing the robotaxi opportunity; Dillon Loomis (per Bhakdi) flagged that Dallas/Houston saw only 5–10 active vehicles roughly 94 days after launch.

## Autonomous Driving
- Electrek reports Tesla logged 207 Autopilot/FSD-involved crashes in May 2026 per NHTSA SGO data — the highest single month on record, exceeding all of 2021. Tesla has redacted narrative detail on 99.9% of its 3,763 crash reports since 2019, versus near-zero redaction from GM, Ford, Honda and Toyota; H1 2026 crashes (826) are already close to all of 2025 (1,043).
- France's Transport Minister Philippe Tabarot said France won't yet authorize FSD Supervised, citing findings that the system allows speeding and doesn't guarantee adequate driver attention in urban settings; France wants an EU-wide framework rather than relying on the Netherlands' April approval (Denmark approved in June). Sweden separately urged the EU in June to reject FSD unless the speeding behavior is removed.
- FSD paid customers reached nearly 1.5 million globally (+56% YoY), with over 55% of Q2 North America deliveries including an FSD subscription at delivery.
- FSD V14 Lite is rolling out more broadly to HW3 vehicles; two drivers reportedly completed a 4,200-mile Key West-to-Washington trip with zero manual inputs.

## Humanoid Bots
- Musk called Optimus manufacturing "the hardest product to scale" Tesla has attempted, given the lack of an existing supply chain; the Fremont line (former Model S/X plant) is under construction with an expected long, flat ramp before scaling.
- Optimus 4, to be built in Austin, targets roughly 10 million units/year — an order of magnitude above Optimus 3's ~1 million/year goal — with much greater vertical integration, which Musk called "insanely difficult."
- "Digital Optimus," a generalized computer-use AI built with SpaceX's Grok as task manager, was disclosed alongside a multi-source training data strategy (factory observation, dedicated demo teams, internet video, planned reinforcement-learning flywheel).

## Market & Competition
- Regulatory credit phase-down and reduced EV subsidies across US, China and EU are reshaping industry economics even as EV demand keeps growing, per earnings-call panel discussion.
- Ford's rumored ~$30,000 EV pickup with NACS port and LFP battery was spotted at a Tesla Supercharger, targeting launch next year.
- Grok is expanding into Tesla vehicles across India, Thailand, Singapore, Philippines and Malaysia.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Asked directly about a Tesla-SpaceX merger, Musk said the topic "can't be talked about... on an earnings call" and deferred to General Counsel Brandon, who confirmed only the existing investment/framework agreement — a notable change from outright deflection, per panelists.
- SpaceX's first earnings call is set for August 4th.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- SpaceX introduced the Starlink Mini Dish 2, a compact battery-powered receiver for mobile use, with early speeds of roughly 1–20 Mbps. Starlink V5 will be integrated directly into Cybercab for in-vehicle connectivity.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX's next Starship test flight (13th) is scheduled for Thursday, July 23rd.

## Energy
- Tesla deployed 13.5 GWh of energy storage in Q2, up 53% sequentially. Musk said SpaceX has bought large numbers of Megapacks to smooth AI-training power fluctuations and argued batteries paired with solar could roughly double effective US power output.

## Electric Vehicles
- Model Y is now the best-selling car of any kind in the world, per Musk; deliveries hit a Q2 record with the largest order backlog since 2023.
- Tesla raised US Model 3 lease prices up to 15.2%. Tesla opened its first public Megacharger station for Tesla Semi in Bloomington, CA (6 stalls, 1+ MW).
