## Brief
- Tesla Q2 revenue $28.24B tops $100B TTM, but EPS misses badly
- Robotaxi expands to Tampa and Orlando, now 7 metro areas
- France delays FSD approval, wants EU-wide sign-off first
- Electrek: Tesla crash reports hit record 207 in May 2026
- Optimus 4 targeted for 10M units/year, built in Austin
- Musk fields SpaceX merger question, hands it to counsel

## Financials
- Tesla posted Q2 revenue of $28.24 billion (+26% YoY), first time trailing-12-month revenue topped $100 billion, beating consensus of ~$26.4 billion. Non-GAAP EPS came in at $0.33 (GAAP diluted $0.32), down sharply and well below the ~$0.53–0.55 Street expected.
- Operating income fell 57% to $398 million; operating margin sank to 1.4% from 4.1% a year ago as opex jumped 47% to $4.35 billion on AI, Optimus, Robotaxi, and stock-comp spend. Automotive gross margin ex-credits fell from 19.2% to 16.3% quarter-over-quarter, which the CFO attributed mostly to non-repeating warranty/tariff benefits.
- Free cash flow was negative $1.1 billion — Tesla's first cash-burning quarter since early 2024 — but beat panel fears of negative $3–5 billion. Capex roughly doubled to $5.7–5.8 billion; Tesla reaffirmed full-year capex guidance above $25 billion and disclosed new debt facilities allowing up to $30 billion in borrowing.
- Regulatory credit revenue collapsed to $146 million, down 67% YoY and down from $380 million in Q1, a trend not expected to reverse given the expired federal EV tax credit and changed fuel-economy penalty rules.
- A $1 billion unrealized mark-to-market gain on Tesla's SpaceX stake boosted net income; without it, panelists estimated EPS would have been far lower (roughly $0.10 by one estimate).

## Robotaxi
- Tesla launched unsupervised Model Y Robotaxi rides in Tampa and Orlando on July 21, bringing the service to 7 metro areas; vehicle counts in the new markets weren't disclosed.
- Electrek calculates paid Robotaxi miles added per quarter flatlined at ~900,000 in both Q1 and Q2 2026, breaking the prior acceleration trend, despite cumulative miles surpassing 2.4 million. Electrek also reports the active unsupervised fleet has shrunk from an Austin peak of ~25 cars in late April to roughly 17 today, and that California rides still run with a Tesla employee safety monitor onboard.
- On the earnings call, Ashok Elluswamy said the program has driven over 380,000 unsupervised miles across six cities with zero notable safety incidents, with mile growth compounding at 10%+ week-over-week; management gave no vehicle-count figures or public Cybercab deployment date.
- Cybercab began production at Giga Texas with engineering test drives on public roads and employee rides now starting; management said manufacturing is being paced to match unsupervised-mile growth.
- Cantor Fitzgerald reiterated an overweight rating with a $510 price target citing the Robotaxi opportunity; a third-party analyst (Dillon Loomis, per Jobhakdi) flagged that Dallas/Houston still show only 5–10 active vehicles roughly 94 days after launch.

## Autonomous Driving
- Electrek reports Tesla logged a record 207 Autopilot/FSD-involved crashes in May 2026 (per NHTSA data), exceeding all of 2021 combined; H1 2026 crashes hit 826, up 73% YoY, with Tesla redacting 99.9% of narrative detail versus near-zero redaction by GM, Ford, Honda, Toyota.
- France's Transport Minister said France won't yet authorize FSD Supervised, citing speeding behavior and inadequate driver-attention monitoring in urban settings; France wants an EU-wide framework rather than following the Netherlands' April approval (Denmark approved in June). Sweden separately urged EU rejection in June over the same speeding issue.
- FSD subscriptions/purchases reached nearly 1.5 million paid customers globally, up 56% YoY, with 55%+ of Q2 North America deliveries including FSD at delivery.
- FSD V14 Lite is widening to HW3 owners; one pair reportedly drove 4,200 miles coast-to-coast with zero manual inputs.

## Humanoid Bots
- Musk said Optimus 4 (built in Austin) targets roughly 10 million units/year, an order of magnitude above Optimus 3's ~1 million/year goal, calling manufacturing scale-up "the hardest product to scale" Tesla has attempted; the Fremont line is under construction and the ramp will start "flat and long."
- "Digital Optimus," a generalized computer-use AI built with SpaceX's Grok model acting as task manager, was described alongside confirmation that Samsung, TSMC, Panasonic, and Micron are investing in supporting compute and battery capacity.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Musk fielded a direct merger question on the earnings call, citing growing Tesla-SpaceX overlap (Terafab, Starlink-Cybercab integration, Grok) but deferred to General Counsel, who confirmed only the existing investment/framework agreement.
- SpaceX's first earnings call is set for August 4th; commentators expect Colossus-related deals as a near-term profit catalyst, though estimates of scale are self-described speculation, not company guidance.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Starlink V5 terminal rollout began, and V5 will integrate directly into Cybercab for in-vehicle connectivity, per Musk and a Tesla AI team member. SpaceX also launched the Starlink Mini Dish 2, a compact battery-powered receiver for mobile use, with early speeds around 1–20 Mbps.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX's 13th Starship test flight is scheduled for Thursday, July 23rd.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla delivered a record 480,126 vehicles in Q2 (+25% YoY); services revenue rose 50% YoY. Model 3 U.S. lease pricing was raised up to 15.2%.
- A new public Megacharger station for Tesla Semi opened in Bloomington, CA with six stalls delivering over 1 MW peak power.

## Energy
- Tesla deployed 13.5 GWh of energy storage in Q2, up 53% sequentially, but energy gross margin fell from 39.5% to 20.4% due to a ~$240 million warranty true-up on legacy vendor cells; management expects margins to normalize in the mid-to-low 20% range.

## Market & Competition
- The Boring Company's Las Vegas Loop has reportedly moved from 12 workers per boring machine to 2, with its next-gen Prufrock 6 machine designed for fully remote, zero-person operation.
