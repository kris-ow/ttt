## Robotaxi

1. **Two new cities unlocked for unsupervised robotaxi deployment:** Over the 48 hours prior to the video, Tesla deployed one unsupervised robotaxi each in Houston and Dallas, Texas. Jo Bhakdi emphasizes the significance of Houston specifically, describing it as America's third-largest metropolitan area (after New York and Los Angeles), making this expansion meaningful both symbolically and operationally. This brings the total number of unsupervised robotaxis (vehicles with no one inside transporting commercial passengers) to 15.

2. **Total robotaxi fleet at 602, including supervised vehicles:** According to the robotaxi tracker referenced (robotaxitracker.com), 602 total robotaxis are currently deployed, including those with safety drivers. Jo Bhakdi argues that the safety-driver vehicles are effectively equivalent to unsupervised ones because no interventions are occurring — the supervision is regulatory and precautionary rather than technically necessary.

3. **Texas "super area" saturation thesis:** Jo Bhakdi argues that Tesla could plausibly deploy 20,000 to 30,000 unsupervised robotaxis across Austin, Houston, and Dallas without meaningfully impacting car ownership. He further suggests connecting San Antonio into a broader Texas super-corridor — Houston, San Antonio, Austin, Dallas — as a near-term geographic scaling opportunity.

4. **April 2026 inflection point thesis — partially confirmed, partially revised:** Jo Bhakdi acknowledges his earlier prediction of approximately 1,800 unsupervised robotaxis deployed by April 2026 will likely prove incorrect. However, he argues the significance of April is not the specific fleet number but rather that the deployment expansion into new cities now signals entry into the "inflection point zone" he had been predicting for over 10 months.

5. **Stock repricing thesis contingent on robotaxi scale:** Jo Bhakdi reiterates his view that if Tesla rolls out tens of thousands of unsupervised robotaxis, the stock will reprice to $1,000 or more. He distinguishes between a technical stock recovery to $490 (which he says could happen without a robotaxi catalyst) and a genuine repricing through $500–$600 and beyond, which he says requires confirmed robotaxi scaling. Tesla's stock is described as being around $400 at the time of the video.
