## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla Q2 2026 Deliveries: Blowout Beat:** Tesla delivered 480,126 vehicles in Q2 2026, up 25% YoY and well above Wall Street's consensus estimate of ~406,000 — a beat of roughly 74,000 units. This marks Tesla's best Q2 ever and its 4th best delivery quarter in company history. For context, Q2 2025 was 384,122 and Q2 2024 was 443,996. Total Q2 2026 production came in at 451,758 vehicles. Notably, Sawyer Merritt highlights this was achieved despite the U.S. EV tax credit having ended last year, framing it as evidence of durable consumer demand. Tesla is also said to be on track to deliver its 10 millionth cumulative vehicle this quarter (Q3 2026).

2. **Tesla Energy: 13.5 GWh Deployed in Q2 2026:** Tesla deployed 13.5 GWh of energy storage in Q2 2026, the company's second-best quarter ever. No prior quarterly record figure was cited for comparison, but the milestone underscores continued scaling of the Megapack business.

3. **Model Y L U.S. Launch — Specs, Pricing, and Features:** Tesla officially launched the larger Model Y L in the U.S., initially as a Limited Edition Premium AWD "Launch Series" trim priced at $61,990. The Launch Series bundle includes 1 year of FSD (Supervised), 1 year of Supercharging, and 1 year of Premium Connectivity, plus a choice of exclusive options. Key specs include a 0.216 drag coefficient (among the lowest of any SUV Tesla has built), an 83 kWh battery, and an aluminum liftgate that is ~17.6 lbs (8 kg) lighter than steel. The low-drag wheel design contributes a 0.003 Cd improvement. Notably, the Model Y L comes with PowerShare in the U.S., making it only the second Tesla (after the Cybertruck) to include vehicle-to-home/load capability. Tesla also launched the Model Y L in the UAE, with deliveries there beginning in November 2026. Engagement on the U.S. launch post was high (8.4K likes, 776 reposts), indicating strong community interest.

4. **EV Competitive Landscape — Ford and Rivals:** Ford sold just 9,746 EVs in the U.S. in Q2 2026, down 40.7% YoY (Mach-E: 7,032 / -31%; F-150 Lightning: 2,421, noted as discontinued; E-Transit: 293 / -30%). Rivian delivered 12,194 vehicles in Q2 2026 (+14.4% YoY), raised its full-year 2026 delivery guidance to 65,000–70,000 (from 62,000–67,000), and produced 12,613 units. Lucid delivered 3,953 vehicles in Q2 2026 (+19% YoY) and produced 4,774, while announcing a CFO change. These figures collectively highlight Tesla's dominant competitive position — Tesla's single-quarter deliveries dwarf rivals' annual trajectories.
