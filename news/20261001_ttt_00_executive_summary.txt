## Autonomous Driving
- EU FSD Supervised decision now expected December 6, not October, per German transport ministry reporting (via Alex Voigt); cited concerns include speed-limit exceedance and driver-responsibility clarity. FSD Supervised is live in 16 countries, including eight in Europe.
- FSD v15 is said by Ashok Elluswamy to add seven new features, with hosts highlighting an expected ~10-billion-parameter model meant to extend the system's short-term driving "memory." Elements of v15 reportedly already run in Cybercabs operating in Austin, per one commentator.
- A third-party review (Genma) of Xpeng's XNGP 2.0 in Wuhan logged 12 interventions in 5 hours, three critical, with the reviewer comparing its maturity to an earlier Tesla FSD version; a separate commentator estimates Xpeng is 3-4 years behind Tesla's current system.
- Cited collision-rate data: Tesla FSD at one per 1.86M miles vs. Waymo one per 1.5M miles vs. human drivers one per 265,000 miles — with the caveat that Tesla's figure reflects supervised, not driverless, miles.

## Robotaxi
- Tesla has opened AI safety/vehicle operator roles across 55 cities globally (36 US, plus London, Dublin, Singapore, Mumbai, and others), per a tracker cited by futureaza, suggesting broad groundwork for robotaxi expansion.
- Texas DMV shows 126 Cybercabs registered alongside 420 Model Ys in the supervised fleet, per Herbert Ong; he expects Cybercab registrations to grow into the hundreds over coming months and projects an eventual 80-90% Cybercab mix in the ride-hailing fleet.
- Elon Musk reportedly told Chinese state media driverless robotaxis are coming "soon" to Nevada and Florida, with California targeted for mid-2027; Tesla has not yet applied for the required California permit.
- Herbert Ong does not expect material robotaxi revenue before 2027 given repeated delays, though he believes TSLA could re-rate once fleet counts reach 1,000-5,000 vehicles across multiple states.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla China upgraded every current Model 3 trim with a 16-inch touchscreen, a new light-gray interior option, and up to 2,200W of V2L power export; the US Model 3 keeps its 15.4-inch screen and remains excluded from V2L entirely (Electrek).
- China Model 3 orders by October 31 get a 5,000 yuan discount plus 0% financing and insurance/paint subsidies, advertised from 222,500 yuan — though base list price is unchanged at 235,500 yuan, still above BYD's new Formula S at 189,900 yuan.

## Market & Competition
- Xpeng is ranked by one commentator as the strongest non-Waymo autonomy competitor, ahead of BYD and Huawei; Xpeng is also demonstrating its system on Amsterdam roads, seen as a bid for European regulatory approval alongside Tesla's.
