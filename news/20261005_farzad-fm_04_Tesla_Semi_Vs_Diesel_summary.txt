This video breaks down the per-mile fuel cost economics of the Tesla Semi compared to a diesel big rig, using PepsiCo's real-world deployment as its case study. The core thesis is that electricity costs make the Tesla Semi dramatically cheaper to run than diesel trucks on a per-mile basis.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Tesla Semi fuel cost vs. diesel:** The video states that on fuel alone, the Tesla Semi costs about a quarter to a third of what a diesel truck costs per mile, over the truck's entire operating life.
2. **PepsiCo's real-world Tesla Semi deployment:** PepsiCo (Pepsi and Frito-Lay) was the first major company to operate Tesla Semis, taking delivery of its first units in December 2022 and running them out of a Sacramento, California facility; by 2026, PepsiCo is running roughly 50 of these trucks across multiple California sites.
3. **Energy consumption and cost per mile:** PepsiCo's Tesla Semis use about 1.7 kWh per mile, which at typical depot electricity rates translates to roughly 20–30 cents per mile in energy cost (the video rounds this to about 25 cents).
