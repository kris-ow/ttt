Imagine buying a $400 million airplane,
flying it once from New York to London,
and just like chuck it in the ocean.
Just say, "Bye-bye. Bye plane. Goodbye."
That's how the rocket industry worked
for 60 years. But Starship flies, lands,
refuels, and flies again like an
airplane. The newest version, V3,
targets around 100 metric tons to low
Earth orbit. That's roughly 10 times
what the Falcon 9, their current
workhorse, can carry. and they're
targeting a 60 to 80 times reduction in
the cost of getting a kilogram all the
way to space. This means that the access
to space for literally anything,
satellites, orbital factories for
materials that are too sensitive to
gravity, asteroid mining, colonizing the
moon and Mars, orbital compute, all of
it becomes not just accessible, but so
affordable that it would be insane not
to do it.
