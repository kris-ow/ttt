## Brief

## Financials
- Tesla Q2 deliveries came in at 481,000 versus ~460,000 expected — best second quarter in company history, fourth-best quarter overall, per futureaza.
- EPS estimates for the quarter range from $0.63 (flat auto margins) to near $0.90 (strong FSD uptake), up from $0.40 prior quarter; each point of margin improvement adds roughly six cents, per Randy Kirk.
- Tesla's SpaceX stake mark-to-market gain estimated to add ~$0.35/share to EPS, partly offset by a Bitcoin holdings loss of a few hundred million dollars.
- Inventory overhang reportedly fell from ~50,000 vehicles entering the quarter to ~20,000 exiting it, recapturing full production cost as free cash flow.
- RBC Capital raised its Tesla price target to $500 from $475, explicitly building in a 25–30% valuation premium tied to a possible SpaceX-Tesla merger scenario — a rationale both futureaza hosts questioned.
- UBS analyst Joseph Spak raised his Tesla price target to $442 from $364, citing Tesla's physical AI/robotics potential.

## Robotaxi
- Tesla reportedly launched unsupervised robotaxi service in a new Florida city (likely Miami) without a gradual supervised transition — hosts believe this marks a fourth city and second state with unsupervised operation.
- NHTSA head Jonathan Morrison said the agency will "absolutely" consider dropping steering-wheel requirements for driverless vehicles, calling the mandate senseless for vehicles never meant to be human-driven — a direct regulatory tailwind for the steering-wheel-free Cybercab.
- A previously fenced Cybercab storage lot in Austin, estimated to hold 50-60+ units, had its privacy fencing removed — read by hosts as a signal of imminent broader deployment; drone footage separately showed 50+ additional Cybercabs shipped from Giga Texas.
- Tesla brought Cybercab to the National Federation of the Blind's convention in Austin for accessibility testing, incorporating feedback like braille door-latch lettering and phone-based walking directions.
- Tesla's impact report details Cybercab manufacturing: two-seat design (85%+ of ride-hail trips carry two or fewer riders), no steering wheel/pedals, parallel "unboxed" build process, injection-molded panels replacing a paint shop, and a new drive unit cutting China-sourced critical-mineral dependence.

## Market & Competition
- SpaceX (SPCX) drew a wave of bank price targets after joining the Nasdaq 100: ~$200-225 from most banks (Goldman, JPMorgan), an outlier $800 from Raymond James — versus SpaceX trading near $150/share (~$1.6T market cap).
- Grok 4.5 launched, scoring competitively with Anthropic's Opus tier on coding benchmarks while using far fewer tokens per task and costing roughly one-tenth as much, per brighterwithherbert.
- New Jersey proposed mandating radar/lidar for robotaxi operation — futureaza hosts called it technologically misguided and predicted it will push Tesla to avoid the state, echoing past Supercharger removal there.
- Mercedes unveiled an electric CLA AMG with simulated engine sounds and gear shifts; a Tesla Plaid separately beat a McLaren Artura hybrid in a drag race despite the Artura's ~3-second 0-60 time.
- 1X unveiled new tendon-driven humanoid robot hands (25 DOF, IP68, made in USA) — a competitive benchmark for Optimus hand development.
- A German auto supplier faces insolvency after Porsche withdrew orders through September, affecting 140+ employees.

## Energy
- Tesla logged over $9 billion in new Megapack project announcements across six weeks, totaling more than 43 GWh — comparable to roughly a full year of prior storage deployments (49 GWh trailing 12 months).
- Tesla Semi Megacharger buildout continues, letting trucks charge during loading/unloading rather than requiring attended diesel refueling; QX Logistix is adding 20 Tesla Semis to its Southern California fleet.

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus battery and lithium supply chain is being localized in Texas, with batteries sourced from a facility on the Giga Texas campus and lithium from Corpus Christi — framed as a scaling advantage over Chinese humanoid makers.

## Electric Vehicles
- The new Model Y Launch Series is being built exclusively at Giga Texas, not Fremont, as Fremont capacity is reportedly constrained partly by space reallocated to a new Optimus line.
- LG Energy is expanding cylindrical battery production at its Nanjing factory to meet rising Tesla Model Y demand, with existing lines running at full utilization.
- Tesla's 2025 Impact Report shows Model 3 starting price (~$36,000) well below the average new U.S. car price (~$49,000).
