Tesla hasn't announced a catalog of
sleeper pods or mobile offices or
whatever RVs, but CyberCap gives us a
working example of separating the
passenger space from the job of driving.
And I think that opens up an enormous
amount of room for different products.
One operator could focus on affordable
rides across town. They can keep the
vehicle very simple and comfortable and
easy to clean. Another could focus on
people who need a quiet place to work
between appointments with a properly
designed work surface and a cabin built
around calls and concentration or just
peace. A third could develop a service
around longer trips and a vehicle
designed for sleeping safely. Each of
those would need a vehicle and interior
design and approved for its use with a
driving system validated for that
vehicle. But you can reuse a lot of the
engineering and manufacturing knowledge
underneath that platform.
