Tesla has rebranded its FSD system in mainland China to "Tesla Assisted Driving" (特斯拉辅助驾驶), dropping the word "Intelligent" from its previous Chinese name "FSD Intelligent Assisted Driving." The article frames this as a concession to stricter Chinese regulatory standards and a tacit acknowledgment that the "Full Self-Driving" name is misleading.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD remains a Level 2 ADAS system:** Despite being marketed as "Full Self-Driving" and priced at up to $15,000 (or via subscription), Tesla's system is classified as Level 2 under standardized self-driving taxonomy — meaning the human driver remains legally responsible. The article notes Tesla has added "(Supervised)" to the name in the US following pressure from California regulators, but no substantive capability change accompanied this.

2. **China-specific naming history:** In China, the system has gone through multiple name iterations: originally "FSD Intelligent Assisted Driving," then dropping "FSD," and now "Tesla Assisted Driving." Notably, Tesla still uses "Full Self-Driving" on its Hong Kong English-language site and "fully automatic driving function" in Hong Kong Chinese — reflecting Hong Kong's distinct regulatory status as a special administrative region.

3. **FSD availability in China remains unclear:** Tesla stated in an English-language tweet this week that FSD is available in China, but the article notes it is unclear whether this included any new capabilities, given Tesla has previously launched and then rolled back FSD in China.

## Market & Competition
1. **Fierce Chinese ADAS competition:** Multiple Chinese automakers offer competing ADAS systems, some at no cost to consumers. A large-scale Chinese media real-world test covering both highway and city driving found Tesla's system ranked first — but by a narrow margin, not a commanding lead. The article notes Chinese automakers are deploying ADAS capabilities faster than Western competitors overall.

## Editorial Stance
The author argues that "Full Self-Driving" has always been a misleading name for a Level 2 system, and that Tesla's repeated renaming in China reflects the difficulty of building a brand around a claim the product cannot fulfill. The author criticizes CEO Elon Musk for a decade of unfulfilled promises that full autonomy is "at the end of next year."

Notable quotes and data:
- FSD has been priced at up to **$15,000** upfront; China still offers upfront purchase (vs. subscription-only in the US)
