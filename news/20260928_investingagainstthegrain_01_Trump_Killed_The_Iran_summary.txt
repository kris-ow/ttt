## Robotaxi

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Colossus 1 and 2 GPU counts and near-term expansion:** The host quotes an Elon Musk post detailing SpaceX/xAI's Colossus compute: Colossus 1 comprises 150,000 H100s, 50,000 H200s, and 30,000 GB200s; Colossus 2 comprises 110,000 GB200s and 440,000 GB300s. Musk stated an additional 220,000 GB300s would be fully operational the following week, another 220,000 in November, and potentially another 220,000 by late December.
2. **SpaceX/xAI compute compared to NeoCloud peers:** The host presents a chart showing SpaceX/xAI's compute already exceeding competing NeoClouds (named: CoreWeave, Nebius, Crusoe, and Lambda) combined, with further scaling planned by year-end that would put it ahead of all of them combined by an even wider margin.
3. **Speculative $100B ARR run-rate claim:** The host says that if Musk's target of $100 billion in annualized run-rate revenue by the end of December for the NeoCloud business plays out, it would exceed Google Cloud's current revenue and approach Microsoft Azure's, while noting AWS does about $167 billion a year — framed explicitly as contingent on Musk's stated target being hit, not as a confirmed figure.
