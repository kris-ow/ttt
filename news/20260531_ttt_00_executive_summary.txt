## Robotaxi

- **Texas Level 4 self-certification:** Tesla reportedly self-certified the Cybercab as Level 4 autonomous under a new Texas law — an underreported development from Friday, May 30. Per Jo Bhakdi, this could have meaningful implications for commercial launch timing in June.

- **Cybercab "reporting for duty" — Ashok signals imminent Austin entry:** Days before recording, Elon posted a 37-second clip of a Cybercab driving itself out of Giga Texas. Ashok quoted the post and added: "Soon it'll be driving itself into Austin City, reporting for duty." Because Ashok controls FSD versioning and route validation, the host interprets "soon" as weeks, not aspirational quarters. The factory-to-Austin drive would also occur outside any current geo-fence, suggesting either a whitelisted route or meaningful model generalization.

- **Cybercab testing now coast-to-coast:** Bhakdi flagged fresh Cybercab testing footage from Pennsylvania dated May 31, with driverless sightings reported across multiple U.S. cities — a pattern he characterized as "climactic" buildup toward deployment.

- **Production approaching 1,000 units; 10,000 cumulative projected by June:** Bhakdi estimates roughly 1,000 Cybercabs produced to date and projects the cumulative count crosses 10,000 during June when tallied against the production run rate. He sees ~2,000 total units by July, at which point he believes major manufacturing constraints resolve.

- **All three production lines running simultaneously at Giga Texas:** Joe Tegtmeyer confirmed that Model Y, Cybertruck, and Cybercab lines are all running concurrently for the first time in roughly six to eight months, with vehicles interspersed in the output yard — confirming the Cybercab line is live.

- **FSD V15 is the gate; V14 Light for HW3 targets late June:** On the Q1 2026 earnings call, Elon stated broad commercial robotaxi expansion is explicitly gated on FSD Version 15, which he called a major architectural improvement. Separately, Ashok confirmed V14 Light — bringing all V14 features to Hardware 3 vehicles — is targeted for late June 2026.

- **Revenue not material until 2027:** Elon reiterated on Q1 2026 that robotaxi revenue will not be material in 2026; meaningful P&L contribution is a 2027 story.

- **Bhakdi's June thesis:** Bhakdi identifies mid-June (~June 15) as the highest-probability window for a major robotaxi catalyst or commercial launch signal, citing the one-year anniversary of the robotaxi announcement and FIFA World Cup timing as converging factors. He targets $490/share on a Cybercab scaling catalyst, framing it as a third attempt at that level after two prior rejections.

## Autonomous Driving

- **FSD V14.3 rolling out:** Joe Tegtmeyer received FSD 14.3 the morning of May 31; Brian (FutureAzA) expected it imminently. The on-site Giga Texas data center cooling towers — all six fans operational — were flagged as a positive indicator of active FSD training throughput.

- **End-to-end architecture and the reasoning model already in V14.x:** In his October 2025 ICCV article, Ashok Elswami detailed why Tesla uses one end-to-end neural network: gradients flow from controls back to sensor inputs, the architecture handles long-tail edge cases that break rule-based systems, and it sits on the correct side of compute scaling. A small version of the reasoning model is already running in FSD V14.x on customer cars — a detail flagged as underappreciated by investors.

- **Neural world simulator enables closed-loop RL at scale:** Tesla's simulator takes the current world state and proposed action, then generates the next state — including all camera feeds — causally and in real time, with a demonstrated 6-minute photoreal rollout at 24 fps across eight cameras. Use cases include replaying fleet failures against new models and large-scale reinforcement learning targeting what Ashok called "superhuman performance."

- **Fleet produces 500 years of driving data per day:** Ashok described the data pipeline as the "Niagara Falls of data" — 500 years of driving equivalent produced daily — with sophisticated curation pipelines selecting the most informative samples for training.

- **FSD real-world adoption broadening:** A user named Keith has driven 73,240 miles on FSD Supervised since last November (~400 miles/day). Separately, a post about a 75-year-old Model Y owner using FSD after three months — the top-engagement post of the day at 2,700 likes — reinforced the narrative that FSD is reaching non-enthusiast demographics.

- **Edge-case capability: autonomous reversal on narrow mountain road:** Video shared by Sawyer Merritt showed FSD reversing autonomously down a narrow, rainy mountain road to yield to oncoming traffic before proceeding — described as the farthest self-initiated reversal observed to date.

## Humanoid Bots

- **North Campus Optimus factory: 10M units/year target, steel now going up:** The dedicated Optimus manufacturing facility north of Cortex 2 at Giga Texas has progressed from earthwork to footings to first steel erection, with beams and columns staged in adjacent fields. Phase 2 reclaims a former mining site and will nearly double the footprint, making the completed factory approximately the length of Giga Texas itself. Target capacity: at least 10 million units per year — versus the Fremont pilot line targeting ~1 million units/year.

- **Cortex 2 partially operational for Optimus training since April 2026:** The second Giga Texas AI data center went partially online in April 2026 (confirmed in Q1 earnings). Temporary cooling units are supplementing permanent chillers; full operational target is end of summer / fall 2026.

- **Shared AI stack: same simulator trains the car and the robot:** Ashok's October 2025 article stated explicitly that "the same video generation models also work for Optimus robots navigating the Tesla Gigafactory." The car and the humanoid share the same data engine, simulator, and training loop — a structural advantage the brighterwithherbert host identified as the most important line in the article for the Optimus bull case.

- **Gen 3 prototype expected early Q3 2026; real revenue likely 2028+:** Signals discussed point to a Gen 3 Optimus unveil around early Q3 2026. Production at Fremont targets late 2026. Elon has framed Optimus as potentially 80% of Tesla's total value; the host pegged meaningful revenue at 2028 or later.

- **Digital Optimus / "Macrohard" — software agents on the same neural stack:** Ashok is leading a joint Tesla AI and xAI project building digital workers (software agents, not physical robots) using the same neural foundation. The host described this as the most underpriced element of the Tesla thesis, arguing any concrete product announcement would move the AI services story from speculative to real.

## Energy

- **~400 Megapacks planned across Giga Texas:** Approximately 130 Megapacks are installed at Cortex 2, with ~18 more recently delivered. Joe Tegtmeyer estimates roughly 250–260 total at Cortex 2 when complete, plus ~140 across two additional on-site locations, for approximately 400 total Megapacks on the property.

## Electric Vehicles

- **Lower-cost Cybertruck production underway; deliveries mid-to-late June 2026:** Production of the lower-cost Cybertruck variant has started, with VINs being assigned to early customers. Tegtmeyer describes demand as "pretty big." Deliveries expected around mid-to-late June.

- **4680 cell capacity expanding aggressively:** Equipment that previously filled the storage yard near the racetrack has been moved into the factory as part of a major capacity expansion — concrete slabs removed, new steel and cabinets installed. The 4680 is identified as critical for Cybercab, Cybertruck, Optimus, and the Semi (which requires ~10–11× the cells of a Model Y).

- **Model Y demand signals positive; referral credits reduced:** Tesla has cut referral credit amounts — which Tegtmeyer interprets as a positive demand signal — while FSD is increasingly cited by buyers as a purchase motivator.

## Financials

- **Tesla/SpaceX merger "change in control" clause waives all operational milestones:** The Electrek analysis flags language in Musk's 2025 pay agreement stating that "the Operational Milestones shall be disregarded" in a merger or buyout scenario. Milestones that would be bypassed include 20 million vehicle deliveries, 10 million FSD subscriptions, 1 million Optimus bots, and 1 million commercial robotaxis. Only market cap milestones would remain in play. *Note: The piece is editorially opinionated and should not be read as neutral financial analysis.*

- **NASDAQ rule change cuts SpaceX listing wait from one year to ten days:** A NASDAQ rule change would allow SpaceX to be included in indices just 10 days post-IPO, accelerating passive fund buying at the IPO valuation. The Electrek article argues this, combined with merger-permissive IPO prospectus language, creates a mechanism for shareholder dilution across broad index holders.

## Market & Competition

- **SpaceX IPO June 12 at estimated $1.75–$2T valuation:** Bhakdi is skeptical of buying SpaceX at IPO, expecting an initial pop from index inclusion followed by a prolonged gap before meaningful cash flow — which he does not see until late 2027 at the earliest. He does not expect the IPO to trigger a major Tesla selloff, estimating a modest ~5% dip at most.

- **SpaceX Starshield: $2.29B US Space Force contract, delivery by end of 2027:** The US Space Force awarded SpaceX a $2.29 billion contract to build a military satellite communications backbone in LEO, separate from Starlink, targeting completion by end of 2027.

- **American Airlines adds Starlink to 500+ aircraft starting 2027:** This brings total Starlink airline partnerships to 38 airlines and 6,300+ aircraft installed or under contract. Delta remains the holdout, having chosen Amazon LEO — which Larry Goldberg doubts will be ready by 2027 given Amazon LEO's current 300-satellite constellation.

- **Joint Tesla/SpaceX chip fab at Giga Texas; production-ready ~2028:** Soil testing for a joint SpaceX-Tesla advanced chip fabrication facility completed approximately one month prior to recording, sited south of Cortex 2. The facility will develop AI chips through generation six and beyond, feeding a larger volume "Terafab" planned for East Texas. Given current construction state, meaningful production is not expected until 2028.
