## Robotaxi

- **Texas Level 4 self-certification — underreported catalyst:** Tesla reportedly self-certified the Cybercab as Level 4 autonomous under a new Texas law, a development Jo Bhakdi flagged as potentially significant for commercial launch timing in June and described as underreported since its Friday announcement.

- **Ashok's "reporting for duty" post read as a weeks-not-months signal:** Elon posted a 37-second clip of a Cybercab driving itself out of Giga Texas; Ashok Elswami quoted it and added "Soon it'll be driving itself into Austin City, reporting for duty." The brighterwithherbert analysis argues that because Ashok controls the FSD version, safety data, and route validation, his use of "soon" implies real-world weeks. The post also implies V14.3 is already trusted on private Tesla property and that the factory-to-Austin route may be whitelisted or reflect model generalization beyond the current commercial geo-fence.

- **Cybercab spotted driverlessly in Pennsylvania:** Fresh testing footage from Pennsylvania dated May 31 was circulated, with Bhakdi noting Cybercabs are now being observed driving driverlessly across multiple U.S. cities — a pattern he characterizes as "climactic" buildup toward deployment.

- **Bhakdi estimates ~1,000 Cybercabs produced; projects 10,000 cumulative in June:** Jo Bhakdi estimates roughly 1,000 Cybercabs have been produced to date and projects cumulative output will cross 10,000 units in June, with approximately 2,000 units by July — a threshold he associates with resolution of major manufacturing constraints. These are Bhakdi's own estimates, not confirmed by Tesla.

- **June 15 flagged as highest-probability catalyst window:** Bhakdi cites the one-year anniversary of the robotaxi announcement, accumulating Cybercab sightings, and the FIFA World Cup mid-June timing as converging signals, identifying around June 15 as the most likely date for a launch or major announcement.

- **FSD V15 is the stated gate for broad commercial expansion:** Per Q1 2026 earnings, Elon explicitly tied broad robotaxi network expansion to V15, which he described as a major architectural improvement that would significantly raise safety probability. Ashok characterized V15 as a major upgrade over the V14.3 currently running on robotaxis. Robotaxi revenue is not expected to be material in 2026; meaningful P&L contribution is guided to 2027.

- **Robotaxi app adds pickup search by name/address:** Tesla pushed a UX update enabling users to search pickup spots by name or address — a friction-reduction improvement Sawyer Merritt flagged as oriented toward less technically savvy users ahead of broader deployment.

## Autonomous Driving

- **FSD V14.3 rolling out; V14 Light for HW3 targeted late June:** Joe Tegtmeyer confirmed receiving FSD V14.3 the morning of May 31. Per Ashok's Q1 2026 earnings commentary, V14 Light — delivering all V14 features to Hardware 3 vehicles — is targeted for late June 2026.

- **Reasoning model already running in FSD V14.x on customer cars:** Ashok noted in his October 2025 ICCV article — flagged by brighterwithherbert as worth circling — that a small version of Tesla's reasoning model is already running in FSD V14.x on production vehicles.

- **Giga Texas on-site data center running at full cooling capacity:** Joe Tegtmeyer observed all six cooling fans on the southeast data center running simultaneously, which he connects to active FSD training workloads.

- **FSD edge-case capability: autonomous reversal on narrow mountain road:** A video shared by Sawyer Merritt showed FSD reversing autonomously down a narrow, rainy mountain road to yield to oncoming traffic — described as the farthest self-initiated reversal he has observed. Anecdotal but relevant to the safety and capability narrative underpinning regulatory confidence.

- **FSD real-world adoption reaching non-enthusiast demographics:** Sawyer Merritt shared his 75-year-old mother's active FSD usage data after three months with a 2026 Model Y (2,700 likes), alongside a separate user logging 73,240 miles on FSD Supervised since last November. Both data points reinforce FSD engagement across diverse user profiles, though no disengagement or intervention rate data accompanied either post.

## Humanoid Bots

- **North Campus Optimus factory at Giga Texas now in steel erection phase:** The dedicated Optimus manufacturing facility north of Cortex 2 has progressed from earthwork and footings to first steel erection, with beams and columns being staged in an adjacent field. The target is at least 10 million units/year across two phases, with Phase 2 reclaiming an adjacent mining site to nearly double the footprint — bringing the building to roughly the same length as Giga Texas itself, though narrower.

- **Cortex 2 partially operational since April for Optimus training; full capacity targeted end of summer/fall 2026:** Tesla confirmed at Q1 earnings that Cortex 2 went partially operational in April 2026. Roughly half capacity is now live, augmented by temporary cooling units; full build-out is targeted for end of summer or fall 2026.

- **Shared AI stack between car and robot is Ashok's core architectural claim:** Ashok's October 2025 ICCV article states explicitly: "The same video generation models also work for Optimus robots navigating the Tesla Gigafactory." The neural world simulator, data engine, and reinforcement learning loop are described as common infrastructure across both programs, with Ashok now leading Optimus directly.

- **Gen 3 Optimus prototype unveil signals point to early Q3 2026:** Based on signals discussed by brighterwithherbert, the Gen 3 Optimus prototype reveal looks like early Q3 2026 at the earliest. Production at Fremont is targeted for late 2026, ramping toward 1 million units/year. Real revenue from Optimus is framed as a 2028-or-later event.

- **Digital Optimus / "Macrohard" — AI software agents as the most underpriced thesis piece:** Ashok is lead engineer on a joint Tesla AI/xAI project building software-based digital agents — applying the same neural foundation and fleet learning loop to knowledge work. The brighterwithherbert framing: any concrete product announcement here moves the AI services thesis from possible to real.

## Energy

- **~400 Megapacks planned across Giga Texas; ~130 installed at Cortex 2 today:** Joe Tegtmeyer estimates approximately 130 Megapacks currently installed at Cortex 2, with an additional ~18 recently delivered. Across all sites on the Giga Texas property, the total planned installation is approximately 400 Megapacks.

## Electric Vehicles

- **All three Giga Texas production lines running simultaneously for the first time in ~6–8 months:** Joe Tegtmeyer observed Model Y, Cybertruck, and Cybercab units interspersed in the output staging yard — confirmation that all lines are live concurrently, a milestone not consistently seen over the prior six to eight months.

- **Lower-cost Cybertruck variant in production; deliveries expected mid-to-late June 2026:** Production has begun and the earliest customers are receiving VINs, with deliveries expected around mid-to-late June 2026. Joe Tegtmeyer describes demand as "pretty big."

- **4680 cell production capacity expanding aggressively:** Previously full storage yards near the racetrack have been largely cleared as equipment moves into the factory for a capacity expansion. The 4680 cell is identified as critical for Optimus, Cybercab, Cybertruck, and the Tesla Semi — the Semi alone requiring roughly 10–11× the cells of a Model Y.

## Financials

- **SpaceX/Tesla merger "change in control" clause waives all operational milestones for Musk's pay:** Per Electrek's analysis, the 2025 performance stock agreement contains language stating "the Operational Milestones shall be disregarded" upon a merger or buyout — meaning Tesla would not need to hit targets including 20 million vehicle deliveries, 10 million FSD subscriptions, 1 million Optimus bots, or 1 million commercial robotaxis for Musk to collect some or all of 12 tranches of stock. The article is explicitly opinion-editorial and characterizes the structure as deliberate wealth extraction; investors should read it as a flagged governance risk, not a neutral analysis.

- **Tesla P/E at ~400; NASDAQ rule change compresses SpaceX listing window to 10 days:** Electrek notes Tesla is trading at approximately 400× earnings. A NASDAQ rule change reportedly reduces the post-IPO index inclusion waiting period from one year to ten days for SpaceX, which Electrek argues forces passive index funds to buy at peak valuation — with potential dilution implications for Tesla shareholders if an all-stock merger follows.

## Market & Competition

- **Joint SpaceX/Tesla chip fab at Giga Texas; production-ready estimated 2028:** South of Cortex 2, a joint advanced technology chip fabrication facility is in early development — soil testing completed approximately one month prior to May 31. The facility targets AI chip generations 5 and 6-plus for SpaceX data centers, serving as a development precursor to a larger volume "Terafab" planned for East Texas. Given current construction state, Joe Tegtmeyer estimates the Giga Texas fab will not be production-ready until 2028.

- **SpaceX IPO June 12 at estimated $1.75–$2T valuation; Bhakdi cautious medium-term:** Bhakdi is skeptical of buying SpaceX at IPO, warning of a prolonged gap between IPO and meaningful cash flow improvement — which he does not expect until late 2027 even in the most optimistic scenario. He estimates a minor Tesla dip of ~5% at most from retail rebalancing, while noting a strong SpaceX IPO could exert a merger-speculation "magnet effect" on Tesla's market cap.
