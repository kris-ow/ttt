## Robotaxi

- **Texas Level 4 self-certification — underreported catalyst:** Tesla reportedly self-certified the Cybercab as Level 4 autonomous under a new Texas law, a development Jo Bhakdi flagged as having significant implications for commercial launch timing. He identified mid-June — around June 15th — as the highest-probability window yet for a launch or major announcement, citing the event's one-year anniversary and FIFA World Cup timing as converging signals.

- **Ashok's "reporting for duty" post read as a real-weeks signal:** Days before the video's publish date, Elon posted a Cybercab driving itself out of Giga Texas; Ashok quoted it and added "Soon it'll be driving itself into Austin City, reporting for duty." The brighterwithherbert analysis argues that because Ashok owns the FSD version, safety data, and route validation, his use of "soon" carries operational weight — not aspirational framing. The factory-to-Austin drive also implies either a whitelisted route or model generalization beyond the current commercial geo-fence.

- **Cybercab sightings now reaching Pennsylvania:** Fresh Cybercab testing footage from Pennsylvania dated May 31 was highlighted by Bhakdi, with driverless Cybercab sightings reported across multiple U.S. cities — a pattern he described as "climactic" buildup.

- **Bhakdi's production estimate: ~1,000 units produced, 10,000 cumulative by June:** Bhakdi estimates Tesla is approaching 1,000 Cybercabs produced to date, projects crossing a 10,000-unit cumulative run rate in June, and expects ~2,000 units by July — the point at which he believes major manufacturing constraints resolve.

- **FSD V15 remains the gate for broad commercial expansion:** On Q1 2026 earnings, Elon was explicit that broad robotaxi network expansion is gated on FSD Version 15, described as a major architectural improvement significantly raising safety probability. V15 is a major upgrade over the V14.3 build currently running on robotaxis. Per brighterwithherbert, Ashok's shipping schedule — not manufacturing capacity — is the pacing constraint.

- **Revenue timeline unchanged:** Elon confirmed on Q1 2026 that robotaxi revenue will not be material in 2026; meaningful P&L contribution is pushed to 2027.

- **Robotaxi app adds pickup search by name/address:** Tesla pushed a UX update enabling users to search for pickup spots by name or address — a friction-reduction improvement aimed at non-early-adopter users and relevant to fleet utilization as the service scales.

## Autonomous Driving

- **FSD V14.3 rolling out; V14 Light for HW3 targeted late June:** Joe Tegtmeyer received FSD V14.3 the morning of the Giga Texas drone session. Separately, Ashok confirmed on Q1 2026 earnings that V14 Light — carrying all V14 features currently on AI4 — is targeted for Hardware 3 vehicles by late June 2026.

- **Small reasoning model already running in FSD V14.x on customer cars:** Ashok noted almost in passing in his October 2025 ICCV article that a small version of Tesla's reasoning model is already live in FSD V14.x on customer vehicles — a detail brighterwithherbert flagged as worth circling for investors tracking the capability staircase.

- **Tesla's generative Gaussian splatting runs in ~220ms:** Versus tens of minutes for standard 3D Gaussian splatting, Tesla's version requires no external initialization, models moving objects, can be trained jointly with the end-to-end model, and runs on production vehicle cameras — a key enabler of the neural world simulator used for closed-loop RL training.

- **Fleet data engine: equivalent of 500 years of driving produced every day:** Ashok's October 2025 article quantified the scale: seven cameras, 36 fps, 5-megapixel inputs, 30 seconds of history, plus navigation, 100 Hz vehicle data, and 48 kHz audio — approximately 2 billion input tokens compressed to 2 output tokens (steering angle and acceleration). Tesla curates this stream via sophisticated data engine pipelines.

- **FSD edge-case demo — autonomous reversal on narrow mountain road:** A video circulated showing FSD reversing down a narrow, rainy mountain road to yield to oncoming traffic before proceeding — described by Sawyer Merritt as the farthest he's seen FSD reverse autonomously. Anecdotal but relevant to the safety case underpinning regulatory confidence.

- **Giga Texas on-site data center running all six cooling fans:** Joe Tegtmeyer observed all six cooling tower fans on the southeast corner of the main building operating simultaneously, which he connects to active FSD training workloads on the dedicated on-site data center.

## Humanoid Bots

- **North Campus Optimus factory: first steel now erected, 10M units/year target:** The dedicated Optimus manufacturing facility north of Cortex 2 at Giga Texas has progressed from earthwork and footings to first steel erection, with beams and columns staged in an adjacent field. Phase 1 is on flat land currently under construction; Phase 2 reclaims an adjacent mining site to roughly double the footprint. When complete, the building will approach the length of Giga Texas itself. The target is at least 10 million Optimus units per year — versus the Fremont pilot line targeting ~1 million units per year.

- **Cortex 2 data center ~50% live for Optimus training; full capacity targeted fall 2026:** Cortex 2 went partially operational in April 2026 per Q1 results. Temporary cooling units are augmenting permanent chillers while Phase 2 construction continues, with full capacity targeted around end of summer or fall 2026. Tesla has stated the facility is dedicated to Optimus robot training.

- **Shared AI stack: same simulator trains both car and robot:** Ashok's October 2025 article stated explicitly that the same video generation models used to train FSD also work for Optimus robots navigating the Gigafactory. The neural world simulator — which generates photoreal camera feeds for all vehicle angles in response to control inputs — is being used for large-scale closed-loop reinforcement learning for both products.

- **Ashok now leads Optimus program:** Ashok Elswami took over the Optimus program from its prior lead and is now the lead engineer. The brighterwithherbert analysis frames FSD, robotaxi, Optimus, and Digital Optimus (Macrohard) as one unified AI stack with four different bodies — all under Ashok's architecture.

- **Gen 3 prototype unveil signals point to early Q3 2026; revenue probably 2028+:** Based on signals discussed, the Gen 3 Optimus prototype unveil looks like early Q3 2026 at the earliest. Fremont production is targeted for late 2026. Elon has framed Optimus as potentially becoming 80% of Tesla's total value; the brighterwithherbert host places real revenue at 2028 or later.

## Energy

- **~400 Megapacks planned across Giga Texas; ~130 installed at Cortex 2 today:** Joe Tegtmeyer's aerial survey counts approximately 130 Megapacks installed at Cortex 2, with ~18 recently delivered and partially staged. Two additional Megapack sites on the property total ~140 units, bringing the planned grand total to approximately 400 across the site when fully installed.

## Electric Vehicles

- **All three Giga Texas production lines running simultaneously:** Model Y, Cybertruck, and Cybercab production lines are all live at the same time, with vehicles interspersed in output staging areas — a configuration not consistently seen over the prior six to eight months, per Joe Tegtmeyer.

- **Lower-cost Cybertruck production underway; deliveries expected mid-to-late June 2026:** The lower-cost Cybertruck variant is in production with suppliers and lines brought up to meet what Tegtmeyer describes as "pretty big demand." Earliest customers are receiving VINs now.

- **4680 cell capacity expanding aggressively:** Equipment previously staged in the racetrack storage yard has been moved into the factory as part of a significant 4680 production expansion — concrete slabs removed, new steel and cabinets installed. The 4680 is identified as critical for Optimus, Cybercab, Cybertruck, and the Tesla Semi (which requires ~10–11× the cells of a Model Y).

## Financials

- **Tesla/SpaceX merger "change in control" clause waives all operational milestones:** The 2025 performance-based pay agreement contains language stating that "the Operational Milestones shall be disregarded" in a merger or buyout scenario, leaving only market cap milestones. Targets that would be bypassed include 20 million vehicle deliveries, 10 million FSD subscriptions, 1 million Optimus bots, and 1 million commercial robotaxis. The Electrek piece is strongly opinionated and explicitly not a neutral financial analysis — flag accordingly.

- **NASDAQ rule change cuts SpaceX post-IPO listing window from 1 year to 10 days:** The article argues this forces passive index funds to buy SpaceX at IPO valuations; SpaceX's prospectus reportedly permits a merger within the 180-day post-IPO lockup period.

## Market & Competition

- **SpaceX IPO June 12; Bhakdi skeptical above $1.75–2T valuation:** Bhakdi expects SpaceX shares to rise initially on index inclusion but warns of a prolonged gap before meaningful cash flow improvement — which he does not expect until late 2027 even in optimistic scenarios. He does not anticipate more than a ~5% Tesla dip tied to retail rebalancing out of Tesla into SpaceX.

- **SpaceX merger speculation: magnet effect or drag risk for Tesla:** Bhakdi argues a strong SpaceX IPO could pull Tesla's market cap upward via merger speculation; a weak post-IPO performance could pressure Tesla symmetrically.

- **Joint SpaceX/Tesla chip fab breaking ground at Giga Texas; production-ready ~2028:** South of Cortex 2, soil testing completed approximately one month prior to the drone session for a joint advanced technology chip fabrication facility. It will develop and test AI chips (Gen 5 nearly complete; Gen 6 and beyond to follow) as a precursor to a larger "Terafab" planned for East Texas. Given current construction state, Tegtmeyer estimates the Giga Texas fab will not reach meaningful production until 2028.
