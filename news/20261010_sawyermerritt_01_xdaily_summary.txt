## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Cybercab fleet registrations nearly double overnight in Texas:** Per Texas DMV data cited by Sawyer Merritt, Tesla registered 150 new Cybercabs in a single day, bringing the total registered to date to 319 (up from 169). Cybercabs now reportedly make up 43% of all registered vehicles in Tesla's Texas Robotaxi fleet, up from 28% the prior day — a fast-moving mix shift toward the purpose-built robotaxi vehicle. These are DMV registration figures, not RobotaxiTracker.com's active/unsupervised fleet metrics (which remain stale at 839 active / 48 unsupervised as of early August), so they should be read as a leading indicator of fleet growth rather than a replacement for those canonical figures.
2. **On-the-ground reports of fleet growth and low wait times:** Merritt, posting from downtown Austin and later noting Friday rush-hour conditions during an X Takeover event (with many Tesla fans in town requesting rides), says Cybercab wait times are "very low" and that there are "a lot" of them on the road — a qualitative, first-hand observation suggesting Tesla has added vehicles to its active Austin fleet recently. Separately, he noted Giga Texas's orientation/tour room now features Cybercab-style seating, a minor but visible sign of Cybercab integration into Tesla's public-facing operations.
3. **SpaceX/Starlink spectrum and carrier ambitions:** T-Mobile's CTO commented (per a post relayed by Merritt) that SpaceX acquiring nationwide low-band spectrum is being called by some the "last critical piece" needed for Starlink to compete with national wireless carriers, though the CTO added there's "a big difference" between that and actually doing so (quote cut off) — a notable but still qualified claim about Starlink's mobile carrier trajectory. Separately, Delta Airlines CEO Ed Bastian said in an interview that Delta is not adopting Starlink for in-flight Wi-Fi, citing Delta's own pioneering role and satisfaction with its current approach — a competitive data point on Starlink's aviation penetration.
4. **Elon Musk floats extreme SpaceX valuation scenario:** Musk said it's "possible" SpaceX could one day be valued at over a quadrillion dollars, per Merritt — an aspirational, unquantified long-term remark rather than a near-term valuation target or guidance.
5. **Tesla AI rebranded to Tesla SI (Super Intelligence):** Tesla's AI division/account has been officially renamed to "Tesla SI," with Merritt framing "SI" (super intelligence) as a more precise term than the generic "AI" for what Tesla and SpaceX are ultimately building toward — a branding/positioning shift rather than a technical or financial disclosure.
6. **VW ID. Tiguan EV unveiled (competitive context):** Volkswagen unveiled an all-electric ID. Tiguan with an estimated starting price around $48,000, estimated range up to 310 miles, 58 kWh or 77 kWh battery options, and towing capacity of 4,400 lbs — relevant as incremental EV competition context for Tesla's model lineup, though figures are explicitly estimates per the post.
