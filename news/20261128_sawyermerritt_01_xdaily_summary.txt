## Key Posts / Themes

EMAG is a German manufacturing systems supplier headquartered near Stuttgart, and it also makes parts for humanoid robots. This is a vertical-integration step that could support Optimus manufacturing. It also adds machine-tool capability in Germany. With 2.3K likes and 173 reposts, it was the day's most-shared Tesla post.

2. **SpaceX's Starbase data center and Tesla Megapacks:** This was the day's most viral post, with 8.2K likes and 694 reposts. Sawyer says the site of a new SpaceX data center at Starbase "was literally just dirt less than 90 days ago" and is being built "at breakneck speeds." He estimates from imagery that "over $250M of Tesla Megapacks" appear to be installed there. That figure is his visual estimate, not a disclosed number. The post highlights how fast SpaceX is adding AI compute. It also shows SpaceX's buildout becoming a meaningful source of Tesla Energy storage demand.

He says it saved him $445 on kitchen stools and $200 on a TV, and that "useful agents" will change how people shop. These are early signs of consumer AI revenue for SpaceX's AI segment, but no pricing details beyond "thousands per month" were given.

4. **Starlink Black Friday promotions:** Starlink is running holiday deals. Combining the new Starlink Mini kit price with the $100 referral reward makes the kit effectively free.
