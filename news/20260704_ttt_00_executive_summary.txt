## Brief

- Tesla launches Unsupervised Robotaxi service in Miami
- Musk stays silent on record Q2 deliveries, posts Optimus and Grok instead
- Tesla stock fell ~8% on delivery day despite record beat
- Michael Burry discloses Tesla short at $416
- Texas Model 3 driver charged with manslaughter in fatal FSD-related crash
- Fremont Model S/X line converted to Optimus production

## Robotaxi
- Unsupervised Robotaxi service launched in Miami on 2026-07-03, with rides captured on video including operation in rain on day one. Unsupervised service now runs in Miami, Dallas and Houston, Austin runs a mix of unsupervised and safety-monitor rides, and Bay Area remains safety-monitor only.
- Miami's fleet size is not yet reflected in RobotaxiTracker.com's canonical breakdown, which remains last-known at Active fleet 752 (Bay Area 620, Austin 105, Dallas 16, Houston 11).
- Hardware 3 vehicles are broadly seen as excluded from Cybercab/robotaxi use; only Hardware 4 is viewed as a plausible candidate, and likely only in favorable weather/geography, per futureaza discussion.
- Steering-wheel-less production Cybercabs began public road testing in Austin this week, per Sawyer Merritt's roundup.

## Autonomous Driving
- V14 Lite began rolling out to HW3 owners this week; early results described as promising despite Hardware 3's memory-bandwidth limitations, per futureaza guest.
- A Hardware 4 owner reported driving 598 of 600 highway miles on FSD with a single disengagement (a missed supercharger stop, not a driving failure), part of a personal 421-mile uninterrupted streak.
- Highway-only unsupervised driving is viewed as a more tractable near-term outcome for Hardware 3 than full city-street autonomy, given the "straight-line" vs. "omnidirectional" complexity gap.
- A Texas Model 3 driver was charged with manslaughter after a fatal house crash; officials cited May search history about making FSD "not aggressive enough" and said the accelerator was pressed to override the vehicle mid-turn.

## Humanoid Bots
- Musk posted July 1 photos walking the Optimus line in Fremont, now built on the former Model S/X line (last S/X built early May); Tesla's Lars Moravy said the line is modular and reconfigurable as hardware evolves.
- Optimus V3 production design reveal and production start are both targeted for late July–August, per Musk's timeline; Fremont's eventual target is 1 million robots/year (explicitly a target, not current output).
- Tesla's robotics and autonomy spend this year is reported above $25 billion, covering Fremont, a planned second Optimus factory at Giga Texas, Cybertruck ramp, and a chip fab. Musk has said next-gen AI chips will go to Optimus and training clusters before vehicles.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla delivered 480,126 vehicles in Q2, its best-ever Q2 and fourth-highest quarter overall, beating estimates by roughly 60,000–74,000 units (estimates vary by source).
- Tesla China posted its strongest month of 2026, up 24% year-over-year, extending an eight-month growth streak.
- Giga Berlin plans to raise weekly output toward 7,500 vehicles starting October, adding roughly 1,000 more jobs.
- Model Y L production has begun at Giga Texas; second-row heated seats reportedly reach 104°F within 2 minutes at 5°F ambient.
- Cumulative production (~9.861M) and deliveries (~9.721M) are approaching the 10-millionth vehicle milestone.

## Market & Competition
- Michael Burry disclosed shorting Tesla at $416 on June 30, saying he was "happy it jumped back to this level"; he is also short Nvidia, Caterpillar, Applied Materials, and a semiconductor ETF, framing AI capex as a bubble. Position size undisclosed.
- Tesla stock fell about 8% on delivery day despite the record quarter, after running up ~12% into the report — read by one host as the market having moved past deliveries as the key valuation driver.
- Grok 4.5, built on a 1.5-trillion-parameter model, is in private beta at SpaceX and Tesla, with Musk saying early performance is "close to, perhaps exceeding" Anthropic's Opus (unverified, internal).
- Lucid delivered ~4,000 vehicles (+20%, CFO replaced), Rivian ~12,000 (+14%, guidance raised), Ford ~9,000 EVs with Mach-E down 31% YoY and F-150 Lightning discontinued.
