## Brief

- Robotaxi launches Unsupervised in Miami, third unsupervised city
- Tesla Q2 deliveries hit 480,126, best Q2 ever
- Musk stayed silent on delivery record, posted Optimus and Grok instead
- Stock fell ~8% on delivery day despite the beat
- Texas Model 3 driver charged with manslaughter in fatal crash
- Michael Burry discloses Tesla short at $416

## Robotaxi
- Miami launched Unsupervised Model Y rides on 2026-07-03, including operation in rain on day one. Unsupervised service now spans Miami, Dallas, and Houston; Austin runs a mix of unsupervised and safety-monitor rides; Bay Area remains safety-monitor only. Miami's fleet size is not yet reflected in RobotaxiTracker.com's canonical breakdown.
- Production Cybercabs (no steering wheel or pedals) began public road testing in Austin this week, part of a cluster of catalysts alongside V14 Lite's HW3 rollout and the delivery record.
- Hardware 3 vehicles are broadly seen as excluded from Cybercab/robotaxi use; commentators argue only Hardware 4 is a plausible candidate, and likely only in favorable weather/geography.

## Autonomous Driving
- A Hardware 4 owner reported driving 598 of 600 highway/city miles on FSD with zero driving-related disengagements, part of a personal 421-mile uninterrupted streak.
- Commentators argue Hardware 4 can already handle full unsupervised driving in most conditions, while Hardware 3 is fundamentally limited by camera/memory bandwidth and likely capped at supervised FSD or, at best, highway-only unsupervised operation.
- A newly released lightweight "V14 Lite" build for Hardware 3 is reportedly showing promising early results despite the hardware ceiling.
- Texas officials charged a Model 3 driver with manslaughter after a fatal house crash, citing May search history about making FSD "more aggressive" and stating the accelerator was pressed to override the car mid-turn.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla delivered 480,126 vehicles in Q2, up 25% and beating estimates by roughly 60,000–74,000 units — the best Q2 in company history and fourth-highest quarter ever.
- China posted its strongest month of 2026, up 24% year-over-year, an eighth straight month of growth; Europe demand rebounded across France, Sweden, Denmark, and Spain on high fuel prices.
- Giga Berlin plans to raise weekly output toward 7,500 vehicles starting October, adding roughly 1,000 jobs.
- Model Y L (long-wheelbase) production has started at Giga Texas.
- Competitors lagged sharply: Lucid delivered ~4,000 units and replaced its CFO amid ongoing losses; Rivian delivered ~12,000 (+14%) but raised guidance; Ford sold ~9,000 EVs, with Mach-E down 31% YoY and F-150 Lightning discontinued.

## Financials
- Tesla stock fell about 8% on delivery day despite the record quarter, after running up ~12% into the report — read as the market treating deliveries as no longer the key valuation driver.
- Musk posted no acknowledgment of the delivery record, instead posting about Optimus production and Grok 4.5, which commentators read as a deliberate narrative shift toward robots/AI.
- Tesla's robotics and autonomy spend this year is reported above $25 billion, covering the Fremont Optimus line, a planned second Optimus factory at Giga Texas, Cybertruck ramp, and a chip fab.

## Humanoid Bots
- Musk posted July 1 walking the Optimus production line in Fremont, now built on the former Model S/X line; Tesla's Lars Moravy said the line is modular and reconfigurable as Optimus hardware evolves.
- Optimus V3's production design reveal and production start are both targeted for late July–August, with the robot reportedly already walking.
- Musk said next-generation AI chips will go to Optimus and training clusters before vehicles, a reprioritization away from cars.

## Market & Competition
- Michael Burry disclosed a Tesla short at $416 on June 30, alongside shorts on Nvidia, Caterpillar, Applied Materials, and a semiconductor ETF, framing AI capex as a bubble; position size undisclosed.
- Grok 4.5, built on a 1.5T-parameter "V9" model, is in private beta at SpaceX and Tesla, with Musk saying internal evals show performance "close to, perhaps exceeding" Anthropic's Opus.
- xAI has been folded into SpaceX; SpaceX reportedly bought ~$269 million of Tesla Megapacks this spring to power xAI data centers near Memphis.

## Energy
- Tesla deployed 13.5 GWh of storage in Q2, its second-highest quarter ever; trailing-12-month deployment hit a new high of 49 GWh, with cumulative lifetime deployment approaching 140 GWh.
