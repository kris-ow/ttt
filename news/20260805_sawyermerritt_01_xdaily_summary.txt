## Key Posts / Themes

1. **SpaceX Q2 2026 earnings — first as a public company, beats expectations:** SpaceX reported revenue of $7.81B, up 92% YoY from $4.07B and 66% QoQ from $4.69B, beating expectations of $6.81B. Net loss narrowed to $541M (from $1.008B YoY), loss from operations improved to $143M, and adjusted EBITDA reached $3.5B. The company holds $100B in cash and spent $18.3B in capex during the quarter. CFO Bret Johnsen called it a "momentous year" with "strong operating leverage."

2. **Starlink — record subscriber growth and "Mobile" upside:** Starlink hit 12 million subscribers by end of Q2 2026, doubling YoY and up 1.7 million sequentially (Shotwell called it the "best quarter to date"). Connectivity revenue rose 32% sequentially and 66% YoY to $4.3B. Shotwell pointed to Starlink Mobile as a major growth vector, citing a roughly $600B/year addressable revenue pool across Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile combined, saying SpaceX expects to "acquire quite a few" of their customers via better service and elimination of dead zones. Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile shares reportedly fell ~4.5% in after-hours trading following these comments. Musk separately said it's "not out of the question that Starlink will deliver a majority of the world's internet." A historical juxtaposition post noted Hughesnet's 2020 dismissal of Starlink's economics vs. Hughesnet's 2026 bankruptcy filing.

3. **SpaceX AI & Compute — explosive growth, Nvidia partnership deepens:** AI revenue was up 213% sequentially and 247% YoY to $2.6B, driven by new Cloud Service Agreements and Grok/X subscription revenue. CFO Johnsen said SpaceX is tracking toward $100B ARR by year-end from AI compute deals, citing $6.7B of additional cloud services revenue already contracted in the first few weeks of Q3, and said datacenter infrastructure investments are seeing under-1-year payback periods. SpaceX currently has 1.4GW of nameplate compute draw and expects to exceed 2GW by year-end, with cumulative compute "several times higher" by end of next year — Musk said "closer to 10GW than 5GW." SpaceX will build exclusively on Nvidia hardware going forward (citing Vera Rubin), and Musk said Nvidia will allocate SpaceX a "significant percentage" of its GPUs in 2027. SpaceX also announced a new Nvidia partnership to design the "Starmind AI1" satellite compute payload, using Nvidia Rubin GPUs and Vera CPUs for space-based datacenter-class compute. Musk noted Grok 4.6 is coming next week, Grok 4.7 in 3-4 weeks, and Grok 5 by year-end will incorporate "the entire corpus of SpaceX data."

4. **Starship heat shield "solved," V3 Starlink satellites on next flight:** Musk said on the earnings call: "I don't want to jinx it... I think I would call the heat shield problem solved at this point," based on data and visual inspection, with no technical obstacles seen to further progress. SpaceX will fly next-generation Starlink V3 satellites on the next Starship test flight, deploying them into orbit and operational for the first time, and will attempt to catch the ship. Separately, new satellite imagery showed a recovered Starship booster/ship being towed back to shore.

5. **SpaceX revenue outlook and Musk on hardware leadership:** Musk said internal projections for SpaceX reaching $1 trillion in revenue have moved up from 2031 to 2030, with a "nonzero chance" of 2029. He also said Tesla and SpaceX are, "outside of China... the two best companies at hardware, maybe even including China."

6. **Tesla–SpaceX financial linkage — Megapack purchases:** SpaceX's 10-Q filing disclosed it purchased $295 million of Tesla Megapacks in Q2 2026 for its datacenters, bringing cumulative Megapack spend to over $800 million since last year — a direct Tesla Energy revenue benefit tied to SpaceX's AI compute buildout.

7. **Tesla EV sales momentum — Australia and China records:** Tesla sold 4,778 vehicles in Australia in July, its best July ever in the country, up 404% YoY, with more than half being Model Y L units (the first month Tesla separated six-seater Model Y L sales). China-made EV sales rose to 93,579 units in July, up 37.8% YoY — Tesla's best July ever and best month of 2026 so far, marking nine straight months of growth per CPCA data.

8. **Tesla international expansion:** Tesla is preparing to launch vehicle sales in Argentina, posting multiple Buenos Aires job listings (Sales Advisor, Service Manager, Delivery Manager, etc.). Separately, Tesla hired a Business Development Manager to support the Tesla Semi's entry into the French market.

9. **Tesla EV incentives and FSD:** California is offering first-time EV buyers $3,500 off eligible new Model 3/Y inventory vehicles, for orders placed on or after August 3, 2026 while funds last. A post also showed Tesla's FSD V14.3.7 driving in the new Model Y L, though without specific performance claims beyond the demo.

10. **Autonomous driving competition — Waymo Dallas expansion, Nvidia Alpamayo 2 Super:** Waymo opened fully autonomous rides to everyone in Dallas, having served nearly 150,000 riders since its February launch. Nvidia unveiled "Alpamayo 2 Super," a new open frontier model targeted at robotaxis and autonomous vehicles, focused on handling rare/complex edge-case scenarios.
