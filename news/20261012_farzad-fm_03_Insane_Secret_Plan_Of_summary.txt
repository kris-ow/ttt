Notable quotes and data:
- Anthropic opened its own wet lab (announced September 23) and stated in June it decided to start its own drug development programs, all currently preclinical and focused on neglected diseases.
- Anthropic's September 23 experiment: ~950 Claude AI agents worked ~21 hours, narrowing 200,000+ enzymes to ~3,500 candidates and then 20 reports, discovering a new enzyme system similar to CRISPR (described by CRISPR pioneer Feng Zhang as "really interesting," and by Harvard's Philip Kranzusch as "not a breakthrough yet").
- Anthropic claims its in-house protein design experts, using its most powerful model, have accelerated individual drug development steps "approximately 10 times" versus the public model, which blocks professional biology/drug-development requests.
- Merck's Keytruda was expected to generate $31.6 billion in sales in 2025 alone, illustrating the profit gap between selling AI tokens and owning a drug.
- Takeda paid $4 billion for a drug partly developed using Schrödinger's software; Schrödinger received about $147 million for its role.
- Isomorphic Labs (Google DeepMind) has deals with Lilly and Novartis worth nearly $3 billion in potential milestone payments; first human trials have slipped from late 2025 to late 2026.
- On average it takes about 10.5 years to go from Phase I trials to drug approval, and only about 7.9% of drugs entering Phase I are ultimately approved; the FDA has not yet approved any drug developed by generative AI.
- On April 10, 2025, the FDA announced plans to shift from animal antibody testing toward AI models and lab-grown mini-organs; Charles River Laboratories' stock fell about 28% that day.
- Pharma's lobbying group spent a record $38.2 million on lobbying in 2025.
- Dave Blundin (October 6, on X): "You develop AI to the point where it can help someone build a weapon, but not far enough to cure cancer... I argue that this is exactly the situation we are in now."
- Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis (60 Minutes): drug development could shrink "from years to maybe months, maybe even weeks," adding "I think one day we might be able to cure all diseases with AI... Maybe within the next decade or so."
