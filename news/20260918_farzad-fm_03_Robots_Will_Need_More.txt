Physical AI in the form of robots will
need just as much intelligence, probably
far more than anything in the digital
world. And that intelligence will need
to be inferenced at the edge, which is
basically AI compute that can make
decisions on its own without having to
beam back to a super powerful brain in
the cloud somewhere like let's say
Skynet, but instead it will operate on
the device itself. that puts an AI
computer into virtually every physical
product that can create a useful action,
including self-driving cars, human or
robots, drones, construction equipment,
mechanical arms, ships, boats, and
everything in between. And in the case
of Tesla, they already have two massive
products that will require a massive
amount of chips that Terraab will build.
For example, Tesla's Cyber Cab is its
purpose-built autonomous taxi.
