Electrek's only Tesla/SpaceX-relevant article today covers a new Tesla Energy software feature for Powerwall.

## Energy
1. **Powerwall 3 adds heat pump optimization in Europe:** Tesla launched Heat Pump Optimization, letting Powerwall 3 and 3P send "normal" or "boost" signals to compatible heat pumps via the SG-Ready standard so homes can store surplus or cheap solar energy as hot water, per Tesla's support page and an X post from Tesla Energy. The feature requires specific firmware, an SG-Ready-certified heat pump with wired connection installed by a Tesla-certified installer, and is Europe-only with no stated US/Canada plans; Electrek notes there's no on/off toggle once wired. Fred Lambert calculates that a 300-liter tank heated 30°C extra could store roughly 10.5 kWh of thermal energy using about 3.5 kWh of electricity at a heat pump COP of 3, freeing the Powerwall's 13.5 kWh capacity for other loads. Lambert notes Tesla is "late to this," citing sonnen's 2023 software update enabling similar functionality and SG-Ready control already being standard among German PV energy managers; he calls the feature "smart" and "cheap" and frames it as part of Tesla squeezing more value from its installed Powerwall base (Tesla announced its 1 millionth Powerwall last year, per the article). [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/02/tesla-powerwall-3-heat-pump-optimization-sg-ready/)

## Editorial Stance
Fred Lambert's take is positive but notes Tesla trails competitors like sonnen in offering this type of heat-pump/solar integration.

Notable quotes and data:
- "Heat Pump Optimization allows your Powerwall to boost heating when there's surplus solar or energy is inexpensive." — Tesla Energy on X, cited by Electrek
- Germany sold 299,000 heating heat pumps in 2025, up 55% year-over-year, per BWP figures cited in the article
- Feature requires Powerwall firmware 26.26+ and Tesla app version 4.59.1+, per Tesla's support page
