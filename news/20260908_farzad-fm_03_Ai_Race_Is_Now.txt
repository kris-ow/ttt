Now the question becomes how did a
rocket company close this gap this
quickly and why could it eventually win
the whole AI race? The answer is that
the AI race is becoming a physical
systems race. Yes, the models are still
going to matter. They are the tools that
companies use to generate answers and
actions and they are the tools that
regular people like you or I use to find
out about the weird itch on our feet or
whatever. But models need data centers
and data centers need chips and chips
need power and users are going to want
physical products powered by this entire
system. And all of these layers need to
improve together over and over again at
a cost people can actually afford. And
the thing is, SpaceX is the only company
that's building the entire system, which
you can best think of as an intelligence
factory.
