Dillon Loomis (posting as "electrified") delivers an abbreviated video focused on near-term Tesla momentum — a major Houston robotaxi geofence expansion, F1 weekend robotaxi validation, an analyst upgrade tied to merger speculation, and a technical-analysis note clarifying his prior bearish-leaning video — alongside SpaceX's massive new GPU financing deal and expanded Anthropic compute agreement.

## Robotaxi
1. **Houston geofence expansion:** Tesla's robotaxi service area in Houston expanded roughly tenfold, from about 25 square miles to roughly 300 square miles, now comparable in size to Austin's geofence, with operating hours extended to 1:00 a.m. This expansion came 191 days after Houston service launched, and Tesla's Houston coverage is now about six times larger than Waymo's, whose geofence there remains around 50 square miles.
2. **Texas fleet growth:** Per RobotaxiTracker.com-sourced figures cited in the video, Tesla has 970 vehicles registered across Texas, 463 of which are Cybercabs; the host notes 424 robotaxis were added to the Texas fleet in October alone, and projects (his own extrapolation) that Tesla could reach around 2,000 registered robotaxis by year-end if the pace continues.
3. **F1 weekend validation:** Cybercabs served the U.S. Grand Prix at Circuit of the Americas in Austin over the weekend (attendance cited as easily over 100,000), an area Waymo's service does not cover; the host cites anecdotal rides including one Cybercab trip from COTA costing about $77 versus over $112 for an Uber covering the same route, and another post-midnight ride totaling about $60 for roughly an hour.
4. **Munro Live Cybercab teardown:** Munro Live released a deep-dive engineering video (over an hour long) on the Cybercab. Tesla engineers reportedly graded the Model Y structural battery pack a "D" internally (hard/costly to build and service) while the Cybertruck's structural pack improved to roughly a "B-minus." The video states the Cybercab line is currently outpacing robotaxi service deployment needs, so the line isn't running daily, and that the Cybercab is targeted for at least 500,000 miles of expected useful life.

## Financials / Market & Competition
1. **Barclays upgrade on merger speculation:** Analyst Dan Levy upgraded Tesla from underperform to overweight, raising the price target from $370 to $450, citing potential SpaceX-Tesla merger speculation plus strengthening core Tesla businesses and an inflection point in robotaxi.
2. **Merger timeline reporting and political risk:** The host cites reporting that a merger deal announcement is being targeted for the first half of 2027, with completion sought before the current presidential term ends in January 2029. SpaceX's IPO documents reportedly warned that a shift in congressional power could materially and adversely affect its growth and competitive position; the host also plays clips of commentators (Robert Reich, Kara Swisher) discussing political/regulatory risk to Musk's companies, to which Musk reportedly replied "Message received."
3. **TSLA technical setup:** TSLA closed up 5.21% on the day; the host says this (per a figure consistent with the reference table, around $391.62) marked a close above a downtrend line he's been tracking since earlier in the year, which he frames as a potential (not confirmed) trend-reversal signal, with next resistance seen near $405 and $420+ possible on a confirming close. He stresses this is a near-term observation, not a reversal of his prior caution about sustainability.
4. **SPCX price action:** SpaceX shares closed at $146.90, down on the day, while the Nasdaq-100 was up about 1.09%.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Cybertruck cancelled for Australia/New Zealand:** Tesla cancelled Cybertruck reservations for Australia and New Zealand, citing inability to fulfill them; affected customers will be refunded and offered a free one-year FSD subscription if they buy a new Model 3 or Model Y instead.
2. **Turo FSD integration:** Turo will begin advertising which Tesla listings have FSD enabled, verified directly via Tesla's API, with eligible hosts getting a one-month FSD trial; the integration goes live October 28. Turo says Model 3 and Model Y are its third and fourth most popular vehicles this year, and cites survey data showing 55% of Americans (71% of Gen Z) are interested in driverless tech for travel; Turo had over 5.5 million bookings platform-wide in 2025.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD Europe approval progress:** FSD has now been approved in 10 European countries, most recently Latvia, but this covers only about 14.3% of EU population versus the roughly 65% threshold needed for broader rollout; Musk commented that the smaller European countries are "moving fastest."

## SpaceX — AI & Compute / Corporate & Valuation
1. **$40B GPU financing deal:** SpaceX announced a binding commitment for a $40 billion master finance lease covering next-generation Nvidia Vera Rubin chips for its terrestrial compute buildout, which the host calls the largest GPU financing ever arranged. The lease is subject to closing conditions, expected to close before year-end, with lease schedules commencing upon GPU delivery in the first half of 2027. Musk reportedly said debt markets have largely shut off financing for companies lacking substantial non-AI-datacenter revenue, framing SpaceX's investment-grade status (backed by Starlink and launch revenue) as a differentiator versus "Neocloud" competitors.
2. **Anthropic compute deal escalation:** The host traces a rapidly escalating Anthropic-SpaceX compute agreement: initially about $1.25B/month (~$15B/year, ~$45B total over 3 years) earlier this year; raised in September to a potential $85B over 3 years; and per an Anthropic filing referenced today, raised again to a potential total of $121 billion through 2029 — an incremental $36B over the prior cap. Current run-rate is cited as $3.1 billion/month (annualizing to over $37 billion/year). These agreements remain largely cancellable with 90 days' notice, which SpaceX says is intentional.
