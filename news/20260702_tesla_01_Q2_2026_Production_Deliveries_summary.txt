Tesla released its official Q2 2026 production, deliveries and deployments figures pre-market on July 2: 451,758 vehicles produced, 480,126 vehicles delivered, and 13.5 GWh of energy storage deployed. Deliveries beat the company-published Wall Street consensus of 406,024 by roughly 74,000 units (+18%) and set a new Q2 record, surpassing Q2 2023's 466,140.

## Electric Vehicles

1. **Official Q2 2026 figures:** Model 3/Y production 442,936 and deliveries 467,762; Other Models production 8,822 and deliveries 12,364; totals of **451,758 produced** and **480,126 delivered**. 2% of deliveries were subject to operating lease accounting (both segments).

2. **Deliveries outpaced production by 28,368 units**, drawing down the inventory built in prior quarters (Q1 2026 alone added ~50,000 units to inventory on production of 408,386 vs. deliveries of 358,023). Quarter over quarter, deliveries grew ~34% from Q1's 358,023.

3. **Best Q2 in company history.** The 480,126 total exceeds the prior Q2 record of 466,140 (Q2 2023) and comes against a depressed Q2 2025 comparison base, when volumes were held back by factory retooling.

## Energy

1. **13.5 GWh of energy storage deployed** — up ~53% from Q1 2026's 8.8 GWh, slightly below the 13.8 GWh Wall Street consensus.

## Financials

1. **Forecast scoreboard:** The result landed far above the Tesla-published analyst consensus of 406,024 and above nearly every community estimate tracked ahead of the report — Gary Black 420K, FutureAzA 431K, Troy Teslike 466K, Khi 468K. The 482K calls (James Stephenson, "PY") were closest; FutureAzA's Randy had flagged 490K+ as possible on inventory drawdown.

2. **Q2 earnings scheduled for Wednesday, July 22, 2026**, after market close, with the Q2 update and management Q&A webcast at 4:30 p.m. Central Time. Net income and cash flow results will be reported then.
