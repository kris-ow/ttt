## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Merger timeline: Yashu Chararma (Hit That Bid) argues no serious Tesla-SpaceX merger vote happens before mid-2027, citing post-IPO price discovery, insider lockup normalization, and unresolved Tesla-China/SpaceX-defense questions; he calls merger overhang the single biggest factor suppressing TSLA short-term.
- Merger mechanics debated: a 1-for-1 exchange ratio would likely fail a Tesla shareholder vote, per the discussion; Yashu estimates a plausible range of roughly one to just over three SpaceX shares per Tesla share based on typical 15–20% M&A premiums, stressed as speculative.
- Counter-view relayed (attributed to analyst Alexandra Merz): Musk and Gwynne Shotwell comments in separate interviews hinted at merger openness, and Musk may want to move before U.S. midterms — Yashu disputes the midterms rationale.
- SpaceX's AI segment (xAI/X, folded in pre-IPO) has flipped from a money-losing narrative to EBITDA-positive and fast-growing.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Cloud/compute deals ramping: $14.1B in Colossus/Colossus 2 cloud agreements signed in Q2 (customers include Google, Anthropic), plus $6.7B more signed in early July; AI/compute segment swung to ~$1.1B EBITDA-positive from a $600M loss last quarter.
- Compute capacity: ended June at 1.4 gigawatts, targeting 2GW+ by year-end and ~10GW by end of next year per Musk, with power/cooling lined up for 20GW; SpaceX says it has gone exclusive with Nvidia for a very significant share of next year's GPU output.
- New compute capital guided to pay back in under a year at current ~$35B/GW/year pricing, per CFO — Brian Wang notes payback stretches toward 1.5 years if pricing decays, meaning bull-case revenue needs sustained high pricing to hold.
- Grok 4.5 tripled token consumption in July; the $60B Cursor acquisition is expected to close within weeks, possibly next week per The Information.
- Yashu (Hit That Bid) is skeptical SpaceX hits its ~$100B run-rate/trillion-dollar 2029 targets on schedule, calling them "north star" recruiting goals; he sees Grok lagging Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's Codex in enterprise use today.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- V3 satellite step-change: ~10x more capable than V2 per Musk, with ~10x as many being launched — a third-party analysis (Cern Basher) puts this at over 20x bandwidth per launch, implying roughly 100x total constellation bandwidth. First V3 expected on the next Starship flight, tentatively end of August.
- Enterprise/government Starlink revenue up 108% YoY; Shotwell says the company has never lost an enterprise customer. Aviation wins this quarter include American, Southwest, Virgin Atlantic, Iberia, Aer Lingus, with under 10% market penetration; over $6B in U.S. government (mostly Space Force/Starshield) contracts signed in Q2.
- FCC approved transfer of EchoStar spectrum to SpaceX (65 MHz, 13x current spectrum); next-gen mobile satellites (~10x current count) start flying next year per Shotwell, targeting the ~$600B U.S. carrier market via rooftop-mounted small cellular base stations.
- United Airlines reportedly told sources/consultants that business travelers specifically book Starlink-equipped flights, with Wi-Fi satisfaction scores over double the rest of the fleet — a secondhand account, not a formal United statement.
- Consumer Starlink hit 12 million subscribers at end of June (double a year prior), adding a record 1.7 million in the quarter; pricing steady at $66/month, generating $1.7B operating income (39% margin).

## SpaceX — Starship
- Flight 13 (July) deployed 20 production V3 Starlink satellites, re-lit a Raptor in space, and achieved the softest splashdown yet with the ship intact; Musk says he considers the heat shield problem solved with no remaining technical obstacles to full reusability, while hedging against jinxing it.
- Next flight tentatively targeted for end of August, aiming for a tower catch pending regulatory approval. Additional roadmap items — flight-a-day cadence within a year, lunar factories, boots on the moon around 2028 — are flagged as high-uncertainty and not required for the 2027 revenue floor.
