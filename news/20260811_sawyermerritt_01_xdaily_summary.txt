## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Cybercab gets official Starlink integration:** Tesla unveiled a Starlink dish integration on the Cybercab, described as having a gold cover and flush mounting. Notably, this connectivity isn't just for vehicle operations — Cybercab riders will reportedly be able to use Starlink to stream 4K video, work, and browse the internet during rides, per the poster. This is a tangible, product-level linkage between the two companies' theses: Tesla's robotaxi passenger experience directly leveraging SpaceX's satellite network.

2. **Model Y L demand strong, sold out into December:** The Model Y L is reported nearly sold out for 2026 in the U.S., with new estimated delivery dates showing December across the country, despite deliveries only beginning in September. This suggests strong near-term demand for the model.

3. **"Project Crystal Sun" — $10B Texas facility:** Drone footage showed land near Houston where Tesla reportedly wants to build a $10 billion manufacturing facility referred to as "Project Crystal Sun." The project is claimed to create approximately 9,700 permanent full-time jobs and increase Texas state GDP (the specific GDP figure was cut off/incomplete in the post). No further detail on what will be manufactured there was given.

4. **Starlink commercial expansion continues:** Lufthansa Group announced it will begin rolling out Starlink Wi-Fi next week across its partner airlines (over 850 aircraft), with the first Starlink-equipped flight on August 19th; full group-wide equipping is targeted by 2029. Separately, Bolivia's president announced plans to install more than 5,000 Starlink kits in unconnected rural areas over the coming months to serve rural schools. Both reflect continued Starlink enterprise/government adoption momentum.

5. **Austin Robotaxi meetup anticipation:** A Tesla fan event (@theXtakeover) is expected to draw roughly 2,000 attendees to Austin in October, with the poster speculating Tesla will need a larger Robotaxi fleet by then to meet demand, noting many attendees would be taking their first Robotaxi ride. This is speculative commentary, not a confirmed fleet expansion plan.

6. **Franz von Holzhausen media appearance:** A new episode of Jay Leno's Garage featuring Tesla Chief Designer Franz von Holzhausen aired, showing his personal 2006 Pontiac Solstice that he designed prior to Tesla. This is a lifestyle/legacy-design feature rather than Tesla product news.
