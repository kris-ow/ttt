The article reports that former Tesla SVP Drew Baglino has quietly founded Sadi Thermal Machines, a startup building the residential heat pump Tesla publicly discussed in 2022 but never shipped, per TechCrunch's reporting.

## Energy
1. **Tesla teased a residential heat pump years ago, then dropped it:** On Tesla's Q4 2021 earnings call (early 2022), Baglino and Elon Musk discussed a residential heat pump for home HVAC and water heating; Baglino called it well-aligned with Tesla's mission and said it would be easier to build than a vehicle version, while Musk said "it is a thing we will do, but we're not committing to a time frame." Tesla never released such a product.
2. **Baglino's new startup, Sadi Thermal Machines:** Incorporated in June 2025, based in Scotts Valley, California (shared HQ with Baglino's other startup, Heron Power), reportedly staffed with several Tesla alumni and operating largely in stealth with no public product yet. Baglino was a named inventor on Tesla's "octovalve" thermal system patent used in the Model Y and rose to SVP over Tesla's powertrain and energy engineering before leaving in April 2024; his first post-Tesla venture, Heron Power (solid-state transformers), raised $140 million in February.
3. **Market context — competitors moving into all-in-one heat pumps:** Differ Power plans to launch a "9-in-1" heat pump (D1 Series) at Intersolar Europe in Munich, combining heating, cooling, hot water, solar inversion, battery management, backup power, and energy management; Samsung launched an all-in-one residential heat pump in January; the article notes a broader wave of US/UK startups pursuing similar integrated products.

## Financials
1. **Tesla's capital shifted toward robots/robotaxi over energy products:** The article notes Musk has said roughly 80% of Tesla's future value will come from Optimus (not yet in meaningful production), and that Musk has requested a pay package worth up to $1 trillion tied largely to building/controlling Tesla's robots and the robotaxi push — cited as context for why non-robot, non-autonomy energy products like a home heat pump never materialized.
2. **Quebec class action over vehicle heat pump defects:** Tesla was hit with a class action in Quebec this month alleging heat pump defects in its vehicles, potentially covering more than 55,000 owners and up to $400 million in damages, following one Model 3 owner facing a $4,477 repair bill.

## Editorial Stance
Electrek's Fred Lambert argues Baglino has strong "founder-market fit" for a heat pump startup given his Tesla thermal-systems background, but notes Sadi is still stealth-mode with no product yet to evaluate.
