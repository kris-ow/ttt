## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla releases first EU FSD safety data:** Tesla published its first FSD (Supervised) safety dataset from its European Union fleet, claiming 5.2x fewer collisions in FSD-approved countries, 8.5x safer highway driving (41.9 million km with zero collisions), 13.4x fewer automatic emergency braking events, 9.2x less harsh acceleration, and 7.1x [additional metric cut off]. This is a notable regulatory/PR data point as Tesla pushes for broader FSD approval in Europe.

2. **FSD framed as mobility solution for seniors/disabled — media coverage:** Autoline's John McElroy commented that FSD "opens mobility for everybody," specifically citing elderly and disabled users, describing it as a "guardian angel." Separately, a Detroit News article (highlighted by Sawyer as "fantastic") profiled seniors using FSD to maintain transportation independence. Combined engagement (946 and 4.8K likes) suggests strong resonance with the FSD-as-independence narrative that Tesla and supporters are pushing.

3. **Model Y Canada demand signal:** Every Model Y variant in Canada is reportedly now showing delivery dates at the end of 2026 extending into early 2027, which Sawyer interprets as Tesla having sold through nearly all of its planned 2026 Canadian Model Y allocation with five months still remaining in the year — a bullish demand signal, though this is the poster's own interpretation rather than a confirmed Tesla statement.

4. **Tesla signs two new long-term solar PPAs:** Tesla struck a deal with ContourGlobal to buy 90% of output (509MW solar+battery, per post) from Project Sterling in Arizona, scheduled to begin operating in 2028. Separately, Tesla signed a PPA with Zelestra for the entire output of the 140MWac Lumen Farm solar project in Texas, expected to start construction in 2027 and reach full operations in 2029. Both deals reflect Tesla's growing appetite for long-term clean power procurement, likely to support Supercharging, data centers, and manufacturing load.

5. **Tesla wildfire relief gesture:** Tesla is offering free Supercharging (open to all EV owners, not just Tesla) at select locations in wildfire-affected areas of France and Spain through midnight August 5th — a goodwill/PR move highlighted favorably against traditional oil companies.

6. **Ford EV unit posts steep Q2 loss:** Ford's Model e EV division lost $900 million in Q2 with a -89.6% EBIT margin; EV revenue fell 58% YoY to $1 billion, attributed to Mach-E production right-sizing and model discontinuations. This underscores the competitive/profitability gap between legacy automakers' EV efforts and Tesla.

7. **Starlink expands to Iraq:** Starlink is now available in Iraq, with residential 100 Mbps plans starting around IQD 131,500/month (~$100 USD/month) — incremental international expansion for Starlink's subscriber base.

8. **Cybercab anticipation:** A brief, speculative post about watching "a giant fleet of Cybercabs return to base at the end of their shifts" — forward-looking commentary with no new operational data, reflecting sentiment rather than a concrete update.
