Trains are spectacularly, almost
unbelievably cheap at moving stuff.
Moving 1 ton of cargo 1 mile by rail
costs somewhere around 2 to 5 cents per
mile. As we already know, that same
cargo costs way, way, way more in a
truck. So, then why does anything move
by truck at all? Why isn't everything on
a freaking train? Because trains have
one fatal flaw. A train only goes where
the tracks go. It runs on a fixed line
on a fixed schedule, and your factory
and warehouse is almost certainly not
sitting next to a rail line. And neither
is the store you shop at. So, even when
you do ship something by rail, you still
need a truck on both ends just to get
the stuff to and from the tracks. Trains
are stupid cheap, but insanely rigid.
