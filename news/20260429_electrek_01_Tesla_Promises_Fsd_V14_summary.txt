Tesla posted on X promising to expand FSD V14 Lite to HW3 vehicles in international markets, but only after the US rollout is complete and with no timeline given — a response to growing backlash from European and international HW3 owners who were excluded from Tesla's first European FSD approval.

## Autonomous Driving

1. **Tesla's vague international HW3 commitment:** Tesla's statement says V14 Lite will expand internationally after the US HW3 rollout, subject to "technical verification, regional adaptation & relevant regulatory approvals" — at least three separate dependencies. The US rollout of V14 Lite for HW3 is currently targeted for end of June 2026, meaning international HW3 owners would be waiting well into the second half of 2026 at the earliest. Tesla confirmed during the Q1 2026 earnings call that HW3 "simply does not have the capability to achieve unsupervised FSD," meaning V14 Lite remains a Level 2 supervised system.

2. **European FSD approval triggered the backlash:** Tesla received FSD Supervised approval in the Netherlands — its first European approval — but limited it to HW4 vehicles only. HW3 owners who paid up to €6,400 for FSD as far back as 2019 were excluded. A Dutch Model 3 owner launched a collective claim site, attracting roughly 3,000 owners from 29 countries representing €6.5 million in FSD purchases.

3. **Musk's "micro-factories" for HW3 retrofits:** During the Q1 2026 earnings call, Musk revealed Tesla plans to build dedicated micro-factories to retrofit approximately 4 million HW3 vehicles worldwide with the newer AI4 computer, new cameras, and new wiring harnesses — work deemed too complex for existing service centers. One day after the earnings call, Tesla announced HW4 Plus with doubled memory, raising questions about whether HW4 owners face the same obsolescence cycle.

## Editorial Stance
The author argues Tesla is doing "the absolute bare minimum" to address a crisis of its own making — having sold FSD on explicit promises of future-proof hardware — and that the micro-factories proposal is an admission the original promise was fundamentally broken, while HW4 Plus signals the upgrade treadmill will continue.
