Tesla is telling customers that the current window to transfer a paid Full Self-Driving (FSD) package to a new vehicle will end this quarter, framing it as the final such opportunity — a claim the article says Tesla has made and reversed repeatedly since first introducing the "one-time" transfer program in Q3 2023.

## Autonomous Driving / Electric Vehicles
1. **FSD transfer deadline and subscription shift:** Tesla is urging owners with the FSD package (which the article says has cost customers up to $15,000) to upgrade to a new vehicle before March 31, 2026, to use what it describes as the last FSD transfer window. This coincides with Tesla's previously announced plan, disclosed last week, to stop selling FSD as a one-time purchase starting next month, moving to a subscription-only model — meaning this could be the last chance to secure a perpetual FSD license on a new vehicle, if the deadline holds.
2. **History of repeated "final" transfer windows:** The FSD transfer program began in Q3 2023, when Elon Musk reportedly called it a "one-time amnesty" and urged immediate use, saying it would not recur. The article states Tesla has since reopened the transfer window multiple times, turning what was billed as a one-time offer into a recurring end-of-quarter sales incentive tied to delivery pushes.

## Editorial Stance
The author (Fred Lambert) argues this is a cynical sales tactic: Tesla is using its own failure to deliver the Level 4-5 unsupervised autonomy it originally sold with FSD as a demand lever, effectively holding customers' $15,000 payments hostage to pressure new-vehicle purchases. He predicts the transfer window will be reopened again despite Tesla's claims, and argues FSD should instead be permanently tied to the customer's account until true unsupervised driving is delivered. He states the shift to subscription pricing "changes nothing" about this dynamic.
