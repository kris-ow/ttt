The article reports that Tesla Megapacks are powering Ørsted's newly connected Old 300 Storage battery project in Needville, Texas, part of the ERCOT grid.

## Energy
1. **Old 300 Storage project specs:** The 250 MW/500 MWh battery energy storage system (BESS), built with Tesla Megapacks, is fully connected to the ERCOT grid and can deliver 250 MW of power for two hours at full output. Tesla's current two-hour Megapack configuration provides about 1.9 MW of power and 3.85 MWh of storage per unit; the Megapacks were manufactured at Tesla's Megafactory in Lathrop, California.

2. **Grid role and independence from solar:** The BESS sits next to Ørsted's 430 MW Old 300 Solar farm (operating since 2024, generating enough power annually for around 80,000 Texas homes/businesses per Ørsted) but operates independently, charging from and discharging to the wider ERCOT grid rather than being limited to the solar farm's output. Ørsted Americas Onshore president Melissa Peterson said battery storage has played a large role in providing power to ERCOT during tight supply-demand periods.

3. **Economic and portfolio impact:** The combined solar and battery projects are expected to generate around $110 million in property tax revenue for local schools, infrastructure, and emergency services. The deployment expands Ørsted's operating US onshore portfolio to around 6 GW; Tesla says it has more than 58 GWh of industrial storage systems operating in over 65 countries.
