Electrek's Tesla-relevant coverage today centers on a sharp drop in Tesla's domestic China sales.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Tesla's China domestic sales crash to four-year low:** Per CPCA data cited by Electrek, Tesla China wholesaled 67,829 vehicles in October (up 10.3% year-over-year), but 59,012 of those were exports, leaving just 8,817 cars for domestic Chinese buyers — down 66% year-over-year and Tesla's worst domestic month since July 2022. Per CnEVPost, nearly all were Model Ys (7,807) versus 1,010 Model 3s. Compared to September's 64,837 domestic sales, the drop was 86%. China's overall NEV retail market fell 10% year-over-year in October per CPCA preliminary data, and the passenger car market fell 25%, while Xiaomi delivered over 50,000 vehicles and Leapmotor delivered 106,127 (up 51%). Through October, Tesla's year-to-date China sales are down 15% year-over-year to 389,905, its lowest January–October total in at least four years, while exports are up 101% to 420,984 — meaning Shanghai now exports more than it sells domestically, reversing last year's pattern. Fred Lambert argues the usual "first month of the quarter" seasonal pattern doesn't explain the magnitude of the drop, attributes part of the export surge to automakers front-loading shipments ahead of China's VAT export rebate cut from 13% to 9% effective April 2027, and contends fading Chinese demand for an aging Model 3/Y lineup — against fast-refreshing rivals — is a real and separate problem; he frames November–December deliveries as the key test of whether this was timing or a structural demand issue. [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/10/tesla-china-sales-crash-october-2026-lowest-four-years/)

## Editorial Stance
Fred Lambert's China sales piece is openly skeptical of Tesla's "seasonal pattern" explanation, arguing the domestic collapse reflects genuine fading demand in China rather than just quarterly timing, and characterizes Tesla's export shift as not "a vote of confidence" in its China business.

Notable quotes and data:
- Tesla's October China domestic sales: 8,817 units, down 66% year-over-year, lowest since July 2022 (CPCA data via Electrek)
- October exports from Giga Shanghai: 59,012, up 66% year-over-year; year-to-date exports up 101% to 420,984, now exceeding domestic sales (420,984 vs. 389,905 YTD)
- China NEV retail sales down 10% year-over-year in October; overall passenger car market down 25% (CPCA preliminary data)
- Tesla's YTD China sales down 15% year-over-year to 389,905, about 22% below the 2024 peak
