This short video relays a claim from Elon Musk, made at an unspecified conference, that SpaceX is leveraging its rocket-engine casting expertise to manufacture gas turbine blades and nozzles in-house, bypassing a severe industry-wide supply bottleneck for these components.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Turbine blade/nozzle bottleneck:** Per Musk, gas turbine blades and nozzles are the long-lead-time bottleneck for new turbines, with suppliers reportedly taking 12–18 months to deliver just these two parts while all other turbine components arrive faster. He attributed the difficulty to the extreme precision casting required, since the blades sit directly behind the flame in gas hotter than the metal itself can withstand.
2. **SpaceX applying rocket foundry to turbines:** Musk said the same SpaceX team that casts rocket engine parts — which he described as SpaceX's foundry being the most modern of its kind — is now casting turbine blades for the turbines powering its AI data centers, which he claims lets SpaceX bring power online 12–18 months faster than competitors relying on the standard supply chain.
