## Autonomous Driving

- **Denmark approves FSD Supervised**, the fourth European country in approximately eight weeks, following the Netherlands, Lithuania, and Estonia. The Danish authority conducted its own independent technical review before accepting the Dutch RDW provisional type approval, notable given Denmark had previously raised EU-level concerns about FSD speeding and icy-road performance.

- **Netherlands FSD safety data (April 10–June 5, 2026):** Over 23.6 million km driven, supervised FSD logged 3.5x fewer collisions than manual driving overall. On highways specifically, 3.4x safer across 16.6M km with zero collisions; 1.6x safer on non-highway roads across 7M km with three collisions. Additional metrics: 15x fewer automatic emergency braking events, 8.8x less harsh acceleration, 7.3x less harsh braking.

- **EU-wide vote window shifts to October 2026 at earliest.** The June 30 TCMV meeting agenda lists only continued discussion of the Dutch authorization request — no vote. Per Electrified's analysis, a surprise vote is near-zero probability given the required two-week advance circulation of a formal draft act. Actual EU-wide rollout could slip to early 2027.

- **Critical downside risk: provisional approvals could lapse.** If the EU Commission formally rejects the Dutch authorization, the provisional approval becomes invalid after six months, taking all four national approvals — and the ability to market FSD in those countries — down with it.

- **German OEM Level 3 retreat hands Tesla competitive ground.** Mercedes-Benz paused Drive Pilot from upcoming S-Class and EQS facelifts; BMW discontinued its Personal Pilot L3 system entirely, pivoting to Level 2+. FSD Supervised is now the most capable driver-assistance system widely available in Europe. Belgium and Greece are reportedly moving quickly toward national approval; Germany, France, Italy, and Spain remain in review.

## Robotaxi

- **Cathie Wood publicly endorsed the unsupervised Austin Robotaxi experience**, generating 6.5K likes on X. Her quote: *"The fact that I was talking to you the whole time and didn't pay any attention to the ride itself means that I think it's completely safe; I'm excited for Tesla. I'm excited for Tesla shareholders."*

- **Texas fleet registrations: 59 VINs** now listed on the Texas Motor Carrier website, up from ~40 approximately two weeks ago. All 59 are currently Model Ys. Per Electrified, this represents Tesla's maximum deployable Texas fleet at this moment, not confirmed active vehicles.

- **Cybercab inventory visible in Texas lots.** A Houston flyover showed 21 Cybercabs staged alongside Model Ys; a Dallas lot video showed dozens more. Electrified cautions that no Cybercab without a steering wheel and pedals has been spotted on-site, keeping near-term Cybercab commercial deployment timelines in question.

- **Cybercab testing now spans approximately 20 US states**, indicating broad geographic preparation well ahead of commercial launch.

- **Arizona Level 4 operating domain disclosed.** A Tesla first responder interaction plan for Arizona, dated April 24, lists the robotaxi service's design domain as SAE Level 4 across Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Mesa, Paradise Valley, Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, and unincorporated Maricopa County — a combined population of approximately 4.8 million people.

- **Q1 slide deck city launch timeline under serious pressure.** Tesla's Q1 deck indicated launches in five cities within a specific window; with approximately 20 days remaining as of June 9, Electrified assessed the target as not achievable.

- **Bhakdi's price target trigger:** In response to a viewer question, Jo Bhakdi stated that if Tesla reaches 15,000 robotaxis deployed, he believes the share price will go "way above $700." He described himself as "pretty bullish, not very bullish" on near-term robotaxi progress.

## Energy

- **Texas Megapack factory approaching operational status.** At full ramp, the facility located approximately 40 minutes from Houston will produce 50 GWh of Megapacks annually and employ up to 1,500 people. This brings Tesla's projected total global Megapack manufacturing capacity to approximately 110 GWh across three facilities: California (40 GWh), China (20 GWh), and Texas (50 GWh).

## Electric Vehicles

- **Model 3 RWD wins Edmunds real-world efficiency test.** The vehicle achieved 393 miles on a full charge versus its 363-mile EPA estimate — 30 miles or 8.3% beyond the official figure — and returned 21.7 kWh per 100 miles versus the EPA's 25 kWh rating, making it 13.2% more efficient than the EPA estimate and the most efficient EV tested by Edmunds currently in production.

- **Tesla ranks 6th overall and 3rd among luxury brands for 250,000-mile durability** in an iSeeCars study covering 174 million vehicles, with a 4.6% probability of reaching that threshold. This compares favorably to the luxury segment average of 3.2% and outranks BMW, Mercedes, Porsche, Volvo, and Mazda. Vehicle longevity is a direct input to robotaxi fleet economics and DCF assumptions on useful vehicle life.

- **Tesla Semi European commercial buildout underway.** Tesla posted a Business Development Manager role for the Semi, based near Munich, focused on B2B fleet sales and charging infrastructure. No European production or delivery timeline disclosed; Electrified does not expect Semi sales in Europe before 2027.
