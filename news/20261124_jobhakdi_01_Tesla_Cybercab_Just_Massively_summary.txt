In this live stream, Jo Bhakdi argues that Cybercab deployment has quietly accelerated, citing a counter on his Pioneer Lands platform that jumped to 582–583 units after about 48 hours of not checking it. He pairs this with the FSD v15 rollout and the pending Tesla–SpaceX merger as reasons Tesla is holding up better than SpaceX and the NASDAQ on a down day. Much of the stream is channel promotion, community talk, and a Nvidia/Anthropic conspiracy tangent, which is omitted here.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab counter jumps:** The host's tracker, which consolidates several robotaxi trackers including robotaxitracker.com, showed 582 Cybercabs (583 later in the stream). He says 285 were deployed in the last week, "actually over the weekend", which he calls roughly 100% growth and more than halfway to 1,000. He also says 776 are registered and expects to pass 1,000 registered in about two weeks. He projects about 2,000 Cybercabs registered by year-end and close to 2,000 deployed, and says Tesla could then scale to thousands "with the press of a button". These are his projections.
2. **Unit economics rule of thumb:** The host estimates, by his own admittedly conservative math, that 100,000 Cybercabs would double Tesla's net income. By the same scaling, 50,000 would add about 50% and 10,000 about 10%. He adds that returns could be roughly twice that if Tesla keeps prices high.
3. **Rider anecdote and expansion:** A viewer reports a first Cybercab ride in Dallas and calls it incredible. Asked why Tesla is holding Cybercabs back from Las Vegas, the host says he does not know and that details remain to be cleared, but that it is coming very soon.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD v15 reception:** The host says v15 was released November 21 and is getting rave reviews. He credits it for part of Tesla's relative strength. He highlights the new real-time, high-resolution visualization, which he says looks like a video game but is real. He also highlights improved pothole avoidance and human gesture recognition, plus the release video's "25 times safer" claim. He says v15 is being deployed in the Cybercab. A viewer notes the recent release went to select groups with mass deployment expected in December. The host says German officials are pushing for FSD approval but does not know when it will go live there.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Merger status:** The host cites an FT report that SpaceX and Tesla are working on an all-stock merger, with both sides wanting it done before year-end and two committees involved. He believes the merger is pressuring SpaceX shares and supporting Tesla's. His own estimate is a roughly 30% premium for Tesla holders, around $500 a share. He says he would vote yes and will model what Tesla shareholders actually receive once an offer exists. He also cautions that long-dated Tesla options should be structured to pay off even if the merger does not happen.
2. **SpaceX weakness and the unlock:** The host attributes SpaceX's roughly 5% drop on the day to the merger and to correlation with neoclouds such as Nebius (down about 11%) and CoreWeave (down 11–13%). He argues SpaceX is effectively a "hyper neocloud" and that the selling makes little sense. Viewers cite an 800-million-share unlock on December 9 (the lockup ends December 8 per the SpaceX prospectus). The host expects it to be a modest headwind if the price is near 145–150, and says holders are unlikely to sell under 135.
3. **SpaceX upside case:** The host says he is heavy in SpaceX calls at a $170 strike. He expects SpaceX to reach $170 before Tesla reaches $480, probably in January, because of its cash flow. He says SpaceX's annualized run rate could approach $150B if about 3 GW of compute is online, with results due on the January earnings call. He also cites David Tepper holding SpaceX as his fourth-largest position.
