Tesla's amended 10-K/A filing (submitted to the SEC on April 30, 2026) reveals $573.4 million in revenue from Elon Musk's other companies in fiscal year 2025, alongside tens of millions in expenses paid to entities Musk controls, plus a $2 billion equity investment that converted from xAI stock into SpaceX stock.

## Financials
1. **Related-party revenue totals $573.4 million:** Tesla recognized $430.1 million in revenue from xAI — primarily Megapack sales to power xAI's data centers — and $143.3 million from SpaceX, primarily vehicle sales. Megapack sales to xAI continued into 2026, with $78.1 million in additional revenue recognized through February alone. Tesla's total 2025 revenue was approximately $97.7 billion, making the xAI Megapack sales alone roughly 3.4% of Tesla's entire energy segment revenue.

2. **Expenses paid to Musk-affiliated entities:** Tesla paid $11.4 million to SpaceX under commercial, licensing, and support agreements; $400,000 for use of a SpaceX aircraft; $4 million to xAI for consulting and support; $3.3 million to X (formerly Twitter) for advertising; $900,000 to The Boring Company under commercial agreements; and $4.8 million to a Musk-owned security company for services provided to Musk, including in connection with his Tesla duties. Total expenses to Musk-affiliated entities: $24.8 million.

3. **$2 billion investment converts from xAI to SpaceX equity:** On January 16, 2026, Tesla agreed to invest $2 billion in xAI Holdings' Series E round. After SpaceX subsequently acquired xAI Holdings, Tesla's xAI preferred stock converted into SpaceX Class A common stock. Tesla completed the investment on March 12, 2026. The filing does not disclose Tesla's resulting stake, though reporting indicates it is less than 1% of SpaceX.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **SpaceX Cybertruck purchases inflate sales figures:** SpaceX purchased 1,279 Cybertrucks in Q4 2025 alone — representing 18% of all U.S. Cybertruck registrations that quarter — at a time when the Cybertruck was facing weak consumer demand. These purchases account for the bulk of the $143.3 million in revenue Tesla recognized from SpaceX.

## Energy
1. **Megapack sales to xAI are material to energy segment:** The $430.1 million in Megapack revenue from xAI in 2025, plus $78.1 million already recognized in early 2026, represents a significant and ongoing related-party revenue stream. The filing states transactions were conducted at rates generally available to unaffiliated third parties and were reviewed by Tesla's Audit Committee.

## Editorial Stance
Electrek views the cumulative scale of related-party entanglement as a genuine governance concern — not because any individual transaction is unreasonable on its face, but because Tesla's CEO controls counterparties on both sides of hundreds of millions of dollars in transactions, and a shareholder-approved $2 billion AI investment ultimately converted into stock in yet another Musk-controlled company, raising questions about whether Audit Committee review constitutes sufficient oversight.
