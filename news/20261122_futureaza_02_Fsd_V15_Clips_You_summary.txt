The host reviews early user-submitted clips of FSD V15 after Tesla teased the release the previous day, focusing on the new parking visualizations and path planning, with a fog-driving example. His thesis is that Tesla has taken an unassailable lead in FSD visualizations and in what the system actually does. He does not yet have V15 himself.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Teased V15 claims:** Tesla's teaser for FSD V15 promised a system 25 times safer, with road debris detection, pothole avoidance, and human hand gesture recognition. The host was skeptical until real users, not just influencers, posted footage. He says the clips that arrived overnight look fantastic, both visually and in function.

2. **Parking selection and visualization:** Clips from Zach Black (Model 3), Sawyer Merritt, and Como Code show the car asking which part of the parking lot you want to park in. The screen displays arrows on the road, marked spots, and the planned path, and the driver picks a spot and starts the maneuver. The host says this makes clear what the car sees and knows about its surroundings, and that it is more than the squiggly lines of earlier visualizations. A Sawyer Merritt clip also renders the inside of his garage as the car pulls in.

3. **Obstacle handling in a parking clip:** In a clip from Truth Tom, a fallen tree blocks the road during a parking attempt. The visualization shows the tree and its shadows. The car determines there is not enough room, aborts and backs up, then retries with a wider turn and gets around it. The host notes the trees jiggle in the visualization but says he does not care.

4. **Fog performance:** A clip shared by Yseph shows FSD handling fog. The host argues that lidar struggles because it reads suspended water droplets as obstacles. He adds that radar can see through fog but has very low resolution, with only dozens to hundreds of pixels even for advanced units, while the camera-based system handled the fog well in the clip.

5. **Rollout status and demand:** The host checked for the update repeatedly, including a manual recheck at midnight, and has not received V15. He argues the visualizations alone could persuade some people to buy a Tesla, and says commutes of around 10 hours a week are where supervised autonomy matters most.
