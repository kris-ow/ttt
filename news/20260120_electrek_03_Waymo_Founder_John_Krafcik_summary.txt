Former Waymo CEO John Krafcik, in comments to Automotive News surfacing from CES 2026, renewed his criticism of Tesla's camera-only approach to self-driving, arguing the hardware itself is inadequate for true autonomy.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Krafcik's sensor critique:** Krafcik argues Tesla's seven-camera setup (5-megapixel, mostly wide-view with one narrow-view) produces effective vision comparable to roughly 20/60 or 20/70 — worse than a passing DMV vision test — and that Tesla's removal of radar and ultrasonic sensors, combined with its refusal to adopt LiDAR, has "handcuffed" the car's AI to noisier, less reliable data than competitors like Waymo or Zoox use. He calls the resulting system a "bad case of myopia" that struggles with edge cases such as blinding sunlight, heavy rain, or low-contrast conditions.
2. **Past prediction on Robotaxi cited as credibility marker:** The article recalls that Krafcik predicted in early 2025 that Tesla would "fake" its Robotaxi launch by mimicking a working service rather than deploying genuine unsupervised autonomy — a claim the article says was borne out when Tesla's Austin pilot relied heavily on remote monitors and safety drivers.

## Robotaxi
1. **Safety driver removal timeline slipping:** The article states Musk said safety monitors would be removed a few months after the June launch, then in December said removal would happen within three weeks; the article says Tesla is now about a month past that December timeline with safety monitors still in place, which it frames positively given what it describes as Tesla Robotaxi's high crash rate even with safety drivers present.

## Editorial Stance
Electrek's Fred Lambert argues there is no evidence Tesla can reach Level 4 autonomy with its current hardware, that Tesla has consistently missed its own unsupervised-driving timelines, and that Krafcik's hardware critique carries extra weight given his track record of correctly predicting the Robotaxi launch's limitations. The author frames the current scoreboard as favoring Krafcik since Waymo operates real robotaxis while Tesla's system remains supervised.

Notable quotes and data:
- Krafcik: "Human vision is so much more capable than the vision of a car equipped with seven 5-megapixel cameras, only one of which is narrow-view, while all the others are wide-view... the actual effective vision more like 20/60 or 20/70. The rest of the cameras in a car like that wouldn't even pass a DMV vision test."
- Krafcik: "That's truly superhuman. With that level of data, you can do amazing things, as opposed to a car that has a really bad case of myopia, should be wearing glasses, and operates on a very limited data cycle."
- Musk said safety drivers would be removed a few months after the June Robotaxi launch; in December he said removal would occur within three weeks — the article says that timeline has now passed by about a month.
