Filling a parking lot with unused
vehicles accomplishes very little, but
where the demand is there, cheaper cars
make it easier to provide the
availability people need before they'll
depend on the service. I had to wait 30
minutes for the cyber cab to arrive
because the fleet is so new and there
aren't enough cars out there relative to
demand. Even if a lower rate is
appealing, somebody who needs to leave
now may still choose the more expensive
ride. Improving availability lets you
compete for that customer as well. This
is how I think Tesla builds a moat,
meaning an advantage that becomes
difficult for somebody else to catch up
