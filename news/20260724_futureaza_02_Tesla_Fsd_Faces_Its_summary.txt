This is a casual, on-the-ground interview at an EV enthusiast festival in Longview, Oregon, between a Futuraza host and FSD user/YouTuber David Moss, covering Moss's real-world FSD experience, his upcoming travel plans to test FSD and Cybercab, and comparisons between Tesla's hardware generations. The content is anecdotal rather than data-driven, offering qualitative insight into FSD reliability and public perception rather than hard metrics.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD user shares extensive real-world mileage:** Moss has used FSD for roughly 50,000 miles since last September, and describes it as generally reliable, contrasting it with common misconceptions from drivers of other brands' driver-assist systems (e.g., lane-keep assist), including an anecdote about a Hyundai Kona owner whose lane-keep system caused a crash.
2. **Hardware 3 vs Hardware 4 FSD comparison:** Moss reports that on Hardware 3 ("light" compute), FSD runs on about 15% of the compute used by Hardware 4/AI4, but delivers largely the same UI and functionality, with only minor differences — no mid-drive FSD activation via button (must use the stalk) and no street counter/API usage tracker. He describes Hardware 3 performance as getting positive user feedback.
3. **International FSD testing plans:** Moss plans to test FSD in Australia (where a Tesla Model Y owner is running a version he identifies as 14.33, without a "Mad Max" mode equivalent) and hopes to test in China soon, plus a planned future Europe trip (English Channel to Italy) once FSD becomes available there.
4. **Planned 50-state FSD road trip:** Moss plans to attempt using FSD to drive across all 50 US states in seven days later this summer, aiming to do so with minimal driver intervention; he says the route has been mapped and checked but calls it untested until attempted.
5. **Hardware 3 coast-to-coast attempt planned:** Moss and friends plan a coast-to-coast Hardware 3 FSD drive, tentatively around Labor Day, once a newer FSD release gets wider distribution to Hardware 3 vehicles; he says he personally doubts Hardware 3 will be able to complete it but wants to document the hardware 3 vs. hardware 4 differences.

## Robotaxi
1. **Miami robotaxi launch and Cybercab pursuit:** Moss says he is traveling to Miami for the opening of Tesla's robotaxi service there, then to Austin afterward, where he intends to stay as long as necessary to ride in a Cybercab.
