Electrek reports that Tesla is discounting unsold Model Y inventory in India after weak demand following its 2025 market launch, citing a Bloomberg report.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **India discounts on unsold Model Ys:** Bloomberg reportedly states Tesla is offering discounts of up to ₹200,000 (~$2,200) on unsold Model Y SUVs in India. The article states Tesla imported about 300 vehicles into India, of which roughly 100 remain unsold after early reservation holders canceled orders.
2. **Weak order volume and sales:** The article cites previous reports that Tesla received only about 600 orders for the Model Y in India, and that Tesla had sold only about 100 cars in the months following its launch (as of November, per Electrek's prior reporting).
3. **Pricing structure:** Tesla's Model Y starts at nearly $70,000 in India due to import duties as high as 110%, after Tesla chose to import vehicles fully rather than commit to local manufacturing under a government scheme that would have lowered tariffs in exchange for a local factory commitment.

## Editorial Stance
Author Fred Lambert argues the high India pricing was predictable and unworkable, noting he had written at launch that the pricing "didn't make sense," and says the addressable market at ~$70,000 is tiny in India. He argues Tesla had a viable path (committing to local manufacturing in exchange for lower duties) but declined it, likely because Tesla already has excess production capacity relative to demand; he says it's unclear how Tesla's India operations (including service centers and Superchargers already built) are sustainable at current scale without either local manufacturing or a change in government rules. A reader comment (quoted, not endorsed by Electrek) argues Musk has no long-term strategy and is chasing news cycles rather than viable market entry.
