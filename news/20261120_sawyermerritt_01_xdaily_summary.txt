## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Cybercab public launch in Dallas:** Tesla announced that Cybercab rides are officially launching for the public in Dallas, Texas, which Sawyer describes as Tesla's first Cybercab ride expansion to a new city beyond Austin. Rides are now live. The Dallas Cybercabs have Starlink installed directly on the back of the vehicle from the factory, a notable Tesla–SpaceX product tie-in. Earlier in the day, Sawyer spotted a fully driverless Cybercab with Starlink in Dallas, noting that Tesla teased the Dallas launch the day before and had registered an additional 200 Cybercabs with the Texas DMV a couple of days earlier. Sawyer also notes that Cybercab V1.5 has a rear trunk camera, presumably so the vehicle can tell when riders have taken all their belongings.

2. **Cybercab personal-ownership specs:** Tesla invited a few more people in Texas to order Cybercabs for personal use, and the specs page was seen for the first time. Listed specs include a $29,990 price, 279-mile range, 20.1 cu ft of storage, and 176" length, along with warranty terms.

3. **Optimus and China manufacturing:** Sawyer shared a new look at Tesla's Optimus production line. Separately, Elon visited Giga Shanghai for the first time since 2023, said Tesla will keep increasing production in China, and thanked the team.

4. **Policy and competition:** Sawyer reports that US lawmakers in Washington are closing in on a deal to ban Chinese cars from the US, which would keep automakers such as BYD and Geely out of the market. This would be a protective factor for Tesla's US position.

5. **Model Y L availability:** Tesla is still only selling the $62k Launch Series version of the Model Y L in the US, more than 4.5 months after its introduction. Sawyer notes this is more than twice as long as the smaller Model Y Launch Series was available in early 2025, a sign of slower ramp or demand-management for this variant.

## Signal Strength: **Bullish** — Cybercab's first public expansion beyond Austin, with factory-fitted Starlink and opening personal ordering, shows concrete robotaxi progress, partly offset by the extended Model Y L Launch Series-only availability.
