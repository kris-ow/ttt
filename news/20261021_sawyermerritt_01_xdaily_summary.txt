## Executive Summary
The day was dominated by SpaceX's Q3 2026 earnings call — its first as a public company reporting a GAAP profit — with blockbuster growth across Starlink, the SI (AI/compute) business, and launch, plus eye-popping forward guidance from Elon Musk on ARR and compute payback periods. Tesla-specific news was thinner, limited to a minor FSD point release, Roadster hardware details, and a Ferrari CEO's praise of the Roadster unveil, while Musk also addressed the Tesla-SpaceX merger question directly.

---

## Key Posts / Themes

1. **SpaceX Q3 2026 earnings: revenue and profit beat, capex surges:** SpaceX reported Q3 revenue of $14.58B (+161% YoY, vs. $12.7B estimate), adjusted EBITDA of $8.81B (+306% YoY), and GAAP net income of $4.40B (versus a $1.05B loss a year earlier). Capex hit $19.59B (+185% YoY), with $16.4B of that (~84%) going to SI (formerly "AI") infrastructure capex; the company reported 1.7 GW of compute installed as of Sept 30. Wall Street had been expecting this to be SpaceX's first adjusted-profitable quarter since its June 2026 IPO ($0.13/share estimate). Sawyer Merritt called it "the best earnings call from any of Elon's companies to date," citing the video format and executive depth.

2. **SI (AI/compute) business becomes SpaceX's largest segment:** SpaceX's SI business generated $7.56B in Q3 revenue, up 830% YoY and 195% quarter-over-quarter, making it the company's largest revenue segment — ahead of Starlink and launch. Elon Musk said compute payback period is currently under 12 months and suggested "it's not impossible" SpaceX could reach a 6-month payback period for compute next year. Musk also said he's personally spending up to 4 days a week in Memphis, with SpaceX tracking to bring 200,000 Nvidia GB200s online per month for the rest of the year (continuing into next year), and expressed "fairly confident" guidance on bringing an incremental 10 GW of compute online (statement cut off in source).

3. **Starlink momentum: subscribers, aviation, consumer growth:** Starlink consumer subscribers reached 13.6 million by end of Q3, up from 12 million in Q2 (+89% YoY). More than 3,800 aircraft were equipped with Starlink as of September 30, 2026, and Gwynne Shotwell said Starlink is now contracted with 12,000 aircraft — about a quarter of all aircraft — with aviation production having quadrupled since the start of the year and expected to double again by year-end.

4. **Forward guidance: Q1 2027 revenue range and 2027 ARR target:** CFO Bret Johnston guided to total company revenue of $33–37 billion in Q1 2027. Separately, Musk said SpaceX could exit 2027 with an annualized revenue run-rate (ARR) somewhere between $500 billion and $1 trillion — a guidance figure Merritt flagged as stunning, though it remains an unquantified Musk projection rather than a committed forecast.

5. **Custom AI chip roadmap and Grok Bot growth:** Musk said SpaceX aims to produce its first test chip in May/June 2027 at the Research Chip Fab facility at Giga Texas, with volume production targeted for roughly Q3 2028 at a larger "Terafab" facility in Texas. Separately, Grok Bot was reported to have 800,000 paid weekly users as of earlier this month, more than doubling month-over-month; Musk called its growth "the fastest growth of a product" he's seen, noting per-user usage is increasing rather than declining, and said enterprise Grok Bot adoption would expand (statement cut off in source).

6. **Starship flight 15 catch attempt confirmed:** Musk confirmed SpaceX will attempt to catch Starship on flight 15 next month, releasing a new simulation video depicting the planned catch.

7. **Tesla-SpaceX merger: Musk addresses shareholder committees:** Musk stated that both Tesla and SpaceX have formed special committees at shareholders' request to evaluate a potential merger, but that as a conflicted party he cannot participate in those discussions. He cautioned that "we should not assume a merger is a certainty," per the partial quote in the post.

8. **Tesla Roadster: hardware details and outside praise:** A post detailed the Roadster's rocket trim, which uses a larger 400-liter carbon overwrapped pressure vessel (COPV) in the passenger seat (making that version a one-seater), combined with a 200-liter tank in the rear available only on the Thrust and Rocket trims. Separately, Ferrari's CEO praised Tesla's Roadster unveil, saying it shows "there's still a lot to be done" in the automotive world and praising Tesla for "showing us how... you can still innovate and dare."

9. **Minor Tesla FSD update:** Tesla FSD v14.3.13 began rolling out, with the post noting "same release notes" as a prior version — a minor/incremental update with no new capability details disclosed.

10. **Elon Musk wealth/economics comments (Isaacson interview):** In a new interview with Walter Isaacson, Musk pushed back on wealth-related criticism, saying his "so-called wealth is simply a percentage ownership in SpaceX and Tesla" and arguing critics conflate resource allocation with consumption.
