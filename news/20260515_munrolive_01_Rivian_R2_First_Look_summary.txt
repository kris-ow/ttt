This video features a walkthrough of the Rivian R2 at ACT Expo 2026, conducted by Munro Live with Sam Anderson, Rivian's Fleet Sales Customer Success Manager. The discussion covers the R2's key specifications, exterior design philosophy, interior features, and the deliberate engineering and cost-reduction decisions that differentiate it from Rivian's R1 platform.

## Electric Vehicles

1. **Launch Edition Specs:** The R2 launch edition features approximately 330 miles of range from an 87–89 kWh usable battery pack, 0–60 mph in 3.5 seconds, and just over 650 horsepower. It uses a dual-motor all-wheel drive configuration with motors that are entirely new and bespoke to the R2 — described as smaller, more powerful, and more efficient than those found in the R1 lineup.

2. **Design Identity and Proportions:** The R2's exterior deliberately carries over Rivian's brand DNA — including the halo headlights and classic SUV silhouette — but features several distinguishing design elements including a clamshell hood, more exaggerated wheelbase proportions, squared-off mirror arms, and a rear charge port positioned on the driver's rear side. The overall effect was described as a scaled-down R1S, maintaining short front and rear overhangs for interior headroom and occupant space.

3. **Aerodynamic Rear Spoiler and Rear Wiper:** The R2 features a rear spoiler to maintain aerodynamic efficiency and silhouette continuity. Unlike some competitors (noted comparisons include Hyundai Ioniq and Jaguar/JLR models) that sacrifice a rear wiper for aerodynamic gains, Rivian retained a rear wiper on the R2, housed in a recessed "garage." Extensive testing was conducted on drainage, ice, and debris accumulation in this recessed area.

4. **Frunk Cost Reduction and Simplification:** The R2's frunk reflects a deliberate cost-reduction effort compared to the R1. Changes include relocating the frunk release button to a touch-capacitive button centered near the logo (moved from the bottom of the fascia), switching from a powered opening mechanism to gas strut operation, eliminating the two-stage load floor with reversible rubberized/carpeted mat from the R1, and reducing overall part count and fasteners. The hood liner is now a compression-molded PET piece serving primarily an NVH/acoustic function, contrasted with the finished injected-molded piece used in the R1.

5. **Interior Value Retention Despite Cost Focus:** Despite targeting a lower price point, the R2's interior retains numerous premium elements including fabric-wrapped IP components with live stitching (not faux stitch), vinyl wrap components with live stitching, vinyl seats with stitching patterns and perforation, ventilation and heating, backlit ambient LED lighting, and magnetic wireless charging in the center console. Simplifications include a single-hinge, manually lifted center console door replacing the two-hinge electronically released design from the R1.

6. **Halo Haptic Steering Wheel Controls:** The R2 features electromechanical steering wheel scroll wheels called "Halo haptic wheels." These wheels scroll up and down, toggle left and right, and can be pressed from both the front and rear. Critically, the detents are not mechanical — they are electromechanically generated, allowing the force, throw, and detent count to be adjusted contextually in software depending on what is being controlled. For example, fan speed uses seven distinct detents with resistance at the end of travel, while temperature control uses a finer, more granular resolution. This allows over-the-air software updates to modify the tactile feel. The Munro Live host noted these appear to be among the only examples of this electromechanical (non-mechanical) application in this form factor in the automotive industry.

## Market & Competition

1. **Segment Positioning and Competitive Context:** The R2 is explicitly positioned to be cost-competitive in a segment below the R1S, with cost reduction described as "the name of the game." Aerodynamic comparisons were drawn to Hyundai Ioniq and Jaguar/JLR vehicles. Rivian claims the R2 has one of the largest — if not the largest — frunks in its segment.
