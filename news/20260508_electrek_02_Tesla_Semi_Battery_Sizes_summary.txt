California's Air Resources Board has officially confirmed Tesla Semi battery capacities via regulatory filing (Executive Order A-374-0095, signed April 15), revealing the Long Range variant carries an 822 kWh usable pack and the Standard Range 548 kWh — both using NCMA lithium-ion chemistry with 4680 cells.

## Electric Vehicles

1. **Battery specs smaller than Musk's 2022 estimate, same range achieved:** Elon Musk estimated ~900 kWh for the 500-mile Long Range Semi in December 2022. The certified 822 kWh pack is roughly 80 kWh smaller, yet Tesla still rates the truck at 500 miles at full 82,000-lb GCW. The efficiency gain is partly explained by the production version weighing nearly 1,000 lbs less than the prototype, as revealed in Tesla's March refreshed design.

2. **Shared powertrain specs across both trims:** Both variants use an 800 kW (1,072 hp) tri-motor drivetrain with 525 kW sustained output and support 1.2 MW Megacharger speeds via an MCS 3.2 connector. At 1.2 MW, the Long Range can recover roughly 60% charge in 30 minutes.

3. **Standard Range as strategic product:** The 548 kWh Standard Range is estimated at $260,000 — $30,000 less than the $290,000 Long Range — and covers 325 miles, sufficient for the majority of Class 8 regional haul routes under 300 miles. The article notes the modular battery architecture appears to remove one of three parallel modules, yielding almost exactly a one-third reduction from 822 kWh to 548 kWh.

## Market & Competition

4. **Tesla Semi leads competitors on range and efficiency:** The Freightliner eCascadia tops out at 550 kWh for 230 miles; the Volvo VNR Electric at 564 kWh for 275 miles; the Nikola Tre BEV claims 330 miles. At 1.7 kWh per mile (~0.6 miles/kWh), the Semi is materially more efficient than competitors at roughly 0.4–0.5 miles/kWh.

## Financials

5. **Cost of ownership and delivery projections:** The article cites a TCO analysis showing Semi energy costs of approximately $0.20/mile at $0.12/kWh versus $0.67/mile for diesel at $5.35/gallon, with estimated 10-year savings exceeding $400,000. Analyst projections for 2026 deliveries range from 5,000 to 15,000 units, with the author noting even 1,000 deliveries would be a positive signal for the Nevada production ramp. Tesla's Megacharger network currently spans 66 locations across 15 states, with pricing starting at $188,000 for a two-post setup.

## Editorial Stance
The author argues the 822 kWh Long Range pack achieving 500 miles is more impressive than a 900 kWh pack doing the same — reflecting genuine efficiency gains — but contends the Standard Range at $260,000 will be the more impactful product for fleet adoption, as most Class 8 operations run regional routes where the cheaper, lighter truck wins on economics.
