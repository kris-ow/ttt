## Brief

## Robotaxi
- Morgan Stanley analyst Andrew Percoco says Tesla needs more vehicles, greater city density, rising unsupervised miles, and better unit economics to strengthen investor conviction; the note flagged FSD attach rate on new deliveries at 55%, roughly double what the firm had modeled.
- Cybercab now features official Starlink dish integration (gold cover, flush mounted); Tesla says riders will be able to stream 4K video, work, or browse during rides. Drone footage showed Cybercab production at Giga Texas slowing, possibly tied to integrating the dishes, with some spotted units lacking steering wheels.
- 42 Cybercabs spotted in Las Vegas, all still with steering wheels; an unconfirmed driver source says they remain in HVAC testing with no deployment timeline.
- Jo Bhakdi (Pioneer Lens) frames human-to-vehicle remote oversight ratio as the key scaling variable: Waymo reportedly near 1-to-40 (unconfirmed), Tesla rumored near 1-to-3 (unconfirmed), with Musk previously citing ~1-to-100 as an inflection point; legal requirements may keep a floor near 1-to-500.
- A researcher using the pseudonym "Gen Mo" rebuts the theory that Musk is delaying robotaxi to enable a cheap SpaceX-Tesla merger, arguing Musk's voting power (~62% even under an extreme 10:1 exchange ratio) makes a low Tesla price unnecessary for control.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Musk told SpaceX employees AI revenue will exceed all other SpaceX revenue combined as early as September and "significantly exceed" it by Q4.
- SpaceX targets 10 gigawatts of AI compute online by end of next year, which Musk estimated at $30–$50 per watt of value, implying $300–$500 billion/year in potential revenue.
- Semianalysis reportedly assessed SpaceX's 2027 6–8 gigawatt (potentially 10+) data center plan as credible, estimating ~$300–500 billion in 2027 capex and projecting ~$500 billion in annual recurring revenue; Musk's only public response was "interesting."
- Jo Bhakdi's team is more skeptical, calling Musk's compute targets consistently exaggerated though "directionally right," and modeling a 2027 bull case of only ~5 gigawatts versus the 8–10 gigawatts attributed to Musk.
- Musk said Grok will be trained on the full corpus of SpaceX data, with Grok 4.6 expected in about a week and 4.7 to follow; he projected AI could represent 99% of SpaceX's value within four to five years.

## Energy
- Tesla filed a Texas tax-incentive application for "Project Crystal Sun," a $10.1 billion vertically integrated solar cell and module factory in Fort Bend County, projecting 9,712 permanent jobs on ~3,050 acres with commercial production starting Q1 2029.
- The $10.116B splits into ~$1.5B real property and ~$8.6B equipment (2026–2028), covering a fully vertically integrated process from raw polysilicon to finished cells/modules — rare for US solar manufacturing.
- Electrek's Fred Lambert cautions the filing is a negotiating tool for tax breaks — Tesla explicitly reserves the right to build elsewhere — and notes Tesla's history of unmet solar promises (SolarCity Buffalo, Solar Roof), while still calling a true vertically integrated US plant significant given Energy is currently Tesla's strongest segment.

## Humanoid Bots
- Drone footage showed a large Optimus factory (stated capacity of 10 million units/year) under construction at Giga Texas alongside Cortex 2, an AI data center for Optimus training reportedly ~40-50% utilized with phase two targeted for full operation this fall.
- Tesla is hiring manufacturing engineers to lead Optimus general assembly production launch; the host cites Lars Moravy confirming Tesla is moving toward Optimus production in Fremont.
- Bhakdi frames Optimus V3 demo delays as a strategic shift to a commercial phase rather than stalling, estimating six to nine months to fuller commercialization; the host expects a longer, multi-year timeline, citing 20,000-30,000 units needed for general-purpose training.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Musk said Starlink now operates in 167 countries with 22 million mobile subscribers and 13 million high-bandwidth subscribers, with nearly 11,000 satellites in orbit — more than double all other operators combined. He expects subscriber growth of more than 10x over time and floated Starlink eventually carrying over 90% of global internet traffic.
- Lufthansa Group will roll out Starlink Wi-Fi across 850+ partner-airline aircraft starting August 19, targeting full group-wide equipping by 2029; Bolivia's president announced plans to install 5,000+ Starlink kits in rural areas for schools.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Musk said Starship should take SpaceX from ~2,500 tons delivered to orbit annually today to over a million tons/year, and possibly close to 10 million tons/year — tonnage he says is needed for sustainable Moon and Mars presence.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- Falcon program is approaching its 700th flight; Dragon has flown 78 crew members to date, with Musk saying the program will surpass 100 astronauts flown "before long."

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- 911 million restricted SpaceX shares became eligible for trading Thursday, versus 580 million previously in the float — nearly tripling available shares. Sources report shareholders are declining to sell below the $135 IPO price despite the stock having traded as high as ~$225-250, and one host models a "selling wall" near $135 that could take ~10 trading days to clear before the stock is freer to move higher.
- In audience Q&A, one host said he does not expect a Tesla-SpaceX merger this year, though any announcement would likely carry a premium.

## Market & Competition
- Texas Governor Abbott paused new data center approvals pending a grid audit, requiring projects to self-fund power/water; hosts argue Tesla's Cortex 2 already meets these criteria (on-site Megapacks, water recycling, self-funded, non-residential site) and was already under construction before the policy.
- Repeated delays to the Tesla Roadster reveal are characterized by Bhakdi as a low-priority "gadget" Tesla is deprioritizing versus AI/robotics initiatives.
