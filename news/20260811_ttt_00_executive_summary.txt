## Brief

## Robotaxi
- Morgan Stanley analyst Andrew Percoco says Tesla must show robotaxi fleet growth, city density, rising unsupervised miles, and better unit economics to strengthen investor conviction; the note flagged FSD attach rate on new deliveries at 55%, roughly double Morgan Stanley's model.
- Cybercab production at Giga Texas reportedly slowed, possibly to integrate Starlink dishes; Tesla officially unveiled a gold-covered, flush-mounted Starlink dish on Cybercab, with Musk saying riders will be able to stream 4K sports/movies or work during rides.
- 42 Cybercabs spotted in Las Vegas, all still with steering wheels and reportedly undergoing HVAC testing with no deployment timeline (unconfirmed, per a driver source).
- A researcher using the pseudonym "Gen Mo" rebutted the theory that Musk is delaying robotaxi to enable a cheap SpaceX-Tesla merger, arguing Musk's existing SpaceX voting power (~62% even under an extreme 10:1 exchange ratio) removes any incentive to suppress Tesla's price; cited operational bottlenecks instead include remote-intervention load, new Cybercab geometry requiring fresh FSD training data, and possible service/cleaning infrastructure gaps.
- Bhakdi (Pioneer Lens) says the human-to-robotaxi remote-oversight ratio is the key scaling variable: Waymo reportedly near 1-to-40 (unconfirmed), Tesla rumored near 1-to-3 (unconfirmed), with Musk referencing ~1-to-100 as an inflection point.

## Humanoid Bots
- Drone footage (Joe Tegtmeyer) shows a large Optimus factory under construction at Giga Texas alongside chip fab foundations and Cybercab infrastructure; Cortex 2, the AI data center supporting Optimus training, is reportedly ~40-50% utilized with phase two targeted for full operation this fall.
- Tesla is hiring manufacturing engineers to lead Optimus general-assembly production launch; Bhakdi frames V3 demo delays as a shift from hype to commercial-readiness phase, estimating six to nine months to fuller commercialization, while the host expects a longer multi-year timeline requiring 20,000-30,000 units for training data.

## Energy
- Tesla filed a Texas JETI tax-incentive application for "Project Crystal Sun," a $10.116B vertically integrated solar cell and module factory in Fort Bend County (3,050 acres), split into $1.5B real property and $8.6B equipment, targeting Q1 2029 production and 9,712 permanent jobs.
- Electrek's Fred Lambert cautions the filing is a negotiating tool — Tesla states it's evaluating other states and the site is less economical without the incentive — and notes Tesla's history of unmet solar promises (SolarCity Buffalo, Solar Roof), while acknowledging Energy is currently Tesla's strongest segment.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Musk told SpaceX employees in a quarterly address that AI revenue will exceed all other SpaceX revenue combined as soon as September and "significantly exceed" it by Q4; he projects AI could be 99% of SpaceX's value within four to five years.
- SpaceX targets 10 gigawatts of AI compute online by end of next year, which Musk said could generate $300-500B/year in revenue at $30-50 per watt of value; Semianalysis reportedly assessed a 6-8GW (potentially 10GW+) 2027 buildout as credible, at ~$50B/gigawatt capex.
- Jo Bhakdi pushed back on Musk's figures, calling him prone to exaggeration though "directionally right," and said his own team's 2027 bull case is ~5GW versus the 8-10GW Musk has cited, citing energy constraints; he also called a $100B run-rate this year unlikely.
- Musk said Grok will be trained on the "sum total" of SpaceX's data, with Grok 4.6 arriving in about a week and 4.7 to follow.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- 911 million restricted SpaceX shares unlocked Thursday versus 580 million previously trading float — nearly 200% more shares than existing float. Shareholders interviewed reportedly said they won't sell below the $135 IPO price despite the stock having traded as high as ~$225-250.
- One host estimates 100-300 million unlocked shares are queued as sell orders near $135, projecting roughly 10 trading days to clear before the stock can move higher; unlocks continue roughly every two weeks through year-end.
- A host said he doesn't expect a Tesla-SpaceX merger this year, though any announcement would likely carry a valuation premium.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- Falcon program approaching its 700th flight; Dragon has flown 78 crew members to date, with Musk saying the program will soon surpass 100 astronauts flown.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Starlink now operates in 167 countries with 22 million mobile subscribers, 13 million high-bandwidth subscribers, and nearly 11,000 satellites in orbit — over twice the combined total of all other operators; Musk expects subscriber growth of 10x-plus over time and floated scaling toward 100,000 satellites with Starlink V3.
- Musk said Starlink could eventually carry over 90% of global internet traffic, citing a claim attributed to Cloudflare that AI traffic could reach ~1,000x human traffic within five years.
- Lufthansa Group will begin rolling out Starlink Wi-Fi next week across 850+ partner-airline aircraft (first flight August 19, full rollout by 2029); Bolivia's president announced plans for 5,000+ Starlink kits for rural schools.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Musk said Starship should take SpaceX from ~2,500 tons delivered to orbit per year today to over a million tons annually, "highly likely" approaching 10 million tons per year — tonnage he says is needed for sustainable Moon and Mars settlements.
