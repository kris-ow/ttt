This short video relays comments from a SpaceX earnings/reporting conference in which Elon Musk laid out aggressive AI compute capacity targets, framing energy, cooling, and buildings — not chip supply — as the current bottleneck for scaling AI infrastructure.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **13-gigawatt compute target set as a "floor":** Musk said he would be surprised if total AI computing power came in below 13 gigawatts by the end of 2027, calling this his minimum expectation. He broke the ramp down as roughly 2.5–3 gigawatts connected by the end of this year, plus another 10 gigawatts added in 2027.
2. **Growth trajectory cited as evidence of pace:** A SpaceX report put compute capacity at 1.7 gigawatts as of September 30 (2026), up from 0.6 gigawatts a year earlier — cited as the base rate the new targets would need to build on.
3. **Bottleneck reframed as energy and infrastructure, not chips:** Musk said the binding constraints on AI scaling right now are energy, cooling, and buildings rather than chip availability, and said he has assigned the SpaceX team responsible for launching roughly 90% of the world's cargo into orbit to work on data center buildout.
4. **Nvidia GB300 chip deployment pace:** The plan is to deploy approximately 200,000 Nvidia GB300 chips per month by the end of this year.
5. **Heavy personal time commitment to Memphis buildout:** Musk said he spends four days a week in Memphis, underscoring the priority placed on the data center/compute expansion effort there.
