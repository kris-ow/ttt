Tesla CEO Elon Musk has claimed the long-delayed next-generation Roadster will be unveiled by the end of April 2026 — nearly nine years after the prototype debuted in November 2017. The article traces the full history of broken timelines and assesses whether this latest promise is credible.

## Electric Vehicles

1. **A decade of delays and shifting timelines:** Originally unveiled in November 2017 with a promised 2020 production start, the Roadster has missed every subsequent deadline. Musk pushed the target to 2022, then 2023, then 2024, then 2025, and at the November 2025 shareholder meeting pushed production to 2027–2028. He initially said an April 1, 2026 "demo" would occur — explicitly noting the date gave him "deniability" — before shifting language in March 2026 to an "unveil" in late April, describing it as "a banger next-level" and noting the production vehicle will be "very different than what we've shown previously."

2. **Original specs and escalating performance claims:** The 2017 prototype promised 0–60 mph in 1.9 seconds, 620-mile range from a 200 kWh battery pack, top speed above 250 mph, and a base price of $200,000. By February 2024, Musk raised the target to 0–60 in under one second. The optional "SpaceX package," first announced in 2018, reportedly includes roughly 10 small cold-air rocket thrusters for cornering, braking, and acceleration enhancement.

3. **Limited but tangible signs of development:** In February 2026, Tesla filed two USPTO trademark applications, including one showing an updated silhouette with a sleeker profile and squarer roofline. Tesla also filed patent applications for an integrated single-piece composite seat. In October 2025, Tesla posted a job listing for a "Manufacturing Engineer, Roadster" focused on battery manufacturing equipment — which simultaneously signaled activity and confirmed the vehicle was still in early manufacturing development.

4. **Reservation holder frustration and unfulfilled commitments:** Deposits of $50,000 (standard) and $250,000 (Founders Series, first 1,000 units) were collected starting in 2017. The issue became public in October 2025 when OpenAI CEO Sam Altman — who placed his reservation in July 2018 — tried to cancel and found Tesla's Roadster contact email had been shut down. Musk claimed a refund was processed within 24 hours, but only after public pressure. Tesla also promised approximately 80 free Roadsters to top referrers and discounted units to hundreds more through its referral program; none of those commitments have been fulfilled.

## Market & Competition

1. **Competitors have moved while Tesla stalled:** Since 2017, Xiaomi's SU7 Ultra and BYD's Yangwang U9 Xtreme have set real-world performance records, and the Rimac Nevera has been in customer hands for years, eroding whatever competitive advantage the Roadster's specs once represented.

## Editorial Stance
The author expresses deep skepticism, stating they have been "fooled 167 times" and that the Roadster has become "a hype machine" rather than a vehicle in development. They note that even if an unveil occurs in April 2026, production remains targeted for 2027–2028, meaning first depositors will have waited a full decade — and express hope, without expectation, that Tesla proves them wrong.
