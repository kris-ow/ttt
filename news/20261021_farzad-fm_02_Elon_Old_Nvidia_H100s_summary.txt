This short clip covers comments Elon Musk made during an earnings call about the economics of AI computing hardware, specifically arguing that older Nvidia H100 chips are appreciating rather than depreciating in rental value, and that SpaceX is structuring its compute-leasing business to capture this trend.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Old H100s now rent for more than when new:** Musk stated that renting older Nvidia H100 chips now costs more than renting them did when they were new, which he says runs counter to how equipment like used cars normally depreciates.
2. **Musk's thesis — intelligence per watt rises over time:** Musk's explanation is that as AI models improve, the same chip consuming the same power produces more useful intelligence over time, and since pricing is driven by the intelligence delivered rather than the equipment itself, the same watt of compute becomes more valuable rather than less as models advance.
3. **SpaceX uses short-term, spot-priced compute leases:** Per Musk, SpaceX leases its computing power on short-term contracts at spot prices rather than long-term agreements, and these contracts have been renewed at a higher price per watt, not a lower one.
4. **Hyperscaler demand exceeds supply:** Musk said hyperscalers continue to ask SpaceX for more compute than it can provide, and that SpaceX could rent out its computing power multiple times over if it struck deals with every interested party. He said he believes this compute deficit will persist for a long time.
