## Autonomous Driving
- **Tesla homepage FSD redesign:** Tesla relaunched its website homepage to prominently display the live cumulative FSD (Supervised) mile counter alongside owner-recorded videos. The post drew 4,300 likes — the day's highest engagement — signaling a deliberate push to use real-world data as a credibility and marketing tool.
- **Danish regulator refutes Reuters criticism:** The Danish Road Traffic Authority issued an official statement saying it has "at no time expressed either positive or negative opinions about the approval of Tesla's FSD," directly contradicting a recent Reuters report. The clarification removes a potential regulatory headwind for FSD's European narrative.
- **AI 5 chip specs and roadmap:** Per brighterwithherbert, Tesla's AI 5 delivers 2,500 TOPS and 144 GB memory, representing up to a 40x system-level improvement over Hardware 4. Elon's stated roadmap: AI 4 gets safety far beyond human levels; AI 5 makes cars "almost perfect"; AI 6 targets Optimus and data centers; AI 7 (Dojo 3) is space-based.

## Humanoid Bots
- **Optimus revenue framing:** Elon's stated long-term target — 1 billion units/year at $30,000/unit — implies $30 trillion in annual revenue. Per brighterwithherbert, even 1% of that figure ($300 billion) would exceed Tesla's entire current revenue base. Elon called Optimus the "infinite money glitch," describing three compounding exponentials: production capacity, capability, and cost reduction.
- **Model S/X factory conversion:** Tesla has reportedly discontinued the Model S and Model X and is converting that factory space to an Optimus production line — a strategic reallocation of manufacturing capacity, not an incremental addition.
- **Etherloop architecture extends to Optimus:** Per cernbasher's Phil, the Ethernet-based time-division multiplexing developed for the Cybercab will apply directly to Optimus limb control (e.g., forearm controller articulating hand movements), while inter-bot communication will run via cloud orchestration over Wi-Fi, 5G, and Starlink.
- **Optimus supply chain is structurally clean:** Virtually every Optimus component is new — suppliers cannot pull from existing inventory. Harder to scale initially, but Tesla carries zero legacy architecture debt, a structural advantage over any competitor building from existing industrial supply chains.

## Electric Vehicles
- **New blue paint options for Model 3 and Model Y:** Tesla launched Marine Blue (available on Premium trims, $1,000) and Frost Blue (Performance trims) in the U.S., replacing the discontinued Deep Blue Metallic. The announcement drew 4,300 likes and 307 reposts.
- **Model S/X Signature delivery event — May 12:** Tesla confirmed production of the Signature edition is underway, with the delivery event set for Tuesday, May 12, 2026.
- **Cybertruck PCS2 board failure — recall pressure building:** The PCS2 board — managing AC charging, Supercharging, and PowerShare — is failing across a wide range of Cybertruck VINs, with failures documented up to approximately VIN 77,000 and concentrated between VINs 25,000–35,000. When one of two redundant AC-side components fails, charge rate drops from 48A to 24A with no driver-facing alert. Out-of-warranty repairs initially ran $4,000–$7,000; Tesla has recently offered a goodwill rate of approximately $1,000. Parts are backordered 4–8 weeks; Tesla is providing free Supercharging to verified affected owners while they wait. Per Craig (Red Review via futureaza): "I think we can all agree at this point there's so many vehicles affected that there needs to be a recall on this part." The 2026 Cybertruck carries a new 7-year/70,000-mile ZEV powertrain warranty; 2024–2025 trucks are covered only under the standard 4-year/50,000-mile warranty, which many high-mileage owners have already exceeded.
- **Honda delays five models, cites $15.7B EV losses:** Honda is pushing several redesigns — including the Odyssey, Accord, and HR-V — to 2030 or later, reinforcing the structural gap between legacy OEMs and Tesla in the EV transition.
- **Jay Leno praises Tesla Semi:** Leno cited the Semi's acceleration, 500-mile range, and 30-minute 60% recharge as impressive — modest mainstream credibility signal for the Semi program.

## Financials
- **Terafab: $25B captive chip fab announced March 21:** Tesla's vertically integrated chip fabrication facility will cover logic, memory, and advanced packaging. The AI 5 chip is first off the line. Elon stated Tesla plans to build more AI chips than every other company combined — more than Nvidia, Google TPUs, AMD, and all hyperscaler custom silicon — which would represent the largest captive chip supply in the world.

## Market & Competition
- **XAI dissolved; SpaceX AI born:** Elon Musk announced XAI is being folded into SpaceX as a product group called "SpaceX AI." Colossus 1 (220,000 Nvidia GPUs, 300+ MW) is now leased to Anthropic for inference, while Colossus 2 remains dedicated to Grok training. Brian Wang's estimated compute rental revenue: ~$16.8B/year; Larry Goldberg (lumacenti.com) estimates the combined SpaceX entity could show ~$20B in profit in its first year.
- **Anthropic and OpenAI open PE distribution channels:** Anthropic announced a joint venture with Blackstone, Helman & Friedman, and Goldman Sachs (valued at $1.5B; $300M each from the three anchor partners). Hours later, Bloomberg reported OpenAI is raising $4B for a new "Development Company" venture at a $10B valuation, with TPG, Brookfield, and Bain Capital named. Per Bhakdi (jobhakdi), the structural logic is that private equity owns the mid-market companies that are the primary untapped deployment channel for enterprise AI — making these deals a distribution breakthrough rather than a capital event.
- **Hyperscaler AI CapEx: ~$700B in 2026:** Microsoft and Google at $190B each, Amazon at $200B, Meta at $145B — up ~70% year-over-year. Global AI data center CapEx is estimated at $400–$450B in 2026, with projections reaching $1 trillion/year by 2028. Per brighterwithherbert/cernbasher, ~50% of data center projects are stalled due to inability to secure energy or permitting.
- **Grok launches on Apple CarPlay:** A distribution milestone for xAI; no Tesla-specific integration details provided.
- **SpaceX–Tesla merger: speculation only:** Sawyer Merritt flagged merger talk as "purely speculation" with no confirmed timeline, noting any deal would require more than 50% of outstanding Tesla shares to vote yes. Multiple sources debated the thesis but presented no new confirming facts.
