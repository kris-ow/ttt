## Key Posts / Themes

1. **FSD V14.3.8 and 2026 Summer Update roll out:** Tesla has begun rolling out FSD V14.3.8 alongside its broader 2026 Summer Update, confirmed directly by the poster downloading it to his Model Y. The update bundles several UI/UX features: a new Grok voice-command system that can execute multiple chained commands at once (e.g., folding mirrors, adjusting wiper speed, setting cabin temperature, opening the self-driving app, opening the glovebox), ambient lights that pulse when Grok speaks, phone call functionality, seat heating/cooling controls, control-panel search, and a new self-driving camera "zoom preference" customization option. This is a meaningful software/UX release demonstrating continued iteration on in-car AI assistant capability, though no safety or disengagement data was shared.

2. **Robotaxi: extended hours and fleet claims:** Tesla announced new Robotaxi operating hours and said its service now has "upgraded intelligence" aimed at shorter wait times. Tesla also stated its Unsupervised (driverless) fleet is "much bigger" than before — a claim from Tesla itself, but no specific new fleet count was given, so it should be read as directional/unconfirmed commentary rather than a hard number. This comes as the canonical RobotaxiTracker.com Unsupervised fleet figure (48, as of 2026-07-30) is stale by ~28 days, so an update — if substantiated — would be notable, but the post gives no digit to update the tracker with.

3. **Tesla China denies Shanghai FSD data center rumors:** Tesla's China team publicly denied rumors that its Shanghai FSD data center had been vacated, stating the facility is operating normally and that it has reported the rumors to police. The team also said recruitment for driver-assistance-related roles is accelerating. This directly rebuts a bearish rumor about Tesla's China autonomy operations, though it's a company statement rather than independently verified.

4. **SpaceX Starlink referral program switches to cash payouts:** SpaceX now offers a $100 cash payout (paid directly to a linked bank account) per Starlink referral, as an alternative to the previous service-credit model, in markets where cash payout is available. This is a incremental change to Starlink's subscriber-growth/referral economics rather than a disclosure of subscriber numbers or revenue.

5. **Starbase police Cybertrucks and other minor items:** Starbase, which officially became an incorporated city in May of the prior year, unveiled new police Cybertrucks as it builds out its police department (six officers, two sergeants solicited this month, reporting to Chief Glenn Bennett, who accepted the role in June). Separately, Tesla is reportedly seeking a Norway location for a new Semi Megacharger station per a regulatory filing, and Tesla's shop added new pet-related merchandise (dog mode chew toy, Cybertruck pet liner, etc.) — low-signal for investors.
