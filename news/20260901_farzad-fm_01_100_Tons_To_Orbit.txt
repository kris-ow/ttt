times or a thousand times, its build
cost gets divided across all of those
missions. And the same factory output
also creates much more launch capacity
because each ship keeps working after
its first flight. So you're not
constantly replacing prior rockets. This
leads to by far the most important
number in space flight, tons of orbit
per year. SpaceX says Starship V3 is
designed to carry 100 metric tons to
orbit while fully reusable. The company
says a later V4 version of the rocket
could carry 200 tons, but a 100 ton ship
or a 200 ton ship flying one time is
still just one time. But when you have a
fleet of reusable rockets that you can
fly again and again becomes millions of
tons over time with the same rockets.
