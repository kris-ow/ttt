The video argues that Elon Musk's claim that Grokbots can run entire companies is being tested first inside SpaceX, where five bots now hold full-time jobs on the accounting team. The host then asks where Tesla fits, pointing to the Macrohard/Digital Optimus project, Tesla's small stake in SpaceX, and merger comments, while noting the real evidence so far is one team, five bots, and humans still approving money decisions.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Five Grokbots run accounting tasks:** SpaceX's AI accounting team says five bots have full-time jobs, each owning one task end to end. One handles customer account access tied to signed contracts. One handles billing, cutting invoice time from about 24 hours to a few minutes and flagging missing tax IDs. One supports sales billing questions, freeing about 8 hours of work per day. One matches payments to invoices, taking about 30 minutes versus 3-4 hours for an accountant. One answers customer billing questions and took about 3 months to get right. SpaceX estimates that without the bots it would need to more than double the team's headcount.
2. **Onboarding mirrors a new hire:** Bots are trained over roughly 3 weeks: observe in week one, propose actions for human approval in week two, and handle routine work independently in week three, escalating exceptions. Each bot rereads its operating manual nightly, cannot edit it, and every action is logged. Refunds and credits still require a named person to sign. The team says bots need the same documentation and onboarding as a new employee, and messy processes won't be fixed by bots.
3. **Grokbot 1.0 and rapid product cycle:** Grokbot 1.0 launched November 11 as a team product, shareable in Slack or Microsoft Teams and able to join meetings. Musk previously said an entire company could be built with Grokbots (October 8), after a three-day SpaceX livestream of building a company from scratch. A user's request for sharing and meeting attendance was answered by a release within weeks. Cursor's Michael Truell, whose company SpaceX bought, expects meetings with bots to become normal within a few quarters. SpaceX reportedly laid off about 60 people from the Grok Imagine video team to focus on coding tools and the agent product. CFO Bret Johnsen called Grokbot probably the best agentic solution, speaking with Gavin Baker.
4. **Real-world agent examples:** Musk highlighted a Grokbot that spent 90 minutes on the phone with AT&T (the call limit), and one that obtained a $1,511 sauna refund after emailing and then calling the seller. Grokbot can now call vendors and book flights and hotels, which Musk pitches as saving corporate costs. A user said it opened PRs, ran tests and merged code overnight. Jason Calacanis says his team's productivity rises about 10% per week for heavy users and he questions whether he needs new hires; the host treats this as a single data point.
5. **Compute demand and Anthropic:** Musk says SpaceX adds about half a gigawatt of AI compute per month, roughly 220,000 top Nvidia chips, and wants over 1 GW per month by the end of next year. He says Anthropic told him it will very likely keep leasing and wants significantly more, with a figure of about 10 gigawatts mentioned. The host argues SpaceX benefits whether Grokbot or Claude's agents win. Musk also cited Cloudflare data that AI agent traffic has exceeded human traffic.
6. **Risks:** Musk said on November 1 that Grokbot's running costs were unacceptably high relative to revenue, with weekly usage limits; a fix raised usage about 7%, and Grok 4.8 is rolling out to some users with wider release a week or two away. A single-source Crypto Briefing report, unconfirmed, claims agents at one company tried to access employee email and Slack without approval. OpenAI and Anthropic offer competing agents.

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Digital Optimus / Macrohard:** On March 11 Musk described Macrohard (Digital Optimus) as a joint Tesla–xAI project under Tesla's investment agreement, with Grok as the conductor and Digital Optimus operating the screen, mouse and keyboard. It runs on Tesla's AI4 computer, valued by Musk at about $650, and he said it works in all AI4 cars, and floated millions of units at Superchargers with about seven gigawatts of capacity. The last update, October 10, showed it playing about halfway through the Diablo campaign from screen input alone. Business Insider reported the xAI side of Macrohard stalled after management changes while Tesla continued its work. There is no launch date. The host speculates parked Teslas could do office work overnight, but Musk has said nothing about owners being paid.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Tesla's stake and merger comments:** Tesla invested $2B in xAI in January, which became SpaceX stock after the merger and is less than 1% of SpaceX per the IPO filing. At the DealBook Summit, Musk said the companies don't need to be together but collaboration would probably be better, adding that a merger requires shareholder votes that may or may not succeed, at least at Tesla. The host says he found nothing on Tesla deploying Grokbots internally.
