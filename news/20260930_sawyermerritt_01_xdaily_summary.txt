## Executive Summary
A dense news day: Tesla posted a large $30B financing package and an SEC-cleared retail shareholder voting program, alongside FSD mileage/regulatory milestones and a notable Semi truck fleet order. On the SpaceX side, Elon Musk gave fresh public remarks (alongside Jensen Huang and Gavin Baker at a White House event) on Starship's V3 Starlink deployment and 2026 cadence targets, plus a Musk/Trump "superintelligence safety" declaration — while AT&T's CEO publicly dismissed SpaceX's direct-to-cell phone strategy as not "viable."

## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla secures $30B financing package:** Per a new SEC filing, Tesla has secured a $30 billion financing package expanding its borrowing capacity: a $20B delayed-draw term loan facility, an $8B five-year revolving credit facility, and a $2B 364-day revolving credit facility. This significantly boosts Tesla's available liquidity/borrowing capacity, a meaningful financials signal for investors tracking capex and cash flow flexibility.

2. **SEC clears retail shareholder voting program:** The SEC has cleared Tesla to launch a voluntary retail shareholder voting program letting retail shareholders set a default/standing voting preference for future shareholder meetings, intended to make it easier for retail holders to participate in votes going forward.

3. **FSD mileage and European regulatory expansion:** Tesla's global fleet has crossed 15 billion miles driven on FSD (Supervised), up from 14 billion on August 27th — roughly 1 billion miles in about a month. Separately, FSD (Supervised) has been approved in Croatia, the 8th European country to approve it, joining Belgium, Netherlands, Denmark, Lithuania, Estonia, Slovenia, and Czechia.

4. **Tesla Semi fleet order:** Henry Malukas, co-founder of HMD Trucking (which operates ~700 diesel semi trucks), says the company has ordered 50 Tesla Semi trucks, citing efficiency, low maintenance, and fuel cost benefits. This is a customer-level commercial adoption data point for Tesla's Semi program.

5. **Q3 delivery/energy consensus and Supercharger milestone:** Tesla released its company-compiled Q3 analyst consensus: analysts expect 462,000 vehicle deliveries and 15.9 GWh of energy storage deployment for Q3 2026. Separately, Tesla installed its 3,000th Supercharger in Canada, described as having a special design.

6. **Musk, Huang, and Baker on Starship, AI, and superintelligence at White House event:** In a livestreamed appearance with President Trump, Elon Musk said he and (per the "joint declaration") others agreed to "superintelligence safety" measures including joint monitoring, board special committees, and mutual oversight ("grading each other's homework"). In a joint interview with Jensen Huang and Gavin Baker, Musk discussed power needs, superintelligence, and Starship; on the prior day's Starship launch, Musk said SpaceX "deployed V3 Starlink satellites" with a wingspan "of a 737" and stated SpaceX expects Starship to reach a weekly or twice-weekly launch cadence next year (2027, per date context). Musk also commented on AI helping average Americans, citing anecdotes of AI assisting with medical issues, including cases where a doctor was reportedly wrong.

7. **AT&T CEO disputes SpaceX's direct-to-cell strategy:** AT&T's CEO publicly said SpaceX's phone/satellite strategy is not "viable" and framed AT&T as the real disruptor, arguing satellite technology "at its best" over the next decade still won't beat fiber — a competitive pushback on SpaceX's Starlink direct-to-cell ambitions from a major telecom rival.

8. **OpenAI valuation raise (context item):** OpenAI is reportedly looking to raise $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation, up from a previous $852 billion valuation in March — included as AI-industry context relevant to the broader AI compute/valuation landscape SpaceX and Tesla operate in.
