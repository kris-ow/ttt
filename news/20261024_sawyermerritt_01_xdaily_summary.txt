## Key Posts / Themes

1. **NHTSA proposes removing regulatory barriers for pedal/wheel-free autonomous vehicles:** NHTSA announced proposed rule changes aimed at eliminating federal barriers for autonomous vehicles without steering wheels or pedals, explicitly citing Tesla's Cybercab as an example. Cited changes include eliminating requirements for manually operated turn signals (list was cut off in the post). This is a potentially significant regulatory tailwind for Tesla's driverless Cybercab deployment plans, though the rule is still in proposal stage, not finalized.

2. **Cybercab registrations and Austin Robotaxi expansion:** Per Texas DMV data, Tesla registered 69 more Cybercabs, bringing the cumulative total to 420 registered to date. Separately, Tesla expanded its Austin Robotaxi service area to cover the area around the city's F1 racetrack ahead of a race weekend expected to draw 400,000 attendees, and extended nightly service hours from 12 AM to 1 AM. These are incremental operational expansions rather than fleet-size changes to the broader canonical Robotaxi tracker figures.

3. **SpaceX/NASA Artemis and Starship lander milestones:** NASA published a new SpaceX development milestones timeline for upcoming Artemis missions: propellant transfer demo and "Cabin 1" operations testing in Q1 2027; landing leg demo, Cabin 1 airlock demo, and barge transport to Cape Canaveral in Q2 2027; and the Artemis III mission plus an uncrewed demonstration tanker flight in Q3 2027. NASA and SpaceX also released new images of "Cabin 1" — built at Starfactory in Starbase, Texas — intended for future astronaut missions, along with renderings of the Artemis III docking adapter, and a new Starship lander concept of operations for Artemis mission 4. These are concrete, NASA-sourced program milestones rather than speculative dates.

4. **Major carriers lobby FCC against Starlink's direct-to-cell network:** AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile reportedly met with the FCC on October 20th and are jointly lobbying to preserve existing rules as SpaceX moves closer to launching an independent cellular network (Starlink Mobile / direct-to-cell) that would compete directly with the three carriers. The poster frames this as the carriers reversing an earlier dismissive stance on Starlink Mobile's competitive threat. This is a regulatory risk factor to watch for Starlink's terrestrial cellular ambitions, though the outcome of the lobbying effort is unknown.

5. **Tesla Roadster visibility and financing changes:** Tesla released a new video showing the next-gen Roadster's user interface, and the vehicle will be on public display for the first time (four days starting Oct 24) at the Peterson Automotive Museum in LA. Separately, Tesla raised financing APR rates on most Model Y trims in the U.S. (e.g., base and Premium trims moving from 1.99% to 2.49%; Performance unchanged at 3.99%), a minor demand/financing signal worth monitoring alongside Tesla's stated Q3 2026 inventory position (referenced but not detailed in the post).
