Electrek's Tesla coverage today centers on the unraveling explanation for killing the Cybertruck, a new Robotaxi city launch with questionable real coverage, and Musk's own admission that Tesla lacks the talent bench to run multiple vehicle programs at once.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Musk's shifting Cybertruck story contradicted by Tesla's own numbers:** Musk claimed on X that Cybertruck "order rate far exceeds" production and that the line is being cut not for lack of profitability but because Cybercab is a better use of resources, per Fred Lambert's reporting. The author counters with Tesla's own data: the Cybertruck line has run at roughly a quarter of its installed capacity (Tesla's shareholder letter lists over 125,000 units of installed capacity; Q3 2026 "Other Models" deliveries of 8,295 imply a roughly 30,000-unit annual pace, down 48% year-over-year), Tesla discounted and re-trimmed the truck all year, pulled a single-motor trim after 173 sales, and Cox Automotive estimates show US sales falling from 14,416 in Q3 2024 to 3,519 in Q1 2026 and 3,744 in Q2 2026. The same day Tesla announced ending production it raised Cybertruck prices up to 10% (cheapest trim from $69,990 in August to $92,490) and paused orders for the cheapest trim. Lambert argues this is Musk spinning a last-call sales rush as proof of demand that never existed, and notes SpaceX itself bought 1,279 Cybertrucks in Q4 2025 (about 19% of US registrations that quarter across Musk's companies). [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/musk-cybertruck-demand-claim-data/)

2. **Musk says talent shortage, not space, is forcing the Cybertruck-to-Cybercab tradeoff:** Musk told X that Tesla has only "a very limited number of smart, dedicated people" and can't maintain Cybertruck's production line, supply chain and engineering while scaling Cybercab, per Fred Lambert's reporting — a third explanation in four days after citing "space" and then "opportunity cost of complexity." Lambert documents that 7 of the 14 executives Tesla paraded at its March 2023 Investor Day as "bench strength" have since left, including the former CFO, VPs of powertrain, software and hardware engineering, and the heads of Supercharging and energy/charging. Cybercab's own program leadership has also repeatedly turned over, with four named departures since February 2026, one to Rivian's R2 program. The author's view: Tesla has never sustained multiple vehicle programs well, but now "the slack is gone," and Musk is "openly telling us" the real constraint is people, not factories — calling it "focus" when it looks more like "a constraint Tesla can't hire its way out of." [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/tesla-deep-bench-gone-musk-admits-talent-shortage/)

## Robotaxi
3. **Tesla launches Robotaxi in San Antonio with a geofence Electrek says likely overstates real coverage:** Tesla announced driverless Robotaxi service in San Antonio starting at 5am the next day, with an advertised launch geofence Robotaxi Tracker measures at about 274 square miles, covering roughly 1.0 million residents and 721,000 jobs — more than four times Waymo's roughly 59-square-mile San Antonio zone (Waymo opened to all riders, airport included, in October; Tesla's map excludes the airport). As of the article, Robotaxi Tracker hadn't logged a single Tesla vehicle operating in the city. Citing independent checks by Koby Pierce, who tested real addresses in the Tesla app, Fred Lambert reports that Tesla's advertised geofences in six other cities only actually accept rides across 59%–78% of the mapped area (Austin 59%, Miami 61%, Orlando 70%, Fort Worth 72%, Houston 74%, Dallas 78%). Tesla also expanded Austin's zone to 443 square miles, per Robotaxi Tracker, up 178 square miles since mid-September. Lambert's take: San Antonio's real usable area is likely closer to 160–210 square miles, and "square miles" is a metric Tesla fully controls and can inflate cheaply, while Waymo still runs over 4,000 driverless vehicles nationally versus Tesla's 666 per Robotaxi Tracker; he calls robotaxi still "a small pilot" bottlenecked by safety. [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/09/tesla-robotaxi-san-antonio-launch-geofence/)
