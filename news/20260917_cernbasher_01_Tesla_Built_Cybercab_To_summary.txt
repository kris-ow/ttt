## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab lifetime-mileage economics modeled:** The host's article models cost to deliver a billion miles of fleet capacity: at a $30,000 vehicle cost and 300,000-mile lifetime, ~$100M in production spend is needed (implying ~3,333 vehicles); spending an extra $3,000/vehicle to extend life to 500,000 miles cuts that to $66M (~2,000 vehicles); reaching a 1-million-mile lifetime halves it again to $33M (~1,000 vehicles). Per-mile production cost falls from ~10 cents (300k-mile life) to 6.6 cents (500k) to 3.3 cents (1M).
2. **Fleet-wide cost breakdown by utilization:** For a 300,000-mile vehicle, modeled total cost per mile ranges ~45–55 cents depending on utilization, with vehicle cost the largest component (9–10 cents); at 500,000 miles, total cost falls to roughly 30–40 cents; at 1 million miles, non-vehicle costs (cleaning, parking, insurance, tires/maintenance) can exceed the vehicle-cost component itself.
3. **10,000 vehicles enough for a billion miles:** At 100,000 miles/vehicle/year, only about 10,000 Cybercabs would be needed to generate a billion miles annually — illustrating how fast fleet mileage scales even at seemingly modest fleet sizes.
4. **Dynamic pricing vs. Uber surge pricing:** The hosts argue Tesla's control over fleet supply and positioning (via rapid manufacturing and dispatch) avoids Uber's surge-pricing gaming problem (drivers staying out of high-demand zones to inflate prices), and could enable both surge and discounted "dynamic" pricing — e.g., steep discounts for rides that reposition empty vehicles toward anticipated demand (concerts, games).
5. **Palo Alto sighting — large Cybercab depot:** Near Tesla's Palo Alto engineering campus (Page Mill Road), the host counted roughly six Cybercabs driving with safety monitors plus an estimated ~100 more parked at a depot — some production-ready with no driving controls, others with temporary controls and Florida/Texas plates (suggesting return from field testing rather than imminent shipment) — alongside ~25–30 Model Ys labeled robotaxi, read as an active dispatch/engineering depot rather than a sign of imminent broader Cybercab deployment.
6. **Robotaxi fleet counts (canonical):** Active Robotaxi fleet stands at 839 vehicles (Bay Area 657, Austin 135, Dallas 22, Houston 25) and unsupervised fleet at 48 (Austin 32, Dallas 6, Houston 6, Miami 1, Tampa 3), per RobotaxiTracker.com — both figures flagged stale/unchanged for over a month.

## SpaceX — Starship
1. **Starship Flight 14 scheduled for September 22 at the earliest:** It will be the first Starship flight to reach full Earth orbit (prior flights used intentionally passive suborbital trajectories for public safety); planned as a roughly 10-hour, six-orbit flight splashing down in the Pacific west of Chile.
2. **Starlink V3 deployment and first tile reuse planned:** Flight 14 will deploy 26 Starlink V3 satellites (only launchable via Starship, not Falcon 9), providing about 1 terabit/second of added constellation capacity — roughly 10x the capacity of a single Falcon 9 V2-mini Starlink launch. The flight will also reuse two heat-shield tiles recovered from a prior ship, marking the first tile reuse for Starship; per Elon Musk, a successful Flight 14 would set up an attempt to catch the ship on Flight 15.
3. **Falcon program wind-down flagged:** The host says Elon Musk has indicated Falcon launches will be phased out as Starship/V3 Starlink capability comes online, since V3 satellites can only fly on Starship.
