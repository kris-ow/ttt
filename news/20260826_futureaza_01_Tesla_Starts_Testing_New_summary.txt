## Electric Vehicles
1. **Tesla Semi validation testing observed at the factory:** Footage showed roughly nine new Semi trucks in the outbound lot, some visibly dusty from being driven on-site. One truck was filmed doing turning-radius circles on a test track, then reversing to hook up to a 53-foot trailer to test the fifth-wheel coupling, cable connections, and hitch mechanism. Per the footage's source, this validation sequence is performed on every newly built truck leaving the factory.
2. **Speed and road-surface testing:** The same truck was then driven "bobtail" (without a trailer) past posted 30 mph and 60 mph speed signs and a stop sign, then over a series of road-surface bumps of varying sizes (described as NVH — noise, vibration, harshness — testing) to assess in-cabin noise and vibration against spec. After completing the validation run, trucks were observed returning into the factory building, for reasons the source could not determine (possibly data hookup or further inspection).
3. **A previously unidentified white A-frame fixture, spotted in earlier factory footage, was identified by a viewer as a calibrated radar/ADAS sensor-calibration target arch used alongside weighted target sleds for forward-collision and obstacle-detection testing** — resolving a mystery from the prior week's episode.

The purpose of a cleared area near existing parking (extra parking vs. some other use) remains unconfirmed speculation.
