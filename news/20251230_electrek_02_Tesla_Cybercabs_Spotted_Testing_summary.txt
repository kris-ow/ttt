Electrek reports a new sighting of two Tesla Cybercab prototypes testing on public roads in Austin, Texas, equipped with steering wheels — reigniting debate over whether Tesla can deliver the vehicle without manual controls as originally promised.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab prototypes seen with steering wheels in Austin:** A Reddit user posted images of two Cybercab prototypes driving together on South Lamar in Austin, both fitted with steering wheels. The article notes this is a repeat of an earlier sighting at Giga Texas and notes that steering wheels on testing prototypes are standard practice for engineering/safety-driver purposes, even on vehicles designed to ultimately ship without them.
2. **Tension with Musk's "no steering wheel" design pledge:** At the Cybercab unveiling, Elon Musk said the vehicle would have "No mirrors, no pedals, no steering wheel." The article contrasts this with Tesla Chairwoman Robyn Denholm's previously reported comments acknowledging Tesla might add a steering wheel to the Cybercab if needed — which the article calls a rare break from Musk's rhetoric.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Current FSD state doesn't support a wheel-less launch, author argues:** The article states Tesla has not yet solved unsupervised autonomy; consumer "Supervised FSD" still requires constant driver attention. It also references a prior report that Tesla delayed its next-gen AI5 chip to mid-2027, meaning Cybercab would likely launch on current AI4 hardware — hardware the article argues hasn't achieved unsupervised autonomy in millions of Model 3/Y vehicles already on the road.

## Editorial Stance
Author Fred Lambert states his view that Tesla will likely be forced to launch the Cybercab with a steering wheel because unsupervised self-driving won't be ready for commercial launch at scale in 2026; he argues regulations cap volume for vehicles without manual controls (citing a 2,500-unit exemption), and predicts Tesla will sell a cheaper Cybercab model with a wheel and pedals that can become a robotaxi later once software matures, similar to how it marketed other Tesla models.
