## Brief

- Zero-intervention coast-to-coast FSD run now "breezing by," per Elluswamy
- Record 245 Cybercabs spotted at Giga Texas outbound lot
- Tesla general counsel confirms SpaceX investment/framework deal on earnings call
- TSLA slides toward $300s amid broad AI-stock selloff
- Musk: Semi self-driving targeted end of 2026/early 2027
- SpaceX's first-ever earnings call set for August 4

## Robotaxi
- A record 245 Cybercabs were spotted in Giga Texas's outbound lot per Sawyer Merritt, none with visible decals and some possibly retaining steering wheels — read by commentators as normal test-vehicle buildout ahead of geographic rollout, not a red flag.
- Fleet sightings expanded beyond Austin: Houston visible cars grew to roughly 13 within an hour, with 130+ Cybercabs reportedly spotted at a Houston Tesla facility lot; new ride reports emerged in Tampa, and Cybercab/Model Y sightings with robotaxi hardware appeared in Charlotte, NC and cold-weather testing in New Zealand.
- Driverless Cybercabs were spotted in suburbs near Austin and on public roads outside Giga Texas, characterized by commentators as validation-stage testing preceding wider rollout.
- Tesla reported zero critical incidents over 380,000 unsupervised Robotaxi miles on the earnings call, though one commentator (Jeff Le) cautioned the sample is too small for a meaningful safety rate.
- Uber and Waymo are ending their multi-year exclusivity arrangement in Atlanta and Austin, per futureaza.
- Commentators continue to flag that Tesla has not given objective criteria for a wider Robotaxi rollout beyond vague metrics like "10% weekly compounding miles."

## Autonomous Driving
- A team led by David Moss completed a fully autonomous, zero-disengagement cross-country Cannonball run of ~2,800 miles in 47 hours 15 minutes, beating the prior record by ~2 hours 40 minutes; separately a coast-to-coast zero-intervention drive on FSD 14.3.6 pushed the car's total to 20,000 FSD miles.
- Musk said on the July 22 Q2 earnings call that Tesla expects self-driving to work on the Tesla Semi "probably around the end of this year or early next year," while noting Semi autonomy work is taking a backseat to Model 3/Y/Cybercab for the next six months; Electrek notes Tesla's roughly 10 prior self-driving deadlines since 2013 have all been missed.
- FSD version 15 has begun testing in Austin Robotaxi operations, with roughly three of seven planned new features integrated so far, per Elluswamy/host estimate.
- Tesla confirmed FSD is moving toward a personalized model that remembers driver habits and specific interventions.
- FSD 14 Light restarted for Hardware 3 vehicles with brake confirmation, destination parking changes, and other UI updates.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- On the Q2 earnings call, Musk did not deny Tesla-SpaceX merger synergies and described overlaps (Grok in cars, Starlink, Terafab); Tesla's general counsel then confirmed the companies deepened ties earlier this year via an investment and framework agreement covering Terafab and Digital Optimus.
- Merger timing remains pure speculation and sources disagree sharply: one host predicts announcement between Q3 earnings and year-end with consummation by mid-2027; another argues no announcement before 2027 given SpaceX shares are still in price discovery; a third sees the general counsel's appearance as decisive evidence a merger is imminent. None of this is confirmed by either company.
- SpaceX's first-ever earnings call is set for August 4.
- SpaceX shares are down roughly 34–35% over the past month and ~49% from peak, which Musk reportedly attributed to prioritizing Moon/Mars funding over near-term profitability.

## Financials
- TSLA continued declining toward the $300–313 range post-earnings, down from mid-$400s days prior; commentators attribute this to a lack of objective Robotaxi rollout criteria on the call, a broader AI-stock drawdown (Micron, Palantir, Marvell, Astera Labs all down 26–42% from highs), and rising perceived odds (30%, up from zero) of a Fed rate hike.
- FSD attach rate was cited at 55% in countries where FSD is available, per the earnings call.

## Market & Competition
- A Chinese memory maker's (CXMT) IPO surged ~465% and triggered a broad AI-chip selloff (Nvidia -5%, AMD -7%, Micron -5%+ intraday); Jo Bhakdi called the panic overblown, arguing CXMT lacks HBM-grade yield/technology to threaten Micron/SK Hynix for 18–24+ months.
- BYD sales growth turned positive (~5% YoY in June) after eight months of declines, and the company is hiring heavily on demand recovery.
- GM is pursuing a ~$1 billion Army contract for ~10,000 Colorado tactical vehicles, beating Oshkosh and Polaris as the only other bidders.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla's 2026 Summer Update is rolling out with route-memory navigation, deeper Grok voice integration (glove box, climate control), self-driving stats sharing, and other minor features.
- Tesla launched a non-motorized kids' balance bike (ages 2–5) restricted to existing owners; one resold on eBay at a premium.
- Tesla officially launched in Uruguay.
- Cybertruck sales weakness persists in commentary (Bloomberg comparison to the Ford Edsel), while Tesla rose to #9 in a Consumer Reports reliability ranking.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Musk said Starlink connectivity will be built into Cybercab and eventually all Teslas where available, citing cellular dead zones and enabling in-vehicle 4K streaming.
- Starship's V3 test flight showed strong camera/Starlink performance, per Sawyer Merritt, with a record 50 onboard cameras.

## Humanoid Bots
- Musk described "Digital Optimus" as AI that reads and reacts to computer screens like a human, orchestrated by Grok — distinct from prior demo-replay tools, per commentary on the earnings call.
- Commentators note Optimus's core bottleneck remains generalized task performance without human tele-operation, not manufacturing.
