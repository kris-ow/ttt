## Market & Competition
1. **Ford rehires engineers after AI-driven quality failures:** Ford reportedly laid off experienced engineers and replaced quality-control and warranty-system functions with AI, then had to rehire roughly 350 former engineers (per the hosts) after the automated vision system used for factory quality control failed to work as intended; a Ford VP of vehicle hardware engineering is quoted (via an article the hosts read) saying the company mistakenly believed introducing AI alone would produce a high-quality product, and that departing veteran staff took undocumented institutional knowledge with them.
