This live stream from Jo Bhakdi covers the breaking news of a strategic compute partnership between Anthropic (makers of Claude) and SpaceX, specifically granting Anthropic access to the Colossus One data center's 220,000+ Nvidia GPUs and 300 megawatts of capacity. Bhakdi's core thesis is that this deal represents the early formation of a "magic trinity" — Nvidia, SpaceX, and Anthropic — that he believes will define the trajectory of AGI and ASI development, with orbital AI compute being the ultimate long-term business model for SpaceX. He also briefly touches on Tesla's robotaxi progress and his personal portfolio performance.

## Market & Competition

1. **Anthropic-SpaceX Colossus One Deal:** Anthropic has signed an agreement with SpaceX to use compute capacity at the Colossus One data center, gaining access to over 220,000 Nvidia GPUs (described as mostly Blackwell B200s) and 300 megawatts of power capacity. Bhakdi emphasizes this is happening "within the month" (May 2026) and considers it far more significant than Anthropic's other compute deals, including a 5-gigawatt agreement with Amazon and a separate 5-gigawatt agreement with Google and Broadcom (slated to come online in 2027), and a $30 billion partnership with Microsoft and Nvidia. He argues 300 megawatts of Blackwell-class GPUs is likely at least three times more effective than equivalent wattage in Amazon Tranium or Google TPU chips.

2. **XAI Merged Into SpaceX; Colossus 2 Frees Up Colossus 1:** Elon Musk announced that XAI has been fully integrated into SpaceX. Musk noted that SpaceX AI had already moved its training workloads to Colossus 2, which freed up Colossus One to be leased to Anthropic. Bhakdi notes this was never the original design intent for Colossus One and represents a significant strategic shift — SpaceX is now openly positioning itself as a compute provider to third parties, including those who could be considered competitors to Grok.

3. **Elon's "Evil Detector" and Strategic Rationale:** Musk publicly stated he spent significant time with senior Anthropic leadership to evaluate whether Claude poses risks to humanity, and that "no one set off my evil detector." Bhakdi interprets this as genuine — not merely commercial — motivation, arguing Elon understands ASI is close and wants to ensure leading AI labs are not dangerous. Bhakdi frames the partnership as 10% business and 90% strategic/safety-motivated on Elon's part.

4. **The "Magic Trinity" Thesis — Nvidia, SpaceX, Anthropic:** Bhakdi's central analytical argument is that three entities are converging into a de facto strategic alliance: Nvidia (unbeatable on AI chips), SpaceX (uniquely positioned for unlimited orbital energy and compute), and Anthropic (which Bhakdi considers the clear leader in frontier AI, ahead of OpenAI and Google). He argues Anthropic has partially developed AGI/ASI — particularly in coding and design — and is accelerating AI development at a pace no competitor can match. Each party needs the others: Anthropic needs compute and energy, Nvidia needs somewhere to deploy chips at scale with sufficient energy, and SpaceX needs a killer application to monetize orbital energy.

5. **Orbital AI Compute as SpaceX's Core Business Model:** Bhakdi argues the ultimate business opportunity is orbital AI compute — harvesting effectively unlimited solar energy in space and converting it into compute capacity. He predicts orbital compute will begin in earnest within the next 12 months, reach significant scale by 2028, and enter hyperscale before 2030. He frames the Anthropic deal as the first real-world commercial step toward this vision, noting SpaceX is now building a terrestrial-to-orbital compute continuum. Bhakdi states he is updating his internal SpaceX financial model to merge the terrestrial Colossus compute business with the future orbital compute business into a single vertical.

6. **Anthropic's Compute Constraints and Competitive Position:** Bhakdi references rumors that Anthropic withheld a major model release not for safety reasons but because of compute constraints. The Colossus One deal, alongside other partnerships, is Anthropic's solution. He also notes Anthropic is expanding compute internationally to meet data residency requirements in regulated industries (finance, healthcare, government), and has committed to covering consumer electricity price increases caused by its data centers in the US, with plans to extend that commitment internationally.

7. **SpaceX IPO and Tesla Merger Speculation:** Bhakdi briefly discusses the SpaceX IPO, which he believes will be highly hyped and potentially overvalued at launch, and states he is unlikely to buy SpaceX at IPO. He expects the Tesla-SpaceX merger, if it happens, is more likely in 2027 though possibly as early as November 2026. He notes that a higher Tesla stock price (e.g., $500) could complicate the merger economics since a premium would need to be paid on top.

8. **Terafab Capex Update:** Bhakdi briefly mentions that Tesla's Terafab has a newly announced capex plan of $55 billion, which he describes as realistic.
