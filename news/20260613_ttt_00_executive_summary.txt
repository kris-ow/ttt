## Autonomous Driving

- **Belgium approves FSD Supervised nationwide; five European countries now cleared.** Belgium's Ministry of Transport of Flanders signed approval covering both Flemish and Belgian roads, days after Denmark's approval was announced. The five cleared markets — Belgium, Netherlands, Denmark, Lithuania, and Estonia — represent approximately 11% of European countries. Spain is cited as a likely next candidate.

- **FSD Supervised crosses 11 billion cumulative miles globally.** Tesla's fleet reached the milestone roughly one month after crossing 10 billion, implying ~1 billion FSD miles every 37 days. The pace is accelerating as European markets open and the fleet grows.

- **Denmark real-world data: FSD Supervised 3.5x safer than human drivers.** After four months of European road use, Danish data shows meaningfully fewer hard braking and hard turning events versus human baselines. Hosts argue this data should compel faster regulatory approvals across the continent.

- **Piper Sandler's Alex Potter declares Tesla has achieved Level 4 autonomy in most conditions.** In a note published approximately June 11, Potter wrote that "Tesla has solved the self-driving puzzle" and FSD is "ready for dissemination beyond early adopters," citing a personal April drive from Missoula, Montana to Minneapolis as primary evidence.

- **FSD V14 is the version being deployed in Denmark's new market rollout.** Tesla is distributing 30-day free trials of FSD Supervised V14 to Danish owners following regulatory clearance, per Sawyer Merritt. A "V14 light" port for Hardware 3 vehicles had been discussed for end of June 2026, but no update has emerged; the HW3/HW4 capability gap continues to widen.

- **Cybertruck Smart Summon announced as coming soon.** Tesla's official Cybertruck account confirmed Smart Summon is imminent for the Cybertruck, with engineers citing rear-wheel steering and variable front steering as the technical challenges that extended development time.

## Robotaxi

- **17 NHTSA-reported incidents since the June 2025 Austin launch; safety picture remains clean.** Only one incident involved a speed of 17 mph; the rest were fender benders. Only 7 of the 17 were attributed to Tesla fault, and 2 of those 7 involved human remote operators rather than the autonomous system. Hosts note NHTSA's "crash" classification includes events such as a tire touching a curb with no damage.

- **Texas DMV shows 69 Cybercabs registered, growing ~10/day.** The official verified count is 69 (up from 42 previously), per both FutureAzA and brighterwithherbert. Community photos show clusters of 20–30 Cybercabs sitting idle in Dallas and Houston lots, interpreted as vehicles staged and awaiting deployment authorization rather than supply constraints.

- **Hosts argue Cybercab requires its own supervised dataset before mass unsupervised deployment.** Using Cybertruck Smart Summon's delayed launch as a direct analogy, the thesis is that Cybercab's unique form factor demands sufficient platform-specific FSD miles before Tesla will scale driverless operations — regardless of Model 3/Y performance.

## Market & Competition

- **SpaceX IPO closes first day at ~$161, up ~19–20%, market cap ~$2.1 trillion.** The offering raised approximately $86 billion including overallotment — described as roughly three times the size of Saudi Aramco's record IPO. SpaceX ranks seventh globally by market cap; Tesla is cited at tenth at $1.46 trillion. Elon Musk now owns two of the world's ten largest companies.

- **Gwynne Shotwell publicly acknowledges Tesla-SpaceX synergies and potential merger.** In an interview on IPO day, SpaceX's President stated: "That might make Elon's life a little easier. There's no question that there are synergies between Tesla and SpaceX and our futures definitely there's a convergence of what we're all trying to achieve." No timeline or formal plan was mentioned, but the comment marks the first on-record senior SpaceX executive acknowledgment of the merger thesis.

- **BMW unveiled an all-electric M3 confirmed for 2027 production.** Key specs: 800V architecture, 100 kWh battery, 400 kW peak charging, BMW M sixth-generation cylindrical cells, structural battery. Relevant to Tesla's competitive positioning in the premium performance EV segment.

- **BYD China sales down ~30% year-to-date, eighth consecutive month of declines.** Competitive pressure from Geely, Xiaomi, Changan, and SAIC is cited. In April, the China top three were Geely, Xiaomi SU7, and Model Y; Model Y had ranked first the prior month.

## Electric Vehicles

- **Tesla China May 2026 sales: 85,982 units, up 39% year-over-year and 8% month-over-month.** At this pace, Giga Shanghai approaches ~1 million units annualized. Hosts note May is a stronger read on domestic demand than April, which is typically skewed toward export production.

- **2026 Model 3 RWD named most efficient EV in Edmunds real-world testing.** It traveled 393 miles on a full charge versus the EPA-rated 363 miles, and consumed 21.7 kWh per 100 miles — 13% better than EPA — the best figure Edmunds has recorded for any production EV. The review also flagged improved cabin finish quality.

- **Tesla ranks sixth in iSeeCars long-term durability study of 174 million vehicles.** Tesla is twice as likely as Subaru to reach 250,000 miles. Hosts argue the ranking is understated due to fleet youth limiting sample size, and predict Tesla eventually ranks first as more vehicles accumulate high mileage.
