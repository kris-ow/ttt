## Autonomous Driving

- **Five European countries now approved for FSD Supervised.** Belgium's Ministry of Transport of Flanders signed territory-wide approval this week, days after Denmark's approval was announced. Approved markets now: Belgium, Netherlands, Denmark, Lithuania, Estonia — roughly 11% of European countries. Brian White (FutureAzA) speculates Spain may be next.

- **FSD Supervised crosses 11 billion cumulative miles globally**, up from 10 billion one month prior — approximately 1 billion miles every 37 days. The pace is accelerating as European approvals expand the addressable fleet.

- **Denmark real-world data shows FSD Supervised 3.5x safer than human drivers** across hard-braking and hard-turn metrics, after four months of European road use. Per brighterwithherbert, the hosts argue this data should compel regulators to accelerate remaining European approvals.

- **Piper Sandler's Alex Potter declared FSD achieves Level 4 autonomy in most conditions** in a note released approximately June 11. Potter cited a personal April drive from Missoula, Montana to Minneapolis as evidence, writing "there is no substitute for personal experience." He characterizes FSD as "ready for dissemination beyond early adopters."

- **FSD V14 is the version being deployed in Denmark**, with Tesla distributing 30-day free trials to owners there following regulatory approval, per Sawyer Merritt.

- **Hardware 3 vs. Hardware 4 capability gap continues to widen.** A "FSD V14 light" port for HW3 vehicles was discussed as potentially arriving by end of June 2026 — sourced to a prior Ashok Elluswamy statement — but no update has emerged, per Brian White.

- **Cybertruck Smart Summon launching soon**, per Tesla's official Cybertruck account. Engineers cited rear-wheel steering and variable front steering as the technical hurdles that extended development. The hosts frame this as a direct analogy for why Cybercab also needs its own platform-specific dataset before mass unsupervised deployment.

## Robotaxi

- **~69 Cybercabs registered with Texas DMV** as of approximately June 11, up from 42 previously, growing at roughly 10 new registrations per day. Community photos show large clusters of parked Cybercabs in North Houston and Dallas lots. Per FutureAzA, the hosts interpret the buildup as vehicles ready to deploy pending additional supervised miles and regulatory confidence on the Cybercab form factor specifically.

- **NHTSA safety record since June 2025 launch: 17 reported incidents**, per FutureAzA. Only one involved a speed above walking pace (17 mph); the rest were fender benders. Only 7 were attributed to Tesla fault; 2 of those 7 involved human remote operators rather than the autonomous system. The hosts note NHTSA classifications include curb touches with no damage.

- **One host speculates Tesla deliberately paced the Cybercab rollout** to avoid a high-profile incident overshadowing the SpaceX IPO. With the IPO complete, he expects robotaxi news flow to accelerate — though he does not expect true mass-scale deployment until later in Q3 2026.

## Electric Vehicles

- **Tesla China May 2026 sales: 85,982 units** (Model 3 + Model Y combined), up 39% year-over-year and up 8% from April. Annualized, the pace approaches ~1 million units per year from Giga Shanghai. Tesla has added purchase incentives and charging benefits in China.

- **European Tesla sales Jan–May 2026 ran ~95,000 units ahead of Jan–May 2025**, per analyst Roland Percher's tracked subset. The hosts note the comparison is partially inflated by 2025 factory retooling shutdowns and prefer 2024 as the cleaner baseline.

- **2026 Model 3 RWD achieved 393 real-world miles** in Edmunds testing — 30 miles (8.3%) beyond EPA rating — and consumed 21.7 kWh/100 miles, 13% better than EPA's 25 kWh figure. Edmunds calls it the most efficient EV it has tested. Cabin finish quality was also noted as improved.

- **Tesla ranked sixth in iSeeCars long-term durability study** of 174 million vehicles — twice as likely to reach 250,000 miles as Subaru, and ranked above Mazda, Subaru, and Porsche. The hosts attribute the ranking to sample-size limits from Tesla's relative newness, predicting a future top-one finish as more high-mileage vehicles accumulate.

- **BYD China sales have fallen ~30% year-to-date**, marking eight consecutive months of decline, per data highlighted by Michael Dunn. Geely, Xiaomi SU7, and Model Y were April's top three in China. Separately, Xiaomi is estimated to be losing ~$5,000 per vehicle sold.

## Market & Competition

- **SpaceX priced at $135 and closed its IPO day at $161 (+20%)**, reaching a market cap of ~$2.1 trillion — ranked seventh globally. Tesla was cited at ~$1.46 trillion, ranked tenth. The offering raised ~$86 billion including overallotment, described as roughly three times Saudi Aramco's prior record. Elon Musk now publicly owns two of the ten largest companies on Earth.

- **SpaceX President Gwynne Shotwell commented on a potential Tesla-SpaceX merger**: "That might make Elon's life a little easier. There's no question that there are synergies between Tesla and SpaceX and our futures definitely there's a convergence of what we're all trying to a[chieve]..." No timeline or concrete plans were mentioned, per Sawyer Merritt.

- **BMW unveiled the all-electric M3**, confirmed for production in 2027: 800V architecture, 100 kWh battery, 400 kW peak charging, sixth-generation cylindrical cells, structural battery. Relevant to Tesla's competitive position in premium performance EVs.
