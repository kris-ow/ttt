This video features host Brian of FutureAzA interviewing Joe Tegtmeyer, a drone videographer who provides aerial coverage of Giga Texas, in a tour of major construction developments at the Austin site. The core thesis is that Tesla's on-the-ground construction activity — particularly the new Optimus factory, Cortex 2 AI data center, and Advanced Technology Fab — represents concrete, verifiable proof that Tesla's autonomous and humanoid robot ambitions are real and on an aggressive timeline. A follow-up video covering Cybercab specifically is teased for the next day.

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Optimus Factory Construction Underway at Giga Texas North Campus:** A major new construction project at the north end of Giga Texas has been officially confirmed by Tesla during the earnings call as the dedicated Optimus factory, targeting a 10 million units per year run rate. Joe Tegtmeyer reports the site is approximately 2 million square feet in ground slab alone, with active foundation work including footings, rebar cages, and geoper drilling equipment compacting gravel into the ground for structural support. A large excavation pit — described as a "plus" shape, roughly 2–4 meters deep — has been observed in the middle of the building footprint, possibly analogous to robot base infrastructure seen in the main factory's body-in-white welding area. The construction is believed to be phased, with the south end showing a different edge profile than the north, and a large adjacent field potentially reserved for future expansion.

2. **Construction Timeline Points to 2027 Readiness:** Based on observed construction pace and comparison to other Giga Texas builds, Joe Tegtmeyer estimates the Optimus factory will take approximately 18–20 months to complete to the point of initial operations, placing readiness well into 2027. This timeline is noted to roughly align with Samsung's chip fab for AI5, suggesting coordinated supply chain planning.

3. **Cortex 2 AI Data Center Partially Operational, Serving as Optimus Training Hub:** Cortex 2, located adjacent to the cathode building, is described as twice the size and capacity of Cortex 1 on the south end of the main factory. It is already partially operational, being used for Optimus training. Six cooling towers are active and supplemented by trailer-based heat exchangers. The facility is framed as "Optimus University" — the place where the robot learns tasks through data collection and training. This is considered critical infrastructure to support the Fremont facility, where Model S/X lines are being converted for Optimus production, with Tesla indicating initial production could begin as early as fall 2026.

4. **Optimus Test Track Under Construction at Southwest of Site:** A new multi-loop test track is under construction at the far southwest of the Giga Texas site. It features a large outer oval, a smaller inner oval, a tighter pad with chicanes, and turnarounds at each end — described as significantly more versatile than the Fremont test track. Active trenching and pipe installation for water management is underway. Spray oil has been applied to driving surfaces to mark them. Paving is expected soon. There is speculation it could also serve as a Cybertruck off-road testing area.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab Crash Testing Active at Giga Texas:** Extensive Cybercab crash testing is ongoing at the Giga Texas crash test facility (and also at Fremont), with the goal of certifying the Cybercab as a production vehicle rather than a prototype. Joe Tegtmeyer explains this is significant because achieving full vehicle certification means Tesla will not be constrained by a production cap (such as the 2,500-unit prototype limit), and can deploy the fleet as fast as vehicles can be built. The crash test facility is described as a critical enabler of fleet scaling.

2. **Cybercab Production Line Has No Body Bank Requirement:** Unlike the Model Y and Cybertruck lines, which use a "body bank" system — storing pre-built bodies until needed — the Cybercab production area does not require this inventory buffer. This means there is significant open space around the Cybercab production area that could be used for future expansion of that line.

3. **Cybercab Deep-Dive Video Teased:** Joe Tegtmeyer previously received special access to view the interior of the Cybercab unboxed assembly line at Giga Texas, including details he could not initially share. Those details have since been published, and a dedicated follow-up video covering everything about the Cybercab is planned for the following day.

## Energy
1. **~400 Megapacks Planned for Giga Texas to Support Cortex 2:** Cortex 2 alone is planned to have approximately 260 Megapacks, with an additional 140 already on site elsewhere at Giga Texas, totaling roughly 400 Megapacks across the campus. Each unit is identified as a Megapack 2XL rated at approximately 3.9 megawatts. The primary purpose is power quality — smoothing grid fluctuations to provide clean, stable power to AI computing infrastructure. Currently, only about half of the Cortex 2 Megapack allocation is installed, with the crane and pad prepared for the remainder.

2. **Tesla's Transformer Stockpile a Strategic Advantage:** Joe Tegtmeyer highlights that Tesla has been stockpiling electrical transformers for years, anticipating a supply shortage now affecting at least half of planned U.S. AI data centers. With approximately 200 additional transformers visible on the west side of the site, Tesla's forward procurement is described as a key differentiator allowing uninterrupted buildout of power systems and Megapack infrastructure while competitors face bottlenecks.

## Market & Competition
1. **Advanced Technology Fab (Joint Tesla/SpaceX Venture) Breaking Ground:** A new Advanced Technology Fab — described as a chip research and development facility and a joint venture between Tesla and SpaceX — is being prepared on land previously used as a contractor staging yard on the east side of Giga Texas. The investment is stated at approximately $3 billion. The footprint is estimated at around 2 million square feet. Soil sampling with six or more drilling rigs is actively underway to assess foundation requirements. This facility is explicitly distinguished from the separately planned Terafab, which is described as a different and much larger future project to be built elsewhere.
