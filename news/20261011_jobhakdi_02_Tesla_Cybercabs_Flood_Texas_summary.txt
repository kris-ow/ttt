## Robotaxi

The host interprets this as roughly 10x Tesla's prior-year Robotaxi rollout pace, up from his earlier claim of 4x.
3. **300 Cybercabs reportedly staged in Dallas:** The host cites social-media posts/photos showing a large number of Cybercabs parked in Dallas ahead of expected public deployment, alongside a local commenter's claim that Dallas is "flooded" with Robotaxi vehicles awaiting the Cybercab launch; he expects Dallas deployment within the next one to two weeks.
4. **Host projects Waymo overtake in Austin within weeks:** Based on the current Cybercab growth rate, the host predicts Tesla will overtake Waymo's Austin fleet size in under four weeks, calling this a signal that could convince mainstream Wall Street analysts of the thesis.
5. **Host's revenue/profit model for scaled Cybercab fleet:** The host estimates roughly 2,000–5,000 commercially deployed Cybercabs would generate about 3–5% of Tesla's (he says 2025, likely meaning annualized) earnings, projecting that at a 50–100%-per-month growth rate this could double Tesla's total profit within about four to five months of hitting that threshold. He separately estimated per-Cybercab profit potential of $50,000–$70,000/year at current pricing in "full run mode," using Uber's revenue/driver-cost structure as a comparison (treating this as his own rough estimate, not a sourced figure).
6. **Extrapolation to year-end fleet size:** Using the current weekly growth rate, the host's own simulation would extrapolate to about 3,000 Cybercabs by year-end, but he says he thinks that's likely exaggerated versus actual realistic levels.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **NHTSA audit vs. pending rule change:** The host reiterates his view that Tesla would currently fail an NHTSA audit of its robotaxi self-certification, but believes a new NHTSA rule in the implementation phase — which he predicts could be adopted around December — would effectively neutralize the audit's main objection before it escalates. He frames this as the primary remaining regulatory hurdle to mass Cybercab rollout, with California requiring longer (he estimates mid-next-year).
2. **FSD v15 seen as unlock for mass deployment:** The host expects a "more or less perfect" version 15 of FSD by end of year, which he says would enable rollout of tens of thousands of Cybercabs, contingent on the regulatory rule adoption discussed above.
