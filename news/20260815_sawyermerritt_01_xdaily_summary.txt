## Key Posts / Themes

1. **SpaceX ownership stakes revealed via SEC filings:** New SEC filings disclosed today show Google, Nvidia, and AMD's ownership stakes in SpaceX, valued at SpaceX's closing share price of $140/share: Google's stake is worth $77.1 billion, Nvidia's $17.2 billion, and AMD's $463 million. These figures reflect current market valuation of SpaceX stock (SPCX, NASDAQ) following its June 2026 IPO, underscoring how deeply integrated major tech/AI players are as shareholders.

2. **SpaceX closes $60B Cursor acquisition:** SpaceX officially closed its previously announced $60 billion acquisition of Cursor (the AI coding tool company). No further deal terms or integration details were given in the post.

3. **Starlink momentum — in-flight and hardware:** Sawyer Merritt reported his own in-flight Starlink experience on a United Airlines flight, clocking 370+ Mbps Wi-Fi speeds — described as roughly 215x faster than a prior non-Starlink flight — while cruising at 576 mph and 35,000 ft (anecdotal, first-person account). Separately, a new regulatory filing suggests SpaceX has developed its first Wi-Fi 7 router for Starlink, per Merritt.

4. **Tesla FSD safety and expansion content:** Tesla released a new compilation video showing FSD (Supervised) avoiding accidents and preventing pedestrian collisions across multiple European countries, including ones where FSD isn't yet regulator-approved; Merritt argues delaying approval "will cost lives" (his framing, not a quoted regulator/company statement). A separate follower-submitted clip showed a Tesla using FSD (Supervised) avoiding a collision in sun-glare conditions in Ontario, Canada — anecdotal evidence, not fleet-wide data.

5. **Tesla product and manufacturing updates:** Tesla launched Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) capability for all U.S. Model Y Premium trims, enabling the car's battery (via the Tesla Outlet Adapter) to power external devices like space heaters, power tools, and camping gear. Tesla also launched a Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) program in the U.S. and Puerto Rico, including one free month of FSD (Supervised) and Premium Connectivity plus a 115-point quality inspection. In manufacturing, Tesla is piloting new lithium recovery technology to support its lithium refinery, aiming to extend closed-loop recycling to other critical minerals like graphite, nickel, and cobalt.

6. **Model Y variants — delivery windows and China filing:** Multiple U.S. Model Y L order holders reported receiving estimated delivery date windows, per Merritt's conversations with several buyers (anecdotal, not an official Tesla disclosure). Separately, a Model Y Performance variant appears to be preparing for a China launch, having surfaced in China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology filings with listed range and battery specs.

7. **Competitive ADAS landscape — Rivian:** Rivian published a blog post reaffirming plans to release point-to-point assisted driving later in 2026, with a wider rollout targeted for 2027, positioning it against Tesla's FSD in the assisted/autonomous driving race.
