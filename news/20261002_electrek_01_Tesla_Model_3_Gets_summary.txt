Tesla rolled out a 16-inch center touchscreen across the entire US Model 3 lineup, alongside range and performance upgrades to the dual-motor variants, a day after making the same screen change in China.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **16-inch screen now standard in US Model 3:** Tesla replaced the 15.4-inch display with a 16-inch touchscreen across all US Model 3 trims, including the base RWD at $36,990 — the same screen added to the Model Y Premium in January and to all China-market Model 3s the previous day.
2. **Performance trim gains range and power:** The Model 3 Performance now has a black headliner, EPA-estimated range increased from 309 to 316 miles, and peak power is described by Tesla as "improved" to 528 hp (up from 510 hp at its 2024 launch); 0-60 mph remains unchanged at 2.9 seconds. Tesla did not explain the source of the added power/range or whether it applies to previously delivered cars.
3. **Premium AWD gets a bigger performance jump:** The Model 3 Premium AWD's 0-60 mph time improved to 3.8 seconds (from 4.2 seconds), with range listed at 346 miles.
4. **Pricing unchanged:** Base Model 3 remains $36,990, Premium RWD $42,490, Premium AWD $47,490, and Performance $54,990 despite the spec upgrades.
5. **Features still missing in the US:** Vehicle-to-load (V2L) remains unavailable on the US Model 3, even though China's Model 3 now supports 2.2 kW V2L output via adapter; in the US, V2L is limited to Model Y Premium, Model Y Performance, and Cybertruck. The new light gray interior offered in China is also absent from the US configurator, and the black headliner is exclusive to the Performance trim. Canada was not included in this update — the Canadian configurator still showed the older 15.4-inch screen, which the article notes is notable since Canadian Model 3s come from Giga Shanghai, the same factory supplying the updated China-market cars.

Notable quotes and data:

- Model 3 Premium AWD: 0-60 mph improved from 4.2s to 3.8s; range 346 miles.
- China Model 3 V2L: 2.2 kW AC output via adapter; not available on US Model 3.
