Herbert recaps three days of Elon Musk's posts that he argues together lay out SpaceX's long-term plan. SpaceX and Musk are each giving one share to every active Trump account. On the same day, the FCC granted a license for up to a million orbital computing satellites, and Musk said success at becoming multiplanetary and harnessing solar energy would make SpaceX worth about a million times the global economy. The host also covers SpaceX's AI compute leasing, a run of Starlink milestones, the Arizona data-center plan and a few Tesla items, then closes with risks and his theory on why SpaceX made the share gift now.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Two SpaceX shares per child:**
   - On Wednesday (2026-11-25), SpaceX said it will give one newly issued Class A share to each currently active Trump account, with transfer next month (December 2026).
   - Musk said he will personally match it with one share of his own. His shares will transfer when his lockup expires in June 2027.
   - Musk first announced his personal one-share pledge in October. Bloomberg called the company gift "doubling down on the promise."
   - Per Sawyer Merritt's calculations, the total is about 142 million shares: roughly 70 million from SpaceX, roughly 70 million from Musk, and over 2 million that Gwynne Shotwell pledged earlier this year. CNBC reports Shotwell's shares are also expected in December.
   - That is about $18 billion at Wednesday's price, or roughly $250 of stock per child.
   - SpaceX's stated rationale is that ownership of the company building these systems should extend to the next generation of Americans.

2. **What parents need to know about Trump accounts:**
   - These are new tax-advantaged savings accounts for any child under 18 with a Social Security number. They open on July 4th.
   - Children born 2025–2028 can receive a one-time $1,000 Treasury deposit. Family and friends can contribute up to $5,000 per year.
   - CNBC reports families have invested over $1 billion, and total deposits exceed $9 billion.
   - The White House said in October that about 70 million accounts had been created, mostly automatically. CNBC notes it is unclear how many count as active.
   - Michael Dell, a main backer of the program, urges parents to claim and activate accounts at trumpaccounts.gov. The host recommends doing so, since nobody has defined "active."
   - Under Treasury rules, gifted shares cannot be declined. They generally cannot be sold for five years or until the end of the year the child turns 17, whichever comes first.
   - When asked about foster children, Musk said SpaceX would try to give them shares anyway.

3. **Musk's "million times the global economy" post:**
   - Musk wrote that if SpaceX makes life multiplanetary and harnesses even a millionth of the Sun's energy, it would be worth about a million times the current global economy.
   - In the same post he called these "absurdly difficult goals" and advised holding the shares.
   - The host calls it the largest figure Musk has ever attached to SpaceX. He stresses it is not a price target.

4. **Risks and the host's ownership theory:**
   - **Dilution:** the new company-issued shares dilute existing holders. The host roughly estimates the dilution at under 1%. The release says the issuance is still subject to third-party and government approvals.
   - **Merger talks:** the host expects the merger committees now negotiating to factor the new shares in.
   - **Share price:** SPCX has been under pressure amid merger talks and the large lockup expiration on December 8.
   - **Host's theory (his own, not SpaceX's stated reason):** the roughly $9 billion of newly issued company shares buys SpaceX one of the largest individual shareholder bases of any company almost overnight. That base would be locked in for at least five years and inclined to root for Starlink and Starship.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **FCC licenses up to a million orbital compute satellites:**
   - FCC Chairman Brendan Carr announced approval of SpaceX's request for up to 1 million satellites serving as orbital computing modules. He called it the first license in US history for data centers in space. Musk replied that he was very grateful.
   - The design is a data center split across solar-powered satellites that communicate with Starlink by laser.
   - The FCC's statement framed it as moving humanity toward a Kardashev Type II civilization.
   - Per Sawyer Merritt, the FCC milestones are:
     - 1 satellite by 2033
     - 100,000 by 2035
     - 500,000 by 2038
     - the full million by 2040, under a 15-year license
   - The FCC rejected objections from Amazon's Kuiper, ViaSat and the American Astronomical Society.
   - The host notes the license is a permission, not a deployment.

2. **Commenter math on orbital compute and Musk's lead claim:**
   - A commenter estimated each satellite costs about $3 million to build and about $750,000 to launch. On his estimate, a satellite pays for itself in a couple of years even if SpaceX cuts compute prices to a third of today's.
   - The host flags this as one commenter's rough assumptions.
   - Musk replied that no advantage is insurmountable, but SpaceX is now an order of magnitude (about 10x) ahead of the rest of the world, and the gap appears to be widening.

3. **SpaceX lagging in AI models but winning as a compute landlord:**
   - On Monday (2026-11-23), the New York Times reported that Musk told SpaceX employees the company was behind in AI. Per the report, he shared comparison data and said renting out SpaceX compute would be more profitable.
   - Musk responded that the Times was presenting the obvious as news.
   - Separately, he called the researcher-vs-engineer split a "false dichotomy."
   - He also praised Google's new Gemini Neon model. Sundar Pichai said it set a first-day usage record for Google's paid APIs and that coding tokens rose 82% in six weeks. Two weeks earlier Musk had called Anthropic's Opus 5.5 a masterpiece.
   - The host's read: Musk evaluates Grok honestly while SpaceX rents compute to the leaders. Anthropic is the largest client and Google also leases capacity, so SpaceX gets paid either way.
   - Musk says Anthropic told him it wants another 10 GW from SpaceX.

4. **SemiAnalysis report on Anthropic's older GPUs:**
   - Per SemiAnalysis, Anthropic will not continue leasing about 50,000 older GPUs from SpaceX, and SpaceX lowered short-term pricing on its new chips.
   - The host says this is about 15% of the deal and newer chips are unaffected.

5. **Arizona and Texas data centers:**
   - SpaceX unveiled a supercomputer plan for Cochise County, Arizona, which Musk referred to as Colossus 3. The plan includes:
     - over $50 billion of investment
     - 1,000 permanent jobs and up to 5,500 construction jobs
     - no gas-fired power plants and no industrial water use, with closed-cycle dry cooling
   - SpaceX says it will pay for its own electricity and invest $3.6 billion in grid upgrades. The host says local residents' rates could fall by up to 40%.
   - A public meeting is set for December 9.
   - In Texas, SpaceX's $24 billion Starbase-area project received the first data-center permit approved after a statewide suspension.

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **Subsidy swap for rural broadband:** SpaceX agreed to forgo $738 million in federal broadband subsidies and instead provide free Starlink equipment to rural households. The deal guarantees minimum speeds, an affordable plan and 10 years of service. In exchange, SpaceX avoids most of the paperwork and audits that come with subsidies.

2. **Global expansion and price cuts:**
   - **Africa:** Musk said Starlink is now approved in 30 of Africa's 54 countries; Nigeria was first, in 2023.
   - **Vietnam:** Viettel, a major Vietnamese carrier, has started selling Starlink.
   - **Maritime:** Starlink is on almost 67,000 ships, up from none five years ago. By 2024 it was already the largest maritime operator by revenue, at about 39%.
   - **Pricing:** Starlink Mini with a roam plan in the US dropped to $99, from its $599 launch price in June 2024, about a sixth of the original.
   - **Aviation:** United has Starlink on 729 aircraft, covering more than half its daily flights.

## Robotaxi
1. **Starlink built into Teslas:** Asked about Starlink in all Tesla models, not just the Cybercab, Musk said he hopes to have it by the end of 2027, except in China, where Starlink is banned. The host notes the new Cybercabs in Dallas already have Starlink hardware built in. He argues uninterrupted connectivity matters once a driverless car becomes a place to work or watch video. He treats the timeline as a goal to "write down in pencil."

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD v15 in heavy rain:** Musk shared a video of FSD v15 driving through a heavy downpour with a badly obscured windshield while the screen still tracked every surrounding car, writing "Tesla has superhuman vision."

2. **Grok in Teslas:** A user reported that his father and stepmother ran out of Grok credits on a seven-and-a-half-hour trip in their new Model Y Performance. Musk also promoted the Grok bot as a tool for tracking genuine Black Friday price drops.
