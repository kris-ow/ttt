## Executive Summary
Today's digest centers on Tesla's robotaxi expansion (Cybercab rides for employees, Pull Over button feature discussion) and the Optimus manufacturing ramp at Fremont, alongside a regulatory fight in New Jersey that could effectively block Tesla's robotaxi deployment there. Secondary signals include FSD V14 Lite's international expansion to South Korea, a possible Model Y Performance return in China, and continued rental program growth — all incremental positives, tempered by real regulatory friction.

---

## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Cybercab employee rides imminent at Giga Texas:** Tesla officially announced that Cybercab rides — with no steering wheel or pedals — for employees at Giga Texas will begin "soon," marking a concrete step toward public Cybercab deployment. No specific date was given.

2. **Optimus production line prep at Fremont:** Tesla released a new video showing teardown of the Model S and Model X production lines at Fremont to make way for Optimus manufacturing lines. Per the post, when fully ramped this space will be able to produce 1 million Optimus robots per year — a significant capacity claim tied directly to Fremont retooling.

3. **"Pull Over" button feature request for personal FSD:** Sawyer Merritt noted that Tesla's Unsupervised Robotaxis have a "Pull Over" button allowing the vehicle to stop early or at an alternate location, and expressed a desire for this same feature in personal FSD-equipped cars. This is commentary/feature request, not an announced product change.

4. **New Jersey regulatory pushback on autonomous vehicles:** NJ Democratic State Senator Andrew Zwicker is backing bill S.1677/A.3968, which would require cameras plus two additional sensor types (likely LiDAR and radar) for autonomous vehicles — a requirement that would effectively ban Tesla's camera-only robotaxis in the state. Zwicker framed it as pro-safety, not anti-Tesla, citing NJ traffic deaths last year (~600). Merritt publicly pushed back, calling it "anti-competitive favoritism" that delays lifesaving AV tech. This is a notable regulatory risk flag for Tesla's robotaxi expansion plans.

5. **FSD V14 Lite goes international to South Korea:** Tesla FSD V14 Lite has begun rolling out to US-built Model 3/Y owners with HW3 hardware as early access in South Korea — the first international expansion mentioned for this version.

6. **Model Y Performance possibly returning in China:** A filing with China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology reportedly shows specifications matching a Model Y Performance variant, suggesting Tesla may reintroduce this trim in the Chinese market. Based on regulatory filing, not an official Tesla confirmation.

7. **Vehicle rental program expansion:** Tesla is expanding its vehicle rental program (per job postings) to Maryland, Kansas, Washington, and Pennsylvania. Terms include unlimited miles, free Supercharging, FSD included, typically 3-7 day rentals; pricing at $60/day for Model 3/Y and $75/day for Cybertruck. Timing for launch is unspecified ("TBD").

8. **Tesla Semi public display in Laredo, TX:** The Tesla Semi will be publicly displayed July 12-14 at the Port Laredo Global Trade Summit at the LISD Performing Arts Center — a visibility/marketing event for the commercial trucking product.

## Signal Strength: **Mildly Bullish** — Concrete robotaxi/Optimus manufacturing progress and FSD international expansion are positive, but the New Jersey regulatory threat highlights real friction risk for autonomous vehicle rollout in certain states.
