## Robotaxi
- Tesla's Cybercab launch event is imminent: riders through August 23 enter a lottery for tickets, winners announced August 25. Several hosts read this as confirmation commercial Cybercab launch is near.
- Cybercab production estimates (Bhakdi, unconfirmed): ~2,000 built to date, run-rate near 2,000/month by September, over 10,000 by year-end called "extremely likely" — though deployment pace is far more contested, with one third-party estimate (Genma) as low as ~1,000 in service by next year.
- Tesla VP Lars Moravy told Sawyer Merritt there is no 2,500-unit cap on Cybercab, pushing back on a widely cited production-limit narrative.
- Cybercabs spotted in Baltimore, suggesting geographic testing beyond core markets; ~97 Cybercabs also seen staged at a Houston lot, with unconfirmed geofence-expansion rumors.
- New Austin charging-hub permit near Tesla's St. Elmo site: phase 1 is 12 V3.5 cabinets/48 posts starting construction September 7 (targeted January completion); a "80 wireless chargers" reference appears to be a design-firm error per analyst Marco — actual wireless charging pads, if any, likely slip to Q1 next year.
- Persistent rider complaints continue: multiple accounts describe pickup/drop-off looping adding 5–15 minutes, one 47-minute detour remote support couldn't fix, and one drop-off that overshot at nearly 70 mph before a 40-minute looping episode.
- Tesla posted a Palo Alto "events manager for Robotaxi" job listing to scale Robotaxi/Cybercab events and media partnerships.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Starship's flight 13 vehicle tipped over and remained afloat in the Indian Ocean instead of exploding — a first for the program. After ~24 days at sea and rough conditions Musk called "not looking good," the recovery team towed it to calmer water off Christmas Island for inspection before return to Starbase.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX completed its acquisition of Cursor; the team joins SpaceX's AI org to work on Grok, Grok Build, Grok Bot, Grok API and Cursor itself.
- Musk floated 2029 as a target for orbital AI compute, citing Earth power/permitting limits — flagged as a hedged ("probably") claim given his history of missed dates.
- Grok 4.6 launched at Grok 4.5's price, tying for #1 on a community agentic benchmark (score 59) alongside Claude Opus 5 Max; Grok 4.7 (2.1T parameters, trained partly on SpaceX engineering data) expected within weeks.
- Microsoft added Grok 4.6 to GitHub Copilot's CLI, IDE and cloud products — a distribution win given Microsoft's OpenAI ties.
- Grok bot (autonomous AI agent) entered public beta at ~$200/month standalone or bundled with $300/month Super Grok Heavy; investor Gavin Baker called it "another Claude Code moment for AI."
- Terafab site (Grimes County, TX) land-clearing underway: ~6,640 acres owned, 2,300 more in final recording, 2,000–3,000 additional acres needed for a 4+ mile facility; Phase 1 estimated at ~$17B, full project up to $120B across 10 phases.

## Electric Vehicles
- Einride ordered 500 Tesla Semis (roughly $125M, largest Semi deal to date), tripling its fleet from ~250 to 750 trucks; deliveries run September 2026–September 2028. Einride's 2028 breakeven plan needs 1,500–2,000 trucks, suggesting more orders may follow. This lifts tracked Tesla Semi orders past 1,100, up from ~686.
- Model Y L hit 358 miles in Edmunds range testing, 38 miles (about 12%) over its 320-mile EPA rating, despite extra weight from a Performance-trim rear motor.
- Roadster may be unveiled as early as this month per The Information (unconfirmed): a limited SpaceX-branded, non-street-legal thruster variant reportedly costing hundreds of thousands of dollars, plus a separate consumer version without thrusters; Musk has said the thruster variant could do 0-60 in under a second.
- Sweden's ~1,021-day IF Metall strike ended with no collective agreement signed; over 90% of Tesla's Swedish workforce stayed on the job throughout, and Model Y remained the country's best-selling car.
