The article covers statements made on Tesla's Q3 earnings call about Cybercab customer deliveries and unsupervised FSD timing, arguing the two timelines create a contradiction where customers would own a non-functional vehicle for months.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab customer deliveries targeted for end of 2026:** On the Q3 call, VP of Engineering Lars Moravy said Tesla is trying to get Cybercabs into customers' hands by the end of 2026, noting that vehicles currently produced in Texas are going to Tesla's internal Robotaxi fleet, with customer deliveries starting small before scaling. Musk added that Cybercabs would mostly remain Tesla-owned transport-as-a-service vehicles, which the article says somewhat contradicts Tesla's separate call for Cybercab fleet buyers issued the same day as the Cybercab launch event.

## Autonomous Driving
2. **Unsupervised FSD timeline shifted to mid-2027:** In response to a question about when unsupervised FSD will reach customer cars, Tesla's answer changed from the longstanding "end of next year" to "middle of next year" — i.e., Q2 2027, per the article's framing of the call. Tesla also reportedly said it will not give customers access to its unsupervised software even in areas where Robotaxi already operates until Q2 2027.
3. **Current Robotaxi deployment scope:** The article notes Robotaxi is currently available only in small portions of 6 cities across 2 states, contrasting this with Musk's prior prediction that Robotaxi would cover half the US population by the end of last year.

## Editorial Stance
The author argues that because Cybercab has no steering wheel or pedals, and unsupervised FSD won't arrive until roughly six months after promised customer deliveries, Tesla appears to plan to sell a vehicle that is unusable by owners for that period — the author suggests this is either a poorly thought-out plan or that Tesla's timelines are not reliable. The author also argues that if Cybercabs are delivered with steering wheels (as most current ones reportedly have), Tesla should simply call it a Model 2 and sell it at the previously discussed ~$25k price point.
