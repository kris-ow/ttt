## Key Posts / Themes

1. **UBS Raises Tesla Price Target on Physical AI/Robotics Thesis:** UBS analyst Joseph Spak raised his Tesla price target to $442 from $364, citing Tesla's long-term potential in physical AI and robotics — a direct nod to the robotaxi/Optimus thesis underpinning TTT's coverage. This is a notable sell-side validation of the non-auto growth narrative.

2. **NHTSA Signals Openness to Removing Steering Wheel Requirements:** NHTSA head Jonathan Morrison said the agency will "absolutely" consider ending requirements that driverless vehicles include steering wheels, stating it "doesn't make any sense" to require manual controls in vehicles designed never to be human-driven. This is a meaningful regulatory tailwind for Tesla's Cybercab (which lacks a steering wheel) and the broader robotaxi rollout.

3. **Giga Texas Production & Cybercab Shipments:** Drone footage (via Airwave Dynamics) reportedly showed high production output at Giga Texas, specifically for Model Y, alongside more than 50 additional Cybercabs being shipped out — a visual/anecdotal data point on robotaxi fleet scaling, not an official Tesla disclosure.

4. **LG Energy Expanding Battery Capacity for Tesla Demand:** LG Energy is expanding cylindrical battery production at its Nanjing, China factory, citing rising demand for Tesla EVs including the Model Y; existing lines have reportedly been running at full capacity amid steadily increasing utilization — a supply-chain signal pointing to sustained/rising Model Y demand.

5. **Tesla Semi Commercial Adoption:** Southern California logistics provider QX Logistix is adding 20 Tesla Semis to its fleet, a incremental commercial EV adoption data point.

6. **Competitive Landscape — Mercedes CLA AMG EV and 1X Humanoid Hands:** Mercedes unveiled an all-electric CLA AMG EV with simulated gas engine sounds, fake gear shifts/rev counter, and a "seat shaker" to mimic engine feel, offering up to 416 miles WLTP range (sedan) / 398 miles (Shooting Brake) — a competitive EV entrant, notable for its ICE-mimicry gimmicks rather than performance edge. Separately, U.S. humanoid robotics company 1X unveiled new tendon-driven robot hands (25 DOF, 5:1–15:1 tendon drive ratio, 3 DOF wrist, fully backdrivable, pressure+location tactile sensing, IP68 waterproof/food-safe, made in USA) — a relevant competitive benchmark for Tesla's Optimus hand development.

7. **Tangential SpaceX Items:** Posts on SpaceX's new "Starmind" AI satellite constellation website and AI1 satellite specs (150kW peak compute, 70m wingspan, chip-agnostic architecture), plus Starlink's 2026 H1 deployment pace (1,589 satellites vs. 1,489 in H1 2025) and a Trump social media post thanking Gwynne Shotwell — included for context but limited direct Tesla investment relevance.
