The article reports on Kelley Blue Book/Cox Automotive data showing US EV prices rose month-over-month in September 2026 even as they remain down year-over-year, driven by shrinking manufacturer incentives.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **EV average transaction price rose in September:** The average transaction price for a new EV in the US was $55,879 in September 2026, up about 2.4% from August's revised average of $54,574, but down 3% from September 2025. Kelley Blue Book attributes the year-over-year decline to a stronger mix of more affordable models.
2. **EV-to-ICE price premium holds near 11%:** The price premium for an EV over a comparable "ICE+" model was 11% in September, roughly where it has stood through 2026, down from an average of nearly 18% in 2025.
3. **Incentives shrink to 2026 low:** EV discounts averaged 11% of ATP in September, the lowest level this year, down from 14.2% a year earlier (when the $7,500 federal tax credit was still in effect before expiring). At September's average price, that equates to roughly $6,147 in incentives industry-wide; the article notes actual offers vary by model, citing the Polestar 4's rebate of up to $25,000 off its $56,400–$57,800 MSRP, available until November 2.
4. **Tesla pricing details:** Tesla's ATP rose 3.3% month-over-month to $54,345 in September, roughly flat (+0.4%) year-over-year. Model 3 and Model Y made up nearly 97% of Tesla sales, with average prices of $53,804 and $53,052 respectively. Nearly 1,500 Cybertrucks sold in September at an average transaction price of $98,109.
