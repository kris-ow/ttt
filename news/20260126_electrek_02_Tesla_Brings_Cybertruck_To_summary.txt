Electrek reports that Tesla has begun Cybertruck deliveries in the UAE — its second international market after South Korea — even as US Cybertruck sales have collapsed, arguing the overseas push can't offset the domestic decline.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **UAE Cybertruck launch:** Tesla delivered Cybertrucks to customers in the UAE, with around 63 trucks handed over at a launch event in Dubai's Al Marmoom desert area. The Dual Motor AWD starts at AED 404,900 (~$110,000) versus $79,990 in the US, and the Cyberbeast starts at AED 454,900 (~$123,000) versus $114,990 in the US. Tesla also opened the configurator in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Jordan, and Israel, having first opened Middle East orders in September 2025.
2. **Second international market, after South Korea:** South Korea became the first market outside North America to receive Cybertruck deliveries, on November 27, 2025. The article says Tesla chose the UAE and South Korea strategically because both allow the Cybertruck without the modifications required for European compliance.
3. **Blocked in the EU:** The Cybertruck remains barred from sale in the European Union; the article cites a prior Electrek report that the US Army could not import Cybertrucks for personnel in Europe and that Germany's Federal Ministry of Transport denied exemption requests because the vehicle "deviates significantly" from EU regulations. The stainless-steel body reportedly violates EU pedestrian-protection crumple-zone standards and exceeds the 3.5-tonne weight threshold; the article says UK authorities have seized imported Cybertrucks as illegal.
4. **US sales collapse:** Citing Cox Automotive data, the article reports US Cybertruck sales of 38,965 units in 2024, falling to 20,237 units in 2025 (a 48% decline), with just 4,140 units in Q4 2025 (a 68% year-over-year drop). These figures fall far short of Elon Musk's earlier predictions of 250,000–500,000 annual sales, and the article notes Tesla's own Q4 delivery report called the Cybertruck "the big miss." The article also states that Musk's other companies began buying unsold Cybertrucks, with SpaceX purchasing tens of millions of dollars' worth.
5. **Pricing drift from original promise:** At its 2019 unveiling, Tesla announced a $39,900 base price; the article states the current cheapest Cybertruck (Long Range RWD) starts at $69,990, which it calls a 75% increase (a reader comment included in the article notes the RWD trim was later discontinued and had very low production volume).

## Editorial Stance
Electrek's Fred Lambert argues the Middle East expansion is strategically sensible given regional appetite for expensive trucks, but says the UAE and broader Middle East are too small to offset the US sales decline. He suggests the Cybertruck's commercial future is in question, noting Musk has said Tesla could pivot to a more traditional truck if Cybertruck fails, and argues a redesigned "more palatable" Cybertruck is increasingly unlikely given Tesla's focus on robotaxis and humanoid robots, leaving it a regional product limited to North America, South Korea, and the Middle East.
