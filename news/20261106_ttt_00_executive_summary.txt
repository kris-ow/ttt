## Brief

- Tesla to wind down Cybertruck production mid-2027
- Cybertruck prices hiked up to 10% after wind-down news
- Starship Flight 15 targeted Nov 13, first ship catch attempt
- TSLA, SPCX both sold off sharply post-election
- IMC Logistics takes delivery of first Tesla Semi
- $150M Megapack system goes online in Canberra, Australia

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla will wind down Cybertruck production at Giga Texas around mid-2027, per Musk's X post, citing a need for more Cybercab capacity. Electrek's Fred Lambert disputes the capacity rationale given Giga Texas's 10M+ sq ft and Cybercab's low registered volume, and argues (his editorial view) Tesla is ending the truck because it never hit planned volumes — estimating losses of "a couple of billions."
- Tesla raised US Cybertruck prices up to 10% days after the wind-down announcement and pulled the base Dual Motor AWD trim from order; Premium AWD now $92,490, Cyberbeast $102,490 — the entry orderable price is up 32% in about ten weeks, per Electrek.
- IMC Logistics, the largest US marine drayage provider, took delivery of its first Tesla Semi, the first of 50 ordered in September for California operations.
- A FreightWaves editorial director who test-drove the Semi loaded and unloaded reversed his skepticism on its 500-mile range claim, citing strong torque on grades, minimal reliance on service brakes, and competitive ride quality versus diesel.
- A fleet buyer (HMD, 50 Semi tractors on order) said the Semi could cut the ~20 depot workers currently needed in Chicago winters to keep diesel trucks warmed up.

## Energy
- A ~$150 million Tesla Megapack system went online in Canberra, Australia — described as the largest battery storage system in the region's history, capable of powering roughly one-third of Canberra's electricity needs for two hours at peak demand.

## Market & Competition
- TSLA and SPCX sold off sharply post-election, with SPCX falling below its $135 IPO price and TSLA below $350; a bull panel on brighterwithherbert called the reaction overblown given Texas's regulatory structure insulates near-term robotaxi and data-center policy from the incoming Democratic governor (next commissioner turnover not until August 2027).
- Analyst Dan Ives, cited by the panel, estimated the Democratic sweep could slow AI data center buildout and that ~10% of a planned 800-1,000+ US data center pipeline may not happen.
- Ted Cruz said he remains a fan of Tesla, SpaceX and Musk as "a great business leader" but objects to Musk's $200M in political spending against him — drawing a line between opposing Musk's politics and supporting his companies' technology.
- NHTSA is launching an initiative to modernize recall processes with a focus on OTA updates, which could reduce friction/cost on future Tesla FSD-related recalls.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX's Colossus 2 complex near Memphis comprises three buildings — Macrohard (~210MW, ~110,000 GB200 servers), a larger renovated warehouse (~443MW, ~220,000 GB300 servers), and a newer, smaller "Mini Hard" template building that packs comparable density into a much smaller footprint, per satellite-imagery analysis from cernbasher.
- SpaceX is replicating the Mini Hard design at a new 444-acre site near Starbase, Texas (recently annexed under Starbase permitting authority), with two buildings under construction estimated ~60% larger than the Memphis version; adjacent 157-acre and 893-acre parcels are also SpaceX-owned with no confirmed construction yet.
- SpaceX's compute roadmap was cited at scaling from ~3GW now to ~13GW next year, with a potential path to ~20GW; a new 6GW Arizona data center project includes a reported $3.6 billion SpaceX commitment to grid transmission, projected to cut area electricity rates 10-40%.
- Musk reportedly said orbital data centers are "looking pretty appealing right now" in response to political uncertainty around terrestrial permitting.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Starship Flight 15 is targeted for November 13 (unconfirmed date), per SpaceX VP of launch relayed by Sawyer Merritt, and is expected to include the first-ever attempt to catch the Starship vehicle itself, distinct from prior Super Heavy booster catches.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- United Airlines became the first airline to let passengers stream personal Spotify accounts on Starlink-enabled seatback screens via QR code.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- A Wall Street-sourced claim (per panelist Alexandra Merz, ~95% confidence) holds that October 20 — when SpaceX and Tesla announced special merger committees — will anchor merger premium calculations, limiting the current stock selloff's impact on eventual deal terms.
- Merz estimates the merger needs roughly 8-10 of 10 voting shares to approve, given ~30% of Tesla shares won't vote, requiring an attractive premium and ideally a price floor; Jeff Lutz cited the Marvell-Lumentum deal's uncollared ~40% stock drop as a cautionary precedent.
- Lutz said he found no precedent for a state blocking a federally approved merger, pushing back on narratives that the election threatens the Tesla-SpaceX deal.
