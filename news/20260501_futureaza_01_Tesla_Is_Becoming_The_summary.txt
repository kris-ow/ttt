This video is a tour of Unplugged Performance, an aftermarket Tesla modification shop located near SpaceX's Hawthorne facility in Los Angeles. The host, Brian of Futurazza, speaks with company representatives Ben and Danny about the most popular Tesla modifications, their Pikes Peak race car, and their growing police/law enforcement vehicle program. The video's core thesis is that Teslas are highly customizable platforms that can be upgraded for performance, comfort, off-road use, and law enforcement applications.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Unplugged Performance Background and Tesla Focus:** Unplugged Performance started 26 years ago focused on Japanese sports cars, but pivoted toward Tesla modifications after becoming neighbors with Tesla's design studio in 2013. The company now offers close to 1,000 products for Tesla vehicles, covering suspension, wheels, brakes, wraps, police upfits, and motorsports builds. Ben emphasizes that Tesla's continuous over-the-air firmware upgrades are considered "untouchable" — Unplugged Performance does not attempt to modify powertrain software.

2. **Luxury Suspension for Model Y — Most Popular Consumer Modification:** The most popular consumer product is the luxury suspension kit for the pre-refresh Model Y, which softens the ride without invasive chassis changes. Ben notes it can be installed and removed the same day, and describes it as making older Model Y models feel comparable to the refreshed version. He attributes the original stiffness to spring rates and damping rigidity, and says the kit hits the right balance of valving and spring rate without requiring bushing replacement.

3. **Road Warrior Wheels — Lifetime Guarantee, Aerodynamic, and Indestructible:** Unplugged Performance's Road Warrior forged aluminum wheels come with a lifetime guarantee against damage, a claim they back up by noting zero replacement wheels have been needed by law enforcement clients who intentionally drive over curbs. The wheels also feature an integrated aerodynamic design (described as an "aero disk") that users report saves meaningful range. A curb guard accessory pops on and off for additional protection.

4. **Lowering for Range Gains:** Ben states that lowering a Model Y by one inch can yield approximately 7–10% range improvement by reducing the coefficient of drag. He references published data comparing one-inch, one-and-a-half-inch, and two-inch drops. He notes that Tesla's air-suspension vehicles (Model S, Model X, Cybertruck) automatically lower at highway speeds for the same reason.

5. **Pikes Peak Race Car "Dark Helmet" — Factory Firmware, Unlimited Class Winner:** The company built a heavily modified Tesla Model S Plaid called "Dark Helmet" that won Pikes Peak competing in the unlimited class against LMP4 race cars, open-wheel cars, and gasoline-powered cars with over 1,000 horsepower. The car runs factory Tesla firmware and technically retains FSD capability, though Ben jokes it is not a practical FSD use case. It generates 4,000 lbs (two tons) of downforce, runs 19x13-inch wide wheels with 350-wide tires front and rear, and features Öhlins TTX four-way adjustable coilovers. The main weakness is battery and drivetrain heat management, as the factory firmware still enforces thermal limits. During qualifying at Pikes Peak (approximately 70 cars total), Dark Helmet consistently placed in the top 15 of all categories and came within approximately one second of a multi-million dollar Porsche 935 motorsports car.

6. **Law Enforcement Cybertruck and Model Y Police Upfits — Rapidly Growing Segment:** Unplugged Performance is currently building hundreds of Cybertruck patrol vehicles alongside Model Y police units. Ben says demand is surging partly due to rising gas prices. Police departments benefit from lower fuel costs, no idling losses, low center of gravity, and instant torque that ends pursuits faster because suspects cannot pull away. The company hosts EVOC (Emergency Vehicle Operations Course) training events to let officers experience the vehicles firsthand. Most departments are currently ordering all-wheel-drive Model Y configurations; a newer standard all-wheel-drive trim (launched less than two months prior to filming) is expected to become the majority choice due to its lower price — approximately $7,000 less than the premium all-wheel-drive trim — while still being suitable for law enforcement use. Zero wheel damage has been recorded across all law enforcement deployments despite intentional curb strikes.

7. **Other Popular Modifications and Products:** Beyond suspension and wheels, Unplugged Performance does a high volume of camber arm adjustments (for Model S, X, 3, and Y) to correct tire wear, sway bar upgrades for sharper handling response, steel bullbars, side-step rockers, and steel bumpers for Cybertruck, and full vehicle wraps. All modifications are designed to be installable by any standard mechanic shop without specialized tools. Products ship worldwide.

8. **Commentary on the Robotaxi Future and Car Ownership:** Ben briefly reflects that people are currently in a window where they can buy, modify, and enjoy personal vehicles, but that the coming robotaxi era will reduce personal car ownership over time. He frames this as a reason to enjoy car modification while it lasts.
