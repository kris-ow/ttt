This episode of Electrified, hosted by Dillon Loomis, covers a wave of Cybercab deployment signals including the publication of first responder guides, ongoing fleet stockpiling across multiple cities, and a major FSD software milestone with V14 Lite rolling out to Hardware 3 vehicles. The core thesis is that Tesla is nearing meaningful Cybercab deployment in Austin and potentially Houston, while the FSD V14 Lite release for Hardware 3 opens a large incremental monetization opportunity. The video also touches on Waymo's Uber partnership ending in Phoenix and VW's autonomous driving struggles.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab First Responder Guide Now Live — Last Major Pre-Deployment Hurdle Cleared:** The Cybercab first responder guide is now officially published on the Texas Department of Public Safety website, which Dillon Loomis characterizes as the last major hurdle before Tesla can deploy the Cybercab in Austin. He notes that when Tesla uploaded the Model Y first responder guide, deployment followed within a few weeks, but that timeline could be shorter now that the service has been operating for over a year. He cautions that even after deployment, the initial fleet size will likely be under 10 vehicles for at least the first week or two.

2. **Major Announcement Teased for July 7th:** Lars Moravy stated that a "big announcement" related to scaling efforts on the Giga Texas campus is coming on Tuesday, July 7th, though he did not specify what it is. Dillon Loomis interprets this as most likely relating to the Cybercab, but acknowledges it could be any number of things.

3. **Cybercab Houston Launch Rumor — Mid-to-Late July:** Per the host (unconfirmed), a rumor attributed to Kobe Pierce — citing a "very reputable source" — suggests a Cybercab launch in Houston is expected in mid-to-late July. The source also indicated a service area expansion in Houston is coming, potentially encompassing two distinct areas including NRG Stadium and George Bush Intercontinental Airport, with the service area reportedly expected to be roughly 50% larger than Waymo's current Houston service area. The host flags this as speculative.

4. **Cybercab Stockpiling Across Multiple Cities Without Deployment Decals:** Large numbers of Cybercabs are being reported in staging lots across Houston (78 vehicles per SE Robinson), Dallas (dozens visible in a Reddit-shared image), and Phoenix. Critically, none of the vehicles in Houston, Dallas, or Phoenix have the Cybercab word mark decals — which, per the first responder guide, are present on vehicles actually in robotaxi service. This is presented as evidence these vehicles are not yet deployment-ready or cleared for service.

5. **Cybercab Testing at Giga Texas — Fully Autonomous, No Steering Wheel:** Per Joe Tegtmeyer, Cybercabs continue testing on the Giga Texas track, with recent sightings showing vehicles with Cybercab logos being driven fully autonomously. Separately, a Cybercab was spotted at the test track with no steering wheel and a safety monitor on the passenger side.

6. **Austin Robotaxi Fleet at 84 Registered Vehicles:** Per the host's robotaxi dashboard (sourced from RobotaxiTracker.com), Tesla now has 84 vehicles officially registered for the robotaxi service in Austin, with 15 vehicles added on June 27th (Saturday).

7. **Five New Cities Deadline Missed — Phoenix, Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Las Vegas:** Tesla's Q4 2025 slide deck had targeted the first half of 2026 for launching robotaxi service in five new cities (Phoenix, Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Las Vegas), but the Q1 2026 slide deck (uploaded in April 2026) downgraded this to "preparations underway." With June 30th being the last day of H1 2026, the host confirms Tesla has missed this self-imposed deadline. He attributes the slowdown to Tesla internally seeing promising results with V15 and deciding to wait for that model before expanding.

8. **Cybercab First Responder Guide — Key Technical Details:** The guide reveals several notable Cybercab specifications: it features a 400V high voltage battery system (not 800V) with the battery enclosure serving as the cabin floor with only a thin high-strength steel layer separating the two; it does run the new 48V low voltage architecture (Etherloop-capable); 10 airbags in the production version (five per side); an active hood pedestrian protection system that activates between approximately 15–32 mph using pyrotechnic actuators; two-way speakers and exterior microphones on the B-pillars; infrared webbing on seat belts for night passenger visibility; and no wireless charging hardware noted anywhere in the documentation. The vehicle can operate on all public and private roadways at all hours and under light-to-moderate precipitation. It is designed to stop and park if a seatbelt becomes unbuckled or a door remains open.

9. **Cybercab Test Vehicles Being Converted to Production Configuration in the Field:** Per Bradford at Rebellionaire (as cited by the host), Tesla has been observed removing the steering wheel and associated hardware from test vehicles to convert them to the production (no-steering-wheel) configuration out in the field.

10. **FSD Responding to Police Hand Signals — Impressive Real-World Demo:** A video shared by DJ showed a Tesla running FSD stopping at a green light because a police officer was directing traffic in the intersection, then proceeding only after the officer waved the vehicle through — with the host noting the car appeared to anticipate the officer's signal slightly before it was given.

11. **Dallas and Houston Unsupervised Service Remains Very Limited:** Dillon Loomis reiterates that since Tesla's April 2026 launch in Dallas and Houston (each with one vehicle), service in those cities has been very limited and sometimes unavailable entirely, suggesting the launches were timed to provide a talking point for Q1 earnings.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD V14 Lite Rolls Out to Hardware 3 Early Access Customers:** A show (unspecified) confirmed FSD V14 Lite is now rolling out to Hardware 3 early access customers, with broader rollout expected over the following few weeks. The build distills the driving behavior from Hardware 4's V14 series into the camera and compute configuration of Hardware 3. It includes destination options and speed profiles on city roads and, most importantly per the notes, significantly improved safety. Elon Musk congratulated the AI team on X, noting that Hardware 3 has only approximately 15% of the effective memory bandwidth of Hardware 4, making this a technically difficult achievement.

2. **V14 Lite Limitations and Missing Features:** V14 Lite for Hardware 3 will not include the MadMax speed profile, the FSD stats app in the car UI, FSD streak counters or celebrations, or the unified smart summon model. Automatic pulling over for emergency vehicles is listed as TBD. Early reviews are described as very positive, and the host notes that Hardware 3 vehicles never received version 13, making this a significant generational leap.

3. **Hardware 3 Fleet Monetization Opportunity:** The host estimates there are somewhere between 3 and 4 million vehicles equipped with Hardware 3. As of recording, fewer than 1% of these vehicles have V14 Lite. He frames this as a meaningful future FSD subscription revenue opportunity, as FSD subscriptions are highly margin-accretive. He suggests Tesla may run another free 30-day FSD trial once the rollout broadens to drive subscription conversions.

## Market & Competition
1. **Waymo Ends Uber Partnership in Phoenix:** Waymo robotaxis are no longer available on Uber's ride-hail app in Phoenix, ending a three-year partnership. Waymo said the vehicles from the Uber pilot have been integrated into its own Phoenix fleet available through the Waymo app. Waymo's vehicles remain available on Uber in Austin and Atlanta, and the two companies are set to compete against each other in London later in 2026.

2. **Uber Teasing a New Autonomous Partner in Phoenix — Speculation Swirls:** Uber announced it is preparing to launch a new autonomous vehicle partnership in Phoenix but did not name the partner. Given the Tesla stock move on the same day, some are speculating Tesla is the unnamed partner. The host says he thinks the most likely candidate is Lucid and Mobileye (Nuro), noting Lucid builds vehicles in Arizona, has said its robotaxi will start operations later this year, and that Uber has already committed to adding approximately 20,000 Lucid vehicles to its fleet over the next few years. The host is skeptical Lucid and its partner will be meaningfully operational this year. He reiterates his long-standing view that Tesla does not need Uber — Uber needs Tesla.

3. **VW Reportedly Ending Autonomous Driving Partnership with Bosch:** Days after VW announced potential layoffs of 100,000 employees and closure of four plants, German reporting citing internal sources indicates VW plans to end its automated driving partnership with Bosch, ending a collaboration launched in 2022 through VW's software unit Cariad. Internal assessments reportedly found the technology was not yet competitive after approximately $1.7 billion invested. VW is said to be seeking a new hardware and software partner. Bosch and Cariad issued a statement saying they do not comment on market rumors. The host suggests the right answer would be Tesla, but speculates it will likely be Nvidia.

4. **Tesla Expands to Uruguay:** Tesla announced it will begin selling vehicles in Uruguay. The host notes light vehicle sales in Uruguay run approximately 6,000 units per month (~75,000/year), with BEVs having reached approximately 20% market share in 2025 (~13,500 BEVs annually). National policies and incentives are favorable toward EVs, though lower-priced Chinese EVs are already present in the market.

5. **NHTSA Closes Power Steering Loss Probe into Tesla:** NHTSA announced it has closed its engineering analysis into Tesla vehicles over power steering loss. The investigation, which began in 2023 and was upgraded to an engineering analysis, was closed following an over-the-air software recall Tesla issued in early 2025. Tesla stated the recall was not in response to NHTSA's investigation.

6. **Munro Live Involvement in Slate Auto:** Munro Live released a video over the weekend revealing it was "seemingly very involved" in the development of Slate Auto's vehicle platform. The host views this as a positive signal for Slate's ability to keep costs low and make sound engineering decisions early on.
