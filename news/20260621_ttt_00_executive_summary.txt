## Autonomous Driving
- **Fatal crash in Katy, TX under Autopilot investigation:** Around 8 p.m. on June 20, a Tesla failed to make a right turn, drove through the front wall of a home, and killed a 76-year-old woman inside. The 44-year-old driver told Harris County deputies the car was on Autopilot; that claim remains unverified. No charges have been filed.
- **NHTSA engineering analysis underway:** An October 2025 probe into approximately 2.9 million Tesla vehicles over FSD running red lights and wrong-way driving was upgraded to an engineering analysis in March 2026 — the final step before a potential recall. A separate NHTSA investigation into Tesla's alleged failure to report Autopilot and FSD crashes remains open.
- **FSD v14 end-of-June deadline questioned:** A passing mention in Herbert Ong's SpaceX discussion noted Tesla promised FSD v14 "light" to owners by end of June 2026; the host added "none of us are holding our breath on that one." Per a viewer report, FSD 14.2.26 in Belgium produced "multiple serious issues and many takeovers" over ~200 km. Bhakdi says v14.3.4 represents the biggest leap so far and should materially improve performance.
- **10,000-mile FSD streak milestone:** Owner @DavidMoss became the first to cross 10,000 continuous hands-free miles on Tesla's FSD streak counter — equivalent to 40% of the way around Earth — a qualitative proof point of FSD maturity, though no aggregate fleet safety data accompanied it.

## Robotaxi
- **Deployment, not accumulation, is the stock catalyst:** Bhakdi acknowledges Cybercabs are being spotted across Houston, Austin, Nevada, and Florida, signaling serious production scaling, but says Wall Street will not reward Tesla for undeployed inventory. He believes deployment of even ~50 units could send Tesla to $490 and still expects this possible in June 2026, though he admits his June thesis has so far been wrong.
- **Dedicated service infrastructure expected:** Bhakdi says existing Tesla service centers lack capacity for robotaxi maintenance and expects Tesla to establish dedicated service centers, pointing to a Dallas real estate acquisition as likely evidence of this buildout.

## Humanoid Bots
- **Optimus v3 reveal targeted for ~late July 2026:** Bhakdi notes Tesla guided to an Optimus Version 3 reveal roughly one month out (from June 21), but flags that if a merger vote is in motion simultaneously, Tesla may be forced to push the reveal into 2027. He and a viewer agree Optimus supply chain is at least 60% China-sourced, possibly higher — a risk in a merger scenario given ITAR constraints.
- **Cortex 2 designated "Optimus University":** Per FutureAzA's guest John, Cortex 2 in Austin is already partially operational and is expected to serve as the primary training cluster for Optimus, running alongside Cortex 1's FSD training workloads. Humanoid robot scaling is characterized as a driver of effectively infinite chip demand for "decades and decades."

## Energy
- **"Megapod" trademark filed under Tesla:** Tesla filed a trademark for "Megapod" — a concept for micro-scale AI GPU compute units deployed at existing Supercharger sites, leveraging existing electrical permits to bypass multi-year data center permitting delays. Bhakdi argues this could bring gigawatts of compute online faster than centralized competitors including Project Stargate. He flags that the filing was made under Tesla rather than SpaceX as a possible signal that a merger is imminent. He estimates that deploying a few gigawatts of distributed compute could double, triple, or quadruple Tesla's current ~$3.5–4B annual free cash flow.

## Market & Competition
- **Terafab and Intel partnership:** Tesla's announced Terafab domestic chip fab in Texas — co-locating raw material ingestion through wafer fabrication, etching, testing, and packaging — aims to compress chip iteration cycles from roughly one year to a much shorter loop. The Intel partnership targets a non-EUV, sub-5nm process node, which the FutureAzA guest characterized as a "Hail Mary" that, if successful, would be transformative. SpaceX is sharing costs, funded in part by its recent capital raise. Samsung and TSMC will continue building Tesla chips in the near term while Terafab is constructed; the guest expressed significant skepticism about the fab being operational anywhere near one year.
- **SpaceX–Tesla merger thesis dominates weekend discourse:** Both Bhakdi and Herbert Ong published detailed analyses converging on a late-July to mid-August 2026 announcement window, timed around Q2 earnings (estimated ~August 11). Bhakdi's proposed structure involves SpaceX acquiring Tesla at $600/share (~$1.88T enterprise value) with a ratchet mechanism ensuring Tesla shareholders always receive proportional upside — without which he argues Tesla holders would not vote yes. The combined entity would imply a ~$4 trillion valuation. Bhakdi is publishing detailed merger math on June 22.
- **SpaceX share unlock schedule as Tesla relative-value trade:** Bhakdi argues SpaceX faces severe selling pressure beginning August 11, 2026, when the first major unlock adds ~20% to float (a 400% increase in sellers), with further tranches through October 25. He is tactically selling SpaceX calls at $210–$250 strikes and says he cannot see buying SpaceX before November 2026. His core argument: Tesla offers better relative value and direct merger upside at current prices.
- **Key structural risk — ITAR:** Merging a top US defense contractor (SpaceX) with a company producing ~one-third of its cars in Shanghai would likely trigger regulatory demands from both US and Chinese authorities. Herbert Ong calls this the single biggest reason deal close likely slips into 2027 even if an announcement comes this summer.
- **Rivian FSD skepticism:** FutureAzA's guest characterized Rivian's current driver-assistance as equivalent to Tesla's Autopilot from ~five years ago and expressed serious doubt that Rivian can reach FSD-level capability by end of 2027, citing insufficient training data and no visible large-scale compute infrastructure.

## Financials

The Q2 report is expected around July 2, 2026. Bhakdi's guest on the Herbert Ong stream also flagged an expectation of a strong energy (Megapack) quarter as a meaningful positive catalyst.

He describes a prior aggressive in-the-money short-put strategy as a mistake that cost him.
