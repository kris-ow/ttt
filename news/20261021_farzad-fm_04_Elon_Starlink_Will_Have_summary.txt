This short clip summarizes comments from SpaceX leadership on an earnings call about Starlink's planned bandwidth expansion, tying it to Starship launch capacity and an AI-driven demand thesis for satellite connectivity.

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **100x bandwidth target in ~3 years:** A SpaceX executive said Starlink aims to deliver roughly 100 times its current bandwidth within about 3 years, achieved through satellites with roughly 10x more bandwidth each and roughly 10x more satellites in orbit — reaching an estimated 100,000 satellites.
2. **Demand could scale 1,000x, driven by AI agents:** The executive said Starlink demand could grow a thousandfold within 3–5 years, attributing this to AI agents consuming significantly more data traffic than individual human users and eventually outnumbering people; he expects Starlink revenue to scale with the volume of data transferred.

## SpaceX — Starship
1. **Starship flight 14 delivered 26 terabits/sec of Starlink capacity, half-loaded:** The 14th Starship flight delivered 26 terabits per second of Starlink bandwidth to orbit, which the executive said is equivalent to about 10 Falcon 9 launches — despite the ship carrying only half its Starlink satellite payload capacity, underscoring Starship's role as the vehicle for scaling toward the 100,000-satellite target.
