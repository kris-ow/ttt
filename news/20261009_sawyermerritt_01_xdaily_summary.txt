## Executive Summary
Today's digest is dominated by SpaceX's Starlink Mobile push into the U.S. wireless carrier market (spectrum acquisition triggering a 7% after-hours drop in Verizon/AT&T/T-Mobile stock) and American Airlines' expanded Starlink fleet-wide rollout. On the Tesla side, the FSD (Supervised) rebrand to "Tesla Assisted Driving" in Europe, a potential ninth EU country approval (Slovakia), a new Optimus tactile-sensor patent, and White House recognition of Elon Musk (National Medal of Science) round out a moderately bullish news day with no major negative signals.

---

## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Starlink Mobile's carrier ambitions rattle telecom incumbents:** SpaceX announced it has acquired nationwide 800 MHz spectrum licenses, a move described as bringing Starlink Mobile closer to becoming a "full-fledged mobile carrier" in the U.S. The news reportedly sent Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile shares down 7% after-hours. SpaceX pushed back on AT&T's CEO's claim that Starlink Mobile "won't be able to penetrate walls," saying the new 800 MHz spectrum will enable better indoor coverage, including signal penetration through walls and obstacles. Separately, SpaceX introduced a new Starlink referral program paying both referrer and referee $100 cash (or a service credit alternative) after two months of subscription, a customer-acquisition incentive.

2. **American Airlines dramatically expands Starlink fleet rollout:** American Airlines officially announced it will install Starlink across its entire mainland fleet of 1,030+ aircraft, up from a previously announced 500, with installations beginning in early 2027 using Starlink's Performance Aero Terminal. This adds to a growing list of major U.S. carriers that have announced Starlink adoption (American, Southwest, United, Frontier, Alaska/Hawaiian), while Delta and JetBlue have not yet announced adoption — a notable competitive gap in the in-flight connectivity market.

3. **Tesla rebrands FSD (Supervised) to "Tesla Assisted Driving" in Europe:** Tesla has officially renamed FSD (Supervised) to "Tesla Assisted Driving" ("TAD") across all of Europe, following discussions with Germany's Transport Ministry; the change is intended to help secure EU-wide regulatory approval. Separately, Slovakia's Transport Minister Jozef Raz said the country expects to approve Tesla's FSD (Supervised) "within days," which would make Slovakia the ninth EU country to approve the feature; Raz cited data showing the cars reduce accident rates.

4. **Elon Musk receives National Medal of Science; Trump praises Tesla/SpaceX:** At a White House Science & Tech Innovation Summit, President Trump awarded Elon Musk the National Medal of Science, the nation's highest honor for scientific and technological achievement, calling him an "industrial titan" and stating SpaceX "saved NASA from" reliance on other countries' rockets (per posted transcript excerpts). Trump also credited Tesla as having produced "the first commercially viable electric cars with the first consumer self-driving technology." Musk is also set to appear virtually at the @theXtakeover event this Saturday for a live-streamed interview.

5. **Optimus tactile sensor patent and robotaxi night/rain performance:** A newly published Tesla patent application describes a 3D tactile touch sensor for a humanoid robot, intended to let Optimus detect touch, pressure, and force across its hands to improve grip and object manipulation. Separately, a post highlighted (with video, no specific metrics given) that Tesla's vision-only robotaxis reportedly perform well driving at night during heavy rain, pushing back on earlier skepticism about camera-only systems in adverse conditions.

6. **Energy and charging infrastructure updates:** Tesla's Virtual Power Plant program in California has seen Powerwall owners collectively send a cumulative 5 GWh of electricity back to the grid across 10 major VPP events in 2026, described as enough to power roughly 1.2 million U.S. homes (unit basis not specified — likely a brief peak-demand window, not continuous). Tesla also opened its first Semi truck Megacharger station on the U.S. East Coast, located in Georgia, featuring 8 charging stalls each capable of up to 1.2 MW (1,200 kW).

7. **Tesla Roadster unveil date set:** A Tesla Roadster unveil is scheduled for one week from the post date (around 2026-10-15), per Sawyer Merritt; no further product details were given.
