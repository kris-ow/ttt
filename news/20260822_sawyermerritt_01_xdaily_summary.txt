## Key Posts / Themes

3. **FSD mileage milestones:** Tesla owners in South Korea have cumulatively driven over 50 million kilometers (31 million miles) on FSD (Supervised). Globally, Tesla FSD cumulative miles have surpassed 13.6 billion miles.

Separately, Zijin Longking in China rolled out what's described as the largest pure electric mining truck, with a 1,400kWh battery, 90% charge in 25 minutes, 350-ton gross weight, and 230-ton payload capacity. These illustrate broadening EV competition across passenger and industrial vehicle segments but have no direct Tesla financial impact.
