## Brief

- Tesla launches unsupervised Robotaxi in Miami, no safety driver
- Waymo suffers gridlock, vandalism failures in SF over July 4th
- Cybercab sightings surge across Dallas, Tampa, Jacksonville, New Orleans
- Musk: Optimus 3 in "final stages," production starts this summer
- Tesla Q2 deliveries hit 480,000, beating estimates of 406,000
- Tesla files "Megapod" trademark for AI data-center hardware

## Robotaxi
- Miami Robotaxi service went live driverless, with Ashok Elluswamy confirming full unsupervised operation; riders reported smooth ride quality but a slower, less efficient route than personal FSD, and fleet presence appeared extremely thin (one vlogger estimated only "one, maybe two or three" cars active in his area).
- Cybercab production continues ramping at Giga Texas — over 100 units reportedly spotted in outbound lots — but Musk has said Robotaxi "likely will not see material revenue until at least 2027," with safety validation on rewritten FSD v15 (targeted late 2026/early 2027) cited as the bottleneck, not mapping.
- Austin's fleet remains small: city officials cite ~50 vehicles, with Electrek's own tracking showing the unsupervised subset down from a peak of ~25 cumulative cars to ~14 active — a contraction, not the reduction reflected in RobotaxiTracker.com's stale figures.
- New NHTSA rule change removing steering-wheel/pedal requirements is being read by one commentator as regulatory readiness ahead of wider Cybercab deployment.
- Tesla showcased Cybercab accessibility features (braille controls, wheelchair-height seating) at the National Federation of the Blind's Austin convention.
- Anticipation is building around a Tuesday Giga Texas/Austin event teased by VP Lars Moravy, with speculation ranging from a scaling blueprint to a possible new vehicle concept — unconfirmed.

## Humanoid Bots
- Tesla VP Grace Tao said at a Beijing conference that Optimus will enter large-scale mass production by end of 2026, with a long-term annual capacity target of 1 million units at a planned "Freeman" factory.
- Musk (~June 20 remarks) called Optimus 3 the "most advanced robot in the world," said production starts this summer on a slow S-curve, with high-volume output likely around next summer and Optimus 4 design finalized in 2027.
- Leaked background footage in an official Tesla video reportedly shows an already-installed Optimus production line with workers assembling robot hands, per Sawyer Merritt spotting — read by one commentator as evidence production readiness is closer than markets assume.
- Google DeepMind announced a partnership with humanoid startup Apptronik, feeding Apollo 2 real-world data into Gemini robotics — a competitive data point, though dismissed by one Tesla-bullish commentator as no threat.

## Market & Competition
- Waymo suffered a high-profile operational failure in San Francisco over July 4th weekend: vehicles froze at intersections, some required 3-4 hour tows after dead batteries, and some were vandalized or set on fire amid holiday congestion; separately, ~20 Waymo vehicles reportedly gridlocked near a fireworks display and other Waymos entered freeway construction zones, triggering a recall.
- Florida Governor DeSantis publicly accused the New York Times of an anti-Elon narrative after a Texas Autopilot crash story was reframed as driver-caused (intentional override, since charged with manslaughter); DeSantis said Florida is "reviewing possibilities" for adding Teslas to state fleets.
- South Korea's Kospi crashed ~8% Thursday, spilling into US tech/AI-infrastructure names including Tesla-adjacent suppliers; one commentator argues this — not fundamentals — drove Tesla's post-delivery-beat sell-off.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla Q2 deliveries came in at 480,000, beating Wall Street's 406,000 estimate and most independent tracker projections.
- Model Y L launched domestically in the US after success in China and Europe; Launch Series units spotted at Giga Texas with new Cosmic Silver/Zen Grey trim options.
- UK EV market surged 30%, with Tesla UK sales up 42% year-over-year.
- California's new $3,500 EV rebate applies a standard price cap to Tesla (no longer CA-headquartered) while Rivian and Lucid get a higher cap; roughly half of Tesla's lineup still qualifies.

## Autonomous Driving
- A viral video showed a Tesla driver asleep at ~100 km/h on a BC highway with two children in the car; Tesla's camera-based monitoring failed because the driver wore sunglasses, reverting to the weaker steering-wheel-torque nag.
- Tesla's supervised FSD fleet is crashing at roughly four times the average human rate, per Electrek, with a string of Austin crashes reported to NHTSA.
- Investigation into a fatal Michael Butler crash reportedly shows the driver overrode FSD (accelerator to 100%, no braking) after searching how to make FSD "less timid" — undermining claims that FSD caused the crash.

## Financials
- Tesla filed a US trademark for "Megapod" (June 18, 2026) — modular AI data-center hardware — with no product, price, or ship date yet; framed by one analyst as Tesla shifting from AI compute buyer to seller.
- Cathie Wood said a Tesla-SpaceX merger isn't imminent, believing Musk wants Tesla's robotaxi story to prove out first; a guest commentator countered a merger could land between now and mid-2027.

## Energy
- Tesla signed a deal with Nat Power for 25 GWh of Megapack storage across Italy/UK (phase one), part of a program exceeding 100 GWh and worth over $15 billion lifetime — phase one alone exceeds half of Tesla's global battery deployments in 2026 to date.
