## Brief

## Robotaxi
- Tesla's Robotaxi network now spans five regions — Miami, Dallas, Houston, Austin, and Bay Area (as Ride Hail) — with Miami launching unsupervised and Ashok Elluswamy confirming full driverless operation. Rider footage showed driverless Model Ys handling heavy rain; Miami's geofence remains much smaller than Waymo's.
- A vlogger (investingagainstthegrain) rode Miami Robotaxi with no safety driver and found it smooth but slow-routed, arriving ~10-11 minutes behind his personal FSD on the same trip; he estimated only a handful of vehicles are active in the area and flagged awkward pickup logistics and an under-8 age restriction.
- Electrek reports Austin's city-cited fleet at roughly 50 vehicles, with the unsupervised portion falling from a peak of ~25 to ~14 active cars — a contraction, not growth. Austin's operating geofence covers only ~20 cars despite metro-wide mapping.
- Cybercab production is ramping at Giga Texas (first unit rolled off in February, continuous production confirmed on the Q1 call) with Musk calling it a "stretched out S-curve" going "exponential towards the end of the year," but Musk also said Robotaxi "likely will not see material revenue until at least 2027" pending FSD v15 (targeted late 2026/early 2027).
- New Cybercab/testing vehicles were spotted in Dallas, Tampa, Jacksonville, and New Orleans (a non-launch city), plus unprotected-left-turn and passenger drop-off testing in Florida — read by some commentators as staging for a faster nationwide rollout.
- Tesla showcased Cybercab accessibility features (braille controls, wheelchair-height seating, service-animal space) at the National Federation of the Blind's Austin convention.
- Anticipation is building around a Tuesday Giga Texas/Austin event teased by VP Lars Moravy; multiple hosts speculate it concerns Cybercab fleet entry or a broader announcement, but details are unconfirmed.

## Autonomous Driving
- A viral video showed a Tesla driver asleep at ~100 km/h on B.C.'s Trans-Canada Highway with two children aboard; Electrek notes Tesla's camera-based monitoring (since FSD v12.4) fails to activate when a driver wears sunglasses, per Tesla's own manual, reverting to the weaker steering-wheel-torque nag.
- Vehicle data in the fatal Michael Butler crash reportedly shows the driver pressed the accelerator to 100% approaching a stop sign while FSD was slowing the car, with no brake applied; search history reportedly showed the driver had searched for FSD being "not aggressive enough."
- Electrek cites Tesla's supervised fleet crashing at roughly four times the rate of an average human driver, with a string of Austin crashes reported to NHTSA.

## Humanoid Bots
- Tesla China VP Grace Tao reportedly said at a Beijing conference that Optimus will enter large-scale mass production by end of 2026, with a long-term target of 1 million units/year at a planned "Freeman" factory.
- Musk (~June 20 remarks) called Optimus 3 in "final stages of completion," the most advanced robot in the world, with production starting this summer, a slow initial S-curve, and high-volume output around next summer; Optimus 4 design finalizes next year.
- Leaked background footage in an official Tesla video reportedly shows an installed Optimus production line with workers assembling robot hands, per one commentator's analysis (unconfirmed as a formal disclosure).

## Market & Competition
- Waymo suffered a high-profile SF operational failure over July 4th weekend: vehicles froze at intersections, some had dead batteries requiring 3-4 hour tows, and some were vandalized or burned amid holiday crowds; Waymo still operates under 4,000 cars after roughly six years.
- Florida Governor DeSantis accused the New York Times of an anti-Elon narrative after a previously reported Autopilot crash was revealed to involve a driver who intentionally caused it; DeSantis said Florida is "reviewing possibilities" for adding Teslas to state fleets.
- Google DeepMind announced a partnership with humanoid startup Apptronik, feeding Apollo 2 platform data into Gemini robotics efforts.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla reported Q2 deliveries of 480,000, beating Wall Street's 406,000 estimate and most community trackers; stock reaction was muted ("sell the news").
- Model Y L launched in the US after success in China and Europe, seen as a meaningful product event given consumer demand for larger vehicles.
- UK EV market rose 30% with Tesla UK sales up 42% year-over-year; California introduced a $3,500 EV rebate that most of Tesla's lineup qualifies for, though Rivian and Lucid get a higher price cap as CA-headquartered firms.

## Financials
- Tesla filed a US trademark (June 18, 2026) for "Megapod," described as modular AI data-center hardware — an intent-to-use filing with no product, price, or ship date, feeding speculation Tesla could become an AI-compute seller.
- Cathie Wood said a Tesla-SpaceX merger isn't imminent, believing Musk wants the robotaxi story to become clear to investors first; other commentators argued a merger could come sooner, with no consensus.

## Energy
