## Financials
- SpaceX Q3 revenue of $14.6 billion beat Wall Street's ~$12.7 billion estimate, with net income of $4.4 billion versus a $1.1 billion loss a year ago; adjusted EBITDA hit $8.8 billion, up 306% YoY. Operating profit swung from a $143 million loss last quarter to $2.8 billion this quarter.
- Capex totaled $19.6 billion in Q3, mostly AI compute buildout; Q4 capex guidance was raised to $35–40 billion. Backlog stood at $63 billion, with ~$42 billion expected recognized within a year.
- SpaceX reiterated a goal of $100 billion annualized run-rate revenue by December and issued Q1 2027 revenue guidance of $33–37 billion.
- Tesla reports Q3 2026 earnings Wednesday after market close. Consensus calls for $28.06 billion revenue (roughly flat YoY) and $0.45 non-GAAP EPS (down 10% YoY), per Electrek — though Tesla missed Q2 consensus badly ($0.33 vs. $0.53 expected) as operating margin fell to 1.4%.
- Tesla deployed 486,532 vehicles in Q3 (down 2.1% YoY but ahead of the 461,974 consensus) while building only 464,391 — a second straight quarter of working down inventory. Energy storage deployments of 13.7 GWh missed the ~15.9 GWh estimate.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX's AI/compute ("superintelligence") revenue grew 830% YoY, from $813 million to $7.6 billion, swinging from a $1.7 billion loss to $431 million operating profit. Compute capacity nearly tripled to 1.7 GW over 12 months, with ~2.2 GW expected by end of October.
- Musk said SpaceX is on pace to bring ~200,000–220,000 GPUs online monthly through year-end, is "highly confident" of adding 10 incremental GW in 2027 (~13 GW total), and sees a rising but small chance of exceeding 20 GW. He cited turbine casting capacity — repurposed from rocket-engine manufacturing — as a 12–18 month advantage over competitors.
- Grokbot reached 800,000 paid weekly users this month, more than doubling in a month; Musk called its growth the fastest he's seen for any product, and enterprise customers over $1M run-rate grew 50% QoQ.
- Terafab chip plans: a functional test chip targeted for May/June 2027 at an Austin research lab, with aspirational volume production around Q3 2028 — targets Musk explicitly flagged as uncertain with "big error bars."

## SpaceX — Starship
- Flight 14 reached orbit for the first time and deployed operational Starlink V3 satellites. Flight 15, expected next month, will attempt to catch both booster and ship — a first full recovery of an orbital launch vehicle if successful; Musk called it "more likely than not."
- SpaceX announced a new Louisiana spaceport, potentially with 20+ launch towers; Shotwell cited an internal goal of first launch there in ~2028, with Musk calling full-scale operations by 2029 "highly likely."
- Cadence target: weekly Starship flights in H1 2027, twice-weekly or more by end of 2027. Q3 included 35 total launches across Falcon 9, Falcon Heavy, and Starship, including six national-security missions.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Connectivity revenue hit $5.2 billion, up 69% YoY; consumer ARPU rose to $69/month. Subscribers reached roughly 13.6–14 million (sources vary), adding 1.6 million in Q3; Starlink Mobile topped 22 million monthly users, up 37% QoQ.
- Starlink is now contracted on ~12,000 aircraft (~25% of addressable fleet); FCC approved 15,000 Mobile V2 satellite launches as SpaceX pushes toward becoming a major US mobile carrier, targeting direct-to-cell service in early 2028.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Musk confirmed special committees of independent directors at both companies are discussing a possible SpaceX-Tesla merger, with Musk excluded as a conflicted party; no terms have been agreed. He called it "overstating it" to call a merger certain, though he personally believes combining the companies "would be a good idea," and noted Tesla's side needs 50% of all outstanding shares to approve — a "high hurdle."
- Musk said he'll gift one SpaceX share to every American child next year (~70 million accounts) via Trump accounts, calling it "the first of many."
- Tesla's December 3 AGM proxy contains no merger vote — only board reelection, say-on-pay, and auditor ratification. Analysts and hosts now expect any merger announcement, if it comes, in 2027.

## Robotaxi
- Tesla's registered robotaxi fleet in Texas reached 803 vehicles, up 34 recently (254 added in October alone); operating hours reportedly expanded to 6 a.m.–midnight.
- Top shareholder questions ahead of Tesla's earnings call centered on Cybercab's regulatory path amid an open NHTSA safety review and unsupervised FSD timing — the two highest-weighted questions by shares represented.

## Autonomous Driving
- A US appeals court threw out class-action certification in a California lawsuit alleging Tesla misled buyers about FSD capabilities, citing too many individualized factual questions. This is a procedural win for Tesla; the case continues on an individual basis, and the ruling does not address the underlying merits.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla's new Roadster drive unit delivers 1 megawatt of power (~33% more than Model S Plaid) at half the mass, using new 4680 cells with updated chemistry, per Tesla.
- Media criticism of the Roadster reveal centers on a 2028 production target after ~9 years of delays, reduced range (~300–318 miles vs. the original ~620-mile promise), loss of rear seats, and no disclosed pricing or 0-60 figures.

## Market & Competition
