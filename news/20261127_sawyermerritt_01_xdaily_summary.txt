## Executive Summary
Thanksgiving-eve posts centered on three items. Tesla's Cybercab registrations in Texas set a single-day record and passed 1,000 in total. Tesla's U.S. homepage now features FSD V15. ## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Record Cybercab registrations cross 1,000:** Citing Texas DMV data, Sawyer reports that Tesla registered a record 285 Cybercabs for its Robotaxi fleet in a single day. That is the largest single-day increase ever and brings cumulative registrations to 1,107, the first time the total has crossed 1,000. The post was the most-engaged of the day (4.5K likes, 413 reposts). Registrations show vehicles being readied for the fleet. They are not the same as vehicles actively in service, so the figure is a leading indicator of Cybercab scale-up rather than a count of deployed robotaxis.

2. **FSD V15 takes the spotlight on Tesla.com:** Tesla updated its U.S. website homepage to feature FSD V15. This prominent marketing placement suggests Tesla is leaning into the new version as a selling point heading into the holiday period.

3. **Musk's SpaceX share gift to American children:** Elon Musk announced that one SpaceX share will be gifted each year to each of the roughly 70 million children in America. Sawyer puts the cost at over $9 billion per year at SpaceX's current share price, and notes the gifts' value would rise with the stock. A follow-up post frames the shares as being donated by both Musk and the company. The poster did not specify the split between Musk's personal shares and company-issued shares. Investors may want to watch for details on the funding mechanism and any dilution implications for SPCX holders.
