## Market & Competition
1. **Rivian R1T pricing and condition:** The featured used 2023 R1T quad-motor with roughly 40,000 miles is priced at about $55,000, which the hosts estimate is roughly half its original new price; one host calls it "the best $130,000 truck you can buy for $80,000" given how Rivian is described as losing money on each vehicle.
2. **Charging speed criticized versus battery size:** One host recalls a prior test of the R1S Max Pack (battery size estimated around 130 kWh) where a charging stop at an older 150 kW station took 38 minutes and only reached about 72%, calling this "grossly disappointing" and arguing Rivian is pursuing bigger batteries rather than aerodynamic efficiency to achieve range.
3. **Company survival and autonomy strategy questioned:** One host says he isn't confident Rivian will survive long-term or maintain adequate service center coverage, and separately says he is "less enthusiastic" about Rivian's approach to autonomy, noting the R1T isn't designed with autonomy as a priority.
4. **Tesla and Cybertruck comparisons made only in passing:** One host says the R1T's ride quality is "not quite Cybertruck good" but is on par with the Ford F-150 Lightning; another host notes Rivian's exterior design, once considered unusual, "is now the very conservative one" after seeing the Cybertruck, and separately says the R1T's driving dynamics feel like what he wants "as a Tesla driver."
5. **Practical truck tradeoffs versus gas trucks:** The hosts note EV trucks like the R1T offer little cab/bed size configurability compared to gas trucks (e.g., Tacoma, Ram), and that the R1T's roughly 4.5-foot bed is smaller due to its quad-cab configuration; they also note the R1T isn't commonly seen in contractor use, unlike cargo vans.
