## Electric Vehicles

1. **Tesla Semi Efficiency and Pilot Results:** During the broadcast, the hosts discussed Tesla Semi 2025 pilot data showing an average energy efficiency of 1.55 kWh per mile, improved from a previously published figure of approximately 1.7 kWh/mile and significantly better than the Daimler eCascadia Freightliner at 2.1 kWh/mile. Driver feedback from the pilot was described as positive, including on demanding routes such as the 7,200-foot climb over Donner Pass. The hosts noted a break-even cost threshold of approximately $300,000 for commercial fleet operators to prefer electric semis. One host mentioned the Tesla Semi has been running between Giga Nevada and Fremont for at least five years.

2. **Tesla Semi European Expansion Planned:** The hosts discussed a job posting for a Tesla Semi sales position in the European market as evidence that Tesla plans to manufacture the Semi at Giga Berlin. The 4680 battery cell production line equipment is currently being installed at Giga Berlin, suggesting Semi production there could begin within one to two years. Right-hand-drive adaptation was noted to be irrelevant for semi trucks. The hosts estimated it would be a minimum of three years before a production Tesla Semi is seen operating in the UK.

3. **FSD in Europe — Slow Progress:** A Tesla manager in the UK informally told one host in February 2026 that FSD regulatory clearance in the UK was expected in March 2026, but as of the broadcast date (June 2026) there is still no approval. Several EU countries — including Belgium, Estonia, Lithuania, Denmark, and Sweden — have individually approved FSD on a temporary basis. Concern was raised that if the EU as a whole does not approve FSD at an upcoming meeting, those individual approvals could time out after six months, though Grok's assessment suggested countries that approved individually would likely maintain access regardless of an EU-wide vote. The hosts noted that Germany's domestic automakers (BMW, Mercedes) have no competitive autonomous driving product, giving Tesla and Chinese manufacturers a structural advantage if autonomy is permitted.

4. **Cybertruck Mentioned as Popular in Brownsville/Starbase Area:** The hosts joked that SpaceX employee millionaires would rush to buy more Cybertrucks, but that the Starbase/Brownsville area already has so many that an increase wouldn't be noticeable.
