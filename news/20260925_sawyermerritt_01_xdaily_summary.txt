## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla Semi factory opens in Sparks, Nevada; large fleet customers reportedly taking delivery:** Tesla held a countdown to a livestream rollout event (9 PM ET) celebrating the opening of its new Semi factory in Sparks, Nevada, which the poster states has installed capacity to produce 50,000 Tesla Semis per year. Separately, the poster said it "looks like" DHL, PepsiCo, and US Foods would be taking delivery of a new Tesla Semi the next day, noting each has placed large orders — this is a forward-looking expectation from the poster rather than a confirmed delivery report. Tesla also added four new Semi-branded apparel items to its online shop (tees, hat, kids' shirt), a minor but timed-to-launch merchandising signal. The factory lobby was also shown in a photo post.

2. **FSD (Supervised) regulatory approval expands in Europe; Ireland approval in progress:** Tesla announced FSD (Supervised) received regulatory approval in the Czech Republic, making it the seventh European market to approve the feature. Tesla also said it is "working with authorities" in Ireland toward approval there, indicating that market's approval is pending, not yet granted.

3. **Cybercab spotted in Devon, Pennsylvania:** A post shows roughly 35 Cybercabs in Devon, PA (about a 30-minute drive from Philadelphia) — a location outside Tesla's currently known active robotaxi operating cities (Bay Area, Austin, Dallas, Houston per RobotaxiTracker.com), suggesting staging, transport, or testing activity rather than an active local robotaxi launch. No further detail on purpose was given.

4. **Wells Fargo issues bullish Starlink subscriber and revenue forecast:** Wells Fargo projects SpaceX will reach 47 million Starlink subscribers by 2028, with Starlink revenue growing from $19 billion at the end of 2026 to $30 billion by end-2027 and $51 billion by end-2028, per the poster relaying the bank's forecast. This is an analyst projection, not a company-confirmed figure.

5. **Starlink commercial and consumer expansion:** Starlink announced select seafaring users will get gigabit speeds by December 2026. Separately, Vietnamese low-cost carrier Vietjet partnered with Starlink for free in-flight Wi-Fi, initially on 120 aircraft (100 Airbus A321/Boeing 737, 20 Airbus A330), with plans to expand fleet-wide over time.

6. **Starship Flight 14 preview:** Starship's 14th flight, expected next week per the post, will carry 26 Starlink V3 satellites to orbit for the first time and is expected to meaningfully increase the overall Starlink constellation's capacity — though no specific capacity figure was given.
