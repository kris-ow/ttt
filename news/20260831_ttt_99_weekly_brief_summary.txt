## Brief

## Robotaxi
- Cybercab's commercial debut lands Thursday Sept 3 in Austin; Ashok Elluswamy posted "the street won't be the same anymore" and Musk replied "yes" when asked if Tesla is "about to flood Austin," though panelists caution scale is unconfirmed — estimates range from a token ~10 vehicles to "hundreds" within a month or two.
- Dallas robotaxi service area expanded 158% (roughly 31 to 80 square miles), the first expansion since April's launch, adding downtown Dallas — all rides remain unsupervised.
- Cybercab validation footprint reached 88 cities across 24 states (up from 56 cities 20 days earlier), with sightings now including Manhattan and Denver; Tesla is hiring AI safety operators in 36 US cities and began hiring vehicle operators in Frankfurt.
- Texas robotaxi registrations hit roughly 270 vehicles (all Model Y, no Cybercabs yet), up 89 in August alone; Tesla extended service hours to 6am–10pm across all six active cities.
- Nevada's 5,000-vehicle statewide permit (vs. Waymo's 1,000) is now confirmed in filings; Tesla executives reportedly indicated satisfaction with ~2,500 in-state by mid-2027.

## Autonomous Driving
- A Tesla on FSD 14.3.7 nearly struck an oncoming train at a crossing, manually braked by the driver — at least the third documented railroad-crossing failure in 2026, per Electrek, as NHTSA's FSD probe now covers ~2.9 million vehicles.
- A separate HW3 vehicle on FSD v14.1 Lite drove over a log at 32 mph without braking; a technical breakdown attributes both incident types to a known neural-net limitation detecting thin horizontal objects, described as an unsolved industry-wide problem, not Tesla-specific.
- Waymo published "10 AI lessons" arguing (without naming Tesla) that upgrading L2 driver-assist to full autonomy is a "false summit" and camera-only sensing can't deliver safe L4 — an explicit challenge to Tesla's approach, citing 200M+ autonomous miles versus Tesla's reported 380,000 unsupervised miles.
- Musk said Hardware 3 will "almost definitely" need a hardware upgrade for unsupervised FSD, leaving unresolved remedy questions for roughly 300,000 owners who bought FSD outright.
- Tesla's summer update bundles Grok voice control (climate, calls, mirrors, wipers) via ThinkFast 2.0, ranking #1 on the Artificial Analysis speech-to-speech index.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla raised Cybertruck prices up to 7% (Dual-Motor AWD to $74,990, Premium AWD to $84,990) even as H1 US registrations fell to 8,400 from 12,800 a year earlier, per S&P Global — commentators split between reading it as constrained supply versus a genuine demand signal.
- Tesla Semi's Sparks, NV factory is producing at volume; Einride confirmed it will take 75 of its 500 ordered trucks by end of 2026 (pulled forward from 2028) with the remainder by end-2027, alongside WattEV's 370-unit order — the two largest electric-truck orders on record. A Semi rollout event is set for September 24 at the Nevada factory.
- Model Y L customer test drives began at select US showrooms; Tesla discontinued the European Model Y Premium white interior in favor of "Zen Grey."

## Energy
- Tesla booked over $9B in new Megapack projects in six weeks (43+ GWh) — comparable to the ~49 GWh delivered over the prior 12 months.
- 4680 battery cost reduction is roughly 25% of the way to Tesla's ultimate target, per analyst Jordan Giesige, with performance on par with — not ahead of — competing high-nickel cells; strategic supply-chain risk is judged "mostly" addressed.
- Tesla opened its second public Megacharger station (Vernon, CA) as part of a planned ~46-station network by 2027.

## Humanoid Bots
- Giga Texas's Optimus factory went from bare dirt in March to a steel-and-roof structure in five months, with an estimated 6-8 months of construction remaining.
- Investor Jason Calacanis, after a factory visit, called Optimus hardware "one and a half generations" ahead of Chinese rivals and its "brain" roughly five generations ahead, predicting 1 billion units within a decade — his own projection, not company guidance.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX unveiled Starbase Louisiana, a ~14,000-acre, $100B Gulf Coast spaceport in Vermilion Parish targeting 30+ Starship flights/day and over a dozen towers; construction starts next year, first launch targeted 2029, with 3,000 direct jobs pledged under an $820M state incentive deal.
- Flight 13's ship was recovered intact off Christmas Island after 24 days adrift but will not be reflown; Flight 14, expected in early September, will instead attempt Starship's first full orbital insertion after the ship-catch attempt was delayed to "a few months" out.
- On-site propellant production at Starbase Louisiana is designed to avoid an estimated ~1,000 truckloads/day of logistics otherwise required — a figure Musk called "mostly correct."

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX and Nvidia are co-designing "Star Mind" orbital AI satellites on an adapted Vera Rubin NVL72 payload, targeting first launch Q4 2027; Colossus (Memphis) runs at ~1GW today (~770,000 GPUs), with Musk projecting >2GW by year-end and near 10GW by end of next year.
- OpenAI ended its partnership with Cursor following SpaceX's acquisition of it, citing inability to confirm SpaceX would honor its terms of service — though OpenAI models reportedly serve only ~5% of Cursor's traffic.
- SpaceX is building a US turbine-blade factory to accelerate natural-gas turbine deployment for AI datacenters by up to 18 months, per Musk.
- Grok 4.6 scored 61 on Artificial Analysis's intelligence index, ahead of a comparable OpenAI model, at $2/million input tokens versus $5.
