## Robotaxi

## Autonomous Driving
1. **NHTSA audit on Cybercab expected to fail — but on a slow timeline:** The host says NHTSA has opened an audit into Tesla's Cybercab, primarily over the requirement for human-operable brake pedals, and states his view (not sourced to any NHTSA statement) that Tesla has no chance of passing under current rules. He argues the audit likely won't conclude this year, giving time for a NHTSA-proposed rule change (proposed in June) — which would remove the brake-pedal requirement — to potentially be adopted before the audit concludes, which he sees as the most likely and most bullish outcome, expecting the rule adoption news around November–December.
2. **FSD version 15 already partially deployed in Cybercabs:** The host relays Ashok Elluswamy's confirmation that version 15 is already running in Cybercabs, pushing back on a commentator ("Herbert")'s claim that version 15 itself would be the biggest robotaxi catalyst, since it's already in use. Per a Grok research summary the host generated live, Ashok described roughly seven major improvement tracks for version 15 on a July 22nd update, with about 40% of those tracks merged into the build as of a September 11th update, and mentioned "24/7 robotics" as an upcoming track expected to merge next month or so.
3. **FSD Supervised expanding across small European countries:** FSD Supervised is now rolling out in Czech Republic, Slovenia, Lithuania, Estonia, Belgium, the Netherlands, and Denmark, though the host notes larger European markets like Germany are lagging (with some activity reportedly happening in Brandenburg).
