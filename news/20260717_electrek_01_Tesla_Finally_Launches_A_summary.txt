Tesla has launched a new "Balance Bike for Kids" on its online shop for $225, disappointing fans who have long asked for a Tesla e-bike.

## Market & Competition
1. **Product details:** The pedal-less, motorless balance bike is aimed at children aged 2 to 5, featuring a lightweight magnesium frame, an adjustable seat with five height levels, and Tesla branding (wordmark on the side, "T" logo on the front). Minimum leg length is listed at 13.7 inches with a maximum weight limit of 77 lbs; assembly tools are included.

2. **Pricing context:** At $225, the bike is priced above most balance bikes on the market ($50-$120 range) and at the high end of premium options like the Woom 1 ($249) and Strider 14x Sport ($220).

3. **Fan expectations vs. reality:** The article notes years of surveys and fan renderings calling for a Tesla e-bike ("Model B"), which Musk has both discouraged and occasionally encouraged over time, contrasting with competitors like BMW and Rivian's spinoff e-bike brand Also that have entered the e-bike market.

## Editorial Stance
The author frames the release as a letdown relative to long-standing fan demand for a real Tesla e-bike, and criticizes the pricing as excessive for a toddler's balance bike, calling it priced "right alongside the most expensive balance bikes on the market — and above what most parents should ever spend on one."
