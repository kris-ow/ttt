This episode of a Tesla-focused interview series covers a new FSD collision-avoidance safety feature, Croatia's approval of managed FSD in Europe, a postponed Roadster event, Anthropic's $2 trillion IPO filing and its implications for AI compute demand (including an Elon Musk reply), and an extended discussion with guest Jo Bhakdi on why TSLA shares have been falling despite Cybercab fleet growth.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **New FSD collision-avoidance feature reactivates system automatically:** A Tesla driver demonstrated a new automatic collision avoidance feature (reported to have appeared around FSD version 14.3.9/14.3.10) that re-engaged FSD mid-turn after the driver manually disabled it, preventing a curb strike. Tesla AI chief Ashok Elluswamy responded that "a guardian angel is always watching over you." The host notes the feature only requires an active FSD subscription, not FSD engaged, though he hopes it eventually works without a paid subscription too.
2. **Croatia approves managed FSD, EU vote pushed to December:** Croatia became the 16th country worldwide (8th in Europe) to approve FSD Supervised, following recent approvals in the Czech Republic and Slovenia. However, the EU-wide vote, previously expected around October 6, has been postponed to December — Elon Musk reportedly reacted with visible frustration. Separately, the European Transport Safety Council is calling on member states not to approve two specific FSD functions — a speed-offset feature allowing FSD to exceed posted limits, and a traffic-flow-speed matching feature — which Bhakdi characterizes as a partial objection rather than a full rejection. Bhakdi speculates, without evidence, that political and automotive-industry lobbying (e.g., Germany's stake in Volkswagen) may be contributing to delays behind the scenes.
3. **Xpeng also being tested for European approval:** Xpeng's autonomous driving system is reportedly undergoing testing in Europe alongside Tesla's. Bhakdi, who has called Xpeng the strongest true competitor to Tesla for over a year, argues it is more technologically formidable than BYD due to its end-to-end approach, proprietary chips, and free FSD offering to build data volume, though he believes Tesla holds greater political influence in the US and Europe.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab fleet and registrations growing quickly, per Pioneer Land tracker:** The host's own Cybercab tracker (via pioneerlands.org) shows registered Cybercabs in Texas and robotaxi sightings rising more than 50% in the past week, with roughly 50 vehicles deployed less than a month after launch. Bhakdi argues the ramp trajectory is now meaningfully faster than the comparable period roughly a year earlier and views scaling Cybercab production as the single most important metric of Tesla execution.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Roadster reveal event postponed to October 15 due to weather:** Tesla postponed its outdoor Roadster presentation, originally slated near end-September, to October 15 citing forecasted severe weather. Bhakdi says expectations for the event are so low ("everyone says it's complete nonsense") that he sees asymmetric upside risk — little chance of a negative stock reaction, with some speculative chance of a positive surprise (e.g., a drone-related reveal).

## Financials
1. **Why TSLA stock keeps falling despite positive fleet/FSD news:** Bhakdi attributes the recent decline primarily to a JPMorgan note suggesting Tesla deliveries would underperform, compounded by a lack of fresh buyer momentum and capital rotation into AI stocks (Nvidia, SpaceX) and Bitcoin. He describes Tesla's price action as characteristically "vertical" in both directions and says he expects strong resistance near the $360 level (TSLA closed at $352.84 on 2026-09-29), calling the stock "significantly undervalued" and predicting a move to $500–600 once a catalyst — likely Cybercab scaling or merger-related news — arrives, though the timing is unpredictable and could happen suddenly.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Anthropic files for $2 trillion IPO; comparison drawn to SpaceX valuation:** Anthropic filed IPO paperwork targeting a $2 trillion valuation, with the listing likely occurring after the US midterm elections (pushed back from an original expectation of September). Reported fiscal 2025 revenue is roughly $4.5–5 billion (Bhakdi notes this implies ~400x revenue at $2T), with an $8 billion operating loss, ~$7 billion in compute/infrastructure costs, $20 billion cash on hand, and roughly $518 billion in future cloud/infrastructure commitments disclosed in the filing. Bhakdi contrasts this with SpaceX's valuation (previously cited around $1.7 trillion), arguing SpaceX's valuation looks comparatively cheap given its scale and recent achievements, though he does not elaborate on the specific SpaceX milestone referenced (deferred to a future show).

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Elon Musk flags power/compute as the binding constraint for AI leaders; SpaceX positioned to supply capacity:** Responding to a claim that Anthropic and OpenAI will be significantly constrained next year without solved compute/power problems, and that SpaceX could become the most valuable AI company if Musk reaches a stated gigawatt-scale power target, Elon Musk replied "that's one possible outcome." Bhakdi (speculating, and noting Musk may have been referencing his own video) suggests SpaceX plans to offer both ground and orbital compute capacity for rent rather than hoarding it, and floats that orbital AI computing could become a major cost-relief factor by 2028, though he stresses this is his own inference rather than something Musk confirmed directly.

## Market & Competition
1. **AI model rankings shift; Musk moderates Grok timeline:** Per a ranking cited in the video, Anthropic's Opus 5.5 and OpenAI's latest model are currently rated top-tier, with Grok (versions 4.5–4.7) clustered just behind, and Google and Meta's models reportedly jumping sharply in ranking recently. Bhakdi notes Elon Musk has revised his public timeline — previously suggesting Grok 4.7 would surpass competitors, now saying Grok 5 is the version expected to catch up, targeting parity with Anthropic's current top model — which Bhakdi reads as increased caution/accuracy from Musk rather than overpromising.
