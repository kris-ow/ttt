## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Starbase, Louisiana officially announced — a $100B+ spaceport with massive scale ambitions:** SpaceX officially announced a new spaceport, "Starbase, Louisiana," near Pecan Island, described as a "$100+ billion" project. Construction is slated to start in 2027, with the first Starship launch from the site planned for 2029. The site is intended to support "thousands" of launches and, per one post, could bring "10,000" jobs to Louisiana, per Elon Musk's on-site comments. Musk also stated intent to make the site publicly visitable "like they do at Cape Canaveral." Renderings show multiple launch pads at the site. Separately, a report (based on apparent site plans) suggests SpaceX will build a "Terabay" at the Louisiana site with three large doors designed to support a cadence of "30 Starship flights per day," which the poster projects to "thousands per year" — this cadence figure is described as a forward design capacity, not a current or confirmed operational rate. Including Starbase Louisiana, SpaceX now reportedly has 17 Starship launch pads under development company-wide, which the poster calculates could eventually support a combined capacity of "15,000+ launches per year" — more than twice the total number of rocket launches in all of history, per the poster's framing. These are long-horizon infrastructure and capacity claims, not near-term operational figures.

2. **Starlink community discount tied to Starbase, Louisiana:** SpaceX introduced a 50% discount on Starlink Residential monthly subscriptions (with no upfront hardware costs) for new and existing customers in Vermilion Parish, Louisiana — the parish where Starbase, Louisiana will be built — framed as a community goodwill gesture tied to the new spaceport.

3. **Cybertruck price increase:** Tesla raised U.S. Cybertruck prices by up to 7%: Dual-Motor AWD rose to $74,990 (from $69,990, +7.1%), Premium AWD rose to $84,990 (from $79,990, +6.2%), while Cyberbeast stayed at $99,990. The poster notes this is the first price increase for the Premium AWD trim since its launch roughly two years ago, and highlights that early buyers who paid $60k for the Dual-Motor AWD got a considerably better deal, as the starting price is now $15k higher than that original price.

4. **Tesla Semi factory teaser ahead of Sept 24 event:** A new, larger Tesla Semi factory in Nevada — distinct from and much bigger than the smaller pilot line used for Tesla's 2022 Semi delivery event — will host the upcoming event on September 24. The poster states that when fully ramped, the new factory will be able to produce a stated annual unit figure, though the number is cut off in the source post and not fully captured here.

5. **Megapack installation video and other minor items:** Tesla released a new video detailing how Megapacks are installed and connected to the grid — an educational/marketing piece rather than a data disclosure. Also noted: a driverless Model Y robotaxi clip showing off-road-like behavior (light/humorous content, low analytical signal), and a Starlink-on-Starship Florida launch teaser with no new operational details.
