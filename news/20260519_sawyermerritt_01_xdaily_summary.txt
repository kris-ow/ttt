## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Elon Musk's 10-Year Autonomous Driving Forecast (High Signal):** In a live interview at the Samson International Smart Mobility Summit in Israel (conducted at 2 AM Texas time), Musk stated: *"10 years from now, probably 90% of all distance driven will be driven by the AI in a self-driving car. It will be quite a niche thing in 10 years to actually be driving your own car."* He added that AI already drives 90%+ of the miles he personally travels. The interview also covered Tesla's unsupervised robotaxi rollout, Starship V3, and Neuralink. This is a notable public restatement of Musk's long-term AV thesis at a high-visibility international venue, reinforcing the core bull case for Tesla's autonomous platform. The post drew 5K likes and 954 reposts, indicating strong viral reach on a significant claim.

2. **FSD V14.3.3 — Reduced Nag Frequency:** A post from Sawyer Merritt notes that FSD V14.3.3, part of Tesla's 2026 Spring Update, now provides over a minute before issuing a driver attention nag in some cases — a meaningful improvement in the hands-free driving experience. The same update introduces a **Blind Spot Warning Accent Lights** feature, where accent lights turn red on the side where a blind-spot object is detected when the turn signal is engaged, or when an approaching object is detected while parked. While these are incremental software refinements rather than regulatory milestones, continued nag-reduction is directionally relevant to the unsupervised FSD/robotaxi thesis.

3. **Bipartisan EV Road-Use Fee Proposal:** U.S. House lawmakers have introduced bipartisan legislation that would require EV owners to pay a **$130 annual fee** for road repairs, and **$35 annually** for some plug-in hybrid models. This is part of a five-year highway reauthorization bill. While framed as a fairness measure replacing gas-tax contributions, it represents a modest but real addition to EV ownership costs. For Tesla specifically, this is a minor demand headwind, though the per-vehicle dollar amount is small relative to total cost of ownership.

4. **OpenAI Trial Verdict & Appeal (Tangential):** The jury in the Elon Musk vs. OpenAI trial found Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, and OpenAI not liable on all claims, ruling that Musk's lawsuit was filed outside the statute of limitations. Musk announced he will appeal to the Ninth Circuit, with his lawyer Marc Toberoff characterizing the verdict as precedent-setting for charitable organizations. This has no direct Tesla operational or financial relevance, but ongoing legal distraction for Musk is worth monitoring for potential management attention implications.

5. **Ford Energy Storage Deal (Competitive Context):** Ford announced a five-year agreement to supply up to **20 GWh** of battery energy storage capacity to renewable developer EDF, with EDF able to procure up to **4 GWh of DC Block battery energy storage systems annually** through Ford's energy unit. This signals growing non-Tesla competition in the grid-scale energy storage market, a space where Tesla's Megapack currently holds a strong position.
