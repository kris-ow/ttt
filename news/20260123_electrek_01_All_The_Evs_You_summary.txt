This electrek roundup lists EV models from multiple automakers currently offering 0% financing deals in January 2026, including one brief mention of a Tesla offer alongside Chevrolet, Dodge, Ford, Kia, Subaru, and Volkswagen models.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Tesla Model Y Standard financing offer:** The article notes the Tesla Model Y Standard, a few months old, is offering 0% financing for up to 72 months through January 31. It states the model "isn't the runaway sales success Tesla might have hoped for," and notes Tesla saw its second consecutive full year of decline in EV deliveries in 2025, per the article.
2. **Other automakers' 0% financing deals:** Chevrolet is offering 0% financing for up to 60 months on the Equinox EV and Silverado EV; Dodge offers 0% for up to 72 months plus large discounts on the Charger; Ford offers 0% for 74 months plus $2,000 bonus cash on the Mustang Mach-E; Kia offers 0% for up to 72 months plus $3,500 bonus cash on the EV6 and 0% for up to 60 months plus $3,000 bonus cash on the EV9; Subaru offers 0% for up to 72 months on the 2026 Solterra; Volkswagen offers 0% for up to 72 months on the ID.4.

## Editorial Stance
The author frames the piece as a consumer deals roundup, noting that while the federal EV tax credit ended in 2025, automakers are using 0% financing and cash incentives to keep showroom traffic up; no broader investment thesis commentary on Tesla is offered beyond noting its 2025 delivery decline.
