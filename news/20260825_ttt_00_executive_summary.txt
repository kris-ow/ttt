## Robotaxi
- Cybercab testing footprint jumped to 24 states and 88 cities, up two states and 32 cities in 20 days, per community tracker Py; Jo Bhakdi estimates 1,000–2,000 Cybercabs deployed nationally, with roughly half carrying steering wheels for ongoing validation.
- Tesla's Cybercab launch event is set for September 3rd in Austin, with a golden-ticket sweepstakes for public riders; Bhakdi expects the initial rollout to exceed a token handful of vehicles.
- Nevada approved Tesla, Uber, and Waymo to operate robotaxis in Las Vegas — Tesla starting with 10 vehicles in a geofenced area, with the state DOT authorizing up to 5,000 statewide, per futureaza's account of reporting including Sawyer Merritt.
- Brandenburg, Germany's transport minister confirmed talks with Tesla on deploying driverless shuttles for underserved rural bus routes; Bhakdi calls it politically significant but preliminary, estimating a combined US/Europe rural TAM near 2 million vehicles (self-flagged as roughly uncertain).
- Cybercabs have reportedly been spotted staged in Baltimore and Detroit, taken as a signal Tesla may be preparing additional city launches.
- Tesla still has not applied for a California AV testing permit, per futureaza's hosts.

## Humanoid Bots
- Giga Texas Optimus factory site went from bare dirt in March to a steel-and-roof structure in five months, per Joe Tegtmeyer footage; an estimated 6–8 months of construction remain, with a stated eventual target of 10 million units/year.
- Jason Calacanis, after an Optimus factory visit, claimed the hardware is "one and a half generations" ahead of Chinese humanoid rivals and the "brain" roughly five generations ahead, predicting 1 billion Optimus units in the world within about a decade — his own projection, not company guidance.
- Jo Bhakdi's Pioneer Lands model reportedly projects roughly half a billion Optimus units by 2040 in its base case, near two billion in the bull case.
- A separate account states Tesla's Q4 (January) earnings call announced discontinuing Model S and Model X to convert Fremont lines to humanoid robot production at a stated goal of millions of units/year.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla quietly raised Cybertruck prices $5,000 on two of three trims (Dual Motor AWD to $74,990, Premium AWD to $84,990; Cyberbeast unchanged at $99,990) via an August 24 configurator update, with no spec changes, per Electrek.
- Electrek reports Cybertruck sales run under 20,000/year against a 250,000-unit factory capacity, with roughly 3,000 trucks in US inventory; excluding SpaceX and Musk-entity purchases, Q4 2025 registrations would have fallen 51% year-over-year, per the article's figures.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX announced a new high-cadence spaceport in Vermillion Parish, Louisiana, near Pecan Island, chosen after roughly seven years of site search; Musk floated informally naming it "Starbase, Louisiana."
- SpaceX pledged at least 3,000 permanent jobs (Musk separately estimated "probably more than 10,000"), plus coastal wetland restoration in an area losing shoreline at 3–23 feet/year.
- Falcon program is approaching its 700th flight; Musk recounted early failures before achieving routine booster reuse.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Musk said Starlink has grown to almost 11,000 satellites — more than double all other operators combined — with future generations targeting up to 100,000 satellites, and said he believes Starlink could eventually carry over 90% of global internet traffic.
- Grok Voice is reportedly selling roughly 3,000 Starlink kits per week and resolving over 15,000 support calls per day, per Sawyer Merritt's digest.
- Elon Musk denied a Bloomberg-sourced rumor that SpaceX sought to buy $6 billion of 800 MHz spectrum from Grain Management, calling it "Not true"; commentators noted ambiguity remains about future spectrum moves.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Nvidia and SpaceX announced a space-optimized Vera Rubin NVL72 rack for the first-generation Starmind AI satellite, targeted to launch Q4 2026 with scale-up planned for 2028; Musk described it as simpler, denser, and lighter than a terrestrial rack.
- Nvidia separately said SpaceXAI will deploy Vera CPUs to accelerate agentic AI applications behind Grok, per Sawyer Merritt's digest.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- JP Morgan reiterated an overweight rating on SpaceX with a $240 price target, citing Grok momentum and Cursor's reported $4 billion ARR (75% from businesses).
- Commentators continue to debate Tesla-SpaceX merger timing and logic, framed around consolidating capital structure and chip/data-center integration; one host guessed a merger is unlikely before "next year or maybe the year after."
