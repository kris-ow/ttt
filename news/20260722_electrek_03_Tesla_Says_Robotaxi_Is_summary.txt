The article argues that Tesla's Q2 2026 shareholder update used a cumulative "paid Robotaxi miles" chart (surpassing 2.4 million miles) to imply growth, but that breaking the figures down by quarter shows the service has plateaued rather than expanded.

## Robotaxi
1. **Quarterly paid-mile growth has flatlined:** By differencing Tesla's cumulative totals, the author calculates paid Robotaxi miles added per quarter as: ~150,000 in Q3 2025, ~450,000 in Q4 2025, ~900,000 in Q1 2026, and ~900,000 in Q2 2026 — meaning Q2 added no more miles than Q1, breaking the prior acceleration trend.
2. **New cities added without disclosed fleet size:** On July 21 (the day before earnings), Tesla added Robotaxi service areas in Tampa and Orlando but did not disclose vehicle counts in either market.
3. **Fleet size reportedly shrinking, not growing:** The author states the active unsupervised fleet is roughly 21 vehicles across Austin, Dallas, Tampa, and Orlando, down from an Austin peak of about 25 cars in late April to about 17 currently; the article references its own prior reporting from May that the fleet was shrinking.
4. **Rides still supervised by employees:** The article states that Robotaxi rides in California still run with a Tesla employee in the car as a safety monitor, meaning the service is not yet operating driverless in the way marketing implies.
5. **Comparison to Waymo:** The article states Waymo operates hundreds of thousands of paid, fully driverless rides weekly across several US cities with no one in the front seat, dwarfing Tesla's entire cumulative Robotaxi mileage to date.

## Editorial Stance
Author Fred Lambert (Electrek) argues Tesla chose the cumulative chart specifically because it obscures a stalled quarter-over-quarter growth rate, and contends this data supports his prior reporting that Robotaxi remains a supervised pilot/test program rather than a scaling business; he notes Musk has missed his own Robotaxi guidance for three consecutive earnings reports and questions why Q2 mileage matched Q1 despite an expanded service area.
