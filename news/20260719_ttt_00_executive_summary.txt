## Brief

## Robotaxi
- Cybercab count hit a reported record of 245 units at Giga Texas per drone footage (Airwave), alongside a separate report of 125 running simultaneously — signals of accelerating production/staging ahead of wider fleet deployment, though no official Tesla figures were disclosed.
- One host extrapolates from these sightings to a production run-rate exceeding 1,000/month in July and ~2,000/month by August, with cumulative fleet projections of 5,000 by September and 15,000–20,000 by December — explicitly his own forecast, not a Tesla figure.
- Tesla began driverless robotaxi operation in Miami with a small initial vehicle count, viewed by one commentator as deliberate, cautious scaling rather than a rushed rollout.
- Shareholders are pressing Tesla on missed self-set robotaxi guidance across three consecutive earnings reports — from 50% US coverage by end-2025 to seven new cities in H1 2026 — with one host calling the criticism fair.
- Per Joe Tegtmeyer (Starbase drone photographer), Cybercab production is running at "dozens per day" (roughly 100–200/week) since around April, with capacity ahead of current fleet-absorption needs; he cites four gating factors for wider rollout: production (largely solved), FSD software readiness, fleet management infrastructure, and fragmented state regulation.

## Financials
- Q2 free cash flow could disappoint sharply: Wall Street consensus is around -$3.25B per analyst Alexandra Merz (as cited by one host), with her own estimate closer to -$5B and cash position potentially $2B below the ~$41B Street expects.
- Full-year 2026 capex guidance, already raised from $20B to $25B last quarter, could move toward $35–40B per Merz's estimate — though the host cited views this as likely exaggerated.
- Stock reaction to earnings is expected to hinge more on management's tone than on the numbers themselves, per one host, citing past quarters where strong results were undercut by downbeat commentary.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Starship 13 (Ship 40) will deploy 20 V3 Starlink satellites (14 standard, 6 with sensors/thrusters) on a suborbital test trajectory, following a full 33-engine, 25-second static fire validating Raptor 3 and new stage-zero water deluge systems.
- SpaceX targets roughly monthly cadence after this flight, with Kennedy Space Center buildout underway; Flight 15 may launch from there per Gwynne Shotwell, with a Giga Bay expected around end-2026.
- Heat-shield tile performance remains a top engineering hurdle, with expanded sensor coverage and drone/buoy imaging planned to assess reentry performance.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- V3 satellites represent roughly a 10x downlink and 25x uplink bandwidth leap over V2 Mini, per host estimates; Starship 13 marks the first deployment of fully functioning V3 units.
- SpaceX launched 258 V2 Mini satellites between mid-June and mid-July, a cadence now described as routine.

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus Factory construction (One Herald Green Road) is outpacing Giga Texas's early build speed, with seven cranes active and floor decking/roof structure already in place; estimated at 9–10 million sq ft across four floors.
- Rough timeline per Tegtmeyer: equipment installation possibly beginning next spring, small-scale production in late 2027, with 2028 as the major ramp year.

## Market & Competition
- Tesla trades near $380, down sharply over the past month; SpaceX secondary shares reportedly down ~14% weekly and ~20% monthly, below IPO price — attributed by one host to a broad AI-infrastructure rotation rather than company-specific news.
- China's Kimi K3 open-source model is fueling selloff fears around US AI hardware demand, though one host argues this reasoning is backwards and that open-source releases should increase, not decrease, compute demand.
