## Robotaxi
- **245 Cybercabs spotted at Giga Texas**, described as a record sighting per Sawyer Merritt's digest; separately, jobhakdi cites the same 245/day figure alongside 125 running simultaneously, extrapolating (his own forecast, not confirmed) to over 1,000/month production by July, ~2,000/month by August, and a fleet of 15,000–20,000 by December.
- Joe Tegtmeyer (via cernbasher) independently estimates Cybercab production at "dozens per day, many dozens" — roughly 100–200/week — running since around April 2026, and says Tesla is not yet "pushing the gas pedal" on output because the deployed fleet isn't large enough to absorb more units.
- Tegtmeyer identifies four gating factors before wider rollout: production (largely solved), autonomy software readiness, fleet-management infrastructure (charging, cleaning, repair), and a fragmented state-by-state regulatory picture. He believes Tesla is pre-testing new cities with employee drivers ahead of official expansion, expecting inclusion "sooner than later."
- investingagainstthegrain flags that Tesla has missed self-set robotaxi guidance across three straight earnings reports — from 50% US coverage by end-2025 down to seven new cities in H1 2026 — calling investor pushback on this fair; he views Miami's small initial driverless launch as deliberate, cautious scaling rather than a rushed rollout.
- Steering-wheel presence on production Cybercabs remains visually unresolved per jobhakdi's frame-by-frame review of imagery.

## Financials
- Q2 free cash flow expected to disappoint: Wall Street consensus is around -$3.25B, with analyst Alexandra Merz (cited by jobhakdi) estimating closer to -$5B and cash position roughly $2B below the ~$41B Street expects.
- Full-year 2026 capex guidance (raised from $20B to $25B last quarter) could move toward $35–40B per Merz's estimate, though jobhakdi says he views that figure as likely exaggerated.
- investingagainstthegrain argues Tesla's earnings-day stock reaction will hinge more on management's tone than the underlying numbers, noting past quarters where strong results were undercut by downbeat commentary.

## Market & Competition
- Tesla trades near $380, down sharply over the past month; SpaceX secondary shares are down ~14% over the past week and ~20% over the past month, below IPO price — both names caught in a broader AI-infrastructure selloff, per jobhakdi.
- investingagainstthegrain disputes that a scrubbed Starship launch drove SpaceX's decline, instead framing the selloff as part of a broader AI/frontier-model rotation that also drags Tesla down given its AI narrative exposure.
- China's Kimi K3 open-source AI model is described by jobhakdi as a "Sputnik moment," reportedly beating Anthropic's Claude Opus on some coding benchmarks; he argues (as his own view) this should increase rather than decrease AI compute demand.
- investingagainstthegrain cites Gavin Baker's thesis that margin dollars are shifting from frontier LLM labs to infrastructure and vertically-integrated players like SpaceX and Meta, and notes Polymarket odds of Anthropic reaching a $1.5T valuation by year-end fell to 67%.

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus Factory (One Herald Green Road) construction is outpacing Giga Texas's build speed at the same stage, per Tegtmeyer — floor decking and roof structure already in place with seven cranes versus six historically at Giga Texas; estimated at 9–10 million sq ft across four floors.
- Tegtmeyer expects equipment installation possibly next spring, small-scale production in late 2027, and 2028 as "the big year" for Optimus ramp; battery cell supply for a rumored 10-million-unit/year target remains an open, unconfirmed question.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Starship 13 will deploy 20 V3 Starlink satellites (14 standard, 6 with cameras/sensors/thrusters) on a suborbital trajectory — the first flight capable of carrying full-capability V3 units, per cernbasher's host.
- A full 33-engine, 25-second static fire validated Raptor 3 engines and a new stage-zero water deluge/flame diverter system ahead of the flight; SpaceX aims for roughly monthly cadence afterward, working toward 2027 Artemis 3 milestones.
- Kennedy Space Center buildout is underway, with boosters/ships to be transported horizontally via a converted drone ship; Gwynne Shotwell reportedly said Flight 15 might launch from there, with a Giga Bay expected by end of 2026.
- Heat-shield tile performance remains, per Tegtmeyer, one of the last major engineering hurdles; recovery drones and Starlink-equipped buoys will image reentry for comparison against on-orbit data.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- V3 satellites represent an estimated ~10x downlink and ~25x uplink bandwidth leap over V2 Mini, per cernbasher's host; 23 V3 units are estimated to equal the capacity of roughly 260 V2 Minis.
- SpaceX launched 258 V2 Mini satellites between mid-June and mid-July 2026, a cadence the host now calls routine.
