Tesla teased an October 1 reveal event for the new Roadster via a "Go for launch" image with rocket-launch style lighting, matching a countdown clock on Tesla's Roadster webpage, though no venue or livestream details were given.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Roadster reveal teased for October 1:** Tesla posted a teaser image captioned "Go for launch" with the date "10.01," strongly suggesting the long-promised thruster-assisted "flying" demo, which has been discussed since 2018 and reportedly uses SpaceX-developed cold gas thrusters.
2. **Nine years of delays:** The Roadster prototype was unveiled in November 2017 with a $200,000 base price ($250,000 for the Founders Series), 620 miles of range, sub-two-second 0-60 mph, and 250+ mph top speed, originally targeting 2020 production. The article says Tesla has since pushed the timeline at least eight times (2022, 2023, 2024, "2025-2026"), with Musk saying at last November's shareholder meeting that production wouldn't start until "2027 or 2028," and various promised "demo" dates (April 1, late April, August, this summer) also slipping. Marques Brownlee and Sam Altman are both said to have canceled their reservations.
3. **Scaled-back demo:** Per The Information's August reporting cited by the article, the planned demo has been scaled back from an elaborate version (driving up a ramp, upside down, righting itself, hovering) to something closer to a simple hover, performed remotely with nobody inside and spectators kept hundreds of yards back due to thruster noise. The article states the showcased car won't be street legal, will likely cost hundreds of thousands of dollars or more, and Tesla has reportedly discussed selling it to a small number of buyers through a Ferrari-style track program.

## Editorial Stance
The author argues the Roadster is no longer a real car program but a promotional vehicle for the Tesla-SpaceX relationship, timed to coincide with Musk building Wall Street support for a Tesla-SpaceX merger; the piece frames this as part of a broader September pattern of Tesla re-hyping old product reveals (Roadster, Semi, robotaxi) ahead of an expected shareholder merger vote.
