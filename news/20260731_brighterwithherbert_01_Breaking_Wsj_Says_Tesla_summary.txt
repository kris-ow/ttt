2. **Panelists see the report as a deliberate pre-merger disclosure, not an imminent sale:** Alexandra argues the story was likely leaked deliberately to surface the "what happens to Tesla China" question before a merger announcement, so it doesn't overshadow the eventual deal; she stresses no sale is happening immediately and any transaction would occur at high value, not "for $0."
