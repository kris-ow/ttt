## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Large share unlock cited as key catalyst:** The host says SpaceX unlocked roughly 900 million shares (about 150–160% of the existing float, which he estimates was 500–650 million shares) around the most recent earnings, and that the stock nonetheless rose about 6% into the close that day rather than selling off as feared.
2. **Employee shareholders reportedly reluctant to sell below IPO price:** The host says he interviewed three unnamed SpaceX shareholders/employees who unlocked shares and told him they would not sell any shares below $135 (the IPO price), given the stock has traded as high as roughly $225–$250 previously; some said they want to diversify but only starting at $135, citing pressure from wealth advisers to reduce concentrated positions.
3. **Host's $135 "selling wall" thesis:** He estimates that of the 900 million unlocked shares, roughly 20% (his rough estimate, could be "100 million to 300 million") are likely queued as sell orders around $135, creating resistance he expects to take about 10 trading days to work through (roughly two days elapsed, eight to go as of this video), after which he expects the stock to be freer to move higher.
4. **Unlock schedule detail:** The host describes a systematic unlock schedule occurring roughly every two weeks (citing dates including August 8, August 9, and August 20), with a larger unlock later in the year and a final scheduled unlock in December, plus longer-term unlocks after that; he predicts unlocks above $135 will cause modest stock drops (a few trading days each) while unlocks below $135 will not trigger selling.
5. **Trading positioning disclosed:** The host says he sold a small amount of $135-strike weekly covered/short calls but took a larger short-put position at strikes around $135, $130, and $128 (more weight at $130), expiring soon, as his preferred way to express a bullish-but-capped-near-term view; he also mentions selling short puts on Tesla and Micron and being long Nvidia.
6. **Possible Tesla–SpaceX merger discussed as speculative:** In audience Q&A, the host says he does not think a Tesla–SpaceX merger will happen this year, contrary to one commenter's conviction, though he agrees any merger announcement would likely come with a "premium," and suggests speculative short-dated out-of-the-money calls as a low-cost way to bet on it.
