## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Terafab: SpaceX and Tesla's joint $16.8B chip fab announced in Texas:** SpaceX and Tesla jointly announced Terafab, an advanced semiconductor fabrication facility to be built in Grimes County, Texas, intended to "bridge the divide between current global chip supply and the compute demand of the future." The companies state the initial phase will require approximately $16.8 billion in combined capital investment from SpaceX and Tesla, with potential future expansion phases pushing total investment "much higher." The facility will employ at least 3,000 people. Tesla has also listed 36 job openings tied to Terafab, including roles like Senior Process Engineer (Lithography, Dry Etch, Plating, Epitaxy), Industrial Hygienist, EHS Manager, and Environmental Engineer. Renderings shared show a highly futuristic factory design. This is a major joint capital commitment spanning both companies' theses — relevant to Tesla's AI/compute ambitions (e.g., Dojo successor chips, AI5/AI6) and SpaceX's Colossus-style compute initiatives, though the posts don't specify a breakdown of Tesla vs. SpaceX contribution or a fab completion timeline.

2. **Starlink in-flight connectivity draws strong airline executive endorsement:** SpaceX COO Gwynne Shotwell relayed that an airline customer reported passengers choosing shorter connecting flights specifically to ensure they'd be on a Starlink-activated aircraft — something the airline said it had never seen before in the business. Separately, Andrew Ross Sorkin praised his Starlink experience on a United Airlines flight, calling it "amazing" and speculating other airlines will need to match it, framing it as a likely competitive differentiator for future NY–LA-type routes. Both posts (unverified anecdotes relayed secondhand) point to strong demand-side signal for Starlink's aviation vertical, though no revenue or subscriber figures were given.

3. **Tesla Megapack manufacturing expansion:** Tesla began Megapack 3 production at its Texas Megafactory — now Tesla's third operational Megafactory and second in the U.S. Specs cited: 5 MWh usable AC energy per unit, ~86,000 lbs weight, 28-foot shippable enclosure, optimized for up to 8-hour discharge applications. Separately, a new $125 million Tesla Megapack facility (250 MW/500 MWh) went live in Needville, Texas, now fully integrated into the ERCOT grid and co-located with a 430 MW solar project — reinforcing Tesla's energy storage buildout in Texas.

4. **Robotaxi and autonomy progress — nighttime unsupervised testing in Austin:** A video shared shows Tesla's Unsupervised Model Y robotaxis operating at night in Austin (~9 PM local time), described as incremental ("step by step") progress in expanding operating conditions beyond daytime.

5. **Tesla reliability/recall comparison vs. Kia:** A post cites 2026 physical recall counts (Tesla: 2, Kia: 6), Consumer Reports reliability rank (Tesla: 9, Kia: 10), and owner satisfaction rank (Tesla: 4, Kia: 15), framed favorably for Tesla alongside a comment that "only Teslas can self-drive."

6. **Ford enters midsize EV truck market:** Ford officially announced its new midsize EV truck, "Fathom," starting at $28,350, with pre-orders beginning early 2027 and deliveries in late 2027. Ford touted next-generation BlueCruise capability and bidirectional power capability — relevant as a future competitive entrant against Tesla's EV truck/lower-price lineup, though it's over a year from market.

7. **Tesla battery recycling milestone:** Tesla released a video on U.S. battery recycling, stating it recycled over 3,000 metric tons of battery material at its Nevada facility in 2025 — equivalent to material for about 9,000 Model Y RWD vehicles. Tesla also said its Texas recycling facility is expected to add capacity (no specific figures given for Texas output).
