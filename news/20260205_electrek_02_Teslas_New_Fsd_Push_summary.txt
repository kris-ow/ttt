Electrek reports that Tesla is emailing customers to buy Full Self-Driving (Supervised) before a February 14 deadline, after which it becomes subscription-only, but the email's fine print appears to quietly change FSD's ownership terms.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Email push and ownership-language change:** Tesla's marketing email, titled "Purchase by February 14 to Own FSD (Supervised)" with the tagline "Purchase It Now, Own It Outright," states that purchased FSD "stays with your Tesla as long as you own it." The article says this contradicts the historical understanding that FSD transferred with the vehicle on private resale, and notes Tesla's own FSD support page states the opposite: that a vehicle transferred to a new owner retains FSD if it was bought outright by the previous owner (only subscription FSD requires the new owner to resubscribe). Electrek contacted Tesla for clarification and had not received a response at publication.
2. **Stated history of FSD transfer policy:** The article recounts that pre-2019 FSD was understood to transfer with private vehicle sales; in November 2019 Tesla began remotely removing FSD from vehicles sold at auction; in 2023 Tesla introduced temporary "FSD Transfer" programs letting owners move FSD to a newly purchased Tesla, with restrictions; in January 2026 Tesla announced FSD purchases would end February 14, moving to subscription-only; and now the email's language suggests FSD no longer transfers at private resale.

## Editorial Stance
Fred Lambert calls the timing "suspect" and, if the email's wording is accurate rather than the support page, characterizes it as "a classic bait-and-switch" — using urgency and "Own It Outright" messaging while allegedly reducing what customers actually receive (a non-transferable license) and undermining Elon Musk's past claim that FSD makes Tesla vehicles "appreciating assets." The piece frames this as looking like a "desperate cash grab" and calls for Tesla to be transparent about what customers are buying.

Notable quotes and data:
- Email quote: "When you purchase Full Self-Driving (Supervised),* it stays with your Tesla as long as you own it. And with over-the-air software updates, FSD (Supervised) gets better overnight."
- Support page quote: "If the previous owner purchased FSD (Supervised) with a one-time payment rather than subscribing, then the vehicle will be transferred to you with FSD (Supervised). If the previous owner subscribed to FSD (Supervised) for a monthly fee, you will need to subscribe using your own Tesla Account."
- FSD one-time purchase price referenced: $8,000; subscription referenced at $99/month.
