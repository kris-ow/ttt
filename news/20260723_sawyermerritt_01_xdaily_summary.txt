## Executive Summary
This digest is dominated by Tesla's Q2 2026 earnings release and call, covering financial results, robotaxi/FSD metrics, Optimus production ramp, Cybercab and Semi manufacturing progress, and AI compute/silicon roadmap commentary from Elon Musk and CFO Vaibhav Taneja. Headline: revenue beat estimates ($28.2B vs $27.6B est) but EPS missed ($0.33 vs $0.55 est) and free cash flow was negative (-$1.1B), while FSD subscription and robotaxi mileage metrics showed strong sequential growth.

---

## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Q2 2026 Earnings Results — Mixed Beat/Miss:** Tesla reported Q2 revenue of $28.2B (vs. $27.6B est), non-GAAP EPS of $0.33 (vs. $0.55 est), GAAP net income of $1.11B, gross margin of 16.8%, and negative free cash flow of -$1.1B. Taneja noted the company exited Q2 with its largest order backlog since 2023 and is focused on increasing production across all factories. Energy gross margin is expected to settle in the "mid to low 20% range" long-term.

2. **FSD Subscription Growth & Attach Rates:** FSD subscriptions rose 56% in Q2 2026 to 1.48 million (+200,000 from Q1), the largest quarterly subscriber add in Tesla's history. Average daily new FSD (Supervised) subscribers hit 2,200 in Q2. North America FSD attach rate on new deliveries hit a record over 55%. Tesla now generates approximately $775 million annually from FSD subscriptions.

3. **Robotaxi Fleet & Safety Data:** Tesla's Ashok Elluswamy stated the current Robotaxi fleet is running early versions of FSD V15, with "zero notable incidents" across more than 380,000 miles of Unsupervised robotaxi driving. Tesla logged 2.4 million paid cumulative robotaxi miles in Q2, up from ~1.7 million in Q1. Musk said the fleet will continue scaling "more than 10% per week" in miles driven, but reiterated safety is the binding constraint: "If we injured even one person, it will be world headline news." Musk also confirmed Tesla will not partner with Uber/Lyft, relying instead on its own app for "enough demand."

4. **Cybercab & Semi Production Milestones:** Cybercab production began at Giga Texas, with installed annual manufacturing capacity now listed as >125,000 units; engineering test drives of production Cybercabs on public roads began in Q2, and employee rides on the Giga Texas campus started this month — both framed as precursors to robotaxi fleet deployment. Tesla Semi is "commissioning" and remains on track for production this year at its new Nevada factory; new end-of-line photos were shared.

5. **Optimus Manufacturing Ramp:** Tesla decommissioned its Model S/X manufacturing lines at Fremont to install first-generation Optimus production lines, with production expected to start "soon." Initial builds will go toward an "Optimus Academy" for training. Musk called Optimus "the hardest product to scale that we've ever had at Tesla," citing an entirely new supply chain buildout; new production line photos were released. Tesla is also reportedly preparing its app to support Optimus operating in home environments, including a consent flow for collecting video/spatial data (mirroring FSD's shadow-data approach) — per an unconfirmed report shared by the account.

6. **AI Compute & Silicon Roadmap:** Musk outlined plans to upgrade all pre-HW4 vehicles (any car with cameras) to a "next-gen AI board," citing an upgraded AI4 chip expected around mid-2027 followed by AI5. He described "Megapod" — pairing x86 computers with Tesla's AI4 chip in a deployable box ("Digital Optimus in a giant box") — enabling distributed AI compute co-located with power generation, including at Superchargers. On "Terafab," Musk said Tesla has placed equipment orders for a development fab in Austin and will announce the final location separately. Tesla plans to raise Texas onsite compute capacity to nearly 400 MW in H2 2026, a 60% increase from June 2026 and 220% increase from end-2025; Cortex 2 compute (supporting both vehicle and Optimus autonomy software) more than doubled in H1 2026.

7. **FSD Semi & Battery Constraints:** Musk expects self-driving on the Tesla Semi around "end of this year or early next year," but is deprioritizing it to avoid distracting from safety improvements ("march of nines") on Model 3/Y and Cybercab. Tesla reiterated that battery pack capacity remains the limiting factor on global vehicle production ramp, with progress on new battery/material factories including pack capacity in Berlin.

8. **Regulatory & Supply Chain Notes:** France's Transportation Minister said in a video that FSD's "safety trade-offs are not yet sufficient" to authorize it, explicitly stating the system is "not a self-driving system" — a regulatory headwind in the French/EU market. Separately, Micron has given Tesla a "significant" memory production allocation for coming years, per Musk. Tesla also received zoning approval for a new 56-stall Robotaxi charging facility in San Antonio, TX, for its private fleet (pending final City Council vote).
