Tesla announced an expansion of its unsupervised Robotaxi geofence to cover the entire Austin metro area, but the actual fleet serving that territory has shrunk to approximately 20 active unsupervised vehicles — down from a peak of 25.

## Robotaxi
1. **Geofence expands to all of Austin metro, fleet does not:** Tesla's official Robotaxi account on X confirmed "Unsupervised Robotaxi now in the entire Austin Metro area." Previously, unsupervised vehicles were restricted to a small part of South Austin. The geofence has grown through several phases over the past year, roughly doubling each time, but the unsupervised fleet peaked at 25 cumulative vehicles in late April before declining to ~20 active. Dallas and Houston, which launched in April with tiny geofences, remain stuck at 3 and 6 vehicles respectively with zero growth since launch.

2. **Total fleet has collapsed:** The total active fleet — including supervised Bay Area vehicles — dropped from 165 to just 34 active vehicles. Per RobotaxiTracker.com, roughly 20 unsupervised vehicles now nominally cover potentially thousands of square miles of Austin metro.

3. **Scale gap vs. Waymo:** Waymo operates approximately 3,000 robotaxis across its US markets and completes over 500,000 paid trips per week, across more than 1,400 square miles in 11 cities. Waymo recently raised $16 billion to fund expansion including to London and Tokyo.

4. **FSD v15 cited as scaling prerequisite:** CEO Elon Musk has said Tesla is waiting for its FSD v15 software rewrite before aggressively scaling the fleet, pushing any meaningful ramp to late 2026 or early 2027. The article notes Musk has previously tied fleet growth to other software milestones that passed without scaling.

## Editorial Stance
Author Fred Lambert argues that geofence expansions are costless and meaningless without vehicles to serve them, characterizing Tesla's pattern as moving the goalpost to the easy-to-change metric (territory) while fleet size and rides stagnate or decline. He states Tesla must demonstrate it can safely scale the fleet before any service area expansion translates into an actual business.
