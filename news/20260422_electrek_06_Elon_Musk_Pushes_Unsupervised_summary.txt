During Tesla's Q1 2026 earnings call, CEO Elon Musk confirmed that unsupervised Full Self-Driving (FSD) for consumer vehicles will not arrive until Q4 2026 at the earliest — the latest in a series of delayed timelines stretching back to promises made in 2018.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Unsupervised FSD pushed to Q4 2026, geography-limited rollout planned:** Musk said consumer unsupervised FSD would arrive "probably in the fourth quarter," with rollout happening gradually, geography by geography, as Tesla validates safety in each area. He cited complex intersections, unsafe road markings, and weather challenges as complicating factors. He acknowledged that Tesla knows "major architectural improvements" remain in the pipeline and said it would not make sense to deploy at large scale until those are incorporated.

2. **FSD v15 framed as "complete overhaul" running on "pure AI":** Musk described version 15 as a full architectural overhaul, expected "hopefully by the end of this year, but certainly by early next year." Tesla noted the fleet is approaching 10 billion FSD miles — the threshold Musk set in January 2026 as the prerequisite for safe unsupervised deployment. Even within v14, Musk claimed Tesla is "significantly safer than human" driving, but no peer-reviewed data was cited. By contrast, the article notes Waymo has published data showing 85% fewer injury crashes across tens of millions of autonomous miles.

3. **HW3 vehicles officially excluded from unsupervised FSD:** Musk confirmed that Hardware 3 vehicles "simply do not have the capability to achieve unsupervised FSD," citing HW3 having "only one eighth of the memory bandwidth of hardware 4." Tesla is offering HW3 owners discounted trade-ins to HW4 cars or a hardware upgrade requiring replacement of both the computer and all cameras, with "micro factories" planned in major cities to handle volume. A "distilled" version of v14 for HW3 is planned by end of June 2026.

## Robotaxi
4. **Robotaxi revenue not material in 2026, expected significant in 2027:** Musk said he hopes to have unsupervised robotaxi operations running in "a dozen or so states" by end of 2026, but conceded that "unsupervised FSD or robotaxi revenue will not be super material this year." He added it would be "material, probably in a significant way, next year" (2027).

## Financials
5. **Q1 2026 earnings slight beat; delivery miss and inventory build:** Tesla reported $0.41 per share on $22.38 billion in revenue, a slight beat on earnings, but missed delivery expectations and built over 50,000 more vehicles than it sold.

## Editorial Stance
Author Fred Lambert characterizes Musk's repeated timeline deferrals as a predictable cycle of overpromising and underdelivering, arguing that Musk's stated rationale — delaying deployment because improvements are always in the pipeline — is "a perpetual delay machine disguised as responsible engineering." Lambert contends FSD remains a Level 2 supervised system despite years of "Full Self-Driving" branding.
