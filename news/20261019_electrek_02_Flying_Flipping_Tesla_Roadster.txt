If last week’s headlines proved anything at all, it’s that the long wait for flying, flipping, fully self driving electric vehicles is finally over – especially if you’re shopping at Tesla’s competitors. On today’s episode of Quick Charge, we’ve got a full rundown of hot new EVs that you can fly, flip, and (most importantly) take delivery of today.

After nearly a decade of waiting, Tesla Roadster 2.0 buyers still don’t have a car – and, despite last week’s thrilling demo, the fine print says they may never get their new Roadster, flying or otherwise.

If you’re ready to walk into a dealership and place an order on a new electric truck, a true one-ton capable electric drive dually that car drive itself, drive upside down, or go chasing real speed thousands of feet in the air, we’ve got tremendous news: you can buy electric vehicles that can do all that stuff today.
