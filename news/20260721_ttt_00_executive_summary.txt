## Robotaxi
- Tesla launched Robotaxi service in Tampa and Orlando simultaneously, bringing total operating areas to seven (or six active cities excluding Bay Area), per Sawyer Merritt and Electrek. Geofences are estimated at roughly 15–30 square miles each; one rider reportedly couldn't request a ride in Orlando shortly after launch.
- Electrek reports Austin's unsupervised fleet remains around 17 cars, down from a peak of roughly 25 in late April; combined with Dallas, Tesla's total unsupervised operation is around 21 vehicles, versus Waymo's roughly 3,000 driverless vehicles and 500,000+ weekly paid trips.
- Musk's past robotaxi guidance has repeatedly missed: prior claims of 50% US population coverage, 8-10 metro areas, and Nevada/Florida/Arizona launches by year-end all failed to materialize, per electrified's review of past earnings calls.
- Tesla's Bay Area ride-share now serves SFO airport, but under a human-safety-monitor permit rather than full driverless authorization; SFO can set rules independently of state regulators.
- Starlink V5 is now directly integrated into Cybercab per official Tesla and Starlink posts and showroom infographics; Tesla's VP of AI (Ashok Elluswamy) said connectivity isn't required for safe operation but supports navigation, customer service, and fleet management. Electrek's Fred Lambert argues this solves a problem Tesla's small, dense-metro operation doesn't yet have, while adding a subscription revenue line for SpaceX.
- Cybercab production estimates cluster around 1,000 units/month in July, with expectations of ~2,000/month by September, per Joe Tegtmeyer and jobhakdi; drone footage reportedly showed hundreds of built units in an outbound logistics lot, though these figures remain unconfirmed estimates.
- Of 22 Robotaxi safety incidents since launch, 3 were attributed to Tesla's safety/teleoperator (most recent in May), and 9 to other parties, per electrified's tracker.

## Autonomous Driving
- NHTSA issued a 24-part information demand to Tesla in its EA26002 investigation, covering ~3.2 million vehicles tied to nine low-visibility crashes (one fatality, one injury); Tesla must respond by August 12 or face penalties up to $27,874/day, capped near $139 million.
- The agency specifically demands Tesla's internal "Radar Saves Us" document, calling it relevant to Tesla's understanding of vision-only system limitations in the same conditions as the crashes; the document's contents have not previously been public.
- NHTSA also lists 15 Tesla/Musk marketing statements for review (including "FSD Supervised can drive in whiteout conditions" and the "$99/month robotaxi" post) and is probing a 2024 change that downgraded the "FSD Degraded" alert from red to yellow with no chime.
- FSD V14 Light is rolling out more broadly to Hardware 3 owners, including in Canada, removing the need to press the brake to confirm activation; Ashok Elluswamy highlighted a tester's positive review, and the build is seen as likely bound for wide release.

## Financials
- Tesla reports Q2 2026 earnings July 22 after market close. Deliveries hit a record 480,126 (up 25% YoY, ~74,000 above consensus), while production of 451,758 meant Tesla drew down inventory.
- Revenue consensus sits at roughly $25.9–26.4 billion (up 15–17% YoY); EPS consensus is about $0.53, though estimates range widely ($0.27–$0.74). Options markets price a ~7.6% post-earnings stock swing.
- Q1 2026 automotive gross margin ex-credits was ~12.5%; whether it held at record volume is the key unknown as regulatory credits keep shrinking post-federal-tax-credit expiration.
- Shareholder Q&A drew 425 questions from 8.6 million represented shares; top questions ask about Optimus Gen 3 ramp status and why robotaxi guidance has been missed three quarters running.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla prices are rising faster than the market: Model 3 up 15% YoY, Model Y up 13.5% (CarGurus index), against overall market price growth of just 2.3%.
- Australian H1 EV sales surged; Model Y sales rose 95% YoY to just over 20,000 units, nearly double the next-closest EV (BYD, ~12,500 units).
- Ford's upcoming ~$30k EV pickup with native NACS port was spotted Supercharging for the first time, ahead of a targeted 2027 launch.

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus factory footprint at Giga Texas is now roughly 10 column-grids wide and 400–450 feet long and still growing, per Joe Tegtmeyer footage.
- Terafab chip-fabrication construction is also underway nearby at Giga Texas, described as an early "baby" version of the eventual full facility.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- SpaceX confirmed its first public-company earnings report for August 4, with a call at 4:30 PM ET; a detailed lock-up release schedule follows, starting with 8% on August 6 and further tranches through December 9.
- Musk called a circulating report of a ~$52 billion, ~1 million-GPU Colossus order "fake news"; the report claimed the buildout would push Colossus toward ~2 GW with Foxconn as manufacturing partner.
- Responding to a viral (unverified) post about short sellers, Musk said "SpaceX will be worth more than Earth if we achieve our goals" and that short-sellers' survival probability is "very low."

## SpaceX — Starlink
