## Brief
- Robotaxi expands to Tampa, Orlando day before earnings

- SpaceX sets first public earnings date: August 4

## Robotaxi
- Tesla launched Robotaxi service in Tampa and Orlando simultaneously, bringing the total to seven operating areas including Miami, Dallas, Houston, Austin, and the Bay Area; Tesla gave no vehicle counts, and one user reportedly couldn't request a ride in Orlando shortly after launch (per electrek, electrified).
- Electrek reports Austin's unsupervised fleet remains around 17 active cars, down from a peak of roughly 25 in late April; combined with Dallas, Tesla's total unsupervised operation is around 21 vehicles, versus Waymo's ~3,000 driverless vehicles and 500,000+ weekly paid trips.
- Musk's past Robotaxi guidance has repeatedly missed: prior claims of 50% US population coverage, 8–10 metro areas, and Nevada/Florida/Arizona launches by year-end did not materialize, and a cited "well over 500" paid vehicles figure stayed flat rather than doubling monthly (per electrified's review of past earnings calls).
- Tesla's Bay Area ride-share now serves SFO airport, but under a human-safety-monitor permit, not full driverless authorization — SFO and California don't yet classify it as true robotaxi service.
- Cybercab production estimates cluster around 1,000 units/month in July with expectations of ~2,000/month by September, per Joe Tegtmeyer and separately per jobhakdi's host; drone footage reportedly showed hundreds of built Cybercabs in an outbound logistics lot, with a batch of 55 dispatched at once described as a deployment-pacing test (Randy Kirk).
- Starlink V5 is now officially confirmed integrated into Cybercab via antenna in the trunk; Tesla's VP of AI said it's not required for safe operation but supports navigation, customer service, and fleet management.

## Autonomous Driving
- NHTSA issued a 24-part formal information demand to Tesla under Engineering Analysis EA26002, covering ~3.2 million vehicles tied to nine reduced-visibility crashes (one fatality, one injury); Tesla must respond by August 12 or face penalties up to $27,874/day, capped near $139 million.
- The demand specifically seeks Tesla's internal "Radar Saves Us" document, which NHTSA says bears on Tesla's understanding of vision-only system limitations in the same conditions as the crashes; the document's contents have not previously been public.
- NHTSA is also probing 15 specific Tesla/Musk marketing claims (including "FSD can operate in all conditions" and the "$99/month robotaxi" post), a 2024 change that muted the "FSD Degraded" alert, and Tesla's "Direct Photon-Count Capability" and "Vision Failsafe Ranker" features.
- FSD V14 Light is rolling out more broadly to Hardware 3 owners, including in Canada, removing the need to press the brake to start self-driving; Ashok Elluswamy highlighted a tester's positive review.

## Financials
- Tesla reports Q2 2026 earnings July 22; deliveries hit a record 480,126 (up 25% YoY, ~74,000 above consensus), while production of 451,758 meant Tesla drew down inventory.
- Revenue consensus sits around $25.9–26.4 billion and EPS around $0.52–0.53, but Q1's ~12.5% automotive gross margin (ex-credits) and fading regulatory credits leave profitability murky; options markets price a ~7.6% post-earnings stock swing.
- Shareholder Q&A drew 425 questions from 1,710 participants (8.6M shares); top questions ask about Optimus Gen 3 ramp status and why Tesla has missed short-term robotaxi guidance three quarters running.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla vehicle prices are rising faster than the broader market: Model 3 up 15% YoY and Model Y up 13.5% (CarGurus index), with average Tesla price around $32,300 versus the overall market average of $29,000.
- Giga Berlin guidance points to a "significant increase" in 2026 production volume versus the prior year, per a newly filed report.
- Australia's H1 EV sales surged, with Model Y up 95% YoY (over 20,000 units) as the top-selling EV, ahead of the next-closest EV at roughly 12,500 units.

## Energy
- Tesla deployed 13.5 GWh of energy storage in Q2 2026, up over 40% from 9.6 GWh a year earlier, though slightly below analyst expectations of ~13.8 GWh.

## Market & Competition
- Chinese EV brands are each selling roughly 5,000 units in markets like Australia, intensifying competition beyond Tesla and BYD.
- Grok 4.5 reportedly matches a top rival coding model's benchmark performance at roughly one-eighth the cost ($1.50 vs. $11), per jobhakdi.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- SpaceX confirmed its first public-company earnings report for August 4, with a call at 4:30 PM ET; a lock-up release schedule was disclosed running from an 8% tranche on August 6 through further tranches into December.
- Musk called a widely circulated report of a ~$52 billion, 1-million-GPU Colossus order "fake news," though the host of one show noted SpaceX's broader compute buildout trajectory remains aggressive regardless.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Starlink's next-generation V3 satellite specs were detailed (per "Musk Musconomy"): ~1 Tbps downlink (~10x V2), ~160 Gbps uplink (~22x V2), six 400 Gbit/s laser links, and roughly double V2's solar power, positioning it to enable direct-to-phone service at LTE-like speeds.
