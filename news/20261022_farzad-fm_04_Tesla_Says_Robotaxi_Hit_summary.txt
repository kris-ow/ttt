This short update covers Tesla's self-reported Q3 metrics on its driverless robotaxi operations, centered on a steep rise in unsupervised miles driven and confirmation that a dedicated Cybercab production line is now running in Texas.

## Robotaxi
1. **Unsupervised miles surge eightfold quarter-over-quarter:** Tesla reported that cumulative unsupervised (unattended) Robotaxi mileage rose roughly eightfold in Q3 versus the prior quarter, with the fleet approaching nearly 2 million unsupervised miles total; Tesla also cited steady double-digit weekly growth in this metric.
2. **Cybercab robotaxi service launched in Austin:** Tesla began offering Cybercab robotaxi rides on September 3, with service hours in Austin now running from 6:00 a.m. to midnight.
3. **Cybercab production line operational in Texas:** Tesla lists a Cybercab production line at Giga Texas as already operational, with installed annual capacity of over 125,000 vehicles — a measure of the line's production capability rather than actual output to date.
