The article reports that average new EV transaction prices fell in August even as the overall US new-vehicle average price rose above $50,000 for the first time in 2026, narrowing the EV price premium.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **EV prices fall as industry average rises:** New EV buyers paid an average of $54,813 in August (Kelley Blue Book), down 1.2% month over month and 2.7% year over year, while the industry-wide average transaction price rose to $50,089, up 0.5% month over month and 1.9% year over year — the first month in 2026 above $50,000.
2. **EV price premium shrinks:** The EV price premium over the industry average fell to 9.4% in August, down sharply from over 16% a year earlier, leaving EVs about $4,700 more expensive than the average new vehicle — described as the smallest gap yet.
3. **Incentives pull back but remain elevated:** EV incentive spending averaged 12% of transaction price in August, down from 12.2% in July and 14.6% a year earlier, but still nearly double the industry-wide incentive average of 6.5%.
4. **Tesla pricing declines:** Tesla's average transaction price fell to $52,616 in August, down 2.4% from July and 3.4% year over year; the article notes Tesla's large share of US EV sales gives its pricing an outsized effect on the overall EV average.
