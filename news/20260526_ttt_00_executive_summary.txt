## Robotaxi
- **Cybercab testing expands to Arizona.** Tesla has begun Cybercab real-world validation in Arizona, extending the known testing footprint beyond California and Texas. No fleet size or timeline details were disclosed, per Sawyer Merritt.
- **Starlink equipment confirmed across Cybercab fleet.** SC Robinson posted footage of 14 Cybercabs on-site in Houston; the Cybercab Spotter account confirmed multiple tandem sightings all equipped with Starlink, ruling out one-off installations.
- **Bhakdi: thousands of Cybercabs per month within one to two months.** Jo Bhakdi argues mass manufacturing of the Cybercab only began in approximately April–May 2026 and projects production reaching thousands of units per month as soon as June or July. He expects multiple hundreds of unsupervised robotaxis operating by August 2026.
- **Bhakdi's end-2026 fleet forecast: 10,000–20,000 Cybercabs, central case 15,000.** He previously forecast 1,800 unsupervised units by April–May 2026 — a miss he acknowledges — and attributes to roughly a one-year delay. His core logic: once thousands of Cybercabs exist, the economic pressure to commercialize becomes irresistible within three to six months. He projects 100,000+ units in 2027 if the 2026 ramp materializes, and millions before or around 2030.
- **Bhakdi's long-run Cybercab COGS target: ~$10,000.** He argues that concentrating production on a single high-volume platform — as opposed to BYD's 43 trims — is the mechanism that enables this unit economics target.

## Autonomous Driving
- **FSD 14.3 coast-to-coast Canada run: 1,776 miles completed, zero interventions.** David Moss, Spencer, and Deon Olsen are driving a 2025 AI4 Model 3 from Vancouver, BC to Nova Scotia (3,700 miles total). At Day 3 of the attempt, they had covered half the route on FSD version 14.3 with no disengagements; the only notable event was a bird strike. A construction flagger holding a stop sign was navigated without driver intervention.
- **Tesla patents autonomous camera lens cleaning system.** The patent covers a controller that detects debris via image quality degradation and triggers a fluid dispenser and contoured wiper blade — no human intervention required. Per the electrified channel, this is critical infrastructure for unsupervised autonomy in snowy or dirty conditions.
- **~1 million Tesla vehicles now paying FSD subscription.** Jeff, the supply chain guest on brighterwithherbert, cited this figure against a pool of approximately 4–5 million FSD-capable vehicles, framing it as the AI revenue flywheel beginning to scale.

## Humanoid Bots
- **Optimus v3 unveil: approximately 60 days out.** Bhakdi calculates from Elon Musk's April 22, 2026 earnings call statement of "90 days" that the reveal lands in July, with August as a conservative estimate. He frames it as a potential above-$500 stock catalyst.
- **Bhakdi on Optimus consumer pricing and scale.** He argues a barely functional Optimus at $30,000–$40,000 per unit could sell millions to enterprise and consumer buyers on novelty alone, and equates selling millions of units at ~$40,000 each to doubling Model Y revenue. He expects critical functionality by 2027 at the latest.
- **Figure signs commercial deployment deal with Catalyst Brands.** Figure will deploy humanoid robots in Catalyst's distribution and logistics network (JC Penney, Aeropostale, Brooks Brothers) starting in Reno, Nevada, focused initially on the facility's Joey Pouch sorting system. No pricing, scale, or specific timeline was disclosed.

## Energy
- **Meta purchasing $200M of Tesla Megapack for Wyoming AI data centers.** A separate Enbridge-Meta project adds a 365 MW solar / 200 MW Megapack storage installation, also sourced from Tesla Energy, per brighterwithherbert.
- **Tesla moving into longer-duration storage with "Mega Block."** The brighterwithherbert channel referenced a Houston project as Tesla's next step beyond the Megapack for extended-duration grid applications.

## Electric Vehicles
- **Cybertruck Q1 2026 sales: 4,186 units, down from ~7,000 in Q1 2025.** Overall Tesla Q1 2026 vehicle registrations came in at approximately 105,000, down from approximately 130,000 in Q1 2025, per Jo Bhakdi. Bhakdi attributes the Cybertruck weakness primarily to mispricing: originally promised at $39,000, it launched at approximately $95,000.
- **Cybertruck Dual Motor AWD VINs now being assigned.** Sawyer Merritt confirmed Tesla has begun assigning VINs for the $59,000 variant, with first deliveries expected in coming weeks. Separately, new Premium AWD orders in most U.S. areas have shifted to August–September 2026 delivery — a 3–4 month wait suggesting demand ahead of production.
- **Ferrari Luce unveiled at $640,000; Q2 2027 US delivery.** Specs: 122 kWh battery, 280-mile EPA range, 350 kW peak charging, 1,050 hp, Jony Ive interior. Bhakdi notes the Tesla Model 3 Performance at ~$60,000 outperforms it on key metrics at less than one-tenth the price. Sawyer Merritt's posts calling it "one of the ugliest EV designs ever" drew 6.6K and 7.9K likes.
- **Tesla Roadster confirmed for Giga Texas; unveil not in May 2026.** Lars confirmed the build location on the Ride the Lightning podcast, with "a lot of things unfolding in the next months."
- **Model 3 RWD lease now only $30/month more than Toyota Corolla Hybrid** on the same term and same money down, despite a roughly $10,000 higher sticker price, per Bhakdi.
- **Tesla opens Supercharger prepaid passes to non-Tesla EVs in China via WeChat.** Trial pass: ~$12 for 50 kWh (~$0.23/kWh); enjoy pass: ~$88 for 400 kWh (~$0.22/kWh), usable at V3 and V4 stations.
- **Volvo to access Tesla Supercharger network across Europe starting Q4 2026.** More than 20,000 stations across 29 countries; Volvo also planning NACS transition in Japan and South Korea by 2029.
- **First Tesla Supercharger goes live in Colombia** (Bogotá), per Tesla Charging.
- **Elon corrected viral battery patent claim.** The new dry battery electrode patent for 4680 cathode production will "significantly reduce the cost of battery cathode production" — not cut total battery costs by nearly half, as had been circulating.

- **Bhakdi's Tesla fair value: ~$890 this year; potential overshoot to ~$1,000** at 15,000 Cybercabs. He characterizes market consensus at $550–$600 as failing to price in a large-scale 2026 robotaxi deployment.

Chinese EVs remain blocked from US and European markets.

Kalshi showed odds as high as 70% briefly; the contract for a merger before May 1st sat at 44% as of recording, per electrified. The electrified host characterized the sourcing quality as weak but noted the discussion has entered public discourse.
