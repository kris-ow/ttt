3. **Model Y L showroom rollout:** The Model Y L has started arriving at select U.S. Tesla showrooms for customers to sit in, though demo drives are not yet available — a step toward U.S. launch/demand generation for the model.

Separately, Frontier Airlines announced Starlink WiFi across its entire ~1,000-aircraft fleet (including other Indigo Partners airlines), targeting first equipped aircraft in early 2027; total commercial aviation Starlink adoption is now cited (per the poster's own research) at over 7,500 aircraft across 46 airlines/operators. These items are SpaceX-specific and only tangentially relevant to Tesla investors as a signal of Musk-ecosystem momentum.

Minor competitive/market color, not directly quantifiable for Tesla's thesis.
