This video is a deep dive into Nvidia's new Vera Rubin chip platform, announced alongside a blockbuster earnings report, and argues that its main economic effect will be a collapse in the cost of "agentic" AI work (multi-step AI tasks) rather than faster chatbots. The SpaceX/Tesla relevance is limited to one detail: SpaceX's AI division reportedly plans to use standalone Nvidia Vera CPUs for CPU-heavy workloads supporting Grok's AI agents.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **SpaceX AI to use Nvidia Vera CPUs for Grok agent workloads:** The video states that SpaceX's AI division plans to use standalone Nvidia Vera processors (the CPU half of the Vera Rubin platform) to handle CPU-heavy work — code execution, tool management, data processing — surrounding Grok's AI agents, while Rubin GPUs handle the core model computation.

## Market & Competition
1. **Nvidia's Q2 earnings and Vera Rubin platform:** On August 26, 2026, Nvidia reported over 100% revenue growth despite already being a ~$5 trillion company, which the host calls unprecedented. Nvidia's new Vera Rubin platform (Vera = CPU, Rubin = GPU, combined in a 72-GPU rack called VR200 NVL72) is designed around the demands of AI "agents" — systems that plan, use tools, and run long multi-step tasks — rather than simple prompt-response chat.
2. **Published Nvidia performance claims for Vera Rubin:** Per Nvidia's own specifications cited in the video: one Rubin GPU offers up to 5x the low-precision inference throughput of Blackwell; each GPU has up to 288GB of HBM4 memory with up to 22 TB/s of bandwidth (~2.8x Blackwell); NVLink 6 gives each GPU 3.6 TB/s of two-way bandwidth across the 72-GPU rack; the Vera CPU has 88 cores and up to 1.2 TB/s memory bandwidth, completing agentic training/data tasks up to 1.8x faster than typical Intel/AMD server CPUs; power controls allow up to 40% more GPUs within the same megawatt limit; and tray assembly time drops from hours to about a minute due to cableless design.
3. **Benchmark and revenue-per-gigawatt claims:** On the SemiAnalysis-created "Agent X" benchmark using a large model referred to as DeepSeek-V4-Pro, Nvidia claims Vera Rubin delivers up to 30x more agent throughput per megawatt than GB200 Blackwell and up to 35x lower cost per million tokens. Nvidia also stated on its August earnings call that its revenue opportunity per gigawatt of power has risen from roughly $18 billion with Hopper to $25 billion with Blackwell to $40 billion with Vera Rubin — meaning falling per-token costs coincide with rising Nvidia revenue capture per gigawatt.
4. **AWS/Nvidia and financial-sector partnerships:** Nvidia and Amazon AWS plan to deploy 2 million additional Nvidia GPUs (including Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, and Rubin Ultra) across AWS's global infrastructure in 2027 and 2028, even as AWS continues using its own in-house chips. Nvidia also announced partnerships with six major investment firms aiming to mobilize more than $500 billion in outside capital for AI infrastructure over time.
