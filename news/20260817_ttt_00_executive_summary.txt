## Robotaxi
- Nevada's Transportation Authority granted Tesla only 10 fully autonomous vehicles versus the 5,000 requested for Clark County, capped at 45 mph, no airport pickups, mandatory labeling and human supervision; expansion requires new approval. Zoox holds a 100-vehicle Nevada permit and is already charging for Strip rides; Waymo and Uber's Aviari have pending applications. Electrek estimates Tesla's total national unsupervised fleet at roughly 20 vehicles, calling the 5,000-vehicle ask "delusional" given current deployment scale.
- Cybercab (no steering wheel) is reportedly slated to launch in Austin by end of August, starting with employee rides before limited public rollout days later; Tesla has reportedly completed emergency-responder training for Cybercabs. Sightings have expanded to King of Prussia, PA and Franklin, TN (15 units).
- Safety data through mid-July shows zero at-fault Tesla robotaxi incidents in June and partial July; only two at-fault incidents total since February, both minor.
- An unsupervised Robotaxi drove through plastic bollards in Austin over the weekend — no damage or injuries, but flagged as a scaling-readiness concern.
- Waymo received CPUC approval to expand across the Bay Area and LA and add Sacramento and San Diego.
- Morgan Stanley's Andrew Percoco says investors want clearer evidence of robotaxi scaling (fleet size, city density, unsupervised miles, unit economics); called FSD adoption reportedly hitting 55% a bright spot.

## Autonomous Driving
- Electrek's Fred Lambert reports his Tesla on FSD v14.3.6 activated a turn signal and began exiting toward a shoulder for an exit already passed, decelerating from 110 to 102 km/h before he disengaged manually — he argues high reliability is breeding dangerous complacency.
- Tesla's v14.3.6 notes cite an upgraded reinforcement-learning training stage and an MLIR-rewritten AI compiler for 20% faster reaction time; Electrek notes Tesla reported a record 207 Autopilot/FSD crashes to NHTSA in one month, with narrative details redacted on 99.9% of 3,763 driver-assist crash reports since 2019.
- Tesla's supervised FSD is active in 12–13 countries versus Waymo's three (US, UK, Japan), per one commentator's framing; an EU regulatory vote in October could widen the EU footprint.

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus factory in Texas is under fast steel construction — a four-floor building roughly 3,000 ft by 1,000–1,100 ft, per Joe Tegtmeyer — with Cortex 2 nearing completion and expected to use ~260 Megapacks once fully built; Cortex 2 was exempted from a new Texas data-center construction pause due to its near-complete status.
- Hyundai's Atlas robot handed off a ball at a World Cup halftime; hosts assessed it as largely pre-trained sequences with some teleoperation, requiring a custom radio link to beat stadium RF interference.

## Energy

Hiring targets tied to tax abatements: 375 by end of this year, 750 by end of 2027, 1,500 by end of 2028.
- New Megapack projects: a $115M, 112-unit Steel River East project in Newcastle, Australia (online late 2027), and a $290M, 300 MW/1,200 MWh Arevon Energy facility online in Poway, California.
