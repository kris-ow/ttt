Electrek's one Tesla-relevant article today covers Italy granting provisional approval to Tesla's FSD (Supervised) in Europe, the first big EU market to do so, ahead of a bloc-wide committee vote expected in December at the earliest.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Italy approves Tesla FSD ahead of EU vote:** Per Electrek, Italy's transport ministry granted provisional approval to Tesla Assisted Driving, the name Tesla now uses in Europe for FSD (Supervised), making Italy the 12th European country to allow it. Transport Minister Matteo Salvini announced it on X after what he called a thorough technical feasibility check, saying Italy was getting ahead of an EU process he described as still being defined. Tesla Europe said rollout begins soon, with no firm date. The system remains Level 2 with full driver responsibility. Electrek calculates that Italy's roughly 59M people nearly double the share of Europeans covered, to close to 30%. For the EU-wide vote, which needs at least 15 of 27 states representing 65% of the population, Electrek estimates Germany (which said in October it would back approval) plus the 12 approving countries would reach roughly 47%. France refused national approval in July and Spain hasn't recognized the Dutch approval. Fred Lambert calls it a real win for Tesla and says it shifts momentum going into December. [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/20/tesla-fsd-italy-approval-assisted-driving/)

## Editorial Stance
Electrek frames Italy's approval as a genuine win for Tesla and its HW4 owners, while remarking that it is "pretty crazy" that Europe will now get a safer FSD than the US, and speculating this could show up in the data if it is ever released.

Notable quotes and data:
- Per Electrek, Italy is the 12th European country to approve FSD (Supervised); ten others recognized the Dutch RDW's April 10 approval before it, mostly small markets.
- Electrek: the Netherlands plus the ten earlier countries total about 70 million people (roughly 15% of the EU); Italy alone has about 59 million.
- Electrek: Germany plus the 12 approving countries would cover roughly 47% of EU population versus the 65% needed.
- Per Electrek, Tesla dropped the "Full Self-Driving" name in Europe after Germany's Transport Ministry called it misleading and cut the maximum speed margin from 50% to 10% over the limit; on November 16 the RDW approved stricter driver monitoring, including hands on the wheel above 130 km/h.
