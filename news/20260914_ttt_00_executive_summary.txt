## Robotaxi
- A Cybercab ride from 99 Ranch Market to the Domain took 70 minutes instead of ~10, after the vehicle avoided highways and at-grade rail crossings, running parallel to Capital Metro's Red Line before crossing via an East Austin bridge, per Electrek. The article notes Tesla's surface-street-only routing applies fleet-wide, to Model Y robotaxis as well as Cybercab, and comes days after NHTSA opened an audit into Tesla's self-certification of the pedal-free Cybercab and a reported bollard collision.
- Texas Robotaxi registrations rose from 186 (Aug 13) to 439 a month later, a 136% increase (~20% week-over-week), per brighterwithherbert, driven by simultaneous growth in vehicle count, service area, and hours in Austin; Dallas and Houston remain small pilots.
- Tesla is reportedly hiring AI safety operators in Chile, Argentina, Uruguay, and Colombia, interpreted as prep for South American Robotaxi expansion.
- A speaker cited Waymo at roughly 4,000 vehicles versus Tesla's roughly 1,000 (Robotaxi plus ride-hail combined), with Waymo six years into operation versus Tesla's roughly 14 months.

## Autonomous Driving
- Ashok Elluswamy said upcoming FSD v15 will bring earlier hazard prediction, faster reaction time, and significantly better collision avoidance; a newer FSD rollout this weekend reportedly adds automatic collision avoidance beyond standard automatic emergency braking for FSD users.
- Electrek reiterated its prior claim that Tesla's robotaxi fleet crashes roughly once every 57,000 miles, about four times worse than an average human driver, contrasting with Waymo, which it says already runs fully driverless on freeways.
- Hyundai said it is "deliberately delaying" its autonomous driving timeline, betting a slower data-collection approach lets its Atria AI system overtake Tesla's FSD within five years — Hyundai's own stated claim, not an independent assessment.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla confirmed the next-generation Roadster unveiling for October 1 in Waco, Texas; invites went to reservation holders and the Roadster website was updated for the first time in about nine years. Elon Musk previously told Joe Rogan the demo would be "crazier than anything" from James Bond; speculation about flying/hovering capability remains unconfirmed.
- A guest commentator (Jeff Lutz) called the Roadster a low-volume "halo" product unlikely to move Tesla's sales needle, and flagged high odds the live demo doesn't go smoothly.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX CFO Brett Johnson announced a new AI compute deal worth $1.1B/month ($13.3B/year) starting December 1, customer unnamed, reaffirming a path to $100B in annual recurring compute revenue; total compute run rate is estimated near $41B/year by December.
- SpaceX confirmed acquiring turbine maker APR Energy to bring turbine blade/vane casting in-house using rocket-pump material science; per ARK Invest's Daniel Maguire, this could pull SpaceX's AI compute buildout forward ~18 months, from ~2GW toward ~10GW by end of next year.
- Musk said he is "highly confident" SpaceX will launch Nvidia Vera Rubin (VR NVL72)-based AI computers in space next year — a more aggressive timeline than most forecasters, who reportedly expect later in the decade.
- Musk cited an analyst consensus of at least a 15GW AI chip power shortfall in 2027, equated to power for ~12 million average US homes, framing SpaceX's turbine and orbital-compute buildout as a hedge against terrestrial grid bottlenecks.
- SpaceX and Tesla are each targeting roughly 100–200GW of solar manufacturing capacity by 2029, split between Houston (terrestrial) and Texas (space-based) panel types.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Starbase Louisiana (125,000 acres, described as a $100B facility) sits near Henry Hub's natural gas infrastructure, positioned to supply both Starship rocket fuel and turbine-generated power for data centers, per farzad-fm.
- A Starship test flight (flight 14) is reportedly delayed to September 22, per jobhakdi.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Barron's writer Al Root published a reversal on the SpaceX–xAI merger, writing "we were wrong about SpaceX" and that shareholders now look like winners as revenue "simply accelerated faster than anyone expected."
- A host (Jo Bhakdi) attributed a mid-week SpCX price drop to a share unlock (~328M shares, ~15% of float), following a similar ~300M-share unlock the prior week, with another expected in ~10 days.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- Falcon 9 executed autonomous liftoff, stage separation, orbital insertion, and droneship landing; Musk cited over 600 autonomous rocket landings to date. Falcon 9 flew ~180 missions last year and is expected to keep flying for years under multi-year military contracts even as Starship matures.

## SpaceX — Starlink
