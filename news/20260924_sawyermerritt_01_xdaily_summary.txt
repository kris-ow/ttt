Separately, CAA's 2026 EV Circuit (a Canadian EV comparison) ranked the Model 3 and Model Y near the top for energy efficiency and lowest public charging costs, with the Model 3 posting the lowest cost at $7.28 per 100 km among vehicles tested. These are modest but positive demand/cost-competitiveness signals.

3. **Bentley enters the EV market with the Torcal SUV:** Bentley unveiled its first EV, the Torcal SUV, starting at $218,000, with 373 miles WLTP range, a 113 kWh battery, peak charging at 400kW (10–80% in 16 minutes), and a curb weight of 6,000 lbs. Notably, Bentley added synthetic engine noise generated from real recorded instruments (drums, viola, bass guitar) to preserve a "Bentley sound," and kept a traditional grille (now housing illuminated diamond-style lighting behind polycarbonate) for brand identity reasons — illustrating how legacy luxury automakers are approaching electrification while preserving ICE-era brand cues. This is competitive/market context rather than a direct threat to Tesla's mass-market positioning, given the price point.
