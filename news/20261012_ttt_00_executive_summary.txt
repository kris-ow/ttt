## Robotaxi

- NHTSA's Office of Defects Investigation opened petition file DP26010 on October 9 (received September 23) targeting model year 2026 Robotaxis (Model Y and Cybercab) over alleged school-zone speeding, per electrek. Evidence is limited to three social media videos and four NHTSA-logged incidents, with no crashes or injuries; NHTSA has until roughly January 21, 2027 to decide whether to open a formal investigation, which could lead to a recall.
- NHTSA's filing estimates the affected Robotaxi fleet at about 1,000 vehicles — the first federal figure given for fleet size, per electrek, though the article speculates this may reflect Texas DMV-registered vehicles rather than cars actively in commercial service.
- This is the third federal action against Tesla's driverless fleet in about five weeks, following an October 2025 FSD investigation into traffic-law violations and an existing NHTSA Special Order demanding sworn proof the Cybercab (no pedals or steering wheel) meets federal safety standards; Tesla's answers were due September 30 and have not been made public.
- Separately, Tesla registered 150 new Cybercabs with the Texas DMV, per Sawyer Merritt, bringing the cumulative registered total to 319, up from roughly 169 — nearly double. An unnamed source cited by a trading-focused channel said Dallas deployment is next with ~300 Cybercabs staged there (unverified).
- Tesla's own Robotaxi app claims the Austin fleet scaled to 300+ Cybercabs one month after launch, an 8x increase in 30 days, per investingagainstthegrain.
- Sawyer Merritt reported anecdotally that during a fan "X Takeover" event in Austin, Cybercabs appeared more numerous giving rides than Waymo vehicles in clips taken minutes apart, and that Cybercab wait times stayed short throughout the event despite roughly 2,000 attendees in town — both firsthand, unverified observations.

## SpaceX — Starship

- Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas, quoted by Sawyer Merritt, called the upcoming Starship Flight 15 (targeted roughly one month out) potentially "SpaceX's biggest test towards Starship reusability since the IPO," with Flight 16 (targeted before year-end) framed as potentially an even bigger milestone.
