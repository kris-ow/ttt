## Robotaxi

1. **Cybercab production trajectory and fleet size estimate:** Jo Bhakdi presents his own conservative production estimates based on drone footage (attributed to Joe Tegtmeyer) and sightings across multiple cities. He estimates a minimum of 10 Cybercabs per day produced in April (≈220 units), 20 per day in May across 22 production days (≈440 units), and 30 per day so far in June (≈360 units), putting the total produced above 1,000 Cybercabs as of mid-June 2026. He projects roughly another 1,000 units added by the first week of July, reaching approximately 2,000 by July and 3,000–4,000 by August, arguing that no manufacturing operation of this scale proceeds without a high-conviction deployment plan.

2. **Cybercab sightings in Houston, Las Vegas, and beyond:** Bhakdi shows images dated June 13, 2026 of Cybercabs staged in a parking lot in Houston and a separate batch in Las Vegas, both fitted with decorative wheel caps. He interprets the wheel caps — which were absent during earlier testing — as a cosmetic preparation signal suggesting public deployment is near. He also references sightings in California (Palo Alto), Nevada, Texas (Austin), Chicago, Boston, and Florida.

3. **Near-term deployment prediction and stock impact:** Bhakdi states he remains convinced that "multiple dozens" of Cybercabs will be deployed (likely unsupervised) in June 2026. He estimates that a release of 40–50 unsupervised Cybercabs would be a 10–20% short-term catalyst for the stock, sufficient to push Tesla back to its prior all-time high area of approximately $490. He notes that 1,800 unsupervised robotaxis would represent a more fundamental repricing of the stock — sufficient to add roughly 5% to free cash flow — and could push Tesla to $600–$800 or higher with exuberance.

4. **Elon's earnings call statement on FSD version gating:** Bhakdi references Elon Musk's comments from the most recent Tesla earnings call, in which Elon stated clearly that robotaxi rollout does not require waiting for FSD version 15. Bhakdi summarizes: the rollout will happen at "huge scale" on version 14, and version 15 is scheduled for approximately end of 2026.

5. **Tesla comment on new robotaxi cities:** A viewer (Marty) comments that four to five new robotaxi cities should be going online in the next two weeks, referencing something Tesla said. Bhakdi acknowledges this but expresses uncertainty about whether Tesla's stated timeline can be relied upon.

## Autonomous Driving

1. **FSD 14.3.4 released and receiving exceptional reviews:** FSD version 14.3.4 was released on Friday, June 13, 2026. Bhakdi shares detailed feedback from a prominent Tesla community member ("Doge Army General") who reports driving 250 miles on the build. Key highlights from the review: it is described as "by far the best overall FSD build by a wide margin," more refined than 14.3.3 (which was in some respects a regression from 14.3.2), the car no longer brakes hard for animals but eases off the accelerator more naturally, parking destination selection is improved, and the reviewer describes it as "one step closer to robotaxi." The reviewer notes that major upgrades are arriving roughly every two to three weeks and that the pace is accelerating.

2. **Architectural significance of the 14.3.x rebuild:** Bhakdi explains that the FSD 14.3 release (the major one in the series) represented a fundamental rebuild of the underlying system that removed legacy components, enabling faster iteration cadence going forward. He emphasizes that a change in the slope of progress — not just a new release — is the meaningful signal, and that 14.3.x represents exactly such an inflection.
