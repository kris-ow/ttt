## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla Semi enters commercial demo with Covenant Logistics:** Covenant Logistics kicked off a 3-week Tesla Semi demo on May 13, with an executive accompanying a professional driver on the first delivery run in California — a full freight load using the 500-mile range variant, starting with a 200-mile round trip. This is a notable commercial validation moment for the Semi, as a major logistics operator is actively evaluating the vehicle under real operating conditions. The demo's outcome could influence fleet purchase decisions across the trucking industry.

2. **Model Y municipal fleet adoption in Covington, KY:** The city of Covington, Kentucky announced it is adding seven Tesla Model Y vehicles to its police fleet, directly replacing seven internal combustion vehicles. Research sponsored by the city estimates the switch will save approximately $23,000 annually in fuel and maintenance costs. While a small deployment, municipal fleet adoptions provide steady, visible demand and serve as credibility signals for broader EV fleet procurement decisions.

3. **Trump-Elon-Jensen Huang meet China VP in Beijing:** President Trump, Elon Musk, and Nvidia's Jensen Huang met with Chinese Vice President Han Zheng in Beijing on May 13, as part of a larger delegation of U.S. business leaders. No specific announcements were reported in the posts, but the meeting is notable for Tesla investors given Tesla's significant manufacturing and sales exposure in China. Any outcomes related to tariff relief, market access, or trade normalization could be materially relevant to Tesla's near-term financials and China demand trajectory.

4. **Model Y new "Frost Blue" color:** Tesla appears to have introduced a new "Frost Blue" color option for the Model Y, which Merritt noted approvingly with an image. Minor product news, but new color variants can modestly refresh demand and average selling price mix.

5. **Competition — Mercedes AMG GT EV with simulated engine sounds:** The upcoming all-electric Mercedes AMG GT 4-Door EV features artificial V8 engine noises and simulated gear shifts. This is a market color data point illustrating how legacy automakers are navigating EV adoption by preserving internal combustion sensory cues — a strategy Tesla has not pursued. Noteworthy as a differentiation and brand philosophy contrast.

6. **Starlink displacement of legacy satellite broadband (tangential):** Hughesnet has lost 57% of its broadband subscribers (~880,000) since Starlink launched in December 2020 and has issued a going-concern warning citing insufficient cash to service its debt. While primarily a SpaceX/Starlink story, it reinforces the competitive moat and cash-generating potential of the broader Elon Musk enterprise ecosystem, which some Tesla bull cases incorporate through conglomerate or Elon-premium valuation frameworks.
