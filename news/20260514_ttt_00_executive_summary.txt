## Autonomous Driving
- **NHTSA milestone — 2026 Model Y first U.S. vehicle to pass new ADAS benchmarks.** The Model Y cleared evaluations for pedestrian AEB, lane-keeping, blind spot warning and intervention, forward collision warning, and crash imminent braking. NHTSA administrator Jonathan Morrison called it "a significant step forward" that "sets a high bar for the industry."
- **Tesla Vision predictive pre-crash deployment.** Tesla's camera-based AI detects an imminent collision and pre-deploys airbags and seat belt tensioners up to 70 milliseconds earlier than conventional impact-sensor systems. The feature ships standard on new vehicles and arrives via free OTA update to existing Tesla Vision cars.
- **Photon count reconstruction demonstrated.** Elon Musk shared before/after images showing Tesla AI rebuilding a sun-blinded driving scene from raw photon data — restoring lane markings, vehicles, and road geometry invisible to the human eye, reinforcing the cameras-only perception case.
- **Belgium announces 5,000 km FSD Supervised trial.** The Director of Transportation for Flanders stated Belgium will test FSD over approximately 3,000 miles of Flemish roads to evaluate infrastructure differences from the Netherlands, targeting a provisional EU-wide type approval. Officials said Tesla could begin testing immediately with administrative monitoring and stated they are "removing all obstacles to be first."
- **Sweden and Ireland also advancing.** The Swedish Transport Agency issued a public statement supporting FSD and said a broader consumer launch could come "at the earliest during the summer or autumn of 2026." Ireland confirmed it is in active talks with Tesla.
- **Tesla hiring self-driving data collection supervisors in 33 cities**, including Bellevue, WA — a signal of expanding unsupervised robotaxi infrastructure.
- **FSD 14.3.2 real-world performance:** Brian White (FutureAzA) described an unsolicited maneuver where FSD detected a hazard-lit stopped vehicle, initiated a reverse-and-signal workaround, then cancelled autonomously when the obstacle cleared — without driver input.

## Robotaxi
- **Cybercab production running at ~20–40 units/day** per Brian White (FutureAzA), with output expected to increase gradually. Joe Tegtmeyer's drone footage counted 70+ Cybercabs in the factory outbound lot.
- **Cybercabs spotted in Miami, San Francisco, and Houston** on public roads. Production units are visually distinguishable from validation units — shiny finish vs. matte gold. Florida Governor DeSantis commented on the Miami sighting, calling Tesla "absolutely top-notch."
- **One-to-three month estimate before commercial service.** The hosts' view: the FSD software stack is proven; the new Cybercab form factor still needs real-world validation miles before entering true commercial robotaxi operation in Austin.
- **Cybercab charge port is bottom-rear, provisional design.** Wireless charging requires wideband FCC approval for precise vehicle positioning — that approval was only granted approximately February–May 2026, making mass wireless charging hardware deployment in this timeframe physically impossible.

## Humanoid Bots
- **Fremont production line converting to Optimus.** With Model S and Model X production ended, Tesla is removing existing manufacturing equipment and installing the Optimus assembly line. Long-term target: up to 1 million Optimus units per year at Fremont.
- **Optimus hiring across 7+ locations signals summer deployment on track.** Tesla is actively recruiting "data collection operator – Optimus" roles in Austin, Fremont, Palo Alto, San Diego, Marina del Rey, Henderson (NV), and Lyra — consistent with Musk's plan to deploy the first 5,000 units internally for training data generation.

## Electric Vehicles
- **Last Model S produced; farewell delivery event May 20th.** The final unit was signed by employees and will be displayed at Fremont; Steve Jurvetson — who bought the first Model S — is reported to be purchasing the last. The model launched in 2012 and delivered approximately 750,000 units combined with the Model X over their lifetimes.
- **Tesla U.S. Q1 dominance: ~110,000 combined Model Y and Model 3 sales** (roughly 78,000 and 31,000 respectively per KBB/EV Wire data). The nearest competitor sold approximately 10,000 units — roughly 7x fewer than the Model Y alone.
- **Europe sales up 29% YoY; Giga Berlin hits 750,000 cumulative vehicles** and announced a 20% production increase — a forward demand signal. Tesla broke a 13-month European sales losing streak, selling 13,740 units across EU/UK/EFTA.
- **Giga Shanghai April exports described as record-level since 2022**, with approximately 79,478 Model 3 and Y units produced. Domestic China rankings that excluded exports were characterized by the hosts as deliberately misleading.
- **Lower-cost Cybertruck variant (~$59,000) deliveries expected June 2026.** Steel wheels — the visual indicator — had not yet appeared in factory drone footage as of today.
- **Tesla Semi enters 3-week commercial demo with Covenant Logistics** (began May 13), running a full freight load on the 500-mile range variant in California. A major logistics operator actively evaluating under real conditions is a meaningful commercial validation step.
- **Covington, KY adds seven Model Y police vehicles**, replacing seven ICE units, with an estimated annual savings of $23,000 in fuel and maintenance.
- **Model Y new "Frost Blue" color option** appears to have launched.

## Energy
- **Solar Roof effectively discontinued.** Tesla peaked at ~23 installations per week (Q2 2022) — 97.7% below its 2016 target of 1,000/week — and has installed roughly 3,000 total U.S. systems per Wood Mackenzie. Tesla stopped reporting solar deployment figures in Q1 2024 and redirected customers to third-party certified installers.
- **Pivot to TSP-420 conventional panel, launched early 2026.** Assembled at Gigafactory Buffalo, the panel features an 18-zone power optimization system that addresses Solar Roof's string-inverter shading weakness. Tesla also launched a new solar lease product and its first solar team expansion in five years.
- **Musk announced 100 GW/year U.S. solar manufacturing target at Davos (January 2026)** — roughly 300x Tesla's current ~300 MW annual capacity in Buffalo. Tesla is reportedly in talks to purchase $2.9 billion in Chinese solar equipment from Suzhou Maxwell Technologies.

Nissan remains in deep financial distress; a proposed Honda-Nissan merger collapsed. Mazda is delaying its first dedicated EV platform to at least 2029 and cutting EV investment from ~1.5 trillion yen to ~830 billion yen through 2030. Porsche announced 500+ job cuts and is exiting three non-car business lines.

Anthropic's revenue run rate reportedly climbed from ~$9B (end-2025) to ~$30B (early April 2026). The deal doubles Claude rate limits and removes peak-hour throttling — material for any Tesla investors tracking the broader Elon ecosystem.
