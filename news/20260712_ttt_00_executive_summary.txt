## Brief

## Robotaxi
- Cybercab interior details emerge: roughly 21-inch display versus Model 3's 15.4-inch, with cabin airflow direction and temperature controllable from Tesla's robotaxi app ahead of wider deployment.
- Bhakdi says Tesla is stuck in a trading range until "tangible Cybercab deployment news," which could land "any day" or take two to three months; he argues the jump from 1,000 to 10,000 Cybercabs would be "very marginal" once scaling begins.
- Bhakdi separately floats $1,000/share as achievable "if we suddenly see 10,000 cyber caps... without even Optimus."

## Autonomous Driving
- FSD V14 "Lite" spotted running on HW3 hardware in South Korea, signaling continued international rollout of the newer FSD version on older compute (per Sawyer Merritt digest).
- A single-user anecdote claims 1,000 miles since last disengagement on FSD (Supervised) — not company-verified.

## Humanoid Bots
- Tesla's Optimus vacuum-robot feature appears operational based on shared media, an early qualitative signal of expanding household task capability; no technical details disclosed.
- Cern Basher (futureaza) argues SpaceX could become one of Optimus's largest long-term customers for off-planet use, though Tesla's addressable Earth market (potentially hundreds of millions of units) dwarfs the off-planet opportunity (thousands of units), per the host's counterpoint.

## Financials
- AI5 chip has "reached tape-out" at Samsung Foundry, per a Samsung Principal Engineer, to be manufactured at the Taylor, Texas fab on Samsung's latest 2nm process for integration into "Tesla's newest products"; no mass-production timeline confirmed.
- Basher notes SpaceX purchased roughly $500 million in Megapacks from Tesla last year plus ~$147 million in other goods/services — modest against Tesla's ~$100 billion revenue base, cited as context for merger-related tax efficiency arguments.

## Market & Competition

- Bhakdi flags a broader "sentiment shift" across AI/mega-cap names (Micron, Nvidia, Meta, Tesla) following Grok 4.5's launch, Meta's new "Muse" model, and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 release; he has made Micron and Nvidia his second- and third-largest holdings behind Tesla.
