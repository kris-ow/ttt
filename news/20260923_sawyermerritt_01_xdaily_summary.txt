The poster notes having had early access for a couple of weeks prior to launch. This deepens Tesla's software/AI ecosystem and ties Grok (xAI) more tightly into the in-car experience, a potential differentiator versus competitors.

5. **FSD licensing offer to automakers reiterated:** A post reiterates that Tesla has for years openly invited other automakers to license FSD, and that none have accepted — a qualitative point (no new numbers) underscoring Tesla's isolated position in supervised/autonomous driving software licensing versus competitors.

7. **Starlink promotional offer (minor):** SpaceX is offering select U.S. Starlink Residential customers a free Starlink Mini (normally $200) as a portability perk, a minor customer-acquisition/retention tactic with limited standalone investment signal.
