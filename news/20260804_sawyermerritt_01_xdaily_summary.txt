## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Redline Reviews' first-ever FSD test is highly positive:** Redline Reviews, a car review channel with 1.1M YouTube subscribers, tested Tesla FSD (Supervised) for the first time while reviewing the Model Y L, calling it "the future" and comparing it favorably to GM's Super Cruise, which the reviewer had previously called the best system. The channel explained the delay was due to Tesla not having a press fleet for years, combined with the reviewer's heavy travel schedule testing other brands (~60% of time) and testing 2-3 press cars weekly. This is a high-reach (3.1K likes) third-party validation moment for FSD's perception among mainstream auto reviewers.

2. **FSD V14.3.7 real-world demonstrations:** Multiple posts (including from Sawyer Merritt himself) showcased FSD V14.3.7 on the Model Y L/Premium Long Wheelbase, including a viral clip of the car correctly interpreting a construction worker's hand signals to proceed, and a 40-minute Santa Monica-to-West Hollywood drive with zero interventions, handling a construction zone, an unexpected u-turn by another car, and other real-world edge cases. A separate post covered driving impressions of the Model Y L Premium Long Wheelbase, noting adaptive damping improves ride comfort over the shorter Model Y Premium; the Model Y Performance was also referenced but details were cut off.

3. **FSD cumulative miles milestone:** Tesla's global fleet crossed 13 billion cumulative miles driven on FSD (Supervised), up from 12 billion on July 17, 2026 — roughly 1 billion miles added in about two weeks.

4. **Tesla European registrations growth:** Tesla registrations in France were up 86% year-over-year in July (second-best July ever), and up 52% in Denmark from July 2025. The poster suggested Tesla is prioritizing incentive-rich markets like France and Germany, where volumes could rise further in H2 2026.

5. **Starlink competitive pressure on legacy satellite internet:** Hughesnet parent EchoStar filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy after running low on cash and losing subscribers to Starlink; EchoStar's Q2 broadband subscribers fell to 622,000, a loss of 59,000 in the quarter. This is a concrete competitive data point showing Starlink's market share gains against legacy satellite ISPs.

6. **Starlink Mobile / direct-to-device roadmap:** SpaceX stated that by the end of 2028, Starlink Mobile will have rapidly upgraded the constellation via launching thousands more satellites, aiming to provide continuous full global direct-to-device coverage, positioning itself as the first and likely only provider of "true" global coverage in this category.

7. **Elon Musk comments on SpaceX stock:** Musk was quoted/referenced discussing SpaceX stock as "a big opportunity right now" (video/clip referenced, no further detail on venue given in the post).

8. **Boring Company expansion:** A new Boring Company tunnel station opened at the SAHARA hotel in Las Vegas, with more tunnels and stations planned — a minor but relevant infrastructure expansion signal for a Musk-adjacent venture.
