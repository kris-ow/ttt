The article reports that Elon Musk announced on X (on Christmas Eve) that Tesla is changing its mission statement wording from "Sustainable Abundance" to "Amazing Abundance," dropping "sustainable" entirely, with Musk explaining the new phrase is "more joyful."

## Market & Competition
1. **Mission statement evolution:** The article traces Tesla's mission statement history: originally "To accelerate the advent of sustainable transport," then broadened to "sustainable energy" after the SolarCity acquisition and energy storage expansion, then shifted to "Sustainable Abundance" around the Master Plan Part 3 launch and the rise of Optimus, and now to "Amazing Abundance" per Musk's latest announcement. The author interprets this as signaling Tesla's primary focus has shifted from environmental/climate goals toward an economic "abundance" narrative tied to Optimus and AGI, with EVs and renewables recast as tools toward that end rather than the goal itself.
