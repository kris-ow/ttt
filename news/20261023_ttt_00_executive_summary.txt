## Brief

- Robotaxi operating hours extended to 11pm
- Fort Bend County OKs up to 35 years tax breaks for Tesla solar plant

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Tesla and SpaceX each confirmed board-level special committees to evaluate "deepening Tesla's relationship with SpaceX," explicitly including — "without limitation" — a possible business combination, per both companies' official statements and 10-Q filings. Musk said no agreement exists yet and any deal needs 50% of all outstanding Tesla shares to vote yes; SpaceX's filing reportedly says no shareholder vote is needed there given Musk's >80% voting control.
- Merger premium and timing remain pure speculation across sources, with no consensus: estimates range from 13% (rejected as inadequate by one host) to a host-stated 30% minimum (~$485/share), to Alexandra Merz's personal 4.2 exchange-ratio preference (~61% premium), to James's 30–40% "Wall Street standard" and a ~3-SPCX-per-Tesla-share estimate. A December 2027 closing window was floated by Merz as speculative, tied to Musk's 2028 tax bill — explicitly flagged as guesswork.
- SpaceX raised its 2027 annual recurring revenue guidance to $500 billion–$1 trillion, up from a reported $300–500 billion range given internally in August, and is guiding to $33–37 billion in Q1 revenue next quarter — up from roughly $18 billion in revenue six months ago.
- SpaceX is building "miniyard" data center sites in Texas (near Starbase/Brownsville), productized to build in about five months each; one site has three under construction with room for up to ten more, each potentially generating $30–50 billion in annual revenue if spot power pricing holds, per panel discussion.

## Robotaxi
- Cybercab sightings in Austin rose from 45 (Aug 31) to 169, with Model Y robotaxi sightings up from 269 to 420 (combined 589), per an account cited by hosts; a separate tracker logged 868 total Cybercab sightings in Austin for the week (670 driverless) and ~300 Cybercabs spotted in Dallas. Week-over-week registered-vehicle growth is estimated around 17%.
- Robotaxi operating hours extended from 10pm to 11pm; Musk said the prior cutoff was mainly about avoiding hard-to-see animals at night ("gray kittens on gray asphalt in the dark").
- Tesla VP Ashok Elluswamy said Cybercab and Model Y share the same software stack (soon in a V15 release) and expects the first customer-owned vehicles to join the robotaxi fleet around Q2 next year, with the fleet still mostly company-owned at that point. Cybercab/robotaxi service is expected to next expand to Dallas, with Las Vegas, Jacksonville, Phoenix and San Antonio also named.
- A third-party analysis cited by one panel warned Tesla could hit a Cybercab production ceiling as early as April next year if capacity doesn't exceed a stated 125,000-unit level and mileage growth continues; Lars Moravy said Tesla has been "bursting" the line to test throughput.
- Tesla's cumulative autonomous/Robotaxi miles reportedly passed 3.3–3.4 million, with week-over-week growth accelerating from ~17% to ~21% across recent data points — still roughly two orders of magnitude behind Waymo's 271M+ cumulative autonomous miles (through June).
- On the earnings call, Tesla said Robotaxi may become "noticeable" around Q2 2027 and "meaningful" in Q3/Q4 2027 — a year-long slip from Musk's earlier mid-2026 timeline, per one host's analysis.

## Autonomous Driving
- A German Ministry of Transport official reportedly presented in favor of EU-wide FSD approval after Tesla agreed to cap speed-limit overshoot at 10% (down from up to 50% previously allowed) as part of resolving regulatory objections; Musk thanked the official publicly. FSD is now live in nine European countries, and Musk said he'd be surprised if EU-wide approval took longer than Q1.
- China progress on FSD and Optimus remains comparatively stalled, with no confirmed Optimus production line in Berlin or Shanghai, versus active lines in Fremont and planned larger-scale capacity in Austin.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla has cancelled Cybertruck for Australia and New Zealand, emailing reservation holders (some with deposits since November 2019) that plans were discontinued; Tesla will refund payments within 30 days and offer a free one-year FSD (Supervised) subscription to those who order a Model 3 or Model Y instead. Electrek estimates Cybertruck sales at 7,000–8,000 units in Q3 2026, a ~30,000/year pace versus a line built for 250,000 units annually.
- Tesla is adding a record average of 2,800 FSD subscribers per day, generating $92 million/month (~$1.1 billion annualized), with half of all FSD users now on a paid subscription.

## Financials
- Tesla's Q3 net profit was $787 million, down 43% year-over-year; TTM net income of $3.2 billion remains far below mega-cap peers (Nvidia, Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft each generate $129B–$244B TTM net income), per one host's analysis.
- Auto gross margin ex-credits came in at 18.1% versus ~17% street expectations; free cash flow beat was reportedly driven by reduced capex rather than improved profitability. Energy gross margin was 33% in the quarter, with Tesla guiding to further improvement despite earlier guidance for margins to normalize to the "mid-to-low 20%" range long-term.
- Tesla reiterated a target of roughly 5 million vehicles produced annually by 2030, with most of the incremental ~3 million expected to come from Robotaxi units.
- Tesla's 2027 Megapack production capacity is "nearly sold out," per the Q3 earnings call.

## Energy
- Fort Bend County, Texas commissioners voted 4-0 to approve agreements offering up to 35 years of local tax breaks for Tesla's proposed $10 billion "Project Crystal Sun" solar factory, including a 10-year 100% property tax abatement followed by a 20-year 90% rebate period (extendable to 25 years if Tesla sustains 12,000 average employees for two years). Tesla has not committed to building there and must start construction by June 30, 2027 to qualify; Electrek's Fred Lambert flagged weak clawback protections and questioned whether Tesla is using the offer as leverage against a competing state.

## Humanoid Bots
- Musk indicated Tesla doesn't plan to sell Optimus commercially until possibly end of 2027, pushing meaningful revenue contribution to 2028 at the earliest, per one host's reading of the earnings call.
