The host (jobhakdi) explains why SpaceX stock surged roughly 15% over two days (6.5% on the day of filming), crediting Morgan Stanley analyst Adam Jonas's bullish note but arguing Jonas missed the real driver — SpaceX's terrestrial compute buildout — while also laying out his own trading strategy around SpaceX's share-unlock schedule and views on a future Tesla-SpaceX merger.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Morgan Stanley reiterates bullish stance:** Morgan Stanley analyst Adam Jonas reiterated an "overweight" rating on SpaceX with a $300 price target, calling the stock "unusually cheap" and citing a roughly 40% discount to mega-cap AI peers on a growth-adjusted basis; the host puts SpaceX's current market cap at $2.28 trillion. The host agrees with Jonas's bullish conclusion but disagrees with his reasoning, arguing Jonas understates the importance of SpaceX's compute buildout versus power/chipmaking bottlenecks and the "successful Starship catch" as catalysts.
2. **Share unlock dynamics driving short-term price action:** The host attributes recent price suppression and swings to scheduled SpaceX share unlocks — a 328 million share unlock on September 24 and another 328 million share unlock due October 9 (roughly 60 million shares estimated to be sold, per the host). He estimates sellers dump 10-20% of unlocked shares into the market over several days, suppressing price ahead of the unlock date; a larger ~1.3 billion share unlock is expected in roughly 31 days from the video's filming. The host disclosed he has opened a tactical short (via short calls) ahead of the October 9 unlock while remaining long via far out-of-the-money short puts, and plans to go "all long" after the large unlock passes.
3. **Tesla-SpaceX merger odds and terms:** The host estimates a ~90-95% chance of a Tesla-SpaceX merger by 2028, and puts roughly 25% odds (which he called "high") on a merger announcement specifically around October 15. He now expects SpaceX would need to pay only a conventional acquisition premium (his estimate: roughly 30%) rather than a larger "ratchet" protection, reasoning that SpaceX's post-IPO volatility has declined and its pricing has become more predictable since the unlocks began trading in a tighter range. He illustrates that a 30% premium on a $500 Tesla share price would imply roughly $650, which he says would be easily affordable if SpaceX's valuation approaches $200 (unspecified units, likely billion) by year-end.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Compute buildout framed as core profit driver:** The host argues SpaceX's primary growth driver is delivering AI compute rather than Starlink or Starship catches, estimating each gigawatt of compute capacity brings in roughly $40 billion in annual profit (his estimate). He references Elon Musk's commentary on 220,000 Blackwell 300 GPU clusters coming online, with the host expecting two guaranteed and "very likely" three such clusters this year, and at least three additional gigawatts added next year (versus Elon's stated figure of 10, which the host says he doesn't believe).
2. **Star Mind satellite development:** The host describes "Star Mind" as a new AI-compute satellite reportedly being developed jointly by SpaceX and Nvidia, distinct from the current Starlink V3 satellites. He describes it (his own characterization, not sourced to a specific announcement) as having a 75-meter wingspan versus Starlink V3, roughly six times the solar panel area, with an estimated 250 kilowatts of power per satellite (such that four satellites would yield 1 megawatt and 4,000 satellites would yield 1 gigawatt), housing Nvidia NVL72 racks with Vera Rubin chips, intended to come online starting in 2028.
3. **2027 revenue estimate:** The host projects a 2027 revenue run-rate estimate of around $250 billion for SpaceX, with nearly all of it coming from compute buildout rather than Starlink, based on his assumption of roughly five total gigawatts of compute capacity deployed (three additional gigawatts next year beyond existing ones).

## SpaceX — Starship

Flight 15 is expected in the coming weeks (unannounced date, estimated roughly a month out by Jonas).
