## Brief

- Tesla China domestic sales down 11.9% YTD; exports more than doubled
- Canada pauses Shanghai Model 3 Premium orders on disputed "quota" claim
- SpaceX plans double-catch attempt for Starship flight 15, before Thanksgiving
- SpaceX requests launch cadence jump to 456/yr at Starbase
- Digital Optimus now clears ~half of Diablo campaign by vision alone
- NHTSA reviewing Tesla Robotaxi school-zone speeding petition

## Robotaxi
- Tesla says it scaled to 300+ Cybercabs in Austin one month after launch, an 8x increase in 30 days (319 registered); Ashok Elluswamy said Cybercabs are now "everywhere" in the city, with Dallas launch expected soon.
- A weekend X takeover showed Austin camera data with Cybercab sightings briefly exceeding Waymo's (21.6 vs 18.3 per 10,000 snapshots) during peak hours — Electrified's host calls this a limited anomaly, not a trend; on a normal day Waymo led (~3 vs ~1.57 per 10,000).
- NHTSA is reportedly examining whether Tesla Robotaxis exceeded speed limits in school zones, a preliminary probe covering an estimated 1,000 vehicles; no decision yet on a full investigation.
- Tesla faces a ~December 18 deadline to launch robotaxi service in Las Vegas under its 120-day permit window.
- Unit economics remain contested: Morgan Stanley pegs Tesla's robotaxi cost near 81 cents/mile vs. ~$140/mile for Waymo's current system, while Goldman sees a 5–30 cents/mile Cybercab cost advantage at scale; a viral ~$1.25/mile margin estimate was flagged as unrealistic given Tesla's long-term target of roughly 20 cents/mile net margin.

## Autonomous Driving
- Tesla is quietly testing unannounced dynamic headlight leveling on some Model 3 and Cybertruck units, adjusting low-beam aim for pitch changes; described as early-stage and still gathering data.
- V15 is partially live with only ~40% of planned features deployed; Elluswamy previously tied 24/7 robotaxi operations to the V15 rollout.

## Humanoid Bots
- Elon Musk said Digital Optimus — a vision-only screen agent — can now complete roughly half of the Diablo campaign and is being trained on Counter-Strike and League of Legends, aiming to generalize skills toward browsers, spreadsheets and enterprise software, with eventual transfer to the physical Optimus robot.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla's China domestic sales fell 11.9% YTD to 381,088 units (lowest nine-month total in years); Giga Shanghai exports more than doubled to 361,972 units, now driving wholesale volume as Beijing prepares to cut the EV export VAT rebate from 13% to 9% on April 1, 2027 — an Electrek estimate (not Tesla-reported) pegs the cost at roughly $600M/year.
- Tesla paused orders for the Shanghai-built Model 3 Premium in Canada, citing an exhausted import quota; Canada's official tracker shows two-thirds of the national quota unused, and Electrek calls Tesla's explanation questionable.
- US EV average transaction price rose to $55,879 in September (+2.4% MoM, -3% YoY) as incentives shrank to an 11% discount rate, a 2026 low; Tesla's ATP rose 3.3% MoM to $54,345, roughly flat YoY, with Cybertruck averaging $98,109 across ~1,500 units sold.
- Tesla Semi deliveries are underway with strong early reception, though one analyst cautioned published cost-per-mile figures understate true TCO; Tesla's US EV market share rose to 52% in Q3 from 41% a year earlier, despite the federal tax credit's expiry.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Musk said SpaceX now plans to attempt catching both ship and booster on flight 15, characterizing the prior flight 14 engine issue as a minor software glitch and estimating flight 15 could fly shortly before Thanksgiving; he wants flight 16 to reuse both stages entirely.
- Ship 40 is now vertical at Starbase alongside three other Starships, intended to generate data on a flown ship's heat shield, Raptors and avionics.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- SpaceX is seeking to raise Starbase's annual launch/landing limits from 25/50 to as many as 456 launches and 912 landings at full build-out; FAA/DOT public comment runs through November 30.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX's largest compute/hosting deals were cited at a combined $41.2B/year (Anthropic ~$15B, a new December-starting customer ~$13.3B, Google plus Reflection AI ~$14.2B) — the stated inputs don't fully reconcile to the total as given; SpaceX's CFO reportedly guided to $100B in contracted annual revenue by year-end.
- Musk detailed the Terafab chip-manufacturing project at Giga Texas, aiming for roughly 10 design iterations a year versus an industry norm he described as 2–3 years, while acknowledging he still has much to learn about chip manufacturing; a guest pushed back on both ends of that framing as overstated.
- Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas highlighted SpaceX's hybrid orbital-terrestrial "distributed inference cloud" combining Star Mind satellites and terrestrial data centers; Musk called the thesis "a very big deal."
- Musk said effective coding AI requires large training datasets, citing this as the rationale behind SpaceX/xAI's Cursor acquisition, which also brought enterprise sales relationships.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- A 13%-of-float SpaceX share unlock (328M shares) occurred around October 9, with another 11%-of-float unlock (328M shares) due October 24; SpaceX reports earnings November 3.
- Musk confirmed phones launching in 2027–2028 will include chips supporting a new, higher-bandwidth Starlink mobile spectrum, which he called the "final important element" for full US phone coverage.
