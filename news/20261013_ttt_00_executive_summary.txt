## Brief

- China: domestic Tesla sales down 12%, exports double YTD
- Beijing to cut EV export VAT rebate April 2027, ~$600M/yr hit estimated
- Canada pauses Model 3 Premium orders on import quota claim
- Robotaxi: Dallas fleet additions, wait times "basically zero"
- Grok AI assistant now live inside Cybercab rides

## Electric Vehicles

- Tesla's China domestic sales fell 11.9% YoY through September to 381,088 units, the lowest nine-month total in at least four years; Q3 retail dropped 16% to 142,133 units, underperforming the broader NEV market's ~10% Q3 decline (per Electrek, citing CPCA data).
- Giga Shanghai exports more than doubled YTD to 361,972 vehicles, already exceeding full-year export totals for 2023–2025 combined years individually; Shanghai's Q3 wholesale volume (275,111 units) equaled 57% of Tesla's global Q3 deliveries, with domestic Chinese buyers just 29% of that total.
- Tesla paused orders for its Shanghai-built Model 3 Premium (RWD and AWD, $39,490 CAD) in Canada, citing an exhausted import quota, with resumption expected March 2027; only the Performance AWD trim remains orderable. Canada's own quota tracker shows just 15,931 of 49,000 annual permits used as of October 9 — two-thirds remain unused — a discrepancy Electrek flags as unexplained, possibly tied to an undisclosed per-manufacturer set-aside.
- Smart unveiled a new electric city car starting at €20,000 with up to 290 km WLTP range, a 35.8 kWh LFP battery, and 65 kW DC fast charging — a new low-cost European competitor.

## Financials

- China's VAT export rebate on NEVs drops from 13% to 9% on April 1, 2027. Electrek estimates this could cost Tesla roughly $600 million annually at current export volumes (~480,000 vehicles/year), based on an assumed ¥220,000 average export value per car — explicitly the outlet's own estimate, not a Tesla-reported figure.
- China's NEV exports topped 4 million units in the first nine months of 2026, more than double last year, meaning the rebate cut pressures the broader industry, though Tesla is especially exposed since exports now account for roughly half of Giga Shanghai's production.

## Robotaxi

- Tesla added more Robotaxis to the Dallas fleet and Cybercab wait times are now "basically zero," per Sawyer Merritt, signaling expanding service availability; no new fleet totals were disclosed beyond RobotaxiTracker.com's stale baseline.
- Grok's "Bot" assistant is now integrated into Cybercab rides, letting riders sign in once with sessions carrying over between trips; demonstrated use case included adding an item to an Amazon cart mid-ride.

## Market & Competition

- Canada's EV import quota mechanics: a national cap of 49,000 Chinese-made EVs per year (growing to 70,000 by 2030), allocated first-come-first-served with no published per-manufacturer limit — raising questions about whether Tesla's claimed exhaustion reflects an undisclosed OEM-specific allocation.

Notable quotes and data:
- Tesla's Canada order page: "Model 3 Premium orders paused after high demand exhausted Tesla's import quota... expected March 2027."
- China Model 3 Premium wholesale export value assumption: ¥220,000/vehicle → ~¥8,800 ($1,235) rebate-cut cost per car (Electrek estimate).
- Canada sub-$35,000 EV imports: 2,597 units in August, then 7 in September, 0 in October — despite 33,069 of 49,000 annual permits still unused.
