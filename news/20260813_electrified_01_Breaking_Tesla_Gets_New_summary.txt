This video breaks down Tesla's newly issued Nevada Transportation Authority permit for robotaxi operations in Las Vegas, correcting widespread misreporting of the vehicle cap, and covers several other Tesla developments including a new Powerwall leasing option, resolution of a multi-year Swedish labor dispute, and a large new Supercharger filing in San Francisco.

## Robotaxi
1. **Nevada grants Tesla a limited robotaxi permit, not the widely reported 5,000-vehicle figure:** The Nevada Transportation Authority (NTA) approved Tesla to operate as an autonomous vehicle network company, but capped the fleet at 10 fully autonomous vehicles — far below the 5,000 vehicles Tesla requested in its June 5, 2026 application for the first 12 months. The host clarifies the 5,000 number circulating online refers to Tesla's original request, not the approved figure from the July 27, 2026 NTA decision document.
2. **Approval comes with tight operational restrictions:** Operations must stay within the Las Vegas Strip Corridor (roughly a 4-mile stretch of Las Vegas Boulevard) in Clark County, are barred from roads with speed limits over 45 mph, and pickups are prohibited within a quarter-mile of Harry Reid International Airport unless separately authorized. Tesla's request for access to Harry Reid and Henderson Executive airports was not granted. Vehicles must be marked "robotaxi," passengers must be notified they're in a driverless vehicle, and Tesla must report any safety/system incident within five business days.
3. **"Human supervision" requirement refers to remote operators, not in-car safety monitors:** The host clarifies that the SAE-standard human supervision language in the permit refers to Tesla's remote operations support for passengers, not a safety driver physically in the vehicle — though he notes Tesla could still choose to launch with an in-car safety monitor given the NTA's cautious approach.
4. **Waymo has also applied for the same Nevada permit and appears close to approval:** Waymo has been testing in the area with employee-only rides but has not yet received the permit as of this video. A new multi-year partnership between Waymo and the Las Vegas Raiders (offering dedicated pickup/drop-off at Allegiant Stadium and VIP fan perks) is cited by the host as a signal that Waymo's approval is likely imminent.
5. **Host expects a new Tesla city launch this month or next, possibly San Antonio before Las Vegas:** The host reiterates a prior prediction of a new city launch within the current or following month and suggests San Antonio could launch before Las Vegas, with Las Vegas needing additional weeks to operationalize after approval. He references Tesla's Ashok Elluswamy's comments (from an unspecified call) emphasizing prioritizing geographic breadth over speed in any single city due to varying infrastructure and regulatory challenges.
6. **Texas DMV fleet-count website reportedly taken offline:** The host says the public Texas DMV site that had been providing Tesla vehicle registration counts has been shut down, with the last known reading at 181 registered vehicles (not necessarily active) in Texas; he plans to try to regain access.
7. **Tesla hiring for Las Vegas robotaxi support roles:** Tesla is hiring fleet support specialists for day and night shifts in Las Vegas, consistent with preparations for a local launch.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Cybercab light bar color changes spotted in Austin:** A Cybercab was photographed with a purple light bar in Austin, following an earlier sighting this week with an American flag-colored light bar, suggesting Tesla is customizing light bar colors for events and/or to help riders identify their pickup vehicle.

## Energy
1. **Tesla launches Powerwall leasing via Tesla Electric:** Customers can now lease two Powerwall units with no installation cost, paired with a Tesla Electric plan; Tesla cited an $87/month bill credit example. Tesla Electric manages charging/discharging as a grid asset and guarantees a minimum 20% backup reserve. A buyout option is available after year five. Eligibility requires living in a Texas area with retail electric choice and excludes homes with existing solar, since solar complicates the pure grid-asset load profile Tesla Electric relies on for the credit.

## Market & Competition
1. **Tesla Sweden labor dispute ends after Tesla buys out striking union members:** Union EF Metall has suspended all industrial action against Tesla Sweden after Tesla bought out remaining striking employees, ending a roughly three-year standoff without Tesla signing a collective bargaining agreement. This ends associated sympathy strikes and blockades affecting docks, postal service, transport companies, and Superchargers. No buyout dollar figure was disclosed. The host frames this as a win for Tesla despite the union's critical framing.
2. **Large new San Francisco Supercharger site filed:** Per a third-party report cited by the host, planning permits show a 124-stall V4 Supercharger station with a 400-square-foot amenity building, open to the general public (not robotaxi-exclusive). The host notes only two bathrooms are planned for 124 stalls, speculating Tesla expects heavy robotaxi usage at the site.
3. **Giga Texas chip fab ("Terafab") under construction:** A third-party video reportedly shows construction underway on Tesla's Terafab chip fabrication facility at Giga Texas.
