The space shuttle, which
flew from 1981 to 2011,
cost about
$54,000 to put
one kilogram into
low Earth
orbit.  $54,000
for something that weighs
about the same as carry-on
luggage.  At those
prices, the only
customers were governments,
like NASA,
the Department of Defense,
or the European
Space Agency.    No
one else could
afford it.
Obviously, if you
were a startup with a
brilliant idea or an
orbital business
, it didn't matter
how good
your idea was.
The ticket there was too
expensive.  So, the door
was locked,
no one could get through, and only
sovereign states
had the key.  Then
SpaceX came along with the Falcon 9
and brought the cost down to
about
$2,700 per kilogram, a
95% reduction.
