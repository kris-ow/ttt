## Brief
- Tesla's 2025 Impact Report confirms Cybercab as primary Robotaxi vehicle
- New multi-GWh storage deals: Essiosoft (~15 GWh) plus NAT Power (~25 GWh)
- Robotaxi testing spotted expanding to New Orleans
- Q2 deliveries beat estimates but TSLA stock fell ~4% same day
- Tesla launches "Cell Giga Challenge" to crowdsource Giga Berlin 4680 fixes
- Cyber SUV speculation grows after background model spotted near Cybercab

## Robotaxi
- Tesla's 2025 Impact Report confirms Robotaxi launched June 2025 in Austin with Model Y, with Cybercab designated the primary fleet vehicle going forward — a two-seat, steering-wheel-free design using 4680 cells and steer-by-wire, built via a new reaction injection molding process that cuts manufacturing cycle times from hours to minutes.
- Cybercab production figures remain contested: Lars Moravy previously hinted at "multiple hundreds" (250 cited elsewhere), one host speculates north of 500 already deployed, and another claims drone footage shows over a thousand units accumulating in Dallas, Houston, and Austin lots. None of these are independently confirmed.
- Robotaxi testing footprint is expanding: Model Ys with test equipment spotted in New Orleans, and testing is reportedly underway in over 30 cities total, though commentators caution this doesn't mean imminent official launches.
- Cybercab accessibility testing conducted with blind/visually impaired riders ahead of broader deployment, per Sawyer Merritt.
- Nine branded Cybercabs seen staging for testing in Austin; four spotted together in Miami, where unsupervised service is reportedly live with an unknown but likely small fleet.
- Tesla leased Austin's largest-ever speculative industrial building (682,000 sq ft), five miles from Giga Texas, completion expected January — purpose unconfirmed but widely read as Robotaxi/Cybercab-related infrastructure.

## Autonomous Driving
- Impact Report data: FSD (Supervised) drivers see 8x fewer major collisions, 7x fewer minor collisions, and 6x fewer off-highway collisions than the U.S. national average; FSD also measured 5% more energy-efficient than manual driving across 65 million sampled 2025 miles.
- FSD (Supervised) expanded to Australia, China, New Zealand, and South Korea in 2025, with Belgium, Denmark, Estonia, Lithuania, and the Netherlands added in 2026.
- New Tesla app update (limited rollout) will show users whether FSD is actively engaged during a drive.
- Commentators continue to frame FSD capability — not manufacturing, regulation, or maintenance — as the sole gating factor for Robotaxi scaling.

## Financials
- JPMorgan reiterated Neutral, $475 price target, citing Q2 deliveries and energy storage above expectations and a strong European recovery; Baird set $522, citing Q2 beats and energy deployments up 41% YoY. Neither analyst commits to specific Robotaxi numbers.
- Tesla stock closed at $42.90, down ~4%, despite the Q2 delivery beat — commentators attribute this to a "buy the rumor, sell the news" dynamic, noting delivery beats alone no longer move the stock absent Robotaxi scaling progress.
- Tesla launched a "Cell Giga Challenge" inviting startups to pilot manufacturing tech on its live Giga Berlin 4680 line, applications open through July 24, program starting August 2026. Tesla previously committed an added $250 million to more than double Grünheide's cell target from 8 GWh to 18 GWh (cumulative investment nearing €1 billion); Electrek notes this signals real gaps remain in a program it calls "Tesla's most troubled manufacturing effort."

## Energy
- New storage deals: Essiosoft (~15 GWh across UK, Western Europe, GCC, India) plus a previously announced ~25 GWh NAT Power deal total ~40 GWh — comparable to a full year of Megapack production, potentially nearing $10 billion in Tesla revenue over the next few years.
- Tesla launched "Tesla Home," a home energy management system built on its existing Opticaster AI optimization software, now standard in every Powerwall with new smart-breaker integration (reportedly via Eaton); commentary frames it largely as a repackaging of existing tech rather than a new capability.
- Impact Report: Supercharger network 100%-renewable-matched for 5 years running (Giga Berlin for 3); customers saved over $1 billion in energy costs in 2025; 213,000+ Powerwalls enrolled in VPP programs delivering 20+ GWh to the grid; Tesla Semi delivers 50% TCO reduction in California, 20% across the US.

## Electric Vehicles
- Cybertruck demand looks weak: combined Q2 deliveries for Cybertruck, Model S, Model X, and Semi totaled 12,364 units, which one commentator called inadequate for a line built for a quarter-million annual run rate; others counter that order backlogs stretching to January suggest intentional pacing rather than a demand shortfall.
- Speculation intensified around a possible "Cyber SUV" using composite body panels on the Cybertruck platform, potentially as a Model X replacement, after a small background model was spotted near the Cybercab display — no official confirmation. A six-door variant was reportedly floated by Elon Musk in recent weeks but treated as pure brainstorming.
- Hundreds of Model Y L units have arrived in Japan following the country's best-ever Tesla sales month (~4,000 registrations, +183% YoY).

## Market & Competition
- Commentators argue legacy automakers (GM rehiring ex-Cruise staff, Ford's Blue Cruise) are pursuing autonomy as a sales feature rather than building robotaxi fleets, leaving Tesla as the only brand offering a test-drivable point-to-point autonomous vehicle in North America.
- Nvidia's Alpamayo self-driving demos are characterized as multiple years behind Tesla's FSD stack, per one commentator's assessment.
- Rivian announced a 75-million-share public offering (~$1.5B, ~5% dilution), cited as a data point on competitor capital pressure.
