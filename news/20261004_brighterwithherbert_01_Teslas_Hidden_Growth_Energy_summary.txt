The video, featuring analysis attributed to Cern Basher, reframes Tesla's energy storage business (Powerwall and Megapack) as "electric vehicles without wheels" by converting battery deployments into car-equivalent units based on a 75 kilowatt-hour pack size, arguing this reveals stronger underlying growth than vehicle delivery numbers alone suggest.

## Energy
1. **Energy business expressed as EV-equivalents hits a record:** Using a 75 kWh battery pack as the conversion unit, Tesla's energy storage deployments (Powerwall and Megapack) over the trailing 12 months equate to 669,333 "equivalent cars" — described as a record figure for the current quarter.
2. **Multi-year growth trajectory:** The equivalent-car figure for energy storage has more than doubled over a few years, rising from roughly 314,000 (two years prior) to about 500,000 (one year prior) to the current 669,333.

## Financials
1. **Combined deliveries plus energy equivalents reach a record:** Adding official vehicle deliveries to the energy-storage EV-equivalent figure produces a combined total of 2.412 million units, which the speaker calls a record for the company and presents as a more complete picture of Tesla's overall growth.
