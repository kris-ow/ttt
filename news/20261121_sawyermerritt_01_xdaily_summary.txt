## Key Posts / Themes

1. **FSD in Europe reaches a 12th country:** Sawyer reports that FSD (Supervised), branded Tesla Assisted Driving in Europe, was officially approved in Italy, making it the 12th European country. Approvals so far include Belgium, the Netherlands, Denmark, Lithuania, Finland, Estonia and Slovenia, among others. In a separate post showing FSD on tight Italian streets, Sawyer says it is "only a matter of time" before EU-wide approval. That is his opinion, not an announced decision.

2. **Robotaxi expansion and Cybercab demand:** Tesla expanded the Dallas Robotaxi service area by 1% to 82 square miles, a small incremental step. Sawyer also asked whether highway access is coming soon, a question rather than a confirmed development. Marques Brownlee said he will honor the spirit of his 2024 Cybercab bet. He was invited to buy one, submitted a $1,000 non-refundable deposit and selected Austin, Texas. The post drew 5.5K likes, the most of the day.

3. **Optimus manufacturing scale-up:** Sawyer says Tesla's new 7 million sq ft Optimus factory at Giga Texas looks set to use Giga Presses. This is his read of what is shown rather than a Tesla announcement. It suggests Tesla is applying its vehicle casting approach to humanoid robot production at very large scale.

4. **SpaceX compute financing:** Apollo and Goldman Sachs priced $44 billion in senior and subordinated notes to fund SpaceX's purchase of Nvidia Vera Rubin chips and related equipment. The banks call it the largest GPU-backed financing in history. The deal was reportedly oversubscribed and upsized. It shows capital markets will fund SpaceX's AI compute buildout with debt secured against the hardware.

5. **SpaceX launch-site interest:** Philippine President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. said the country is trying to get into the business of offering launch sites and is in talks with SpaceX. No agreement or timeline was stated.

6. **Tesla international and other ventures:** Tesla opened vehicle ordering in Argentina, its 4th South American country after Chile, Colombia and Uruguay. Model Y and Model 3 deliveries start in March 2027. The Boring Company's Las Vegas loop currently has 13 active stations, with more on the way.
