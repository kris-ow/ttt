## Market & Competition
1. **Anthropic's wet lab and drug ambitions:** Investor Dave Blandin (on the Moonshots podcast) states Anthropic has opened a wet lab — a facility for experiments with real cells and chemicals — announced on September 23rd, and argues Anthropic is working model generations ahead of what it releases publicly, intending to use advanced models to develop and sell drugs rather than provide them to customers; this is presented as Blandin's interpretation/prediction, not a confirmed Anthropic plan. Anthropic itself frames the lab as being for fundamental biology research, not drug discovery, though its head of life sciences said in a past statement that the company has started its own drug development programs — a small number of early, pre-human-testing programs targeting neglected diseases. The video contrasts AI's current per-million-token pricing with the $31.6 billion in 2025 revenue cited for Merck's Keytruda as motivation for AI firms to move into pharma economics. Anthropic says it will limit itself to Phase 1 (first human trials) to avoid competing with pharmaceutical clients, but CEO Dario Amodei reportedly wrote that the company is "also interested in these later stages," which the video's narrator suggests signals that limit could shift as profits grow.
