This short update covers Tesla's latest Full Self-Driving adoption figures, including North American attach rates on new deliveries, global active subscription counts, and recent European regulatory approvals.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD attach rate hits record in North America:** Tesla says over 75% of new North American deliveries now include the FSD supervised system, a record figure that rose by almost 20 percentage points in one quarter.
2. **Active FSD subscriptions climb to 1.74 million:** Active FSD subscriptions reached 1.74 million, up from 1.48 million the prior quarter and 1.04 million a year earlier — a 67% year-over-year increase. The count includes one-time purchases and monthly subscriptions but excludes free trials.
3. **FSD gains approval in four more European countries:** Since the end of the second quarter, FSD has received approval in Croatia, Slovenia, the Czech Republic, and Slovakia, bringing availability to nine EU countries with over 100,000 customers using it there.

Notable quotes and data:
- Over 75% of new North American Tesla deliveries now include FSD supervised, a record, up nearly 20 percentage points quarter-over-quarter.
- Active FSD subscriptions: 1.74 million (up from 1.48 million last quarter, 1.04 million a year ago; +67% YoY).
- FSD approved in Croatia, Slovenia, Czech Republic, and Slovakia since end of Q2; now live in nine EU countries with 100,000+ customers.
