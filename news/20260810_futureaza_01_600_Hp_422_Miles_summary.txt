This video is a dealership walkthrough and test drive of the Chevy Silverado EV (3LT trim) at Plat Auto in Milwaukee, Oregon, hosted by Brian with salesperson Jack. The core content is a feature tour (interior, towing capability, range, Super Cruise driver assist, cameras) and driving impressions, with only incidental Tesla comparisons.

## Market & Competition
1. **Tesla Autopilot no longer standard on any trim:** The host states that Autopilot is no longer included on new Teslas "on any of them" — buyers must now pay a subscription for FSD rather than getting basic driver assist for free, which he calls a "huge negative" and predicts will be a dealbreaker for part of the market, since competitors (GM Super Cruise, Nissan ProPilot Assist 2.0) now offer free hands-free or lane-centering highway systems.
2. **Silverado EV vs. Ford Lightning and Cybertruck comparisons:** The hosts compare the Silverado EV's suspension favorably to a rented Ram pickup and note it doesn't feel "Cybertruck impressive" on turning radius but doesn't need to be; they also reference AAA using Ford Lightnings as mobile EV-charging recovery vehicles and mention Cybertrucks being used similarly at car shows to charge other vehicles.
3. **Tesla vehicle ride comparisons:** Jack notes the Silverado EV's ride is smoother than his own Tesla Model Y and that a Model 3 Performance driven in an autocross setting still feels its weight — offered as general ride-quality color commentary, not a substantive thesis point.

Notable quotes and data:
- Silverado EV (as configured): roughly 600+ horsepower, 422 miles of rated range, with a tow package.
- Towing cuts range roughly in half, leaving an estimated 200+ miles of range while towing, per Jack.
- Fully loaded truck weight cited as approximately 9,000 lbs.
- Premium package (Super Cruise, 22-inch wheels, midgate) priced just under $7,000 as an option, per Jack.
- Host: Tesla Autopilot "no longer available... let alone included" — "it's FSD or nothing," and FSD "you must rent it."
- Jack: GM Super Cruise is "right up there with the kind of Tesla highway capabilities" in his eyes.
