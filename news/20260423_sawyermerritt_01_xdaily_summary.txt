## Executive Summary
Tesla's Q1 2026 earnings call delivered across-the-board beats on revenue, EPS, gross margins, and free cash flow, while providing a dense stream of forward-looking catalysts across FSD/Unsupervised autonomy, Cybercab, Optimus, and Energy. The signal is strongly bullish: Cybercab production has started, robotaxi paid miles nearly doubled sequentially, FSD subscriptions hit a new record, Optimus factory construction is underway at two sites, and Elon laid out a detailed — if still cautious — roadmap toward unsupervised FSD and large-scale robotaxi expansion.

---

## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla Q1 2026 Earnings Beat Across the Board:** Tesla reported Q1 2026 results that beat consensus on every major metric. Non-GAAP EPS came in at $0.41 vs. $0.33 estimated; revenue was $22.4B vs. $21.4B estimated; gross margin was 21.1%; GAAP net income was $1.45B vs. $1.17B estimated; and free cash flow was $1.44B. Sawyer characterized the report as "unexpectedly great." Capital expenditure guidance was raised to $25B for 2026, up from the prior "$20+ billion" figure, reflecting accelerated investment across AI, Optimus factories, chip fabs, and energy infrastructure. GPU training capacity is expected to nearly double in Q2 2026.

2. **FSD Roadmap: V14.3, V15, and the Path to Unsupervised:** Several high-signal posts from the earnings call define Tesla's FSD trajectory. FSD V14.3.2 began rolling out to early access users on the same day, with a notable architectural change: Tesla has unified the model across Actually Smart Summon, FSD, and Robotaxi for more capable and consistent behavior. Elon confirmed that FSD V14.3 is "the last piece of the puzzle for Unsupervised FSD," but said major architectural improvements are coming before large-scale robotaxi deployment will make sense. FSD V15 — confirmed to run on AI4 hardware — is targeted for end of 2026 and described as "a complete overhaul." Elon also confirmed that AI5 is not a near-term requirement for cars since AI4 will achieve Unsupervised FSD, though an AI4 upgrade using "neuro gene" architecture is planned. Critically, HW3 hardware has been officially confirmed as incapable of achieving Unsupervised FSD; Tesla is offering discounted trade-ins for AI4 vehicles and hardware upgrade paths for HW3 owners, with plans to set up migration infrastructure. FSD V14 Light will be released for HW3 owners at end of June 2026. Elon's best guess for Unsupervised FSD reaching customer-owned vehicles is Q4 2026, with a geography-by-geography gradual rollout approach.

3. **Robotaxi Expansion: Paid Miles, Fleet Milestones, and Regulatory Clarity:** Tesla reported 1.7 million paid robotaxi miles as of Q1 2026, up from 610,000 at end of Q4 2025 — nearly a 3x increase sequentially. Tesla said paid robotaxi miles "nearly doubled" sequentially in Q1. Elon targeted having robotaxi operating in "a dozen or so states by end of this year," emphasizing a cautious approach with no injuries reported to date. A significant regulatory development emerged: Tesla's Lars confirmed that the Cybercab will not be subject to California's annual 2,500 autonomous vehicle cap (applicable to vehicles with steering wheels), meaning Tesla's deployment scale will be limited only by its own production and operational capacity. Tesla noted continued groundwork for geographic expansion of the robotaxi service.

4. **Cybercab Production Has Started:** Elon confirmed on the earnings call that Cybercab production has begun at Giga Texas. He cautioned that initial production will be slow given the entirely new product and manufacturing setup, with an exponential ramp expected toward year-end. Tesla stated that once in production at scale, Cybercab will begin replacing the existing Model Y robotaxi fleet and is expected to become the highest-volume vehicle in the fleet over time. Q2 2026 will see Tesla's GPU training capacity nearly double, supporting the AI development needed for the robotaxi platform.

5. **Optimus: Two Factories, Production Timeline, and Gen 3 Reveal:** Optimus-related announcements were among the most significant of the call. Tesla officially confirmed the new Optimus factory at Giga Texas, with a stated long-term annual production capacity of 10 million robots. The first large-scale Optimus factory — replacing the Model S/X lines in Fremont — is targeting a production start in the "late July/August timeframe," with Model S/X production ending at end of May 2026, followed by several months of new line installation. A second Optimus factory is being constructed at Giga Texas and will start production in "Summer of next year" (Summer 2027). Regarding Optimus Gen 3 (V3), Elon said it "maybe" will be unveiled in mid-2026, but Tesla is deliberately withholding a reveal because competitors conduct frame-by-frame analysis and copy their work. Tesla also introduced the concept of "Digital Optimus" — an AI intelligence layer designed to automate digital workloads, complementing real-world AI for vehicles and humanoid robots.

6. **FSD Subscriptions Hit Record; China Approval Progress:** Tesla set a new Q1 record with 180,000 new FSD subscribers, bringing the total to 1.28 million paid subscribers — a 51% increase quarter-over-quarter from Q4 2025. The bulk of Q1 FSD revenue growth came from subscriptions. At $17.8M/month in subscription revenue, this represents a growing and recurring revenue stream. Tesla confirmed continued progress toward FSD regulatory approval in China, with a target of Q3 2026 for approval. Tesla separately said preparations for FSD approval in China are ongoing.

7. **Energy Segment: Megapack 3, Nevada LFP Ramp, New Solar Panel:** Tesla confirmed that 2026 energy storage deployments are still expected to exceed 2025 levels. Megapack 3 production will begin later in 2026 at Tesla's new Megapack factory in Texas. Tesla has begun ramping its new LFP battery factory in Nevada, as well as cathode material and lithium refining operations in Texas. Battery pack capacity remains the bottleneck for vehicle production ramp. Tesla also began meaningful customer deployments of its first in-house-designed solar panel produced at Gigafactory New York, featuring 18 individual power zones — 3x more than a conventional residential panel — for better shaded-condition performance.

8. **Terafab and Chip Development:** Elon provided detail on the research chip fabrication facility (Terafab) being built at Giga Texas — a $3B investment. Construction will start in 2026. SpaceX will handle the initial phase of the scaled-up Terafab, while Tesla is responsible for the research fab. Tesla has also finalized plans for Dojo 3, though it remains at the conceptual discussion stage. Cortex 2, Tesla's latest AI training cluster, is now online and running training workloads.

9. **Model Y Price Increases in Europe; Rivian R2 Launches:** Tesla raised Model Y prices by approximately €1,000 across multiple trims in Norway, France, the Netherlands, and Germany. On the competitive front, Rivian officially began R2 production with first customer deliveries expected in spring 2026, with management describing reservations as "encouraging" — a modest competitive datapoint to monitor.
