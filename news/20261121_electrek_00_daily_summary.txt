Electrek's Tesla coverage today covered the company's launch in Argentina, the start of the FSD v15 rollout, and a turning-radius update for 2027 Cybertrucks. Across all three, Fred Lambert pairs the reported details with skepticism about Tesla's priorities and claims.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD v15 rolls out, aimed at known weak spots:** Electrek reports Tesla has begun rolling out FSD v15, with release notes citing improved detection of road signs, potholes, debris and small animals, better speed handling in school zones, speed-camera slowing, a new "Tesla Augmented Reality" visualization (called "Reality View" in the announcement) and Bike Rack Mode. Tesla says the new vision encoder improves the critical intervention rate by 25x but does not share the underlying figure. Tesla is also decreasing driver-monitoring strictness for higher-speed driver profiles. Lambert estimates 25x on v14's earlier-year figure would mean roughly 50,000 miles between critical interventions, and cautions that the claim is hard to verify without the actual number. He notes FSD has historically improved unevenly, with point releases sometimes regressing. [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/tesla-fsd-v15-rollout-25x-safety-reality-view/)

## Electric Vehicles
2. **Musk stalls at Tesla's Argentina launch:** Electrek reports Tesla opened online orders in Argentina on Friday for five Model 3 and Model Y versions imported from Gigafactory Shanghai, priced from $40,990 (Model 3 RWD) to $53,990 (Model 3 Performance), VAT included, plus about $600 in fees. First deliveries are not expected until March or April 2027. Argentina is Tesla's fourth direct-sales market in South America, and BYD already leads the country's EV market. Musk joined the event with President Javier Milei by video and said he did not know what to say; the call ended about two minutes in. Lambert reads it as a sign Musk no longer cares about selling cars, citing the 8.6% drop in 2025 deliveries to 1.636 million. [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/elon-musk-tesla-argentina-launch-awkward-video-milei/)

3. **Cybertruck gets an even tighter turning radius:** Per Electrek, Tesla is rolling out an update (software 2026.44 or newer) that tightens the Cybertruck's turning circle, but it applies only to model year 2027 trucks. Tesla gave no new figure; the owner's manual lists about 43.5 ft before the update. Tesla has not said whether the rear wheels now reach the 10 degrees a Tesla engineer promised in 2024, or why earlier trucks are excluded. Lambert argues the update is hard to get excited about given Cybertruck production is slated to end by the end of summer 2027, with only the $109,990 Cyberbeast sold. He estimates deliveries of about 7,000 to 8,000 per quarter. [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/tesla-cybertruck-tighter-turning-radius-update/)

## Editorial Stance
Electrek's Fred Lambert credits Tesla's engineering and the FSD v15 visuals but is skeptical of unquantified claims like the 25x improvement, and frames the Argentina launch and Cybertruck update as evidence that Tesla's car business has taken a back seat.
