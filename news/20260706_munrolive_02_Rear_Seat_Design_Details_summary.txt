## Electric Vehicles
1. **Headrest tube stamping design compared:** The presenter examines a headrest guide tube that appears round but is actually a flat stamped piece with a keyed seam and a depression stamped in to create a flat welding edge, then rolled into shape; a comparison vehicle from Ford uses a similar flat-rolled-and-locked approach but without the depression, resulting in a less-than-perfectly-round tube. The presenter notes perfect roundness isn't functionally necessary since contact points are only on certain surfaces, and frames this as the kind of granular detail used to assess a manufacturer's design and manufacturing understanding and to find cost or simplicity savings.
