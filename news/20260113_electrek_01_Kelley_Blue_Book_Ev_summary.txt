The article reports Kelley Blue Book data showing EV incentives hit record highs in December 2025, helping drive the strongest monthly EV sales since the federal tax credit expired.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **EV pricing and incentives trends:** The average transaction price (ATP) for a new EV in December was $58,034, down from $58,638 in November but up 2.4% year-over-year from $56,691. EV incentives rose to 18% of ATPs in December, a record, up both month-over-month and year-over-year, helping push EV sales past 84,000 units for the month — the best since the $7,500 federal tax credit was canceled on September 30.

2. **Tesla pricing and incentives:** The average price paid for a new Tesla in December was $53,680, down 2.9% year-over-year and lower than November. Sales incentives on new Tesla models averaged 19.5% in December, more than double the rate from a year earlier.

## Market & Competition
1. **2025 full-year US EV sales:** Total US EV sales in 2025 reached about 1.28 million units, a roughly 2% decline versus 2024. Cox Automotive expects 2026 sales to remain mostly flat, supported by new model launches and continued charging network improvements.
