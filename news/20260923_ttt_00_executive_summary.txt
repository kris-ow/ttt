## Robotaxi
- Tesla posted a signup page inviting third parties to become Cybercab fleet owners or infrastructure/service providers (cleaning, repairs), read by commentators as a deliberate move to offload capital and maintenance burden as the fleet scales.
- Cybercab registrations per Texas DMV filings rose to 67 (+9) then 69 (+2) by end of day, per Sawyer Merritt — a distinct, fresher data series from the stale RobotaxiTracker.com counts.
- Tesla said in a new drive-unit video that Cybercab is "40% more efficient than Model Y," a claim with direct implications for per-ride energy costs and margins.
- A chat participant reported seeing two Cybercabs and 30–40 robotaxis at an Orlando dealership on a Saturday night — unverified and uncorroborated.

## Humanoid Bots
- A leaked image found in Tesla's Android app code (cited version 4.60.5) purports to show Optimus V3; per Jo Bhakdi it's unconfirmed as an official reveal but validated by multiple people as not fabricated, showing a softer, more human-contoured design versus Optimus 2.5.
- A China-based commentator's third-party breakdown claims V3 has fully sealed joints, a monolithic composite torso housing a 2.3 kWh battery, forearm-relocated tendon-driven finger actuators, and slimmed legs/feet — all unconfirmed by Tesla.
- Unconfirmed Chinese supply-chain reports cite a 5,000-unit component batch already shipping, internal chatter of 10,000–20,000 units in 2026, and rumors of a ~1,000-units/week ramp by year-end — none verified by Tesla.
- Host's own estimate: an official V3 reveal is more likely Q2 next year than an immediate unveiling off the leak.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla Semi launches with a major presentation September 24; factory tours (Top Gear, theteslaspace) confirm the Nevada plant skips a paint shop entirely in favor of powder coating, uses a tool that torques all 10 wheel nuts simultaneously, and features structural 4680 battery packs — with per-mile cost cited at ~34 cents electric vs. ~$1 diesel.
- A 2,500-unit electric Class 8 truck order — the largest in U.S. history — names Tesla as sole primary OEM, with Kenworth, RIDE, and Volvo as secondary options carriers could choose instead; split among manufacturers not specified.
- Tesla was granted a US patent for a van-shaped "RoboVan"-style vehicle, with hosts floating uses from mobile vending to a mobile library.
- Roadster reveal confirmed for October 1 in Waco at SpaceX's McGregor facility; an FAA flight restriction there through the event date has fueled unconfirmed speculation about a SpaceX-developed thruster package enabling sub-1.2-second 0-60 and brief "flight" — not officially confirmed by Tesla.
- Hosts expect record Q3 deliveries at Giga Berlin and Giga Shanghai, with Fremont near full capacity; delivery estimates floated range from 530,000 (host) to ~545,000 (Grok), with skepticism voiced about AI-chatbot estimates.

## Energy
- The Limiting Factor's teardown of a Tesla patent ("Electrode foil coatings...," filed Sept 2, 2025) argues it bundles three inventions reducing defects in Tesla's tabless 4680 dry-electrode cells; edge-coating appears already in production use based on the host's own cell teardown. The international searching authority found the claims novel but not inventive, meaning the patent as written would likely be rejected, though it clarifies Tesla's freedom-to-operate.

- European electric truck rivals (Volvo, Mercedes, MAN) reportedly cost more than both diesel and Tesla Semi despite bigger batteries and less range; newer entrants (updated Mercedes, BYD, Wind Rose) are said to be approaching Tesla-comparable efficiency, per futureaza.
