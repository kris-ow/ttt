## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- SpaceX's ~$1.83T valuation is under fresh scrutiny: an investor thesis relayed on brighterwithherbert argues SpaceX's AI compute business alone, priced like Anthropic's rumored IPO multiple (~13-14x revenue), would be worth ~$1.83-1.84T — matching the entire company and implying Starlink, launch, Starship and Grok are priced at zero. A guest on the same show counters that earnings-based valuation (not revenue multiples) justifies even more, projecting a possible $5-10T valuation within a year.
- Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas sharply raised SpaceX estimates, nearly doubling the 2027 revenue forecast to $175B, raising EBIT estimates, pulling forward FCF breakeven by four years to 2031, and cutting ~$280B from projected debt issuance.
- Tesla-SpaceX merger: Musk told SpaceX's October 20 earnings call he personally thinks a merger "would be a good idea" but called it not inevitable, citing a conflict of interest that keeps him out of negotiations; Tesla's October 22 10-Q confirmed a board committee studying a "business combination" with no terms disclosed ahead of the December 3 annual meeting. Polymarket odds cited at ~80% for an announcement by year-end.
- Analysts remain split on shareholder impact: Barclays upgraded Tesla to overweight (Oct 26, $450 target) citing merger anticipation; Gary Black said he doesn't expect a "huge windfall" for Tesla holders since a combined entity would likely trade near SpaceX's lower multiple; retail investors organized a shareholder letter (~9,700 signatories, ~25.7M shares) pushing for a fairer exchange ratio.
- Electrek argues Texas's Democratic sweep (Talarico over Paxton, Hinojosa over Abbott, flipped 15th Court of Appeals) could weaken the favorable legal environment the article says Musk's merger plans may depend on; Musk spent $212M backing Paxton and $474M total in the cycle, per the article.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- SpaceX and Qualcomm announced a multi-year deal (Nov 4) to deploy tens of thousands of terrestrial 5G cellular sites nationwide using Qualcomm's Dragon Wing platform, starting 2027 — Qualcomm's Durga Malladi called it the company's "biggest 5G deal yet." The move responds to satellite coverage gaps indoors and in-vehicle (85.5% national, 52.7% rural).
- Spectrum build-out continues: EchoStar deals (~65 MHz, ~$19.6B, FCC-approved May 2026) plus an October 8 agreement to buy Grain Management's 800 MHz low-band portfolio for ~$8B, still awaiting FCC approval targeted H1 2027. Even after both close, SpaceX would hold only ~80 MHz versus T-Mobile's nearly 400 MHz.
- Starlink Consumer Service reached 13.6M subscribers by September 30 (up from 7.2M a year earlier) at $69/month ARPU; Starlink Mobile passed 22M monthly users via partner carriers, up 37% quarter-over-quarter. Verizon and T-Mobile CEOs publicly ruled out MVNO wholesale access in late-October earnings calls.
- Starlink announced a free dish upgrade for first-generation kit customers to the Standard kit, per Sawyer Merritt's digest (full program details not yet disclosed).

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX targets ~1.2 million Nvidia Blackwell chips across its Colossus 2 campus (Memphis/Southaven) by end of 2026, per Musk's October 31 update; new facilities "Gun Range" (training, online by end of November) and "Spam" (inference, ~200,000 GB300 chips, December) are coming online.
- Anthropic's IPO filing reportedly discloses up to $120B in compute purchases from SpaceX through 2029 (~$3B/month once ramped); one customer — reportedly Anthropic — made up 34.5% of SpaceX's revenue last quarter.
- SpaceX's superintelligence segment (cloud, AI, advertising) generated $7.56B in Q3 revenue but only $431M operating profit (~6% margin), versus $2.367B operating profit from the communications/Starlink segment in the same quarter.
- Grokbot reached 800,000 paid weekly users by early October, more than doubling in a month; Musk said he's "never seen anything grow this fast" and suggested wide enterprise rollout "within weeks."

## Financials
- Tesla's Wall Street price targets average ~$410 despite a 24x spread (from $25 to $600); post-earnings moves since October 21 have mostly been upward (Piper Sandler to $475, Mizuho/BofA to $460), while Goldman held at $360 calling the rating "full."
- Tesla's trailing P/E is ~230x adjusted (~440x GAAP) but falls to ~185x on 2027 consensus and ~125x on 2028 consensus — the gap hinges entirely on which future year's earnings an investor is pricing.
- SpaceX Q3 2026: $14.6B total revenue, $4.4B net income, $5.25B connectivity revenue with operating income doubling to $2.4B.

## Robotaxi
- Sum-of-the-parts bank models show robotaxi dominates Tesla's valuation: Bank of America allocates roughly half its price target to robotaxi; Ark Invest's 2031 base case ($4,400/share) assigns ~80% of value to robotaxi and ~15% to Optimus, assuming ~8.1M robotaxis deployed.
- Ron Baron, more conservative, expects Tesla worth $2,000-$2,500 in 10 years and estimates each robotaxi could generate $50,000-$60,000 in annual cash flow.
- Dallas Cybercab rides are set to begin in November, seen as a near-term test of the robotaxi thesis.

## Autonomous Driving
- Dutch regulator RDW approved changes to Tesla's European FSD ("Tesla Assisted Driving"), including a reduced maximum speed offset — a concrete regulatory step for Tesla's European autonomy rollout.
- A Korean consumer group (Citizens United for Consumer Sovereignty) plans an FTC complaint alleging Tesla marketed FSD to buyers whose Shanghai-built Model 3/Y cars can't legally use it under KORUS rules; Tesla Korea testimony says FSD works on only ~20% of registered Teslas there, with Tesla having sold over 170,000 Shanghai-built vehicles in Korea since 2023. Tesla only filed for Korean FSD certification around October 22.

## Energy
- Tesla's Texas Megapack factory secured Foreign-Trade Zone approval from the Foreign-Trade Zones Board, authorized on a limited 14-month basis — a customs development that could affect input costs.
