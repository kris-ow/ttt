## Market & Competition

He also references a claim that Michael Burry's short thesis about GPUs having short useful lives (3-4 years) has proven wrong, with chips lasting 7-10 years — an argument the host uses to suggest hyperscaler earnings and depreciation schedules understate true profitability.

3. **Neocloud stocks (e.g., Nebius) framed as key beneficiaries:** The host describes Neoclouds (Nebius, CoreWeave, etc.) as GPU-focused data-center operators distinct from diversified hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft), and argues they are structurally positioned to benefit as long as the compute price curve keeps rising. ## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
