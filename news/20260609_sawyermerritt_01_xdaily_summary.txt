The active Robotaxi fleet stands at 719 (per RobotaxiTracker.com, as of 2026-06-07), with the unsupervised driverless subset at 39 vehicles across Austin, Dallas, and Houston.

2. **Tesla's Texas Megapack Factory Nearing Operational Status:** A post with 1.3K likes highlighted Tesla's upcoming Megapack factory located approximately 40 minutes from Houston, Texas. When fully ramped, the facility will produce 50 GWh of Megapacks annually and employ up to 1,500 people. This would bring Tesla's total global Megapack manufacturing capacity to approximately 110 GWh across three facilities: California (40 GWh), China (20 GWh), and Texas (50 GWh). The Texas expansion represents a significant step-up in energy storage manufacturing scale and signals strong confidence in grid-storage demand.

3. **Tesla Model 3 RWD Leads Edmunds EV Efficiency Rankings:** The 2026 Tesla Model 3 RWD was named the most efficient EV currently in production by Edmunds, based on real-world testing. The vehicle exceeded its EPA-rated 363-mile range by 30 miles, achieving 393 miles on a full charge — approximately 8.3% above the official estimate. This real-world efficiency leadership reinforces the product's competitive positioning and has implications for energy cost per mile in fleet/robotaxi scenarios.

4. **Tesla Durability Study: Twice as Likely to Reach 250K Miles vs. Subaru:** A new iSeeCars study covering more than 174 million vehicles found that Tesla vehicles are twice as likely to reach 250,000 miles as a Subaru, and outranked Ford, Kia, Chevy, BMW, VW, Mazda, Mercedes, and Volvo. This post garnered 4K likes and 418 reposts, suggesting wide reach. For the robotaxi investment thesis, vehicle longevity is a critical variable — longer useful vehicle life directly improves fleet economics and DCF assumptions around vehicle useful life.

5. **Tesla Semi European Business Development Hiring:** Tesla posted a job opening for a Business Development Manager for its Semi truck, based in Parsdorf near Munich, Germany. The role focuses on B2B fleet sales, truck configuration, and charging infrastructure expansion. This suggests Tesla is actively building out the Semi's European commercial presence, though no production or delivery numbers were disclosed.

6. **Waymo Acquires Apple's AV Proving Ground for $220M:** Waymo purchased Apple's 5,500-acre self-driving car testing facility in Arizona for $220 million. Apple had spent 10 years and $10 billion on its self-driving program before abandoning it. The acquisition signals Waymo is investing heavily in physical testing infrastructure while simultaneously highlighting the sunk cost of Apple's failed AV effort. This is a mild competitive data point — Waymo is expanding capacity, though Tesla's approach (real-world fleet data rather than proving grounds) contrasts sharply.

7. **SpaceX Announcements (Tangential to Tesla):** The majority of posts covered SpaceX's unveiling of the AI1 satellite (150 kW peak compute, 70-meter wingspan), a new Gigasat factory in Bastrop, Texas (1,000+ acres, 11M+ sq ft building potential, AI satellite production starting end of 2027), next-gen Starlink terminals targeting "a few hundred million" units, and the Terafab facility (~100 million sq ft, targeting 1 TW/year output — 2x current U.S. consumption). While these are SpaceX developments and not directly Tesla, Elon Musk's capacity to execute on multi-company mega-projects may influence sentiment around Tesla's own ambitious manufacturing and autonomy goals. Notably, the Gigasat factory is in Bastrop, Texas, near existing Tesla/SpaceX infrastructure corridors.
