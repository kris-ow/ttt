## Key Posts / Themes

1. **FSD Approved for Public Road Testing in Sweden:** Tesla has received approval from Sweden's Transport Agency (Transportstyrelsen) to test FSD (Supervised) on public municipal roads in Strängnäs, Sweden. The approval is valid for one year following final agency sign-off, requires safety drivers, and covers municipal roads — noting that Tesla already holds state road approvals in Sweden. This is a meaningful regulatory milestone for Tesla's European FSD expansion, demonstrating that the supervised autonomy framework is gaining traction outside the U.S. market. The post drew strong engagement (2.4K likes, 259 reposts), signaling significant investor and enthusiast interest.

2. **Actually Smart Summon Impresses in Real-World Testing:** Sawyer Merritt reported spending approximately one hour riding in the back seat of his Model Y using the updated Actually Smart Summon across four different parking lots, including busy ones. He noted the car handled all scenarios "very well," with behavior so natural that bystanders barely noticed. This firsthand account suggests meaningful improvement in low-speed autonomous maneuvering — a capability directly relevant to robotaxi readiness — though no specific software version was cited.

3. **FSD UI Update — Navigation Added as Disengagement Reason:** A code or UI discovery reveals that "navigation" will be added as a selectable disengagement reason in an upcoming FSD popup. This is a minor but notable signal suggesting Tesla is refining its data collection granularity around why drivers intervene, which could improve training pipelines and safety reporting transparency.

4. **Cybercab Sightings Normalizing:** Merritt commented positively on the increasing frequency of Cybercab sightings becoming "a normal thing," accompanied by media. While no specific production or deployment numbers were provided, the tone reflects growing confidence that Cybercab pre-production or testing activity is visibly accelerating.

These posts reinforce Tesla's software moat narrative but carry no new financial data.

No mileage, range, or financial terms were disclosed. This is a incremental commercial validation data point for the Semi program.
