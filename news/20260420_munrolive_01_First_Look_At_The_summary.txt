This video is a first-look interview filmed at ConExpo, featuring Sandy Munro's channel host Armen Fchnowski speaking with two Mack Trucks representatives — Tim Rinkl (program manager for the new Mack Granite) and Fernando — about the all-new 2026 Mack Granite vocational truck and the newly revealed Mack Keystone tractor. The video has no direct relevance to Tesla, autonomous driving, robotaxis, Optimus, or energy storage, and is entirely focused on heavy-duty commercial truck engineering and product features. The core thesis is that both the Granite and Keystone represent ground-up redesigns from Mack's prior generation (the Pinnacle), with major improvements in driver comfort, active safety technology, powertrain integration, and emissions compliance for 2027 regulations.

## Market & Competition
1. **All-New Mack Granite Cab Design:** The 2026 Mack Granite features a completely new cab that is 9 inches wider at the shoulder and offers 10% more interior room for the driver compared to the previous generation. New amenities include seats with heating, cooling, and massage functions, as well as an air-suspension cab isolation system using airbags and shocks to smooth out road harshness and potholes.

2. **Command Steer System:** A new steering assistance feature called "Command Steer" reduces steering wheel effort by 85% using an electric motor mounted on top of the hydraulic steering gear. The system provides approximately 9 lbs of torque assist — described as equivalent to a bowling ball sitting on the edge of the steering wheel — reducing driver fatigue on rough job sites.

3. **New MP13 Engine Meeting 2027 Emissions Standards:** The Granite is powered by Mack's all-new 13-liter MP13 engine, which meets the 2027 NOx emissions regulation (0.0035 g/bhp-hr). It is positioned as the most powerful 13L engine in the industry at 540 horsepower and 1,950 ft-lbs of torque. An optional Cummins X10 engine is also available, offering up to 450 horsepower, meeting the same 2027 standard, and saving approximately 500 lbs in vehicle weight.

4. **Active and Passive Safety Features:** The Granite introduces driver-side and side curtain airbags as standard (the latter specifically targeting rollover protection, which is cited as a significant risk given the trucks' high center of gravity). Additional features include automatic E-911 call upon impact or rollover detection, electronic roll stability control, front radar-based automatic emergency braking (AEB) and adaptive cruise control, and side radars on both sides for blind-spot monitoring with active blinker alerts.

5. **Crash Structure and Cab Construction:** The cab is constructed from galvanized steel and is the only cab in the industry claimed to meet Swedish impact tests, including A-pillar impact, rear cab impact, and barrier impact testing. The chassis and engine/transmission are engineered to break away and dive downward during a frontal collision to preserve cab survival space.

6. **M Drive AMT Transmission with Creep Mode:** The Granite uses Mack's proprietary M Drive automated manual transmission (AMT), available in 12-speed (standard) and 14-speed (vocational, with creeper gears). The highest gear ratio is 32:1, enabling very slow controlled movement for applications like curb pouring. A new "creep mode" allows the truck to move forward automatically when the driver lifts off the brake pedal, with the electronically controlled clutch managing engagement.

7. **Body Link 4 Upfitter Integration System:** The Granite debuts "Body Link 4," a programmable interface for body builders and upfitters that includes flexible dashboard switches, a dedicated connector on the passenger-side frame rail with reprogrammable pins, CAN bus connections, and the ability to display body-mounted inputs (e.g., dump body raised, crane outriggers deployed) directly in the driver's instrument cluster.

8. **Production Start and Manufacturing Location:** Full production of the Granite (and Keystone) is scheduled to begin in January 2027 to coincide with the 2027 emissions regulations. Pre-production trucks are already in customer hands for testing. Both trucks are manufactured in Macungie, Pennsylvania, USA.

9. **Mack Keystone Tractor Overview:** The Keystone is the tractor/semi-truck sibling to the Granite, sharing a common platform also used by the Mack Pioneer and new Mack Anthem. Unlike the Granite (which always carries a body), the Keystone always pulls a trailer. It is designed for versatile vocational/off-road tractor duty (farms, oil fields, quarries) rather than pure over-the-road aerodynamic efficiency. It is available in day cab, 44-inch sleeper, and 64-inch stand-up sleeper configurations, with the day cab expected to represent the majority of sales.

10. **Keystone Fuel Efficiency and Platform Comparison:** The Keystone is stated to be 6% more fuel-efficient than the outgoing Mack Pinnacle on an MPG basis. Both the Granite and Keystone are described as ground-up redesigns from the Pinnacle, though engineered to preserve what loyal Pinnacle customers valued. The Keystone features a fully integrated Mack powertrain — MP13 engine, M Drive AMT, and proprietary Mack rear axles — all designed in-house to work in tandem.
