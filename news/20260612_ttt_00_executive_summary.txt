## Robotaxi
- **Cybercab production nearing ~1,000 units.** Per Bhakdi, Tesla has produced or will produce approximately 1,000 Cybercabs by June 2026, with units already appearing in locations such as Dallas receiving wheel caps and being deployed in the wild.
- **FSD "super scale" requires version 15, but scaling does not.** Bhakdi reports Musk was explicit that meaningful deployment does not require waiting for FSD v15; however, "super scale" — defined as millions of vehicles, not tens of thousands — will come with version 15.

## Autonomous Driving
- **Waymo enters mainstream marketing with FIFA World Cup TV buy.** Waymo aired its first-ever national TV commercial during the FIFA World Cup (beginning June 12), claiming its driver is "statistically 10x safer" than a human in served cities — a signal that the robotaxi brand war is entering a mass-market phase ahead of Tesla's Cybercab ramp.
- **Waymo launches invite-only Premier membership at $29.99/month.** The program offers priority pickups, 10% Waymo Cash back on every trip, and early feature access — indicating Waymo is investing in customer retention infrastructure as competition with Tesla intensifies.
- **Actually Smart Summon rolls out to Cybertruck.** Tesla expanded the feature beyond Model 3/Y/S/X, continuing FSD feature parity work across the broader fleet.

## Electric Vehicles
- **Tesla Model 3 wins UK's best car to own.** *Auto Express* 2026 Driver Power survey, compiled from over 100,000 driver opinions, awarded the Model 3 top honors, citing improvements in quality and strong owner sentiment — a positive brand signal in a competitive European market.
- **Tesla intraday low of $387.31 on SpaceX IPO day.** Tesla dropped sharply to that level at 11:46 a.m. ET — the exact moment SpaceX began trading — before recovering and decoupling from NASDAQ. Bhakdi interprets this as the final flush of forced SpaceX-rotation selling, with the all-time high of ~$490 (December 16, 2025) as his near-term price target.
- **ArcBest purchases two Tesla Semi trucks following 2025 pilot.** The pilot recorded average energy efficiency of approximately 1.55 kWh per mile, with drivers citing strong visibility and operational performance — a real-world data point confirming continued commercial fleet adoption.

## Energy
- **$150M Megapack deployment completed in New South Wales, Australia.** The system is co-located next to a large solar farm to support grid renewable integration, reinforcing Tesla's energy business momentum and integrated energy strategy.
- **96-stall Tesla Supercharger planned for Willows, California.** The site will feature solar canopies, a Megapack, and a prefab amenity building — one of the largest reported Supercharger footprints, illustrating Tesla's integrated energy-plus-charging infrastructure buildout.
- **GM and Redwood Materials expand into second-life battery repurposing for grid storage.** Redwood's proprietary "Pack Manager" hardware integrates heterogeneous repurposed EV packs into a single DC output, functionally comparable to new containerized LFP solutions; manufacturing is underway at Sparks, Nevada with UL certification complete. GM plans to deploy second-life packs at its Michigan plant beginning 2027. Directionally relevant as a competing grid-storage architecture to Tesla's Megapack.
- **GM and Peak Energy announce sodium-ion grid storage partnership.** Peak Energy's passively cooled, chimney-effect system — designed for 10,000–20,000 full cycles and 20-year life — is being combined with GM's next-generation sodium-ion cell development. The chemistry uses aluminum on both anode and cathode (eliminating the copper anode cost of lithium-ion) and is sourced from abundant domestic materials. Again, a competing grid-storage technology worth monitoring against Megapack.

## Market & Competition
- **SpaceX IPO is the largest in history; opens 25% above issue price.** Priced at $135/share with ~7–8% of the company floated, SpaceX opened trading at approximately $169. The offering was approximately 4x oversubscribed with ~20% retail allocation (cut from 30%). Oppenheimer initiated coverage at Outperform with a $190 price target implying ~$2.5T market cap. GraniteShares is launching leveraged ETFs SPAL (2x long) and SNK (2x short) on Monday.
- **S&P 500 inclusion delayed ~12 months.** The index committee imposed a seasoning period, removing a near-term passive buying catalyst. NASDAQ inclusion is expected within two to three weeks per Bhakdi.
- **SpaceX trades at ~70x revenue vs. Nvidia at ~22x.** Per GraniteShares' Will Rind, the valuation is not justifiable on traditional fundamentals and is explicitly a "future belief" story — he draws a direct parallel to early Tesla, noting that a strict P/E focus would have precluded buying Tesla at any point in its growth phase.
- **Bhakdi flags potential Tesla–SpaceX merger as early as July 2026.** He models a hypothetical one-to-one market cap merger: with SpaceX at ~$2.2T and Tesla at ~$1.26T, Tesla shares would need to approximately double to ~$750 for Tesla shareholders to receive fair value — a structural parallel he draws to the Tesla–SolarCity transaction. He presents this as a possibility, not a certainty.
