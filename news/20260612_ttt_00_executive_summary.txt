## Robotaxi
- **Cybercab production milestone:** Bhakdi estimates Tesla has produced approximately 1,000 Cybercabs or will reach that count in June 2026. Units are visibly appearing in cities including Dallas, being fitted with wheel caps, and actively deploying.
- **Unsupervised fleet threshold for next price target:** Bhakdi argues Tesla needs only ~50 unsupervised Cybercabs on the road to justify a move to $490/share — a level he describes as potentially achievable "even over this weekend." The current unsupervised fleet stands at 39 per RobotaxiTracker.com.
- **FSD version 15 and "super scale" clarification:** Bhakdi says Musk was explicit that scaling does not require waiting for FSD v15, but that "super scale" — defined as millions of vehicles, not tens of thousands — will arrive with v15.

## Autonomous Driving
- **Waymo national TV campaign:** Waymo launched its first-ever national TV commercial, airing during the FIFA World Cup beginning June 12. The ad leads with the claim that the Waymo driver is "statistically 10x safer" than a human in the cities it serves — a direct consumer-facing safety positioning that Tesla has not yet matched with equivalent mass-market advertising.
- **Waymo Premier membership program:** Waymo introduced an invite-only subscription at $29.99/month offering priority pickups, 10% Waymo Cash back on every ride, and early feature access. Together with the TV buy, this signals Waymo is moving into a mainstream brand and retention phase ahead of intensifying Cybercab competition.
- **Actually Smart Summon expands to Cybertruck:** Tesla began rolling out Actually Smart Summon to Cybertruck owners, extending a feature previously limited to Model 3/Y/S/X. A modest but concrete step toward FSD feature parity across the full fleet.

## Electric Vehicles
- **Tesla Model 3 wins UK's best car to own:** *Auto Express* 2026 Driver Power survey — compiled from over 100,000 driver opinions — named the Model 3 the UK's best car to own, citing quality improvements and strong owner sentiment. A positive brand and demand signal in a competitive European market.
- **Tesla Semi efficiency data point:** ArcBest purchased two Tesla Semi trucks following a 2025 pilot that recorded average energy efficiency of ~1.55 kWh/mile. Drivers cited strong visibility, comfort, and operational performance — continued evidence of commercial fleet adoption.
- **SpaceX IPO forced Tesla selling now exhausted (per Bhakdi):** Tesla hit an intraday low of ~$387.31 at 11:46 a.m. ET on June 12 — the exact moment SpaceX began trading — before quickly recovering and decoupling from NASDAQ. Bhakdi interprets this as the final forced rotation sell-off out of Tesla into SpaceX now complete.

## Energy
- **$150M Megapack completed in Australia:** A $150 million Tesla Megapack battery energy storage system was completed in New South Wales, co-located with a large solar farm to support grid renewable integration.
- **96-stall Supercharger planned for Willows, California:** The planned station would feature solar canopies, a Megapack, and a prefab amenity building — one of the largest Supercharger footprints reported to date, reinforcing Tesla's integrated energy-and-charging infrastructure strategy.
- **GM and Redwood Materials: second-life EV batteries for grid storage:** At a GM Energy event, Redwood Materials announced its "Pack Manager" hardware — now UL-certified and in production at its Sparks, Nevada factory — which integrates heterogeneous repurposed EV packs into a single consistent DC output comparable to new containerized LFP storage. GM plans to deploy second-life packs at its own Michigan plant starting 2027. Redwood states recycled domestic critical minerals can already be produced cheaper than newly mined and imported equivalents. Not a direct Tesla development, but relevant context for the broader stationary storage competitive landscape.
- **GM and Peak Energy sodium-ion grid storage partnership:** GM Energy announced a collaboration with Peak Energy on passively cooled sodium-ion grid storage systems targeting 20-year life and 10,000–20,000 full cycles — roughly 3–4x the cycle life of automotive lithium-ion. The chemistry allows aluminum foil on both anode and cathode (vs. copper required on lithium-ion anodes), lowering material costs. A developing alternative to lithium-based grid storage, including Megapack.

## Market & Competition
- **SpaceX IPO — largest ever:** SpaceX priced at $135/share (~7–8% float, ~4x oversubscribed) and opened at ~$169, approximately 25% above issuance. Oppenheimer initiated with Outperform and a $190 price target implying ~$2.5T market cap. The stock is trading at roughly 70x revenue at IPO vs. Nvidia at ~22x revenue — a "future belief" story, per GraniteShares CEO Will Rind, who draws a direct parallel to Tesla's own valuation history.
- **S&P 500 inclusion delayed ~12 months:** The S&P 500 committee imposed a seasoning period, removing a near-term passive buying catalyst. NASDAQ inclusion is expected in roughly two to three weeks.
- **Leveraged SpaceX ETFs launching Monday:** GraniteShares is launching SPAL (2x long SpaceX) and SNK (2x short SpaceX) for retail traders seeking leveraged exposure in either direction.
- **Bhakdi's SpaceX trough thesis:** Bhakdi did not participate in the IPO. He projects SpaceX will trade below its $135 issuance price within the year — driven by 180-day lockup expirations (~December 15, 2026) and the absence of near-term cash flow — and plans to buy the trough targeting a 20x–40x return by 2030 versus ~11x from IPO price.
- **Potential Tesla–SpaceX merger floated:** Bhakdi models a possible merger announcement as early as July 2026, noting that at current market caps (~$2.2T SpaceX, ~$1.26T Tesla), a one-for-one merger would require Tesla shares to roughly double to ~$750 for Tesla shareholders to receive fair value. He draws a structural parallel to the Tesla–SolarCity transaction.
