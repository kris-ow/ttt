## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Autonomous Driving Regulatory Battles & Milestones:** Multiple posts addressed FSD's regulatory trajectory across jurisdictions. In New Jersey, Tesla is actively fighting proposed legislation that the company says would effectively ban its autonomous vehicles from the state market; Tesla sent emails directly to NJ owners urging them to oppose the bill, calling it an "arbitrary roadblock" that delays "life-changing technology." On a more positive note, the Dutch Transport Minister publicly defended the RDW's provisional approval of FSD (Supervised), stating it was based on independent RDW testing — not Tesla-provided data — and noting that Teslas using FSD have driven 24 million miles on Dutch roads "without any noteworthy incidents." Additionally, Tesla announced it has submitted its FSD (Supervised) application documents in Taiwan to the Vehicle Safety Inspection Center, officially entering the regulatory examination process there. Together, these posts paint a picture of Tesla advancing FSD approvals internationally while facing political resistance domestically.

2. **Goldman Sachs Raises Tesla Delivery Forecasts:** Goldman Sachs raised its Tesla vehicle delivery forecast for Q2 2026 from 405,000 to 420,000 vehicles, stating it believes Tesla's Q2 deliveries are "likely tracking ahead of consensus." Goldman also raised its full-year 2026 delivery forecast from 1.72M (the post was truncated, but the upward revision is confirmed). This is a meaningful near-term demand signal heading into the Q2 delivery report.
