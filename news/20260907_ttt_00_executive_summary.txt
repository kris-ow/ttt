## Robotaxi
- Texas robotaxi fleet jumped over 50% in about a week to 432 vehicles, including 45 Cybercabs, per one creator's reporting; observers say pickup/drop-off "circling" issues have eased.
- NHTSA opened an investigation into Cybercab certification the day of launch; commentators (citing analyst Cern Basher) frame this as a normal self-certification audit process, drawing a parallel to a multi-year Zoox review that ultimately led to an exemption, and note Tesla could lean on Model Y robotaxi volume if needed.
- Two independent creators rode Cybercabs in Austin this week and called the experience notably better than Waymo or Model Y robotaxi rides, citing the removal of all driver controls (no wheel, pedals, mirrors), a large cabin screen, and quiet ride quality; wait times were reported around 40–45 minutes, expected to shrink as fleet grows.
- Tesla began circulating an interest form inviting businesses to buy and operate Cybercab fleets on its Robotaxi network with a revenue split. Electrek's Fred Lambert argues this echoes Tesla's unfulfilled 2019 "Tesla Network" promise, citing the 2025 bankruptcy of Dutch lessor MisterGreen (4,000+ Teslas, $40M lost) as a cautionary precedent, and contends Tesla would keep genuinely profitable robotaxi economics in-house rather than sell to third parties.
- Roughly two million people have reportedly signed up for Tesla's non-binding Cybercab purchase-interest form, per a source one host called fairly reliable — commentators caution this parallels inflated early Cybertruck reservation counts.
- MKBHD renewed his bet to shave his head if Tesla delivers a $30,000 Cybercab to a customer before 2027; Musk publicly accepted, offering to shave his own head and donate to cancer research if Tesla misses.
- Pricing comparisons cited: NYC ride-hailing ~$3.50/mile, Waymo ~$3.16/mile, Uber/Lyft ~$2.80/mile, China ride-hail ~$0.50/mile, vs. Tesla's targeted Cybercab operating cost of ~$0.20/mile (~$0.25 at scale).

## Autonomous Driving
- Electrek matched a Tesla NHTSA filing to a July 2025 fatal crash in Buena Vista Township, NJ, where a Model 3 ran a stop sign and killed 82-year-old Stephen Field; Tesla's own filing marks automation "Engagement Status" as "Verified Engaged" but redacts software version and crash narrative, a detail absent from local police coverage. The article says it cannot confirm FSD vs. Autopilot was active due to redactions, and flags a 4 mph logged pre-crash speed as inconsistent with a fatal impact.
- Musk is reportedly personally calling French regulators to push FSD approval ahead of an EU vote expected around October 6, per one host; France and Germany together represent over 35% of EU population, enough to block bloc-wide approval if both vote no.
- Tesla's FSD supervised mile counter stood at roughly 13.6 billion per one analysis; paid autonomous (robotaxi) miles were reported at 380,000 as of the late-July earnings call, with one speaker speculating (unconfirmed) the figure may now be 600,000–700,000.
- FSD supervised version 14.3.9 is rolling out with a new automatic collision-avoidance feature that can activate in imminent-collision scenarios or when a driver is distracted or has disengaged FSD.
- Lemonade's FSD insurance discount (~50% off) expanded to Missouri, its fifth state.
- Commentary (Cern Basher-sourced) argues Tesla's real challenge is liability/evidentiary scaling as fleet grows, not just beating human-driver safety benchmarks, and that fleet-wide overnight software fixes could strengthen Tesla's legal position versus human-driver crash patterns.

## Energy
- A $50 million, 50 MW/200 MWh Tesla Megapack storage facility (50 units) began construction in Colorado to serve the Black Hills Energy grid.

## Market & Competition
- Uber is reportedly aligning with traditional taxi driver unions to slow robotaxi rollout, a move one host frames as evidence Uber's 20+ AV partnerships aren't sufficient to compete with Tesla and Waymo.
- Rival robotaxi companies are emphasizing higher sensor counts as a safety differentiator following Cybercab's public rollout; one commentator argues Tesla's vision-only approach paired with manufacturing scale drives greater aggregate market safety than low-volume, sensor-heavy competitors.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- SpaceX and 4iG signed a Starlink Mobile partnership covering four European countries, launching first in Hungary and Albania (Montenegro potentially next), connecting directly to LTE-compatible phones without new hardware.
- SpaceX purchased roughly 820 acres of additional land in Bastrop, Texas; one host speculates (unconfirmed) it may support turbine manufacturing and could rival Giga Texas in scale.
