This video features commentary from the "brighterwithherbert" channel covering Elon Musk's live appearance (beamed in at 2:30 AM Austin time) at the 9th International Samson Smart Mobility Summit in Israel. The host walks through Musk's key statements on Full Self-Driving, robotaxi deployment, humanoid robots, and his broader civilizational vision, framing them as a coherent worldview rather than isolated product claims. The core thesis is that Musk has moved past trying to prove these technologies are possible and is now treating them as inevitable, pivoting to questions about what kind of future humanity wants to build.

## Autonomous Driving

1. **FSD safety target — order of magnitude safer than humans:** Musk stated plainly that the path to autonomous vehicles being "at least an order of magnitude safer than human drivers" is in his words "very clear" — not a question mark anymore. He described Full Self-Driving as pure AI and cameras, deliberately mirroring how humans drive (vision plus a neural network), rejecting radar or LiDAR. The host emphasized that Musk did not hedge this claim; he presented it as a solved directional problem.

2. **10-year prediction — 90% of all distance driven by AI:** Musk offered a striking timeline: within 10 years, he expects roughly 90% of all distance driven on Earth to be handled by AI in self-driving cars. He said driving your own car would become a niche, recreational activity — analogous to riding horses today. The host noted this is a genuinely staggering projection regardless of whether one accepts the exact timeline.

3. **FSD availability in Israel:** Musk mentioned he was unsure whether Full Self-Driving is currently approved in Israel but said he believed it either already is or approvals would come soon, inviting the Israeli audience to experience it firsthand.

## Robotaxi

1. **US-wide driverless robotaxi rollout targeted by end of 2025 (resolved: end of 2026):** Musk said Tesla already has vehicles operating with no people inside and no safety monitors in three cities in Texas, and he expects driverless robotaxi service to be "widespread in the US by the end of this year" — which, given the publish date of 2026-05-18, resolves to end of 2026. He also expressed hope for expansion to Israel. The host flagged this as one of the two most important FSD-related statements in the interview.

## Humanoid Bots

1. **Robots predicted to eventually outnumber humans:** Musk predicted that intelligent robots will far outnumber humans on the planet — not by a small margin, but by a lot. He referenced the wave of humanoid robot startups and positioned Tesla's Optimus within that broader trend. An Optimus unit was reportedly present at the summit's exhibition floor, which Musk jokingly called "Optimus Subprime."

2. **Economic argument: 10x to 100x larger global economy:** Musk tied the humanoid robot thesis directly to an economic framework: output equals productivity per capita times population, and a massive, highly productive robot workforce could grow the global economy 10 or even 100 times larger than today. This is the foundation for what he called "universal high income" — not a safety net, but genuine abundance — a framing the host found notably more optimistic than universal basic income.

3. **Safety acknowledgment — the Terminator scenario:** When the Terminator analogy was raised, Musk did not dismiss it. He called it "unlikely but not impossible" and said people should always be "a little paranoid" about robot safety. The host credited him for a more nuanced take than he typically receives.
