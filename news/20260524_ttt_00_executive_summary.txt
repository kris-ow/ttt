## Autonomous Driving

- **European FSD approvals accelerating via copy-paste effect.** Netherlands completed ~150,000 km of supervised testing before granting the first European FSD approval. Lithuania followed shortly after, with Case Roland noting subsequent approvals can effectively replicate the Netherlands process — lowering the barrier for each additional country.
- **Greece, Belgium, and Ireland in motion.** Greece was narrowly beaten to second place by Lithuania and is expected to approve imminently. Belgium's Flemish region announced plans for 5,000 km of testing before formal approval, with a second Belgian region signaling intent. Ireland has also indicated it is moving forward. Tesla is actively engaging individual governments.
- **EU member-state vote expected June or September 2026.** Approximately 28 EU member states will participate. FSD is now approved in roughly 10 regions globally, dismantling the prior skeptic argument that FSD was a one-country product.
- **FSD Europe mileage accumulating rapidly.** Per Sawyer Merritt, Tesla owners in the Netherlands and Lithuania hit 15 million cumulative kilometers two days prior to May 24, with the subsequent 48-hour pace implying roughly 2.5 million km (~1.55 million miles) per day from that cohort alone — a strong early signal of adoption and active usage.
- **FSD V14.3.3 behavioral improvements demonstrated.** Sawyer Merritt observed FSD V14.3.3 proactively pulling right to yield for an emergency vehicle. In a separate trip, FSD V14 detected a car's reverse lights after it overshot a stop line in an intersection and made room preemptively — described as qualitative evidence of improving real-time reactive logic.
- **Paid FSD subscriber growth: +170,000 last quarter.** Per FutureAzA, the growth trend is expected to continue as approvals extend to new markets. Active subscribers stand at 1.28 million with cumulative supervised miles at approximately 9–10 billion.

## Robotaxi

- **Cybercab production as a forcing function for fleet scale.** Bhakdi projects 10,000–15,000 Cybercabs produced by end of 2026, with a higher scenario of 40,000–50,000 cited but not used as a base case. He argues hardware capex at that volume creates an implicit forcing function — Tesla would not produce at scale and leave vehicles idle.
- **Robotaxi infrastructure buildout visible in Dallas.** Bhakdi notes Cybercab parking infrastructure has been spotted under construction in Dallas and other locations, signaling active operational expansion beyond Austin.
- **Austin unsupervised mix shift as possible explanation for count fluctuations.** A viewer raised the observation that Austin robotaxi counts appear to be declining; Bhakdi's interpretation is that Tesla may be shifting toward a higher proportion of unsupervised rides rather than maximizing total supervised trips — quality over quantity at this stage.
- **$490 thesis independent of robotaxi; upside scenario is "a completely different number."** Bhakdi separates his near-term $490 stock target from robotaxi scaling, but states explicitly: "If we have 15,000 robotaxis end of the year, we will not be at $490. We will be at a completely different number." He also warns that if the stock reaches $490 and robotaxi still isn't scaling, it becomes vulnerable to correction.

## Humanoid Bots

- **Optimus reveal approximately 4–6 weeks out.** Bhakdi cites a reveal scheduled in late June to early July 2026 and describes it as potentially "very big." He declines to share production number estimates ahead of Pioneer Lands research publication.
- **Optimus still in factory testing, not on production lines.** Per the brighterwithherbert analysis, Optimus has not yet been deployed on active production lines and Tesla has not issued a 2026 production number — the recurring revenue thesis from robotics remains forward-looking, not yet in financial results.

## Financials

- **Tesla 2026 capex guidance: over $25 billion, ~3x prior year.** Disclosed on Q1 earnings call. The company is more than doubling its AI compute capacity in approximately six months. The brighterwithherbert framing: Tesla is executing the same physical-infrastructure-then-monetize playbook as SpaceX, with the thesis hinging on whether that capex converts to service revenue.
- **Tesla valuation re-rating thesis.** If FSD subscriptions, robotaxi network revenue, energy, insurance, and charging convert meaningfully to recurring income, valuing Tesla on automotive multiples "stops making any sense" — per brighterwithherbert. The SpaceX S1 and Anthropic deal are cited as the strongest real-world proof of concept for this playbook, though Tesla's own recurring revenue has not yet shown up materially on its income statement.

## Market & Competition

- **Iran / Strait of Hormuz resolution as the decisive near-term macro catalyst.** Bhakdi argues the situation is "de facto over," citing Trump's CBS comments and Iran's semi-official FARC news agency reporting that negotiators are finalizing an agreement covering Strait reopening, partial asset unfreezing, and face-saving nuclear terms. Combined with Nvidia earnings validating the AGI investment cycle, Bhakdi sees this as setting up a second rally attempt toward Tesla's prior all-time high of $490 (reached December 16, 2025).
- **SpaceX Anthropic deal: $1.25B/month — positive read-through for Tesla.** The SpaceX S1 discloses a cloud services agreement with Anthropic at $1.25 billion/month through May 2029 (~$15B annualized run rate), with capacity ramping through mid-2026. Brad Gerstner estimates $4–5 billion of incremental 2026 revenue from the deal. Bhakdi and brighterwithherbert both argue SpaceX's "EWS" playbook — build physical AI infrastructure, rent unused capacity — is the same playbook Tesla is executing with FSD, robotaxi, and energy, and that SpaceX's success positively correlates with Tesla sentiment.
- **SpaceX IPO liquidity impact on Tesla: negligible.** Bhakdi dismisses fears of Tesla share liquidation to fund SpaceX IPO participation. At ~$75 billion IPO size with ~70% institutional participation, retail impact on Tesla is described as negligible. He has reduced his probability of a SpaceX-Tesla merger in 2026, placing it more likely in 2027 or later.
- **Tesla leading import in South Korea and Japan; #1 car overall in Colombia.** Per FutureAzA, Tesla holds the top import position in both Asian markets and has become the best-selling car of any type in Colombia — cited as evidence of accelerating international market share heading into a projected stronger 2026 sales year vs. 2025.
