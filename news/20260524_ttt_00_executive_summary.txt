## Autonomous Driving

- **European FSD approvals accelerating via copy-paste effect.** The Netherlands, after ~150,000 km of testing over ~18 months, became the first EU country to approve supervised FSD. Lithuania followed shortly after. Per Case Roland, each new approval structurally lowers the barrier for subsequent countries — Greece, Belgium (Flemish region targeting 5,000 km of testing first), and Ireland are all actively progressing.

- **EU-wide member-state vote expected June or September 2026.** Approximately 28 member states are involved. Tesla is actively engaging individual governments to facilitate approvals. FSD has now been approved in roughly 10 regions globally.

- **FSD paid subscribers grew 170,000 last quarter**, per FutureAzA's Brian, with 1.28 million active subscribers total. Cumulative supervised miles stand at approximately 9–10 billion.

- **Netherlands + Lithuania fleet logging ~2.5 million km/day.** Per Sawyer Merritt, those two markets collectively crossed 15 million cumulative kilometers two days prior; the 48-hour pace since implies that daily accumulation rate — a strong signal for fleet training data intake from early European markets.

- **FSD V14.3.3 demonstrated two notable reactive behaviors.** Sawyer Merritt observed the system pulling well to the right to yield for an emergency vehicle, and separately, FSD V14 detecting a car's reverse lights after it overshot a stop line — then proactively backing up to make room. Anecdotal, but directly relevant to unsupervised deployment readiness.

- **Vision-over-lidar thesis: classification, not just detection.** FutureAzA's Herbert and Brian argue Tesla's system identifies *what* an object is (a cow, a moose, a car beginning to turn) and responds preemptively, whereas lidar-based systems detect geometry without semantic understanding. Herbert cited a prior statement from Tesla AI that FSD can identify fog as fog and navigate through it — lidar sees only an opaque wall.

## Robotaxi

- **Bhakdi's $490 target explicitly excludes robotaxi scaling** — that is upside, not base case. He states: "If we have 15,000 robotaxis end of the year, we will not be at $490. We will be at a completely different number." The $490 level simply represents Tesla's prior all-time high (December 16, 2025).

- **Cybercab production as deployment forcing function.** Bhakdi's working range is 10,000–20,000 Cybercabs produced by end of 2026, with a higher scenario of 40,000–50,000. His logic: Tesla would not invest the hardware capex and then leave vehicles idle. Cybercab parking infrastructure is reportedly under construction in Dallas and other cities.

- **Austin supervised count may be declining as unsupervised mix grows.** A commenter raised declining Austin robotaxi counts; Bhakdi's interpretation is that Tesla may be deliberately shifting toward a higher proportion of unsupervised rides rather than maximizing total supervised trips — a quality-over-quantity inflection.

- **brighterwithherbert confirms driverless robotaxi operational in Austin only** as of this reporting. The host identifies expansion to additional cities as the key next milestone to watch, with additional cities by 2027 as the scenario where the thesis "half works."

## Market & Competition

- **SpaceX S1 discloses $1.25B/month Anthropic compute deal through May 2029** — annualized ~$15B run rate, terminable on 90 days' notice. The structure mirrors AWS: SpaceX built Colossus to train Grok, then rented spare capacity to a competitor (Anthropic) — Anthropic's payments effectively fund the infrastructure training Elon's own AI. Brad Gerstner dubbed it "EWS — Elon Web Services."

- **SpaceX stated TAM: $28.5 trillion**, of which $26.5 trillion is AI ($22.7T enterprise applications alone). China and Russia excluded. Longer-term vision includes orbital data centers — SpaceX has requested permission for up to 1 million satellites, with potential deployment as early as 2028.

- **SpaceX FY financials per S1:** $18.7B revenue, $6.8B operating cash flow, $20B capex ($12.7B AI, $7.2B Starlink). Starlink: 10.3M subscribers across 164 countries, ~$11.4B in 2025 revenue, ~$4.4B operating income, ~50% YoY growth.

- **brighterwithherbert draws direct Tesla read-across:** If Tesla's installed base (FSD subscriptions, robotaxi network, charging, insurance, energy) converts to recurring service revenue at scale, the SpaceX filing is "the strongest real-world evidence yet" the physical-AI-platform playbook works. Caveat: that recurring revenue has not yet shown up meaningfully on Tesla's income statement.

- **Iran/Strait of Hormuz resolution as the decisive near-term macro catalyst**, per Bhakdi. Weekend reports indicate negotiators are finalizing a deal covering Strait reopening, partial asset unfreezing, and a face-saving nuclear framework. Bhakdi had assigned ~5% tail-risk to escalation three weeks prior; he now views the conflict as "de facto over." Combined with Nvidia earnings validating the AGI investment cycle, he sees the setup for a renewed NASDAQ and Tesla rally toward $490.

- **SpaceX IPO (~$75B, ~70% institutional) not a liquidity risk for Tesla**, per Bhakdi. Positive correlation via "merger fantasies" outweighs any retail rotation. Bhakdi has reduced his probability of a SpaceX-Tesla merger in 2026, now viewing 2027 or later as more likely.
