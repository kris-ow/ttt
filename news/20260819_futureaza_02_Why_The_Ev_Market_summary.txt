## Electric Vehicles
1. **US EV sales fell sharply in Q1, but the decline may be moderating:** The hosts cite a report that US EV sales dropped 27% year-over-year in the most recent quarter after a 36% decline the prior quarter, pushing quarterly EV sales volume back to roughly late-2022 levels; Randy attributes this mainly to the expiration of the $7,500 federal EV tax credit and to General Motors trimming EV product lines. A separate source (InsideEVs, per the transcript) noted EV sales were down 7.8% quarter-over-quarter, which the hosts characterize as a sign the post-incentive sales drop is slowing.
2. **Brand-level Q1 EV winners and losers:** Per the report discussed, Audi's EV sales fell 90%, Honda's fell 65%, Kia's fell 40%, while Cadillac's EV sales rose 20% (off a small base the hosts joke was about 12 cars) and Toyota's bZ model sold over 10,000 units for the quarter, roughly one month's worth of Tesla Model Y sales or about a week of Model 3 sales by the hosts' comparison.
3. **Tesla's market share framed as a function of a shrinking market:** The hosts note Tesla is gaining EV market share, but caution that share gains are happening within an overall smaller EV market and that market share as a metric can be misleading when the market itself is contracting.
