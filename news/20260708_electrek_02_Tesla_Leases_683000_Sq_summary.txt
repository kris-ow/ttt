Tesla has leased a 682,000-square-foot speculative industrial building in the Austin Hills Commerce Center, part of a broader Musk-controlled real estate expansion in Central Texas; the intended use of the space has not been disclosed.

## Financials
1. **New lease details:** Tesla will occupy phase two of a development at 11801 Decker Lake Road, built by Sansone Group and Principal Asset Management, scheduled for completion in January 2027; the full commerce center will span 1.4 million square feet once finished, per reporting from the Austin Business Journal. The building was "speculative" — built without a pre-committed tenant.

2. **Scale of Musk's Texas footprint:** Musk now controls 2.2 million square feet of leased space around Austin and more than 10 million square feet he owns and built across his companies, including Tesla's Gigafactory Texas (10+ million sq ft), a reported 112,000-square-foot xAI sublease at the Seaholm Power Plant, and other production facilities near Giga Texas.

## Robotaxi
1. **Unclear use case:** The article speculates the new building could support vehicle logistics, parts storage, service operations, energy products, or robotaxi-related operations, noting Tesla has been expanding supporting infrastructure in Austin as it grows its robotaxi program there; no official confirmation was given.

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Optimus production expansion:** Tesla is building out Optimus robot production at Giga Texas's North Campus, with construction trackers suggesting the facility could stretch nearly the full length of the existing Giga Texas main building.

## Market & Competition
1. **Terafab chip venture:** In March, Tesla and SpaceX announced Terafab, a $20-25 billion chip fabrication venture planned for Giga Texas's North Campus with an initial 2-million-square-foot R&D facility; SpaceX has since estimated total project costs could reach as much as $119 billion across all phases.
