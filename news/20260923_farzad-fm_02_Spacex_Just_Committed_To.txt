Very few people are getting their heads
wrapped around what SpaceX and Nvidia
are building together. And it all
started with a massive announcement
during SpaceX's first ever earnings
call. On August 4th, Elon Musk, the CEO
of SpaceX, said that the company had
decided to build exclusively on Nvidia.
He called the new Vera Rubin system,
Nvidia's latest compute hardware, the
best AI computer and said the companies
now have a close partnership on many
levels. SpaceX also announced that it is
working with Nvidia to design the
compute payload inside Starmine, its
planned AI satellite. And Starmine will
use an optimized version of Nvidia's
Vera Rubin NVL72
