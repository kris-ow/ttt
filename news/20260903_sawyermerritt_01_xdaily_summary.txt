3. **France FSD Approval Push:** Elon Musk met with France's Minister of Transportation to advance approval of FSD (Supervised) in the country. A quoted statement indicates French authorities have been working with Tesla on "technical adaptations" to enable approval, suggesting progress toward a regulatory green light in a major European market.

4. **Tesla Battery Safety Data:** Tesla published two blog posts on battery safety. One states that across over 265 billion miles of operation (as of end of 2025), there is no evidence of even a single spontaneous battery failure leading to a vehicle fire in a Model 3, Model Y, Cybertruck, or Semi. The second post details Tesla's "layered approach" to battery pack design, aiming to contain thermal reactions from a single cell issue. This is a defensive/PR data point relevant to safety perception and potential regulatory or insurance discussions.

5. **Political & Competitive Context:** House Speaker Mike Johnson reportedly met with Elon Musk at Giga Texas last week (secondhand mention, no new details). California will offer Uber/Lyft drivers up to $20,300 (new EV) or $14,200 (used EV) toward EV purchases starting this month, plus up to $1,170/year for charging costs — a potential demand tailwind for Tesla given its rideshare presence. Separately, Range Rover unveiled its first EV (Range Rover Electric SUV) starting at $138,000, with 333 miles EPA range, 118 kWh battery, 0-60 in 4.3s, 350kW peak charging, and a native NACS port in North America — notable as another legacy automaker adopting Tesla's charging standard.
