## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Musk's DealBook interview: Optimus, autonomy and Model Y:** Musk said "Optimus is going well" and that Tesla will do a demo "when we feel it's an authentic demo," possibly "in a month or two," showing what you would see interacting with it day to day. He said Tesla will introduce a fully autonomous Model Y with no steering wheel or pedals, and that it makes sense to build cars seating different numbers of people (4, 6, or a whole family). The post drew about 2K likes. On the Model Y L, he called it "certainly our most popular car" and said Tesla should switch all Model Y production to it because it's what most people seem to want.

2. **Tesla-SpaceX merger and SpaceX AI/Starlink items:** Asked about a potential merger, Musk said "SI and robots are what's causing convergence here," citing many areas of convergence that didn't exist in prior years and are increasingly significant. He gave no terms or timing in the quoted remarks. Musk said it is unlikely Anthropic will cancel its compute contracts with SpaceX, despite reports saying otherwise. He says Anthropic tells him it is "extremely likely" to keep renting SpaceX compute and wants "significantly more." He also said SpaceX will introduce a microcell tower device for Starlink Mobile users, placed on a roof, to help signal penetrate thick walls and reach people inside buildings. Separately, Sawyer notes Cybercab production with Starlink is increasing.

3. **Robotaxi and vehicle rollout:** Tesla confirmed Dallas Cybercab rides are about to launch (about 4K likes). The new Tesla Roadster goes on display at the LA Auto Show starting November 20.

4. **Semi and charging infrastructure:** HMD Trucking took delivery of its first 50 Tesla Semis in Illinois, and its founder said "Everybody will get these trucks, there's no question about it." Tesla opened its first Texas Megacharger station for Semis, with 8 stalls and up to 1.2MW peak charging. Tesla now has 7 Megacharger stations across 7 states, including California, Georgia, Texas and Illinois.

5. **Manufacturing and solar buildout:** A new filing shows Tesla planning a $3.1B polysilicon refinery in Nueces County, Texas, producing ultra-high-purity polysilicon for high-efficiency solar cells as part of a vertically integrated solar supply chain. Sawyer's roundup of facilities under construction or planned lists a 500k sqft advanced technology fab and a 7 million sqft Optimus factory at Giga Texas, a $10.1B solar manufacturing facility in Texas, and the $3.1B polysilicon refinery.

6. **Governance and Musk commentary:** Tesla issued a 5-page letter to shareholders pushing back on ISS and Glass Lewis, both of which recommended voting against re-electing Board Chair Robyn Denholm. Musk said he feels "pretty foolish" about his political spending, would have spent less if he could rewind, and that elections are not just about money. He called Jensen Huang a friend and compared him to "Michael Jordan at peak level" in what he means to a team, saying no one can replace him.
