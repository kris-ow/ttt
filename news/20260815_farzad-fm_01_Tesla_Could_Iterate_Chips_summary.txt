This short clip argues that Tesla's in-house chip fabrication effort (Terafab) could let the company iterate chip designs far faster than relying on TSMC, by controlling the mask shop and lithography steps internally rather than sending designs out to a foundry.

## Financials
1. **In-house fab could cut chip iteration time from months to weeks:** The speaker describes a hypothetical in-house process where Tesla creates its own photomasks and runs test wafer lots on its own lithography tools, compressing a design-test-fix iteration cycle to one to two weeks per loop, versus three to four months in the traditional TSMC foundry process — a claimed structural advantage of roughly five to ten times faster iteration, which the speaker calls conservative.
2. **Terafab prototyping scale is small relative to production fabs:** For prototyping and iteration purposes, the speaker states Terafab would need only about two to five EUV lithography machines, contrasted with the roughly 500 machines implied for full-scale production capacity.
