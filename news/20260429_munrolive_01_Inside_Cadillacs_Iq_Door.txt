So, we're looking at some of the torn
down parts from the 2025 Cadillac IQ,
the Escalade. And in looking at this,
we're trying to look at the different
manufacturing points. And there's
something I wanted to point out on the
door. We have this decorative chrome
trim, but you'll notice that this is not
a separate part from this. It is all
integrated. How did they do that? This
is a two-shot mold. They'll injection
mold one part with a polycarbonate,
injection mold over top of that with an
ABS. They'll take this entire part and
put it into the chrome tank. The chrome
only attaches to the ABS. So, they're
not paying for all the expensive chrome
to go over this entire structure, but
they're still able to be one single
component.
