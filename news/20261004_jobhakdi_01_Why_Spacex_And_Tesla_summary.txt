## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Host's terrestrial compute revenue/earnings projection:** The host projects SpaceX will have roughly 5 gigawatts of compute online by the end of next year (his own conservative estimate; he notes Musk has said at least 5GW will be added, which would put the total higher), translating to an estimated $250 billion in revenue and $140 billion in earnings run-rate — his own model, not an official SpaceX figure.
2. **Orbital AI compute timeline — 2028 target:** The host argues SpaceX will have the two necessary components for orbital compute — Starship launch costs below $700/kg (achievable even without reusability) and a space-rated Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 rack delivering 250kW per satellite — in place by 2028, with Nvidia's rack reportedly planned for completion in Q4 2027; he says roughly 4,000 such satellites would yield one gigawatt in orbit.
