## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- New SEC filings disclose Google, Nvidia, and AMD stakes in SpaceX valued at the $140/share closing price: Google $77.1B, Nvidia $17.2B, AMD $463M — the first public look at major tech-giant ownership since SPCX's June IPO.
- SpaceX officially closed its $60B acquisition of AI coding tool Cursor; no integration details disclosed. Cern Basher (brighterwithherbert) separately floats — as his own unconfirmed speculation — that Cursor's LLM could compete with Anthropic (~$100B/year revenue) and represents unmodeled upside for SpaceX given Cursor's ~$8B revenue.

## Robotaxi
- Community trackers (robotaxi radar, Keenan, others) counted 100+ Cybercabs at a Houston lot, 38 Cybercabs plus 8 Model Ys in Fort Worth/Dallas, and 42 in Las Vegas — over 200 photographed across roughly 14 cities/regions as of late July, per brighterwithherbert.
- Nevada's interim permit (issued July 27) caps Tesla at just 10 fully autonomous vehicles on the Las Vegas Strip corridor under 45 mph, despite Tesla's request for up to 5,000 in year one.
- Austin Fire Department trucks were seen at Tesla's robotaxi depot with no matching call logged, and QR shutdown stickers plus a production-integrated Starlink antenna panel point to driverless-readiness prep.
- Chuck Cook's multi-day paying rides in Austin showed solid core driving but recurring pickup/drop-off failures — wrong pin locations, a 34-minute ride for 2.1 miles, and one ride requiring remote-agent intervention — reinforcing that curb-level logistics, not driving capability, remains the bottleneck.
- Tesla filed for a 124-stall V4 Supercharger station in San Francisco on a current taxi storage lot, read as fleet-scale charging infrastructure being built ahead of launch.
- Morgan Stanley reportedly said this week Tesla is in "show-me" territory on robotaxi scaling evidence; Waymo's 691 registered Texas vehicles outnumber Tesla's registered count more than 3-to-1, per brighterwithherbert's sourcing.

## Financials
- Analyst Cern Basher frames Nvidia's Jensen Huang proposal — treating AI compute as a financeable asset class backed by $500B+ from Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Goldman Sachs and KKR — as a potential funding path for Tesla's ~$119B Terafab chip fab.
- Tesla's CFO has said the company plans to raise capex to $25B and raise $30B in debt this year for robotaxi and Optimus, currently self-funded since robotaxi cash flow isn't yet predictable enough for Wall Street financing.
- Separate reporting describes a proposed $25B Musk-backed fab targeting output equal to roughly 70% of TSMC's global wafer volume (~1.4M wafer starts/month), covering inference chips for Tesla/Optimus and a new "D3" chip for SpaceX orbital AI satellites.
- Tesla's in-house fab approach could cut chip iteration cycles to one-to-two weeks versus three-to-four months via TSMC, a claimed 5-10x speed advantage, per a Farzad-FM analysis.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla enabled 2.4 kW V2L for Model Y Premium/Performance in the US and Puerto Rico via an $80 adapter attached to the Gen 3 Mobile Connector — same part and price as Cybertruck's April 2025 launch. Model Y Standard and all Model 3 trims remain unsupported.
- Tesla's 2.4 kW V2L trails rivals: Hyundai Ioniq 5 (3.6 kW since 2021), Ford F-150 Lightning (2.4-9.6 kW), Chevy Silverado EV (up to 10.2 kW), per Electrek.
- Tesla launched a US/Puerto Rico Certified Pre-Owned program with a free month of FSD and Premium Connectivity plus a 115-point inspection; multiple Model Y L order holders reported receiving delivery windows, and a Model Y Performance variant surfaced in China MIIT filings.

## Autonomous Driving
- FSD added roughly 170,000 new paid subscribers in Q1 and 150,000-160,000 more in Q2, per hosts on Futureaza, framed as an adoption tipping point despite limited market availability.
- Tesla released a compilation showing FSD (Supervised) avoiding pedestrian collisions across multiple European countries, including ones without regulatory approval yet.
- brighterwithherbert's host argues FSD v15's expected addition of persistent location memory is the key unlock for fixing pickup/drop-off failures and scaling robotaxi, though timing is unconfirmed.
- Rivian reaffirmed plans for point-to-point assisted driving later in 2026, with wider rollout targeted for 2027.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Sawyer Merritt reported 370+ Mbps in-flight Starlink Wi-Fi speeds on a United flight, and a new regulatory filing suggests SpaceX has developed its first Wi-Fi 7 router for Starlink.
