## Brief

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- New SEC filings, valued at SpaceX's $140/share closing price, disclose Google's stake at $77.1 billion, Nvidia's at $17.2 billion, and AMD's at $463 million — underscoring how deeply major AI/tech players are invested in SPCX post-IPO (per Sawyer Merritt).
- SpaceX officially closed its previously announced $60 billion Cursor acquisition; no integration details disclosed. Analyst Cern Basher separately speculated (unconfirmed) that Cursor's coding LLM, which he says generates $8 billion in revenue versus Anthropic's roughly $100 billion, represents unmodeled SpaceX upside.

## Robotaxi
- Community trackers documented 100+ Cybercabs at a Houston lot, 38 Cybercabs plus 8 Model Ys in Fort Worth/Dallas, and 42 in Las Vegas — north of 200 Cybercabs photographed staging across roughly 14 regions and eight states (per brighterwithherbert).
- Nevada's interim permit caps Tesla at just 10 fully autonomous vehicles on the Las Vegas Strip corridor (45 mph roads only, no airport pickups), despite Tesla requesting approval for up to 5,000 vehicles in year one.
- A production-integrated Starlink antenna panel was spotted on a Cybercab, possibly now standard equipment to address connectivity gaps at geofence edges; Austin Fire Department trucks were seen at Tesla's robotaxi depot in an apparent driverless-readiness training exercise.
- Rider Chuck Cook's Austin trips highlighted pickup/drop-off as the main weak point — a 34-minute ride for 2.1 miles, a missed hotel pin, and a mid-road stop needing remote-agent intervention — while the core driving task looked largely solved.
- Tesla filed for a 124-stall V4 Supercharger station in San Francisco on a former taxi storage lot, read as infrastructure being built ahead of service launch. Waymo has 691 registered vehicles in Texas versus Tesla's roughly 181–186, per community trackers cited by the host; Morgan Stanley reportedly said this week Tesla remains in "show-me" territory on robotaxi scaling.

## Financials
- Analyst Cern Basher argues Jensen Huang's proposal to finance AI compute with institutional capital (Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Goldman, KKR, $500B+ to start) could help fund Tesla's estimated $119 billion Terafab chip factory, structured like debt rather than equity.
- Tesla's CFO has said the company plans to raise capex to $25 billion and raise $30 billion in debt this year for robotaxi and Optimus, self-funded for now since robotaxi cash flow isn't yet predictable enough for Wall Street securitization (per Basher).
- Separately, Elon Musk is reportedly championing a $25 billion fab aiming for volume equal to roughly 70% of TSMC's global output (about 1.4 million wafer starts/month), fully vertically integrated under one roof, producing Tesla/Optimus inference chips and a new "D3" chip for SpaceX orbital AI satellites (per farzad-fm).
- A separate segment argues Tesla's in-house fab could iterate chip designs 5–10x faster than the traditional TSMC foundry loop (1–2 weeks vs. 3–4 months per cycle).

## Autonomous Driving
- Hosts on futureaza cite roughly 170,000 new paid FSD subscribers in Q1 and 150,000–160,000 more in Q2 as evidence of a broader adoption tipping point, alongside viral organic influencer content.
- Tesla released a compilation showing FSD (Supervised) avoiding accidents and pedestrian collisions across multiple European countries, including some where FSD isn't yet regulator-approved.
- The brighterwithherbert host frames FSD v15 — expected to add persistent memory of pickup locations — as the key unlock needed to scale robotaxi curb/pickup logistics beyond manual, one-location-at-a-time fixes.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla enabled 2.4 kW V2L on Model Y Premium (US/Puerto Rico) via an $80 adapter attached to the Gen 3 Mobile Connector — the same part and price point launched for Cybertruck in April 2025. Model Y Standard and all Model 3s remain unsupported; Tesla's 2.4 kW output trails Hyundai (3.6 kW), Ford's Lightning (up to 9.6 kW), and Chevy's Silverado EV (up to 10.2 kW).
- Tesla launched a Certified Pre-Owned program in the US and Puerto Rico, including one free month of FSD (Supervised) and Premium Connectivity plus a 115-point inspection; also piloting new lithium recovery technology aiming to extend closed-loop recycling to graphite, nickel, and cobalt.
- Multiple Model Y L order holders report receiving delivery date windows; a Model Y Performance variant surfaced in China's MIIT filings ahead of an apparent China launch.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Sawyer Merritt reported 370+ Mbps in-flight Starlink Wi-Fi speeds on a United flight (~215x faster than a prior non-Starlink flight); a new regulatory filing suggests SpaceX has developed its first Wi-Fi 7 router for Starlink.

## Market & Competition
