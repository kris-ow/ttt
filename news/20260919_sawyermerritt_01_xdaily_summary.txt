## Key Posts / Themes

1. **NHTSA developing new federal AV safety standard:** NHTSA announced it is creating a new federal safety standard specifically for autonomous driving system performance, aimed at preparing for larger-scale Robotaxi deployments in the U.S. The framework reportedly shifts away from prescriptive requirements (e.g., mandating a steering wheel or specific physical controls) toward performance-based standards — a structural tailwind for Tesla's Cybercab, which has no steering wheel or pedals. This is a significant regulatory signal for robotaxi investors, though the standard's specifics/timeline weren't detailed in the post.

2. **FSD safety data — Australia/New Zealand:** Tesla reported that vehicles driving on FSD (Supervised) in Australia and New Zealand experienced on average 40% fewer collisions than those driven manually — one collision per 1.1 million miles on FSD versus one per 665,000 miles manually. This adds an international data point to Tesla's ongoing FSD safety case.

3. **Cybercab / Robotaxi training with first responders:** Tesla is running Cybercab and Model Y Robotaxi training sessions with first responders, including instruction on the on-screen joystick control used to move the Cybercab since it lacks a steering wheel or pedals. This points to active pre-deployment operational groundwork ahead of wider robotaxi rollout.

4. **SpaceX wins new NASA launch contract:** SpaceX was awarded a new $946 million NASA contract covering launch services for three missions, including ground, launch, in-orbit, return/recovery operations, cargo transportation per mission, and a lifeboat capability. This reinforces SpaceX's core government launch revenue base.

5. **SpaceX Nasdaq-100 weighting increase drives large passive flows:** SpaceX's Nasdaq-100 weighting was set to more than double at the close, an event estimated to trigger $15.5B–$22B in programmatic index-fund buying — one of the largest single-day passive flows of the quarter, per the post. The post notes that when SPCX first joined the index on July 7th, only 4-5% of its shares were held by index-tracking funds, implying substantial room for continued passive accumulation. This is a notable technical/flow catalyst for SPCX shares distinct from fundamentals.

6. **Starlink aviation momentum (United Airlines):** United Airlines said it has flown more than 31 million passengers on Starlink-powered aircraft across more than 464,000 flights, powering 14.2 million connected devices. Starting this week, passengers can watch NFL games on Starlink-enabled seatback screens — an incremental proof point for Starlink's aviation connectivity business line.

7. **Tesla Semi and Megapack manufacturing notes:** Top Gear's video of the production Tesla Semi line revealed the vehicle is powder coated rather than painted (like Megapacks, with no dedicated paint shop) and that the production version is 3% more efficient than the prototype. Separately, Tesla began construction on a $650 million Megapack facility in Belgium using next-gen Megablock technology and Megapack V3, sized to power 385,000 homes with 2.8 GWh of total storage — one of the larger Megapack projects noted to date.

8. **EV pricing and Roadster reservations:** Tesla reopened Roadster reservations, requiring a $5,000 credit card deposit plus a $45,000 wire transfer due within 10 days, unchanged from prior terms; Tesla cautioned any estimated purchase price is not final. Separately, Tokyo is reportedly offering the Model Y at under $23,000 due to a weak yen combined with new federal and city subsidies — one of the cheapest Model Y price points globally per the post.
