## Market & Competition

1. **Nvidia beats on all fronts, validates AGI thesis:** Nvidia reported earnings that beat on revenue and guidance across the board, with data center revenue described as nearly doubling. The company added $80 billion to its buyback plan and is generating approximately $81 billion in cash. Bhakdi frames this as a critical quarterly checkpoint validating the broader AGI investment thesis — not a surprise, but an essential confirmation that the global AGI buildout remains on track. Nvidia's market cap sits at approximately $5.4 trillion, with Bhakdi expecting it to reach $6 trillion. Despite the blowout results, the stock barely moved in after-hours, which Bhakdi attributes to sky-high expectations already being priced in. He describes Nvidia's PE multiple as having dropped to 34 against 85% revenue growth, calling that combination "unbelievable."

2. **Iran conflict and bond yields as macro risk factors:** Bhakdi identifies two key macro forces that drove Tesla and the broader market lower in the days preceding the video. First, oil prices spiked sharply due to the ongoing closure of the Strait of Hormuz, which he warns could cause long-term irreversible economic damage if it persists another two weeks or more. Second, the 10-year yield spiked to approximately 4.68%, pressuring risk assets including high-growth equities like Tesla. He argues these are not just technical factors but genuine fundamental fears. He expresses moderate confidence that Trump will not escalate military action against Iran, citing strong political and economic incentives for de-escalation, but acknowledges tail risk remains, particularly over long weekends when markets are closed and geopolitical events can unfold without circuit breakers.

3. **Tesla stock selloff and recovery context:** Tesla fell from approximately $445 to $395 over two trading days — a drop of roughly 11% — before recovering about 3.25% on the day of this video. Bhakdi describes the drop as partly technical (Tesla had been rejected twice near the $445–$450 resistance level) and partly macro-driven. He maintains a bullish price target of $490 — which he characterizes as the December 16, 2025 all-time high — as his base case, framing the selloff as a healthy retracement within an intact uptrend. He cautions, however, that breaking through $450 resistance will require a real catalyst, and he flags the robotaxi commercial deployment as the most likely such catalyst for a sustained move above $500.
