## Key Posts / Themes

1. **FSD European Rollout Accelerating — Belgium is the 5th Country:** Tesla FSD (Supervised) received official approval in Belgium, making it the 5th European country to approve the feature, joining the Netherlands, Denmark, Lithuania, and Estonia. Sawyer noted this now represents approximately 11% of all European countries, with the list "quickly growing." This is a meaningful regulatory milestone for the Europe-wide FSD rollout thesis, as each new country approval expands the addressable fleet for FSD monetization and data collection. The Belgium announcement alone drew 3,900 likes and 417 retweets — the highest-engagement post of the day — indicating strong community interest. The pace of approvals suggests regulatory momentum rather than isolated one-off clearances.

2. **Tesla FSD Crosses 11 Billion Cumulative Miles — Pace Accelerating:** Tesla's global fleet crossed 11 billion miles driven on FSD (Supervised), up from 10 billion the prior month. The current pace is approximately 1 billion FSD miles every 37 days. Sawyer noted that with more European countries receiving approval and Tesla's growing fleet, this pace is expected to increase further. This milestone matters for the autonomous driving thesis because cumulative miles are a proxy for training data accumulation and real-world edge case coverage — inputs that compound into safety and capability improvements over time. No specific disengagement rate or miles-per-incident figure was provided.

3. **FSD V14 Draws Enthusiastic Third-Party Review:** The Straight Pipes, a YouTube automotive channel with 1.8 million subscribers, reviewed the new Tesla Model Y Performance and spent the majority of their review discussing FSD (Supervised) V14's performance. Direct quotes included: *"It slowed for a bird! The Full-Self Driving is unreal. Mind-blowing. I was driving it in the city on my normal commute."* While anecdotal, reviews from mainstream automotive channels reaching large non-Tesla-specific audiences represent meaningful organic marketing and signal improving consumer perception of FSD quality. The specific version cited is V14.

4. **Actually Smart Summon Expanding to Cybertruck:** Tesla announced that Actually Smart Summon (ASS) — the more capable version of the Summon parking feature — will roll out to Cybertruck owners soon. This continues the pattern of FSD-adjacent features being extended across Tesla's vehicle lineup and represents incremental autonomous capability expansion outside the core FSD stack.

5. **Cybertruck Dual-Motor AWD Deliveries Begin at $59,990:** Tesla has begun deliveries of the Cybertruck Dual-Motor AWD variant, priced at $59,990. No delivery volume figures were provided. This adds a new configuration to the Cybertruck lineup at a lower price point than prior variants, potentially broadening demand.

6. **Ford's Electric Compact Pickup Spotted Testing — ~$30K, 2027 Launch:** Ford's smaller all-electric pickup truck was spotted testing on public roads for the first time. Ford has stated a starting price of approximately $30,000 and a 2027 launch target. It is described as noticeably smaller than the F-150 Lightning with a distinctive bed design. While not an immediate competitive threat to Tesla, a $30K electric truck entry from Ford in 2027 is relevant context for the broader EV competitive landscape, particularly in the truck segment where Tesla's Cybertruck competes.

7. **GM Announces Sodium-Ion Battery Development for Grid Storage:** GM announced plans to develop sodium-ion battery cells specifically for grid-scale energy storage, and plans to enable its EVs to send energy back to the grid (V2G). GM claims sodium-ion can outperform incumbent chemistries for stationary storage applications. This is a nascent competitive development in the energy storage space where Tesla's Megapack currently holds a strong market position — worth monitoring as a long-term competitive signal, though GM has no deployed product yet.
