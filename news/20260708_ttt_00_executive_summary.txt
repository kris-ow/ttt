## Robotaxi
- Cybercab confirmed as the "primary vehicle" for Tesla's Robotaxi fleet, dropping steering wheel/pedals to cut weight and cost, per Tesla's 2025 Annual Impact Report; Cybercab targeted to emit nearly half the GHG per mile of Model 3/Y.
- Lars Moravy said the Cybercab line is designed for ~90% full automation and could scale "far beyond what current market estimates say" (brighterwithherbert interview) — an executive target, not a disclosed plan.
- Finished vehicles now undergo a "full self-hearing" quality check: FSD drives the car over bumps at the factory while microphones and trained software listen for defects.
- Tesla's new 682,000 sq ft Austin lease could support logistics, service, or robotaxi operations, per Electrek, though Tesla has not confirmed the intended use.

## Humanoid Bots
- Steel is now going up on Tesla's new Optimus factory adjacent to Giga Texas, expected to take roughly a year to build before outfitting.
- Fremont's first real Optimus production line is being stood up on the former Model S/X floor; Moravy described ~40 modular sublines being validated in Germany before shipping to Fremont, and Musk visited the floor this week.
- Optimus BOM includes ~10,000 unique parts; no external supplier sells humanoid production lines, forcing Tesla to build in-house.
- A Tesla global VP reportedly said (secondhand) Optimus will reach scaled mass production by end of 2026; one commentator's own estimate pegs production launch for August–September with thousands of units by December.
- Munro Live teardown shows an Optimus knee joint using laptop-style heat pipe cooling — phase-change refrigerant moves heat with near-zero temperature delta.

## Energy
- Tesla Home optimizes solar, Powerwall, Wall Connector, and smart breakers via Opticaster AI; ships standard on every Powerwall, no new hardware, via app v4.58.6.
- Opticaster has logged "over a hundred million hours" of operation and delivered "tens of millions of dollars" in customer value; Tesla's VPP paid Powerwall owners nearly $10 million in 2024.
- Megapack orders exceeded $9B over six weeks (43+ GWh), including a new $3B Esyasoft deal spanning UK, Western Europe, GCC, and India.
- Tesla installed 186 wall connectors across 33 sites in a recent two-week span.
