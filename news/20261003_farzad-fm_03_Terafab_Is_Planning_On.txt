Now the terapab is planning on making
two broad families of chips. One would
be turned for vehicles and Optimus
robots on Earth and another would be
built for the radiation power and heat
limits of orbital compute. If terrafab
works, the model team that builds things
like rock 4.6 or even Tesla's FSD
self-driving model can tell the chip
team which operations consume the most
time and energy on the chip. Then that
team can change the silicon around those
operations. The data center team can
change power and cooling around the
chip. The satellite team can change the
solar array and the radiator around the
same hardware. Then the next versions
can be trained and served on a system
designed around their actual needs.
