This video tracks the current state of Tesla's Cybercab robotaxi rollout, combining fleet-count updates from third-party trackers with firsthand rider impressions from Austin, and closes with news of progress toward FSD approval in Europe.

## Robotaxi
1. **Fleet sightings and registrations climbing in Austin and Dallas:** Citing an account called HXM, the hosts note Cybercab sightings in Austin rose from 45 on August 31st to 169, while Model Y robotaxi sightings rose from 269 to 420 — a combined 589 vehicles. A tracker reportedly recorded 868 total Cybercab sightings in Austin for the week, with 670 described as driverless; separately, about 300 Cybercabs were spotted in Dallas. One host argues registered-vehicle counts are the only independently verifiable metric (versus company-reported autonomous miles), and says current registrations are roughly tracking a ~17% week-over-week growth trajectory, though sustaining that pace will require registering more vehicles each week.
2. **Operating hours extended to 11pm:** Elon Musk announced robotaxi operating hours moved from 10:00 PM to 11:00 PM; he said the main reason for earlier limits was caution around hard-to-see animals at night, citing the example of "gray kittens on gray asphalt in the dark."
3. **Firsthand Cybercab ride in Austin called highly polished:** A rider (Phil), on his first Cybercab trip to the University of Texas, described the experience as exceeding expectations and feeling like a "mature product" rather than a work in progress — citing smooth navigation through a tight parking lot, app accuracy on pickup distance and car-location notifications, and ambient touches like the car's purple light/glow signaling which vehicle was theirs. Riders also noted a parking lot full of newly-built Cybercabs without license plates, apparently fresh from the factory and awaiting service entry.
4. **Direct comparison to Waymo favors Cybercab on styling and confidence, with caveats:** Riders who saw a Waymo vehicle parked next to a Cybercab in Austin said the Cybercab felt more premium and "elegant" by comparison. One host, after recently observing Waymo vehicles in San Francisco, said Waymo seemed to have reached a safe operating point but was notably hesitant entering intersections, which he found more off-putting than Tesla's approach; he cautioned that broader confidence in Cybercab's autonomy needs more riders to personally experience it over more varied driving conditions.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **German regulator reportedly pushing for FSD approval across the EU:** The hosts discuss a presentation by a German Ministry of Transport official (Stefan Bilger) that Elon Musk thanked him for. Per the hosts, Tesla's FSD in Germany may be rebranded (referred to in the discussion as "Tesla's assisted driving," abbreviated "TAD") and Tesla agreed to cap speed-limit overshoot at 10% (versus a previous allowance of up to 50% over the limit) as part of resolving regulators' objections — with the official reportedly now willing to push for approval not just in Germany but across the entire EU.
