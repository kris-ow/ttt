This video is a first-person ride-along review from a Tesla investor/creator who took two Cybercab rides in Austin on what he describes as the second day of its public rollout, sharing impressions of the cabin, ride quality, and the driverless experience compared to Waymo and Model Y robotaxi rides.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab public rollout experience in Austin:** The creator took two rides (roughly 40–45 minutes each) in a Cybercab during what he calls the second day of public rollout via the robotaxi app, noting a wait time of about 40–45 minutes for both rides, which he expects to shorten as more Cybercabs are deployed.
2. **Cabin design and comfort:** He highlights a large interior screen (he'd heard it was 22 inches, calling it the biggest screen Tesla has put in a car), spacious legroom, a quiet cabin, and Spotify/YouTube integration; he found the ride quality closer to a Model 3's road feel than a plush luxury ride, and suggested Tesla could make it more comfort-oriented over time.
3. **Removal of all driver controls:** Unlike a Waymo or a Model Y robotaxi (which retain a steering wheel, empty driver's seat, and speedometer as visual reminders of a "missing driver"), the Cybercab has no steering wheel, pedals, speedometer, side mirrors, or rearview mirror — only a small speed readout — which he says makes the ride feel safer, more stable, and fundamentally different from any other autonomous ride-hailing experience he's had.
4. **Speculation on hardware/software version:** He guesses the Cybercab is running FSD version 15 and speculates it may already have the AI5 chip, contrasting it with his Model Y's AI4 chip and version 14 software, but frames both as "pretty much flawless" — this is stated as his own guess, not confirmed.
5. **Broader implications teased:** He plans a follow-up video covering the "paradigm shift" the Cybercab represents and how he expects Tesla to scale the robotaxi business going forward, and references a prior video (posted the day before) covering an hour of engineer conversations from the Cybercab event.
