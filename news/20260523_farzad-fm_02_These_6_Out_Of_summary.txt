## Market & Competition

1. **The AI War's Compounding Weapon:** The host argues this conflict is structurally unlike any previous geopolitical struggle because the weapon — compute — compounds rather than depletes. Each training cycle produces a smarter model, which attracts better users, which generates better data, which trains an even smarter model. The faction already ahead widens its lead with every iteration, making it mathematically difficult for latecomers to catch up. The host argues the window for new entrants effectively closed in 2024.

2. **Training Cost Escalation:** GPT-3 training costs in 2020 were estimated between $4–12 million. GPT-4 training in 2023 cost between $40–100 million — roughly a 10x increase in three years. Historical data going back to 2016 shows training costs have grown approximately 2.4x per year, implying a single frontier model training run could exceed $1 billion by 2027. This cost trajectory is rapidly shrinking the list of organizations capable of staying in the race.

3. **High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) as the True Constraint:** The host argues that most analysts focus too narrowly on raw GPU counts. The actual binding constraint on AI model training is High Bandwidth Memory (HBM), which coordinates GPU processing. Without sufficient HBM, large GPU clusters cannot work in sync. The faction controlling the most HBM controls the actual training ceiling for frontier models.
