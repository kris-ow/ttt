He also said United initially planned only 65% fleet coverage but moved to an all-in rollout after early passenger feedback showed people switching to regional flights specifically for Starlink access. This is a third-party commercial validation of Starlink's competitive positioning in aviation connectivity, though no specific subscriber or revenue figures were given.
