This is a weekly "Tesla Beat" roundtable (episode 151) from futureaza covering a busy news week: Cybercab accessibility testing, robotaxi expansion to a second state and new city, New Jersey's proposed lidar/radar mandate, Model Y Launch Series production details, Optimus supply chain localization, Tesla Semi Megacharger progress, and various minor Tesla ecosystem items (Impact Report pricing stats, Tesla Diner anniversary, Porsche supplier bankruptcy).

## Robotaxi
1. **Robotaxi service goes unsupervised in a new state and city:** Hosts report Tesla launched unsupervised robotaxi service in a new city (described as a very large Florida city, likely Miami) without a gradual supervised-to-unsupervised transition, marking what the hosts believe is the fourth city and second state with unsupervised operation. Community members reportedly captured footage of the launch.
2. **Cybercab accessibility testing at NFB convention:** Tesla brought Cybercab to the National Federation of the Blind's annual convention in Austin for hands-on accessibility testing, including braille lettering on physical controls and space for service animals; one host describes viewer feedback (e.g., braille on door latches, phone-based walking directions) being incorporated by Tesla engineers ahead of wider deployment.
3. **Cybercab "hive" curtain pulled back in Austin:** A large storage lot in Austin holding what hosts estimate as dozens (50-60+) of Cybercabs, previously hidden behind privacy fencing, has had that fencing removed, which hosts interpret as a sign of imminent broader deployment. A host also mentions seeing footage (attributed to David Moss) of what was described as the first unsupervised Cybercab spotted testing.
4. **New Jersey lidar/radar mandate proposal:** Hosts strongly criticize a New Jersey proposal to mandate radar/lidar for robotaxi operation in the state, arguing it reflects a misunderstanding of the technology and predicting it will make the state less competitive and simply push Tesla to avoid operating there; comparison drawn to New Jersey's past removal of Tesla Superchargers.
5. **Grok voice integration seen as key robotaxi differentiator:** Hosts discuss upcoming integration of Grok as an in-car conversational AI, allowing riders (especially blind or wheelchair users) to ask about surroundings, destinations, or request stops, which they argue would give Tesla's robotaxi experience an edge over Waymo's more limited interaction model.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD vision-only navigation praised in edge case:** A host describes a personal experience where FSD (supervised) correctly navigated an unmapped park area purely via vision, exiting on the correct side without relying on map data.
2. **Ashok Elluswamy's timeline credibility:** Hosts state Ashok Elluswamy is working on adding voice/context input to FSD (e.g., telling the car specific destination details) and assert his public timeline commitments have historically been highly accurate, citing only two minor past misses.

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Optimus battery/lithium supply chain localized in Texas:** A host states Optimus batteries are being sourced from a facility across the campus from the Texas factory, with lithium supplied from Corpus Christi, Texas, framing this vertical/local integration as a competitive advantage over rivals (mostly Chinese manufacturers, said to produce over 95% of humanoid robots) who lack Tesla's scaling capability.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Model Y Launch Series produced only in Texas, not Fremont:** Hosts state the new Model Y variant is being built exclusively at Giga Texas rather than Fremont, attributing this to Fremont's factory being at capacity, partly due to space being reallocated to a new Optimus production line.
2. **Model 3 pricing well below average new car price:** Per Tesla's 2025 Impact Report highlighted in the video, Model 3 starting price is well below the U.S. average new car price (cited as roughly $36,000 vs. an average around $49,000).
3. **Used Model S prices rising:** A host notes anecdotally that older (2015-2017) used Model S prices have risen sharply (from around $8,000 to about $14,000), possibly due to rarity and legacy free Supercharging.
4. **Cybertruck bulletproof body cited in real-world safety incident:** A viral account describes a Cybertruck's bulletproof body being used to shelter a child during a shooting at a Fourth of July gathering; a host personally knows the individual involved.

## Energy
1. **Tesla Semi Megacharger buildout progressing:** Hosts highlight continued buildout of Megachargers for the Tesla Semi, noting the ability to charge during loading/unloading (unlike diesel refueling, which legally requires driver attendance), framing it as a time-saving advantage; speculation about eventual wireless charging expansion for semis.

## Market & Competition
1. **Plaid vs. McLaren Artura drag race:** A Tesla Plaid reportedly beat a McLaren Artura (a hybrid) by several car lengths in a straight-line race, with hosts noting the Artura's 0-60 time was around 3 seconds but still couldn't compete, while costing far more than the Plaid.
2. **German auto supplier facing bankruptcy after Porsche pulls orders:** A German automotive supplier is reportedly facing insolvency proceedings (cited date around October) after Porsche withdrew orders through around September, affecting 140+ employees; hosts link this to broader industry disruption from EV transition.
