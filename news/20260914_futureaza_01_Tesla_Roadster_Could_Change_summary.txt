## Electric Vehicles

The hosts speculate Tesla will likely reopen reservations at the event, possibly via a smaller refundable order fee rather than the original deposit structure.
2. **Unconfirmed pricing and performance rumors:** The host relays unverified rumors that the Roadster could cost $200,000–$250,000, following the original ~$250,000 Founders Series price set roughly nine years earlier (per the host), on top of existing $50,000 deposits some reservation holders have already paid.
