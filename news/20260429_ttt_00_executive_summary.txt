## Autonomous Driving
- **FSD V14.3.2 broad rollout underway.** Software version 2026.2.9.9 is being pushed to a significantly larger owner group, with a new "Navigation" disengagement reason added to the FSD popup — a refinement to Tesla's disengagement taxonomy that may support future unsupervised deployment requirements.
- **Tesla Safety Score V3.0 launches in Florida and Maryland.** New policyholders gain access immediately; existing policyholders transition July 12th. The primary new feature is night driving as a scored behavior category, adding nighttime behavioral data to Tesla's insurance and FSD training flywheel.
- **Tesla promises FSD V14 Lite for international HW3 owners — with no timeline.** Tesla posted on X that V14 Lite will expand internationally after the U.S. HW3 rollout completes, subject to technical verification, regional adaptation, and regulatory approvals. The U.S. HW3 rollout is targeted for end of June 2026, meaning international owners are waiting well into H2 2026 at the earliest.
- **HW3 collective claim crystallizes: ~3,000 owners, 29 countries, €6.5M.** Tesla's first European FSD approval (Netherlands) was restricted to HW4, prompting a Dutch Model 3 owner to launch a collective claim site. Tesla confirmed at Q1 2026 earnings that HW3 "simply does not have the capability to achieve unsupervised FSD." Tesla separately announced plans for dedicated micro-factories to retrofit approximately 4 million HW3 vehicles worldwide; HW4 Plus (doubled memory) was announced one day after the earnings call, raising questions about the upgrade cycle.
- **AI5 chip tape-out cited as key structural catalyst by Cantor Fitzgerald.** The milestone was referenced as a central pillar of the bull thesis and was a reason multiple institutional analysts held their ratings through the post-earnings retail selloff.
- **Genma's technical moat thesis: quantization-aware training on AI4 chips during every training epoch.** Anonymous 15-year ADAS engineer "Genma_JP" argues Tesla's most underappreciated moat is its Cortex cluster — ~40,000 AI4 in-car chips mixed with ~90,000 H100 GPUs — which runs inference on actual vehicle silicon during training, making the deployed model bit-exact with the trained model. He quotes Elon at the 2025 shareholders meeting: *"You actually have to train for integer inference — which everyone else is training for floating point."* Q1 2026 earnings referenced 150,000 H100-equivalent GPUs in Tesla's training infrastructure.
- **FSD Supervised earns positive first impressions in South Korea.** A South Korean reporter described the system as flawlessly handling traffic signal compliance, lane keeping, speed limits, merging, and direction changes — meaningful qualitative signal for international FSD expansion.

## Robotaxi
- **Active Robotaxi fleet: 649 (all modes); unsupervised fleet: 25.** Per RobotaxiTracker.com as of April 30, 2026. Breakdown: Bay Area 542, Austin 101, Dallas 3, Houston 3 (active total); Austin 19, Dallas 3, Houston 3 (unsupervised subset).
- **Bhakdi acknowledges fleet miss but maintains 10,000–20,000 Cybercabs by year-end.** Bhakdi's pre-earnings prediction of ~1,800 unsupervised robotaxis by April/May 2026 was not met; he self-assesses the miss at 2–3 months and projects ~800–900 vehicles by end of May at a ~50–100%/month growth rate. His year-end target of 10,000–20,000 deployed Cybercabs is unchanged. The host's personal estimate is ~15,000.
- **Cybercab hardware in active logistics movement.** Two separate semi trucks loaded with Cybercabs were spotted on April 28th — the second such sighting in a single day — consistent with pre-launch fleet staging activity.
- **Tesla Autonomy Pop-Up opens in Miami at F1 Grand Prix Fan Fest.** Starting April 29th at Lummus Park, attendees can see the Cybercab in person and meet Optimus during the Crypto.com Miami Grand Prix Fan Fest — placing both products in front of a large, affluent, high-visibility international audience.
- **Cybercab production rate referenced at ~300/month currently.** Per Bhakdi, observing increments of 10–20 units at a time. He reasons that if the rate doubles monthly, the fleet exceeds 10,000 by year-end. The host flags Cybercab production above 5,000 units at the Q2 2026 earnings (expected late July) as the manufacturing moat validation threshold.
- **Current operational issues are connectivity-related, not FSD-related.** Bhakdi, citing Dillon Loomis, identifies that known Cybercab service disruptions — screen blackouts, vehicles stopping — stem from AWS/server connection drops, not from FSD failures. Elon described remaining friction on the Q1 call as "convenience issues": frozen red lights, construction zone loops, pickup/dropoff logistics.
- **Cybercab structural cost advantage over Waymo quantified.** Per analysis presented by Brian (Futurazza): Cybercab ~$25,000–$30,000 to produce, ~4,000 parts, no paint shop needed, 500,000–1,000,000-mile target lifespan. Waymo's Jaguar iPace estimated at ~$150,000 per vehicle; Zoox at ~$250,000. Waymo prices ~$4/mile and is not yet profitable; Tesla is pricing Austin rides at ~$1.50/mile with a projected target of ~$1/mile and ~$0.50 profit per mile.
- **Waymo's HD map approach described as a structural scaling ceiling.** Genma argues every construction zone, repaved road, or power outage renders the map wrong, requiring constant human maintenance. Real failure cases cited include Waymo vehicles frozen in Nashville intersections requiring police intervention, and vehicles blocked in Phoenix and Houston construction zones. His conclusion: "The HD map is not a feature. It is proof that the intelligence is missing." Waymo's Zeekr next-gen platform is separately undermined by the 100% U.S. tariff on Chinese-made vehicles.

## Humanoid Bots
- **Optimus summer 2026 production ramp on track.** Per Farzad-FM, Tesla's Fremont factory is described as pivoting toward humanoid robot production, with a long-term target of millions of units per year. The ramp is cited as a core reason Elon seeks to step back from day-to-day CEO duties.

## Electric Vehicles
- **First Tesla Semi rolls off high-volume production line at Giga Nevada.** The dedicated 1.7-million-square-foot factory adjacent to Gigafactory Nevada is designed for 50,000 trucks/year, with analysts projecting 5,000–15,000 deliveries in 2026 (Electrek's author considers the high end optimistic). Two trims: Standard Range ~$260,000 (325 miles), Long Range $290,000 (500 miles) — both positioned as lowest-priced Class 8 BEV tractors on the market. In California's Clean Truck voucher program (Jan 2025–Feb 2026), Tesla Semi accounted for 965 of 1,067 applications.
- **MDB Transportation launches 3-week Tesla Semi pilot at Ports of LA and Long Beach.** The Compton-based drayage operator is tracking energy efficiency, cycle time, and driver experience — the second port trucking company to deploy the Semi commercially. Hight Logistics (Long Beach) also took delivery earlier in 2026 and plans fleet expansion.
- **Megacharger network: 66 locations mapped across 15 states.** First station open in Ontario, California; first Pilot truck stop locations expected by summer 2026. The Semi supports 1.2-MW charging, restoring 60% range in ~30 minutes.
- **"Tesla Semi as a Service" model to be unveiled at ACT Expo, May 4, 2026.** Alyath plans a bundled monthly payment covering vehicle, charging infrastructure, and energy — eliminating fleet capex barriers.
- **Model Y L unveiled in India; deliveries starting this summer.** Model Y L Premium AWD priced at ₹61,99,000 (~$66,600 USD), approximately 9% cheaper than the smaller Model Y Premium RWD in that market.
- **NHTSA closes Model Y steering wheel probe without action.** The preliminary evaluation covering 120,089 2023 Model Y vehicles regarding steering wheel detachment reports was formally closed with no action against Tesla.

## Financials
- **Q1 2026 beat across revenue, gross margin, EPS, and free cash flow.** Bhakdi described auto margins as "positively surprised" — unexpected upside. CIC noted midpoint gross margins showed continued improvement.
- **2026 capex guidance raised $5B to $25B total.** Institutional analysts broadly framed the increase as a growth investment, not a negative. Roth Capital's Craig Irwin explicitly called it a positive and redirected attention to long-term growth including the Terafab facility.
- **Institutional price targets post-earnings: $465–$550.** Cantor Fitzgerald $510 (overweight), CIC $500 (outperform), Baird $522 (lowered from $538, outperform maintained), RBC $465 (buy), Roth Capital ~$550 (buy). No major downgrades.
- **Tesla converted its $2B xAI investment into a direct SpaceX equity stake.** Bloomberg reported the conversion on March 12, 2026, ahead of SpaceX's planned IPO. This is a structural balance sheet shift, not a routine financial transaction.

## Market & Competition
- **SpaceX IPO reportedly being roadshowed at $1.75T valuation, raising $75B, June 2026 target.** Bhakdi and others discuss a potential Tesla-SpaceX merger mechanism post-IPO. Chamath Palihapitiya stated he is "99% sure" a reverse merger will happen. K2 Merger Sub Inc. and K2 Merger Sub 2 LLC were filed in Nevada on January 21, 2026.
- **Xpeng identified as only credible global challenger to Tesla in autonomy.** Genma notes Xpeng went full vision-only, end-to-end from scratch after FSD V12 in July 2024, built its own Turing chip (3,000 TOPS, 200,000+ cumulative units shipped), and is offering VLA 2.0 free to all Turing-equipped owners. Projected ~500,000 vehicles running VLA 2.0 by end of 2026. Remaining gaps vs. Tesla: ~30,000 GPUs (roughly 5x less compute than Cortex) and no Turing chip in training loop. Genma predicts Xpeng unsupervised release in 2028.
- **Legacy OEM catch-up blocked by politics, not technology.** Genma argues a competent team could have a drivable FSD mule in 9–12 months and production intent in 24–36 months — but internal politics (ADAS team redundancies, supplier contract clauses, executive ego) make adoption structurally unlikely. Elon stated he offered FSD licensing to legacy OEMs and they declined.
- **U.S. gas prices at multi-year high: $4.18/gallon nationally, ~$6 in California.** Model Y Premium operating cost ~$0.04/mile vs. $0.104/mile for a Toyota RAV4 Hybrid — a 61% cost-per-mile advantage, structurally strengthening the EV demand case.
