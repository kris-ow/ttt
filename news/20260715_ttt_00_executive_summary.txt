## Robotaxi
- Cybercab's reaction injection molded body panels eliminate the traditional paint shop for most of the vehicle, per Joe Tegtmeyer; only door rings and the steel passenger structure still need e-coating, sharing a smaller paint line with Cybertruck. Panels reportedly bolt on and off in about 20 minutes without repainting, and minor scratches can be repaired without a paint job since color runs through the material.
- Curb weight came in at 3,113 lbs, lighter than Tegtmeyer expected (he anticipated closer to 3,500 lbs), and within roughly 400 lbs of a 1973 Datsun 240Z despite carrying batteries, castings, and modern safety/drive-by-wire systems. Front-wheel drive was reconfirmed.
- Tesla's 2025 impact report states the RIM paint-free process cuts painting-related pollution by 35% and largely eliminates VOC fumes; Cybercab is projected to emit nearly half the greenhouse gases per mile of a Model 3/Model Y, per the report as cited by TheTeslaSpace. Lars Moravy has confirmed 165 Wh/mile efficiency, per the same source.
- Cybercab deployments continue expanding, with sightings reported in Houston, Massachusetts and elsewhere, plus a claim of sub-five-minute wait times in Miami, per Jo Bhakdi.

## Humanoid Bots
- A Samsung Foundry principal engineer, in a since-deleted LinkedIn post cited by TheTeslaSpace, said Samsung has completed manufacturing tapeout prep for Tesla's AI5 chip at its Taylor, Texas plant — distinct from Tesla's own design tapeout, which Musk said was finished earlier this year. The manufacturing deal is described as worth $16.5 billion, per the host.
- AI5 is being aimed primarily at Optimus and Tesla's AI training computers rather than vehicles, since Musk has said AI4 already gives FSD enough compute, per the host. AI5 can draw up to 800W under load but has reportedly been optimized down to roughly 250W to fit Optimus's much smaller 2.3 kWh battery (~32x smaller than a Model Y pack).
- Musk's chip roadmap per the host: AI5 boosts Optimus and vehicle compute, AI6 shifts focus further toward Optimus and training, and AI7 is envisioned for eventual satellite deployment.
- A separate, unverified claim relayed by Jo Bhakdi — attributed to a purported Samsung foundry employee — says Tesla's AI5 chip has completed tape-out.

## Electric Vehicles
- A Swedish used-EV retailer (Carla) analyzed nearly 10,000 real-world battery tests and found CATL LFP Model 3 packs averaged 93.3% health past 62,000 miles, beating every nickel-based Model 3 variant by roughly five points, per Electrek. Tesla has separately reported ~15% capacity loss after 200,000 miles for Model 3/Model Y Long Range packs.
- FSD (Supervised) mileage in Europe has passed 50 million km cumulative, up from 20 million km roughly 7.5 weeks earlier, per Tesla.
- The Model Y L has begun appearing in select U.S. showrooms for customers to sit in, though demo drives aren't yet available.
- ATC Logistics is retiring 11 diesel day cabs for 20 Tesla Semis, running out of a Rancho Dominguez, CA depot with fast-charging targeted for early 2027.
