## Brief

- Cybercab skips paint shop, uses color-molded composite panels
- AI5 chip tapeout complete at Samsung's Taylor, TX plant
- FSD (Supervised) Europe crosses 50M km cumulative
- Cybercab efficiency confirmed at 165 Wh/mile by Lars Moravy
- Tesla Semi lands 20-truck ATC Logistics deployment deal

## Robotaxi
- Cybercab's reaction injection molded (RIM) body panels eliminate the traditional paint shop for most of the vehicle, per Joe Tegtmeyer; door rings and steel structure still need e-coating, sharing a smaller paint process with Cybertruck. Panels are bolt-on and reportedly swappable in ~20 minutes without repainting, a potential repair-cost advantage for fleet economics.
- Cybercab curb weight came in at 3,113 lbs, lighter than Tegtmeyer expected given battery and casting mass, and confirmed front-wheel drive.
- Per Tesla's 2025 impact report (via TheTeslaSpace), the RIM process cuts painting-related pollution by 35% and Cybercab is projected to emit nearly half the greenhouse gases per mile of a Model 3/Model Y; Lars Moravy confirmed 165 Wh/mile efficiency.
- Jo Bhakdi cites Cybercab sightings in Houston and Massachusetts and sub-five-minute wait times reported in Miami, reading the geographic spread as evidence of real operational intent rather than a showcase.

## Humanoid Bots
- A Samsung Foundry engineer's since-deleted LinkedIn post claims Samsung has completed manufacturing tapeout prep for Tesla's AI5 chip at its Taylor, Texas plant, distinct from Tesla's own design tapeout completed earlier per Musk; the manufacturing deal is reportedly worth $16.5 billion (per TheTeslaSpace host).
- AI5 is aimed primarily at Optimus and Tesla's AI training computers rather than vehicles, since Musk has said AI4 already suffices for FSD; the chip draws up to 800W peak but has reportedly been optimized down to ~250W to fit Optimus's much smaller 2.3 kWh battery.
- Musk's roadmap per the host: AI4 powers current vehicles/robotaxis, AI5 boosts Optimus compute, AI6 leans further into Optimus/training, and AI7 is envisioned for satellite deployment.
- Musk says current chip industry capacity can't meet Tesla's future needs, motivating the proposed Terafab facility in Texas — described as ~100 million square feet and years from completion.

## Autonomous Driving
- FSD (Supervised) in Europe has surpassed 50 million cumulative kilometers (31 million miles), up from 20 million km roughly 7.5 weeks earlier, per Tesla.
- Bhakdi says he expects German FSD approval this year, framed explicitly as his own expectation, not confirmed.

## Electric Vehicles
- A Swedish retailer's analysis of ~9,954 battery tests found Tesla's CATL LFP Model 3 pack averages 93.3% health past 62,000 miles, beating all nickel-based Model 3 variants by up to five points (worst: Panasonic NCA), per Electrek.
- Tesla Semi: ATC Logistics is retiring 11 diesel day cabs for 20 Tesla Semis, targeting an early 2027 launch from a Rancho Dominguez, CA depot.
- Model Y L has begun appearing in select U.S. showrooms for sit-ins, though demo drives aren't yet available.
