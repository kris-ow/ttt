The host argues this single idea explains every infrastructure decision Musk has made over the past three years, and that it makes the compute race more important than robotaxi, Optimus, or Mars.

2. **Anthropic rents all of Colossus 1:** As of the week prior to publication (approximately mid-May 2026), Anthropic agreed to rent the entirety of Colossus 1 — 300 megawatts, 220,000 chips. The host notes this is the same company Elon called "anti-western" roughly three months prior, and quotes Musk's explanation for agreeing: "No one set off my evil detector." The host frames this as live confirmation that XAI compute is becoming the default rental market for frontier AI labs.

3. **Google reportedly in talks with XAI and SpaceX:** Google is reportedly in discussions to reserve Starship launches for its own space data centers and potentially rent compute from XAI. ## Infrastructure & Compute Build Details

1. **XAI's Colossus build speed as an industry benchmark:** Colossus 1 was built in an old Electrolux factory in Memphis, Tennessee. 2. **Solaris Energy Infrastructure as XAI's power solution:** Solaris Energy Infrastructure owns a fleet of approximately 600 megawatts of gas turbines, roughly 400 megawatts of which are already serving XAI. The remainder sits in a joint venture where Solaris owns just over half and XAI owns just under half. By Q2 2027, Solaris is targeting over 1.1 gigawatts of operating turbines serving XAI. The host frames this as vertical integration in power infrastructure that no competitor has replicated.

3. **Colossus 1 current state:** As of publication, Colossus 1 has roughly 220,000 GPUs — a mix of H100s, H200s, and newer GB200 Blackwells — operating at approximately 300 megawatts. This is the cluster Anthropic just rented in full.

4. **Colossus 2 — world's first 1-gigawatt coherent AI training cluster:** Colossus 2 is described as the world's first 1-gigawatt coherent AI training cluster. It has 555,000 Nvidia GPUs purchased, $18 billion in hardware, with a target of 1 million GPUs at a single site. "Coherent" means every GPU is networked together with near-zero latency into one unified training machine, as opposed to non-coherent clusters spread across buildings or cities that must train in chunks. XAI partnered with Cisco and Nvidia Spectrum-X to solve the networking problem. The host states most of the industry, including some OpenAI sites, is non-coherent at scale.

The price tag is described as over $20 billion, the largest private-sector investment in Mississippi's history. It adds approximately 350 megawatts of cooling capacity, pushing the total regional cluster toward 2 gigawatts. Operations are targeted to begin around February (2027, implied). The total picture: Colossus 1 (~300 MW), Colossus 2 main building (ramping toward 1 GW), Macrohard building (~350 MW cooling), and Macroharder (~South Haven) together approaching 2 gigawatts — three buildings, two states, one coherent training cluster.
