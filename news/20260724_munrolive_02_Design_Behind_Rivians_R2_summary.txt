This clip is a short segment from Munro Live examining the design of Rivian's R2, focusing specifically on an engineering-driven styling choice on the hood. There is no Tesla or SpaceX content in this excerpt; it is competitor-focused vehicle teardown commentary.

## Market & Competition
1. **Rivian R2 hood cut line driven by European pedestrian protection rules:** A Rivian design executive explains that the R2's hood cut line was relocated from the top of the hood (as on the R1) to the side, in order to create more crushable space for pedestrian protection compliance in Europe. Rather than treating this as a mere constraint, Rivian's design team built it into the vehicle's styling as a main character line running over the headlights and down into the A-line, doubling as the hood gap.

Notable quotes and data:
- "This main cut line here was something that we needed to address for pedestrian protection in Europe." — Rivian design executive
- The R1's hood cut line sits at the top; the R2 moves it to the side of the vehicle to increase crushable space.
