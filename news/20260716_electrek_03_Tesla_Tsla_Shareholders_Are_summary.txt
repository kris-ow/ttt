Ahead of Tesla's July 22 earnings call, shareholders have flooded the Say Technologies Q&A portal (nearly 300 questions submitted) with pointed questions about Tesla's repeated failure to hit its own short-term robotaxi, FSD, and Optimus targets, and with fears about a potential Tesla-SpaceX merger.

## Robotaxi & Autonomous Driving
1. **Top questions appear whale-boosted with softballs:** The two highest-voted questions — on Optimus Gen 3 production ramp/tasks by end of 2027, and on constraints to robotaxi expansion — each carry 4.8 million TSLA shares in votes, versus fewer than 975,000 shares for the third-place question, suggesting the article's author believes a single large shareholder pushed friendly questions to the top just before the deadline.
2. **Missed robotaxi guidance repeatedly:** A heavily upvoted question notes Tesla has missed short-term robotaxi guidance in three consecutive earnings reports, from a goal of 50% US coverage by end of 2025 to, most recently, seven new cities in the first half of 2026. The article notes Tesla quietly softened its expansion timeline for five announced cities in April, from a firm "1H 2026" commitment to vaguer "preparations underway" language, and that the flagship Austin service still runs only about 20 more vehicles than at launch over a year ago. The author argues the real bottleneck is software/safety validation on the Model Y robotaxi hardware stack shared with Cybercab, not Cybercab production capacity.
3. **HW3 refund/retrofit question unanswered:** A top question asks about Tesla's plan for Hardware 3 owners who paid for FSD, given Musk has confirmed HW3 cannot achieve unsupervised FSD — asking whether Tesla will offer free upgrades, transfers, or refunds, on what timeline, and what cost has been accrued. The article notes Musk has now confirmed this HW3 limitation for a second consecutive year, and that on the Q1 2026 call Tesla floated building micro-factories to retrofit affected cars — a plan the author states they've repeatedly argued is unlikely to happen. The author expects Tesla to avoid a concrete answer.

## Humanoid Bots / Financials / SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
4. **Merger and SpaceXAI concerns:** One heavily upvoted question asks Musk to commit to achieving at least half the goals in his 2025 compensation plan before considering any offer to acquire or merge Tesla, reflecting shareholder fear that Musk could fold Tesla into SpaceX (which absorbed xAI via a $1.25 trillion all-stock deal in February) on terms favoring his larger ownership stake there. Another question asks about plans to roll out "Digital Optimus" with SpaceXAI, including Tesla's capital contribution and revenue share — a concern given Musk previously said Tesla's AI team didn't need xAI to build Optimus's brain, and shareholders now fear AI value is migrating to a company they don't own.

## Editorial Stance
The author (Fred Lambert) argues the nature of shareholder questions—demanding accountability on missed robotaxi/FSD/Optimus milestones rather than celebrating record deliveries—shows Tesla's valuation rests almost entirely on unmet promises, and predicts Tesla will spotlight the whale-boosted softball questions on the call while avoiding the HW3 refund and accountability questions; the author frames this as a signal shareholders should watch closely.
