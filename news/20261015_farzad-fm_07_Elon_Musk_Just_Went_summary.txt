## Market & Competition
1. **Musk's tweet and Grok's shopping agent blocked by Amazon:** Replying to a software engineer, Elon Musk said Grok's shopping agent (Grokbot, from xAI, now described as part of SpaceX) would find ways around Amazon's block and had already purchased a Lego set directly from Lego's website; Amazon has listed Grok's bot on its robots.txt disallow list since May, three months before Grokbot's August launch.
2. **Amazon vs. Perplexity lawsuit:** Amazon sent Perplexity a cease-and-desist in late October 2025 over its Comet browser agent allegedly masking itself as a human Chrome user, then sued; a judge granted a preliminary injunction against Perplexity in March, but the Ninth Circuit threw out that ruling on August 4th, calling the agent "a tool, not a person." Amazon has since rewritten its lawsuit, with the next hearing set for November 20th; Comet is not currently under a court order.
3. **Amazon's own agent vs. third-party agents:** Amazon blocks outside shopping agents from checking out on its site (citing account integrity, fraud, and return-liability concerns) while operating its own agent, Rufus (rebranded "Alexa for shopping"), which over 350 million shoppers used in the past year and which drives order values over 40% higher in the US; Amazon CEO Andy Jassy said in January he's "bullish on agentic commerce" and open to third-party agents eventually, but nine months later no outside agent has a deal with Amazon, and Amazon now sells sponsored ads inside Rufus itself.
4. **Revenue incentive behind the block:** The video's framing: Amazon earned $68.6 billion from advertising in 2025 (ads grew 26% last quarter) and $172 billion from third-party seller fees, both of which depend on human browsing behavior (ad views, upsells, impulse buys) that AI agents skip entirely; Amazon reportedly filters bot traffic out of ad billing, meaning agent visits generate no ad revenue.
5. **Rest of industry moving toward agentic commerce:** OpenAI and Stripe launched instant checkout in ChatGPT via the Agentic Commerce Protocol in September 2025 with Walmart on board, though OpenAI later stepped back after Walmart said in-chat purchases converted about a third as well as normal; Walmart and Target now run their own in-ChatGPT checkout apps, and Google launched a "Universal Cart" spanning Walmart, Target, Nike, and Shopify stores (not Amazon) on the day of this video. eBay, like Amazon, keeps checkout closed to agents.
6. **Limits and risks of shopping agents:** An August survey by payments firm NMI found only 11% of US shoppers had let an AI agent complete a purchase; Grok's bot terms make users responsible for all charges it makes; a 2025 Guardio test found Perplexity's Comet agent bought an Apple Watch on a fake Walmart site without asking first, illustrating agent vulnerability to scams.
