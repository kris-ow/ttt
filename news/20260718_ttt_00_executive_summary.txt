## Financials
- Tesla released company-compiled Q2 2026 sell-side consensus ahead of earnings: analysts expect ~$27.6B revenue and ~$1.3B net income, setting the bar for the upcoming report, per Sawyer Merritt.
- Analyst Larry Goldberg projects Tesla's 2026 cash investment will land closer to $40 billion than the $25 billion previously guided, citing Terafab construction, possible space-based data center involvement, robotaxi rollout, and the Optimus ramp. With cash on hand around $40 billion, he estimates Tesla may need to raise roughly $10 billion via bond in Q1 next year to maintain a $12–18 billion cash cushion.
- Goldberg attributes Tesla's ability to fund heavy capex while growing cash to flat management structure and low overhead, contrasting this with Lucid, whose lifetime revenue (~$1.6 billion) he says is dwarfed by a single reported ~$5 billion European Megapack deal.

## Robotaxi
- Tesla Robotaxi appears to have expanded into "another new market," per Sawyer Merritt, though no city or fleet figures were given.
- Goldberg estimates Tesla's in-house Cybercab production cost at $17,000–$20,000 per unit, which he says will act as a cash-flow drag for one to three years before the robotaxi business turns cash-flow positive.

## Autonomous Driving
- FSD (Supervised) crossed 12 billion cumulative miles, up from 11 billion on June 9 — roughly a billion miles added in about five weeks, per Sawyer Merritt.
- A Hardware 3 2020 Model 3 (~180,000 miles) completed a roughly 4,255-mile Key West-to-Washington-state drive on early beta FSD 14 "light" with no driver pedal, brake, or steering inputs, per brighterwithherbert.
- Community member David Moss's intervention-free FSD streak reached ~13,000 miles, with ~99.9% FSD usage across ~25,000 miles spanning two streaks.
- Multiple user videos this week showed FSD handling a nighttime deer avoidance at ~47 mph, a subtle police hand-signal, a self-initiated reverse to accommodate a turning truck, and a complex blocked-road standoff — cited as evidence of maturing edge-case handling.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla opened the world's first public Megacharger station in Bloomington, CA, with 6 stalls delivering up to 1.2MW peak, open to all Semi customers — a concrete infrastructure step for Semi commercialization.
- A Plug In America survey (~4,205 respondents) found 89.8% of EV drivers would choose an EV again and only 0.5% would return to gas; Tesla owner loyalty was 96.6%, roughly matching the overall EV population.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Starship Flight 13 is now targeted for Monday, July 20 at 6:45 PM ET after a scrub of the prior attempt; Sawyer Merritt pushed back on investors selling SPCX shares over the delay.
- A separate host framed the abort (which reportedly moved the stock down ~$5, ~2.5%) as a positive engineering outcome, noting onboard systems detected an anomaly and shut down all 33 engines within milliseconds.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Colossus 2 is estimated at ~440,000 working chips (~330,000 B300s, ~110,000 B200s) as of July 1, per Epic AI aerial-photo analysis — up sharply from ~190,000–250,000 a few months earlier.
- An analyst model (Brian, via brighterwithherbert) projects Google's AI leasing revenue ramping to ~$1 billion/month by October, with Anthropic and Reflection AI deals already generating ~$1.4 billion/month combined; Microsoft is floated as a possible future customer given its reported ~$650 billion in signed AI demand it can't yet fully serve.
- The same model projects Q4 2026 consolidated SpaceX revenue near $18.4 billion (base case) — comparable to Tesla's quarterly revenue — with 2026 full-year AI leasing revenue modeled at ~$47 billion, exiting near a $72–82 billion annualized run rate.
