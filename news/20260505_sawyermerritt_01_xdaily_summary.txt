Separately, the same day set a new record for new Unsupervised vehicle sightings, with 7 new Unsupervised Model Ys spotted across Dallas, Houston, and Austin combined. This suggests Tesla is systematically expanding both operating hours and fleet presence across its initial launch cities, incrementally de-risking the path to broader commercial deployment.

2. **FSD International Rollout Gaining Traction: Netherlands Milestone:** Tesla FSD (Supervised) launched in the Netherlands on April 11, 2026, and has already accumulated 6.2 million miles in just 23 days — an average of over 270,000 miles per day. This rate of adoption is notable as an early indicator of pent-up demand for FSD in European markets. Combined with the separate data point that FSD now accounts for approximately 0.25% of all miles driven by humans in North America annually (~0.05% globally and rising), these figures illustrate the compounding scale of Tesla's real-world FSD data collection and the growing footprint of its autonomous driving system across geographies.

3. **Tesla Europe EV Sales Surge in April 2026:** Tesla's April 2026 European sales data showed strong year-over-year gains in most tracked markets: France (+112%), Sweden (+111%), Denmark (+102%), Ireland (+100%), Romania (+53%), Belgium (+47%), Netherlands (+23%), and Switzerland (+15%). Notable laggards were Portugal (-43%) and Norway (-71%). Europe's broader BEV share of new car registrations rose to 20.5% in the first four months of 2026, providing a favorable macro tailwind. The sharp recovery in most markets likely reflects the refreshed Model Y and improving consumer sentiment following prior quarters of weakness.

4. **Tesla Semi Commercial Momentum: 40-Unit Order from Big F Transport:** Big F Transport has ordered 40 Tesla Semi trucks following a recent demo unit trial. Driver feedback cited smoother and quieter operation, while fleet operators highlighted emissions reduction benefits tied to Scope 3 targets and California's WAIRE point regulatory incentives. This order is a concrete data point in Tesla Semi's commercial adoption story and signals that operational demos are converting to fleet commitments, particularly where regulatory incentives align.

5. **SEC Settles with Elon Musk for $1.5M Over Twitter Disclosure:** The SEC has settled its long-running lawsuit against Elon Musk regarding the delayed disclosure of his 2022 Twitter stake acquisition. Musk will pay $1.5 million without admitting wrongdoing. His attorney Alex Spiro stated Musk has been "cleared of all issues related to the late filing." While the dollar amount is immaterial to Tesla's financials, the resolution removes a lingering regulatory overhang and eliminates distraction risk for Musk — a modest positive for Tesla investor sentiment.
