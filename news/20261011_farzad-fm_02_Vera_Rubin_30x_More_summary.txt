## Market & Competition
1. **Vera Rubin's agent-throughput claims:** Nvidia tested its Vera Rubin architecture on Agent X, an open benchmark from independent research firm SemiAnalysis designed to simulate real agentic AI workloads (long/growing context, tool calls, pauses, memory reuse, subagent work). On the DeepSeek V4 Pro model, Nvidia claims Vera Rubin delivered up to 30 times higher agent throughput per megawatt than GB300 Blackwell, meaning 30 times more agent work for the same fixed energy budget.
2. **Cost-per-token and revenue-per-gigawatt claims:** Nvidia claims the cost of processing a million tokens on Vera Rubin was up to 35 times lower than on prior hardware. Separately, during its most recent earnings report, Nvidia stated potential revenue per gigawatt of capacity rose from about $18 billion with Hopper, to $25 billion with Blackwell, to $40 billion with Vera Rubin.
