## Executive Summary
FSD V15 began rolling out on Nov 21, ahead of the roughly December wide release Musk had flagged, and Sawyer's hands-on posts dominated the day. The headline features are the new "Tesla Reality View" visualization, parking-spot selection, and a claimed 25x improvement in safety-critical case handling. Secondary items included Cybercab rider anecdotes in Dallas, Argentina's launch, and the Dec 3 shareholder meeting invites.

---

## Key Posts / Themes

1. **FSD V15 rollout begins:** Earlier on Nov 21, Sawyer relayed that Musk said V15 would be released widely in December ("It will be something special"), and that it looked tied to Tesla's annual Holiday Update. Tesla then teased it and announced it was starting to roll out. Sawyer reports Tesla's headline claim as "25x better handling of safety critical cases", alongside Tesla Reality View, a live visualization that lets you see and control what FSD is planning to do. He said it was downloading on his Model Y and posted the official release notes. A separate post framed the version lineage: V12 introduced end-to-end neural-network driving, V13 reduced interventions, V14 brought point-to-point travel, and V15 is to add pothole avoidance and AI-based mapping and navigation. The 25x figure is Tesla's own claim and has not been independently verified.

2. **Tesla Reality View and visualization upgrades:** This was the most viral content (the top post, "HOLY CRAP, the new FSD visualizations", drew 12.2K likes). Per Sawyer, the view continuously streams from an arbitrary virtual camera above the vehicle. Upcoming navigation turns appear directly in the driving visualization, with an arrow showing where the car will turn. Road markings, turn signals and roundabout handling are shown in real time, and turn signal sounds are quieter and fade after a few clicks. Users can swipe between Reality View and map view, and a quiet mode is available. Sawyer says the visualization also works with FSD off and alongside Split View. He compared it with FSD visualizations from six years ago.

3. **Navigation and parking improvements:** Per the release notes as relayed, navigation now uses an end-to-end learned model based on route preference data from the fleet. Parking location selection uses personalized parking history, and the map displays multiple parking options at the destination. Sawyer's tests show users can pick a spot from arrows that appear, and that the car points to its intended spot and sometimes chooses a different one on its own. He said it "nailed parking" in his testing. These are single-user impressions.

4. **Robotaxi and Cybercab:** Sawyer posted that after riding Cybercab in Dallas, some riders say they don't want to use any other robotaxi despite competitors' discounts. This is anecdotal, based on a media clip. Tesla also launched a dedicated robotaxi Instagram account.

5. **Vehicles, international and corporate:** Tesla added an 8-year / 80,000-mile (127,800 km) Emissions Performance Warranty for 2027 Model 3 and Model Y in the U.S. and Canada. A software update (2026.44.+) improves the turning radius on model year 2027 Cybertrucks. Tesla launched Model 3/Y ordering in Argentina, the fourth South American country, with Musk joining by video call. Invites for the 2026 annual shareholder meeting at Giga Texas on Dec 3 are going out.

## Signal Strength: **Bullish** — FSD V15 shipped earlier than the December timeline Musk had given, with a meaningful visualization and parking upgrade, though the safety claims are Tesla's own and early feedback is from enthusiast users.
