## Key Posts / Themes

1. **FSD V14.3.5 rollout with new Camera Preview feature:** Tesla began rolling out FSD V14.3.5 on 2026-07-13, per Sawyer Merritt, who noted he was downloading it on his own Model Y. The headline new feature allows drivers to open Camera Preview at any time — including while the car is actively driving (not just in Park) — and this includes the interior cabin camera, which Merritt suggests could help parents monitor children while driving.

2. **Cybercab/Robotaxi footprint expansion:** Merritt posted visual evidence of a Cybercab testing in New Hampshire (his home state), suggesting geographic testing expansion beyond current core markets. Separately, he reported "looks like" Tesla now has 100 Cybercabs testing in Houston — a notably higher figure than RobotaxiTracker.com's canonical Houston count of 13 (active fleet) as of 2026-07-13; given the large discrepancy (>10%), this is treated as Merritt's own independent observation/estimate rather than an update to the tracker's figure, and is caveated as such ("looks like").

3. **Jefferies raises Tesla price target on Q2 delivery beat:** Jefferies raised its Tesla price target to $400 (from $375), citing Tesla's Q2 vehicle delivery beat. For fiscal year 2026, Jefferies raised its EBIT estimate by 4% to $6.2 billion, attributing the increase partly to higher volume driven by higher-priced [vehicle mix, sentence cut off in source].

4. **Tesla Semi partnership expands:** Paper Transport announced a partnership with Tesla to evaluate the Tesla Semi Long Range in dedicated operations within the Chicago market, calling itself "bullish" on parallels to Tesla's broader technology approach.

5. **Competitive landscape — VW job cuts, Lexus EV design:** Volkswagen's CEO told staff in a memo the company may need to cut roughly 50,000 additional jobs to remain competitive, effectively confirming plans to cut 100,000 jobs total; VW stock is reportedly at a 16-year low. Separately, Lexus detailed design plans for its upcoming all-electric LFA supercar, which will use vibrations and sensory feedback to mimic a combustion engine's feel — illustrating how legacy/luxury automakers are approaching EV transitions differently than Tesla.

6. **California EV rebate policy:** Governor Gavin Newsom signed legislation creating a new state point-of-sale rebate program: $3,500 off new EVs with MSRP up to $50,000, and $1,750 off used EVs sold for up to $25,000. This is a potential demand tailwind for Tesla's lower-priced models in California.
