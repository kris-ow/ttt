Tesla's UK registrations fell more than 57% year-over-year in January 2026 to 647 vehicles, a steeper decline than the overall UK battery-electric vehicle market's 6.4% drop, and part of a broader collapse across Tesla's major European markets.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **UK sales collapse:** Tesla registered 647 battery-electric vehicles in the UK in January 2026, down more than 57% year-over-year, the steepest decline among major automakers in the UK BEV market that month.
2. **Chinese and other rivals gain ground:** BYD sold 1,326 battery-electric vehicles in the UK in January, up 21% year-over-year — nearly double Tesla's volume. Ford led the UK BEV market with 2,271 units, more than doubling its sales. Total UK car registrations across all fuel types fell 4.6% to 133,571 vehicles.
3. **Price cuts insufficient:** Tesla launched cheaper Model Y and Model 3 variants in the UK aimed at reviving sales, but the article states this has not stopped the decline.
4. **Pan-European decline:** The article reports Tesla registrations fell sharply across major European markets in January 2026, citing an 88% collapse in Norway (a market that had been a bright spot in 2025), a 67% drop in the Netherlands, and the UK's 57% decline. Only Italy (+75%), Spain (+70%), and Sweden (+26%) showed growth, each from a relatively small base; Italy's growth is attributed to new EV incentives introduced in 2026.

## Editorial Stance
Electrek's Fred Lambert argues the UK was supposed to be a Tesla stronghold given its size and British buyers' historical openness to American brands, calling the 647-unit result a "collapse" rather than a decline. He attributes the broader European downturn to reputational damage from Musk's political activities, says there's no sign of a bottom, and speculates that if Tesla's 2026 EU sales fall below 200,000 (versus roughly 240,000 in 2025), its European business may not be sustainable — though he notes full data from Germany, Switzerland, and other markets isn't yet available, so January's EU-wide picture could worsen further.
