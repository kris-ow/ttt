Elon reportedly called the hand and forearm "more difficult than the entire rest of the robot." The host notes this framing is inconsistent with a CEO trying to pump a stock — it reflects a long-horizon infrastructure build.

4. **Market Size Estimates for Humanoid Robots:** Morgan Stanley estimates the humanoid robot market reaches $5 trillion by 2050. Elon reportedly believes the market is larger than that. Some analysts model Optimus as representing 80% of Tesla's total value by 2050. The host uses these figures to illustrate that the Optimus bet is a 25-year thesis, not a near-term earnings story.

1. **SpaceX–xAI Merger Creates a $1.25 Trillion Combined Entity:** In February 2026, SpaceX absorbed xAI in what Reuters called the largest corporate merger ever recorded, valuing the combined entity at approximately $1.25 trillion. The merger gives SpaceX control of both launch capability and AI compute, combining energy, intelligence, and transport infrastructure in a single company.

2. **Starship as a Potential Replacement for Air Freight:** A thread from an account called "X-Freeze" on May 4th cited Elon's view that the cost to orbit could eventually drop below commercial air freight, and that Starship will be able to fly across the globe cheaper per ton than a Boeing 747. The host frames this as the moment SpaceX transitions from a rocket company to the transportation layer for the planet.

3. **One Million AI-Compute Satellites Filed with the FCC:** Days after the SpaceX–xAI merger, SpaceX filed an FCC application to launch up to 1 million satellites that function as data centers in orbit. The host characterizes this as a 2030–2040 product, not a near-term offering.
