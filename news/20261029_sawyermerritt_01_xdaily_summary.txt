## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Cybercab registrations climb to 573 per Texas DMV:** Tesla registered 29 more Cybercabs, bringing the cumulative Texas DMV registration total to 573, with 222 added in just the past week according to the poster. Note: vehicle registrations are a proxy for manufacturing/deployment pace, not confirmation of active robotaxi fleet size — RobotaxiTracker.com's active fleet figure (839, last updated 2026-08-07) and unsupervised fleet figure (48, last updated 2026-07-30) remain stale and are tracked separately from this registration count.

2. **Starship progress toward first catch attempt:** SpaceX fired a single Raptor engine to simulate a deorbit burn during testing, described by the poster as a step toward the first-ever Starship catch attempt. No date or further test detail was given.

3. **GM publicly criticizes vision-only autonomy:** General Motors stated it will not adopt a vision-only approach to self-driving, calling the strategy of dropping radar a "weird religious stance that ignores physics," arguing radar is cheap and necessary in certain conditions. This is a direct competitive jab at Tesla's camera-only FSD architecture and signals continued strategic divergence among automakers on sensor suites.

4. **Ford EV unit posts steep Q3 losses:** Ford's Model e EV division lost $1.1 billion in Q3 with an EBIT margin of -86.5%; EV revenue fell 33% YoY to $1.2 billion, which Ford attributed to discontinuing the F-150 Lightning and scaling back the Mustang Mach-E. This underscores the profitability gap between legacy automakers' EV efforts and Tesla's EV business.

5. **Elon Musk scheduled for Baron Investment Conference fireside chat:** Musk is set to appear as a guest speaker at the Annual Baron Investment Conference on November 6, in a virtual fireside chat with Ron Baron to discuss "what's next" — this is a confirmed upcoming public appearance within the flagged interview window.

6. **FCC vote on satellite spectrum (Starlink-relevant):** The FCC was set to vote October 29 on a proposal to open an additional 482MHz of spectrum for direct-to-device satellite services (expanding eligible bands from five to more), which the poster frames as easing expansion for services like Starlink Mobile. This is a regulatory tailwind to watch but was pending a vote, not yet decided, as of the post.

7. **Tesla expands into Vietnam; EV tax credit bill revived in House:** Tesla's recruiting team posted five job openings (sales, service, charging) for an official Vietnam market entry. Separately, over 160 House Democrats are backing legislation to reinstate and expand the federal EV credit (doubling new-EV credit to $15,000, used-EV credit to $8,000, and lowering income thresholds) — a bill proposal, not enacted law.
