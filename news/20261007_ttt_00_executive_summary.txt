## Autonomous Driving
- Germany's transport minister publicly backs EU-wide approval of Tesla's FSD (Supervised), saying an EU-level deal is preferable but national approval remains a fallback. A separate unconfirmed, "leaked" report claims Tesla agreed to rename FSD "Tesla Assisted Driving" in Germany and cap overspeed at 10% (vs. a reported 50% in the US) — presented as unverified leaked detail, not an on-record statement.
- Consumer anecdotes continue to surface: a reviewer called FSD in the new Model Y L "the most unbelievable technology" she's experienced in a car, per Sawyer Merritt.

## Robotaxi
- A Tesla Cybercab in Philadelphia lost its entire driver-side door panel in a low-speed crash; fault and whether FSD was engaged remain unknown. Tesla is not on PennDOT's certified list for automated-vehicle operation, suggesting the vehicle was either hand-driven or running as a Level 2 system with a human legally responsible.
- Drone footage shows a large, sight-blocked Houston lot packed with gold, steering-wheel-less Cybercabs alongside red Robotaxi-liveried Model Ys — one host's on-the-fly estimate ranged from ~100 to ~190-200 vehicles, far above RobotaxiTracker.com's stale Houston count of 25 active/6 unsupervised. Treat as unconfirmed staging inventory, not a verified fleet update, but it signals expansion activity beyond Austin.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla's "Other Models" category (now mainly Cybertruck plus small Semi/Cybercab volumes) fell to 8,295 deliveries in Q3 2026 — a five-year low, down 48% year-over-year — per Electrek. The outlet estimates actual Cybertruck deliveries at 7,000–8,000, a run-rate of roughly 28,000–32,000/year versus a planned 250,000/year line.
- Model Y was South Korea's #1 best-selling vehicle (EV or gas) for a third straight month in September, with 12,372 units registered, up 36% YoY.

## Energy
- Tesla launched Powershare Home Backup for new Model 3/Y paired with Powerwall 3 (no extra hardware needed for Powerwall 3 + Wall Connector owners), offering over two days of home backup; eligible for US/Puerto Rico orders placed after October 1.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas reiterated an "outperform" rating and $300 price target on SPCX (shares ~$174-$172), saying the stock trades ~40% below mega-cap AI peers on a growth-adjusted basis; in a room of 40 clients, not one owned the stock.
- SpaceX faces share unlocks of 328 million shares on October 9, another 328 million on October 24, and a larger ~1.3 billion-share unlock in late October/November, per one trader, who is short-term bearish into the unlocks but structurally bullish long-term and expects a possible dip into the $150s-$160s.
- SpaceX is reportedly seeking to raise $40 billion to buy more Nvidia chips (potentially $10B bank loans + $30B investment-grade debt), with the deal expected to close in 2027; the company reportedly holds over $100 billion gross cash (~$60 billion net of debt).

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Elon Musk announced Grokbot will shift to routing tasks to whichever third-party model performs best (Grok, GPT, Anthropic's Opus) rather than relying solely on its own model, with SpaceX's own data centers providing underlying compute — several hosts frame this as a strategy to undercut rivals' API margins, though pricing claims are speculative, not confirmed.
- A leaked compute roadmap cites SpaceX reaching 2.3 gigawatts by December 2026 and 10+ gigawatts by end of 2027, with roughly 60% reserved internally for xAI/SpaceX/Tesla (including FSD and Optimus training) and 40% for external customers including Anthropic and Google — attributed to third-party commentary, not confirmed by SpaceX.
- Grok bot reached 418,000 weekly users for the week ending September 14, up 24% week-over-week (Bloomberg/Ed Ludlow), versus ChatGPT's 1.2 billion weekly users and Meta's Muse reaching ~3 billion people — Grok's growth rate is strong but its absolute scale remains far smaller.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX released video of the first operational Starlink V3 satellites deployed directly from Starship, a milestone combining the Starship and Starlink theses.
- SpaceX subsidiary Coastal Connect Services filed for a 32+ mile, 16-inch natural gas pipeline in Brevard County, Florida to supply Starship propellant, mirroring an earlier pipeline near Boca Chica; each launch requires roughly 1,000 tons of propellant.
- Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas flagged the upcoming "Starship 15" launch this month as a potential major catalyst, calling a successful landing possibly the biggest catalyst since the IPO.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- SpaceX unveiled a "Starmind" AI satellite constellation concept (up to 1 million satellites, explicitly a "notional concept"), each roughly a 250-kilowatt compute rack linked in clusters by laser bandwidth, alongside plans for up to 100,000 Starlink satellites and a new Space Safety website for satellite conjunction screening.
- United plans Starlink on 880 aircraft by end of 2026 and fleet-wide by 2027; Delta has instead committed to Amazon's Kuiper, which isn't expected until 2028–2029.
