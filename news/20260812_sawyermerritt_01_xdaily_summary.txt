## Key Posts / Themes

1. **SpaceX's AI compute business scaling fast, could dwarf core business:** In a new presentation, Elon Musk said SpaceX currently has 1.4GW of nameplate power draw and is targeting 10GW by the end of 2027, which he said could generate $300–500 billion a year in revenue if achieved. He also stated SpaceX's AI revenue will "probably" exceed all other SpaceX revenue as soon as next month (September 2026) and will "significantly exceed" all other SpaceX revenue in Q4 2026, calling AI "an extremely important part of SpaceX's future" and stating SpaceX "must win on AI, because the future is overwhelmingly AI and robots." These are Musk's own forward-looking targets/estimates, not confirmed results.

2. **Starlink subscriber milestones:** SpaceX officially announced over 13 million Starlink consumer subscribers and 22 million monthly Starlink Mobile (direct-to-cell) users.

3. **Morgan Stanley on SpaceX's Cursor acquisition:** Analyst Adam Jonas published a note arguing Cursor's evolution from coding "harness" to "frontier" model could be a major factor supporting the firm's $600/share bull case (implying ~330% upside from current levels per the note), framing SpaceX's opportunity as a full end-to-end enterprise AI platform play.

4. **SpaceX all-hands and corporate visibility:** SpaceX posted a ~29-minute all-hands meeting Musk held with employees; separately, a lighter post noted Musk's growing suite of companies (X, Tesla, SpaceX, xAI) potentially warranting a dedicated "X Money" app — low signal but reflects platform consolidation chatter.

5. **FSD expansion and demand signals:** Tesla is beginning its first FSD (Supervised) ride-alongs in Switzerland, starting this Thursday, letting prospective customers experience FSD from the passenger seat. Separately, a widely-shared testimonial from a former Lincoln Navigator owner credited FSD as the primary reason for switching to a Model Y, and another viral post (3.9K likes) featured a customer praising Tesla's ~$300/month lease plus $100/month FSD subscription and self-driving capability. These reflect strong anecdotal demand signal tied to FSD as a purchase driver.

6. **Cybercab political visibility in Florida:** Florida Governor Ron DeSantis featured a Waymo and Tesla's Cybercab during a press conference, describing the Cybercab's steering-wheel-free, screen-only interior as potentially very beneficial. Tesla notably styled the Cybercab's front light bar with an American flag theme for the event.

7. **EV lifecycle emissions study:** MIT researchers found EVs emit roughly 40–60% fewer lifecycle emissions than gas vehicles across the U.S., holding even in regions with the dirtiest electricity mixes (natural gas/coal-heavy grids) — a data point favorable to EV market positioning broadly, not Tesla-specific.

8. **International EV expansion and commercial trials:** Tesla delivered its first customer vehicles in Estonia, marking its first deliveries since entering the Baltic market in April. Separately, freight carrier Bali Express Services completed a 3-week test of the Tesla Semi, giving a positive qualitative review citing technology, sustainability, and operational benefits (no hard performance metrics given).

9. **Optimus teaser (low signal):** A brief, meme-style post ("Hey Optimus, can I get a Tesla Giga Burger") references Optimus in a consumer-service context but contains no substantive product or capability details.
