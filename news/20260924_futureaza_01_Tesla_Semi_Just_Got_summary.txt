## Electric Vehicles
1. **Tesla Semi launch event and its purpose:** The hosts frame the event as Tesla's push to sell the Semi at scale to industry buyers (fleet operators, auto journalists like Top Gear's Jack Scarlett) rather than a factory opening, timed as Tesla ramps production toward a stated 50,000-unit target; the hosts want to hear the current weekly production rate and whether Tesla is on schedule.
2. **Major fleet order announced:** A shipper alliance referred to as "ZET Scale" (transcribed name, exact spelling uncertain) reportedly ordered 2,500 tractors that morning, which one host says is roughly twice the entire existing US Class 8 electric truck fleet, with Tesla cited as one of the main manufacturers in the order.
3. **Pricing and competitive context:** One host says Class 8 electric trucks from competitors like Freightliner have historically been priced around $450,000 versus a viable threshold near $300,000, and that rising diesel prices have made the economics more favorable for electric trucks; he notes this coincided with Freightliner reportedly winding down its Portland tractor line to relocate production to the southern US.
4. **Efficiency figures — possible improvement from 1.8 to ~1.6-1.65 kWh/mile:** The Semi's known efficiency figure of 1.8 kWh/mile is described as a strong industry benchmark from a couple years ago; one host relays an unconfirmed account from a carrier who tested the truck and reported an improved figure, "maybe 1.65," which the hosts frame as suggesting internal design changes significant enough to require re-testing (compared to a Model Y-style refresh).
5. **Megacharging infrastructure questions:** Hosts expect the event to address the number and planned locations of Tesla megachargers and what facility-level infrastructure customers will need to install.
6. **Low expectations for new feature reveals:** One host compares the event to the Cybertruck delivery event, expecting no major new information — more a celebration of the ~decade-long Semi development and a sales-oriented data dump (pricing, real-world efficiency, safety features) for industry buyers rather than a Roadster-style surprise reveal.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Speculation on FSD/autonomy timeline for Semi:** Hosts reference a comment from Elon Musk on a past earnings call about autonomy timing (roughly "a year or two, or sooner") and say the industry wants clarity on when FSD-based autonomy could apply to the truck; one host doubts a clear robotaxi/autonomous-truck date will be given, expecting instead announcements on discrete safety features (automatic emergency braking, blind-spot detection, automatic collision avoidance) rather than a full autonomy timeline, partly due to anticipated regulatory/permitting hurdles for heavy autonomous vehicles.
2. **Automatic collision avoidance rollout:** One host states that automatic collision avoidance has recently been rolled out as a software update to Tesla vehicles equipped with FSD (not all Teslas), and that this or similar safety features are expected to extend to Semi.
