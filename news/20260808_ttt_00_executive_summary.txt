## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- SpaceX's first quarterly earnings as a public company showed revenue up 92% YoY and AI revenue up 247% YoY, per jobhakdi. A roughly 900-million-share unlock hit two days later; the stock dipped then rallied sharply, and jobhakdi expects resistance as SPCX approaches its ~$135 IPO price, with early holders likely selling near their cost basis.
- Peter Diamandis published an essay arguing SpaceX could become the first $10 trillion company, framing it as five businesses in one (Starlink, AI compute, chips, launch, connective layer); Musk reposted it with two rocket emojis, per brighterwithherbert.
- Terafab, the Tesla/SpaceX chip megafab in Grimes County, Texas, is read by brighterwithherbert as a merger signal given its shared branding across both companies.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX ended Q2 with 1.4 gigawatts of compute readiness capacity, ahead of jobhakdi's own ~1–1.1GW model. Guidance points to 2GW by end of 2026 and over 5GW by end of 2027; Musk reportedly said capacity would be closer to 10GW, which jobhakdi calls likely exaggerated given permitting constraints.
- New cloud agreements added $1.6B of incremental Q2 infrastructure revenue, plus a further $6.7B cloud contract signed post-quarter, ramping in October. SpaceX affirmed Nvidia as its exclusive GPU provider.
- SpaceX and Nvidia are partnering on "Star Mind AI 1," a satellite compute payload targeting first launches in 2027, described as "data center class space compute," per brighterwithherbert. Early units were ~100kW and about a ton, scaling toward megawatt class using the radiation-hardened D3 chip.
- Whether SpaceX can hit gigawatt-scale orbital compute by 2028 is framed by jobhakdi as pivotal for the entire AI-infrastructure trade — a slip to 2030+ risks a terrestrial buildout "grinding halt" and short-term crashes across AI-related stocks.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Starlink reported 12 million subscribers, 10,200 satellites in orbit, and coverage in 167 countries, per SpaceX's Q2 report.
- Starlink Mobile has reportedly spent $17 billion on spectrum, targeting first service late next year, per brighterwithherbert. T-Mobile's CEO publicly downplayed the competitive threat as "exaggerated."

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus's inference chip, AI5, taped out this spring with silicon samples expected later this year and volume production in 2027; Musk reportedly called getting it right "existential." Successor chip AI6 targets a December tape-out with mass production in H2 2027, backed by a $16.5B Samsung supply deal, per brighterwithherbert.
- Musk gave a rough Terafab output split of ~25% for Optimus and ~75% for AI/spacecraft — softer toward Optimus than a prior March framing that had space closer to 80%.

## Financials
- Terafab phase one carries a $16.8 billion committed capital figure from Tesla and SpaceX jointly, with filings allowing up to $119 billion across all phases; Texas is providing a ~78% tax abatement plus a $30 million grant, and SpaceX has already wired the first $10 million of fixed annual payments ahead of schedule.
- A separate report claims Tesla's capex will exceed $25 billion against $103 billion trailing-12-month revenue; futureaza's hosts pushed back, citing ~$30 billion of borrowing capacity and ~$40 billion of cash on hand.
- Tesla filed for a new $10.1 billion vertically integrated solar cell plant ("Project Crystal Sun") in Fort Bend County, Texas, targeting 2026 construction start and 2028 completion, with 9,712 permanent jobs cited in the filing — a filing-stage announcement, not yet confirmed.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla's China wholesale sales grew for a ninth straight month, per futureaza, though one host attributes this mainly to exports rather than domestic demand improvement.
- The new long-wheelbase Model Y L is drawing favorable reviews (Edmunds, Motor Trend cited), with one host calling it the best Model Y variant to date.
- California's MyFirstEV $3,500 rebate funds allocated to Tesla dropped to 30% remaining, down from 50% the prior day, suggesting exhaustion within days.

## Market & Competition
- A cited report claims Cybertruck retail demand fell from 39,000 to 3,500 units, with SpaceX/xAI purchases making up nearly 20% of Q4 units; futureaza's hosts agree Cybertruck has underperformed original volume targets, attributing it mainly to pricing near $80,000–$120,000 versus a targeted ~$60,000.
- A viral battery-degradation comparison claiming rivals "beat" Tesla was disputed by futureaza's hosts as apples-to-oranges: Tesla's cited Model S figure (~12% range loss) was measured at 200,000 miles versus a competitor tested at only 62,000 miles.

## Autonomous Driving
