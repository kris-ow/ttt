This transcript is Elon Musk's internal SpaceX company talk (delivered roughly quarterly, tied to earnings calls), tracing SpaceX's history from Falcon 1 through Starlink, Starship, and Dragon before pivoting to a forward-looking thesis that SpaceX's future value will be dominated by AI and compute rather than launch or satellite internet alone. The core argument is that AI revenue is about to eclipse all of SpaceX's other business lines and that within roughly five years, AI could represent the vast majority of SpaceX's total value.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
1. **Falcon program milestones:** Musk highlighted SpaceX's upcoming 700th Falcon flight and noted Dragon has flown 78 crew members to date, framing both as evidence of the maturity of reusable rocket technology that early industry experts doubted was possible or commercially viable.

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **Massive subscriber and satellite growth, more scale expected:** Starlink now operates in 167 countries with roughly 22 million mobile subscribers and 13 million high-bandwidth subscribers, and has nearly 11,000 satellites in orbit — more than twice the combined total of all other operators. Musk said he expects these subscriber numbers to grow by more than 10x over time, and that the Starlink V3 generation and beyond could scale the constellation toward 100,000 satellites.
2. **Musk's claim on future internet share:** Musk said he believes Starlink could eventually carry over 90% of all internet traffic, and cited a claim he attributed to Cloudflare that AI traffic could be a thousand times larger than human internet traffic within five years.
3. **Star Shield as a national security asset:** Musk described Star Shield as having become "a vital part of America's national security," though he said little detail could be shared given its classified nature.

## SpaceX — Starship
1. **Orbital tonnage ambitions:** Musk said Starship is intended to take SpaceX from roughly 2,500 tons delivered to orbit per year today to over a million tons per year, and said it's "highly likely" the company reaches a million tons annually and possibly approaches 10 million tons per year — tonnage he says is necessary to build sustainable settlements on the Moon and Mars.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **AI revenue set to overtake all other SpaceX business lines:** Musk stated SpaceX's AI revenue will exceed all of its other combined revenue (Starlink, launch, Starshield, etc.) as early as next month and will significantly exceed it in the fourth quarter of this year.
2. **10 gigawatt compute target and revenue projection:** SpaceX is aiming to bring 10 gigawatts of AI compute online by the end of next year. Musk estimated the value per watt at roughly $30–$50, implying — by his own framing — $300–$500 billion a year in revenue from that capacity if achieved.
3. **Grok training on SpaceX's proprietary data:** Musk said Grok will be trained on "the sum total of all SpaceX information," describing SpaceX employees as effectively becoming "parents" of the AI, inheriting their thoughts, ideas, and beliefs; he referenced Grok 4.5 as already available, with version 4.6 expected in about a week and 4.7 described as "really pretty special."
4. **AI could represent 99% of SpaceX's value within five years:** Musk projected that within four to five years, AI will constitute 99% of SpaceX's overall value, calling the resulting valuation "some astronomical number," and floated a long-term vision of AI infrastructure (a "Terafab"-scale facility and eventually AI computing built on the Moon) tied to harnessing solar energy at civilizational scale.
