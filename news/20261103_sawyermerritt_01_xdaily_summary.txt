## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Fort Worth Robotaxi launch expands geofence footprint:** Tesla officially launched unsupervised Robotaxi rides in Fort Worth, Texas on November 2 at 6 AM CT, covering a 143 square mile geofence — the first new city expansion in about three months. With this addition, Tesla's unsupervised Robotaxi service now operates in 7 cities: Austin (272 sq mi), Houston (254 sq mi), Fort Worth (143 sq mi), Dallas (81 sq mi), Miami (31 sq mi), Orlando (26 sq mi), and Tampa (24 sq mi). Combined, these geofences cover roughly 1% of the entire U.S. population, per the poster. A video showed one of the first rides in an unsupervised Model Y, though the poster separately flagged UI issues in the car's on-screen graphics (the car icon colliding with road edges, parked cars rendering on top of trees), calling it "definitely some UI stuff to work on before launch."

2. **FSD mileage milestone and growth rate:** Tesla's global fleet has crossed 17 billion cumulative miles driven on FSD (Supervised). Tesla recently updated its live mile counter to reflect usage growth; the fleet now drives 1,000 miles every 1.8 seconds on FSD, annualizing to about 18.2 billion miles per year — up 35% since July 2026.

3. **Strong European EV registrations:** Tesla posted its best October on record in Norway, becoming the country's top-selling car brand with 9.8% market share as registrations rose 131% year-over-year. Registrations also rose 248% in Sweden and 34% in Denmark versus October 2025.

4. **Tesla marketing/promotional pushes:** Tesla is taking the Cybercab on a European tour across 11 cities in 8 countries between November 2026 and January 2027, letting the public view the vehicle in person. Separately, the Tesla Semi will make its first public appearance in France at Connect Trucks 2026 in Deauville (Nov 3–4). Tesla also released new Cybercab-branded merchandise (backpack, tees, cup) on its online shop.

5. **SpaceX Starfall microgravity service and shop merch:** SpaceX introduced a new website for "Starfall," a service offering affordable, routine access to microgravity conditions in low-Earth orbit for manufacturing, research, and testing, with a stated payload capacity of 1,000 kg (2,205 lb). SpaceX also added new Starshield/Starfall-branded apparel to its online shop.

6. **Competitive and regulatory landscape in autonomous driving:** BMW released a video demonstrating a new navigation-guided "City Assistant" feature capable of largely self-driving between addresses, including highway and city-street navigation, turns at intersections, and roundabout handling. Separately, Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism is reportedly preparing to certify driverless vehicles without steering wheels or pedals under a single uniform safety standard, with new rules expected to take effect in December 2026 (reported, not yet finalized).

7. **Grok Bot / AI compute commentary:** The poster speculated about SpaceX introducing higher-priced subscription tiers for heavy Grok Bot users (suggesting tiers up to $500–$1,000/month) and argued AI compute cost-per-watt will likely stay elevated longer than expected, citing that under 5% (some estimates under 2%) of U.S. consumers currently pay for an AI subscription product. Separately, SpaceX reportedly reset Grok Bot usage limits back to prior levels after a temporary change the day before. Context: Anthropic's amended IPO S-1 filing disclosed aggregate compute/infrastructure purchase and lease commitments of about $895 billion, including $447.5 billion tied to one category of commitments (filing not fully detailed in the post) — included here as broader AI infrastructure market context, not a Tesla/SpaceX figure.

8. **Political commentary on Musk/SpaceX:** President Trump said in a new interview (via New York Post's "Pod Force One" podcast) that he would "protect" Elon Musk and SpaceX if Democrats attempted to nationalize the company or arrest Musk. This is tangential political commentary with limited direct financial implication but is included for completeness given its reach.
