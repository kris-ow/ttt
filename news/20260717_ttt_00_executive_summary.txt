## Robotaxi
- Texas registered Robotaxi fleet reached 175 vehicles with 73 added in a single week — the largest weekly jump yet, all Model Y — though actual active-operation count is unknown, per Electrified. Waymo also expanded its Austin geofence, though Tesla's footprint remains larger.
- Tesla employees are now riding in Cybercab, described by panelists on brighterwithherbert as treating the vehicle like near-production service undergoing final testing ahead of customer rides.
- Robotaxi testing began in New Orleans and Cybercabs were spotted in New York, with 58 additional Model Y VINs added to the fleet, per Tesla Beat Podcast; Bay Area ride availability remains inconsistent.
- A shareholder question representing roughly 900,000 shares accused Tesla of missing robotaxi guidance three quarters running; panelists on brighterwithherbert expect the question's tone to get it dismissed on Wednesday's earnings call.
- One panelist projects, based on a recent unsupervised fleet increase in Texas, that Tesla's fleet could reach on the order of 3.7 million vehicles roughly seven years after its first supervised ride — contrasted with Waymo's reported 3,700 cars after six years (his own projection, not confirmed guidance).

## Autonomous Driving
- FSD V14 Light rolled out to Hardware 3 early-access owners in Korea, per cernbasher, backporting the modern stack onto roughly seven-year-old hardware; a Hardware 3 vehicle also completed a long-distance US trip (Chattanooga to Key West) on FSD.
- Germany's FSD type-approval decision will wait for an EU-wide TCMV vote, with the earliest possible vote in October — later than earlier hoped, per Electrified; KBA is expected to receive a test vehicle this month.
- NTSB report on a Texas home-crash found the driver manually pressed the accelerator to 100%, exceeding 70 mph, overriding FSD — indicating the crash was not attributable to FSD behavior, per Electrified and Tesla Beat Podcast.
- FSD 14.3.5 released with a live camera preview usable while parked; hosts reported mixed results, including a navigation loop failing to locate a charger.
- Tesla FSD Supervised has surpassed roughly 50 million kilometers across the Netherlands, Estonia, Belgium, Lithuania, and Denmark, just three months after first regional approval.

## Humanoid Bots
- Fremont's former Model S/X (SNX) production line, which built roughly 750,000 vehicles, was dismantled in about 46 days and foundations are being re-poured for new equipment, signaling repurposing toward Optimus production, per Tesla Beat Podcast; Musk was photographed with the Optimus team on-site.
- One panelist predicts a "first-off" Optimus Gen 3 production unit around late summer to late September, combining recent executive comments with Musk's remarks on possible July production activity — his own estimate, not confirmed guidance.
- Optimus Gen 3's hand has 22 degrees of freedom versus 11 on Gen 2, per farzad-fm; deployment is expected to start in Tesla's own factories and warehouses before homes.
- Optimus, not manufacturing, is framed by multiple sources as the real bottleneck — generalization/AI learning capability rather than production-line scaling.

## Financials
- Panelists expect Tesla Q2 revenue of $27–28 billion and adjusted EPS near or above $0.60 on Wednesday's call, with possible negative free cash flow this quarter given capex across new factories, Optimus, and Terafab.
- One panelist argues Tesla's Cybercab fleet will be capitalized as fixed assets at roughly $20,000/unit — implying $20 billion in capex per million units annually, later offering a tax shield via bonus depreciation.
- SpaceX's first public-company earnings call is expected one to two weeks after Tesla's, per brighterwithherbert, with August 9 as the outside 10-Q deadline.
- Tesla deployed a new six-stall Mega Charger station in Bloomington, CA, part of a Semi-route buildout with roughly two dozen more Texas sites "coming soon."

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla launches in Latvia (first store, Riga pop-up, Aug 21) and Uruguay within a day of each other, per Electrek — Model 3 already leads Latvia's EV registrations despite no prior Tesla presence; Uruguay EV share rose from ~20% to 29% in 2026.
- Tesla partnered with 7-Eleven Japan for its 150th Japanese Supercharger site; Japan H1 2026 registrations hit ~12,200, up ~166% YoY, already exceeding full-year 2025.
- Tesla Q2 2026 deliveries exceeded 480,000 units, its best Q2 ever, driven by resurgent Europe and rest-of-world demand offsetting soft China and US sales, per Larry Goldberg.
- California's new EV rebate excludes vehicles above a price cap except for California-headquartered EV-only automakers (Rivian, Lucid); one host cited a claim Tesla later opted into the program regardless.

## Energy
- Elon Musk personally acquired APR Energy, a mobile gas-turbine power company, for over $1 billion via SEC filing rather than announcement — widely linked by multiple sources to power needs for xAI's Colossus buildout amid grid-connection delays.
- Tesla's lithium refinery in Texas is now complete and fully ramped, shipping material to Giga Texas for 4680 cell production, per a third-party attendee at Tesla's lithium day.
- Goldberg projects Tesla energy deployment reaching a 20 GWh quarter by year-end or Q1 next year, doubling to ~25 GWh/quarter by end of next year — his stated "hope," not guidance.

## Market & Competition
- Panelists argue no competitor currently matches Tesla's point-to-point autonomy, framing a widening "physical AI" moat across product, manufacturing, and generalized AI.
- A Neutrality Org study reportedly placed Grok as the only near-neutral model among 18 tested; Musk reposted it calling Grok the most neutral and truth-seeking AI.
- Chamath Palihapitiya reportedly compared AI cost-per-million-tokens: Anthropic ~$56, OpenAI ~$26, Grok ~$1.50-ish (transcript inconsistent, said "a buck" elsewhere), Chinese models ~50 cents — secondhand, speculative pricing commentary per jobhakdi.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Starship Flight 13 scrubbed after two Raptor engines failed to ignite during countdown, triggering an automatic abort; Musk said the two engines will be swapped, with a new target of July 20, 6:45pm ET.
- The flight, once relaunched, will attempt the first deployment of 20 V3 Starlink satellites on a suborbital trajectory (expected to burn up ~20 minutes after deployment), with six carrying cameras to scan the heat shield during re-entry.
- One host frames the abort as a positive safety signal — SpaceX catching a problem before catastrophe — contrasting it with a Blue Origin failure that destroyed payload and pad.
- SpaceX passed its 600th Falcon 9 booster reflight, contrasted with China's recent single reusable-booster landing.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- New V3 satellite specs: ~1 Tbps downlink (~10x V2) and 160 Gbps uplink (~22x V2), with 2,048 downlink/uplink beams and phased-array chips supporting a 64x throughput increase per modem chip, per Sawyer Merritt.
- A host estimates current cumulative Starlink capacity (~450 Tbps) could double in as few as ~7.5 Starship launches once V3 satellites are operational later this year.
- New Starlink V5 terminal launched — lighter (2.4 lb vs 6.4 lb), higher wind rating (165 mph vs 110 mph), but slightly lower peak speed (~375+ Mbps vs 475 Mbps) than the V4 dish; framed as a margin play, produced at a highly automated Texas factory at roughly 170,000 terminals/week.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Musk raised AI1 orbital data-center satellite specs: peak power up 67% to 250kW, average power up 33% to 160kW, enough to handle an Nvidia Rubin NVL72 rack (72 GPUs); first AI1 flights planned next year to late 2027 depending on source.
- Colossus in Memphis is running roughly 550,000 GPUs (230,000 in phase one) en route to ~1 million GPUs and ~2 GW of power, per farzad-fm.
- AI compute demand described as growing ~350% year-over-year historically; one speaker argues no single data-center buildout can close the gap, calling for "many Terafabs" plus a project referred to as Starmind.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- SpaceX shares fell to $123.99 (-5.43%), below its $135 IPO price, amid a broader selloff in AI-adjacent and semiconductor names; one host expects a possible drop toward $100 before recovery (his own speculation).
- A bear case cites SpaceX's ~$1.7T market cap against ~$10–11B annual profit run-rate (~170x P/E), plus a looming share-unlock event by November raising tradeable float roughly 10x, as sources of downward pressure.
- A prominent investor reportedly floated a SpaceX-Tesla merger premium as high as 50% for Tesla shareholders in a CNBC appearance; panelists were split on whether this was a deliberate trial balloon or simple provocation.
- A shareholder question asked Musk to commit to hitting at least half his 2025 compensation milestones before considering any merger offer; panelists expect Tesla to decline comment, noting existing proxy language already addresses change-of-control scenarios.
