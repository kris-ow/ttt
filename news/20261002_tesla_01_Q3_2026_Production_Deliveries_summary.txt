Tesla released its official Q3 2026 production, deliveries and deployments figures pre-market on October 2: 464,391 vehicles produced, 486,532 vehicles delivered, and 13.7 GWh of energy storage deployed. Deliveries beat Tesla's company-compiled consensus of 461,974 (24 analysts) by 24,558 units (+5.3%) and came in above every individual estimate in it, but were down 2.1% from the record 497,099 of Q3 2025, a quarter boosted by US buyers pulling purchases forward ahead of the federal EV tax credit's September 30, 2025 expiry.

## Electric Vehicles

1. **Official Q3 2026 figures:** Model 3/Y production 457,387 and deliveries 478,237; Other Models production 7,004 and deliveries 8,295; totals of **464,391 produced** and **486,532 delivered**. 1% of total deliveries were subject to operating lease accounting (Model 3/Y 1%, Other Models 4%).

2. **Deliveries outpaced production by 22,141 units**, the second consecutive quarter of inventory drawdown after Q2's 28,368. Quarter over quarter, deliveries rose 1.3% from Q2's 480,126, while most pre-release estimates had implied a sequential decline.

3. **Year to date: 1,324,681 deliveries** through three quarters, up 8.8% from 1,217,902 at the same point in 2025. The Q3 2025 figure of 497,099 remains the all-time quarterly record.

## Energy

1. **13.7 GWh of energy storage deployed**, up from 13.5 GWh in Q2 2026 and 12.5 GWh in Q3 2025, below the 15.9 GWh analysts expected (per Electrek) and the Q4 2025 record of 14.2 GWh.

## Financials

1. **Forecast scoreboard:** Tesla's company-compiled consensus ranged from 421,758 (Cantor Fitzgerald) to 482,000 (JPMorgan); the result cleared the top of the range. Other consensus figures cited in late September: Visible Alpha 449,000-454,000, Bloomberg 466,000. Prediction markets had priced the low-to-mid 470,000s. Estimates had been cut through late September on weak August sales in the US and China (Goldman Sachs -55,000 to 435,000, JPMorgan -34,000 to 482,000).

2. **Q3 earnings scheduled for Wednesday, October 21, 2026**, after market close, with the Q3 update and management Q&A webcast at 4:30 p.m. Central Time. Net income and cash flow results will be reported then.
