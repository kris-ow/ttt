Tesla added a fifth US Model Y variant — a Standard All-Wheel Drive model priced at $41,990, $2,000 above the base rear-wheel-drive version.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **New Model Y AWD specs and pricing:** The new Standard AWD trim costs $41,990 before incentives, slots between the Standard RWD ($39,990) and Premium RWD ($44,990), and offers dual-motor AWD with a 294-mile EPA range and 0-60 mph in 4.6 seconds — compared to the base RWD's 321-mile range and 6.8-second 0-60 time.

2. **Lineup naming change:** Tesla now offers five Model Y configurations in the US and appears to have dropped the "Standard" label introduced last October for the decontented version, now reserving "Premium" for what was formerly the regular Model Y.

## Market & Competition
1. **Competitive pressure on Model Y:** The author notes Tesla faces increasing competition at the top end of the Model Y lineup from the BMW iX3, Volvo EX60, Mercedes-Benz GLC Electric, and the upcoming Rivian R2.

## Editorial Stance
The author (Fred Lambert) views the $2,000 AWD premium as reasonable and in line with industry norms, calling the new trim compelling value and one of the more affordable AWD electric SUV options available. He argues the timing makes sense given competitive pressure, and that Tesla is better positioned competing down-market with Model Y than against these new rivals at the higher end.
