This Tesla Beat Podcast episode (hosts John, Joe, Matthew/MDR, and Brian) covers a scrubbed Starship test flight, the teardown of Tesla's Fremont Model S/X production line to make way for next-gen robotics, Robotaxi expansion news, a new FSD release, AI5 chip tape-out progress, a California EV rebate that excludes Tesla, the NTSB report on a fatal FSD-related crash, and rapid-fire updates on Tesla Semi, Boring Company, and competitor EVs. The overall tone is bullish on Tesla's multi-front execution (Robotaxi, Optimus, Semi, energy) while criticizing Tesla's passive media strategy on safety stories.

## SpaceX — Starship
1. **Flight 13 scrubbed:** SpaceX's Starship flight 13 was scrubbed after two Raptor engines on the booster failed to ignite during the countdown; the mission planned to deploy V3 Starlink satellites on a suborbital (not full orbital) trajectory for testing, marking only the second live flight of the new Raptor 3 engines. The team was swapping out the two problem engines, with hosts expecting another launch attempt within about a week.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Orbital data center skepticism addressed:** One host argued SpaceX's orbital data center concept is essentially an oversized Starlink satellite reusing existing solar and cooling tech with added compute, and that even if the space application never proves financially viable, the underlying modular compute research would still be usable for terrestrial data centers.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **SpaceX trading below IPO price:** A host noted that amid a broader market selloff, SpaceX's private share price has fallen below its IPO price, well off an unspecified all-time high, which he characterized as a potential buying opportunity.

## Robotaxi
1. **Robotaxi expansion continues cautiously:** Tesla's robotaxi service began testing in New Orleans and Cybercabs were spotted in New York, with 58 additional Model Y VINs added to the robotaxi fleet; hosts noted ride availability in the Bay Area remains inconsistent, and one host questioned why Tesla hasn't scaled up remote supervisors (reportedly staffed partly in the Philippines for cost reasons) faster given ongoing Cybercab production.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **NTSB report clears FSD in fatal crash narrative:** The NTSB's report on a fatal Texas crash (an Uber Eats driver crashing into a house) reportedly found the car was in FSD but the driver had the accelerator fully depressed, overriding the speed limit and reaching roughly 70+ mph; the host argued major outlets mischaracterized the crash as "Tesla in autonomous mode" killed someone, and urged Tesla to adopt a faster, more proactive media response strategy on safety incidents rather than staying silent.
2. **FSD v14.3.5 released:** Tesla rolled out FSD 14.3.5, adding a live camera preview feature usable even while parked; hosts reported mixed real-world experience, including a car that repeatedly failed to locate a charger via an infinite navigation loop and continued minor issues like parking in handicap spots.
3. **Unsupervised FSD confirmed running on Hardware 4:** A host stated unsupervised driving is running on Hardware 4 using the 14.3-series software, meaning the technical capability exists and remaining barriers are regulatory approval and over-the-air release rather than hardware readiness.

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Fremont SNX line torn down for next-gen robotics:** Tesla dismantled its Fremont SNX production line (which historically built roughly 750,000 vehicles) in about 46 days, already re-pouring foundations for new equipment; Elon Musk was noted taking a photo with the Optimus team and Fremont staff, signaling the line's repurposing toward humanoid robot production.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **EV-to-ICE switchback rate near zero:** Citing data, hosts noted only about 0.5% of EV owners have gone back to gas vehicles, which they called shockingly low even accounting for charging-access pain points; anecdotes included a leased Nissan Leaf returned after two years with only 128 miles driven by a multi-Tesla owner.
2. **New California EV rebate excludes Tesla-built cars under original design, later clarified as opted-in:** California's new first-time EV buyer rebate ($3,500 for new, $1,750 for used, capped at a $50,000 vehicle price) exempts the price cap only for EV-only automakers headquartered in California — Rivian and Lucid — while Tesla, despite building over half a million cars a year in Fremont, was initially seen as excluded; one host cited an article claiming Tesla did opt into the program after all, and both hosts argued Rivian/Lucid's low sales volumes mean Tesla would still capture the vast majority of rebate dollars regardless.
3. **BYD Tang motor detachment incident:** A video circulated showing a motor apparently falling out of a BYD Tang while still partially driving; hosts speculated it could be a manufacturing defect given the vehicle's newness, drawing a comparison to a past Fisker Karma drivetrain failure caused by improper servicing.

## Market & Competition
1. **Boring Company production tour released:** A creator (Joe, per hosts) recorded what they say is the first filmed tour of Boring Company's tunneling production line, showing the dirt-removal "cassette" system and a centralized control room that remotely operates drills, similar to SpaceX's mission control model; hosts said other creators have since been allowed to record tours as well.
2. **Tesla Semi and Megachargers scaling up:** A logistics/freight company partnered with Tesla to deploy Tesla Semi trucks in real-world freight operations; hosts noted supercharger permit filings for private fleets in the Dallas area and a second Megacharger site opening in Bloomington, California, alongside broader observations that Tesla's Semi line, Cybercab line, Optimus line, and energy deployments are all ramping simultaneously.
3. **Grok and AI competition:** Hosts discussed a newer Grok model reportedly outperforming prior versions in testing, and one host described using Grok's connectors (e.g., Google Drive integration) to automate expense-report tasks for a small business.
