The video walks through Elon Musk's latest comments on FSD V15, which he says goes to wide release in December as part of the holiday update, and ties them to earlier statements from Ashok Elluswamy and Musk. The host's thesis is that V15 is a full architecture overhaul with a much larger model, AI-based mapping, voice integration and a new interface, and that it should be the release that unlocks unsupervised FSD on customer cars at real scale.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Release timing and rollout expectations:** Musk said FSD V15 goes to wide release next month (December) and confirmed it will be part of the holiday update. He called it "something special" and said V15 is the first version that deserves the name "Super", implying V14 did not. The host notes the early access group could get it very soon, since it sometimes has a build for weeks before wide release. He expects one to three or four releases before the team sends it wide and warns viewers to expect early regressions given how different the architecture is.
2. **Training philosophy:** Musk said supervised fine-tuning (SFT) is "more like getting a haircut with a chainsaw than it is fine-tuning" and should be built into primary training, and that a system prompt is silly because default behavior should be sensible. He said both are already true for Tesla AI. The host reads this as Tesla relying on end-to-end learning of human-like judgment (e.g., handling flooded roads) rather than handwritten rules or after-the-fact patching.
3. **New interface:** Musk said the new interface is amazing and will "rerender reality in a mindbending way." The host speculates Tesla may use Epic Games' Unreal Engine for richer FSD visualizations, citing a report from August last year and a Tesla account post asking "What if we are just driving in a video game?" This is the host's inference, not confirmed by Tesla.
4. **Behavior fixes:** Musk said V15 should fix last-second lane changes to turn or merge. Responding to user requests, he also indicated the new interface and driving improvements are coming, and the host expects V15 to bring pothole avoidance and HOV lane usage. Musk also replied that some ideas in a fan-made satirical set of V15 release notes are worth implementing.
5. **AI-based mapping and navigation:** In October, Elluswamy said Tesla is building a new architecture with AI-based mapping and navigation, that trip starts and ends would use it soon as part of V15, and that over several months it would take over navigation, parking and destination handling. He said Tesla should not depend on external maps. On the Q3 call he said it would be integrated with voice, letting users ask for routes, and would learn from personal and fleet preferences.
6. **Model size and architecture:** In April, Musk said V15 would be a complete software architecture overhaul running on AI4, and that the large model had not yet caught up to the small model's rapid advances, with V15 being the large model. The host says the expectation is that V14 has around 1 billion parameters, so V15 could have around 10 billion. He expects better handling of rare edge cases, scene prediction and parking lots. In August, Musk said V14 would be better than human, maybe 2 to 3x safer, and V15 has a shot at 10x.
7. **Robotaxi fleet already running early V15:** In July, Elluswamy said the robotaxi fleet was running early V15 builds, with roughly 40% of about seven parallel improvement tracks merged. The host argues wide release to customer cars would then bring far more data back into training, a flywheel.

## Robotaxi
1. **Unsupervised for customer cars:** The host says V15 should be the release that unlocks unsupervised FSD on personally owned cars, likely only within robotaxi geofences, and enables adding personal vehicles to the robotaxi network. Musk earlier suggested this might be possible as soon as December with qualifiers, while the recent earnings call pointed to Q2 2027. Hardware 3 will not be capable of unsupervised.
2. **Grok voice control:** On June 17, Musk said the ability to talk to Grok in the car, as with an Uber driver, and give audible instructions to FSD should arrive in about three months. The host notes it is late and expects it with a V15 release.
