This is a watch-along livestream from a panel of Tesla-focused YouTubers reacting in real time to Tesla's Roadster event at SpaceX's McGregor, Texas test facility, hosted by Franz von Holzhausen and Lars Moravy with Elon Musk appearing on stage. The event revealed a next-generation Roadster using SpaceX-derived cold gas thruster and fan-suction technology, demonstrated dramatic stunts (sub-one-second 0-60, upside-down hovering, a barrel roll off a ramp), and opened reservations with tiered packages; the panel spent most of the stream speculating on the mechanics before and reacting with surprise afterward.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Roadster unveiled with dramatic live stunt demo:** At a SpaceX McGregor event hosted by Franz von Holzhausen and Lars Moravy, Elon Musk introduced the new Roadster, which performed a sub-one-second 0-60 run, held itself upside-down via fan suction, and executed a barrel roll off a ramp at the end of a runway — stunts the panel described as far exceeding their expectations.
2. **Three trims revealed with different thruster packages:** Per the order page reviewed live, "Performance" ($5,000 deposit, $45,000 due within 10 days) includes the adaptive wing and multi-mode suction fans but not thrusters; "Thrust" (reported deposit around $250,000, implying a higher total price) adds four rear-facing cold gas thrusters; "Rocket" is invite-only, single-seat, and includes downward-facing thrusters as well, enabling the full aerobatic package (barrel rolls, upside-down hover).
3. **0-60 time and production timeline stated on stage:** Musk said the car broke the one-second 0-60 barrier; a displayed test time showed roughly 0.8 seconds. Franz/Moravy stated production start is targeted for 2028, with Musk calling it the last manually-driven car Tesla plans to make (though technically autonomous-assisted for the demo's safety).
4. **Technical specs disclosed on stage:** Moravy stated the car uses two carbon-overwrap pressure vessels totaling 600 liters holding about 250 kg of air, feeding 10 nozzles; thrust output was stated as over 80 kN, with thrust exiting faster than the speed of sound (visible shock diamonds). An on-screen graphic during the upside-down segment showed a downforce figure that two panelists read differently (roughly 4,700 kg per one reading, versus roughly 6,700 lbs per another) — the discrepancy was not resolved live. Top speed was stated as over 230 mph, with Musk suggesting a higher future target around half the speed of sound.
5. **Steer-by-wire controls and fixed seating raised:** The car uses a steer-by-wire yoke, brake, and throttle; the website reportedly states the seat is in a fixed position (panel speculated this might resemble a build-to-owner approach, reducing weight, with adjustable pedals/wheel instead).
6. **Panel reactions on design:** Panelists were split — several liked the more angular, "aerospace"/Lamborghini-esque look and compared it to the James Bond Lotus Esprit submarine car (cited by Franz as a design inspiration), while some missed the softer look of the original 2017 Roadster concept; reverse pop-up headlights (recessed, popping down rather than up) were highlighted as a notable design detail.
7. **No range or FSD details disclosed:** The panel noted the event included no mention of range/battery size or Full Self-Driving capability for the new Roadster.

## SpaceX — Starship
1. **Raptor static fire used as pre-show:** Before the main event, SpaceX conducted what was described as a live Raptor V3 engine test/static fire at McGregor as a "warm-up," per a post from Sawyer Merritt cited on stream.

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Optimus spotted in Tesla manufacturing content, not at the event:** The panel discussed a Tesla manufacturing social post (from the prior day or two) showing a bring-your-kid-to-work style factory day, which included a brief shot of an Optimus hand design (previously shown at a past shareholder meeting) now apparently mounted on a robot — speculated by the panel to possibly be a decoy/red herring for the real production design. Optimus did not perform at the Roadster event itself, which the panel took as evidence the event stunts were real and not staged/CGI.
