## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Ron Baron's Mega-Bullish Tesla and SpaceX Outlook:** In a new interview, legendary investor Ron Baron made several high-profile statements. On Tesla ($TSLA), he projected the stock reaching **$2,000–$2,500 over the next 10 years**, which at $2,500 would imply a market cap of roughly **$8.3 trillion** — approximately 5.7x today's value. On SpaceX, Baron revealed his firm's **$1.7 billion invested** is now worth **over $15 billion**, and that roughly **one-third of Baron Capital's lifetime profits (~$60 billion total)** have come from investing in Elon Musk companies. He plans to purchase an additional **$1 billion of SpaceX stock at IPO**, though he acknowledged fill uncertainty. He called SpaceX potentially worth **$10–$30 trillion over 10–15 years**. On a potential SpaceX/Tesla merger, Baron was agnostic, saying whatever Elon decides "it's very unlikely it's not a good idea." These comments reflect the thesis that Musk's broader ecosystem — including Tesla — remains a generational investment opportunity.

2. **Belgium Moving Toward FSD (Supervised) Testing Approval:** Flemish mobility minister Annick De Ridder stated that Tesla FSD (Supervised) testing on public roads in Flanders, Belgium, could begin **"within days"** as regulators review Tesla's documentation. This marks meaningful regulatory progress for FSD in Europe, a market where autonomous driving approvals have historically lagged the U.S. The minister noted the vehicle will remain under supervision during testing. Florida Governor Ron DeSantis also commented positively on Tesla FSD, though the specific content of his quote was not captured in the post text.

3. **Tesla Invests ~$250 Million to Expand 4680 Production at Giga Berlin:** Tesla announced it is investing **almost $250 million** to expand 4680 battery cell production at its Giga Berlin factory. The investment more than **doubles the site's annual production capacity target to 18 GWh**. This is a significant capex commitment underscoring Tesla's confidence in the 4680 cell platform and European manufacturing scale. Higher 4680 output supports both cost reduction efforts and supply for future vehicle and energy storage programs.

4. **SpaceX Starship V3 and FCC Spectrum — Indirect Tesla Signal:** SpaceX officially scheduled **Starship V3's first test flight for May 19th** (launch window opens at **6:30 PM ET**), with a wide range of engineering changes including a redesigned Super Heavy with 3 (down from 4) grid fins that are 50% larger. Starship V3 features **50 cameras** and **480 Mbps Starlink connectivity**. Separately, SpaceX received **FCC approval to acquire ~65 MHz of nationwide spectrum from EchoStar** for its next-gen direct-to-device Starlink Mobile service. While these are SpaceX-specific developments, they are relevant to Tesla investors insofar as SpaceX's valuation trajectory and Musk's capital allocation decisions (e.g., potential merger speculation) affect the Tesla narrative.

5. **FSD Subscription Pricing Ideas and Competitor Commentary:** Sawyer Merritt shared his personal wishlist for Tesla FSD monetization options, including an **annual FSD subscription starting at $999/year** (with prices rising as FSD improves) and a **short-term pass (~$99/month or less)** targeted at rental fleets and Turo operators. These are speculative/opinion-based ideas, not official Tesla announcements. In a separate post, he highlighted Audi's new **Q9 three-row SUV** for the U.S. market, dismissing its interior design as looking "8 years old" — a qualitative competitive jab rather than a data-driven comparison.
