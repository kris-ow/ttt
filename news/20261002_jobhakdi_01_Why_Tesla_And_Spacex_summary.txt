2. **JP Morgan's warning and price-target cut:** JP Morgan had warned ahead of the print that Tesla deliveries would disappoint, citing an estimate the host describes as around 460,000, and cut its price target to $415 from (he believes) $480; the host calls this "fake news" that drove Monday's drop, which he says he had expected to be more than offset by the actual delivery beat.
3. **Drivers behind the delivery strength:** The host attributes the beat to EV industry recovery, Tesla's EV market leadership, oil-price/Iran-crisis tailwinds pushing buyers toward EVs, and improving FSD quality as both a demand and margin driver.
