This video argues that AI agents, rather than additional human subscribers, will be Starlink's next major demand driver, and connects that thesis to SpaceX's Starship launch cadence and recent Starlink subscriber growth. The host's core argument is that agentic software can generate far more "useful work" per customer than a human alone, but that this does not translate one-for-one into satellite bandwidth demand, since much of that agent traffic stays within data centers; the opportunity Starlink specifically addresses is connecting remote or hard-to-reach business locations to that agent-driven workload.

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **Elon Musk's 1,000x bandwidth demand prediction:** At SpaceX's October earnings conference, Elon Musk predicted that demand for communications bandwidth could grow a thousandfold within three to five years, driven by AI agents each using more bandwidth than a single human and potentially outnumbering humans.
2. **Host's caveat on the 1,000x framing:** The host argues the thousandfold figure should not be read as implying Starlink traffic will grow thousandfold, since much agent-to-agent and agent-to-cloud traffic can stay within data centers and never needs to traverse a satellite link; the real opportunity is connecting remote business sites (e.g., a satellite-served service location with poor landline access) to cloud-based agent tools.
3. **Subscriber and revenue growth:** Per the company's latest results, Starlink subscribers reached 13.6 million at the end of September (2026), up from 7.2 million a year earlier; the same results also reported growth in corporate and government revenue.
