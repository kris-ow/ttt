This is one of the clearest on-the-ground indicators of a Cybercab ramp to date.

2. **EPA Documents Reveal Cybercab Specs:** Public EPA filings disclosed detailed Cybercab specifications for the first time: front-wheel drive, ~48 kWh battery, 219 horsepower, 163 kW motor, 326V system voltage, 3,113 lb curb weight (lightest in Tesla's lineup), and a GVWR of 3,730 lbs. Notably, the Equivalent All Electric Range is listed at 418 miles — an unusually high figure that likely reflects the vehicle's lightweight design and efficiency. These are official, government-sourced figures, giving them high credibility for modeling unit economics and fleet deployment costs.

3. **Tesla Robotaxi Fleet Safety Record:** Merritt highlighted that the only "crashes" involving Tesla's Robotaxi fleet in recent months have involved other human-driven vehicles rear-ending the Robotaxis — framing this as an ironic endorsement of the system's safety. While anecdotal, this speaks to the operational track record of the growing fleet, which per RobotaxiTracker.com stood at 725 active vehicles (601 Bay Area, 104 Austin, 9 Dallas, 11 Houston) as of June 14.

4. **FSD V14.3.4 Rollout:** A new sub-version of FSD, V14.3.4, began rolling out with the same release notes as its predecessor. This is a minor incremental update, suggesting ongoing refinement rather than a major capability leap — but confirms continued software iteration cadence.

5. **Tesla Model Y L Deliveries Begin in India:** The first Tesla Model Y L deliveries in India have commenced, marking a meaningful geographic expansion milestone. India is a large emerging market and represents a new demand vector for Tesla's EV business.

While primarily a SpaceX story, this is relevant to Tesla investors as it elevates the Elon Musk ecosystem's public profile and could attract crossover capital flows.
