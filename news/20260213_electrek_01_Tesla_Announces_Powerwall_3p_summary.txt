Tesla announced the Powerwall 3P, a new home battery variant with a native three-phase inverter built into a single unit, launching first in Germany, to replace the previous need to stack up to three Powerwalls for whole-home backup on Europe's three-phase residential grids. Sign-ups are live on Tesla's website; detailed specs and pricing have not been released.

## Energy
1. **Powerwall 3P addresses European three-phase requirement:** Most of continental Europe (Germany, Austria, Switzerland, etc.) wires homes with three-phase power, whereas the standard Powerwall 3 supports only single-phase backup per unit, previously requiring three units (at roughly $15,400 per unit before incentives in the US) to cover a whole home. The 3P consolidates this into one box with a native three-phase inverter balancing loads across all three phases.

2. **Unknown specs:** Tesla has not disclosed whether the Powerwall 3P retains the standard Powerwall 3's specs (13.5 kWh usable capacity, 11.5 kW continuous output, up to 20 kW DC solar input, LFP chemistry, 10-year warranty) or adjusts them for the new inverter architecture. Tesla describes the product as analyzing energy consumption, solar production, weather forecasts, and dynamic electricity tariffs to optimize storage/discharge, a feature relevant to Germany's increasingly variable electricity tariffs.

3. **Energy segment performance:** Tesla deployed a record 46.7 GWh of energy storage in 2025, up 48% year-over-year, driving energy revenue up 26.6% to $12.8 billion, with a 29.8% gross margin — nearly double Tesla's automotive margin. Energy storage now accounts for 13% of Tesla's total revenue and 23% of its gross profit, contrasting with an 8.6% decline in global vehicle deliveries in 2025 and a reported 36% crash in brand value. Powerwall installations passed 1 million units worldwide last fall, and Tesla reportedly became the second-most quoted inverter brand in the US in 2024.

4. **Headwinds for the segment:** Tesla recalled more than 10,000 Powerwall units over fire safety concerns, and the article states that Musk-related brand toxicity affecting vehicle sales is also spreading to energy products, with more installers recommending alternatives such as Villara's VillaGrid+, Sigenergy, and EcoFlow's DELTA Pro Ultra X. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act phased out US federal tax credits for residential storage like Powerwall, though commercial credits for Megapack/Megablock continue through the mid-2030s, making international markets like Germany more strategically important.

5. **Competitive landscape:** Rivals already offer native three-phase systems in Europe: Enphase's IQ Battery 5P with FlexPhase (launched in Germany, Austria, Switzerland in 2025, up to 70 kWh modular, available in single- and three-phase versions) was reportedly the most-used solar battery brand among installers in 2025 per industry surveys, with 74% of installers using the product; BYD offers three-phase compatibility via its Battery-Box HVE and Power-Box hybrid inverter; Sonnen (owned by Shell) offers the Sonnenbatterie 10 with virtual power plant integration popular in Germany. The article argues Tesla's advantage is a single-box, native three-phase design versus rivals' multi-module or multi-component setups, and that competitive pricing near a single Powerwall 3's cost could undercut rivals on total installed cost.

## Editorial Stance
Electrek's Fred Lambert calls the Powerwall 3P a "long-overdue fix" to an embarrassing workaround and praises the engineering, but questions whether Tesla's brand issues in Europe are spreading to its energy division; a top-voted reader comment calls the launch "too little, too late," citing Tesla's loss of market position to SigEnergy in Australia (where Tesla was reportedly #1/#2 in 2024) and BYD's lead in Europe.
