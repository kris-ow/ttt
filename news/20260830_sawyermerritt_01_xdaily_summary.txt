The poster notes this aligns with an earlier-reported Tesla filing (details not specified) and a previously posted SpaceX job listing for a "Sr. Operations Engineer" for a blades-and-vanes foundry, suggesting the initiative has been in motion for months rather than being newly announced.

The petition reportedly states Viasat has failed to reach a coordination agreement (further terms not specified). This is a regulatory/competitive dispute relevant to Starlink's spectrum position.
