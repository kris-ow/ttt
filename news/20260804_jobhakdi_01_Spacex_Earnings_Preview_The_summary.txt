## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Colossus revenue surprise sets the baseline:** SpaceX's terrestrial AI compute business (Colossus, in Memphis/South Haven and elsewhere) generated a surprise ~$26 billion/year revenue run rate through leasing compute to Anthropic and Google, on roughly 1 gigawatt of live capacity (~660,000 H100-equivalents) — already larger than SpaceX's Starlink or launch businesses combined, per the host.
2. **Energy/permitting is the real bottleneck, not chips:** About 500 megawatts of the current ~1 gigawatt live capacity relies on 69 unpermitted temporary gas turbines; following a lawsuit and settlement, SpaceX must replace them with fully permitted equivalents by mid-2027 or risk losing roughly half its compute capacity, which would jeopardize the Anthropic/Google deals.
3. **Texas expansion and the APR Energy acquisition:** SpaceX is scouting sites in Texas, where permitting is easier, to add capacity; it separately acquired APR Energy (previously ~850 MW, since expanded past 1.1 GW of leasable mobile turbine capacity) to bypass a multi-year turbine backlog — potentially adding another ~1 GW if Texas siting succeeds.
4. **Host's realistic buildout scenarios:** The host's model projects 2026 base case of 1.6 GW live (bear case 1.3 GW, bull case 2 GW), and 2027 base case of roughly 2.5 GW (bear case ~2x current capacity, extreme bull case 4 GW) — explicitly rejecting circulating claims of 8 GW by 2027 as unrealistic given permitting and turbine-supply constraints.
5. **EBIT and valuation implications:** At a 35% EBIT margin assumption, the base case implies roughly $3+ billion in additional compute EBIT in 2026 and about $20 billion in compute EBIT by 2027 (bull case ~$40 billion, roughly $6 billion EBIT per added gigawatt); at conservative multiples the host estimates the compute business alone could be worth $50–150 per share, with $100/share as a reasonable minimum — a large portion of potential upside from the current price.
6. **Compute buildout also subsidizes Grok/xAI:** Because SpaceX reportedly retains roughly half of its compute cluster for internal use, the host estimates this implicitly subsidizes Grok and related AI training costs by roughly $40 billion a year in EBIT terms, potentially giving xAI a significant cost advantage.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Share unlock mechanics ahead of earnings:** An initial unlock of roughly 911 million shares (about 20% of locked shares) becomes eligible for sale on the second trading day after earnings regardless of share price; a separate tranche of about 455 million shares only unlocks early if the stock trades at or above $175. The host argues unlock-driven selling pressure is likely regardless of valuation, since many employee shareholders will be advised by wealth managers to diversify concentrated positions.
2. **Host's trading stance:** The host says he is not making a large directional bet ahead of earnings but is selling short puts with a $100 strike, citing conviction that SpaceX's compute upside is real but uncertainty over near-term unlock-driven selling pressure and how the market will ultimately value the stock (somewhere between Nvidia- and Tesla-like multiples).
