2. **Host's "supply risk vs demand risk" thesis:** Drawing on Musk's public comments, the host argues the real risk in AI capex is not insufficient demand for compute but the difficulty of actually building gigawatt-scale data centers on time (permitting, power, GPUs/RAM sourcing); he cites the stalled "Stargate" buildout (Sam Altman/SoftBank) as an example of capital not translating into built compute, and concludes this is not a demand bubble but a supply-constrained, bullish-for-compute-owners situation — explicitly naming SpaceX as the company best equipped to execute such builds.
