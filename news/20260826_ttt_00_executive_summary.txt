## Brief

- SpaceX unveils $100B+ Starbase Louisiana spaceport

## Autonomous Driving
- Tesla's summer update adds Grok-powered voice control ("Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0"), letting drivers control climate, seat heating, lighting, and calls by voice; hosts cite it ranking #1 on the Artificial Analysis speech-to-speech index at 79%.
- FSD 14.3.8 ships with a rewritten AI compiler, reportedly delivering ~20% faster reaction time and improved handling near emergency vehicles and school buses, per brighterwithherbert.
- Tesla's supervised FSD fleet has driven 100 million kilometers in Europe ahead of an expected October 8 bloc-wide approval vote; hosts warn a no vote from several countries could sink or retract approval.
- Tesla denied Chinese social-media rumors that it abandoned its Shanghai FSD data center, telling Tencent Tech and National Business Daily the facility "is operating normally" and reporting the rumors to police. China is still absent from Tesla's list of 12 official FSD Supervised subscription markets despite being listed as "available for use" on Tesla's US site — Electrek's Fred Lambert says this points to continued regulatory limbo in China.
- Electrek reports a viral trend of Tesla owners filming themselves sleeping, gaming, or watching TV while FSD drives, using tricks like a weight on the wheel or a folded bill over the cabin camera to defeat driver monitoring; the article cites up to $14.5B in related lawsuits, a $243M verdict lost over a fatal Autopilot crash, an NHTSA probe covering 3.2 million vehicles, and Tesla's own data showing a record 207 Autopilot/FSD crashes in one month, with over 50 reported deaths.

## Robotaxi
- Nevada regulatory filings show Tesla applied for and received approval for 5,000 Cybercabs/robotaxis versus Waymo's 1,000, per jobhakdi, who argues this reflects a scale gap in operational capacity between the two.
- Bhakdi frames the September 3 Cybercab launch as a bullish catalyst for TSLA, flagging $400–420 as a "sell the news" danger zone, and says reaching thousands of Cybercabs (potentially 10,000) by year-end would be strongly bullish.
- Giga Texas's Cybercab line reportedly uses a small footprint thanks to the "unbox" manufacturing process, per Tesla exec Joe Tegtmeyer as relayed by brighterwithherbert, leaving speculation about future robo-van production space (unconfirmed, years out).

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla raised Cybertruck prices up to 7%: Dual-Motor AWD to $74,990 (from $69,990) and Premium AWD to $84,990 (from $79,990), the first Premium AWD hike since launch; commentators attribute it to constrained supply following a supplier dispute, not weakening demand.
- Tesla's new Nevada Semi factory, adjacent to Gigafactory Nevada's battery plant, is now producing trucks at volume, with capacity up to 50,000 trucks/year; pricing is expected around $260,000 (325-mile) and $290,000 (500-mile) versions. Einride ordered 500 units (largest single Semi order to date) and Watt EV ordered 370.
- Tesla teased a European Semi variant with camera-monitor mirrors ahead of the IAA Transportation show (Sept 15–20) in Hanover; US deployment remains blocked by federal mirror rules pending since 2014.
- Semi rollout/inauguration event confirmed for September 24 at the new Nevada factory, expected to center on first Gen-2 Semi ("Atlas") deliveries to outside customers.

## Energy
- Tesla discontinued Solar Roof this month after it never scaled past ~3,000 US installations by end of 2022 (~0.17% of that year's residential solar market).
- Tesla's energy segment hit $12.8B revenue in 2025 (+27% YoY), driven almost entirely by Megapack/Powerwall storage rather than solar; Tesla now sells a self-manufactured 420-watt panel from its Buffalo Gigafactory, defaulting configurator bundles to Powerwall 3, and has shifted installation to a certified third-party network that grew from ~2,000 installers/14 countries to 7,000+ installers/35+ countries.
- Tesla opened its second public Megacharger station (Vernon, CA; six 1.2MW stalls), part of a planned ~46-station network by 2027 led by Texas (19) and California (17).

## Financials
- Bhakdi holds a self-described "north of 85%" conviction TSLA moves toward $380 near-term via short-term options trading (explicitly not investment advice), and sees a path to prior all-time highs (~$480–488) by year-end if Cybercab scaling continues.
- Musk reportedly told employees SpaceX AI-segment revenue would surpass all other SpaceX revenue combined next quarter — last quarter AI revenue was $2.56B versus $5.25B in other revenue, implying AI revenue roughly doubling; Bhakdi speculates (unconfirmed) a new compute-leasing deal, possibly with Microsoft, could be announced around September.
- Terafab's highest cited cost estimate is $119 billion, far below TSMC's ~$290B Arizona buildout and SK Hynix's $400B four-fab plan, though Cern Basher expects Terafab to run over budget given fab capex is effectively open-ended.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX officially unveiled "Starbase, Louisiana," a $100+ billion spaceport near Pecan Island, with construction starting 2027 and first launch targeted 2029; Musk says the site could bring 10,000 jobs and will eventually be open to public visits like Cape Canaveral. A reported "Terabay" design could support up to 30 Starship flights per day as a long-term capacity target, not a current rate.
- Musk confirmed on August 20 that Starship's first upper-stage tower catch — originally planned for Flight 14 in late August — is delayed to "in a few months" due to the precision and heat challenges versus the already-proven booster catch (achieved three times); Flight 14 will instead attempt Starship's first full orbital flight after 13 suborbital flights.
- Musk said the first reflight of a previously used Starship is expected late this year or early next year.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- SpaceX introduced a 50% discount on Starlink Residential subscriptions for customers in Vermilion Parish, Louisiana, tied to the new Starbase announcement.
- Bhakdi projects SpaceX could reach ~2 gigawatts of compute capacity by year-end and ~5 gigawatts by end of next year, calling Musk's stated 10-gigawatt target unrealistic; he expects satellite-based data centers to reach real scale around 2028.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Bhakdi argues Grok (version 4.6, with 4.7 expected within ~2 weeks per Musk) is near the frontier of AI price-performance and, combined with the Grokbot coding agent, could push SpaceX's AI unit toward AGI-level competitiveness; he speculates Grok 4.7 may incorporate proprietary SpaceX engineering data.
- Cern Basher cites a SemiAnalysis claim that SpaceX's Colossus 2 data center site uses about 3,000 workers per day versus roughly 10,000/day at a comparable site elsewhere, suggesting faster, leaner buildout.

## Market & Competition

- Terafab hiring is concentrated in Austin (34 of ~35 job postings), with "first steel" expected soon at the Giga Texas site while the Grimes County, Texas site remains in early land-clearing.
- Boring Company's Vegas Loop grew to 123 total entitled stations after 19 new approvals, though only ~11 of 126 planned tunnel miles are dug and ~600 more permits are needed.
