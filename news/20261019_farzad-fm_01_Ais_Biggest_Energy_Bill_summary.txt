This video is a technical explainer on why data movement, not computation, is the dominant energy cost in AI data centers, using Nvidia hardware and commentary from Jensen Huang as its core example. It has no connection to Tesla, SpaceX, or their investment theses.

## Market & Competition
1. **Data movement dwarfs computation in AI energy use:** The video explains that moving a single number from DRAM memory consumes thousands of times more energy than adding two numbers on a chip, and that on Nvidia's A100 GPU, the majority of processing power during image-model training is spent moving data rather than calculating.
2. **Nvidia's Jensen Huang on optical transceivers:** Huang is cited discussing the energy cost of the six optical transceivers per GPU needed to convert electrical signals to light for inter-machine data transfer, noting the scale of wattage this adds up to across a million-GPU system, and stating these components perform no calculation, only signal movement.
3. **Newer switches integrate optics to cut power:** According to Nvidia, newest switch designs place light connections directly on the chip housing, reducing per-connection power consumption from about 30 watts to roughly nine watts.
