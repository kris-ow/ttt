2. **Registration and sighting data acceleration:** Per the host, Cybercab sightings/registrations (tracked via pioneerlands.org) rose by double digits in a single day, with 169 Cybercabs now fully registered with the Texas DMV, up 43 in the past week. 3. **Host's deployment trajectory and targets:** The host maintains his prediction of reaching 1,800 fully deployed Cybercabs by year-end (acknowledging this is about six months later than his original forecast), and separately predicts "north of 1,000" live Cybercabs by December, "probably 2,000," crossing 1,000 as the key inflection point he expects within the next ~2.5 months. He also says he believes Tesla will produce around 4,000 Cybercabs in October based on manufacturing rate, and ties a new version of FSD (he says version 15) to the deployment ramp — these are his own projections, not confirmed company guidance.
