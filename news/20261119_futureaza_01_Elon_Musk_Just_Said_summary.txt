In a live interview, Elon Musk covers the Nvidia leadership upheaval, the midterm results, Starlink's role in Ukraine, SpaceX's telecom plans, and the prospect of a SpaceX–Tesla merger. The investment-relevant core is his framing of the merger as a shareholder-vote question driven by AI and robotics convergence, his account of Anthropic as a SpaceX compute customer, and the Starlink direct-to-cell roadmap.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Merger framing:** Musk says either path, merger or no merger, is possible. Five years ago he saw no reason for the two companies to combine and calls "synergy" an overused word, but says AI and robotics are creating convergence. Both have major AI software groups (Tesla's focused on real-world AI such as self-driving and Optimus; SpaceX on large models) and both have advanced chip design teams. He says the companies would probably be better together but not that they need to be, and stresses that it is not a "merge no matter what" situation. Shareholder votes may or may not succeed, and he must represent other shareholders and avoid a deal that is bad for them.
2. **No merger compensation:** Asked about his Tesla pay award, Musk says he should not receive any award simply for doing a merger. He argues stock options contingent on Tesla execution should be earned only through Tesla performance, not through a combination with SpaceX.

## Financials
1. **Merger status context:** Musk's remarks that the outcome depends on shareholder votes and on whether the combined company creates more value than the parts fits the status of no announcement as of mid-November, with Bloomberg reporting a possible December 3 announcement.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Anthropic as compute customer:** Musk says he met many Anthropic people before renting them compute, knows many from his OpenAI days, and that no one he met set off his "evil detector." The deals are relatively short-term, and either party can terminate with 3 to 6 months' notice depending on the contract. He says Anthropic told him it is extremely likely to keep renting SpaceX compute and wants significantly more, with its highest-probability outcome being an incremental 10 gigawatts. He acknowledges a worst-case scenario in which extreme curbs on what AI can do could limit their usage.
2. **Orbital compute:** Musk predicts orbital compute will "round up to 100%" of superintelligence compute over time, calling it a relatively easy prediction and a matter of time.

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **Direct-to-cell roadmap:** SpaceX will begin launching its direct-to-cell version 2 constellation next year, which Musk expects to have about 40 times the bandwidth of version 1, with a version 3 to follow that connects ordinary unmodified phones directly from space.
2. **Terrestrial micro-cell layer:** To cover people indoors, especially under thick or metal roofs, SpaceX plans to use the 800 MHz band it bought, with micro ground stations that can be installed like a dish. The design is going into production next year. Musk does not expect AT&T or Verizon to go out of business, though they may lose some revenue, and argues bandwidth demand from superintelligence and robots will be enormous.
3. **Ukraine and Starlink control:** Musk says Starlink is civilian infrastructure not for offensive military use, and that offensive actions belong on Starshield, where the kill decision rests with the US government. He says the rules have been unchanged since the start, asserts that Starlink was not turned off for a strike on the Russian fleet at Sevastopol because beams were not enabled in Crimea, and says Zelensky's claims about Starshield are false (his characterization). He also says SpaceX files only defensive patents and encourages copying its rockets and satellites.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Model Y L and lineup:** Musk says the Model Y L is Tesla's most popular car and that Tesla will probably need to convert all Model Y production to it. He mentions he ordered one himself.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab and autonomous Model Y:** Musk says the two-seat Cybercab covers about 90% of passenger miles, since the average car carries 1.2 people. Tesla also plans an autonomous Model Y without steering wheel or pedals for groups of six or seven people. He confirms the Cybertruck is being sunset to make room for Cybercab production, and says Tesla will keep making cars of varying seating.

## Other Musk Commentary
1. **Nvidia and Jensen Huang:** Musk calls Huang's removal a mistake, likens it to kicking Michael Jordan off a winning team, says he would hire him but that Huang is not looking, and notes the decision rests with Nvidia's board and then its shareholders.
2. **Politics:** Musk says he regrets spending so much on the midterms with little result, that he wanted a balance of power, and that the new Texas governor is likely to be moderate and Texas should be fine.
