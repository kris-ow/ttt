He argues multi-camera awareness makes robotaxis safer than humans; this is a single anecdotal clip, not statistical safety data.

5. **SpaceX: Morgan Stanley on Starbase:** Sawyer quoted Adam Jonas's new SpaceX note, written after attending Starship Flight 15 at Starbase, saying the city has transformed notably over the last few months, from a small rocket testing site into what the note describes as arguably a significant hub. Supportive color on Starship progress, with little hard data.
