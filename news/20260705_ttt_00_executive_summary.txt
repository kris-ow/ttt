## Brief

- Nevada robotaxi permit filed for up to 5,000 vehicles
- Optimus production line conversion at Fremont underway
- Neuralink threads electrodes through dura in live trial
- Tesla Japan posts record June sales, up 183% YoY
- Robotaxi seven-city June 30 target missed, at three cities

## Robotaxi
- Tesla's mid-year goal of seven robotaxi cities was missed; service remains live in three, per brighterwithherbert, with skepticism that an imminent announcement will "redeem" the miss.
- Nevada permit filed June 5 covers Clark County for up to 5,000 vehicles in year one, including Reid Airport service; a depot with superchargers/lifts is built on Mohawk Street and gold-liveried robotaxis have been spotted in Las Vegas, but the permit is still under review with no rides yet.
- Per Lars Moravy (via brighterwithherbert), ~90% of Cybercab automation is already in place with fewer problems than expected; broader ramp is now expected around August–September rather than earlier this year.
- Jo Bhakdi models Tesla's 2027-28 Robotaxi EBIT contribution at roughly $5B (100,000 units) to $50B (1M units) versus SpaceX Colossus deals already generating an estimated $12.5-13.3B EBIT — arguing physical robotaxi deployment is a much heavier lift than SpaceX's compute-leasing model, but not writing Robotaxi off.
- X Money (launched June 26, 6% APY, no processing fee) is speculated — unconfirmed by Tesla — as a future in-house payment rail for robotaxi fares, potentially capturing ~$250M/year now leaking to Apple Pay/Google Pay at $10B/year fare scale.
- Vegas Loop (Boring Company) has carried 3M+ passengers since 2021 using Tesla FSD software with a safety driver still onboard; fleet set to grow to 160 Teslas, seen as a controlled-environment autonomy testbed and future Robovan/Cybercab demand driver.

## Humanoid Bots
- Elon Musk posted about converting the former Model S/X production line at Fremont into an Optimus line, with new stations being installed — cited by two separate sources as evidence of a serious, not abandoned, bet.
- Per Lars Moravy (via brighterwithherbert), one automated production line sourced from Tesla's German automation subsidiary took ~2 weeks to set up, with 40+ additional sublines planned — suggesting full line automation will take longer than hoped.
- Texas Optimus factory (the real production facility, vs. Fremont's prototype line) is roughly a year from fully operational, per Moravy; futureaza separately estimates ~2 years to complete plus ~6 months to reach operation.
- Neuralink announced June 30 it threaded electrode implants through the dura without cutting it, in a live trial; Elon called it a "very big deal" for brain-interface safety. Neuralink's head of surgery said Optimus limb integration "will happen very soon," though this is framed as a 5-10+ year thread, not a near-term catalyst.
- SoftBank sold its ~10% Boston Dynamics stake back to Hyundai for ~$300M, implying a ~$3B valuation — futureaza argues a spun-out Optimus would easily exceed that.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla posted its best-ever June in Japan: 3,997 registrations, up 183% YoY.
- Tesla delivered over 480,000 vehicles last quarter, called a "blowout" driven by Europe strength, per brighterwithherbert.
- A Model Y "L" variant is expected to be announced soon; Goldberg estimates it could expand TAM by roughly a third or more.
- A teased "Cyber speaker" Bluetooth accessory generated engagement with no pricing or release date disclosed.

## Financials
- AI5 is estimated more than a year away and Terafab roughly two to three years away, per Goldberg.
- Bhakdi reiterates a $410 fair-value view on Tesla shares post-selloff, calling the recent drop to the $390s "overdone" and disclosing he bought Tesla calls during the dip; Tesla's 2025 EBIT baseline cited at ~$3.45B.
- Macrohard (Tesla/SpaceX joint project) flagged as a "dark horse" revenue opportunity, but Goldberg expects most resulting revenue to accrue to SpaceX/xAI rather than Tesla, with no meaningful contribution in the next 12 months.

## Market & Competition
- Figure's robot reportedly ran ~200 hours of continuous package-sorting and units are reportedly deployed at BMW, cited as evidence the humanoid robotics category is broadly validating industry-wide, not just at Tesla.
- SpaceX's Colossus 1 (Memphis) is reportedly leased fully to Anthropic for $1.25B/month (~$10-11B estimated EBIT); Colossus 2 partially leased to Google for $920M/month (~$2.4B EBIT). Bullish $170-288B EBIT run-rate claims for Colossus 3 are challenged as implausible given grid capacity constraints — Memphis metro peak demand is ~3 gigawatts, and Colossus 1+2 already represent ~60% of that.
