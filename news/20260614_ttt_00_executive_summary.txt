## Autonomous Driving
- **FSD 14.3.4 released June 13, 2026** and drawing exceptional early feedback. A community reviewer who drove 250 miles on the build called it "by far the best overall FSD build by a wide margin," noting the car now eases off the accelerator for animals rather than braking hard, parking destination selection is improved, and major upgrades are arriving roughly every two to three weeks with the pace accelerating.
- **Robotaxi-oriented UI strings** added in 14.3.4 include "Vehicle will pull over near destination," "Approaching destination. Will pull over near destination," and "Driving to Market Basket" — per Sawyer Merritt, "the kind of stuff a robotaxi would show," signaling active preparation of the passenger-facing driverless experience.
- **14.3.x represents an architectural inflection**, not just an iterative release. Per Bhakdi, the 14.3 major drop removed legacy components from the underlying system, enabling a faster iteration cadence; the change in slope of progress is the signal.
- **Elon confirmed on the most recent earnings call** that robotaxi rollout does not require waiting for FSD v15. The rollout will proceed "at huge scale" on v14; v15 is targeted for approximately end of 2026.
- **Cortex 2 at Giga Texas removes the training bottleneck** structurally. With the first 250 MW phase online since April 2026, the campus now runs 230,000+ H100-equivalent GPUs combined with Cortex 1 — multiples of the largest national lab supercomputers — explicitly taking compute off the critical path for FSD improvement and robotaxi scaling.

## Robotaxi
- **Cybercab production exceeds 1,000 units** as of mid-June per Bhakdi's estimates from drone footage and sightings: ~220 units in April (10/day), ~440 in May (20/day), ~360 so far in June (30/day). He projects ~2,000 total by the first week of July and 3,000–4,000 by August.
- **Cybercabs spotted in Houston and Las Vegas** on June 13, 2026, both batches fitted with decorative wheel caps absent in earlier testing — a cosmetic preparation signal Bhakdi interprets as indicating public deployment is near. Sightings also reported in Palo Alto, Austin, Chicago, Boston, and Florida.
- **Bhakdi predicts "multiple dozens" of unsupervised Cybercabs deployed in June 2026**, estimating 40–50 unsupervised units would be a 10–20% short-term stock catalyst sufficient to push Tesla back toward the ~$490 prior all-time high. He frames 1,800 unsupervised robotaxis as the threshold for a more fundamental repricing toward $600–$800.
- **A viewer cited Tesla as saying four to five new robotaxi cities** should come online in the next two weeks; Bhakdi acknowledged the comment but expressed uncertainty about whether Tesla's stated timelines can be relied upon.
- **One credible criticism acknowledged:** brighterwithherbert notes that as of mid-2026, robotaxi scale remains "still mostly just Austin and Phoenix" — flagging timeline slippage as a legitimate bear-case concern.

## Humanoid Bots
- **Optimus factory at Giga Texas North Campus: first steel went up May 27, 2026.** The dedicated facility covers 5.2 million square feet per permit filings — a footprint stretching nearly the full length of the main Giga Texas building. Tesla's framing suggests low-volume production could begin as early as summer 2026, with the primary ramp running through 2027.
- **Long-term output target is 10 million Optimus units per year** from the single North Campus site — a figure brighterwithherbert explicitly labels a target, not existing capacity, and frames as "smartphone-scale output with no precedent for that ratio of output to footprint" relative to Hyundai's Asan complex (~1.5M vehicles/year) or Apple's iPhone output (~230M/year across multiple campuses).
- **Cortex 2 serves as the compute backbone for Optimus neural net training** alongside FSD. Full 500 MW activation is targeted for mid-2026.

## Energy
- **Tesla's Houston Megapack factory in Brookshire, Texas** carries a $200 million initial investment and targets 50 GWh/year of grid-scale storage. Production is targeted for late 2026, though brightenwithherbert flags the date as likely to slip.
- **New flagship product: Mega Block**, each unit delivering 20 MWh of AC capacity (four Megapack 3 units, a medium-voltage transformer, and switchgear in a plug-and-play format). Tesla claims 23% faster installation and up to 40% lower construction cost versus traditional builds.
- **Combined Tesla Megapack manufacturing capacity exceeds 130 GWh/year** once Houston is online alongside Lathrop and Giga Shanghai — covering roughly two-thirds of the ~200 GWh of global battery storage deployed in all of 2024.
- **Largest U.S. residential Tesla Solar Tile Roof installation** reported on a Maui home listed at $26.5 million; the system cost $1.4 million. No capacity figure was disclosed.

## Market & Competition
- **SpaceX-Tesla merger thesis advanced by Gwyn Shotwell's non-denial.** In a CNBC interview, Shotwell said a merger "might make Elon's life a little easier," acknowledged "there's no question that there are synergies," and described "definitely a convergence of what we're all trying to accomplish" — stopping short of any denial. Wedbush's Dan Ives puts merger probability above 80%, targeting 2027.
- **Bhakdi's merger timing view: early 2027**, contingent on Tesla's robotaxi rollout establishing clarity and SpaceX shareholder lockup periods expiring on newly issued IPO stock. SpaceX's S-1 amendment language suggests the company may be increasing available shares for large acquisitions.
- **SpaceX IPO index inclusion calendar** per Bhakdi: Russell US Indexes fast-entry on June 26, 2026; NASDAQ fast-entry rebalance ~July 6, 2026; Russell semiannual reconstitution December 2026. S&P 500 inclusion blocked by rules change rejection — earliest possible entry approximately one year away.
- **Rivian R2 facing compounding headwinds:** launch edition priced at ~$58,000–$59,500 landed vs. the originally announced $45,000; original price point not expected until spring 2027. Consumer Reports placed Rivian last in reliability across all brands surveyed. Cumulative cash burn across pure-play EVs tracked by analyst AJ reached $24.4 billion as of February 2026.
- **Rivian CEO RJ Scaringe** stated the company will launch "full supervised point-to-point" autonomy across Gen 2 vehicles and R2 later in 2026, with unsupervised capability targeted for 2027 — notable as a competitive benchmark but Rivian remains far behind Tesla in training data (Tesla: 11 billion miles) and compute infrastructure.
- **Volkswagen Group entered a new agreement with Xpeng** for fast joint development of two smart EVs using Xpeng's software, suggesting Rivian's software partnership with VW may be less durable than previously assumed.
