## Autonomous Driving
- **FSD 14.3.4 released June 13, 2026, drawing exceptional early reviews.** Community member "Doge Army General" drove 250 miles on the build and called it "by far the best overall FSD build by a wide margin," noting natural animal-avoidance behavior (easing off accelerator rather than hard braking) and improved parking destination selection. He described the pace of upgrades — roughly every two to three weeks — as "mind-boggling" and accelerating.
- **Robotaxi-facing UI strings debut in 14.3.4.** New on-screen messages include "Vehicle will pull over near destination," "Approaching destination. Will pull over near destination," and "Driving to Market Basket" — language Sawyer Merritt characterized as "the kind of stuff a robotaxi would show," indicating active preparation of the software stack for a passenger-facing driverless experience.
- **The 14.3.x series represents an architectural rebuild, not an incremental update.** Bhakdi explains that the major 14.3 release removed legacy system components, which is enabling the accelerating iteration cadence now visible in the 14.3.x point releases.
- **Elon Musk confirmed on the most recent earnings call that robotaxi rollout will not wait for FSD v15.** Per Bhakdi's summary, Musk stated deployment will happen "at huge scale" on version 14, with v15 targeted for approximately end of 2026.
- **Cortex 2 structurally removes the training bottleneck for FSD scaling.** The first 250-megawatt phase came online April 2026; full 500-megawatt activation is targeted for mid-2026. Combined with Cortex 1, the Giga Texas campus now exceeds 230,000 H100-equivalent GPUs — multiples above the largest national lab supercomputers — explicitly removing compute as a ceiling on FSD improvement velocity per brighterwithherbert.

## Robotaxi
- **Cybercab production estimated above 1,000 units as of mid-June 2026, per Bhakdi's drone-footage-based model.** His unit estimates: April ~220 (10/day), May ~440 (20/day), June to date ~360 (30/day). He projects roughly 2,000 total by the first week of July and 3,000–4,000 by August.
- **Cybercabs spotted June 13 in Houston and Las Vegas, both batches fitted with decorative wheel caps.** Bhakdi interprets the wheel caps — absent in earlier test vehicles — as a cosmetic preparation signal indicating public deployment is near. Additional sightings have been reported in Palo Alto, Austin, Chicago, Boston, and Florida.
- **Bhakdi predicts "multiple dozens" of unsupervised Cybercabs deployed in June 2026.** He estimates 40–50 unsupervised units would be a 10–20% short-term stock catalyst, sufficient to push Tesla back toward the prior all-time high of ~$490. He frames 1,800 unsupervised units as the threshold for a more fundamental stock repricing toward $600–$800 or higher.
- **A viewer cited Tesla as saying four to five new robotaxi cities should go online within two weeks.** Bhakdi acknowledged the comment but expressed uncertainty about Tesla's stated timeline reliability.
- **brightenwithherbert acknowledges that robotaxi scale "is still mostly just Austin and Phoenix" as of mid-2026** — framing this as a legitimate bear-case criticism alongside broader timeline slippage across Tesla's megaproject pipeline.

## Humanoid Bots
- **Optimus factory at Giga Texas North Campus: first steel went up May 27, 2026.** Permitted footprint is 5.2 million square feet — nearly the full length of the main Giga Texas building. Tesla's framing suggests low-volume production could begin as early as summer 2026, with the primary ramp running through 2027.
- **Long-term output target for the North Campus facility is 10 million Optimus units per year** — explicitly labeled a target, not current capacity. Brighterwithherbert contextualizes the ambition: Apple's global iPhone output is ~230 million units per year spread across multiple Foxconn campuses; 10 million complex humanoid robots from a single site would be "smartphone-scale output with no precedent for that ratio of output to footprint."
- **Cortex 2 serves as the compute backbone for Optimus neural net training alongside FSD.** The 130,000+ H100-equivalent GPU cluster dwarfs El Capitan and Frontier (each ~40,000 GPUs), the largest U.S. national lab supercomputers.

## Energy
- **Tesla's Houston Megapack factory in Brookshire, Texas carries a $200 million initial investment and targets 50 GWh/year of grid-scale storage.** Production is targeted for late 2026, though brighterwithherbert flags that date as likely to slip. The new flagship product, Mega Block, packs 20 MWh of AC capacity per unit and claims 23% faster installation and up to 40% lower construction cost versus traditional builds.
- **Adding Houston to Lathrop and Giga Shanghai pushes Tesla's combined Megapack manufacturing capacity past 130 GWh/year.** Total global battery storage deployed in 2024 was approximately 200 GWh; Tesla's factory capacity alone would cover roughly two-thirds of that figure once Houston is fully online.
- **A $1.4 million Tesla Solar Tile Roof on a Maui home listed at $26.5 million is claimed to be the largest residential installation in the U.S.** No kilowatt capacity was disclosed; the installation serves as a high-end brand showcase for Tesla Energy.

## Electric Vehicles
- **Tesla demand signals heading into Q2 2026 look positive per Bhakdi.** Model Y wait times in China are elevated, European sales are rebounding "like crazy," and competing automakers including Chinese brands are described as facing significant problems.
- **Rivian R2 launch edition pricing has ballooned to ~$58,000–$60,000+** versus the originally announced ~$45,000 target. The $44,900 price point is not expected until spring 2027 at the earliest, and FutureAzA host Brian doubts it will be achieved. The Georgia factory intended to deliver scale efficiencies has been delayed due to Rivian's cash crunch, raising concerns that Rivian may be losing money on high-volume sales.
- **Rivian CEO RJ Scaringe stated the company will launch supervised point-to-point autonomy across all Gen 2 vehicles and the R2 later in 2026, with unsupervised capability targeted for 2027.** FutureAzA frames Rivian's autonomy ambitions skeptically, noting it lacks Tesla's 11 billion real-world training miles and visible compute infrastructure at comparable scale.

## Market & Competition
- **Gwyn Shotwell declined to deny a Tesla-SpaceX merger in a CNBC interview, saying it "might make Elon's life a little easier" and that "there's no question there are synergies."** Wedbush analyst Dan Ives, whose assertion prompted the question, puts the merger probability above 80% targeting 2027. Brighterwithherbert and guest Larry Goldberg interpret Shotwell's response as tacit confirmation rather than a denial.
- **Goldberg places a formal acquisition offer in early 2027**, citing three reasons for the delay: Tesla's robotaxi rollout needs more time to drive its share price higher; SpaceX shareholders face lockup periods on newly issued public stock; and SpaceX has a large near-term operational workload. SpaceX's S-1 amendment includes language suggesting the company may be increasing available shares to conduct large acquisitions.
- **SpaceX went public the prior week in what Bhakdi calls the largest IPO in the history of capitalism**, with the stock rising approximately 20% to ~$165 from an IPO price of ~$135. Index inclusion catalysts on the calendar: Russell US fast-entry June 26, NASDAQ fast-entry ~July 6, Russell semiannual reconstitution December 2026. S&P 500 inclusion is at least one year away following a rejected rules change.
- **Bhakdi is not a SpaceX buyer at current prices**, predicting a classic IPO hype-to-trough cycle with a "valley of tears" lasting one to two years before a steep re-acceleration driven by actual cash flow. He expects to buy meaningfully in roughly nine to twelve months.
- **No single competitor — among the Mag-7 or in China — is simultaneously building chip fabs, launch capacity, grid-scale energy storage, and humanoid labor production**, per brighterwithherbert's central competitive claim. Hyperscalers are spending more raw dollars on AI data centers but none cover all layers of the stack.

## Financials
- **Terafab carries $55 billion for phase 1 scaling to $119 billion for full buildout**, per the SpaceX S-1 IPO filing. The S-1 calls this a "framework." Grimes County commissioners approved a 100% 10-year tax abatement for Terafab on June 3, 2026 (4-to-1 vote). Intel joined the Terafab venture April 7, 2026, contributing the 18A process node.
- **After SpaceX absorbed xAI in February 2026, Tesla's $2 billion xAI investment converted into a small SpaceX stake.** Tesla holds no direct xAI equity; Tesla products (FSD, in-vehicle Grok assistant) continue to run on Colossus's output.
