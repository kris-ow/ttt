## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla Q3 2026 deliveries beat expectations despite lost EV credit:** Tesla delivered 486,532 vehicles in Q3 2026, down 2% YoY but ahead of Wall Street's 462,000 estimate; total production was 464,391. This was Tesla's best-ever quarterly delivery total without the $7,500 US federal EV credit, and third-best overall, behind Q3 2025 (497,099, with credit) and Q4 2024 (495,570, with credit). A follow-up post poses whether Tesla can top 500,000 deliveries in a single quarter for the first time in Q4 2026.

2. **Energy storage deployment near record levels:** Tesla deployed 13.7 GWh of battery energy storage in Q3 2026, its second-best quarter ever for storage deployments.

3. **Cybercab registrations accelerating in Texas:** Per Texas DMV data cited by the poster, Tesla registered 32 additional Cybercabs (bringing the total to 158) and then 11 more shortly after (bringing the total to 169) — a reported 43 new Cybercab registrations within an 18-hour window. These are vehicle registrations, not confirmed fleet deployments or active robotaxi units.

4. **Model Y and Model 3 hardware/software upgrades:** Tesla upgraded the Model Y Premium AWD's 0-60mph time to 4.3 seconds (from 4.6s). Separately, Tesla announced all US/Puerto Rico/Mexico Model 3 units now ship with a larger 16-inch center screen; the Model 3 Premium AWD's 0-60mph time improved to 3.8s (from 4.2s), and the Model 3 Performance trim got a new all-black headliner, larger 16" screen, ~2.3% more range (316 miles, up from 309) and ~3% more horsepower (528hp).

5. **EV competitor Q3 2026 results — mixed signals:** Ford sold 6,047 EVs in the US in Q3 2026, down 80.2% YoY (Mach-E: 5,574, -72%; F-150 Lightning: 289, now discontinued; E-Transit: 184, -57%). Rivian delivered 19,248 vehicles in Q3 2026, up 46% YoY and ahead of Wall Street's 18,000 estimate, with production of 19,751 units; Rivian reaffirmed full-year 2026 delivery guidance of 65,000-70,000 vehicles.

6. **SpaceX: Starlink V3 satellites deployed from Starship; Starlink Mini price cut:** SpaceX deployed what the poster describes as the first operational Starlink V3 satellites from Starship. Separately, SpaceX cut the US price of its Starlink Mini Kit to a record low of $149 (or $12.41/month for 12 months) — a 25% drop from the prior $199 price and 75% below the original $599 price; Roam monthly plans are listed at $55/month (100GB), $80/month (300GB), and an unlimited tier.
