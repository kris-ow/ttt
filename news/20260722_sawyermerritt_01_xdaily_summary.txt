## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Robotaxi expands to Tampa and Orlando — now live in 7 areas:** Tesla officially launched unsupervised Model Y robotaxi rides in Tampa and Orlando, Florida, marking the first driverless rides in both cities. This brings Tesla's ride-hailing/robotaxi footprint to 7 areas total: Orlando (new, unsupervised Model Y only), Tampa (new, unsupervised Model Y only), Miami (unsupervised Model Y only), Dallas (unsupervised Model Y only), Houston, plus other previously listed regions (Bay Area, Austin implied from canonical data). Separately, Tesla launched airport rides to SFO for its Bay Area rideshare service, expanding utility of the existing fleet. Per RobotaxiTracker.com, the active Robotaxi fleet stood at 772 vehicles as of 2026-07-21 (Bay Area 634, Austin 105, Dallas 17, Houston 16), with the unsupervised subset last measured at 39 (Austin 28, Dallas 5, Houston 6) as of 2026-05-09 — a stale figure that doesn't yet reflect the new Tampa/Orlando unsupervised launches.

2. **Summer 2026 software update unveiled — Grok integration, FSD parking memory:** Tesla unveiled its Summer 2026 update, headlined by navigation that prioritizes previously-taken routes, and Grok gaining the ability to make phone calls, search, play music, adjust climate, open the glovebox, and answer vehicle questions. Separately, Tesla previewed an upcoming FSD feature letting users select and save a favorite parking spot so they won't need to disengage FSD to park there. Tesla also announced Grok will soon launch in vehicles in India, Thailand, Singapore, Philippines, and Malaysia, and a new self-driving app is now live in the Tesla mobile app. A new intro animation for Model 3/Model Y accompanies the update.

3. **FSD version rollouts continue — V14.3.6 and V14 Lite (HW3) widen:** FSD V14.3.6 began rolling out with the same release notes as prior builds. Separately, FSD V14 Lite has started its wider public rollout to HW3 (older hardware) owners beyond the early access group, expanding capability to legacy Tesla vehicles.

4. **Tesla Diner hits milestone; Model S/X Signature editions get send-off:** The Tesla Diner logged over 580,000 charging sessions in its first year (opened one year ago in Hollywood). Separately, Tesla updated Model S and Model X product pages with a "Signature from start to finish" message, appearing to signal the wind-down/limited-release status of the Signature Editions marking the end of an era for the original S/X lineup.

5. **Model 3 lease prices raised up to 15%:** Tesla raised U.S. Model 3 lease pricing: RWD to $379/month (+15.2%), Premium RWD to $399/month (+14.3%), Premium AWD to $479/month (+6.7%), Performance to $629/month (+5%) — a notable tightening of consumer financing incentives.

6. **Megapack contract and Australia EV demand:** Spearmint Energy received a site permit for a $140 million Tesla Megapack battery storage facility in Minnesota (150MW/600MWh). In Australia, EV sales this year already exceed all of 2025's total; Model Y remains the top-selling EV, up 95% YoY, and was the #3 best-selling vehicle overall (any fuel type) in H1 2026.

7. **SpaceX: Starlink V5 terminal rollout and Cybercab integration teaser:** Starlink customers have begun receiving the new V5 terminal, which is 48% smaller by area, 62% lighter, and 50% more power-efficient than the V4 dish while maintaining capability — described as "incredible engineering." Separately, a clip of Tesla's Ashok Elluswamy discussing Starlink V5 integration in the Cybercab was posted, though no further detail on the integration was given in this digest.

8. **Q2 earnings call preview:** Ahead of Tesla's Q2 earnings call (Wednesday, 2026-07-22), retail investor questions were previewed, including queries on constraints to scaling robotaxi operations relative to Cybercab production timing, and the status of Optimus (cut off in the source text).
