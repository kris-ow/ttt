Electrek's Fred Lambert reports that newly released Epstein files contain emails showing Tesla board members Elon Musk and Kimbal Musk had extensive, long-running contact with Jeffrey Epstein, which the author argues contradicts Elon Musk's past public denials and raises corporate governance concerns that Tesla shareholders are likely to ignore.

## Market & Competition
1. **Emails contradict Musk's public denial of visiting Epstein's island:** The article cites email exchanges from December 2013 in which Musk asked Epstein about visiting his island ("Will be in the BVI/St Bart's area over the holidays. Is there a good time to visit?"), and a calendar entry dated December 6, 2014 reading "ELON MUSK TO ISLAND DEC. 6TH." This is presented as contradicting Musk's past claim that he "REFUSED" invitations to the island; the article notes Musk has since acknowledged asking to visit, while disputing the significance.
2. **Epstein told Musk about young women at his events:** A September 2013 email from Epstein to Musk states "there is no one over 25 and all very cute" when inviting him to a gathering; a separate 2012 exchange has Epstein telling Musk "the ratio on my island might make Talulah uncomfortable," referring to Musk's then-wife. A February 2013 email from Epstein's assistant to Musk's office references sending copies of "all 3 girls passports" in connection with travel tied to Musk, which the article links to a SpaceX facility tour Musk had previously denied occurred.
3. **Epstein discussed business matters with Musk:** The files show Epstein asking Musk about having SolarCity "electrify the caribbean island" and a "new mexico ranch" (Epstein properties), which Musk forwarded to SolarCity co-founder Peter Rive. A 2015 email from Sultan Bin Sulayem to Epstein asks whether Epstein discussed a business "proposal" with Musk over dinner, which the article says shows Epstein acting as an intermediary for Musk business matters.
4. **Kimbal Musk's relationship with Epstein:** Email records show Epstein's assistant scheduling meetings at Epstein's house with Kimbal Musk, and a travel schedule document appears to track a woman named "Jen" traveling with Kimbal across multiple cities (NYC, London, Morocco, St. Barts) over several months. Kimbal emailed Epstein and an associate saying "I am very happy with my time so far with Jennifer. She's great." A separate email shows Epstein, Kimbal, and associate Boris Nikolic jokingly planning a Halloween costume outing together.
5. **No new corporate governance action reported:** The article states the documents do not prove either Musk brother committed crimes, and reports no board investigation, recusal, lawsuit, or shareholder action has occurred in response to the revelations.

## Editorial Stance
The author argues that at a normal public company these revelations (lying about the relationship, evidence of a board member being connected to women arranged by Epstein) would trigger an independent investigation, recusals, possible termination, and shareholder lawsuits, but contends Tesla's retail-dominated, Musk-loyal shareholder base will not hold either Musk brother accountable because "the stock is up" and investors treat governance concerns as irrelevant "FUD."
