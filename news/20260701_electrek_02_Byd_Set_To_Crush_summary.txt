BYD delivered 557,090 fully electric vehicles in Q2 2026 per Bloomberg-compiled figures, reclaiming the global BEV sales lead over Tesla, which is expected to report approximately 396,500 deliveries for the same quarter — a gap of more than 160,000 units.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **BYD reclaims BEV crown after brief Q1 reversal:** Tesla had narrowly led BYD in BEV deliveries in Q1 2026, but that edge stemmed from a temporary dip in BYD's domestic sales after China eliminated its EV purchase-tax exemption — not from Tesla strength. Tesla delivered 358,023 vehicles in Q1 and added more than 50,000 units to inventory it could not sell.

2. **Tesla Q2 expectations ahead of July 2 report:** Tesla's official Q2 2026 delivery figures are due July 2. Wall Street consensus sits at roughly 406,000 vehicles; Bloomberg's estimate is closer to 396,500.

## Market & Competition
1. **BYD's export expansion drives the gap:** BYD's Q2 BEV figure excludes plug-in hybrids — making it a direct apples-to-apples comparison with Tesla's all-BEV lineup. BYD is expanding rapidly into Europe, Southeast Asia, and Latin America, and has told analysts it expects overseas sales to reach 1.5 million vehicles in 2026, above its official 1.3 million target. BYD has also outsold Tesla in Europe for multiple consecutive months, including in the continent's two largest EV markets.

2. **Full-year 2025 context:** In 2025, BYD delivered 2,256,714 BEVs versus Tesla's 1,636,129 — a lead of more than 620,000 units for the full year.

## Editorial Stance
Author Fred Lambert believes Tesla will beat consensus and land closer to 450,000 Q2 deliveries, citing inventory to clear, international volume, and fresh Model Y demand — but argues that even at 450,000, BYD's lead remains ~100,000 units, making the gap structural. He states Tesla's automotive growth story is effectively over, with its valuation now resting on robotaxis and robots rather than vehicle volume.
