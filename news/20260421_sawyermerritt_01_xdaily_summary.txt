## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Robotaxi Fleet Expansion Accelerating Across U.S. Cities:** Multiple posts tracked real-time fleet growth via @RtaxiTracker. Tesla added a second unsupervised Model Y robotaxi in Dallas and a second unsupervised Model Y in Houston on the same day. This incremental growth — while still small in absolute numbers — prompted Sawyer to post a running mockery of skeptics counting individual units, suggesting the fleet is growing faster than critics expected. Separately and significantly, Tesla has begun Cybercab testing in Las Vegas, marking the first known Cybercab on-road test outside of Tesla's home turf and a meaningful catalyst signal for future commercial deployment in a high-traffic, tourism-heavy market.

2. **Morgan Stanley Calls Unsupervised Robotaxi Launch "Tangible Progress":** Morgan Stanley analyst Andrew Percoco published a note on the Houston and Dallas unsupervised robotaxi launches, stating it "represents tangible progress at a time when the market was growing increasingly skeptical about Tesla's robotaxi expansion timeline." The note characterized it as a meaningful milestone. This is notable Wall Street validation arriving alongside real fleet expansion activity, reinforcing the robotaxi thesis for institutional investors who had been growing impatient with timelines.

3. **FSD International Momentum: Spain Safety Record and Netherlands Media Review:** Spain's regulatory body disclosed that Tesla currently has 30 vehicles testing on roads with FSD (Supervised) since November 2025, having driven nearly 50,000 miles with zero reported incidents. The Spanish official suggested the RDW (Dutch regulatory) decision could serve as a precedent for broader European approvals. Separately, The Independent ran a mainstream media review of FSD Supervised in the Netherlands, describing the car as behaving "impeccably" and calling the experience "unremarkable" in the best sense. Together, these posts signal growing European regulatory and public acceptance of Tesla's FSD stack.

4. **Jefferies Raises TSLA Price Target to $350:** Jefferies raised its Tesla price target from $300 to $350, citing higher mid-term growth expectations. The firm highlighted Tesla's "vertically integrated business model and ability to deliver funding and industrial scale" as unique strengths, and noted it is beginning to build autonomous driving and robotaxi assumptions into its model. No specific timeline or unit economics were disclosed in the excerpt, but the directional shift is bullish and suggests sell-side models are starting to more seriously price in the autonomous business.

5. **Tesla-PG&E V2X Approval for Cybertruck:** PG&E and Tesla announced that the Cybertruck, Tesla Powershare Gateway, and Tesla Universal Wall Connector have been approved for participation in PG&E's residential Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) program. This is a meaningful milestone for bidirectional EV integration in California, potentially opening a revenue stream for Cybertruck owners and strengthening Tesla's energy ecosystem.

6. **Tesla Semi Cost Advantage Gets Mainstream Media Coverage:** A mainstream media segment highlighted that a diesel Semi costs approximately $520 to travel ~500 miles in California (at $7.66/gallon average diesel), versus $275 for a single Tesla Semi charge covering the same distance — a roughly 47% fuel cost reduction. While not new information to Tesla followers, broader media coverage of this cost differential could accelerate fleet operator interest.

7. **Uber Takes 11.52% Stake in Lucid, Commits to 35,000 Vehicles for Robotaxi:** Uber disclosed an 11.52% stake in Lucid as part of Lucid's $300M capital raise on April 14, increasing its vehicle purchase commitment to at least 35,000 Lucid vehicles designed exclusively for Uber's future global robotaxi service. This is a relevant competitive data point — Uber is actively building a non-Tesla robotaxi supply chain, though Lucid's manufacturing scale and financial stability remain open questions.

8. **Apple CEO Transition — Tim Cook to Exit, John Ternus Named Successor:** Apple announced that John Ternus (previously SVP of Hardware Engineering) will become the next CEO, with Cook transitioning to Executive Chairman on September 1, 2026. This is tangential to Tesla directly, but relevant for investors monitoring the broader tech leadership landscape. No direct Tesla implications were stated.

9. **Optimus Hand Patents Referenced by Elon:** Elon Musk commented on recently published Optimus hand patents, though the specific content of his remarks was not captured in the post excerpt. The publication of hand-related patents signals continued R&D progress on Optimus dexterity, a key capability bottleneck for real-world deployment.
