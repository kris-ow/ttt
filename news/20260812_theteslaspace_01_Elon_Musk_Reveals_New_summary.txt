This episode covers a cluster of newly revealed Tesla/SpaceX infrastructure projects: the Terafab AI chip mega-factory in Texas, a proposed $10.1 billion Tesla solar factory filing, new Starlink constellation growth targets tied to a "multiplanetary" vehicle vision, and automation progress at the Boring Company's tunneling operations. The throughline is Musk's push toward vertically integrated, in-house manufacturing across chips, energy, and connectivity to support Optimus, robotaxis, and Mars ambitions.

## Humanoid Bots / Robotaxi / SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Terafab chip mega-factory confirmed in Texas:** Tesla and SpaceX have confirmed their jointly announced AI chip factory, Terafab, will be built in Grimes County, Texas, about an hour northwest of Houston, covering more than 100 million square feet under a single roof — roughly ten times the size of Giga Texas. The facility is designed to consolidate the entire chip supply chain (design, circuit boards, memory, packaging, testing) under one roof, targeting over 1 terawatt of AI compute per year, described as double the current annual chip consumption of the United States, to supply Optimus robots, Cybercab fleets, and SpaceX's orbital data centers.
2. **Terafab partners, jobs, and cost:** Intel has joined the Terafab project as a partner; the plant will draw water from a nearby reservoir rather than local groundwater, and filings indicate it is expected to create at least 3,000 jobs. The first phase alone represents a $16.8 billion investment, with total future phases potentially reaching as high as $119 billion — which the host says would make it the most expensive project Musk has built by a large margin.

## Energy
1. **Tesla solar factory filing ("Project Crystal Sun") surfaces in Texas:** A newly surfaced public filing shows Tesla plans for a roughly $10.1 billion vertically integrated solar factory in Fort Bend County, Texas (about 40 minutes southwest of Houston), internally called Project Crystal Sun, projected to create nearly 10,000 permanent jobs. Tesla has not committed to this site — the filing is an incentive application, and Tesla states it is still weighing this location against an alternative in another state. If built, construction would run through 2028 with operations starting in early 2029. The plan calls for full vertical integration (wafer and ingot manufacturing, coating/metallization) rather than buying finished solar cells, which the host ties to comments Musk made earlier in the year at the World Economic Forum about Tesla and SpaceX each targeting roughly 100 gigawatts per year of American-made solar power within about three years.

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **Starlink constellation growth targets and vehicle integration vision:** Elon Musk said he expects the Starlink constellation, including direct-to-cell satellites, to eventually grow beyond 100,000 satellites, and that future Starlink generations could deliver more than 50 times the bandwidth of today's version-two satellites. Musk also said he expects every car to eventually have Starlink built in, framing it as the only way to deliver very high bandwidth to billions of vehicles — the host notes this is about connectivity/data needs, not a requirement for Full Self-Driving, which runs on the car's onboard computer, and links the vision to eventual communications infrastructure for a Mars settlement.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation / Market & Competition
1. **Boring Company tunneling automation:** The Boring Company showcased its Prufrock tunneling machines operating with increasingly minimal human oversight underground, installing pre-cast concrete tunnel rings (six segments per ring, each weighing about 3,750 lbs) as it digs, with operations monitorable remotely from a control center in Texas — described by the host as reducing safety risk and enabling faster construction, paralleling Tesla's autonomous-driving push.
