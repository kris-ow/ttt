## Brief

- Houston Cybercab staging lot shows ~100-200 units
- Texas DMV: 169 Cybercabs registered, up 43 this week
- Germany pushes EU to fast-track FSD approval
- Tesla Germany registrations up 268% YoY in September
- Model 3/Y gain Powershare home backup via Powerwall 3
- Tesla crosses 1,000 India registrations in 13 months

## Robotaxi
- Houston Cybercab staging lot spotted via drone footage, with estimates ranging from ~100 (SE Robinson's drone count, per futureaza/jobhakdi) to 190–200 vehicles including Model Y robotaxis, most lacking steering wheels. Separately, Tesla's purpose-built two-seat Cybercab — no steering wheel, pedals, or mirrors — began carrying public riders in Austin, per farzad-fm.
- Texas DMV Cybercab registrations hit 169, up 43 in the past week, which Jo Bhakdi says is roughly seven times faster than Model Y robotaxi scaling at the same stage last year — his own analysis via pioneerlands.org tracking.
- Separately, Jeff Lutz estimated roughly 100 Cybercabs were added over the prior 10 days, calling recent pace the new baseline growth rate absent a specific limiting factor.
- Bhakdi maintains his own targets of ~1,800 fully deployed Cybercabs by year-end and "north of 1,000" live by December; he also expects Tesla to begin disclosing robotaxi unit economics on this quarter's earnings call or a subsequent one, calling widely circulated profit-per-vehicle estimates ($2,500–$15,000/year) "completely wrong" without offering his own figure.
- Dan Ives (Yorkville Ives) told CNBC he expects robotaxi expansion into more cities by early-to-mid 2027.

## Autonomous Driving
- EU's TCMV technical committee met October 6 in Brussels but did not vote on Tesla's FSD Supervised; a decision is now expected in December, per the Netherlands' pending Article 39 request.
- Germany's transport minister, Stefan Bilga (CDU), is reportedly pushing for EU-wide FSD approval and has suggested Germany could act unilaterally if the EU doesn't move — a stated political position, not a confirmed regulatory outcome, per reports cited across two sources.
- Oppenheimer's note argues FSD could drive both demand and margin simultaneously; Jo Bhakdi estimated (without citing a current take rate) that higher FSD attach could meaningfully lift per-vehicle margin.

## Humanoid Bots
- Dan Ives estimates Optimus rollout in the second half of 2027, following robotaxi expansion; Bhakdi separately called 2027 "the year of the humanoid" industry-wide — both framed as opinion, not confirmed Tesla guidance.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla has registered 993 vehicles in India over 13 months (Sept 2025–Sept 2026), with September alone contributing a record 294 — up sharply after an April launch of the six-seat Model Y L (₹61.99 lakh) and a 15% price cut to the entry Model Y (to ₹50.89 lakh). Tesla remains India's #2 luxury EV brand (~30% share) but holds just 0.8% of the broader EV market; India will not get a Tesla factory, per the government.
- Tesla Germany registrations rose 268% year-over-year in September to 12,552 units, its best month since December 2022; Giga Berlin output is reportedly rising 20% to 7,500 vehicles/week starting this month, per Sawyer Merritt.
- Q3 deliveries beat Wall Street estimates but were down ~2% year-over-year, which futureaza's hosts attribute to a tough comp against last year's tax-credit-driven surge.

## Energy
- Tesla enabled Powershare Home Backup for Model 3 and Model Y, requiring a Powerwall 3 and guaranteed only on vehicles ordered on/after October 1, 2026; existing owners must check for a "Powershare Support" flag. Tesla cites "over two days" of backup for Model 3/Y vs. "more than three days" for Cybertruck, with 11.52 kW max output; no grid-selling, no Model S/X support, US/Puerto Rico only. Electrek's Fred Lambert frames it as catch-up to Ford and GM's existing vehicle-to-home offerings.
- HSBC maintained a sell rating on Tesla despite the delivery beat, citing energy storage weakness — which futureaza's hosts dispute, noting energy had its second-best quarter ever.

## Financials
- Tesla reports Q3 earnings October 21; Bhakdi expects a margin beat on low discounting and higher FSD take rates, with possible upside from new FSD or robotaxi disclosures.
- UBS (neutral, $385 PT) and Goldman Sachs (neutral, $360 PT) both flagged the delivery beat positively but cited energy misses (UBS) and capex/cost pressure on 2026 EPS (Goldman); Goldman said robotaxi, FSD, and humanoid progress matter more than Q3 results for the stock.
- Elon Musk's net worth rose to $1.02 trillion, up $41.5 billion in a day, as both TSLA and SpaceX shares rallied.

## Market & Competition
- Dan Ives put SpaceX-Tesla merger odds above 80% by end of 2027; Bhakdi said he'd go "a little higher." Bhakdi separately argued a merger "of equals" without a premium is unlikely to win shareholder approval.
- Gas-only vehicles fell below 50% of global auto sales for the first time, per H1 2026 data (gas sales down 10% YoY to 20.25 million units).
- Rivian deliveries rose 46% YoY against Tesla's 2% decline; GM is reintroducing hybrids and targets hands-free driving on the Escalade IQ by 2028; Rivian delayed autonomous ride-hailing to add lidar.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- A ~328 million-share SpaceX unlock is expected in roughly three days; Bhakdi describes himself as "strategically bullish, tactically bearish" on SpaceX short-term and has sold calls as a tactical hedge, while recommending a Tesla-heavy allocation over the next couple months.
- Bhakdi projects (his own unverified model) SpaceX could reach $300B revenue and $200B profit next year, implying a ~$4 trillion valuation, and separately floated a $10 trillion valuation within three to four years — neither is company guidance.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX conducted three Falcon launches in one day including a Falcon Heavy flight with all five boosters recovered, plus a Starship test involving satellite deployment, per futureaza.
- Bhakdi's unverified model projects 152 Starship flights by 2030, necessitating a new Louisiana launch site, with annual revenue surpassing $1 trillion by 2033 — framed explicitly as his own unfinished projection.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Bhakdi argues Starlink is comparatively low-profit versus a hypothetical orbital compute satellite business ("Starmine"), estimating 4,000 compute satellites could generate ~$50B revenue/~$35B profit versus Starlink's current ~10,000-satellite constellation — a forward-looking hypothesis, not a reported result.
- SpaceX introduced a 50% Starlink Residential discount in Cameron County, Texas, near SpaceX HQ, with no upfront hardware cost.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Musk is reportedly repositioning SpaceX/xAI messaging from "AI" toward "super intelligence" (SI), including floating a "SpaceX SI" name change.
- TSMC is reportedly in early discussions with Musk's Terafab chip venture; Intel signed on in April (14A process), and ASML has incorporated Terafab into 2027–2028 planning, per Jeff Lutz. Terafab's stated target is one terawatt of compute production per year, ~50x current global AI chip output.
