## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla Robotaxi fleet expansion and near-zero wait times:** Sawyer Merritt reported that more Robotaxis have been added to Tesla's fleet in Dallas (no specific new count given), and separately noted that Cybercab wait times are "basically zero right now," pointing to expanding service availability. A post also highlighted "Three Unsupervised Teslas" via video, consistent with continued unsupervised/driverless ride visibility, though no new fleet totals were disclosed beyond the stale RobotaxiTracker.com baseline (Active fleet: 839 as of 2026-08-07; Unsupervised fleet: 48 as of 2026-07-30, both ~2+ months stale).

2. **Model 3 Premium order pause in Canada:** Tesla has stopped taking new orders for the Model 3 Premium in Canada after exhausting its government-allotted import quota due to high demand. Ordering is expected to resume once Canada issues additional quota, reportedly expected in March 2027. This points to robust Canadian EV demand outstripping supply allowances rather than a demand problem.

3. **SpaceX launches unified "X Subscription" bundling Grok, X, and Cursor:** SpaceX (via X/xAI) introduced a new subscription combining Grok, X, and Cursor access into one plan with four tiers: Plus ($8/mo or $80/yr), Premium ($30/mo or $300/yr), Super ($100/mo or $1,000/yr), and Ultra ($200/mo). Existing subscribers to Cursor, Grok, or X can consolidate into the new plan with prorated refunds for unused time on cancelled plans. This is a product/monetization development for X/xAI rather than core SpaceX launch/Starlink business, but relevant to the broader Musk-ecosystem AI and compute strategy. Elon Musk also commented on a possible renaming of "SpaceXAI" to "SpaceXSI," though no further detail on the rationale was given.

4. **Grok integration in Cybercab:** Riders can now use Grok's "Bot" assistant inside a Tesla Cybercab, signing in once with Bots carrying over to future rides and auto-logging out on exit for privacy. Merritt demonstrated the feature by using it to add an item to an Amazon cart during a ride, illustrating an early in-vehicle AI commerce use case tied to the robotaxi experience.

5. **Competitor EV launch — Smart's new electric city car:** A new all-electric Smart car was unveiled with a starting price of €20,000, up to 290 km (180 miles) WLTP range, a 35.8 kWh LFP battery, RWD, 80 hp, peak DC charging at 65 kW, and up to 11 kW AC charging (10–80% in 3 hours), plus V2L capability. This is a low-cost European competitor entrant relevant to Tesla's Market & Competition positioning in the affordable EV segment, though no sales or market-share data was given.
