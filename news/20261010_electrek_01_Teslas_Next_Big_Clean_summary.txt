ContourGlobal has broken ground on Project Sterling, a 450 MW solar-plus-battery storage facility in Arizona, with Tesla having contracted for roughly 90% of its expected annual electricity output under a long-term power purchase agreement.

## Energy
1. **Project scope and timeline:** ContourGlobal held a groundbreaking ceremony on October 6 for Project Sterling, located about 20 miles north of Lake Havasu City in Mohave County, Arizona, covering more than 2,000 acres in the Mojave Desert. The project is expected to come online in 2028 and will be ContourGlobal's largest renewable energy asset worldwide.

2. **Technical specifications:** Sterling will combine more than 760,000 solar panels (509 MW DC / 450 MW AC capacity) with over 300 lithium iron phosphate battery storage containers using liquid cooling and integrated fire protection. The batteries can deliver up to 360 MW and store about 1.4 gigawatt-hours, enough for roughly four hours of full-power discharge; ContourGlobal says the combined system can deliver clean electricity up to 16 hours a day.

3. **Tesla's power purchase agreement:** The facility is expected to generate more than 1.1 terawatt-hours annually, and Tesla's long-term PPA covers around 1 TWh per year plus associated renewable energy certificates — about 90% of the plant's expected annual output.

4. **Grid connectivity and financing:** Sterling will connect to the Western Area Power Administration's grid and has secured transmission rights into the California Independent System Operator's market. The privately financed project has secured an equity bridge loan from a group of international banks, and construction is expected to employ around 400 workers through 2028, including building a new 5.2-mile access road connecting to Route 66.
