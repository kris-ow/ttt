The article reports that battery-electric vehicles outsold petrol cars in Australia for the first time in August, with Chinese automakers dominating the EV sales leaderboard and Tesla's Shanghai-built models rounding out the top eight.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Australia's EV market hits a milestone:** BEVs reached 27,078 sales (24.9% of the new-car market) in August, outselling both petrol (25,824) and diesel (23,608) for the first time, per VFACTS and Electric Vehicle Council data cited in the article. BEV sales rose 171% year-over-year; including hybrids and plug-in hybrids, electrified vehicles took 51.8% of the market — the first month combustion-only cars were a minority in Australia. The overall market grew 4.9% to 108,760 vehicles.

2. **Chinese brands dominate the top sellers:** Seven of the eight best-selling EV models were Chinese brands (BYD, Geely, Zeekr, Chery's Jaecoo); the BYD Sealion 7 sold 2,213 units, finishing sixth across the entire market, and the Geely EX5 grew over 385% year-over-year. The remaining two top sellers were Tesla's Model Y and Model 3, both built at Giga Shanghai, meaning all eight top-selling EVs in Australia were produced in China. The article notes this follows a similar pattern it flagged in July, when seven of the top sellers were already Chinese.

3. **Tesla's monthly performance vs. BYD:** The Model Y sold 6,414 units in August (up 176% year-over-year), Tesla's third monthly sales win of 2026; as a brand, Tesla ranked third overall with 7,685 sales (up 163%). BYD placed second with 8,231 sales (up 68.8%), achieved with a broader lineup than Tesla's two-model range. BYD had outsold Tesla 10-to-1 in Australia back in January, a gap the article says has narrowed as Tesla recovered, though BYD remains ahead. Toyota led all brands with 19,712 sales, down 5.2%, with Toyota, Mazda, and Kia all described as declining while Chinese EV makers post double- and triple-digit growth.

## Editorial Stance
Author Fred Lambert frames this as fundamentally a Chinese EV boom rather than a Tesla or Western-brand story, since all top sellers (including Tesla) are China-built. He attributes Australia's rapid EV adoption to its near-absence of tariffs on Chinese EVs (unlike the US or EU), calling Australia "the clearest preview anywhere of what an open, tariff-free EV market actually produces" and arguing this accelerates combustion engine phase-out.
