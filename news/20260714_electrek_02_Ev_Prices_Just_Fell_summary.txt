The article reports June 2026 Kelley Blue Book data showing new EV average transaction prices fell year over year for the sixth straight month, with Tesla prices also down slightly but less than the broader EV market.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **EV prices decline broadly:** The average transaction price (ATP) for a new EV was $56,238 in June, down 4.5% year over year (sixth consecutive month of annual declines), though up slightly from May. By comparison, the overall new-vehicle ATP (EVs and ICE combined) was $49,758, up 0.6% year over year and 0.4% higher than May.
2. **Tesla pricing mixed:** Tesla's average transaction price rose to $53,107 in June but was still 2.1% lower than a year earlier — the smallest year-over-year decline Tesla has seen so far in 2026. The Model 3's average selling price saw a modest increase year over year, while the Model Y's ATP fell 2.7% to $51,775; the Model Y accounts for more than 35% of all US EV sales.
3. **Incentives remain elevated:** EV incentives averaged 13% of transaction price in June, down from 14% in May but still well above the 7% industry-wide average, as automakers continue offering discounts and financing deals since federal EV tax credits ended.
4. **Sales momentum:** Early estimates suggest EV sales slowed in June compared to May, which had been the strongest sales month since federal tax credits were eliminated at the end of Q3 2025.
