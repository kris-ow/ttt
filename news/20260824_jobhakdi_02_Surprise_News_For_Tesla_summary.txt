## Robotaxi
1. **Brandenburg exploring Tesla autonomous shuttles for rural transit:** Per a German news report the host translates on-air, Brandenburg's transport minister Robert Kumbach (Social Democrats) confirmed the ministry is in talks with Tesla about integrating driverless autonomous shuttles into the region's existing on-demand rural bus system, citing routes with low ridership, buses running as little as twice daily, and driver shortages. Kumbach reportedly said that once legal approvals are in place, he could envision a comprehensive rollout of autonomous shuttles across rural Brandenburg. Brandenburg is said to be modeling its approach on an existing pilot trial involving a self-driving Tesla in Germany's Eifel region.
2. **Host's rural TAM estimate — roughly 2 million additional robotaxis:** Using back-of-envelope math (not cross-checked against outside data), the host estimates ~50 million underserved rural US residents and ~120 million in Europe (170 million combined), and assumes roughly one robotaxi trip per resident per week.
