## Robotaxi
1. **Widening capability-deployment gap drives investor anger:** The host frames rising Robotaxi technical capability/reliability (referencing a chart from Cern Basher) against slower fleet deployment scaling as the source of investor frustration — arguing the gap is widening not because deployment is failing, but because capability is improving faster than rollout, which he considers fundamentally bullish.
2. **Robotaxi infrastructure is not a bottleneck:** The host reiterates his view that Robotaxi hub infrastructure (three to six hubs across major Texas cities) is easy for Tesla to build quickly, drawing a comparison to the rapid construction of SpaceX's Colossus; he maintains the only real bottleneck is FSD software performance.
3. **Robotaxi economics favor owning the fleet:** Responding to a viewer question about the best Robotaxi business model, the host says his analysis shows Tesla makes the most money by operating and owning the fleet itself rather than a franchise or rental model.
4. **Cantor Fitzgerald reiterates bullish Robotaxi thesis:** Cantor Fitzgerald issued an overweight rating with a $510 price target ahead of Q2 earnings, citing the Robotaxi venture and an upcoming software platform as a lucrative transportation-as-a-service opportunity; the host says he agrees and considers Morgan Stanley's estimate of 30,000 Robotaxis/Cybercabs deployed by 2030 too low.
5. **Independent analyst flags slow city expansion:** The host cites commentary from Dillon Loomis (a third-party analyst, described as turning more critical) noting that new city unlocks (Dallas, Houston) were followed roughly 94 days later by only 5–10 active vehicles in those markets, and expressing skepticism about how much of Tesla's/Musk's messaging to trust.
6. **High confidence in scale within 12 months:** The host says he's "extremely sure" the odds of not seeing Robotaxis at scale within the next 12 months are very slim, predicting thousands to tens of thousands of vehicles deployed by around this time next year.
