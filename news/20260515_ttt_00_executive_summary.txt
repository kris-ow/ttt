## Autonomous Driving

- **17 NHTSA crash reports unredacted.** Tesla was the only ADS operator to have fully redacted its crash narratives under NHTSA's Standing General Order, citing potential "financial harm" from competitor analysis. It has now removed the confidential designation, covering all incidents from July 2025 through March 2026 in Austin.
- **Safety profile is broadly favorable, with notable exceptions.** Of 17 incidents, 7 occurred while the Tesla was stationary (not at fault). Thirteen resulted in property damage only; one required hospitalization. Per Electrek, the most serious injury occurred when the ADS was rear-ended by an SUV while creeping at 2 mph in a slip lane. April 2026 added 2 new incidents, both at 0 mph, with 0 new injuries. Tesla's robotaxi service is approaching approximately 2 million autonomous miles driven.
- **Teleoperator failures are a documented, systemic concern.** Two crashes were caused by remote operators taking control. In July 2025, an operator drove the vehicle up a curb and into a metal fence at 8 mph, causing a minor injury. In January 2026, an operator drove a stopped vehicle into a construction barricade at 9 mph. Electrified's Dillon Loomis flags these alongside connectivity issues as a genuine barrier to scaling.
- **ADS spatial awareness failures cluster around small, thin objects.** Incidents include striking a metal chain entering a parking lot, clipping a trailer's gooseneck hitch protruding into the street, reversing into a wooden electrical pole at 1 mph, and hitting a curb while reversing. Electrek's Fred Lambert notes ultrasonics and radar may have prevented some of these.
- **FSD software release is gating unsupervised fleet expansion.** Per the brighterwithherbert panel, the unsupervised fleet has plateaued because Tesla holds fleet size at a ceiling until a new FSD release is validated to meet performance thresholds. The next meaningful expansion is expected to coincide with the next validated release.
- **Tesla posts FSD data labeling role in China.** The listing calls for labeling images and video from the fleet and from Optimus data collectors to feed Tesla's deep learning network — interpreted by Dillon Loomis as Tesla building a localized FSD training dataset, which he identifies as the primary limiter on supervised FSD performance in China to date.
- **FSD pilot active in rural Germany.** A program in the Eifel region is using Tesla vehicles running FSD (Supervised) to transport students and senior citizens in communities with limited public transit. A passenger quoted: "I feel safer than if I were driving." This is supervised, not driverless, but represents a structured mobility service deployment in a European regulatory context.

## Robotaxi

- **Las Vegas Cybercab maintenance facility: permit filed May 12.** Tesla filed plans with Clark County, Nevada for a "Tesla Center Cybercab Phase 2 Car Wash" — approximately 36,000 sq ft with roughly 55 parking spots, involving a car wash enclosure, tire service equipment relocation, and power raceway installation. The "Phase 2" designation implies concurrent Phase 1 work. Dillon Loomis states Tesla is targeting a Las Vegas robotaxi launch "hopefully sometime in the next 45 days."
- **Austin operational gap after 5 PM.** Rider Ethan McConno reported that while unsupervised rides in Austin officially stop around 5:00 PM on weekdays, supervised cars are also sparse after that time — effectively only 1–2 unmarked monitor cars operating — producing 20–30 minute waits. Dillon Loomis flags this as a product and operational limitation contradicting Tesla's stated operating hours.
- **Driving quality praised; product bugs remain a barrier.** McConno, after stress-testing Austin service over several days, called driving performance "incredibly impressive" in complex traffic but catalogued a "long list of important product issues": bad pickup/drop-off locations, broken audio, connectivity issues, and UI/UX problems.
- **Houston and Dallas geofences remain very small.** Dillon Loomis pushes back on near-term scaling expectations, noting Houston's zone excludes downtown in favor of a more rural area. He states he is "very confident" anyone expecting 1,000 robotaxis across Austin, Dallas, and Houston will be disappointed absent significant geofence expansion.
- **Cybercab spotted with Starlink Mini unit mounted on the back.** Reported as the first known public sighting of this configuration per Sawyer Merritt. Dillon Loomis treats it as a testing observation only — not confirmation of production integration — and clarifies that equipment currently visible on Model Y robotaxis is telecom/GPS hardware for remote operations.
- **~70 Cybercabs spotted in Giga Texas outbound lot.** Up from prior sightings of 30–40 units, per Joe Tegtmeyer. Most appear to be the same units observed the prior day, meaning they sit for multiple days before being moved — so the figure does not represent a single day's output. The brighterwithherbert panel interprets this as evidence of a ramping production line.
- **Waymo issued a recall for 3,800 vehicles** due to software allowing robotaxis to drive onto flooded roadways. San Antonio service remains suspended after a vehicle drove into a flooded road and was swept into a creek (no passengers aboard). Waymo's CPUC permit expansion to Oakland is separately suspended through June 27, 2026, pending further staff review.

## Humanoid Bots

- **Optimus V3 reportedly signed off.** Randy Kirk of FutureAzA references an unverified but trusted source on X stating the Optimus V3 design has been finalized — meaning it has met Elon Musk's standards. This is not an official announcement. Randy frames four remaining milestones to watch: production start, factory ramp at Fremont, pricing/billing model announcement, and a real-world capability demonstration.
- **Optimus production line replacing Model S/X at Giga Texas.** Per the brighterwithherbert panel, Tesla has stopped production on the Model S/X line and is laying the foundation for an Optimus production line at Giga Texas targeting capacity for one million robots. Tesla is also hiring data collection operators for Optimus across approximately 12 offices and factories, consistent with Elon's stated goal of ~5,000 Optimus units training in Tesla facilities. Robots are reportedly being stress-tested carrying 30 pounds continuously.
- **No Wall Street analyst has yet modeled Optimus revenue.** Piper Sandler's Alex Potter explicitly excludes Optimus from his Tesla model, leading his note to conclude investors are "getting Optimus for free" at $400/share. The brighterwithherbert panel notes ARK Invest is reportedly working on Optimus research and may add it to their model following a robotaxi inflection point.
- **Figure AI's F.04 design locked; debut expected fall 2026.** CEO Brett Adcock confirmed the next-generation robot has completed design lock, described as the biggest performance leap between versions to date. Figure's live-stream endurance test passed 50 hours with four robots sorting over 63,000 packages. On hardware, Figure's Gen 4 hands embed all actuators in the hand itself — the opposite of Tesla's originally patented tendon-driven forearm approach. Elon Musk has reportedly indicated Tesla has changed course on that hand design, making Optimus hand architecture a key area to watch. Figure is also nearly fully localized away from China in its supply chain, expected complete by Q3 2026.

## Financials

- **Tesla Q1 2026 profit: ~$63 million per day.** Per a profit attribution chart presented by James (Invest Answers): ~62.8% automotive, ~17.5% energy storage, ~6.9% FSD subscriptions, ~6.9% regulatory credits, with a small but growing slice from robotaxi/services/charging. The panel emphasized this is a profit chart, not revenue, explaining why high-margin lines punch above their weight.
- **Piper Sandler (Alex Potter) price target: $500/share.** His model includes FSD licensing, automaker hardware purchases, and robotaxi revenue, but with dates pushed to 2030 and beyond. FSD subscription revenue projected at $900M (2027), $1.8B (2028), $4.1B shortly after, with continued doubling through 2031. Megapack revenue modeled at $9.7B in 2026, not exceeding $12B until 2030 — a figure the brighterwithherbert panel found inconsistent with company guidance. Tesla insurance modeled at $26B by 2035 and ~$300B by 2045. The panel noted Potter will appear on their show within approximately two weeks.
- **Jo Bhakdi revises 2030 base case price target to $4,000** (down from $6,000), citing increased caution on robotaxi and Optimus scaling timelines. He adds that a 10x outcome is possible "if you see millions of robotaxis and a million humanoids in 2030."
- **Tesla Semi: close to 1,000 orders** at approximately $300,000 per unit, with some states offering up to $150,000 in tax credits. Per James's "significant revenue" timeline (defined as ~$3–5B), Semi is already ramping; FSD significant by early 2027; robotaxi toward end of 2028; humanoid robots and Digital Optimus by late 2028–2029.

## Electric Vehicles

- **Panasonic raises US EV battery sales forecast to 46 GWh (+19%)** for its fiscal year ending March 2027, explicitly citing Tesla's market share recovery despite significant model lineup revisions. Panasonic projects broader US EV market sales roughly flat year-over-year, implying Tesla outperforms the market. Panasonic has direct visibility into Tesla's business through the Giga Nevada partnership.
- **Tesla China officially denied reports of a Model Y price increase.** Rising global DRAM prices since September 2025 have pushed up ADAS system costs — NIO's CEO estimates rising memory costs could add 3,000–5,000 yuan (~up to $750) per vehicle. BYD has already raised the price of its lidar-assisted driving package. Dillon Loomis assesses the margin impact on Tesla as likely minor; Tesla is reportedly working to bring memory production in-house.
- **Honda posted its worst financial results since listing in 1957**, booking more than $9 billion in restructuring costs for its EV business. It is abandoning its 2040 full-EV/fuel cell transition goal, scrapping its 20%-EV-by-2030 target, and indefinitely suspending an $11 billion EV and battery manufacturing investment in Canada.
- **Tesla opened its fourth India showroom** in Bengaluru (Karnataka), continuing geographic expansion into a major growth market.
- **Tesla Semi pilot at Covenant reported positively.** VP of Sustainability Matt Mullen cited the trip planner's ability to account for distance, traffic, wind, temperature, weather, grade, and driver inputs. Economics of the pilot to be shared soon.
