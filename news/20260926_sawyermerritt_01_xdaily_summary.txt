## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla Semi launch: production, deliveries, and new tech unveiled:** Tesla began hand-delivering the first Semi units to customers including PepsiCo, DHL, and US Foods. The company unveiled "Megamanifold," a next-generation heat pump that runs cabin HVAC and powertrain thermal management simultaneously, using excess powertrain heat to warm the cabin. Per Lars (Moravy), the Semi can travel over 600 miles on a single charge at half load, or 500 miles at full 82k lb gross combination weight. Tesla stated it will have over 30 Megacharging stations online by end of 2026, representing 200+ megawatts of charging capacity, with additional private chargers planned. The Semi production line reportedly has installed capacity of 50,000 units/year. Elon Musk was shown in a video calling the Semi "almost like a sports car in truck form," saying the waitlist is "already pretty significant" (no specific waitlist number given). Motortrend's review after driving the Semi called it "surprisingly familiar, shockingly easy" with strong lane-centering and visibility. The Semi was also added to Tesla's homepage for the first time.

2. **Cybercab robotaxi fleet registrations accelerate in Texas:** Tesla registered 56 new Cybercabs in a single day (per Texas DMV data cited), described as the largest single-day registration total to date, bringing the cumulative Cybercab registration count to 125 — surpassing 100 for the first time. An earlier post the same day noted an interim total of 88 after 19 additional registrations. These are DMV vehicle registrations, not confirmed active/deployed robotaxi fleet additions — the figures track manufacturing/registration pace rather than operational deployment.

3. **Next-gen Tesla Roadster teaser hints at SpaceX tie-in:** Tesla released a teaser for the next-generation Roadster showing a front light bar design; one teaser image reportedly shows part of the SpaceX logo, fueling speculation about a SpaceX-related package or component (unconfirmed from the post itself).

4. **EU delays FSD (Supervised) vote:** The European Union pushed back its vote on Tesla's FSD (Supervised) approval from an original October 6 date to December at the earliest, per an agenda published on the EU's website — a regulatory delay for European FSD rollout timing.

5. **SpaceX's Colossus 2 AI compute buildout and Megapack grid battery:** SpaceX launched a new website detailing its Colossus 2 AI training cluster spanning Tennessee/Mississippi. Key disclosed figures: Tesla Megapacks will supply 3.3 GWh of storage to Colossus 2, described as enough to power Memphis for two hours and billed as America's largest grid-connected battery installation. Separate posts describe SpaceX bringing online 220,000 Nvidia GB300 GPUs next week (valued at over $15 billion), with another ~$15 billion tranche expected in November and a potential further $15 billion in December — for a possible total of 660,000 GB300 GPUs online by the end of Q4, representing upwards of $50 billion in GPU spend for the quarter (poster's figures; total GPU value across the buildout described elsewhere as "over $100 billion," "over 2 GW of compute" — these are the poster's own characterizations, not independently verified).
