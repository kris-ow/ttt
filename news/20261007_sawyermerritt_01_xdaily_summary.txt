## Executive Summary
Today's digest is light on hard financial news but offers meaningful product and regulatory signal: Tesla rolled out a Halloween-themed UI/FSD update and a new Powershare Home Backup energy feature for Model 3/Y, while Germany's Transport Minister publicly pushed for EU-wide approval of Tesla's FSD (Supervised). On the SpaceX side, the standout item is video of the first operational Starlink V3 satellites deployed from Starship, plus new detail on SpaceX's planned "Starmind" AI satellite constellation and a dedicated Space Safety website.

---

## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla Halloween Mode rolls out across UI and FSD:** Tesla launched a seasonal "Halloween Mode" with a ghost costume avatar that animates when doors open, spooky sounds and light flickering for trick-or-treaters ("Trick or Treat mode"), a haunting light show, wraps, lock sounds, and a spooky voice mode. Notably, the theming extends into the driving interface itself — pedestrians render as skeletons, surrounding cars turn green, and the FSD path turns purple — which Sawyer Merritt contrasts favorably with other automakers' Halloween modes that don't extend to the driving UI.

2. **Powershare Home Backup launched for Model 3/Y with Powerwall 3:** Tesla officially introduced Powershare Home Backup for new Model 3 and Model Y vehicles ordered October 1, 2026 or later, paired with Powerwall 3. The feature can extend home backup duration by "over 2 days," and for homes with Powerwall 3 and a Wall Connector, no additional hardware is needed. Per the post, every Model 3/Y Premium and Performance trim carries battery capacity equivalent to nearly 6 Powerwalls, while Cybertruck is equivalent to about 9; even the base 2027 Model 3/Y get this capability — linking EV battery packs directly to home energy resilience, a cross-sell between the EV and Energy businesses.

3. **Germany's Transport Minister backs EU-wide FSD approval:** Germany's Transport Minister said he wants Tesla's FSD (Supervised) approved throughout the EU soon, citing innovation and traffic-safety benefits as paramount, and said reaching an EU-level agreement would be sensible — with national-level action as a fallback if EU consensus isn't reached. This is a notable regulatory tailwind signal from a major EU government official, though it remains a stated preference, not a confirmed approval.

4. **FSD consumer reception and trucking anecdotes:** A family-vehicle reviewer ("Kelly from The Car Mom") posted enthusiastic first-person reaction to FSD in the new Model Y L, calling it "the most unbelievable technology" she's experienced in a car — cited by Merritt as evidence of FSD going mainstream. Separately, a million-mile trucker commented after touring the Tesla Semi factory following its recent launch event, saying the experience "doesn't feel like trucking" due to the technology level. Both are anecdotal/consumer-sentiment data points, not quantitative evidence.

5. **Tesla Model Y sales strength in South Korea:** The Model Y was the #1 best-selling car (EV or gas) in South Korea for the third consecutive month in September, with Tesla registering 12,372 vehicles — up 36% YoY — leading the import market for the eighth consecutive month.

6. **Robotaxi fleet visual in Houston:** A post shows what's described as "a couple hundred Cybercabs" in Houston, Texas. Note: this figure is materially higher than RobotaxiTracker.com's last-known Houston breakdown (25 active, 6 unsupervised, as of August 2026), and the post gives no sourcing or verification for the number — it should be treated as an unverified visual claim, not a confirmed fleet count.

7. **SpaceX: first Starlink V3 satellites deployed from Starship:** SpaceX released video of the first operational Starlink V3 satellites being deployed into orbit directly from Starship, described by Merritt as "one of the best shots they've ever gotten" — a notable operational milestone combining the Starship and Starlink theses.

8. **SpaceX unveils Starmind AI satellite constellation concept and Space Safety site:** SpaceX published a new visualization of a planned constellation of up to 1 million "Starmind" AI satellites (illustrative dot placement, not true satellite scale), alongside plans for up to 100,000 Starlink satellites, framed around meeting "growing demand for broadband and supercompute." SpaceX VP Michael Nicolls said the satellites need to "operate in tight formation to get enough coherent compute." This came alongside a new SpaceX Space Safety website addressing safe operation at this scale. These are large-scale stated plans/aspirations, not confirmed deployment numbers or timelines.

9. **Competitive note — Waymo Ojai refresh:** Waymo unveiled a new silver version of its Ojai robotaxi with interior upgrades (new seats, rear armrests, leatherette seating, carpeted flooring, wireless chargers, matching seatbelts, high-contrast trim). Waymo stated it is "ramping" production/deployment, though no specific fleet or unit numbers were given in the post.
