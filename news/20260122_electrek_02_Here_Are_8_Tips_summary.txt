This Electrek article compiles general winter EV driving tips from two industry experts (Richard Reina of CARiD.com and Casey Donahue of Optiwatt), aimed at helping any EV owner maximize range and performance in cold weather. It is not specific to Tesla vehicles or Tesla as a company.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Winter range tips:** The experts recommend parking in a garage (even unheated, as a few degrees of warmth helps retain charge), scheduling departure times to allow preconditioning and full charging, preconditioning before charging (since a cold battery drains faster and impairs regenerative braking), and fully charging overnight at home.
2. **Driving and efficiency habits:** Using Eco Mode, reducing use of power-hungry features like the heating system (heated seats use less power), maintaining proper tire inflation, avoiding rapid acceleration, using cruise control, and driving to maximize regenerative braking (slow acceleration/braking, anticipating stops) all help conserve battery range.
3. **Public charging and emergency prep:** Public charging stations can get crowded in winter, especially around holidays, so planning routes to larger stations in advance is advised. For longer trips or storms, experts recommend locating charging stations ahead of time, keeping phones charged, and carrying warm clothes, blankets, water, food, and a shovel in case of being stranded; also charging to full before severe weather due to outage risk.
4. **Editorial note:** Electrek's editor notes it's reasonable to expect roughly a 20-30% range reduction in sub-freezing temperatures and advises planning trips accordingly.
