Electrek reports on a private Colorado seller listing a brand-new 2027 Rivian R2 Performance for $79,900 — about $20,400 (34%) over its $59,485 delivered price — despite Rivian's short order-to-delivery times, and argues the flip is unlikely to succeed.

## Market & Competition
1. **Rivian R2 flip listing details:** A Launch Edition R2 Performance with 50 miles on the odometer was listed on Cars.com on July 1 for $79,900, versus a $57,990 base price ($59,485 with destination fee) for the same configuration Rivian began shipping first; the listing claims "no test drives" and that the car "was intended as a present but it is no longer needed."
2. **R2 supply and demand context:** Rivian began R2 customer deliveries on June 9, with current order-to-delivery times of two to six weeks; the company targets roughly 20,000–25,000 R2 deliveries this year and raised its 2026 forecast after demand came in ahead of expectations.
3. **Comparison to Cybertruck flipping:** The article notes that early Tesla Cybertruck flippers who asked six figures over sticker mostly had to relist at a loss once Tesla's anti-flipping resale clause and steady production eliminated the resale premium; Rivian reportedly has no equivalent public resale restriction on the R2.

## Editorial Stance
Electrek's Fred Lambert argues the $20,000 markup misreads the R2 as a scarce product when it's actually Rivian's high-volume, attainable model, and predicts the listing will be relisted lower or disappear within a month, calling strong-but-not-scarce demand "a much healthier place for Rivian to be."
