This short update covers SpaceX's earnings-report claim that its 14th Starship flight — which reached orbit for the first time and deployed Starlink V3 satellites — delivered roughly ten times the Starlink throughput of a single Falcon 9 launch, framing Starship as the key driver of future Starlink network growth.

## SpaceX — Starship
1. **Starship reaches orbit, delivers V3 satellites:** On its 14th flight, Starship reached orbit for the first time and delivered 26 Starlink V3 satellites, which SpaceX describes as the first profitable (paid) Starship mission.
2. **10x throughput claim vs. Falcon 9:** SpaceX stated in its earnings report that this single Starship/Starlink flight provided about ten times more throughput than a Falcon 9 Starlink launch, attributed to the larger size of the V3 satellite and Starship's greater mass-to-orbit capacity per flight.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
1. **Quarterly launch cadence:** SpaceX conducted 35 rocket launches last quarter, of which 23 were SpaceX's own missions (as opposed to customer payloads), with most of those carrying Starlink satellites.

## SpaceX — Starlink
