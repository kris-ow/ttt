## Energy
1. **Solar lease document replaced by ancient text:** A Tesla solar customer, who asked to remain anonymous, went to check their decade-long lease agreement in their Tesla account and instead found the Book of Enoch, a roughly 2,000-year-old text, in place of the contract. 2. **Context: Tesla's solar leasing relaunch:** The incident comes as Tesla is trying to rebuild its home solar business, having brought back solar leasing in late 2025 after discontinuing its solar subscription program in 2021.
3. **Possible causes (speculative):** The article speculates, without confirmation from Tesla, on possible explanations: an AI/automation pipeline error (e.g., a hallucinated retrieval or mis-mapped file, given the text's public-domain availability), a backend document ID or filename mix-up, or possibly an intentional joke by an employee.
