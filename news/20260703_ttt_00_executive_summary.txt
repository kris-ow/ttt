## Brief

- Q2 deliveries hit 480,126, best Q2 ever, ~74K beat vs consensus
- Tesla stock fell ~7-8% despite the beat, cause disputed
- Model Y L launches in US at $61,990-$61K, September deliveries
- First production Cybercab begins public-road testing in Austin
- Robotaxi service launches unsupervised in Miami

- Moravy teases Giga Texas "scaling" announcement for July 7

## Robotaxi
- Tesla launched unsupervised Robotaxi rides in Miami, though the published geofence is narrow — covering only West Miami, Doral, and Sweetwater, excluding downtown, Miami Beach, the airport, and most of Coral Gables (per Electrek). Houston also added to the network. Active fleet: 752 per RobotaxiTracker.com (Bay Area 620, Austin 105, Dallas 16, Houston 11).
- First production Cybercab began engineering testing on Austin public roads with no steering wheel or pedals and an occupant not touching manual controls; Moravy reportedly put Cybercab automation readiness at 90%.
- Electrek disputes bullish framing, citing city officials estimating Austin's fleet at only ~50 vehicles with the unsupervised subset down to roughly 14 active cars from a ~25 peak, wait times routinely exceeding 15 minutes, and no cars available in over a quarter of checks.
- Moravy teased a Giga Texas "scaling" announcement expected around July 7, which multiple hosts interpreted as Cybercab-related though content is unconfirmed.
- Waymo dropped its Uber app partnership in Phoenix after nearly three years, pushing riders to its own app.

## Autonomous Driving
- Harris County prosecutors charged Tesla driver Michael David Butler, 44, with manslaughter over a June 19 Katy crash that killed Martha Avila. The complaint says Butler twice overrode FSD's speed and pressed the pedal to the floor for six seconds, reaching ~73 mph, and had Googled "FSD is not aggressive enough" in prior weeks. He faces 2-20 years and $150,000 bond.
- FSD "V14 Lite" is rolling out to Hardware 3 vehicles to narrow the gap with AI4, though it lacks Mad Max mode and other newer features.
- A German parliamentary state secretary response raised the possibility of surprise FSD approval in Germany "this month," per Jo Bhakdi; separately, a commenter cited Swedish authorities recommending the EU block FSD rollout over speed-setting concerns.
- Musk has said safety validation, not mapping, is the bottleneck for Robotaxi scaling, with Tesla awaiting a rewritten FSD v15; independent analysis cited a crash rate ~4x the human average in Austin.

## Electric Vehicles
- Q2 deliveries: 480,126 vehicles, +25% YoY, Tesla's best Q2 ever and 4th-best quarter overall, beating consensus of ~406,000 by ~74,000 units; production was 451,758. Tesla is on track to deliver its 10-millionth cumulative vehicle in Q3 2026.
- Model Y L launched in the US as a $61,990 Launch Series AWD trim with an 83 kWh battery, 0.216 drag coefficient, PowerShare capability, and 325-mile range; US deliveries expected in September. Also launched in the UAE (deliveries beginning November).
- Giga Berlin is ramping from 6,200/week to a targeted 7,500/week by around October, adding roughly 1,000 jobs.
- Ford's Q2 EV sales fell 40.7% YoY to 9,746 units; Rivian delivered 12,194 (+14.4%) and raised full-year guidance; Lucid delivered 3,953 (+19%) — underscoring Tesla's scale advantage.

## Humanoid Bots
- Musk toured the Optimus production line at Fremont and reiterated production will be "extremely slow at first" as Tesla builds new manufacturing muscle memory.
- Hosts speculated first production Optimus units could be a month or two away based on Moravy's comments on automation readiness and field acceptance testing, though scaled production (10,000-100,000 units) remains unconfirmed.
- SoftBank sold its ~10% Boston Dynamics stake back to Hyundai at a cited $3 billion valuation, cited as a competitive contrast to Tesla's in-house manufacturing/AI approach to humanoid robots.

## Financials
- Tesla's stock dropped roughly 7-8% despite the delivery beat; multiple hosts called the move unexplained by fundamentals, with one (Bhakdi) speculating tactical market manipulation.
- Tesla Energy deployed 13.5 GWh of storage in Q2, its second-best quarter ever.
- Tesla capped employee AI spending at $200/week effective July 6, carving out an exemption for xAI beta products — a move Electrek's editor called a conflict of interest given internal preference for Anthropic's Claude over Grok.

## Market & Competition
- SpaceX is reportedly acquiring Cursor parent Anysphere for $60 billion in an all-stock deal expected to close this quarter.
- Rivian also beat delivery expectations and saw a stock pattern similar to Tesla's, used by some hosts to argue against simple "sell the news" explanations for Tesla's dip.
