Wawa, already Tesla's largest Supercharger host with 223 sites and 2,115 stalls, has joined Tesla's Supercharger for Business program and opened its first self-branded, Wawa-owned Supercharger site in Alachua, Florida.

## Energy
1. **Wawa launches self-branded Supercharger site:** Under Tesla's Supercharger for Business program, Tesla handles installation and management while the host sets pricing. Wawa's new Alachua, Florida site has 16 stalls rated at 325 kW, with Wawa setting the rate at $0.37/kWh. Tesla's director of charging North America, Max de Zegher, announced the site on Twitter/X. Wawa has not disclosed how many additional self-owned sites it plans, though the chain operates over 1,200 stores across 14 states and Washington, DC. The Supercharger for Business program launched at the end of November 2025.
