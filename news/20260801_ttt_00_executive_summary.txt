## Brief

- Robotaxi growth models project $40B–$80B revenue run rate by end-2027
- Ashok Elluswamy: robotaxi fleet already running FSD V15
- Cybercab has no fleet-size cap, exec confirms
- Tesla hits 10 million vehicles produced milestone
- First domestic LFP Megapack cells ship from Sparks, NV
- Cybertruck brake system issue resolved, owners reimbursed

## Robotaxi
- Multiple analysts model Tesla's robotaxi scaling off Ashok Elluswamy's Q2 call comment on "double-digit" weekly compounding growth, producing three scenarios (11%, 14.5%, 17% weekly) that yield roughly 500 to 2,000 vehicles and $60M–$180M revenue run rate by end-2026, scaling to $14B–$80B by end-2027 depending on growth rate; Cybercab production capacity (currently 125,000 units/year) only becomes a binding constraint in the most optimistic case, per brighterwithherbert's analysis.
- A Tesla executive referenced as "Lars" confirmed no fleet-size cap exists on Cybercab production or deployment, addressing speculation about a ~2,500-unit limit; self-certification under FMVSS is described as settled, per futureaza's Beat Podcast.
- Cybercab traffic reported as frequent and near-continuous in Palo Alto and at Giga Texas test tracks, with deployment expanding to Tesla sales/service hubs across the US and Canada for regional testing.
- One host pushed back on "betrayal" narratives suggesting Musk is deliberately delaying robotaxi to smooth a SpaceX merger, arguing instead that robotaxi is progressing steadily without needing Musk's direct attention while his focus has shifted to Optimus.
- Separately, futureaza's hosts argue the earnings-call selloff stemmed from poor messaging around robotaxi timelines rather than weak underlying progress, with one host maintaining mass-scale deployment remains plausible later this year.

## Autonomous Driving
- Ashok Elluswamy said the current robotaxi fleet is already running FSD V15, described as a significant step up from prior V14 releases; unsupervised consumer deployment is not yet active.
- Tesla's EMEA account reported FSD supervised is over 5.2x safer than manual driving across roughly 65 million km in five EU countries over the last four months — lower than a roughly 7.4–7.6x figure cited for the US, attributed to smaller sample size and less European road training.
- FSD attach rate on new vehicle sales is now around 55%, with paid FSD sales reportedly up ~180,000 in Q1 and ~150,000 in Q2 (host-cited, sourcing unspecified).
- Volvo will drop LiDAR for a vision-only approach, a shift hosts frame as validating Tesla's camera-only strategy.

## Financials
- Tesla Q2 revenue was $28.2B, up 26% year-over-year and beating estimates; EPS missed at $0.33 vs. $0.49 expected; capex was $5.79B, up 142% year-over-year.
- TSLA has traded roughly flat for five years (mid-$300s to $390 range), which one analyst likens to Nvidia's six-to-seven-year flat stretch before CUDA's payoff; TSLA is also described as more oversold than at any point in over a year following a sharp recent selloff (cited at roughly 38% over five days, 26% over one month, per one host's figures).

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus is described by multiple hosts as Musk's current management focus, with one host claiming Musk overhauled the program's personnel in the prior year after calling it "a mess" (unconfirmed, relayed without a specific source).
- One analyst frames Tesla's multi-billion-dollar Optimus investment as a bet on a "zero billion dollar market" today, directly comparable to Nvidia's pre-adoption CUDA spending.

## Energy
- Tesla shipped its first Megapacks using domestically made LFP cells, manufactured at its Sparks, Nevada facility and integrated at the California Megapack factory — a milestone for domestic manufacturing, though the facility's capacity is limited to roughly 10 GWh/year.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla resolved the pre-2026 Cybertruck brake control system (BCS/PCS) failure via design changes in 2026 model-year trucks; affected owners who paid out of pocket will be reimbursed starting in the coming weeks, and the Foundation Series warranty was extended to 8 years/150,000 miles.
- Tesla raised financing APRs on select Model 3/Y trims (e.g., Model 3 Performance from 0.99% to 1.99%; base Model Y RWD from 0% to 0.99%), with other trims unchanged.
- Tesla hit 10 million vehicles produced (outside Fremont), up from 1 million six years prior.
- A third-party highway-speed range test found the Rivian R2 fell further short of its EPA rating than the Model Y Performance, which exceeded its rating by about 4.1 miles.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Merger speculation intensified after Musk reportedly brought an M&A lawyer onto the Tesla earnings call; neither Musk nor Gwynne Shotwell denied merger speculation when asked, per one host's characterization. Terms and timing remain entirely unconfirmed, with speakers disagreeing on whether a deal would help or hurt near-term profit visibility.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX's most recent Starship test flight executed a clean descent and landing, with the stage reportedly floating intact in the ocean for several days afterward; only one of two stages is currently targeted for catch-tower recovery.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Jensen Huang, on the BG2 podcast, called Musk's 122-day build-out of the 100,000-H100 Colossus supercomputer "superhuman," saying "there's only one person in the world who could do that."
- A previously circulated claim of a $52 billion SpaceX order for a million Nvidia chips was called "fake news" by Musk himself.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
