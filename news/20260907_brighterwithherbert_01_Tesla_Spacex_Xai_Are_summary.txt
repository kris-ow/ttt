This episode of brighterwithherbert, with guest Larry Goldberg, covers Tesla's push for European FSD approval ahead of an EU vote, a new SpaceX/4iG Starlink Mobile partnership in Europe, Lemonade's expanding FSD insurance discount, a new Tesla automatic collision-avoidance feature, Uber's alliance with traditional taxi unions to slow robotaxi rollout, and a personal endorsement of xAI's Grok-based agent ("Grockbot") as a productivity tool.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Elon personally lobbying French regulators on FSD:** Elon Musk reportedly called French transportation authorities directly to push for Full Self-Driving approval in Europe, replying to a follower that he does this "quite a bit." The EU vote is expected around October 6, with France and Germany together representing enough of the EU population (over 35%) that a "no" from both could block approval bloc-wide. Goldberg notes the French government reportedly received only two test cars, which he sees as a weak level of commitment compared to prior test deployments in the Netherlands (15–20 cars per his recollection).
2. **FSD safety data cited amid approval push:** The hosts cite Tesla's FSD supervised safety data as roughly four times safer than human driving in Europe and seven times safer in the US, with over a million kilometers logged in four European cities; an exact cumulative mileage figure was referenced but the speaker could not recall it precisely.
3. **New automatic collision-avoidance feature:** Tesla announced FSD supervised version 14.3.9 is rolling out with a new active safety feature that can activate automatically when an imminent collision is detected and standard automatic emergency braking may not suffice, or if the driver is heavily distracted or has accidentally disengaged FSD. Goldberg expects it will likely be offered as an option even for non-FSD Tesla owners, similar to automatic emergency braking.
4. **Lemonade FSD insurance discount expands to Missouri:** Lemonade's autonomous insurance program, which offers roughly 50% off car insurance for FSD usage, has expanded to Missouri, following a prior expansion to Indiana, bringing its footprint to five states per the host. Goldberg separately notes his own Allstate premium dropped by over 25% this quarter, which he attributes to near-100% FSD usage by himself and his wife (roughly 98–99% and 95% FSD mileage respectively, per Allstate's tracking).

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab reservation signups:** Goldberg says he heard from what he called a fairly reliable source that roughly two million people have signed up for Tesla's robotaxi/Cybercab purchase interest form; both hosts note it is a non-binding form with no deposit, and draw a parallel to high early Cybertruck reservation counts that didn't fully convert to purchases.

## Market & Competition
1. **Uber allies with traditional taxi unions against robotaxi rollout:** Uber is reportedly aligning with traditional taxi driver unions — the same groups it fought historically — in what the host describes as an effort to slow the rollout of robotaxis, framed as a response to competition from both Waymo and Tesla. Goldberg argues this alliance is telling: if Uber's AV partnerships (with over 20 companies, per the host) were sufficient to compete, it would have no reason to help slow deployment. He characterizes Tesla's robotaxi ambition as targeting the entire auto/driving industry rather than Uber or taxis specifically, calling Uber's addressable slice "tiny" relative to the total market Tesla is pursuing.

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **Starlink Mobile European partnership:** SpaceX and 4iG signed a partnership to expand Starlink Mobile across four countries, with agreements signed in Bastrop, Texas by 4iG's CEO and SpaceX President/CEO Gwynne Shotwell. Starlink Mobile will first launch in Hungary and Albania (with Montenegro potentially following), connecting directly to LTE-compatible phones for data/messaging without new hardware; Goldberg notes voice-based direct-to-cell would still require phones supporting the relevant frequencies. He frames this as an early but limited entry into the EU market, calling France and Germany the more important remaining targets.
2. **Bastrop land expansion:** SpaceX has purchased an additional roughly 820 acres of land in Bastrop, Texas; Goldberg speculates (unconfirmed) this may be for a turbine manufacturing facility, and suggests the Bastrop site could eventually rival or exceed the scale of Giga Texas.
