This episode features Jo Bhakdi in conversation with Cern Basher, discussing MicroStrategy (now rebranded "Strategy") as a Bitcoin treasury company, its preferred stock instruments (particularly STRC), and the broader thesis connecting Bitcoin's proof-of-work security model to the challenge of constraining artificial intelligence agents. The core argument is that Strategy's financial engineering — raising permanent capital via perpetual preferred shares to accumulate Bitcoin — is misunderstood as a Ponzi scheme when it more closely resembles the operating model of an insurance company, and that Bitcoin's energy-grounded security may make it uniquely suited to tethering AI agents to the physical world.

## Market & Competition

1. **Strategy as a Bitcoin Treasury Company:** Cern Basher explains that Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) acquires Bitcoin by selling equity, raising convertible debt, and issuing perpetual preferred shares. The company currently holds approximately $64 billion in Bitcoin on its balance sheet and owns roughly 4% of all Bitcoin that will ever be mined. The preferred instruments — particularly STRC — are described not as debt but as permanent capital with no maturity date, meaning there is no obligation to repay principal, only to service dividends.

2. **STRC Preferred Stock Mechanics and Yield:** STRC currently pays an 11–12% dividend yield. Cern Basher argues this is sustainable because Strategy holds roughly five times as much collateral in Bitcoin as it has issued in preferred stock, meaning Bitcoin only needs to appreciate approximately 2.4% per year for the preferred dividends to be fully covered indefinitely. The company also maintains a dedicated cash reserve of $2.25 billion, sufficient to cover approximately 15–16 months of dividend payments without touching Bitcoin holdings.

3. **Capital Raising Pace and Bitcoin Per Share Growth:** Strategy completed what was described as its eighth purchase of over $2 billion in Bitcoin in a single week, the most recent being approximately $2.1 billion raised by selling STRC. Bitcoin per share grew 22.9% in 2025. As of the video's publish date, Bitcoin per share has increased 12.6% year-to-date in 2026, and if the current capital-raising pace continues through year-end, the annualized Bitcoin-per-share growth rate is projected to reach approximately 37% for 2026.

4. **NAV Premium and Leverage Profile:** Strategy's market cap currently trades at approximately a 1.23x premium to its net asset value (MNAV), compared to a historical high of approximately 4x and a low of approximately 1.02x — meaning the stock is much closer to its historical floor than its ceiling. The company carries $8.2 billion in convertible debt, but Cern Basher notes a deal was in progress to retire approximately $1.5 billion of that debt, reducing leverage. Bitcoin would need to fall to approximately $13,000 for the debt to exceed assets. The $15.5 billion in preferred stock is characterized as amplification rather than leverage because it is perpetual and never needs to be repaid.

5. **Tax Treatment of STRC Dividends:** Because Strategy pays dividends from capital raises rather than earnings, the dividends receive "return of capital" tax treatment under a U.S. tax provision described as dating to approximately 1910. This means each dividend payment reduces the holder's cost basis rather than triggering taxable income. At an 11–12% annual payout on a $100 par-value share, a holder's cost basis reaches zero after approximately 9.5 years, after which dividends are expected to be taxed as qualified dividends at long-term capital gains rates. Cern Basher and Jo Bhakdi also note that for married couples filing jointly with taxable income below $98,900, the U.S. long-term capital gains tax rate is 0%, and STRC return-of-capital payments do not count toward that income threshold.

6. **Sharpe Ratio and Investment Positioning:** Cern Basher cites STRC Sharpe ratios above 2.0, which he describes as exceeding typical hedge fund alpha generation. He positions STRC as an income alternative superior to CDs, money market funds, investment-grade bonds, junk bonds, and private credit, particularly for investors seeking yield without locking up capital for extended periods. He also suggests Tesla shareholders who sell covered calls to generate income could use STRC as an alternative that does not expose their Tesla shares to assignment risk, especially if margin rates are below 5.5%, creating a potential spread of approximately 6%.

7. **MicroStrategy's MNAV and the Digital Security Thesis:** Cern Basher argues that because Bitcoin's proof-of-work mechanism makes it a physical-energy-grounded security technology rather than merely money, Strategy's largest-corporate-holder status (4% of total Bitcoin supply) could justify a much higher MNAV than 1.23x if the market begins to price it as the world's largest digital security company rather than simply a leveraged Bitcoin vehicle. He references Jason Lowry's book *Soft War* as articulating the case that Bitcoin is fundamentally a security technology analogous to gunpowder — misunderstood as money because of its name.

## Market & Competition (AI and Bitcoin Intersection)

1. **Bitcoin as an AI Containment Mechanism:** Cern Basher argues that traditional software-based security measures (CAPTCHAs, passwords, permission codes) will become ineffective against AI agents, which can already hire humans via marketplaces to bypass CAPTCHAs. Bitcoin's proof-of-work system, which requires real-world energy expenditure that cannot be faked, may be the only scalable mechanism for constraining AI agent activity. He notes that U.S. Admiral Paparo testified before Congress approximately two weeks prior to the video's publish date that the military is actively testing Bitcoin for protecting U.S. computer systems, following a Congressional mandate from late 2025. A formal report to Congress is expected by end of 2026.

2. **Proof-of-Work vs. Proof-of-Stake for Security:** Cern Basher explains why Bitcoin's proof-of-work is preferred over Ethereum's proof-of-stake for security applications: the energy friction in proof-of-work is precisely the feature that makes it resistant to attack, whereas proof-of-stake can potentially be gamed. He acknowledges Ethereum and Solana may serve complementary roles (smart contracts, high transaction volume) but suggests Ethereum's security is materially weaker than Bitcoin's for containment purposes.

3. **AI Agent Proliferation Timeline:** Both speakers express the view that the inflection point for AI agent proliferation — where agents outnumber human internet actors and create unmanageable security problems — could arrive as soon as 2026, not 2040 as many assume. Jo Bhakdi references Nvidia's enterprise AI deployments and suggests that a company like Anthropic could become one of the largest companies on the planet within a year, citing exponential growth metrics across all AI-related benchmarks.
