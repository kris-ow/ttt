## Brief

- SpaceX targets $1T revenue in 2030, pulled forward from 2031
- First Cybercab spotted with physical Starlink antenna hardware

- Terafab to run on gas plants, not Tesla solar, SpaceX confirms

## Robotaxi
- New Cybercab staging hubs found in Fort Worth and Dallas (~105 units), plus Nashville, Las Vegas, San Diego; independent estimates (unverified) put total staged Cybercabs nationwide above 2,000.
- Tesla's @robotaxi account posted the first Cybercab built with a physical, roof-integrated Starlink antenna, following July's concept rendering; Electrek questions the near-term utility given AI4 doesn't need connectivity to drive and current markets have strong cellular coverage.
- Austin app now lists 6am–10pm operating hours; one rider account described a ~10-minute pickup delay and a ~25-minute, three-attempt drop-off that ended two and a half blocks from the destination, underscoring unrefined terminal maneuvers.
- San Antonio flagged (unconfirmed) as a possible next launch city after six uniquely badged Model Ys were spotted there.
- Tesla's Impact Report cites Cybercab efficiency of 165 Wh/mile and claims roughly half the emissions per mile of Model 3 + Model Y combined, attributed to continuous fleet utilization versus personal cars parked ~95% of the time.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- SpaceX executive said internal projections for $1 trillion in annual revenue have moved up from 2031 to 2030, with a "non-zero chance" of 2029; Musk confirmed this means revenue in the year 2030 itself, not cumulative. SpaceX also expects to exceed $100 billion ARR by December.
- SpaceX's valuation stands at roughly $1.7 trillion, per commentary.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX CFO Brett Johnson said new compute capex is currently paying back in under one year, versus a typical 5-year-plus hyperscaler payback; SpaceX's 10-K shows ~$83B gross PP&E including ~$34B in servers/networking (up from ~$22B+ at year-end).
- Terafab confirmed sited in Grimes County, Texas (~1.5 hours from Houston, not adjacent to Giga Texas), tied to the previously disclosed $16.8B first-phase cost (down from $25B floated in March); target is over a terawatt of compute/year for SpaceX/xAI, with future Optimus and Cybercab chips also planned.
- Musk replied "FEL FTW" to a post suggesting a free-electron-laser EUV lithography approach at Terafab — speculative and unconfirmed.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Musk said Starlink's V2 generates ~$20B ARR this year, with V3 (launched via Starship) expected to deliver ~100x total system bandwidth; even at 10x lower revenue-per-gigabit, he says communications revenue could exceed $200B/year.
- Musk projected Starlink could reach at least 25% market share outside China (>$500B annual revenue) even if the market merely doubles, and said it's "not out of the question" Starlink could carry over half of global internet traffic long-term, implying over $1 trillion/year — both explicitly speculative.
- A separate, unconfirmed analyst estimate pegs Starlink at $4.3B quarterly revenue, 12 million subscribers, ~$66/month ARPU, potentially scaling to a $1 trillion standalone valuation within 18 months–2 years.
- T-Mobile's CEO called satellite competition "exaggerated"; Musk responded "Laughing 100%."

## Energy
- Tesla filed for a $10.1B vertically integrated solar cell/module plant ("Project Crystal Sun") in Fort Bend County, Texas, with construction starting this year and commercial operations targeted for 2029; spend is already part of Tesla's stated capex plans.
- SpaceX confirmed Terafab will run on its own natural gas plants and large battery arrays, not Tesla solar, despite Tesla's co-ownership — part of a broader pattern that includes Musk's ~$1B APR Energy purchase and SpaceX's $2.8B gas turbine commitment.
- Tesla's Impact Report: Supercharger network 100% renewable-matched for 5 straight years; Powerwall fleet delivered 47 million backup hours and $1B+ in owner savings in 2025; Megapack avoided 470,000 metric tons of CO2 but remains a minor player in China.
- SpaceX has purchased $329 million of Megapacks so far this year.

## Autonomous Driving
- 49ers coach Kyle Shanahan crashed his Model S on Autopilot on July 14 after reaching for a dropped phone, sustaining a concussion, broken nose, three broken ribs, a broken hand, and 40 stitches; he took full responsibility. Article cites a reported record 207 Autopilot/FSD crashes in a single month.
- FSD Supervised has been trained/tested on ~2.2 million km across 19 EU countries, per Tesla.
- A driver reported FSD 14.3.6 repeatedly attempting to enter a closed HOV lane on a Miami-to-Maryland trip; the commentator suspects a possible regression versus 14.3.5.
- FSD V14.3.7 began rolling out to early access users with the same release notes as the prior build.
