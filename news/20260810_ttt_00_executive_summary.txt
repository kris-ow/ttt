## Brief

- First Cybercab spotted with physical integrated Starlink antenna

- New Cybercab staging hubs found in Fort Worth, Dallas, Nashville

## Robotaxi
- Tesla's @robotaxi account shared the first images of a Cybercab with a physical, low-profile roof-mounted Starlink antenna at Giga Texas, moving beyond July's concept rendering. Electrek questions the utility given the Cybercab's onboard AI4 computer doesn't need connectivity and current markets (Austin, Houston, Dallas, Miami) have strong cellular coverage; Tesla is framing it as an option for tracking robotaxis in remote areas.
- New Cybercab staging hubs identified in Fort Worth (Giga Texas's fourth hub) and Dallas (~105 units), plus staged vehicles in Nashville, Las Vegas, San Diego and San Antonio. One commentator estimated Tesla may have ~2,000 Cybercabs staged nationwide across dozens of cities — an unverified estimate, not from RobotaxiTracker.com.
- Austin's Robotaxi app now lists 6 a.m.–10 p.m. hours; a rider account described a ~10-minute pickup delay and a ~25-minute drop-off ordeal after three failed attempts, ultimately dropped two and a half blocks from the intended stop — evidence terminal maneuvers remain unrefined.
- Six badged Model Ys spotted in San Antonio have prompted speculation it could be the next launch city, though this is unconfirmed.
- Tesla's Impact Report states Cybercab achieves 165 Wh/mile and roughly half the emissions per mile of a Model 3 plus Model Y combined, attributed to continuous ride-hailing utilization versus personal cars sitting parked ~95% of the time.
- A guest on one podcast flagged that current robotaxi incident rates (anecdotal issues like vehicles looping for parking or getting stuck) aren't yet low enough to support a planned 100x fleet scale-up, since incidents scale with fleet size.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- A SpaceX executive said internal projections for reaching $1 trillion in annual revenue have moved up from 2031 to 2030, with a "non-zero chance" of 2029, plus over $100B ARR expected by December. Musk clarified this refers to revenue generated in 2030 itself, not cumulative revenue.
- SpaceX's valuation is cited around $1.7 trillion in one podcast's commentary.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Musk projected that even if revenue per gigabit drops 10x, SpaceX communications revenue could still exceed $200 billion a year, driven by V3 satellites offering ~100x V2's total system bandwidth. He said Starlink could reach at least 25% market share outside China even in a flat-doubling market (implying $500B+ annual revenue), and floated a longer-term scenario where Starlink carries over 50% of global internet traffic (implying $1T+ annually) — both explicitly speculative.
- A separate, unconfirmed analyst estimate pegs Starlink at $4.3B quarterly revenue, $2.6B adjusted EBITDA, and 12 million subscribers, potentially reaching a $1T standalone valuation within 18–24 months.
- T-Mobile's CEO called satellite competition from Starlink "exaggerated"; Musk responded "Laughing 100%."

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX CFO Brett Johnson said new capital deployed for compute is paying back in under one year, a pace analysts compared favorably to hyperscalers' 5-year-plus paybacks. SpaceX's 10-K shows ~$83B total gross PP&E as of end of June, including ~$34B in servers/networking (up from ~$22B+ at year-end).
- Terafab's site is confirmed in Grimes County, Texas (~1.5 hours outside Houston), tied to the previously disclosed $16.8B first-phase cost (down from $25B floated in March). SpaceX confirmed the facility will run on its own natural gas plants and large battery arrays rather than Tesla solar, despite Tesla's co-ownership — following Musk's ~$1B purchase of gas turbine company APR Energy and SpaceX's separate $2.8B gas turbine commitment.
- Musk replied "FEL FTW" to a post suggesting a particle accelerator at the Terafab site, hinting at free-electron-laser EUV lithography ambitions — speculative and unconfirmed.

## Energy
- Tesla filed for a $10.1 billion vertically integrated solar manufacturing facility ("Project Crystal Sun") in Fort Bend County, Texas, with construction starting this year and commercial operations targeted for 2029. Status of Tesla's earlier, smaller Brookshire solar plans is unclear.
- Tesla's Supercharger network has been 100% renewable-matched for five consecutive years; Powerwall delivered 47M hours of backup power and $1B+ in owner savings in 2025; Megapack avoided 470,000 metric tons of CO2 per the Impact Report.
- SpaceX has purchased $329 million of Tesla Megapacks so far this year.

## Autonomous Driving
- 49ers coach Kyle Shanahan was seriously injured (concussion, broken nose, three broken ribs, broken hand, 40 stitches) in a July 14 crash while on Autopilot after reaching for a dropped phone; he corrected a police report that initially blamed the other driver, saying "I hit her." Article cites a reported record 207 Autopilot/FSD crashes in a single month.
- Tesla says FSD Supervised has been trained/tested on ~2.2 million km across 19 EU countries.
- One host reported a friend's Model Y (HW4, FSD 14.3.6) repeatedly failed to detect a closed HOV lane on a road trip, requiring manual takeover each time — described as a possible regression.

## Electric Vehicles

- Tesla's new RIM paint process cuts Cybertruck paint cycle time from hours to minutes, reducing related emissions by 35% and eliminating VOC emissions entirely.
