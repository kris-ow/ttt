## Brief

- Austin Cybercab fleet hits 300+, up ~8x in a month
- Roadster unveil pushed again, now Oct 15, 7:55pm CT
- Tesla Megapack lands $265M Apollo Power order, 1 GWh
- Model Y took 37.2% of Q3 EV market per Cox Automotive

- Starship Flight 15 targets first ship-catch in 4-6 weeks

## Robotaxi
- Austin Cybercab fleet grew to over 300 vehicles roughly a month after its September 4 public launch, an ~8x jump from prototype-era numbers, per Tesla's robotaxi app and Ashok Elluswamy, who marked the Cybercab's two-year reveal anniversary noting cabs are "everywhere in Austin" and "soon in Dallas."
- Dallas already runs driverless Model Y robotaxi rides, with Cybercabs reportedly queuing outside Tesla's Dallas hub and being tested driverless on public roads; no official Dallas Cybercab launch date yet.
- One tracker (Pioneer Lens, cited by jobhakdi) shows fully autonomous Cybercab count rising from ~70-73 to 178 in a week with 319 registered in Texas — a materially different, independently-sourced figure from RobotaxiTracker.com's stale counts; host targets 1,000+ by year-end.

## Humanoid Bots
- Tesla filed a patent for a 3D "soft adaptive array of tactile sensors" to give Optimus's hands a sense of touch, with flexible curved sensor "pixels" and hints at eventual full-body tactile skin; this is a patent application, not a confirmed product.
- Musk has reportedly called the hand/forearm the hardest engineering problem in Optimus, with hand assembly cited (unconfirmed) as a production bottleneck; newest robot arm has 22 degrees of freedom, roughly double the prior design.

## Electric Vehicles
- Model Y sold an estimated 99,037 units in Q3 2026 (Cox Automotive), 37.2% of the entire EV market and ~9x the next best-selling non-Tesla EV; overall EVs hit ~6.5% of US new car sales, up 7.7% from Q2.
- Tesla will launch vehicle sales in Oman starting 2027, already hiring in Muscat; Canada paused Model 3 Premium orders after exhausting its import quota, resumption expected ~March 2027.
- Model 3 priced around $29,500 in Canada and Model Y at $38,000 in Australia (roughly $500 cheaper than in China), per hosts framing these as aggressive pushes into lower-price segments.

## Energy
- Tesla Powerwalls drove the bulk of a record 745 MW California grid dispatch on October 6 (per Sunrun); Tesla separately reported 85,000+ owners dispatching 600+ MW, about 80% of Sunrun's combined total — up from a 580 MW record a month earlier.
- New Tesla Megapack order: ~$265M from Taiwan's Apollo Power for 1 GWh (260 Megapack 2XL units); Bocanova 2, a Nightpeak Energy Megapack facility near Houston, came online as phase two of a storage project.
- Sunrun's Google-funded San Jose data-center VPP project plans to enroll ~21,000 devices with Tesla and Everyday Electric, with initial dispatches expected this month — part of the 16.8 GW data-center VPP plan announced in June.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX filed for FAA approval to conduct up to 456 Starship launches and 912 landings per year from Starbase, up from the current 25/50 limit, with a phased ramp starting at up to 100 annual launches.
- Musk said Starship Flight 15, targeted for 4-6 weeks out, aims for the first full catch of both booster and ship in one flight, estimating 50-60% odds of catching the ship on the first try; he hopes to reuse the same booster and ship on Flight 16.
- Ship 40 has returned to Starbase, with no detail given on next testing steps.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- SpaceX agreed October 8 to buy nationwide low-frequency spectrum (up to 14 MHz, 800 MHz band) from Grain Management, pending FCC approval and undisclosed price; Musk called it the final piece needed for complete US phone coverage, following the prior $17B EchoStar spectrum deal. Verizon dismissed the move as "just empty talk."
- Starlink has grown from ~1 million subscribers (2022) to over 13 million across 160+ countries; in-flight Starlink now reaches 1,400+ aircraft on 40+ airlines, averaging ~150 Mbps per Ookla.
- Grok Voice 3.0 launched across Starlink and Tesla; SpaceX says Starlink call centers handle ~20,000 calls/day via Grok Voice with ~70% resolved without a human and roughly 1-in-4 sales calls converting.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Musk posted that he sees "a path" for SpaceX to be worth orders of magnitude more than Earth's current economy — potentially over a quadrillion dollars, or "over 1 petawatt per year" in energy terms — while explicitly stressing this reflects non-zero probability, not high confidence.
- Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas flagged SpaceX's "Distributed Inference Cloud" as an underappreciated growth driver investors are largely ignoring — an analyst thesis, not a confirmed business line.
