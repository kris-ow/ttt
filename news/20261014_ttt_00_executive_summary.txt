## Robotaxi
- A Cybercab was seen operating unsupervised in Austin, consistent with Tesla's ongoing driverless rollout there (per Sawyer Merritt digest).
- GM CEO Mary Barra reiterated GM has no plans to enter robotaxi, targeting eyes-off driving by 2028 but betting customers prefer private ownership over ride-hailing — a direct contrast with Tesla's strategy.

## Energy
- Tesla Powerwalls supplied roughly 80% of a record 745 MW California grid dispatch on October 6, part of a joint Tesla-Sunrun virtual power plant event; Tesla separately reported 85,000+ Powerwall owners delivered 600+ MW. Sunrun's combined figure (not broken out by company) also included 669 MW the prior day.
- Output of ~7 kW per home exceeds the Powerwall 2's 5 kW rating, suggesting growing Powerwall 3 penetration or multi-battery homes, per Electrek's analysis.
- Sunrun says it now has 120,000+ home batteries enrolled in California grid programs, and a Google-funded San Jose data-center VPP project with PG&E (involving Tesla and Everyday Electric) plans to enroll nearly 21,000 devices, with initial dispatches expected this month.
- Tesla's new Bocanova 2 Megapack facility (built by Nightpeak Energy) came online in Brazoria County, Texas, as phase two following the 150 MW Bocanova 1.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla sold an estimated 99,037 Model Ys in Q3 2026, ~9x the next best-selling non-Tesla EV, per Cox Automotive. EVs made up 6.5% of all US new car sales in Q3, up 7.7% from Q2 — the first significant growth in about a year.
- Tesla will launch vehicle sales in Oman starting 2027 and has already hired a first team in Muscat.
- Tesla paused Model 3 Premium orders in Canada after exhausting its import quota; resumption expected once new quota is issued, likely March 2027.

## Financials
- Tesla opened Q3 earnings call question submissions ahead of next week's call.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX filed for FAA approval to conduct up to 456 Starship launches and 912 landings per year from Starbase, a major jump from the current limit of 25 launches and 50 landings. The filing reportedly describes a phased approach, with Phase 1 covering up to 100 annual launches and 200 landings.
- Starship test vehicle Ship 40 has returned to Starbase; no further detail given on its testing status or next steps.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX launched Grok Voice 3.0, which it calls the world's most intelligent speech-to-speech model with sub-one-second latency. SpaceX says Starlink call centers handle ~20,000 customer calls per day with it, ~70% resolved with no human involved, and about one in four sales calls converting. The model also reportedly handles millions of daily conversations inside Tesla vehicles.
- Morgan Stanley analyst Adam Jonas argued in a new note that SpaceX's "Distributed Inference Cloud" is an underappreciated growth driver investors are largely ignoring — his own thesis rather than a confirmed SpaceX business line.
