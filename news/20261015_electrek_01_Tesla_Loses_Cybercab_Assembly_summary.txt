Tesla's Senior Manager of Cybercab General Assembly, Jody Kerns, is leaving to become Rivian's Director of General Assembly for the R2 — the second Cybercab launch leader to depart Tesla this year, following Head of Cybercab NPI Michael So in April/May.

## Robotaxi
1. **Kerns' departure and background:** Kerns announced via LinkedIn that he is leaving Tesla for Rivian, where he will lead general assembly for the R2 in Normal, Illinois. He joined Tesla in January 2024 as an end-of-line manager and took over Cybercab general assembly in Austin in February 2026 — the same month Tesla rolled the first steering-wheel-less Cybercab off the line — running final assembly through the ramp to commercial service. Previously he spent 14 years at Volkswagen of America (Director of Assembly 2021–2024, Chattanooga plant, managing about 1,200 people and integrating the ID.4 into an existing line) and four years in Toyota's paint shops.
2. **Michael So's earlier exit:** Michael So, Tesla's Head of Cybercab NPI from September 2024 to April 2026 and Giga Texas employee #1 in 2021 (who helped launch Model Y and Cybertruck there), left in May for a VP of Product Engineering role at Aalo Atomics, a nuclear reactor startup in Austin. So's LinkedIn profile credits him with launching the Cybercab's unboxed manufacturing process, brake-by-wire system, and butterfly doors.
3. **Regulatory backdrop:** Commercial Cybercab rides began in Austin on September 3; a week later, NHTSA issued a Special Order demanding Tesla prove under oath that a vehicle without a steering wheel or brake pedal meets federal safety standards. NHTSA estimates the 2026 Robotaxi fleet (Model Y and Cybercab combined) at about 1,000 vehicles.

## Market & Competition
1. **Rivian's R2 ramp:** Rivian began R2 deliveries from Normal, Illinois on June 9, produced 19,751 vehicles in Q3, and maintained 2026 delivery guidance of 65,000 to 70,000 vehicles. The company has raised $1.5 billion partly to de-risk the ramp.

Notable quotes and data:
- Kerns, on LinkedIn, thanked his Tesla team for "the WEOL project to the launch of the Cyber Cab" and described his R2 goal as making it "the best electric SUV in the market."
- So commented under Kerns' post that he "wouldn't want to launch Cybercab with anyone else."
