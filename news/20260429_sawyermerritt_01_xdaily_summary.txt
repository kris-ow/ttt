## Key Posts / Themes

1. **FSD V14.3.2 Rolling Out Broadly — New "Navigation" Disengagement Reason Added:** FSD V14.3.2 (software version 2026.2.9.9) is now being pushed to a significantly larger group of Tesla owners, with Sawyer confirming receipt on his own Model Y and encouraging owners to check the app. The most notable change flagged is the addition of "Navigation" as a selectable disengagement reason in the FSD popup on the screen — a new data collection category that could help Tesla better understand and categorize when and why drivers intervene during navigation-related scenarios. This is a software-level catalyst worth watching as it may signal Tesla is refining its disengagement taxonomy ahead of unsupervised deployment requirements.

2. **Cybercab Hardware in Motion — Two Semi Trucks Spotted in One Day:** Two separate semi trucks loaded with Cybercabs were spotted and reported on April 28th, with Sawyer noting this is the second such sighting of the day. While no specific destination or fleet size was mentioned, the volume and frequency of sightings suggests active logistics movement, consistent with pre-launch fleet staging. Separately, commentary on the Cybercab's gold glossy finish noted that it gives the color a deeper, more saturated appearance in sunlight — a minor but consumer-facing aesthetic data point.

3. **Tesla Autonomy Pop-Up in Miami at F1 Grand Prix Fan Fest:** Tesla is holding an "Autonomy Pop-Up" in Miami starting Wednesday (April 29, 2026) at Lummus Park, co-located with the official F1 Crypto.com Miami Grand Prix Fan Fest. Attendees can see the Cybercab in person and meet Optimus, with branded merchandise available on-site. This is a notable public-facing marketing moment that places both Cybercab and Optimus in front of a large, affluent, high-visibility audience during a major international sporting event — bullish for brand awareness around Tesla's autonomous and robotics thesis.

4. **Tesla Safety Score V3.0 Launches in Florida and Maryland:** Tesla's Safety Score Version 3.0 is now available for new policy holders in Florida and Maryland, with existing policy holders in both states transitioning to V3.0 on July 12th. The primary new feature in V3.0 is the addition of night driving as a scored behavior category. This is relevant to Tesla's insurance and autonomy data flywheel — better behavioral scoring data from real-world nighttime driving could inform both insurance pricing and FSD training.

5. **FSD Supervised Earns Positive First Impressions in South Korea:** A South Korean reporter experiencing Tesla FSD (Supervised) in South Korea for the first time described the system as flawlessly executing traffic signal compliance, lane keeping, speed limit adherence, merging, and direction changes, expressing no anxiety or frustration during the experience. This qualitative endorsement is meaningful for international FSD expansion momentum, particularly in a new market.

6. **FSD Promoted via Tesla Lease Partners (Chase Auto Finance):** Tesla is now marketing FSD (Supervised) subscriptions through its lease financing partners, specifically Chase Auto Finance, promoting it directly to Tesla owners. This represents a new distribution channel for FSD subscription revenue and suggests Tesla is actively working to grow its FSD subscriber base through third-party financial relationships.

7. **Model Y L Unveils in India — Pricing and Delivery Timeline:** The Model Y L (Long range) was officially unveiled in India today, with orders open and deliveries starting this summer. The Model Y L Premium AWD is priced at ₹61,99,000 (~$66,600 USD), which Sawyer notes is approximately 9% cheaper than the smaller Model Y Premium RWD in that market — an interesting pricing dynamic that could drive demand toward the larger variant.

8. **NHTSA Closes Model Y Steering Wheel Probe Without Action:** The NHTSA has formally closed its preliminary evaluation into 120,089 2023 Tesla Model Y vehicles regarding reports of steering wheel detachment, taking no action against Tesla. This removes a regulatory overhang on a high-volume model year and is a mild positive for Tesla's safety and compliance standing.

9. **EV Cost Advantage Strengthens as U.S. Gas Prices Hit Multi-Year High:** U.S. regular gasoline prices hit $4.18/gallon (California averaging near $6/gallon), a new multi-year high. At current rates, operating a Tesla Model Y Premium costs approximately $0.04/mile versus $0.104/mile for a Toyota RAV4 Hybrid — a 61% cost-per-mile advantage excluding maintenance. Rising gas prices structurally strengthen the EV value proposition and could accelerate demand for Tesla vehicles.

10. **California Coastal Commission Apologizes to Musk/SpaceX Over Regulatory Bias:** The California Coastal Commission issued a formal apology to Elon Musk and SpaceX, acknowledging that some officials made politically biased and improper statements in regulatory decisions, and committed to not considering political views or speech in future decisions. While SpaceX-specific, this has potential downstream relevance for Tesla if similar political bias dynamics have affected any California-based Tesla regulatory matters.
