Two hosts (filling in for the regular channel host) discuss news that ASML's CFO confirmed Terafab is included in the company's production planning for next year, discuss the site's likely equipment timeline, then work through a batch of retail shareholder questions submitted for an upcoming Tesla shareholder meeting, before touring an animation of SpaceX's expanding Bastrop, Texas campus.

## Financials
1. **ASML confirms Terafab in its 2027 capacity plans:** Per a Reuters report, ASML CFO Roger Dassen said on a call that the company is in dialogue with all its customers about their building plans and that "Terafab is also part of those plans." One host notes ASML currently produces roughly 40–60 extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography machines a year and plans to raise that to about 80 next year, partly to meet demand from customers including Terafab.
2. **Retail shareholder Q&A questions signal investor anxiety, not deep insight:** The hosts review the top-voted retail shareholder questions (submitted via a "Say" voting platform) ahead of an upcoming meeting, noting only about a million votes were cast versus roughly a billion shares held by retail shareholders — a small, highly motivated "anxious" slice of the base. One host argues many questions (e.g., on missed Robotaxi guidance, a request to tie any Tesla acquisition/merger to hitting 2025 compensation-plan goals first) are confrontational in tone and unlikely to elicit substantive management responses, unlike the detailed questions Wall Street analysts typically ask.

## Robotaxi
1. **Shareholders press on Robotaxi expansion delays:** A top-voted question states Tesla has missed short-term Robotaxi guidance for three straight earnings reports, from an original goal of 50% U.S. coverage by end of 2025 to, most recently, a goal of seven new cities in the first half of this year. Hosts speculate the causes could include safety, regulatory, or operational hurdles, but say the question's confrontational framing likely won't yield a detailed answer.
2. **Other Robotaxi/Cybercab questions submitted:** Additional shareholder questions ask about constraints on faster Robotaxi expansion versus Cybercab production, why Robotaxi vehicle growth has stalled, when Cybercab will start customer rides, city-expansion milestones and fleet size targets for this year, expected recurring revenue contribution, and when unsupervised FSD with point-to-point summon (functionally equivalent to Robotaxi) will arrive. A separate question asks whether Tesla Insurance — introduced years ago and still unavailable in most states — will be a prerequisite for Tesla assuming liability once unsupervised FSD is released.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Hardware 3 upgrade-path question raised:** A shareholder question states that Elon Musk confirmed Hardware 3 cannot achieve unsupervised FSD and asks about upgrade/transfer/refund plans and associated costs. The hosts say they don't recall Musk making a definitive statement on this and note he has previously said Tesla would look for an upgrade path if Hardware 3 proves incapable, but no final decision has been made; they note the FSD "V14 light" rollout is still in early days.
2. **Additional FSD-related shareholder questions:** Questions submitted include when most Hardware 3 vehicles will receive FSD V14 light and whether it will be the final version for that hardware, and whether AI4 hardware retrofits will begin and whether there will be a cost for customers paying a monthly FSD subscription fee.

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Optimus Gen 3 status questioned by shareholders:** A top question asks about the current status of Optimus Gen 3 production ramp, initial deployment, factory rollout, and external sales timeline/volume for 2027. The hosts state that, based on their understanding, the relevant factory floor area is still empty/being prepared (automation equipment reportedly being staged in Germany), and that current Optimus units are hand-built in a test facility rather than in production ramp.
2. **Optimus V3 and Roadster reveal timing unknown:** A shareholder question asks when Optimus V3 and Roadster reveals will actually happen; hosts say they'd like to know too but note the direct phrasing may not be ideal for eliciting a detailed answer.

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **Starlink terminal production scale at Bastrop:** SpaceX's Bastrop, Texas facility (near the Boring Company's original site and X/Twitter's headquarters) reportedly produces around 170,000 Starlink terminals per week out of a building estimated at roughly 1.6 million square feet, with a highly automated production line described as having almost no visible workers on the floor.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Bastrop campus expansion for AI satellite production:** SpaceX owns roughly 2,000 acres at the Bastrop site (comparable to Tesla's roughly 2,500 acres at Giga Texas) and has announced plans for additional buildings including an AI satellite development facility, a solar cell factory, and an AI satellite production facility that the hosts estimate could be as large as 10 million square feet. Hosts note construction appears underway on multiple of these buildings based on a recent site visit.
