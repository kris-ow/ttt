Tesla disclosed, for the first time, an "Active FSD Subscriptions" metric in its Q4 2025 earnings report: 1.1 million, representing both upfront FSD purchases and monthly subscribers (excluding free trials). Tesla later clarified that outright FSD purchasers make up 70% of that 1.1 million figure. The author notes this is roughly 12% of Tesla's 8.9 million cumulative vehicle sales, even under the generous assumption that every vehicle sold is still on the road and FSD-capable.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD subscriber count disclosed for the first time:** Tesla reported 1.1 million Active FSD Subscriptions, up 38% year-over-year, with Tesla stating that monthly subscriptions "more than doubled" during 2025. The company said the metric includes both up-front payment and monthly subscriptions and excludes free trials, per a footnote tied to Musk's 2025 CEO Performance Award.
2. **Reason for disclosure:** The article states Tesla disclosed this number because active FSD subscriptions is one of the metrics used in Elon Musk's 2025 CEO Performance Award, not as a voluntary transparency move.
3. **Shift to subscription-only FSD:** Starting this quarter, Tesla is transitioning FSD (Supervised) access to monthly-subscription-only, phasing out the upfront purchase option that previously cost up to $15,000, per Tesla's earnings report language quoted in the article.

## Editorial Stance
Fred Lambert argues the 1.1 million figure, while large in absolute terms, represents only about 12% of cumulative Tesla sales after nearly a decade of self-driving promises, price cuts, free trials, and promotion — calling it "not a ringing endorsement." He speculates Tesla only disclosed the metric because Musk's pay package required it, questioning what other numbers the company might otherwise withhold, and suggests the subscription-only pivot both reduces Tesla's liability (since "Supervised" subscribers can't easily claim they were sold full self-driving) and reflects that Tesla has given up convincing buyers to pay large sums upfront.

Notable quotes and data:
- Active FSD Subscriptions: 1.1 million (38% YoY growth); 70% of that figure are outright FSD purchasers per Tesla's later clarification.
- Tesla's footnote: "In accordance with our 2025 CEO Performance Award, metric includes both up-front payment and monthly subscriptions and excludes free trials."
- Tesla's earnings statement: "Starting this quarter, we are transitioning access to FSD (Supervised) to monthly subscriptions only as we begin to sunset the up-front payment option."
- Cumulative Tesla vehicle sales: 8.9 million.
- Author's take-rate estimate: ~12% of cumulative sales, calling the figure "fine."
