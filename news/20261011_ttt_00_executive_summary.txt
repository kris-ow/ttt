## Brief

## Robotaxi
- Tesla officially confirmed its active Cybercab fleet has surpassed 300 vehicles — the company's first-ever confirmation of this figure, distinct from the broader RobotaxiTracker.com all-mode fleet totals (last known: 839, stale since Aug. 7).
- Tesla announced Dallas, Texas as the next city for public Cybercab rides.
- Tesla is adding a feature letting riders pull up the Cybercab's rear and repeater cameras on demand, per Sawyer Merritt, who says the change stemmed from his own rider feedback.
- A viral rider testimonial described a 76-year-old first-time Cybercab passenger praising the ride's comfort, likening it to "flying first class."

## Autonomous Driving
- Germany's federal transport ministry reached an agreement with Tesla in September rebranding FSD in Europe as "Tesla Assisted Driving," with Germany now supporting swift continuation of the EU approval process; a formal EU Commission vote is expected in December. The agreement reportedly allows the system to exceed posted speed limits by slightly more than 10% in some conditions, and Germany's minister reportedly said it would pursue its own path if the EU vote fails, though unilateral approval reportedly carries only a six-month window before a mandated halt.
- Viral driver accounts credited FSD with avoiding a collision by stopping for a vehicle running a red light; Tesla separately said FSD monitoring prevented a T-bone crossing incident, per the host.
- A mainstream reviewer (TheCarMom) called Tesla's driving mode more advanced than GM's Super Cruise or Ford's BlueCruise-style systems after a test drive.
- Tesla's Q2 earnings call cited over 55% of new deliveries including FSD subscriptions; updated figures are expected at the Q3 call on October 21.

## Electric Vehicles
- Global gasoline-only car sales fell 10% year-on-year to 20.25 million units in H1, with share dropping below 50% globally (to 49%) for the first time, per figures cited by guest Brian White; EV sales rose year-on-year in 90 countries.
- September country strength: UK sales up 98.61% YoY (Model 3 the UK's best-selling car); Germany up 268% YoY to 12,500 units, best month since December, with Giga Berlin output rising to 7,500 units/week; Taiwan logged 5,000 Model Y registrations vs. 314 for the second-place competitor; South Korea's Model Y was the overall best-selling car for a third straight month; Spain's Model 3 topped overall new-car registrations; the Netherlands saw Tesla take the top two spots across all categories.
- Tesla quietly refreshed the 2027 Model 3 with new 19-inch "Dark Nova" wheels, a larger 16-inch center screen, a faster 0-60 (4.2s to 3.8s) on Premium AWD, and a 2.3% range gain plus 3% horsepower increase on Performance.
- White attributes the sales surge mainly to high gasoline prices and improved factory supply, with FSD a smaller contributing factor, and projects Tesla will exceed 500,000 deliveries/sales next year while flagging Q4 inventory constraints.

## Energy
- Tesla Energy's PowerShare Home Backup is now available on new Model 3 and Model Y vehicles equipped with Powerwall 3, extending home backup by more than two days during an outage; Powerwall 2/Powerwall+ support is planned. The feature currently uses a basic 110V outlet adapter rather than the Cybertruck's built-in outlets.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Musk said Starship flight 14's engine glitch "doesn't seem to be too much of an issue" and that the heat shield performed robustly; flight 15 is hoped for in 4-6 weeks, with catching the ship as the major goal — which would mark the first fully reusable test.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Musk said SpaceX's Terafab chip-making effort aims to "learn how to make chips better than anyone," while acknowledging he still has a lot to learn, comparing the learning curve to SpaceX's and Tesla's early rocket and car efforts.
- SpaceX's internal AI initiative was renamed from SpaceXAI to SpaceXSI ("Super Intelligence"), per a new account handle.
- Musk said SpaceX's Cursor acquisition was driven by training data and talent, including a strong enterprise sales team.

## Market & Competition
- Musk said humanoid robot demos from competitors aren't "real" AI unless the robot can handle something it has never seen before and still function — a jab at scripted demo videos.
