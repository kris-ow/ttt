## Autonomous Driving

- **Lithuania becomes second European FSD country:** Tesla's FSD (Supervised) launched in Lithuania via mutual recognition of the Dutch RDW certification — bypassing independent testing. The Netherlands was first, approved April 10, 2026.
- **Greece and Ireland moving toward approval:** Greece's transport ministry announced a bill to grant the same type approval as the Dutch, which would make it the third country. Ireland's Department of Transport confirmed Tesla is actively engaging with national authorities including the NSAI.
- **EU-wide approval faces headwinds:** Sweden, Finland, Denmark, and Norway raised concerns about FSD exceeding speed limits and poor icy-road performance. An EU-wide vote requires 55% of member states representing 65% of the EU population; next TCMV meetings are July and October 2026, putting Tesla's stated summer 2026 target in doubt.
- **FSD 14.3 nag reduction approaching unsupervised behavior:** Version 14.3 reportedly reduced or eliminated the driver attention nag — tester Jeff reportedly could not trigger it. A parking-garage looping bug was separately resolved in version 14.3.3, with tester Omar confirming the car rerouted successfully.
- **Li Auto head calls FSD the benchmark:** The head of autonomous driving at Li Auto stated FSD remains the industry standard and criticized competitors' claims of having surpassed it as unfounded — arguing those claims only go unchallenged because FSD hasn't yet entered China. His own team is described as still working toward Tesla's level.
- **China FSD approval: Q2/Q3 2026 window:** Tesla is recruiting autonomous driving test technicians in nine Chinese cities including Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Wuhan, Chengdu, and Chongqing, and urgently hiring data labelers and three FSD testing/validation specialists. Per Jo Bhakdi (brighterwithherbert), this hiring pattern is consistent with a Q2 or Q3 2026 approval window; Tesla's CFO previously expressed hope for Q3 approval.

## Robotaxi

- **Chicago emerges as a new city:** A fleet of unbadged Model Y robotaxis was spotted for the first time in Chicago's Avondale area, alongside two Cybercabs focused on interstate and city streets. A fleet including ultra red and black variants was staged in a Chicago parking lot, suggesting active city-level preparation rather than casual testing.
- **Irving, TX depot filing surfaces:** City documents sourced by Spencer (Scots Rule08) on May 16 show Tesla seeking to lease a 35,049 sq ft facility on ~24 acres, explicitly described as for "storage, dispatch, service, and repair of an autonomous vehicle fleet." Specs include 212 remote fleet parking spaces, 64 surrounding spaces, 17 EV charging stations, and 24/7 maintenance operations. The site has not yet received approval.
- **Las Vegas Cybercab car wash permit filed:** Clark County received a permit on May 12 for "Tesla Center Cybercab Phase 2 Car Wash" at 6170 Mohawk Street (~36,000 sq ft, ~15–20 minutes from the Strip). The "Phase 2" designation implies a deliberate multi-phase scaling plan. Per Herbert (brighterwithherbert), the Cybercab's camera-dependent autonomy makes automated exterior cleaning safety-critical, not optional.
- **Arizona private fleet charging depots:** Tesla submitted plans for two non-public charging sites in the Phoenix East Valley: 56 V4 Supercharger stalls in Chandler (South Roosevelt Ave, "private use only") and a second site in Mesa (East Main Street, stall count undisclosed). Both are in industrial corridors and framed by Herbert as dedicated robotaxi pit-stop infrastructure.
- **Infrastructure math implies ~700–1,400+ vehicles per metro:** Herbert's back-of-envelope: 212 idle parking spots at the Dallas depot, divided by a 15–30% idle fraction for a 24/7 fleet, implies 700–1,400+ cars in service in that single metro at scale. His reasonable estimate is "at least a thousand robotaxis per major city."
- **Cybercab production is the binding constraint:** Current output at Giga Texas is cited at approximately 800 units/month (~200/week) — described explicitly as the bottleneck. Bhakdi projects the run rate will reach thousands per month within ~3 months and forecasts a minimum of 10,000 Cybercabs produced by year-end 2026, with 10,000–20,000 commercially operating robotaxis by end of year. Herbert's independent projection is 15,000 across 30+ cities. Both Bhakdi and Cern Basher converged on the same range independently.
- **Operator hiring active in 33–38 cities:** Tesla has posted AI and vehicle operator roles across Austin, the Bay Area, Phoenix, Dallas, Houston, Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Las Vegas, and others — with total active city count cited at 33–38 depending on source and timing.
- **NHTSA safety data: 17 incidents, 7 Tesla's fault:** Per Herbert, NHTSA has recorded 17 total Tesla robotaxi incidents since June 2025. Of those, 7 were determined to be Tesla's fault, several attributed to curb rashes and a couple to human teleoperator error.
- **Robotaxi trackers may be undercounting Cybercab activity:** Bhakdi pushed back on current tracker accuracy, asserting "hundreds" of Cybercabs are being deployed but not yet appearing on robotaxi trackers, citing Joe Tegtmeyer drone footage as evidence. He characterizes supervised/validation accumulation as a quiet precursor to a sudden commercial launch — an "L-shaped" curve rather than a gradual ramp.

## Electric Vehicles

- **Tesla Semi: 1,200+ California subsidy applications:** California trucking firms have submitted more applications to the state's incentive program requesting Tesla Semis than all other EV truck makers combined since the program launched in 2019 — representing approximately $350M in potential Tesla Semi sales revenue per Sawyer Merritt. The NYT separately published a positive review describing the Semi as a "much-needed hit" for Tesla.
- **Tesla Semi listed on European websites:** The Semi now appears on Tesla's European websites including the UK, with a European launch expected no earlier than 2027. Listed specs: 550 km range, 1 kWh/km, 40 tons GCW, three independent motors, MCS 3.2 charging.
- **California adds $1 billion electric truck rebate program:** The new program builds on an existing California initiative; Oregon, Washington, New Jersey, and New York also have or are expected to have EV truck rebate programs, per FutureAzA.
- **Canadian Model 3 acceleration spec revised a second time:** Tesla's new $39,490 CAD (~$29,000 USD) Model 3 Premium RWD launched May 1 at 4.2s 0–100 km/h; revised to 5.2s within 48 hours; revised again around May 19 to 6.2s with no explanation. The 6.2s figure matches European specs for the same LFP/3D7-motor Shanghai-built variant — suggesting it is accurate. The 3D7 motor produces 194 kW/340 Nm versus the prior 3D6's 220 kW/440 Nm.
- **Model S/X Signature Edition delivery event:** Tesla released photos of the Signature Edition vehicles featuring a new "Garnet Red" fleck-finish color. A delivery event livestream is scheduled for May 20 at 8 PM ET.

## Market & Competition

- **Nvidia earnings validate AGI thesis:** Nvidia reported data center revenue that "almost doubled" year-over-year, added $80 billion to its buyback plan, and is generating approximately $81 billion in cash. PE multiple has dropped to 34 against 85% revenue growth. Bhakdi frames this as a critical confirmation that the global AGI buildout remains on track and a bullish tailwind for Tesla.
- **Tesla stock: $445 → $395 then partial recovery:** Tesla fell ~11% over two days before recovering ~3.25% on May 20. Bhakdi attributes the drop to the 10-year yield spiking to ~4.68% and oil price shock from the Strait of Hormuz closure. His base-case target is $490 (the December 16, 2025 all-time high); he flags robotaxi commercial deployment as the most likely catalyst to break $500 sustainably.
- **AV market still near-zero base:** Wave AI CEO Alex Kendall: of 100 million cars produced globally per year, only 15% have ADAS, and fewer than 10,000 robotaxis exist worldwide today. He predicted that in five years, automakers selling cars without self-driving will see demand "fall off a cliff."
- **BYD expanding aggressively into Canada:** BYD is reportedly opening 20 dealerships in Canada, cited as a reputational pressure point for Tesla given the repeated Model 3 spec errors at launch.

## Energy

- **CATL Shenxing 3: 15C charge rate, 3m44s to 80%:** Key enablers are an optimized SEI layer doubling lithium-ion transmission and a graphite "pillar" modification improving ion flow, resulting in ~0.25 milliohm cell resistance (roughly half a comparable LFP cell) and 90% capacity retention after 1,000 full fast-charge cycles (~500,000+ miles). Per The Limiting Factor's Jordan Gizigy, this is a genuinely impressive result.
- **CATL condensed matter battery reality check:** The hyped 500 Wh/kg figure applies only to the aviation variant. The automotive version achieves 350 Wh/kg and 760 Wh/L — within the range of advanced liquid-electrolyte cells (Panasonic 2170 used in Tesla: 271 Wh/kg, 755 Wh/L; Chinese 4680 variants: ~302 Wh/kg). The gel electrolyte does offer a fire-resistance advantage, but energy density is not the step-change implied by earlier announcements.
- **CATL sodium-ion mass production delayed to Q4 2026:** Contrary to earlier assumptions, Naxtra has not yet entered mass production; manufacturing challenges around cathode moisture reactivity remain. CATL signed a 60 GWh / 3-year supply deal with Hyper Strong (~20 GWh/year through 2029), representing only ~2% of CATL's approaching-1-TWh/year total output.
