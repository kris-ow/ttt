This transcript is Elon Musk's quarterly internal SpaceX company address, recapping the company's history (Falcon 1, booster reusability, Falcon Heavy, Dragon, Starlink, Star Shield) before pivoting to a forward-looking thesis: SpaceX's future value will be dominated by AI compute and Grok, layered on top of the existing Starship, Starlink, and launch businesses. Musk frames AI/robots as central to SpaceX's next chapter, alongside continued progress toward a multiplanetary civilization.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
1. **Falcon program milestone:** Musk noted the Falcon program is approaching its 700th flight, crediting the Falcon and launch/manufacturing teams for reusable booster operations that now allow launch and recovery every few days.
2. **Dragon crew total:** SpaceX has flown 78 crew members to date on Dragon, with Musk saying it "won't be long" before the program surpasses 100 astronauts flown to orbit and back.

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **Subscriber and satellite figures:** Starlink now operates in 167 countries with 22 million mobile subscribers and 13 million high-bandwidth subscribers, and has nearly 11,000 satellites in orbit — more than twice the combined total of all other operators. Musk said he expects these subscriber numbers to grow by "more than a factor of 10" over time, and that with Starlink V3 and beyond the constellation could scale to 100,000 satellites.
2. **AI traffic vs. human traffic claim:** Musk relayed a claim he attributed to Cloudflare that, based on current growth trends, AI internet traffic could be roughly 1,000 times human internet traffic within five years.
3. **Long-term internet share prediction:** Musk said he believes Starlink could eventually carry the majority of global internet traffic, potentially over 90% of all internet traffic.

## SpaceX — Starship
1. **Orbital tonnage projections:** Musk said Starship will take SpaceX from roughly 2,500 tons delivered to orbit per year today to over a million tons per year, and said it's "highly likely" the company reaches over a million tons annually and possibly close to 10 million tons per year — tonnage he described as necessary to build a sustainable presence on the Moon and Mars.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **AI revenue overtaking core SpaceX business:** Musk stated SpaceX's AI revenue will exceed all other SpaceX revenue combined as early as next month (September) and will "significantly exceed" all other revenue by the fourth quarter.
2. **10-gigawatt compute target and revenue math:** SpaceX aims to bring 10 gigawatts of AI compute online by the end of next year. Musk said that, depending on value per watt (which he estimated at $30–$50), this could generate $300–$500 billion a year in revenue.
3. **Grok trained on SpaceX data:** Musk announced Grok will be trained on the "sum total" of all SpaceX information, describing SpaceX employees as effectively "parents" of the resulting AI. He gave a timeline of Grok 4.6 arriving in about a week, with 4.7 to follow and be "really pretty special."
4. **AI as long-term majority of SpaceX value:** Musk projected that within four to five years, AI will represent 99% of SpaceX's value, calling the resulting overall company value "astronomical."
5. **Moon-based AI compute vision:** Musk described plans for an AI compute "factory" (referred to as "Star Mind") to be built near existing operations and eventually a facility on the Moon using mass drivers, evoking Heinlein's *The Moon Is a Harsh Mistress*.
