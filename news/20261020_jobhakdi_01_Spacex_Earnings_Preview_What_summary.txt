## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Earnings expectations detailed:** The host cites expected SpaceX Q3 figures: revenue of $12.89 billion (up 65% from Q2's $7.8 billion), adjusted EPS of 15 cents (which would mark the first profitable quarter as a public company), Starlink subscribers at 14.3 million with $4.9 billion in connectivity revenue, AI/compute revenue around $6.4 billion, capex of $19.6 billion, and free cash flow near negative $11.6 billion.
2. **Valuation framed as cheap on forward earnings:** With SpaceX near a $2 trillion market cap and a projected ~$50 billion profit run rate by Q4, the host calculates roughly a 40x price-to-earnings ratio on a run-rate basis, which he argues drops toward a 10x forward multiple if compute capacity doubles to 10 gigawatts as Elon Musk has suggested (the host says he believes the real number will land closer to 5 gigawatts), implying revenue of $200–230 billion and about $200 billion in earnings (before capex) by end of 2027.
3. **Capex and debt flagged as the key earnings risk:** The host says the market's main fear is not SpaceX's revenue or margins but whether rising capex requires additional debt, drawing a parallel to CoreWeave, whose bond yields reportedly jumped from 9% to 15% amid AI-bubble concerns. He expresses confidence SpaceX's finance team will "package" any capex/debt message as skillfully as Amazon and Microsoft did in their recent earnings calls, calling the risk "a question of packaging" rather than fundamentals.
4. **Share unlock reassessment:** A roughly 328 million share unlock (about 11% of float, described elsewhere in the discussion as up to 40% of float) is set to occur around October 24, two days after earnings. After further research, the host now believes nearly all shares involved belong to holders who were already unlocked in prior rounds and are diversifying rather than net-selling, leading him to say he will largely disregard this unlock in his investment strategy, while cautioning his research "can always have holes."
5. **Host's positioning disclosed:** The host says he is entering SpaceX earnings with more than 100% of his target position size (larger than his Nvidia position, smaller than his Tesla position), using short puts at strikes around $150, $147, $145, and $144, and would add more if shares drop toward $140. His overall Tesla-to-SpaceX allocation is roughly 60/40 (stated elsewhere as ~2/3 Tesla, 1/3 SpaceX).
6. **Starlink cited as proof of execution:** The host and a commenter agree that Starlink's path to profitability demonstrates SpaceX's operational execution, which the host says increases his confidence that the orbital AI/compute ("Star Mind") strategy will succeed, calling it something he now sees as effectively without risk.
