Renting old Nvidia H100s
now costs more
than when they were
new.  Elon mentioned
this during
today's
earnings call.  In his
opinion, the value of
computing per watt
will increase over time,
not decrease.  Here is his
logic.  Let's take the
same chip that
consumes the same
power.  As
AI models
improve, the
same watt generates
more useful
intelligence.  And
how much people pay
depends on
intelligence, not
equipment.  Therefore,
old chips do not
lose value like
used cars.
Some of them are even
getting more expensive.  That's why
SpaceX leases
its computing
power on
short-term
contracts at
spot prices,
rather than long-term deals
.  Elon says these
contracts
are renewed at a
higher price per watt,
not a lower one.  He also
says that
hyperscalers
continue to ask for
even more.  SpaceX could
rent out its
computing
power multiple
times if it
agreed with
everyone.  He believes
the deficit will continue for a
long time.
