A Finnish survey firm's "Reputation&Trust 2025" study ranks Tesla last out of 30 companies surveyed in Germany, with the article framing this as evidence of a historic brand reputation collapse tied to CEO Elon Musk's political behavior.

## Market & Competition
1. **Tesla ranks last in Germany with record year-over-year drop:** The Reputation and Trust Analytics survey gave Tesla a score of 2.48 out of 5 in Germany (anything under 2.5 is rated "very bad"), down from 3.25 the prior year — a drop of 0.77 points that the article describes as the largest single-year reputational decline the survey has recorded since it began in 2013. Companies with already-poor reputations scored higher than Tesla, including Temu (2.76), Deutsche Bahn (2.68), Nestlé (2.56), and Shein (2.51); Meta's year-over-year drop of 0.28 points (to 2.95) was about a third the size of Tesla's decline. Of eight surveyed factors (governance, financial performance, leadership, innovation, dialogue, products & services, workplace, responsibility), Tesla scored best in innovation and worst in governance, working conditions, and corporate responsibility.

2. **Tesla also ranked poorly across Nordic countries:** The same survey covered Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Estonia, where Tesla ranked third-from-last in Sweden and Denmark, fourth-from-last in Finland, and fifth-from-last in Norway and Estonia — not last place as in Germany, but still near the bottom in each. In Sweden specifically, Tesla's score fell from a high of 3.88 in 2021 to 3.18 (2023), 2.88 (2024), and 2.43 (2025), which the article attributes partly to Tesla's ongoing labor dispute with Sweden's largest union.

3. **Context cited by the article:** The piece notes other recent negative data points for Tesla in Germany, including a Model Y being named least reliable car in a major German reliability survey, a poll finding 94% of Germans would not buy a Tesla, and Tesla cutting 1,700 jobs at its Berlin gigafactory. It also references a May US brand reputation poll in which Tesla ranked below Temu and UnitedHealth.

## Editorial Stance
The author attributes Tesla's reputation collapse primarily to Musk's political conduct — citing his increasing political involvement, support for Germany's AfD party, and what the article describes as two Nazi salutes on live television in January — arguing this undermined Tesla's prior image as a sustainability-pioneer brand. The author also expresses puzzlement that Tesla investors continue to support Musk, characterizing him as "at best asleep at the wheel, and at worst... near-singlehandedly destroying the company," and cites a Reddit/forum top comment from a self-identified German Tesla driver saying the brand is "burned" in Germany due to Musk's support for the AfD.
