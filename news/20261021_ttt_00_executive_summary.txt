## Brief

- Tesla Q3: revenue flat at $28.05B, GAAP EPS misses by a penny
- FSD attach rate jumps to over 75% of NA deliveries
- Austin Cybercab fleet up 10x since launch per Musk
- Dallas Cybercab launch targeted next month
- SpaceX and Tesla form special committees to weigh merger
- SpaceX Q3: revenue $14.6B, first GAAP-profitable quarter as public company

## Financials
- Tesla Q3 revenue $28.05B (flat YoY), non-GAAP EPS $0.48 beats consensus but down 4% YoY; GAAP EPS $0.22 missed the $0.23 consensus by a penny. GAAP net income fell 43% YoY to $787M.
- Automotive gross margin ex-credits rose to 18.1% from 16.3%; energy margin jumped to 33.1% from 20.4%; services margin hit an all-time high of 15.9%.
- Free cash flow positive at $1.5B+ versus consensus near -$4.5B; cash and investments over $45B. Capex guidance cut to ~$20B for the year; new $30B credit facility secured, undrawn this year. Electrek notes YTD capex of $13.01B trails the ">$25B" full-year guidance that wasn't updated.
- Net income included a $142M mark-to-market loss on Tesla's SpaceX stake, offset by a ~$290M Bitcoin gain.
- R&D spend rose 69% YoY to ~$2.76–3B on AI, Optimus and Cybercab development.

## Robotaxi
- Austin Cybercab fleet grew ~10x since launch a month earlier; cumulative unsupervised miles grew 8x in Q3 with sustained double-digit weekly growth, nearing 2 million cumulative miles by quarter-end (one executive cited ~3 million by call time).
- Dallas robotaxi launch targeted within a month, using the same software stack; six cities currently covered, expanding toward 24/7 operation and higher speeds by year-end.
- Tesla targets Cybercab customer ownership by end of 2026, but Electrek flags a contradiction: unsupervised FSD for customer cars isn't targeted until Q2 2027, meaning early buyers would own a wheel-less, pedal-less car unusable for months.
- Per-mile cost target of $0.30 not yet reached; Taneja says Tesla sees "a path" via fleet scaling.
- Elluswamy clarified Q2 2027 customer-unsupervised timeline means adding cars to Tesla's own Robotaxi geofenced network, not open-ended driving — a narrower promise than Tesla's earlier "drives you anywhere" framing, per Electrek.

## Autonomous Driving
- FSD paid attach rate exceeded 75% of North American deliveries, approximately 20% higher than Q2; over 1.7 million paid FSD customers globally, more than half now subscription-based for the first time.
- Electrek notes the "1.74 million FSD subscriptions" figure (up 67% YoY) counts one-time upfront buyers under a definition tied to Musk's 2025 pay package, and that Tesla doesn't disclose actual monthly payers or retention.
- Four new European approvals (Croatia, Slovenia, Czech Republic, Slovakia); Musk expects EU-wide approval within a few months, "surprised" if beyond Q1.
- Tesla building proprietary AI-based mapping/navigation system, rolling out with V15.

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus entered early production on a high-volume line in Fremont; Musk says reaching 20,000 units/week annualized will take at least a year. Second, larger Optimus factory under construction in Austin.
- Hand design remains the hardest unsolved hardware problem, per Musk and engineers.
- AI5 chip integration into Optimus targeted around April 2027, offering roughly 10x the compute of AI4.
- External customer deliveries targeted around end of 2027, starting with industrial use cases; new non-scripted demo video planned within a month or two.

## Energy
- Megapack nearly sold out through 2027; margins expected to keep improving. Q3 deployments of 13.7 GWh (second-best quarter), but Electrek notes this missed analyst expectations of ~15.9 GWh.
- Energy revenue fell 16% YoY to $2.88B despite higher deployments, partly due to undisclosed "tariff-related customer refunds."
- Megafactory Texas began production (50 GWh planned capacity); first Megapacks shipped using in-house Nevada-made LFP cells. xAI purchased $269M in Megapacks this year.

## Electric Vehicles
- Q3 deliveries fell 2% YoY to 486,532; Tesla delivered 22,141 more than it produced, cutting inventory to 11 days of supply. Tesla blames battery supply constraints; Electrek argues falling ASPs undercut that explanation.
- Model Y was top-selling BEV in Europe and top passenger car in South Korea; records in Australia, Japan, Taiwan.
- Tesla Semi entered production in Q3; Sparks, NV factory had 103 open "Semi" job listings as of October 16.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Tesla and SpaceX have each formed special board committees to evaluate a merger; Musk says he's recused as a conflicted party and stressed "no deal is certain." Discussions ongoing, no agreement reached.
- Musk declined to discuss the merger on Tesla's call, calling it already addressed on SpaceX's call the day before — criticized by panelists as inconsistent.
- Electrek notes Musk's Tesla/SpaceX pay packages include stock grants that vest automatically upon a merger regardless of whether operational milestones are met.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX's SI (AI/compute) segment hit $7.56B in Q3 revenue, up 830% YoY, now SpaceX's largest segment, ahead of Starlink and launch.
- Compute capacity at 1.7 GW as of Sept 30; Musk targets a 13 GW "floor" by end of 2027, with ~200,000 Nvidia GB300 chips/month deployed at Colossus 2 in Memphis.
- Musk says SpaceX could exit 2027 with annualized revenue run-rate between $500B and $1T; separately posted $1T revenue in 2028 is "no longer impossible" — Musk's own projection, not a CFO forecast.
- Terafab: first test chip targeted May/June 2027 at Austin research fab; volume production aspirational for Q3 2028 at Gibbons Creek; capex "tens of billions" through 2028, potentially exceeding $100B long-term, split between SpaceX and Tesla still undetermined.
- Compute payback period currently ~12 months, could compress to ~6 months next year per Musk.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Starlink consumer subscribers reached 13.6 million, up 1.6 million in the quarter; ARPU rose to $69/month from $66.
- SpaceX agreed to buy nationwide 800 MHz spectrum for Starlink Mobile; Musk targets ~100x bandwidth within ~3 years via more satellites (toward ~100,000) and higher-bandwidth satellites.

## SpaceX — Starship
- SpaceX will attempt to catch both booster and ship on flight 15 — first full orbital rocket recovery attempt if successful; further attempts planned before year-end if it fails.
- Flight 14 reached orbit for the first time and delivered 26 Starlink V3 satellites, generating ~10x the Starlink throughput of a Falcon 9 launch, called the first profitable Starship mission.
- SpaceX asked the FAA to permit up to 456 Starship launches/year at Starbase (vs. current 25) and 912 landings/year (vs. 50).

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- SpaceX conducted 35 total launches last quarter, 23 of them its own missions (mostly Starlink).
