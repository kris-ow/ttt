## Brief

- Tesla Q3 revenue flat at $28.05B, GAAP EPS misses by a penny
- Austin Cybercab fleet up 10x since launch, Musk says
- Unsupervised FSD for customer cars pushed to Q2 2027, geofenced only
- Tesla and SpaceX form special committees to explore merger
- SpaceX Q3: GAAP profit, $14.6B revenue, SI compute now largest segment
- Dallas Cybercab launch targeted next month

## Robotaxi
- Austin Cybercab fleet grew roughly 10x in the month since launch; cumulative unsupervised miles up 8x in Q3 with sustained double-digit weekly growth, Musk and Taneja said. Service nearing 2 million cumulative unsupervised miles by quarter-end.
- Dallas launch targeted within a month, expanding the six-city footprint toward 24/7 operation and higher speeds by year-end, per Elluswamy.
- Customer-owned Cybercab deliveries targeted by end of 2026, but Moravy confirmed all current Austin-built units go to Tesla's own fleet; customer FSD vehicles joining the robotaxi network targeted around Q2 2027. Electrek notes this means a Cybercab delivered with no steering wheel could sit unusable for months.
- Per-mile cost target of $0.30 not yet reached; Taneja says Tesla sees "a path" via fleet scaling.
- Cybercab line has 50% fewer parts than any prior Tesla vehicle and is running well below true capacity, Moravy said.

## Autonomous Driving
- Unsupervised FSD for customer-owned cars now targeted Q2 2027 — narrower than prior promises, as it only activates inside Tesla's existing Robotaxi geofenced zones, not open driving, Elluswamy said.
- FSD paid attach rate topped 75% of North American deliveries, up ~20 points sequentially; global paid FSD customers surpassed 1.7 million, over half on subscription. Electrek notes the "1.74M subscriptions" figure counts old one-time purchases too, a definition tied to Musk's 2025 pay package milestones.
- EU-wide approval hoped for within months, no later than Q1 2027 per Musk; China approval remains difficult, per Elluswamy.
- Tesla building its own AI-based mapping/navigation system, rolling out with V15.

## Humanoid Bots
- High-volume Optimus line entered early production in Fremont; reaching 20,000 units/week annualized rate expected to take at least a year.
- External customer shipments targeted around end of 2027; hand design remains the hardest unsolved hardware problem.
- Optimus Academy: Tesla plans 10,000–20,000 bots for self-play training data, lacking a large fleet like cars have.
- AI5 chip integration into Optimus targeted ~April 2027. New non-scripted demo video planned within 1-2 months. Second Optimus factory under construction in Austin.

## Financials
- Revenue $28.05B flat YoY; non-GAAP EPS $0.48 beat consensus but down 4% YoY; GAAP EPS $0.22 missed consensus by a penny. GAAP net income fell 43% to $787M.
- Operating margin fell to 2.6% from 5.8% a year ago as opex rose 39%, R&D up 69% on Optimus/Cybercab/SI spend.
- Capex guidance cut to ~$20B for the year; new $30B credit facility secured, no draws planned in 2026. YTD capex of $13.01B trails the prior "$25B+" guidance.
- Free cash flow positive at $1.5B+; cash and investments over $45B. Net income hit by $142M SpaceX mark-to-market loss, offset by ~$290M Bitcoin gain.
- Electrek flags inconsistency: Tesla cites battery supply as limiting auto volumes while average selling prices keep falling and inventory was drawn down — a sign the constraint framing may not fully hold.

## Electric Vehicles
- Q3 deliveries fell 2% YoY to 486,532; "other models" (Cybertruck, Semi, S/X) down 48% to 8,295. Tesla built 22,141 fewer vehicles than it delivered, cutting inventory to 11 days of supply.
- Automotive gross margin ex-credits rose to 18.1% from 16.3%. Record backlog (highest in four years, no figure disclosed); Model Y led BEV sales in Europe and passenger car sales in South Korea.
- Roadster reveal detailed: three variants (Performance, Thrust, Rocket) with SpaceX-derived thrust engines; production still targeted for 2028, only 4-5 prototypes exist today. Musk called it a side project, not core to Tesla's value thesis.

## Energy
- Megapack "almost sold out" through 2027; Q3 deployments of 13.7 GWh (second-best quarter) but revenue fell 16% YoY to $2.88B on undisclosed tariff-related customer refunds. Gross margin rebounded to 33.1% from 20.4%.
- Megafactory Texas began production (50 GWh planned capacity); first Megapacks shipped using in-house Nevada-made LFP cells.
- "Megapod" distributed compute/power product remains early-stage, more clarity expected Q1-Q2 2027.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Tesla and SpaceX have each formed special board committees to explore a merger, Musk confirmed on SpaceX's earnings call; shareholder approval would be required at both companies, and Musk said he personally cannot participate in talks. Electrek notes Musk's pay packages at both companies reportedly vest automatically on a merger regardless of operational milestones.
- SpaceX Q3 revenue $14.58B (+161% YoY), GAAP net income $4.40B versus a year-ago loss — its first profitable quarter since its June 2026 IPO.
- Analyst bear cases diverge sharply from market pricing: CFRA and Phillip Securities rate SPCX a sell ($115/$75 targets), Morningstar values it at $63/share, versus a reported close around $148.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SI (AI/compute) segment hit $7.56B in Q3 revenue, up 830% YoY, now SpaceX's largest segment, with 1.7 GW of compute installed as of Sept 30.
- Musk targets a "floor" of 13 GW of AI compute by end of 2027, calling power/cooling/infrastructure — not chips — the binding constraint; plans to deploy ~200,000 Nvidia GB300 chips/month this year.
- Musk said 2027 exit ARR could land between $500B and $1T, and posted that $1T in 2028 revenue is "no longer impossible" — both framed as Musk's own projections, not confirmed forecasts; CFO's formal guide is $33-37B for Q1 2027 alone.
- Terafab: first test chip targeted May/June 2027 in Austin, volume production ~Q3 2028 at Gibbons Creek; capital needs in the "tens of billions" through 2028, potentially exceeding $100B long-term, split between Tesla and SpaceX still undetermined.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Starlink reached 13.6 million subscribers, up 1.6 million in the quarter; ARPU rose to $69/month from $66.
- SpaceX agreed to buy a nationwide 800 MHz spectrum block for Starlink mobile; Musk projects ~100x bandwidth growth within 3 years.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Flight 15 will attempt the first full catch of both booster and ship; Musk called success "most likely" but not guaranteed, with further attempts planned if it fails. Targeting weekly flights in H1 2027, rising to twice-weekly-plus by year-end.
- SpaceX asked the FAA to allow up to 456 Starship launches/year at Starbase (from 25 currently) and 912 landings/year.
- Flight 14 reached orbit for the first time and delivered 26 Starlink V3 satellites, delivering roughly 10x the throughput of a single Falcon 9 Starlink launch.
