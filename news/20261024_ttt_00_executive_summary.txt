## Robotaxi
- Tesla's safety report: 3.3 million autonomous miles driven, zero injuries or airbag deployments; unsupervised miles grew eightfold in Q3 to near 2 million by quarter-end. Elon said the Austin Cybercab fleet grew tenfold since its early-September launch.
- Ashok Elluswamy said Tesla operates robotaxi service in six cities (Austin, Dallas, Houston, Miami, Orlando, Tampa); Tesla's robotaxi account separately named four future target cities — Las Vegas, Jacksonville, Phoenix, San Antonio — with no dates set, and a Cybertruck was spotted testing in San Antonio.
- Austin service hours extended to 1 a.m. and the service area expanded roughly 8 square miles to cover the F1 track ahead of a 400,000-person race weekend; Cybercabs also began running on a previously Model Y-only I-35 stretch. Ashok targets 24/7 operation, highway driving, and harder-weather capability by year-end.
- Texas DMV data show Tesla registered 69 more Cybercabs, cumulative total 420; separate tracking showed total Texas robotaxi registrations (mostly Model Y) rising from 769 to 858 week-over-week, with total registrations (including Florida) reportedly crossing 1,000 for the first time.
- Lars Moravy said Tesla is trying to get Cybercabs to customers by year-end 2026, but most units will stay in Tesla's own network; Elon said robotaxi financial impact is minimal this quarter but should be a "big deal" by Q3/Q4 next year. Tesla has not yet hit its 30-cents-per-mile cost target.
- NHTSA's inquiry into how Tesla certified a wheel/pedal-free vehicle is due a response October 30; separately, NHTSA proposed rule changes explicitly citing Cybercab that would remove federal barriers (e.g., manual turn-signal requirements) for such vehicles — a proposal, not yet finalized.

## Autonomous Driving
- Over 75% of new North American deliveries took paid FSD at delivery in Q3, up from 55% prior quarter; paying FSD customers reached 1.74 million, up 67% year-over-year. CFO Vaibhav Taneja confirmed FSD revenue is approaching roughly $1 billion/year and said Tesla will begin disclosing FSD revenue going forward.
- FSD v15 shares its stack with Cybercab/Model Y robotaxis and is coming to customer cars soon; Tesla expects to connect the first customer-owned vehicles to the robotaxi fleet around Q2 2027, via a VIN-based registration system found in app code.
- FSD is live in nine EU countries with 100,000+ users; Elon expects EU-wide approval within a few months, "surprised" if it slips past Q1 2027. South Korea's transport minister confirmed Tesla has applied for FSD approval there.

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus production has begun on a high-performance line in Fremont, with first units now coming off the line. Elon said reaching 20,000 units/week will take at least a year, with roughly 10,000 issues to resolve, targeting resolution around end of 2027 before supplying industrial customers first.
- A video showing Optimus performing unprogrammed actions may arrive in a month or two; Optimus is expected to get the AI5 chip (~10x current compute) around April. Construction has begun on a second Optimus plant in Austin targeting 10 million units/year capacity.

## Financials
- Q3: $0.48 EPS vs. $0.45 expected, revenue ~$28 billion, operating margin just 2.6% as R&D/AI/new-product spend rose ~40% YoY. Deliveries were ~486,000, the second straight quarter above 480,000.
- 2026 capex guidance cut to ~$20 billion from over $25 billion; cash on hand hit a record $45 billion; free cash flow ~$1.5 billion. Capex expected to rise again next year, reaching "tens of billions" by 2028.
- Shares fell ~3% post-earnings amid broader Nasdaq AI-spending jitters, but most analysts raised targets: Piper Sandler $475, Mizuho $460, JP Morgan $430, UBS $425, Morgan Stanley $410; JP Morgan cited rising odds of a Tesla–SpaceX merger.
- Tesla raised financing APRs on most Model Y trims from 1.99% to 2.49%.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Elon confirmed Tesla and SpaceX have each formed special committees to evaluate a potential merger; he said merger isn't inevitable and shareholders must decide, though he personally favors it. Tesla's 10-Q frames approval as requiring shareholder vote; SpaceX's filing frames it as requiring Elon's controlling-shareholder approval. No merger item is on Tesla's December 3 annual meeting ballot.
- Bloomberg reported (unconfirmed) Elon wants to close a merger before the end of the current US presidential term; Reuters reported (unconfirmed) some insiders worry his public comments could delay the process.
- A retail shareholder petition urging the board toward a merger reportedly gathered ~3,800 signers representing ~11 million shares within 24 hours. Organizers floated unconfirmed exchange-ratio speculation (e.g., ~3:1, or a personal 4.2 ratio wish) and speculative valuations (Tesla $8.5T, SpaceX $7.5T, combined up to $100T) — all explicitly unconfirmed estimates, not agreed terms.

## Energy
- Elon said Tesla has sold out almost all Megapack capacity through 2027, driven partly by AI data-center demand. Energy segment margin hit 33% this quarter, but mostly from one-time tariff rebates and supplier reimbursements; energy revenue actually fell 16% YoY. The Texas Megapack plant began production this quarter.
- Fort Bend County, Texas approved tax breaks for Tesla's proposed $10 billion "Crystal Sun" solar plant (~9,700 jobs), targeting solar panel production in early 2029; location not yet finalized.

## Electric Vehicles
- Giga Berlin may expand toward 1 million units/year (currently ~7,000/week); permits reportedly planned for December with a stated 10-year build-out window. Unconfirmed speculation that Model 3 production could shift to Berlin from China.
- China's Model Y led all models in September sales by over 10% but was down 2% year-over-year; Tesla sold 93,000 units in China in July, per cited data.
- US EV adoption across all brands has fallen to ~5%, down from a peak near 11% around IRA expiration.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- NASA published new Artemis/Starship lunar lander milestones: propellant transfer and "Cabin 1" testing in Q1 2027; landing leg and airlock demos plus barge transport to Cape Canaveral in Q2 2027; Artemis III and an uncrewed demo tanker flight in Q3 2027. New images of Cabin 1 (built at Starbase) and Artemis III docking adapter renderings were also released.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile reportedly met the FCC on October 20 to jointly lobby against rule changes favoring SpaceX's incoming direct-to-cell Starlink Mobile network — a reversal from carriers' earlier dismissive stance, per the source.
