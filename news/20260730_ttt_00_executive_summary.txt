## Brief

- Tesla builds 10 millionth vehicle; Electrek says growth stalled since 2023
- FSD supervised: 5.2x safer than manual driving, 65M km, EU data
- Cybertruck tooling lawsuit threatens production, inventory low "within days"
- SpaceX Flight 13: booster landing burn fails, ship survives soft splashdown
- SpaceX wins $1.6B Space Force contract for 18 Falcon 9 launches
- Tesla Energy lands $225M Megapack order from Neoen in Australia

## Autonomous Driving
- FSD supervised is 5.2x safer than manual driving across 65 million km in five EU countries over the trailing four months, per Tesla data; Musk said supervised FSD "unequivocally saves lives." France has signaled it won't yet vote to approve EU-wide, which requires countries representing 65% of the population.
- Volvo discontinued lidar on the EX90/ES90, offering Norwegian buyers €1,800 compensation with no retrofit option; Musk said "I did try to warn them," reiterating his vision-only stance.
- A whistleblower lawsuit alleges a supervisor at a Houston FSD training operations center oversaw 38 vehicles alone; futureaza's Brian disputes the framing (each car had a safety driver) and says the suit likely missed its filing window.
- An unverified Reddit post shows a vehicle apparently drifting into a concrete barrier captioned "FSD14 light hits a wall" — origin and cause unconfirmed.

## Robotaxi
- Jo Bhakdi pushed back his 15,000-Robotaxi-by-year-end call to roughly March 2027 for "10,000 plus," citing a slowing ramp, though he still expects some inflection point before December.
- futureaza's Herbert argues Tesla's stock drop reflected reset expectations on Robotaxi pace rather than financials, framing the current city-by-city phase as a precursor to a later state-by-state scaling jump once a safer FSD version (v15) is ready.
- Software update 2026.26 deepens Grok integration (voice control of Spotify via glove box); new "memory" features let the car learn preferred routes/parking, with speculation this could extend to fleet-wide pickup/drop-off learning.
- A robotaxi update silenced in-cabin turn-signal/hazard sounds, prompting unconfirmed speculation of a new FSD version.

## Humanoid Bots
- Herbert expects Optimus to follow Robotaxi's "slow then sudden" pattern: a near-term hardware version used mainly for training data collection, followed by a mass-production version (H2 2027 per his estimate) targeting 10 million units/year once the factory ramps.

## Financials
- Q2 revenue hit a company record ~$28B (vs. ~$26B consensus), automotive revenue up 23% YoY, trailing-12-month revenue topped $100B for the first time.
- Free cash flow came in near -$1B, better than a feared -$3B, against ~$25B capex and $43.5B cash on hand.
- Margins missed on a sharp drop in ZEV credit revenue after a CAFE standards change; Herbert expects recovery toward 22%+ in Q3/Q4 as FSD subscriptions scale (~1.5M paid FSD users, ~45% subscription).
- Per Bhakdi (relayed, unverified), only ~2 points of Tesla's stock decline is attributable to the actual financials, with the rest tied to a broader AI-sector selloff.

## Electric Vehicles
- Electrek: Tesla hit 10 million vehicles built, but deliveries fell from a 2023 peak of 1.81M to 1,636,129 in 2025 (-9% YoY), with installed capacity over 2.375M units far exceeding current output — a gap the article frames as demand-constrained, not supply-constrained.
- Musk said Tesla will open-source the Model S/X design and software, mirroring the original Roadster approach.
- Canadian Model Y delivery estimates now stretch into early 2027, attributed largely to Giga Berlin (Canada's sole supply source) still adding and training shifts rather than to demand.
- Tesla's emergency lawsuit against Angstrom Automotive Group alleges blocked access to custom Cybertruck tooling, with remaining component inventory potentially running out within days and no alternate supplier able to produce the parts.
- Real-world 70mph range test: Model Y beat its EPA estimate (310 vs. 306 miles); Rivian R2 badly missed its rating (253 vs. 330 miles).

## Market & Competition
- Ford's EV unit lost $900M in Q2 on revenue down 58% YoY, even as Ford beat overall EPS estimates and raised full-year EBIT guidance; Ford plans a sub-$30K NACS/LFP electric pickup for H2.
- Mercedes unveiled the GLA EV (up to ~350 mi range, 800V, 320kW peak charging); Ferrari reportedly hit its 2026 Luce EV sales target after two months, ~500 units sold.
- TSLA closed at $298.32, down 2.97%, alongside a broader AI-stock selloff that Bhakdi attributes to a forced hedge-fund liquidation rather than fundamentals.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Flight 13: booster completed a full 33-engine boost-back but only 8 of 13 landing-burn engines relit, causing a hard splashdown; the ship itself made a soft Indian Ocean landing, deployed 20 functional next-gen V3 Starlink satellites, and completed an in-space engine relight that had been aborted on Flight 12.
- Musk said the flight intentionally used higher acceleration to test heat-shield tile retention and called the test successful; he confirmed a recovery ship is being sent for the floating S40 and said the splashdown was precise enough it would have been caught by tower arms.
- Flight 14 will attempt a tower catch of the ship and an orbital insertion (vs. prior suborbital flights), with the booster again attempting a water landing.
- Musk reiterated a Mars crewed-mission timeline of roughly 5–7 years, with an uncrewed lander a few years earlier.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- SpaceX won a $1.6 billion Space Force task order for 18 Falcon 9 launches from Vandenberg through end of 2027, supporting missile detection and battlefield sensing — the largest publicly announced task order yet under NSSL Phase 3 Lane One.
- The FAA proposed waiving requirements under 13 environmental/historic-preservation statutes for commercial launch licensing (safety and national-security reviews stay intact); a 30-day comment period and likely litigation lie ahead.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Flight 13 deployed 20 fully functional V3 satellites that exchanged two-way RF and laser communication and returned telemetry (including heat-shield imagery) before burning up after ~20 minutes — confirmed engineering communication only, not integration into the operational constellation.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Bhakdi set a SpaceX price target of $65–75 for December, arguing even $70 would undervalue the company if Starlink-adjacent "Colossus" progress and further capital raises materialize this year.

## Energy
- Tesla secured a $225 million Megapack contract with Neoen for a second-stage Australian battery storage project — Neoen's 12th Tesla project in the country, pushing combined capacity beyond 2GW.
