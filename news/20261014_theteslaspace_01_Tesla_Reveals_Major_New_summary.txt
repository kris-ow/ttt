This video covers four developments: a new Tesla patent revealing tactile ("touch") sensor technology for Optimus's hands, the two-year anniversary of the Cybercab reveal alongside its real-world fleet expansion in Austin and Dallas, SpaceX's acquisition of low-frequency spectrum to expand Starlink Direct to Cell, and the upcoming Starship Flight 15 attempt to catch the ship stage for the first time.

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Optimus tactile-sensor patent:** Tesla filed a patent for a "three-dimensional soft adaptive array of tactile sensors" designed to give Optimus's hand a sense of touch, using flexible capacitive or resistive sensor "pixels" that curve to fit the hand's shape (thumb, palm, fingers) rather than lying flat like a sticker. The patent reportedly focuses heavily on how to mass-produce the sensors cheaply and reliably, and hints at eventually extending tactile sensing across the robot's outer shell ("full tactile skin"). The host notes this is only a patent application, not a confirmed product.
2. **Context on hand engineering difficulty:** The host says Elon Musk has reportedly called the hand and forearm the most difficult engineering challenge in the Optimus program, with reports that hand assembly has been a bottleneck slowing robot production (unconfirmed, attributed to reports). Tesla has stated its newest robot arm has 22 degrees of freedom, roughly double the previous design, and job postings describe the hand as a tendon-based mechanism with advanced tactile sensors. Optimus Gen 2 previously demonstrated fingertip tactile sensors by transferring an egg between hands without breaking it (demoed in a past presentation). Tesla says it won't reveal Optimus Gen 3 in full until close to production, to prevent competitors from copying the design.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab fleet expansion in Austin:** Tesla opened Cybercab rides to the public in Austin on September 4, and within about a month the fleet grew to over 300 units — roughly an eightfold increase from the prototype stage two years ago. Tesla AI chief Ashok Elluswamy marked the two-year anniversary of the Cybercab's reveal at the "We, Robot" event by noting the cabs are "everywhere in Austin" and "soon in Dallas." Cybercab has no steering wheel or pedals and cannot be manually driven.
2. **Dallas expansion underway:** Tesla already runs driverless robotaxi rides in Dallas using the Model Y, and expanded its Dallas service area by about 50% in late August. Cybercabs have reportedly been spotted queuing outside Tesla's Dallas robotaxi hub, with some reportedly being tested on public roads without a driver. Tesla has not announced an official Dallas launch date, with Elon Musk joking the company is "still learning to be punctual."

## SpaceX — Starlink
1. **Spectrum acquisition to expand phone coverage:** On October 8, SpaceX agreed to purchase nationwide low-frequency spectrum licenses (up to 14 MHz in the 800 MHz band) from investment firm Grain Management, pending FCC approval, with the price undisclosed. Elon Musk called this the final critical piece of spectrum SpaceX needs for complete phone coverage in the US, as low-frequency signals penetrate buildings better than the higher frequencies used for outdoor satellite coverage. This follows SpaceX's prior $17 billion spectrum purchase from EchoStar, and the FCC has approved plans for 15,000 next-generation Starlink mobile satellites. A Verizon spokesperson dismissed the purchase as "just empty talk," noting SpaceX has yet to build the network.
2. **Starlink's track record cited against skeptics:** The host notes that in 2022 the FCC withdrew nearly $900 million in rural broadband funding previously awarded to Starlink, and a fiber-industry-backed study predicted Starlink couldn't adequately serve more than half of those rural customers. Starlink has since grown from roughly 1 million subscribers at that time to over 13 million subscribers across more than 160 countries today, becoming one of the 10 largest internet providers in the US. In-flight Starlink Wi-Fi now reaches over 1,400 commercial aircraft across more than 40 airlines as of August; United Airlines claims its Starlink Wi-Fi is 50 times faster than prior regional aircraft systems, and Ookla measured average in-flight download speeds of 150 Mbps on Starlink-equipped planes.

## SpaceX — Starship
1. **Flight 15 targeting ship-catch attempt:** On October 10, Elon Musk said Starship's 15th flight will hopefully occur in 4–6 weeks (roughly mid-to-late November), with the main goal of catching both the Super Heavy booster and the ship with the launch tower in a single flight — which would be the first complete recovery of an orbiter since the Space Shuttle. Musk estimated the odds of catching the ship on the first try at about 50–60%. He also said he hopes to reuse the same booster and spacecraft on Flight 16, continuing the booster-catch milestone first achieved exactly two years earlier, on October 13, 2024.
