## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla delivered 486,532 vehicles in Q3 2026, beating the company-compiled consensus of 461,974 by 24,558 units and topping every individual analyst estimate, including JPMorgan's 482,000. Deliveries fell 2.1% from Q3 2025's record 497,099 (a quarter inflated by pre-tax-credit-expiry buying) but rose 1.3% from Q2's 480,126.
- Tesla delivered 22,141 more vehicles than it produced (464,391), the second straight quarter of inventory drawdown, clearing the roughly 50,000-unit excess built up in Q1. Model 3/Y made up 478,237 of deliveries; Other Models (Cybertruck, Semi, remaining S/X) totaled 8,295, down 48% YoY.
- Year-to-date deliveries hit 1,324,681, up 8.8% versus 1,217,902 over the same period in 2025.
- Tesla rolled a 16-inch center touchscreen out to the entire US Model 3 lineup, following the same change in China a day earlier. Performance trim range rose from 309 to 316 miles and power is now described as "improved" to 528 hp (up from 510 hp at 2024 launch); Premium AWD's 0-60 improved from 4.2s to 3.8s. Pricing is unchanged across trims ($36,990–$54,990). V2L remains unavailable on US Model 3 despite launching on China's version.
- Tesla launched "Emergency Drive Away," letting drivers pull away from a V2/V3/V4 Supercharger stall while still plugged in. Tesla says the feature damages both vehicle and charger — up to $25,000 in Supercharger damage depending on site — and requires a mandatory service visit, but has not said who pays for the damage or what penalties apply for misuse.
- Per Sawyer Merritt's roundup: Tesla launched V2L for 2027 Model 3/Model Y in China, Australia and New Zealand; Model 3 added a grey 19" Nova wheel finish and "Zen Grey" interior in those markets; Tesla's global fleet logged 69 million charging sessions in Q3, up 29% YoY.

## Robotaxi
- Cybercab registrations with the Texas DMV reached 158 (up from an estimated ~59 two days earlier per Pioneer Lands tracker, cited by Jo Bhakdi), with 52 spotted on the road — a pace Bhakdi says is running roughly 500% ahead of the original Robotaxi launch's first month. He projects over 1,000 Cybercabs by December at current deployment rates, calling ~1,800–2,000 units the threshold for proving unit economics and triggering a re-rating; these projections are speculative.

## Humanoid Bots
- A commentator (Jeff Lutz) states Tesla has completed an Optimus plant with a stated 10 million unit annual capacity, part of a broader $30 billion buildout that includes Terafab and solar farm construction, which he likens to China's industrial expansion of the late 1990s/2000s.
- Per Sawyer Merritt: Tesla reportedly cut RAM on its AI5 chip by 50% (to 72 GB) and AI6 by 33% (to 144 GB) to help scale Optimus production, per a cited post — no engineering rationale or trade-off detail given. Separately, Boston Dynamics unveiled a next-gen humanoid hand with 13 degrees of freedom and 100+ lb payload, a competitive data point versus Optimus.

## Energy
- Tesla deployed 13.7 GWh of energy storage in Q3 2026, up 9.6% YoY and slightly above Q2's 13.5 GWh, but missed the 15.9 GWh analysts expected and fell short of the 14.2 GWh record set in Q4 2025.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX reportedly selected Nvidia exclusively as its AI infrastructure partner across terrestrial "AI factories" and what is described as its first orbital compute system ("Starmine"), per Farzad FM; no technical or financial details disclosed.
- A commentator (Investing Against the Grain) claims SpaceX combined with xAI compute could exceed Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure's combined scale by December, contingent on Musk's stated goal of $100 billion in annual recurring revenue — not yet achieved, and still short of AWS's cited ~$167 billion in annual revenue.
