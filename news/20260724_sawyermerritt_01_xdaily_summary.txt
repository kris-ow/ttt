## Key Posts / Themes

1. **New FSD safety and usage data:** Tesla released updated FSD (Supervised) safety statistics: miles per major collision are now 5,690,675 for FSD-supervised driving vs. 2,076,014 for manual driving with active safety features, 859,465 for manual driving without active safety features, and a U.S. average of 698,781 — implying FSD-supervised driving has a meaningfully lower collision rate than manual driving. Separately, Tesla's fleet-wide FSD usage is now running at an annualized rate of 13.7 billion miles/year, up 241% year-over-year, and the fleet is averaging 37.5 million miles per day on FSD, up from 28.8M/day "a few months ago" and 14.4M/day "earlier this year" — Tesla frames this as roughly 1,000 miles driven every 2.3 seconds. Tesla also published a new video showcasing FSD collision-avoidance instances.

2. **Elon Musk's Economist interview (90 minutes, filmed at Giga Texas):** Musk discussed several topics with notable investor relevance: on politics, he said "I think I got a little too involved in politics... I got carried away," framing the DOGE effort as an attempt to address the U.S. deficit; on retail investors, he called himself "a big fan," saying they are "on balance... very insightful and have very long-term views"; on his taxes, he stated he pays about 40% national income tax on stock options plus roughly 5% more due to California residency (~45% total), plus an estate tax of roughly another 45% upon death; on SpaceX, he said one reason he took SpaceX public was to let the public own a piece of the company, since private companies face shareholder-count limits.

3. **SpaceX Starship Flight 13 rescheduled:** SpaceX moved its 13th Starship test flight launch target to Friday, July 24 at 6:45 PM ET.

4. **Starlink Mini pricing update:** The Starlink Mini Kit is now available in the U.S. for $16/month for 12 months, with standard service plans starting at $55/month — a pricing move relevant to Starlink subscriber growth and unit economics.

5. **Competitive and industry context (autonomy market):** Aurora launched its second-generation driverless trucks, planning deployment across 10 driverless routes in the U.S. Sun Belt. Ford announced a partnership with Apple to integrate Apple Maps into Ford's upcoming Universal Electric Vehicle (UEV) Platform and its next-generation self-driving development. The IIHS released a 25-page study recommending standardized autonomous-vehicle safety data reporting (uniform VMT reporting, standardized crash classifications, eliminating duplicate crash reports) — relevant to future AV regulatory frameworks Tesla's Robotaxi program would operate under. AT&T's CEO pushed back on the idea that Starlink will become a major wireless/broadband competitor, saying new entrants like Starlink are "coming to the game very late" in a mature industry.

6. **Adam Jonas SpaceX note:** Jonas (analyst) published commentary arguing that in space, "failure is not just an option... it's a vital part of the testing and improvement process," pushing back on the "failure is not an option" framing often applied to space programs — relevant context ahead of the Starship test flight.
