## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Starlink V3: major capacity upgrade unveiled:** SpaceX launched a new website detailing next-gen Starlink V3 satellites, claiming 1 Tbps downlink capacity (~10x higher than V2) and 160 Gbps uplink capacity (~22x higher than V2), plus 2,048 downlink beams and 2,048 uplink beams. Separately, SpaceX said V3's phased arrays use upgraded chips supporting a "~64x increase in throughput handled per modem chip," aimed at improving simultaneous customer service across densely and sparsely populated regions. A new rendering of the V3 satellite was also released. These are substantial, specific capacity claims that materially raise the ceiling on Starlink's revenue-generating potential per satellite.

2. **Starship test flight and new Florida pad:** SpaceX held a Starship test flight, with Sawyer Merritt sharing the livestream link and a "Happy Starship launch day" post. A separate post highlighted the new Starship launch pad in Florida, and another shared a comparison video showing Starship V3 clears the pad notably faster than prior versions — signaling faster turnaround/launch cadence potential.

3. **SpaceX AI1 satellite gets major power boost:** Elon Musk stated SpaceX increased peak power of its upcoming AI compute satellite (AI1) by 67% to ~250kW (up from 150kW) and average power by 33% to 160kW (up from 120kW). Musk said this will be sufficient to handle an Nvidia Rubin NVL72 rack (72 Rubin GPUs). This is a concrete, quantified escalation in SpaceX's orbital data center ambitions and a signal of scaling compute infrastructure investment.

4. **Tesla Semi production tour and misc product/market news:** Tesla will showcase the production-spec 2026 Tesla Semi at the Mid-Atlantic Clean Cities & Communities Advanced Transportation Summit in Harrisonburg, VA (July 21-23) and at Tesla Naperville near Chicago (July 20-21) — an indication Semi production/rollout visibility is increasing. Separately, Tesla launched Model 3/Y orders in Uruguay, its third South American market. Tesla also released a video on its 4680 cell line at Giga Berlin, and posted about a new $225 kids' balance bike product (low-signal but shows continued merchandise/brand expansion). European BEV sales data showed over 1 million units sold in H1 2026 (1.24 million total EVs, up 33.7% YoY) — a positive read on European EV demand broadly, not Tesla-specific.
