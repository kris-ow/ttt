## Robotaxi

- **Texas DOT Executive Director endorses imminent scale-up.** Mark Williams, Executive Director of the Texas Department of Transportation, stated at the 2026 Texas Innovation Invitational that he rode a production Cybercab firsthand and that "the introduction of the Cybercab will dramatically scale these operations over the coming months." Williams also confirmed Tesla is currently running Model Y vehicles in FSD robotaxi mode across Austin, Dallas, and Houston. Bhakdi notes Williams almost certainly received prior briefings from Tesla before making the statement publicly.

- **100+ Cybercabs visible at Giga Texas.** Drone footage credited to Joe Tegtmeyer and a count by Sawyer Merritt showed more than 100 Cybercabs in the Giga Texas lot. Some units were identified as production-spec vehicles without steering wheels — consistent with units destined for commercial deployment rather than supervised testing.

- **No public evidence of supervised Cybercab FSD testing yet.** Despite the production accumulation, there is still no strong public evidence of Cybercabs running FSD under supervision even for test purposes, per analysis by Genma JP cited by Bhakdi.

- **Bhakdi's independent estimate: 1,000+ Cybercabs deployed nationally.** Bhakdi states his own calculation puts Tesla's deployed Cybercab count at "strongly over 1,000" across multiple cities — materially above the RobotaxiTracker.com active fleet figure. He argues Tesla could reach Waymo's ~3,000-vehicle fleet size within approximately five weeks at current production pace.

- **Cybercab EPA certification unlocks full commercial sale.** The EPA certificate of conformity confirms the Cybercab meets all federal emissions requirements and can be legally introduced into U.S. commerce. The self-certification pathway means no unit-count cap applies.

- **EPA filing reveals better-than-expected specs.** The certification documents disclose: single front-wheel drive motor, 219 hp, ~48 kWh battery, 3,113 lb curb weight, 418-mile equivalent all-electric range and 375-mile highway range. The 418-mile figure is materially above Tesla's own stated 300+ mile target and exceeds the base Model 3 despite a smaller battery — suggesting significant efficiency gains from the purpose-built design. The submission vehicle had 2,100+ miles on the odometer; EPA certified useful life is 150,000 miles.

- **Cybercab vs. Waymo unit economics framed as 1-to-9 advantage.** Bhakdi argues the Cybercab's 418-mile range is roughly 3× a Waymo vehicle's operational range, and the Cybercab costs approximately one-third the price of a Waymo — producing a structural 9× cost-efficiency advantage before accounting for maintenance.

- **Bhakdi's deployment timeline: June or August.** Bhakdi places his highest probability on commercial robotaxi deployment beginning in June 2026, with August as the next likely window. He and his co-host forecast 10,000–20,000 Cybercabs deployed by year-end, noting prior consensus had been as high as 50,000. His end-of-year Tesla price target of $800–$1,000 is explicitly conditional on deployment scaling to 15,000 units.

- **Cybercab interior designed around real-world trip data.** Cybercab lead engineer Eric E stated at a December 2024 meetup that ~80% of vehicle trips involve two passengers or fewer; the vehicle was designed around that reality. Payload capacity of ~617 lbs (curb to GVW) is described as sufficient for two passengers plus multiple large suitcases. The Cybercab is the first Tesla vehicle built using the unboxed parallel-assembly manufacturing process.

## Autonomous Driving

- **Zero FSD-at-fault incidents for four consecutive months.** The latest NHTSA filing (covering April 15–May 15) shows one new incident: a stationary robotaxi rear-ended by a pickup truck — not Tesla's fault. The two prior incidents in March were also other vehicles rear-ending stopped robotaxis. Tesla has recorded zero at-fault FSD incidents since early February 2026. Across 18 total NHTSA incidents since June 2022, Bhakdi notes almost none were attributed to Tesla fault.

- **FSD 14.3.4 characterized as a "very big leap."** Bhakdi describes version 14.3.4, launched the prior week, as more than a point release — framing version 14.3 as a major architectural overhaul enabling faster iteration, with 14.3.4 now receiving "crazy positive reviews."

- **New Jersey legislation would effectively ban Tesla AVs; Tesla fighting back.** Tesla sent emails directly to New Jersey owners urging them to oppose proposed state legislation, calling it an "arbitrary roadblock" to "life-changing technology." This is a real but localized domestic headwind.

- **Dutch Transport Minister defends FSD approval.** The minister publicly stated the RDW's provisional approval of FSD (Supervised) was based on independent RDW testing — not Tesla-provided data — and noted Teslas using FSD have driven 24 million miles on Dutch roads "without any noteworthy incidents."

- **Tesla submits FSD (Supervised) application in Taiwan.** Tesla officially entered the regulatory examination process with Taiwan's Vehicle Safety Inspection Center, expanding its international approval campaign.

## Energy

- **Megapack Lathrop lots at peak observed levels.** Drone footage showed Tesla's Lathrop Megafactory lots at their fullest level yet observed, with Sawyer Merritt estimating approximately $500 million worth of Megapacks on site — a strong leading indicator for Q2 Energy revenue and deployment metrics.

- **4680 dry electrode process is a durable manufacturing advantage, not an obsolescence risk.** Jordan Giesige (The Limiting Factor) argues the dry electrode coating process is chemistry- and form-factor-agnostic — compatible with LFP, sodium ion, and solid-state — meaning Tesla's manufacturing infrastructure is not locked into high-nickel chemistry. He frames Tesla as a first-mover in a process the entire battery industry will eventually adopt. Current 4680 cost savings vs. third-party cells are estimated at 10–20% rather than the originally projected 50–60%, but Giesige estimates ~44% of the original ~56% Battery Day cost reduction target remains achievable and durable once the process scales.

## Electric Vehicles

- **Goldman Sachs raises Q2 delivery forecast to 420,000.** Goldman lifted its Q2 2026 Tesla delivery estimate from 405,000 to 420,000 units, stating deliveries are "likely tracking ahead of consensus." Full-year 2026 estimate was also revised upward (from 1.72M), with improving China and Europe sales cited as supporting trends.

- **Tesla Semi production facility showing new-design units.** Fresh drone footage from the Semi Gigafactory shows six new-design Semis in a cordoned area near the assembly line exit — two long-cab and four short-cab variants, distinguished by a full-width headlight bar. ABF Freight added two Tesla Semis to its fleet as of June 15. Bhakdi separately flagged the Tesla Semi as a "massively underrated story" for autonomous commercial trucking.
