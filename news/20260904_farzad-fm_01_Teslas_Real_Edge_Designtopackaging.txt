Tesla's value proposition isn't
competing with TSMC or ASML. It's in
owning the design to packaging loop at a
speed no competitor can match while
outsourcing the ASML intensive work to
foundaries that already have the
machines, at least for the time being.
There's nothing stopping Tesla from
partnering on a new lithography
technology in the future. For example,
Steve Jervson, one of the earliest
investors in Elon's companies, literally
just announced before this video a brand
new company called Lace Lithography,
which is developing a technology that
will make transistors that are 100 times
smaller than current EUV technology
allows. us.
