## Key Posts / Themes

1. **FSD V14.3.9 rollout adds Automatic Collision Evasion:** Tesla has begun rolling out FSD V14.3.9, which includes a new safety feature called Automatic Collision Evasion. Per the post, the system will automatically activate FSD (Supervised) if it detects an imminent crash or an inattentive driver, attempting to intervene proactively rather than relying solely on driver response.

2. **Cybercab production and vehicle specs detailed:** Tesla shared a 4K video of the Cybercab's "unboxed" manufacturing process from last week's launch event, showing a production line designed for one Cybercab to roll off every 10 seconds, with a longer-term goal of ~5 seconds. Separately, specs were shared describing Cybercab as the most efficient production vehicle Tesla has made and its smallest/narrowest model, yet with the most legroom (43.4") and largest screen of any Tesla — battery capacity of 47.6 kWh, over 6.1 miles/kWh efficiency, and curb weight of 3,113 lb.

3. **Starlink now integrated into Cybercab production units:** Cybercabs are reportedly now coming off the production line with Starlink hardware installed on the back, addressing a gap the poster noted from initial Cybercab rides — spotty cell coverage in parts of Austin. This marks a tangible SpaceX–Tesla product integration point, pairing Starlink connectivity directly with the robotaxi rollout.

4. **Tesla soliciting Cybercab rider feedback via surveys:** Tesla sent out a Cybercab rider experience survey, including a feature-preference question ("Which of these features would you most like to have in Cybercab? Select up to three"), suggesting Tesla is actively gathering input to refine the rider experience ahead of broader deployment.

5. **PHEV real-world emissions study (competitive context):** A cited analysis of 220,000 vehicles with fuel monitors found plug-in hybrids emit roughly six times more CO₂ in real-world driving (145 g/km) than official EU lab test figures suggest (24 g/km), a data point relevant to EV-vs-hybrid competitive/regulatory narratives but not Tesla-specific.
