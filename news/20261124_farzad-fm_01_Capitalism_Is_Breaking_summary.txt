## Humanoid Bots
1. **Robot labor cost projection:** The host says a humanoid robot already works out to a few dollars an hour, and his own tracker puts the cost at $3 an hour by 2032, falling to $2 an hour or less a decade out. He compares this with the $38 an hour the average US private sector worker earned in October 2026. Robots working around the clock with no sick days would be hired for any repetitive job they can perform. He projects physical automation eventually displaces more than 40 million US physical labor jobs, in roughly half the time global trade took to hollow out the Rust Belt.
2. **Standardized jobs as the entry point:** Warehouse, factory and delivery roles were built around interchangeable workers, so they are the easiest slots for machines to fill. The host notes that building both the AI brain and the dexterous hands of a humanoid is extraordinarily difficult.
