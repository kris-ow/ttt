## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla Unsupervised Robotaxi Operating Area More Than Doubles in Austin:** Sawyer Merritt noted that Tesla's unsupervised robotaxi operating area in Austin has more than doubled in size, citing apparent geofence expansion. This is a meaningful operational milestone — broader coverage translates directly to higher potential utilization and revenue per vehicle in the fleet. Per RobotaxiTracker.com (as of 2026-06-02), Tesla's active robotaxi fleet stands at 704 vehicles across all modes (Bay Area 584, Austin 104, Dallas 7, Houston 9), with 39 unsupervised/driverless vehicles (Austin 28, Dallas 5, Houston 6). A larger operating zone in Austin could accelerate the transition of more vehicles to unsupervised status.

2. **FSD V14.3.3 Rolling Out with Improved Driver Monitoring:** Tesla began rolling out FSD V14.3.3, featuring improved driver monitoring system (DMS) sensitivity — specifically better eye gaze tracking, eyewear handling, and higher accuracy in variable lighting conditions. While this is an incremental update rather than a major capability release, enhanced DMS is directly relevant to Tesla's regulatory pathway for supervised FSD and the transition toward unsupervised operation. Sawyer confirmed it was downloading on his Model Y.

3. **Lemonade Launches FSD Insurance Discount — Up to 50% Off in Indiana:** Lemonade launched an "Autonomous Car" insurance program in Indiana offering Tesla drivers up to a 50% insurance discount when using FSD (Supervised), citing FSD as "twice as safe." This is a notable commercial validation of Tesla's safety data narrative. For DCF modeling purposes, this suggests that per-mile insurance costs for FSD-equipped vehicles may be materially lower than conventional benchmarks, and the program could expand to other states. The specific framing — "twice as safe" — implies a roughly 50% reduction in actuarial risk, which Lemonade is passing through directly to consumers.

4. **Tesla EV Sales Surge in Germany and Japan:** Tesla registered 5,111 vehicles in Germany in May 2026, up 322% year-over-year per Germany's Federal Motor Transport Authority — a standout result given the difficulty of the European market. In Japan, Tesla registered 1,996 vehicles in May 2026 (+182% YoY), pushing past Audi to become the #3 luxury brand in the country for the month. Year-to-date in Japan, Tesla has registered 8,194 vehicles (+157% YoY), putting it on track for its best year ever there. Sawyer characterized Q2 European sales as "looking very strong." These data points provide meaningful positive signal ahead of Q2 2026 delivery results.

5. **SpaceX Purchases $269M in Tesla Megapacks; IPO Announced at $1.75T Valuation:** SpaceX disclosed in a new filing that it purchased $269 million worth of Tesla Megapacks in April 2026, adding to the $430 million in Megapacks purchased by SpaceX/xAI last year — bringing the cumulative known total to approximately $699 million. Separately, SpaceX officially announced its IPO targeting $74.4 billion in proceeds at $135/share (approximately 555.55 million shares), implying a $1.75 trillion valuation. With a potential overallotment option, the offering could reach ~$85.7 billion. While the IPO is not directly a Tesla event, the Megapack purchases represent a meaningful revenue relationship, and the SpaceX liquidity event has implications for Elon Musk's personal financial position and his ability to fund Tesla-adjacent ventures.
