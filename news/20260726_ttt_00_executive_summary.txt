## Brief

- Starship survives ocean splashdown intact, still transmitting via Starlink
- In-space Raptor relight demonstrated on latest Starship flight
- Q2 earnings: revenue beat at $28.2B, EPS missed at $0.33
- Tesla operating cash flow hits record $18.7B TTM, up 13.1% YoY
- FSD version 15 now running on robotaxi fleet
- Tesla secures new $30 billion debt facility

## Robotaxi
- Four Tesla executives (Moravy, Taneja, Elluswamy, Musk) gave differing rationales on Q2 call for the fleet's small size: city-by-city regulatory clearance, operational kinks to solve first, proving stack generality, and zero-harm caution. Elluswamy said Tesla ultimately expects to roll out to "entire states" at once rather than city by city, a claim the host notes no rival autonomy company makes.
- Waymo has logged over 220 million rider miles through March versus Tesla's roughly 2.5 million total paid robotaxi miles (compounding ~10%/week); Elluswamy cited roughly 380,000 fully unsupervised Tesla miles with zero notable incidents. Waymo separately has filed ~1,400 NHTSA incident reports since 2021 with 117 injuries and two fatal crashes (Waymo was the struck vehicle in both).
- FSD v15 is deployed on the robotaxi fleet with seven major improvements claimed over v14, per the Q2 call.
- Tesla has missed its own short-term robotaxi scaling guidance for three consecutive earnings calls, per one host's analysis.
- One analyst says Musk's recent comments left him "getting less bullish" on a near-term Cybercab inflection point.

## Financials
- Tesla revenue beat at $28.2B; EPS missed at $0.33 vs. $0.50 expected. One analyst attributes the post-earnings stock selloff to higher capex and lack of robotaxi updates rather than the headline miss.
- Trailing-12-month operating cash flow hit an all-time high of $18.7B, up 13.1% YoY, per a social-media figure cited by one host — money he says is being reinvested into Optimus, robotaxi/Cybercab scaling, and Terafab.
- Q2 capex was $6B, with cash falling only $1.2B; Tesla holds roughly $40B in cash and is generating $3–4B/quarter in cash flow, one commentator says, projecting $5–6B/quarter next year.
- Tesla secured a new $30 billion borrowing facility; one analyst predicts long-term debt could reach ~$50B within a year to fund Terafab, absent a Tesla–SpaceX merger.
- Musk reportedly thanked Micron and Nvidia on the earnings call for securing Tesla favorable memory pricing amid what he called insane current memory costs.

## Autonomous Driving
- Cortex 2 compute capacity is over 115 megawatts, roughly doubled over the recent quarter, per one analyst; its first half is being used for FSD, enabling a new FSD version with a roughly 10x parameter increase. The second half of Cortex 2 is still under construction and expected to roughly double total capacity to about a quarter-gigawatt by year-end.

## Humanoid Bots
- Optimus V3 production at Fremont was said to be starting around this quarter, feeding an "Optimus Academy" reinforcement-learning program where bots help train other bots; Elluswamy described the method as "pixels in, controls out," the same recipe used for FSD.
- Elluswamy claimed Optimus's hand dexterity equals or exceeds a human hand — flagged by one host as unverified pending real-task video evidence.
- Fremont's Model S/X lines have been shut down and are being converted for Optimus production, with Musk cautioning the ramp will be slow given roughly 10,000 unique parts.
- One host theorizes V3 is a training-focused generation for the Academy while V4, built in Austin with manufacturing-friendly redesigns, will be the scale generation, timed to AI5 chip volume production Musk said is hoped for next year.

## SpaceX — Starship
- The latest Starship test flight saw the vehicle survive ocean splashdown intact and remain floating, still transmitting live video via Starlink — an unexpectedly positive durability result per social commentary.
- SpaceX released new footage of Super Heavy booster separation and a follower-captured video from South Africa showing an in-space Raptor engine relight, a milestone relevant to propellant transfer, deorbit burns, and Mars transit.
- A brief, unconfirmed tease suggests the next Starship flight could attempt a tower catch of the booster and/or ship.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- One analyst says SpaceX's valuation remains too high to buy at a roughly 500x price-to-earnings ratio, given heavy share-unlock selling pressure, but believes Colossus buildout could lower the effective multiple toward ~50x if realized this year.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- One analyst argues xAI/SpaceX compute deploys cutting-edge chips roughly a year ahead of hyperscaler rivals by building data centers faster, with chips lasting longer than expected (~6 years vs. a predicted 2–3).
- Colossus buildout is flagged by one analyst as a potential near-term EBIT catalyst for SpaceX, pending further phase launches this year.
