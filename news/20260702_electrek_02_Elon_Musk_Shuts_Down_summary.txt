Elon Musk directly rejected a fan theory that Tesla is secretly ahead on Optimus production, stating bluntly that "Optimus production will be extremely slow at first, as everything is new" and that "this is not like making a car."

## Humanoid Bots

1. **Musk rejects "4D chess" theory on Optimus:** Tesla booster "Doctor Jack" (@DoctorJack16) theorized that Tesla's delay in showing an Optimus V3 demo reflected secret progress — citing VP of Vehicle Engineering Lars Moravy's comment that production was "ahead of schedule" — and predicted the eventual reveal would showcase "many Optimus robots on stage." Musk rejected the framing directly in a reply, saying production will be extremely slow due to the novelty of the product and its entirely new production line.

2. **Q1 2026 earnings context:** On Tesla's Q1 2026 earnings call, Musk confirmed Optimus production would begin at Fremont in late July or August, describing output as "quite slow" and calling the production rate for 2026 "literally impossible to predict." He cited roughly 10,000 unique parts on an entirely new line. Tesla has converted the Fremont lines previously used for Model S and Model X to Optimus production.

3. **Pattern of missed targets:** In early 2025, Musk predicted Tesla would build roughly 10,000 Optimus robots that year, with around 1,000 doing productive work by year-end. He later admitted in January that no Optimus robots were doing useful work in factories — only several hundred units were deployed for learning purposes. Every major Optimus production timeline since 2022 has slipped. Tesla's stated 2026 target is 50,000–100,000 units, with Fremont targeting a 1-million-unit annual run-rate by year-end and Giga Texas eventually targeting 10 million units per year.

## Editorial Stance
Author Fred Lambert notes that Musk previously used the prospect of an "army of robots" as leverage to argue for greater shareholder control, and now that he has it, Optimus is "magically going to be extremely slow." Lambert argues the "4D chess" narrative exists because Tesla has systematically overpromised and underdelivered on Optimus, training loyal followers to reinterpret delays as hidden progress; he expresses skepticism that Tesla will lead the humanoid robot space given the broader competitive landscape and the lack of mature physical AI.
