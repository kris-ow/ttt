Electrek's sole article today tracks Tesla's rapid Cybertruck price hikes and trim cuts as the model heads toward discontinuation.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Cybertruck narrowed to one $109,990 trim:** Fred Lambert reports that Tesla's US configurator now only allows ordering the tri-motor Cyberbeast, after the Dual Motor ($82,490) and Premium AWD ($92,490) trims were greyed out; the Cyberbeast itself rose from $99,990 to $102,490 to $109,990 within about a week — a $10,000 (10%) jump in roughly a week and a 57% rise from the $69,990 cheapest Cybertruck available in early August. No new Cybertrucks were found in Tesla's inventory search within 200 miles of Los Angeles at the time of checking. This follows Tesla's November 6 announcement (the same day as Musk's statement ending Cybertruck production) of price hikes up to 10% and a pause on base Dual Motor orders. Musk reportedly said production would extend through summer 2027 to work through backlog, and separately claimed order rate "far exceeds" production and that Tesla would have to return deposits for many buyers. Lambert estimates Tesla delivered 7,000–8,000 Cybertrucks in Q3, roughly a 30,000/year pace against installed capacity Lambert puts at over 125,000 units/year, and notes Tesla was selling a dual-motor Cybertruck for $59,990 as recently as February with delivery dates for some buyers pushed into 2027; Tesla hasn't said whether those orders will be built before production ends. Lambert's take: this looks like a deliberate wind-down playbook — announce the end, raise prices, cut cheaper trims, push remaining demand to the costliest version — calling the "order backlog" narrative self-created and now being monetized, and contrasting the $109,990 price with Tesla's original 2019 promises of a $39,900 base and $69,900 tri-motor Cybertruck. ## Editorial Stance
Electrek's Fred Lambert frames Tesla's Cybertruck pricing moves as a profit-maximizing wind-down rather than a genuine backlog problem, criticizing the gap between original pricing promises and the current $109,990 sole option.
