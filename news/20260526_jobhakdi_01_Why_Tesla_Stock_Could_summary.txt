In this update, Jo Bhakdi reviews the recent performance of his Pioneer Reserve trading strategy following a difficult stretch of six trading days, then pivots to a bullish outlook on Tesla over the next 60–90 days. His core thesis is that Tesla is approaching a convergence of hard catalysts — Cybercab production ramping, Optimus v3 unveiling, and unsupervised robotaxi scaling — that could drive the stock back to its prior all-time high of ~$490 (reached December 16, 2025) and potentially beyond. He also discusses macro conditions, particularly the Iran conflict and bond yield volatility, as near-term risks to monitor.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab Production Ramp as the Key Indicator:** Jo Bhakdi argues that the Cybercab production ramp — not the robotaxi tracker fleet deployment numbers — is the most reliable indicator of Tesla's autonomous vehicle progress. He states that Cybercabs are now being deployed across the United States and that hiring activity is visible in multiple cities. He emphasizes that since these vehicles effectively have no functional steering wheel, they can only be used as robotaxis, making commercial deployment this year essentially inevitable.

2. **Thousands of Cybercabs Per Month Projected Soon:** Bhakdi predicts that Cybercab production will reach thousands of units per month, estimating this could happen as soon as next month (June 2026) or within two months (July 2026). He frames this acceleration as a hard forcing function that has been absent until recently, noting that mass manufacturing of the Cybercab only began "last month" (approximately April–May 2026).

3. **Unsupervised Robotaxi Scale by August:** Bhakdi expects that by August 2026, Tesla could have multiple hundreds of unsupervised robotaxis operating, which he identifies as a significant near-term catalyst. He acknowledges uncertainty about whether robotaxi scaling will reach that level by August but says it is "more likely than not."

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Optimus v3 Unveiling Expected in July–August 2026:** Referencing Elon Musk's statement on the April 22, 2026 earnings call that Optimus version 3 would be unveiled in approximately 90 days, Bhakdi calculates that the reveal is now roughly 60 days away — pointing to July, with August as a conservative estimate. He describes this as an above-$500 catalyst for Tesla stock if the unveiling impresses.

2. **Optimus Consumer Sales Potential Underestimated:** Bhakdi argues that Optimus's consumer sales potential is being underestimated on timeline. He contends that even a barely functional Optimus could sell millions of units to both enterprise/industrial customers and end consumers at a price point of $30,000–$40,000 per unit, simply due to novelty and coolness. He frames selling millions of Optimus units at ~$40,000 each as equivalent in revenue impact to doubling Model Y sales. He expects critical functionality to be present "latest next year" (2027), though he notes it doesn't need to be economically optimal to generate mass demand.

## Autonomous Driving

He sees this as the clearest signal yet that Tesla's autonomous driving ambitions are transitioning from promise to execution.
