This segment lays out the physics-based case for why orbital data centers could beat terrestrial ones on power and cooling economics, framing it as a core pillar of the SpaceX AI/compute thesis. The speaker argues satellites in sun-synchronous orbit get constant, unobstructed solar power and free, fully passive radiative cooling into the near-absolute-zero cold of space, plus no land-use or NIMBY constraints that terrestrial data centers face.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
1. **Constant solar power in orbit:** Satellites placed in sun-synchronous orbit avoid day/night cycles, allowing continuous solar power capture with the sun delivering about 1,400 W/m² in orbit — described as unaffected by clouds or weather, unlike ground-based solar.
2. **Passive, free cooling advantage:** Waste heat can be radiated directly into the vacuum of space (cosmic background around 3°K), eliminating the need for chillers, water, or any energy expenditure on cooling — pitched as the most efficient possible cooling system because it's entirely passive.
3. **No land or permitting constraints:** Orbital infrastructure avoids the land constraints and "NIMBY" opposition that limit expansion of terrestrial data center capacity.
