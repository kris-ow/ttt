## Robotaxi

- **Nevada permit: up to 5,000 Cybercabs in Clark County (Las Vegas) Year 1.** Tesla filed with the Nevada Transportation Authority to operate an autonomous vehicle network in Clark County — which includes Las Vegas, Harry Reid International Airport, and Henderson Executive Airport. Multiple sources note 5,000 vehicles is approximately 1.5× Waymo's entire nationwide fleet, and that regulators would not be handed an inflated figure, making it a credible near-term deployment signal. A separate permit was also filed for Reno.
- **Tesla pre-built Clark County infrastructure before filing.** Permit records from May 2026 show Tesla was already renovating a 36,000 sq ft facility on Mohawk Street in the Las Vegas area for Cybercab maintenance, washing, and charging — indicating ground-level preparation preceded the formal network application.
- **Austin unsupervised zone expands to full metro — fifth expansion since launch.** Coverage now includes Pflugerville, Manor, Interstate 35, and Austin Bergstrom Airport, growing from the original 20 sq mi South Austin patch. This is the fifth geographic expansion since the program launched in June 2025.
- **Texas DMV registered 10 additional unsupervised robotaxis in a single day**, per the brighterwithherbert panel. A Houston staging lot was observed holding 133 vehicles with 20+ Cybercabs, which Jeff Lutz cited as evidence Tesla will not leave inventory idle.
- **Bhakdi targets 10,000+ Cybercabs deployed by December 2026**, with deployments potentially beginning this month. He frames Tesla's robotaxi inflection as happening now versus SpaceX's equivalent catalysts being 12–18 months away.
- **Jeff Lutz: FSD V15 is not a prerequisite for near-term robotaxi expansion.** He distinguishes current prototype-phase operations from Elon's long-term million-vehicle vision, arguing V15 is the threshold for the latter, not the former.

## Autonomous Driving

- **Belgium is the 5th European country to approve FSD Supervised**, joining Netherlands, Lithuania, Estonia, and Denmark. Belgium conducted approximately 5,000 km of internal testing before signing. The pace of approvals has accelerated to roughly every two days; multiple sources note peer-pressure dynamics are now driving faster regulatory action across the continent.
- **FSD cumulative miles crossed 11 billion**, up from 10 billion the prior month. Current pace: 1 billion miles every 37 days, expected to accelerate as the European fleet gains access.
- **FSD V14 Light targeting Hardware 3 rollout by end of June 2026**, per Ashok Elluswami. Approximately 3–4 million HW3 vehicles are on the road; even a modest take rate would add hundreds of thousands of subscribers and meaningful recurring revenue.
- **Tesla's own promotional videos are now a liability in FSD litigation.** A May 26 video showing a driver making espresso hands-free while FSD Supervised drives (5.6M views, captioned "your Tesla can drive you anywhere") and a June 9 Denmark launch video — in which FSD drove in a bus-only lane, made an illegal right turn, ignored a no-entry sign, and drove on a bike path — directly contradict Tesla's courtroom argument that drivers bear full responsibility. NHTSA's open Engineering Analysis already covers 3.2 million vehicles with 80+ documented FSD violations on file. Tesla faces $14.5 billion in exposure across 21+ litigation tracks; the $243M Benavides verdict was upheld in February 2026 after Tesla rejected a $60M settlement.
- **China full FSD approval did not materialize** despite a high-profile US trade delegation. A limited approval for a small number of vehicles on an older FSD version remains the status quo; Herbert speculates EU-wide approval may not come until September at earliest.
- **Alexandra Merz reports zero FSD disengagements in over a year** of personal use, and notes Tesla's reduction of nag prompt frequency has not produced a measurable uptick in accident data.

- **Cybertruck Dual-Motor AWD deliveries began at $59,990**, adding a lower-priced configuration to the lineup. No delivery volumes were reported.
- **Actually Smart Summon rolling out to Cybertruck owners**, continuing the pattern of extending FSD-adjacent features across the full vehicle lineup.
- **Tesla Semi high-volume production began late April 2026** near Giga Nevada, targeting 50,000 trucks/year. Tesla posted a Semi business development role in Parsdorf, Germany, beginning the European fleet sales cycle ahead of a targeted 2027 launch. Over 1,000 California rebate applications for the Semi have already been submitted, with state rebates covering more than half the purchase price.
- **New Roadster unveil targeting August 2026.** Chief designer Franz von Holzhausen told attendees at Tesla Takeover Europe in Austria the reveal is coming "in a few weeks" — the most concrete timing signal from a Tesla executive in years. The Information separately reported August 2026. The car is confirmed as a Gigafactory Texas build and an official Tesla/SpaceX collaboration.
- **Ford's smaller all-electric pickup spotted testing**, with a stated ~$30K starting price and 2027 launch target. Longer-term competitive context for the Cybertruck segment.

## Market & Competition

- **Tesla stock dropped ~$40 in the week prior to June 11** (from ~$420–430 to ~$380), with multiple sources attributing the move primarily to retail investors liquidating Tesla to fund SpaceX IPO allocations, compounded by macro factors (CPI +4.2% YoY, Middle East tensions). Tesla recovered $18 on the day of the stream.
- **SpaceX IPO priced at $135/share (~$1.75T valuation)**, described as at least 4× oversubscribed. BlackRock committed $5B; retail allocation reduced to ~20% from a targeted 30%. Bhakdi calculates the implied price-to-sales at ~100× and implied P/E at ~400×, calling it "a total joke" on conventional metrics, while simultaneously assigning a DCF fair value of $8–12T on a long-term orbital compute basis.
- **Tesla/SpaceX merger thesis active but disputed on timing.** Alexandra Merz places 90% probability on an announcement between July 7 and August 15, 2026, citing Elon's ~303M stock options converting to restricted stock with an August 15 exercise deadline and ~$7B tax obligation due January 2028. James (Invest Answers) disagrees, placing the announcement in 2027. Bhakdi calls the merger "pretty much a done deal" and argues a minimum 1:1 exchange ratio implies Tesla at ~$475/share — a significant discount to its current ~$380 price.
- **GM announced sodium-ion battery development for grid storage and V2G capability.** No deployed product yet; early-stage competitive signal for Tesla's Megapack business.
