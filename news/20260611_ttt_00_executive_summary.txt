## Robotaxi

- **Nevada permit: 5,000 vehicles, Year 1, Clark County.** Tesla filed for an autonomous vehicle network company permit with the Nevada Transportation Authority covering Clark County (Las Vegas), requesting authorization for up to 5,000 autonomous vehicles in the first 12 months. The filing specifically names Harry Read International Airport and Henderson Executive Airport — high-density, around-the-clock demand nodes. For context, 5,000 vehicles would be roughly 1.5× Waymo's entire current nationwide fleet.

- **Las Vegas ground infrastructure already in place.** May 2026 permit filings revealed Tesla is already renovating a 36,000 sq ft facility on Mohawk Street in Nevada for Cybercab maintenance, washing, and charging — indicating operational preparation predated the formal network permit filing.

- **Reno permit also filed.** Tesla separately applied for a robotaxi permit in Reno, near Giga Nevada. No vehicle count for the Reno application was disclosed.

- **Austin unsupervised service expands to full metro — fifth zone expansion.** Tesla extended its unsupervised robotaxi operating zone to cover the entire Austin metropolitan area, now including suburbs (Pflugerville, Manor), Interstate 35, and Austin Bergstrom Airport. This is the fifth expansion since the program launched in June 2025.

- **Texas DMV registered 10 additional unsupervised robotaxi units in a single day.** Per the panel on brighterwithherbert, these are classified as unsupervised units. One panelist observed a Houston parking lot with 133 vehicles containing 20+ Cybercabs, expressing confidence Tesla will not leave them idle through summer.

- **Scale timeline: Bhakdi targets 10,000+ Cybercabs by December 2026.** Jo Bhakdi stated conviction that Tesla reaches robotaxi scale — defined as "thousands and thousands, very likely over 10,000" — by year-end, with meaningful deployments potentially beginning this month.

- **Multiple panelists expect major deployment expansion within two to six weeks.** Jeff Lutz (Rational Bull) argued Tesla is unlikely to pre-position Cybercabs in cities without intent to deploy imminently, drawing an analogy to pre-positioning inventory ahead of a factory ramp. He does not believe FSD v15 is a prerequisite for near-term expansion.

## Autonomous Driving

- **Belgium is the 5th European country to approve FSD Supervised.** Belgium's Ministry of Transport signed approval covering both the Flemish and broader Belgian regions, having completed approximately 5,000 km of internal testing beforehand. Approval order to date: Netherlands, Lithuania, Estonia, Denmark, Belgium. The pace has compressed from weeks between approvals to roughly every two days.

- **Tesla's global FSD fleet crossed 11 billion cumulative miles.** The fleet crossed this milestone up from 10 billion the prior month — a pace of approximately 1 billion miles every 37 days. Sawyer Merritt noted this pace is expected to increase as more European countries come online.

- **FSD V14 Light targeting Hardware 3 rollout by end of June.** Ashok Elluswami confirmed this target per FutureAzA. Estimated Hardware 3 vehicles on road: 3–4 million. Even a modest FSD take rate on that base represents hundreds of thousands of incremental subscribers and meaningful recurring revenue.

- **China FSD approval remains pending.** Despite a U.S. trade delegation including President Trump and ~15–17 tech CEOs visiting China, no full market-wide FSD approval materialized. Limited approval exists for a small number of vehicles on an older FSD version. EU-wide approval is speculated no earlier than September.

- **Tesla's own Denmark FSD promotional video shows five traffic violations.** Tesla Europe posted a video celebrating FSD's Denmark approval — reshared by Elon Musk — that Danish newspaper Politiken analyzed frame-by-frame and found FSD driving in a bus-only lane, making an illegal right turn, ignoring a "no entry" sign, driving on a street closed to cars, and driving on a bicycle path. Denmark's FDM (equivalent to AAA) called the findings "worrying" and "quite critical." Tesla declined to comment.

- **Espresso video compounds legal exposure.** A separate May 26 Tesla post showing a driver making espresso hands-free while FSD Supervised drives has 5.6 million views. The caption reads "With FSD Supervised, your Tesla can drive you anywhere you want. Try it yourself" — directly contradicting Tesla's courtroom argument that drivers bear full supervisory responsibility. Tesla faces $14.5 billion in active litigation across 21+ tracks; the $243 million Benavides verdict was upheld in February 2026, after which Tesla quietly settled at least four additional Autopilot lawsuits. NHTSA's open Engineering Analysis covers 3.2 million vehicles with 80+ documented FSD violations already on file.

- **FSD V14 earns enthusiastic mainstream automotive review.** The Straight Pipes (1.8M subscribers) reviewed FSD V14 on the new Model Y Performance, with direct quotes including: "It slowed for a bird! The Full-Self Driving is unreal. Mind-blowing." Organic positive sentiment from non-Tesla-specific automotive audiences is a meaningful commercialization signal.

## Electric Vehicles

- **Roadster unveil targeting August 2026.** Chief designer Franz von Holzhausen told attendees at Tesla Takeover Europe in Austria the reveal is coming "in a few weeks." The Information separately reported August 2026 as the target — approximately seven weeks from the recording date. The car is confirmed as a Tesla/SpaceX collaboration, built at Gigafactory Texas. Panelists characterized it as a narrative/marketing event rather than a near-term financial catalyst absent a production announcement.

- **Cybertruck Dual-Motor AWD deliveries begin at $59,990.** A new lower-priced Cybertruck configuration has entered delivery. No volume figures were disclosed.

- **Actually Smart Summon expanding to Cybertruck.** Tesla announced the rollout is coming soon, extending FSD-adjacent autonomous parking capability across the truck lineup.

- **Tesla Semi high-volume production underway near Giga Nevada; European groundwork begins.** High-volume Semi production targeting 50,000 units/year began in late April 2026. Tesla posted a Germany-based business development role in Parsdorf for Semi fleet sales, with European availability targeted by Elon Musk for "hopefully 2027." Over 1,000 California rebate applications have already been submitted for the Semi.

## Humanoid Bots

- **Optimus production announcement — not just a demo — cited as the key stock catalyst.** Jeff Lutz argued a reveal paired with a concrete deployment figure (e.g., "3,000 units built, deploying into Tesla factories next week") could produce a lasting stock re-rating, unlike prior demo-only events. He frames Optimus as Tesla's distinguishing "physical real-world AI" revenue line.

- **Optimus described as a joint Tesla/SpaceX convergence project.** Multiple panelists characterized Optimus alongside Terafab and the solar 100 GW initiative as projects that will serve as breadcrumbs pointing toward deeper Tesla/SpaceX integration.

## Market & Competition

- **Tesla stock fell ~$40 on the week before recovering $18 on June 11.** The decline from ~$420–$430 to $380 is attributed by multiple commentators to retail investors liquidating Tesla to fund SpaceX IPO allocations, combined with broader macro pressure (4.2% YoY CPI print, Middle East tensions). FutureAzA notes only 31% of Tesla shares are held by retail, limiting the IPO-driven selling to a partial contributor. Multiple panelists characterized $380 as a buying opportunity.

- **SpaceX IPO described as at least 4× oversubscribed.** BlackRock committed $5 billion; Ron Baron sought $1 billion; retail allocation reportedly reduced from 30% to ~20% due to institutional demand. James (Invest Answers) set an opening-day price target of $159 vs. $135 IPO price; index inclusion expected ~June 19–22 creates a second buying wave, with additional waves in September and December.

- **Jo Bhakdi makes the case for Tesla over SpaceX on valuation.** At ~$20B revenue and ~$6.6B adjusted EBITDA, SpaceX IPOs at ~100× sales and ~400× earnings — well above Palantir at ~60× revenue, the prior high-water mark for public AI companies. Bhakdi's DCF fair value for SpaceX is $8–12 trillion, but argues the forcing functions (orbital compute, XAI enterprise AI) won't materialize until H2 2027–H1 2028 at earliest, leaving the stock vulnerable in any market downturn. Tesla's equivalent forcing functions — robotaxi revenue, Optimus — begin in H2 2026.

- **Tesla/SpaceX merger thesis active but timing disputed.** Alexandra Merz (brighterwithherbert panel) states 100% confidence in a merger "sometime this year" and 90% confidence the announcement comes between July 7 and August 15, 2026, driven by Elon's personal incentive to drive Tesla stock higher before exercising ~303 million options by the August 15 deadline (associated tax obligation ~$7 billion, due January 2028). James (Invest Answers) disagrees, placing announcement no earlier than 2027. Bhakdi calls the merger "pretty much a done deal" and argues a 1:1 ratio implies Tesla at ~$475/share — making current Tesla at $380 the better entry vs. SpaceX at IPO.

- **Ford spotted testing a ~$30K electric compact pickup for 2027 launch.** Smaller than the F-150 Lightning; not an immediate Cybertruck threat but worth monitoring for the entry-level truck segment.

- **GM announces sodium-ion battery development for grid storage with V2G capability.** No deployed product yet; a nascent long-term signal for Tesla's Megapack competitive position.
