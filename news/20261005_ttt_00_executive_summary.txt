## Brief

- Q3 deliveries hit 486,532, beating consensus by ~5%
- Starship Flight 14 deploys 26 Starlink V3 satellites, first revenue flight
- SpaceX unveils "Starmind" orbital AI compute satellites with Nvidia
- AI5 memory spec nudged back up to 96GB per Musk
- Morgan Stanley reiterates SPCX overweight, $300 price target
- Robotaxi app now downloadable across UK, Australia, Germany, Italy, Sweden

## Financials
- Tesla delivered 486,532 vehicles in Q3, beating consensus by over 24,500 units (~5%), its third-best quarter ever and best quarter without the EV tax credit in effect. Deliveries outpaced production by over 22,000 units, pointing to very low dealer inventory heading into Q4.
- Tesla Energy deployed 13.7 GWh in Q3, its second-best quarter on record, though installed production capacity across all factories was only 66 GWh/year as of Q2 — implying the segment remains capacity-constrained rather than demand-constrained.
- Tesla's next earnings call is expected around October 21–22, per host speculation (investingagainstthegrain).

## Electric Vehicles
- Cybertruck deliveries rose to an estimated ~8,000 units in Q3, up from ~5,000/quarter previously but still annualizing to only ~32,000/year.
- Germany: Tesla registrations more than tripled in September to over 12,500 — the highest monthly total since December 2022 — putting Tesla ahead of BYD for the year there; nine-month registrations exceeded 44,800, up 202% year-over-year.
- Jason Oppenheim's viral "life-changing" FSD endorsement, combined with Tesla's 9% YoY delivery growth against Ford EV sales down 68% and GM EV sales down 43% (both YTD), is being read as evidence FSD — not just gas prices — is driving demand.
- 2027 Model 3 gets a minor refresh: new 19" "Dark Nova" wheels, a larger 16" display, Premium AWD 0-60 improved to 3.8s, per Sawyer Merritt.
- Tesla Semi fuel costs run roughly a quarter to a third of diesel per mile; PepsiCo now operates ~50 Tesla Semis across California sites, using about 1.7 kWh/mile (~20–30 cents/mile in energy cost) versus diesel at ~6–7 mpg and ~$5.60/gallon.

## Robotaxi
- Texas robotaxi registrations hit 589 vehicles including 169 Cybercabs, per a spotter tracker; daily Cybercab sightings jumped from under 500 to over 750 on October 3. Separate estimates put over 300 Cybercabs operating in Dallas.
- Per-ride Austin pricing swung sharply with demand: $3.75/mile during the Austin City Limits festival weekend, dropping toward $2/mile once the festival ended.
- Robotaxi operating hours expanded to 6am–11pm (from a prior 10pm cutoff), per Musk, with the service reportedly continuing through a heavy Austin storm without shutting down; one rider also reported a Cybercab stuck in a 14-minute pickup-lot loop requiring remote intervention.
- Tesla's robotaxi app is now downloadable in the UK, Australia, Germany, Italy, Sweden, Singapore and other countries — app-store availability only, not active service, and likely tied to an upcoming Austin event rather than imminent global launch.
- Tesla's official Robotaxi site is now soliciting investor interest in purchasing Cybercab fleet vehicles or funding mobility-hub infrastructure, per jobhakdi.

## Humanoid Bots
- AI5 memory spec was nudged up to 96GB of LP5 (from a planned 144GB) so Tesla isn't the sole user of minimum-spec LP5, avoiding higher per-chip costs; AI6 was cut from 216GB to 144GB of LP6. Musk said Optimus performance won't be affected.
- AI4.1 ("AI 4 Plus") was confirmed as the next chip for customer vehicles (not AI5), raising per-SoC RAM from 16GB to 32GB and lifting compute/bandwidth ~10%.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Starship Flight 14 reached orbit and deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites — described across multiple sources as the vehicle's first revenue-generating commercial flight — followed by successful reentry and a controlled sea landing. Flight 15 is expected in coming weeks.
- Starship reached orbit for the first time on September 28, 2026, exactly 18 years after Falcon 1's first orbital flight.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- SpaceX unveiled "Starmind," a roughly 75-meter orbital AI compute satellite carrying an Nvidia-based supercomputer generating over 250 kilowatts of solar power (about 20% more than the ISS); Musk said he's "cautiously optimistic" it can run Nvidia's Vera Rubin chips near that power level.
- Google sent four of its own AI chips to orbit aboard a Falcon 9 under Project Suncatcher — the first real-world test of solar-powered AI compute in space.
- Elon Musk said SpaceXAI will be renamed SpaceXSI ("SpaceX Super Intelligence"), with no further detail given.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- The FCC voted to open roughly 500 MHz of new spectrum for services like Starlink (a ~25% downlink capacity increase) and exempted satellite constellations from federal environmental reviews.
- Musk said both United and American Airlines now have Starlink; Delta's CEO has resisted adoption, which hosts framed as a competitive disadvantage.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- Three Falcon rockets launched in a single day this week, including a Falcon Heavy NROL mission (its 14th flight) and a crewed Falcon 9 carrying NASA's Crew 13, which reached the ISS in roughly 7 hours 55 minutes — the fastest U.S. crew transfer on record.
- Musk reportedly said SpaceX aims to reach hourly launches within two to three years.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- SpCX shares rose roughly 15% over two days (6.5% on the day), with Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas reiterating an overweight rating and $300 price target, calling the stock "unusually cheap" versus mega-cap AI peers; Jonas noted an informal poll of ~40 clients found almost none owned SPCX shares.
- Scheduled share unlocks are cited as a driver of near-term price swings: 328 million shares unlocked September 24, another 328 million due October 9, and a larger ~1.3 billion-share unlock expected roughly a month out.

## Market & Competition
- CATL holds ~39% global EV battery share (Jan–Aug 2026), BYD ~15%, with seven Chinese firms holding 73% combined share — both CATL and BYD offer sub-6-minute fast-charging batteries, an area U.S. automakers lag.
- Minneapolis City Council passed a 7-6 (non-veto-proof) ordinance requiring human monitors in driverless robotaxis, which the mayor vetoed.
- GM CEO Mary Barra reportedly still projects fully self-driving cars by 2028.
