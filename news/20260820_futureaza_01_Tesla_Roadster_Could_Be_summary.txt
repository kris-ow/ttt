This video (a discussion between the host, Brian, and guest Herbert) examines fresh rumors about Tesla's long-delayed Roadster, sparked by a report from The Information claiming an unveiling could happen soon featuring a SpaceX-developed cold-gas thruster package, alongside a Jay Leno's Garage appearance by Franz von Holzhausen teasing an imminent reveal. The hosts are skeptical of specifics but believe the car is likely finished and being held back strategically rather than for technical reasons.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Roadster unveiling rumored "as soon as this month":** A report from The Information, citing three unnamed sources, claims Tesla could unveil the Roadster within roughly two weeks of the video's publication, potentially featuring a SpaceX-developed cold-gas thruster system allowing the car to hover briefly; the hosts note Tesla has not officially announced any such event, and Herbert is skeptical of the report's specific detail about a loop-the-loop demonstration, though the host separately says an unconfirmed rumor he'd heard about 18 months ago (a hover demo at a remote airfield with no bystanders) aligns with this new report.
2. **Design overhaul under consideration:** Per the report cited, Tesla has reportedly explored multiple new designs for the Roadster, potentially abandoning the 2017 concept in favor of a different aesthetic, including ideas resembling a repurposed Plaid or a more Lamborghini-like look; no leaked images exist to confirm any new design direction.
3. **Franz von Holzhausen teases "very soon" reveal:** In a Jay Leno's Garage appearance, Tesla's chief designer said the Roadster reveal is coming "very soon," which the hosts read as a more concrete signal than prior vague timing comments, though they still expect no market impact from the eventual reveal itself.
4. **Likely built in Giga Texas:** The host believes new equipment recently observed being installed at Tesla facilities (per a Joe Tegtmeyer video from August 10th analyzed on the host's other show) is not for Cybertruck, Cybercab, or Model Y production and could plausibly be Roadster-related tooling; combined with proximity to SpaceX's McGregor, Texas test site, this supports Giga Texas as the manufacturing location rather than earlier speculation about the design studio or the Model S/X line.
5. **Demo location expected at McGregor, Texas:** Both hosts expect any live hover/flight demonstration to occur at SpaceX's McGregor rocket testing facility, given the need for a remote airfield away from bystanders; Herbert expressed high confidence in this venue.
6. **Skepticism on full "flight," debate on downforce use:** The hosts speculate the thrusters may be framed similarly to BYD's demonstrated (but not customer-usable) jumping car — showing thrusters are powerful enough to fly without actually offering flight to owners for safety and liability reasons — with Herbert suggesting the real intended use case may be aerodynamic downforce for cornering rather than genuine flight.
7. **Free Roadster reservation holders got "launch edition," not SpaceX package:** The host clarifies that early reservation holders who were promised a free Roadster received the base "launch edition" and likely will not receive the rumored SpaceX thruster package, potentially disappointing those expecting the more elaborate version.
8. **Roadster seen as low investment priority:** Herbert's view is that the Roadster is a "nice to have," fun side project for Tesla rather than a business priority, and that its eventual reveal — while exciting and reputation-boosting — is not expected to move Tesla's stock price; the host agrees, adding the stock could just as easily move down on unrelated news regardless of the reveal.
