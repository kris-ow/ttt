Bhakdi argues a more modest (but still extreme) outcome is plausible: SpaceX reaching a valuation in the one-to-several-quadrillion-dollar range, driven by orbital computing power rather than launch or satellite revenue alone.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
1. **Bhakdi scales back Musk's "orders of magnitude" claim to a quadrillion-dollar thesis:** Bhakdi says Musk suggested SpaceX could become orders of magnitude larger than the Earth's economy, and he pushes back on that framing, saying he doesn't believe that exact path exists. Instead, he argues for a path "one order of magnitude" smaller — reaching a valuation of roughly one to two or three quadrillion dollars — which he still calls achievable and "not that difficult" for SpaceX, though he notes it's conceptually difficult for people to grasp such large numbers.
2. **Valuation math: $50 trillion profit at a 20x P/E:** Bhakdi lays out simple arithmetic for his quadrillion-dollar valuation thesis: a company earning $50 trillion in profit at a price-to-earnings ratio of 20 would be valued at $1 quadrillion. He asserts that hitting $50 trillion in profit is "absolutely not" difficult for SpaceX to achieve, though he does not detail a timeline or specific revenue breakdown for how that profit would be generated.
