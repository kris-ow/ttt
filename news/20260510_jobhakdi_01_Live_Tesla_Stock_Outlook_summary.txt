## Market & Competition
1. **Tesla stock recovery thesis:** Jo Bhakdi argues that Tesla is fundamentally stronger than it was when it reached its all-time high of $490 on December 16th of the prior year, yet has been trading well below that level (ranging from the low $360s up to $428 during the recent five-day period reviewed). He contends that while the broader market and the "Magnificent 7" returned to all-time highs, Tesla lagged dramatically, creating what he called a "Goldilocks moment" after earnings with very limited downside. His price target remains $490, which he views as nearly inevitable absent a macro shock. By comparison, he notes Google is up 26% year-to-date while Tesla is down approximately 4.75% year-to-date.

2. **Macro risk assessment:** Bhakdi dismisses several potential macro risks. He argues the Iran situation is effectively resolved due to forcing functions larger than any rhetorical escalation, that the Federal Reserve is paralyzed by its dual mandate (inflation plus employment pressure) and unlikely to make major moves, and that the AI bubble narrative has been thoroughly debunked — pointing to the massive, historically unprecedented capital inflows into Anthropic as proof that AI is not a bubble but a genuine civilizational transformation.

3. **Anthropic as AI bellwether:** Bhakdi describes Anthropic as having "invented AGI," citing the company's explosive growth, unprecedented cash inflows from investors (including Amazon's approximately 20% ownership stake), and extraordinary shipping velocity across sectors. He argues this is broadly bullish for all AI-adjacent stocks including Tesla, Nvidia, hyperscalers, SpaceX, and xAI.

## Robotaxi
1. **Current fleet status per RobotaxiTracker.com:** At the close of the session, Bhakdi pulls up the robotaxi tracker and confirms the unsupervised (driverless) fleet sits at 39 vehicles, describing the count as "stagnating a little bit." The total active fleet registers at 666, which he notes with a passing comment ("The devil is in the room. 666, whatever that means."). He says he is watching for the next push in deployment.

2. **1,800 unsupervised robotaxi inflection point thesis:** Bhakdi reiterates his long-standing prediction that roughly 1,800 unsupervised robotaxis constitutes the critical inflection point at which mainstream, non-enthusiast investors will accept that Tesla has definitively proven its robotaxi thesis and will begin scaling into the hundreds of thousands and eventually millions of vehicles. He had predicted this threshold would be reached in April or May 2026, and acknowledges he may have been slightly early but does not believe he was far off. He emphasizes this inflection is separate from and additive to his $490 price target.

3. **FSD reliability framing:** In response to a comment about FSD version 14.3.2 having edge case failures (e.g., getting stuck in a parking garage), Bhakdi reframes the problem: once safety is solved, the remaining challenge is a "convenience problem" — reducing the percentage of rides that are "messed up" (e.g., 5% to 3% to 1%) to a level acceptable for scaling. He argues Tesla has solved the safety problem and is now iterating on convenience/edge cases.

## Humanoid Bots
1. **Optimus reveal within 90 days:** Bhakdi states that Optimus is coming — with a reveal expected in less than 90 days from the date of the session — which he describes as a "huge, huge soft catalyst" for the stock. He also references a news item from the Pioneer Lands news feed noting that "Tesla pauses Optimus production line to retool for faster assembly," citing this as a signal of active development momentum rather than a setback.

2. **Optimus Gen 3 competitive positioning:** In response to a community member questioning comparisons of Optimus Gen 3 to competing humanoid robots given that nothing has been publicly shown, Bhakdi expresses high confidence that Tesla is leading in humanoid robotics. He explains the lack of public disclosure as deliberate competitive strategy — consistent with guidance from Elon Musk — rather than as evidence of a development gap.
