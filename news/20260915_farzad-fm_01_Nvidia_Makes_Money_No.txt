Nvidia makes money when OpenAI wins. It
also makes money when Anthropic wins. It
makes money when XAI wins. It can make
money when a Chinese open model becomes
good enough for 10,000 trillion
companies to download. It can even make
money when a customer uses several
models and switches between them every
hour. The winner at the model layer can
change at any point, but the chips
underneath the entire system still run
24/7 regardless of which company ends up
winning. And now Wall Street is getting
into the game of financing the chips and
the other giant players getting involved
makes much more sense.
