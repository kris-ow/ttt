Tesla design chief Franz von Holzhausen told Jay Leno on "Jay Leno's Garage" that the next-generation Roadster is coming "very soon," but the article treats this as the latest in a nearly decade-long string of unfulfilled timelines with no new date or detail provided.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Von Holzhausen repeats "very soon" with no specifics:** On Jay Leno's Garage, Leno pressed von Holzhausen on the Roadster's arrival; von Holzhausen twice answered "very soon," offering no date, specs, or new information. Leno responded skeptically, noting it's "just shy of 10 years" since the car was first promised.
2. **A long history of delays:** Tesla unveiled the Roadster prototype in November 2017 with production promised for 2020; the timeline then slipped to "12-18 months" out, then 2022, 2023, and 2024. In February 2024 Musk promised a production unveil by year-end 2024 with early-2025 deliveries, which did not happen. Last November Musk set an April 1, 2026 demo date (which he admitted gave him "deniability"), later pushed to "late April," then "a month or so" (per the Q1 earnings call), then August — and as of publication, still no demo has occurred.
3. **SpaceX thruster package still not ready:** The article states the current holdup is a SpaceX-developed "cold gas thruster" package (internally called "A71"). Tesla and SpaceX reportedly showed Musk an early prototype in late April, but it wasn't ready for public showcase, pushing the reveal to August, which has also passed without the reveal happening.
4. **Original specs and pricing (as originally promised):** The original pitch, made when the prototype was unveiled, was a 0-60 mph time of 1.9 seconds, a 250-mph top speed, 620 miles of range, and a starting price of $200,000; Founders Series reservation holders put down $250,000 nearly a decade ago and are still waiting.

## Editorial Stance
Electrek's Fred Lambert argues that "very soon" has become a running punchline for Tesla's Roadster, and while he believes Tesla will eventually hold some kind of updated reveal, he suspects it will be used to stall actual delivery to long-time reservation holders for years more, if not indefinitely.
