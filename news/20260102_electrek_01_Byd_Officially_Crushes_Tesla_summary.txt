The article reports that BYD outsold Tesla in all-electric vehicle sales for full-year 2025, based on data released by both companies, with the author framing it as a clear sign the two companies are moving in opposite directions.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **BYD surpasses Tesla in BEV sales:** BYD sold 2,254,714 all-electric vehicles in 2025, a 27.9% year-over-year increase, while Tesla delivered 1,636,129 vehicles, a roughly 9% decline from 2024 — a gap of over 600,000 units in BYD's favor.
2. **BYD's total volume and powertrain mix:** BYD sold 4,550,036 vehicles total in 2025, with the rest being plug-in hybrids (2,288,709 units, 50.4% of sales). PHEV sales fell 7.9% year-over-year even as BEV sales surged, putting BYD's split near 50/50 between BEV and PHEV.
3. **BYD's overseas growth:** BYD's overseas sales topped 1 million units for the first time, up 150% from the prior year, even as domestic sales reportedly weakened in late 2025 amid competition.
4. **Tesla's stale lineup cited as a factor:** The article attributes Tesla's decline to a stale vehicle lineup, noting the refreshed Model Y and Model 3 helped but the company lacks a cheaper, next-generation platform needed to grow volumes.

## Editorial Stance
Author Fred Lambert argues that with real management and an independent board, BYD's overtaking of Tesla would be a wake-up call, but blames Tesla's leadership ("a moron tyrant") for failing to execute on the next growth phase; he also argues BYD's BEV sales outpacing its own PHEV sales validates a view that hybrids are merely a transition technology toward all-electric vehicles.
