On flight 4 in 2024, Starship made it
through re-entry even after losing tiles
and suffering terrible damage around a
forward flap. The video looked like the
flap was being eaten by fire, yet the
ship still reached the ocean and made a
controlled landing burn. That flight
proved a basic vehicle had more margin
than many people expect. It also showed
SpaceX exactly where the shield and flap
design were weak. Later flights tested
different tiles, attachment methods,
gaps, seals, material under the tiles,
and areas with tiles intentionally
removed. Some flights pushed the ship
into harder re-entry angles. Some move
the flaps around to create really
challenging environments. SpaceX was
trying really hard to learn how the
whole shield could fail.
