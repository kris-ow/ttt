There is real tension here. SpaceX
eventually builds a generalpurpose AI
processor that replaces Nvidia and asks
developers to leave CUDA, which is
Nvidia software system used to build and
run AI. That relationship could become
competitive very quickly. But a hybrid
approach fits both companies incentives.
SpaceX and Tesla can design custom chips
for jobs where cost, power, or space
reliability matter more than having the
fastest general AI platform. Nvidia can
remain the leading system for training
the largest new models and running those
models for billions of users. Terrafab
can make some custom Elon chips and some
Nvidia designed hardware. The factory
gets volume from both. Nvidia gets more
secure supply. SpaceX gets control
without rebuilding the entire software
world from scratch.
