This short clip relays comments from Elon Musk during a Tesla earnings call about SpaceX's upcoming Starship flight 15, where the company will attempt to catch and recover both the booster and the ship for the first time, marking a step toward full orbital rocket reusability.

## SpaceX — Starship
1. **Flight 15 to attempt full catch of booster and ship:** Musk said SpaceX will attempt to catch both the Super Heavy booster and the Starship upper stage during the next flight (flight 15), which he said would be the first time an orbital rocket has been fully recovered if successful. He stopped short of promising success, saying only that it would "most likely" succeed, and noted that if flight 15 fails, SpaceX will make further attempts before year-end.
2. **Cadence to ramp into next year:** Musk said he expects Starship to fly weekly in the first half of next year, increasing to twice a week or more by the end of next year.
3. **Flight 14 context:** The prior flight (flight 14) saw Starship's upper stage reach orbit for the first time, setting up flight 15's goal of full recovery of both stages.
