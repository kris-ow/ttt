## Robotaxi
1. **NHTSA audit framed as non-delaying, market corrects Friday's drop:** The host reiterates his thesis (previously laid out in another video, and supported by a write-up from Cern Basher) that Friday's stock drop after the Cybercab event was caused by fear around an NHTSA audit of Cybercab, not a "sell the news" reaction to the event itself. He argues the audit, while likely to result in NHTSA restricting scaling until certain rules are approved, is unlikely to meaningfully delay Cybercab, and says today's rebound reflects the market coming around to this view after his and others' commentary.
2. **Live Cybercab count and future scaling widget:** The host's Pioneer Lands platform now tracks a live count of Cybercabs in commercial operation, currently 41 (pulled from Robotaxi Tracker and other sources), with growth having stalled over the past weekend (about one added). He is building a widget letting users project a year-end fleet count based on the current trajectory and compare it against Tesla's actual pace.
3. **Pushback on Gene Munster's stated reasoning:** The host disagrees with a claim he attributes to Gene Munster that Tesla's stock would rise "tomorrow" on news that Tesla was ready to roll out a large number of Cybercabs in Austin, with an estimate of reaching roughly 300 within three months — the host argues that news predated Friday's drop and was already known, so it couldn't explain a later rally; he otherwise praises Munster's analysis generally.
4. **Disagreement with Chris Camillo's "already priced in" view:** The host disputes trader Chris Camillo's claim that the entire robotaxi business is already priced into Tesla's stock, calling it wrong, but characterizes Camillo as a tactical, non-fundamental trader who has openly said he could be wrong and would re-enter the stock if so.
5. **Viewer fleet-size question deferred:** In response to a question about minimum fleet size needed for robotaxi fleet operators to be viable, the host says he has a "fleet theory" he plans to cover in a dedicated video.
