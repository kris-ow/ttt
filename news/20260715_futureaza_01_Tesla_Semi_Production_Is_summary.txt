## Electric Vehicles
1. **Tesla Semi visual production signals:** Hosts observed seven Tesla Semi trucks on site (different from a prior week's seven, based on differing components), interpreted as evidence that production is ongoing, albeit at what they describe as a slow pace. They also noted an apparent expansion of parking/staging areas near the assembly line, which they speculated could indicate more trucks coming off the line soon.
2. **Long-range vs. standard-range visual differences:** The host claims to have newly identified a visual distinguishing feature: long-range Semi trucks have a larger cab and a bright white center light bar, while standard/short-range trucks have a visibly dimmed light bar — a detail he says he hasn't seen others point out.
3. **Exposed battery pack shot:** Footage reportedly showed three battery modules arranged laterally down the center of a long-range Tesla Semi chassis, a rare unobstructed view according to the hosts.
4. **Charging capability and infrastructure:** Per the hosts, Tesla Semi customers must currently purchase and install their own chargers (with options including a megawatt charger or smaller units); Tesla is building some charging along routes, but day-one customers are expected to self-provide charging. The truck does not currently support NACS/home Level 2 charging, though hosts speculated Tesla could add that capability in the future; test mules reportedly charged at Supercharger stations previously.
5. **Factory capacity for expansion:** In response to a viewer question, the hosts assessed that while the factory has room for additional stamping machines and partial line upgrades to increase throughput, there isn't sufficient open floor space for an entirely new full production line.
6. **Customer delivery visibility:** Neither host has personally observed a new "Atlas"-version production Semi with a real (non-Tesla) customer in the wild, though they believe some units are already in the hands of a beverage-industry customer (previously wrapped in company livery for marketing) and that a delivery ramp has begun.
