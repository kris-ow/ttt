The article reports that EU/EFTA EV sales hit a milestone in December 2025 — battery electric cars outsold petrol-only cars for the first time — while Tesla's sales fell more than any other major automaker in Europe for the year.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **EVs cross a sales threshold in Europe:** In December, EU battery electric (BEV) sales rose 51% year-over-year, plug-in hybrids rose 36.7%, and petrol hybrids rose 5.8%, while petrol-only sales fell 19.2% and diesel fell 22.4%. This pushed BEV sales (not including PHEVs) past petrol-only sales for the first time in a single month. Including EFTA countries and the UK, BEVs outsold petrol-only cars 26.3% to 21.7% in December. For the full year, BEVs made up 17.4% of EU sales versus 26.6% for petrol-only cars.

2. **Overall market context:** Total EU auto sales grew 1.8% year-over-year but remain below pre-pandemic levels. The UK saw a third of new December car sales be electric, already meeting its 2026 target.

## Market & Competition
1. **Tesla posts the worst sales decline of any major automaker in the EU:** Tesla's 2025 EU sales dropped 37.2% year-over-year — the steepest decline of any brand excluding Jaguar and Lancia (sub-brands). The article notes this is notable given Tesla is an all-EV brand in a rapidly growing EV market.

2. **BYD posts the largest gain:** BYD had the largest percentage sales increase of any brand in 2025, up 228% for the full year, growing from 39,000 to 129,000 cars sold.

## Editorial Stance
The author argues Tesla's sales decline, despite favorable EV market tailwinds, is largely attributable to Elon Musk's political actions — specifically characterizing his involvement in European politics as attempting to orchestrate what the author calls a "white supremacist takeover of the continent" — and says Europe's negative reaction is understandable given history. The author also criticizes the European auto industry for lobbying to loosen emissions rules, calling it a surrender to overseas EV competition.
