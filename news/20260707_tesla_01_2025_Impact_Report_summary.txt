Tesla published its 2025 Impact Report, an annual disclosure covering calendar year 2025 (with some data points into early 2026), framing the company's mission as expanding beyond EVs and energy into autonomy and robotics to build what it calls "a world of amazing abundance." The most newsworthy disclosure is the report's account of the Robotaxi service, launched in June 2025 in Austin, Texas with Model Y, alongside detailed sustainability and efficiency data on FSD (Supervised), Cybercab, and the energy storage business.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **FSD safety comparison:** The report states drivers using FSD (Supervised) experience **8x** fewer major collisions, **7x** fewer minor collisions, and **6x** fewer off-highway collisions versus the U.S. national average.
2. **Geographic expansion:** FSD (Supervised) access expanded to Australia, China, New Zealand and South Korea in 2025, with Belgium, Denmark, Estonia, Lithuania and the Netherlands added in 2026.
3. **Efficiency gains:** Tesla sampled **65 million miles** driven in 2025 with FSD (Supervised) engaged, finding it is **5% more efficient** than manual driving, with efficiency peaking in the 25–35 mph range typical of urban and suburban driving.

## Robotaxi & Cybercab
1. **Service launch:** Tesla's Robotaxi service launched in **June 2025** in Austin, Texas using Model Y, with plans to deploy Cybercab as the primary Robotaxi fleet vehicle.
2. **Utilization rationale:** The report notes the average passenger vehicle is used only about **10 hours per week**, motivating a shift toward hyper-utilized autonomous ride-hailing fleets.
3. **Cybercab design:** Built without a steering wheel or pedals, as a two-seater since **85%** of ride-hailing trips carry one or two passengers; it uses 4680 battery cells, steer-by-wire technology, 48-volt architecture and Tesla's "unboxed" manufacturing process.
4. **Manufacturing emissions cuts:** A new reaction injection molding (RIM) paint process cuts manufacturing cycle times from hours to minutes, delivering a **35%** reduction in manufacturing/supply chain GHG emissions for parts processed through it and a **100%** reduction in VOC emissions versus traditional paint processes.
5. **Emissions per mile:** Tesla states Cybercab will avoid nearly **2x** as many GHG emissions per mile as Model 3/Model Y.

## Environmental Impact / Vehicles
1. **Customer emissions avoided:** Tesla's combined vehicle, solar/storage and Megapack/Powerwall portfolio enabled customers to avoid emitting nearly **37 million metric tons** of CO2e in 2025, equivalent to avoiding about **90 billion miles** of ICE driving.
2. **Lifetime vehicle emissions:** After about **four years / 38,500 miles** of driving, a Tesla's lifetime emissions fall below a comparable ICE vehicle's; after **17 years / 200,000 miles**, a single Tesla avoids about **32 metric tons** of CO2e globally (**52 metric tons** in the U.S. specifically).
3. **Cost per mile:** Model Y RWD's total cost of ownership is **$0.77/mile** over 5 years/60,000 miles, compared to $0.81 (Toyota RAV4), $0.85 (Hyundai IONIQ 5) and $1.15 (BMW X3).
4. **Vehicle pricing:** Model 3 RWD starts at **$36,990**, versus a reported U.S. average new vehicle price of **$49,353** (as of June 2026).
5. **Net-zero target:** Tesla reiterates a goal of achieving net-zero GHG emissions by **2040** across its full value chain.
6. **Semi trucks:** Combination trucks represent about **1.2%** of the U.S. vehicle fleet but **15.8%** of vehicle emissions; Semi uses less than 2 kWh/mile and can travel up to 500 miles on a charge, delivering a **50%** TCO reduction in California and **20%** across the U.S. A third Megacharger station opened in Los Angeles in early 2026.

## Energy
1. **Demand growth:** Global electricity demand rose **3%** in 2025, with a projected **~3.7%** increase in 2026; data center electricity demand grew nearly **20%** in 2025 and is set to double by 2030.
2. **Supercharger network:** Tesla added over **12,000** charging stalls in 2025 and delivered more than **6.5 TWh** of energy, avoiding over **12 million metric tons** of CO2e versus ICE vehicles; the network has been matched with 100% renewable electricity for five years running, with a reported **99.95%** uptime metric.
3. **Powerwall savings and VPPs:** Customers saved over **$1 billion** on energy costs in 2025 through solar, Powerwall and grid exports; **95%** of energy used to charge Powerwalls came from solar. Tesla's VPPs prevented outages for **6.5 million homes** globally and delivered **47 million hours** of backup power, with over **213,000** Powerwall units enrolled in VPP programs by year-end delivering more than **20 GWh** to the grid.
4. **Megapack impact:** The global Megapack fleet avoided about **470,000 metric tons** of CO2e in 2025 (~60 mt per unit — nearly 2x the lifetime emissions of one Tesla vehicle). Megapack projects can be deployed in 6–24 months versus multi-year timelines for gas peaker plants.
5. **Disaster relief:** Tesla ran **11** disaster relief operations across **6** countries and territories, deploying **32** Mobile Powerwall Units and delivering approximately **26 MWh** of clean energy.

## Manufacturing & Supply Chain
1. **Water reduction:** Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg, Gigafactory Shanghai and Fremont Factory used **50% less water** per vehicle produced in 2025 versus the industry average.
2. **Natural gas reduction:** Electrification of burners in Berlin-Brandenburg's paint shops cut natural gas usage by **4,000 MWh** annually.
3. **Renewable procurement:** Tesla secured **2 GW** of wind and solar energy across North America and Europe since 2023; Berlin-Brandenburg's electricity usage was matched with 100% renewable electricity for three years running.
4. **Battery supply chain:** Battery materials represent **26%** of Tesla's total supply chain GHG emissions; Tesla cut battery supply chain emissions by **9%** in 2025, used **3.5 TWh** of renewable energy in that supply chain, and found **94%** of battery cell, cathode, anode and mineral suppliers shared an assessed decarbonization pathway.
5. **Gulf Coast lithium refinery:** Now complete, it will become North America's largest lithium refinery, producing **20,000 metric tons** annually (~30 GWh) of battery-grade lithium hydroxide monohydrate using a new alkaline leach process, cutting GHG emissions over **30%** versus hard rock refining and reducing water discharge by **80%** versus the industrial acid-alkaline process.
6. **Supply chain ranking:** Tesla was ranked **#1** by Lead the Charge for supply chain sustainability for the second year in a row, ahead of Ford, Volvo, Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen and others.
7. **Human rights:** Since 2023, **13,000+** workers were reimbursed around **$555,000** in improperly charged recruitment fees by Tesla suppliers at Tesla's request; external DRC cobalt supply chain audits have not identified child labor or ASM-site sourcing to date.

## People
1. **Job creation:** Tesla reports creating nearly **135,000 jobs** globally as of 2025.
2. **Safety trends:** The reported ASTM injury rate was **2.3** in both 2025 and 2024 (down from 3.6 in 2021), alongside a rise in "Take Charge" employee safety suggestions to approximately **758,000** in 2025.
3. **Stock performance:** Tesla notes its share price reached **$498.83** in mid-December 2025, tying employee equity programs to personal financial milestones.
