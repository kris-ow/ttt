Jo Bhakdi walks through Tesla's FSD v15 release, including its headline safety claim, its feature list and its new photorealistic visualization. He argues the release is the inflection point that lets Cybercab deployment scale into the tens of thousands and eventually trigger a repricing of TSLA. He backs this with a third-party Cybercab deployment counter that shows the fleet roughly doubling in a week.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Safety claim: 25x over v14:** Tesla says v15 delivers 25 times better handling of safety-critical cases than v14. Elon Musk clarified that this means 25 times (2,500%), not 25%.
   - The host asserts that v14 is roughly 2x safer than the average human driver. On that basis he concludes v15 is about 50x safer than humans.
   - The multiplication is internally consistent. However, Tesla's figure measures the handling of safety-critical cases, not overall crash rates, so it does not by itself establish an overall 50x safety advantage. That conclusion is the host's extrapolation.
   - The host expects further gains, possibly 100x within a couple of months.
2. **Rollout timing:** Per the host, v15 began rolling out on November 21, 2026, ahead of its December 2026 schedule, and is now "basically available across the board." He reads the early release as evidence that FSD development is accelerating. v15 runs only on AI4 hardware.
3. **Release-note features:**
   - Navigation at the start and end of a trip now learns end-to-end from fleet route preferences. Later v15 releases will extend this to the entire route.
   - Detection is better for signs, animals, deep potholes and standing water.
   - Speed handling is more natural in school zones and inclement weather.
   - FSD can now slow for speed cameras.
   - A bike rack mode allows FSD to be used with hitch accessories.
4. **"Video game" visualization:** The new in-car display re-renders the car's surroundings photorealistically. Users can move a drone-like camera around their own vehicle, and it works in parking garages, at night and in dense fog.
   - Musk described chevrons rendered in the sky for upcoming turns.
   - Drivers can tap any point on the rendered scene to send the car there.
   - The host expects the visuals alone to drive strong demand for FSD.
5. **AI4 sufficiency:** The host relays Musk's view that AI4 is fully sufficient for superhuman driving. He takes this to mean the current mass-produced Cybercab hardware is the end state and can scale to millions of units.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab counter doubling:** The host cites the Cybercab counter on Pioneer Lands at 582 units, up from 285 a week earlier, roughly a doubling. He believes Cybercab already runs v15, though he notes there are few reports confirming it yet.
2. **Path to 2,000 and 10,000:** The host projects about 2,000 Cybercabs by December 31, 2026. He says the "if we continue doing that" rate would get there, but the numbers do not match:
   - Continued weekly doubling over four weeks would give roughly 9,000+, not 2,000.
   - His 2,000 target instead corresponds to the counter's calculator setting of about 27% weekly growth, which doubles every ~20 days and adds roughly 1,400–1,500 units.
   - He maintains his year-long 10,000-by-end-of-2026 call and will claim victory if it is hit in Q1 2027. He would consider himself wrong only if it slips more than three months.
3. **Scaling thesis:** Once 10,000 is reached, the host expects at least 50,000, and possibly 100,000, in 2027.

## Financials
1. **Earnings and stock repricing thesis:** The host claims 50,000 Cybercabs would roughly double Tesla's 2024 earnings, based on current per-mile pricing. He repeats his view that about 1,800 deployed Cybercabs is the forcing function for a TSLA repricing.
   - He urges investors not to be distracted by macro volatility, noting the Nasdaq fell 1.4% on Tuesday after rising nearly 7% on Monday.
   - He teases an upcoming video on the Tesla–SpaceX merger.
