Tesla is recalling 14,575 Model Y vehicles produced between November 17, 2025 and April 21, 2026, due to a possibly missing weight certification label on the driver's side door jamb.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Recall scope and cause:** An automated vision-scanning tool at Tesla's Fremont factory was "performing inconsistently" during the roughly five-month production window, failing to confirm that each vehicle received its required weight certification label (mandated under 49 CFR Part 567). Tesla only discovered the malfunction on April 17, 2026, when a missing label was spotted on a vehicle. Tesla estimates approximately 45% of the 14,575 affected vehicles actually lack the label.

2. **Remedy and timeline:** The fix is physical — Tesla will print and affix new labels. Because this is not a software issue, it cannot be resolved via OTA update; owners must bring vehicles in for inspection. Tesla has since repaired the scanning tool and added manual double-checking during production. Owners will be mailed recall notices on July 17, 2026. No field incidents related to the missing label have been reported. Recall number: SB-26-19-002.

## Editorial Stance
The author characterizes the safety risk as minimal — noting that vehicles typically carry two overlapping labels — but flags the nearly six-month detection gap as a process quality concern, and notes the labor cost of inspecting ~14,575 vehicles at Tesla service centers.
