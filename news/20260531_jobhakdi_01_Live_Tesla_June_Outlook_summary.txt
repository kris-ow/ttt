## Electric Vehicles

1. **Cybercab Capex Classification Prevents Delay:** Jo Bhakdi addresses a viewer question about whether Tesla could "hide" unsold Cybercabs as inventory to mask deployment delays. He argues this is not possible because Cybercabs are capex items — Tesla is not planning to sell them to consumers — and therefore they cannot be reclassified as inventory. He views this accounting reality as making meaningful deployment delay essentially untenable for Tesla from a financial reporting standpoint.
