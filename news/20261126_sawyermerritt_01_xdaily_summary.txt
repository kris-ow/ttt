## Key Posts / Themes

1. **FCC authorizes SpaceX's 1-million-satellite Starmind constellation:** The FCC officially authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million satellites for Starmind, its orbital data center constellation. The FCC called it the first authorization in U.S. history for a constellation of data centers in space. The authorization comes with deployment milestones requiring set numbers of operational satellites by November of each milestone year. This was the day's most viral post (7.6K likes, 924 reposts). It gives regulatory footing to SpaceX's orbital compute plans, a core part of its AI & Compute thesis.

2. **SpaceX share donation to Trump Accounts:** SpaceX will gift one SpaceX share to every currently active Trump Account, the tax-advantaged savings accounts for American children. Elon Musk will personally match SpaceX's share count. The pledge totals 142 million shares, worth $18.1 billion at the current price, going to about 70 million American children:
   - SpaceX: about 70 million shares
   - Musk: about 70 million shares
   - Gwynne Shotwell: 2 million shares

   The stated value implies roughly $127 per SpaceX share. The gift would also put SpaceX stock in front of a very large new base of young shareholders.
