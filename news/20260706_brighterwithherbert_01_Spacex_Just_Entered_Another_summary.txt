## Market & Competition
1. **SpaceX reportedly eyeing US mobile market:** A report says SpaceX told investors it wants to launch a consumer mobile service in the US, competing with Verizon and AT&T; an Oppenheimer analyst described this as a "$2 trillion communications market" opportunity, with AI infrastructure and AI applications as the next-biggest areas for SpaceX. The guest cautioned that a full US mobile rollout is unlikely within a year and a meaningful nationwide network may not arrive until late next year, contingent on Starship deploying larger V3 satellite batches to expand bandwidth.
2. **Starlink direct-to-cell expands to Philippines:** The Philippines became the first Southeast Asian country to approve Starlink direct-to-cell service via partner Globe, allowing standard LTE phones to connect without extra hardware. The guest believes this initial service is text-based, not voice, with voice capability requiring further build-out and new phone compatibility.
3. **SpaceX offers Starlink perks near Memphis data center:** SpaceX is giving Memphis-area neighbors near its Colossus data center free Starlink hardware and roughly 50% off internet plans; the guest characterized this as a community goodwill gesture rather than a broader business shift, comparing it to friction Elon-affiliated projects have faced in other cities (e.g., the Boring Company in Tennessee).
4. **SpaceX acquires Mesh Optical Technologies:** SpaceX received regulatory approval to acquire Mesh, a startup founded by former SpaceX engineers building optical transceivers for AI data centers, aiming to manufacture 1,000+ units per day this year; the company raised a $50 million Series A in February. The guest described it as a classic SpaceX "aqua-hire" aimed at easing data bottlenecks in Colossus-style clusters and potentially SpaceX's orbital data center ambitions, calling it evolutionary rather than revolutionary.
