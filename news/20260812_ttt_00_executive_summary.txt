## Brief

- SpaceX targets 10GW AI compute by end-2027, sees $300–500B revenue potential

## Robotaxi
- Austin riders report persistent pickup/drop-off looping failures and wait times of 15–25 minutes, sometimes up to 55 minutes, despite the badged fleet growing to roughly 90 vehicles; one rider said performance has "significantly regressed" versus three months ago, per Electrified.
- Electrified's host argues the service isn't scaling due to operational readiness issues, not regulation or deliberate slow-walking by Musk.
- A possible new city launch, likely San Antonio, is expected this month or next, with only a handful of vehicles initially.
- Tesla's first Cybercab with an integrated Starlink dish was unveiled; Musk said riders could watch live sports in 4K, stream movies, or work en route — a potential recurring connectivity revenue stream, though not yet modeled.
- Ron DeSantis featured Tesla's Cybercab alongside Waymo at a Florida press event on eVTOL testing, praising its steering-wheel-free interior; Tesla styled the car's light bar with an American flag theme.
- A widely circulated image shows Waymo's Arizona factory lot with just under 1,000 new vehicles, roughly 25% of Waymo's deployed fleet.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- SPCX shares have repeatedly failed to break $135 — the IPO price — with Bhakdi estimating roughly 160–200 million shares of overhang supply clustered at that level, potentially clearing in about 10 trading days.
- Unnamed unlocked shareholders told the host they won't sell below $135, recalling prior trading near $225–$250.
- SPCX closed up 9.65% to $146.15 on 2026-08-12 versus TSLA's 1.59% decline to $327.51 — a divergence Electrified frames within a "merger playbook" narrative.
- Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas set a $600/share SpaceX bull case built on Cursor (estimated $8B revenue by year-end, $33B by 2030), explicitly excluding Grok, X data, and Starlink upside.
- Deutsche Bank reportedly sees a $100B SpaceX revenue run-rate as achievable this year, citing $45–50B in cloud deals plus Cursor.
- Merger speculation continues: Bhakdi argues a near-term SpaceX-Tesla merger is unlikely given unresolved share price discovery and unlocked shares, estimating "earliest next year"; Electrified notes any change-of-control trigger in Musk's Tesla pay package would swap operational milestones for 12 market-cap tranches tied to Tesla's valuation, not SpaceX's.
- Terafab phase 1 (of 10 planned phases) is confirmed at just under $17B with 3,000 jobs; Electrified's host maintains the full project will exceed $100B in capex.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Musk said SpaceX's AI revenue will likely exceed all other SpaceX revenue as soon as next month and "significantly exceed" it by Q4 2026, stating SpaceX "must win on AI."
- Grok 4.7 targeted for readiness in three to four weeks per Musk, with SpaceX proprietary data now being incorporated; Grok 4.6 missed its ~August 7 target date.
- Investor Gavin Baker praised Grok 4.6/Cursor's price-performance (~85% cost discount at similar performance to a rival), though Electrified flagged a SpaceX comparison chart as overstating Grok 4.6's lead.
- Grok Imagine 2.0 ranked #2 globally on text-to-image leaderboards; Grok 4.5 ranked #1 on a design-usage arena.
- Bhakdi argues SpaceX's real moat is becoming the dominant compute provider as AI model costs commoditize, predicting Musk keeps Grok's top capability vertically integrated rather than licensed out.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Musk said he expects the Starlink constellation (including direct-to-cell) to eventually exceed 100,000 satellites, up from roughly 10,000 today, with future satellites carrying up to 50x current throughput.
- Musk said "all cars will have Starlink" as the only way to deliver high bandwidth to billions of vehicles — flagged by hosts as an aspirational claim, not an FSD requirement.
- Musk said it's "possible" Starlink could handle over 90% of IP traffic even if rivals 10x their bandwidth — a hedged claim.
- Lufthansa's first Starlink-equipped A320 Neo enters service August 19.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Ship 13 achieved its softest splashdown yet and was floating intact post-flight, but Musk said "ship recovery is not looking good right now"; heat shield and engine photos were preserved before the ship was apparently lost at sea.
- An FCC filing indicates Flight 14's booster will return to the launch site; Musk said on the earnings call Flight 14 will also attempt a ship catch, combining a first true orbital mission, first operational V3 satellites, and both booster and ship catches — potentially by month-end per Musk, though the filing gives no date.

## Autonomous Driving
- Tesla owner David Moss became the first reported to reach 25,000 miles on the FSD hands-off streak counter.
- Tesla begins its first FSD (Supervised) ride-alongs in Switzerland this Thursday.
- Mixed real-world clips circulated: one showed FSD backing into a shopping cart corral despite clear visual cues; another showed FSD proactively avoiding a BMW that had bounced off a nearby truck.

## Humanoid Bots
- Musk called Digital Optimus "real-time video human emulation," distinct from the new agentic Grokbot product; Electrified's host speculates Digital Optimus runs primarily on local Tesla inference hardware with Grok as cloud orchestrator, with a possible beta later this year.
- Musk reposted an Optimus mission-statement post on scaling physical labor, tied to a previously reported ~25% Terafab output allocation for Optimus.

## Energy
- Tesla filed plans for a $10.1B solar cell factory ("Project Crystal Sun") in Fort Bend County, Texas, with construction starting this year, completion in 2028, and operations beginning 2029; the site is not yet finalized versus an alternative location.
- The plan calls for full vertical integration (wafer, ingot, coating) rather than buying finished cells, tied to earlier Musk comments about Tesla and SpaceX each targeting ~100 GW/year of US-made solar within roughly three years.

## Electric Vehicles
- Tesla ranked #1 in a brand loyalty survey among owners likely to rebuy the same brand, ahead of Ferrari, Toyota, Subaru, and Honda.
- A Tesla patent for plasma-treated dry electrodes in 4680 cells shows wetting time cut from ~72 hours to ~8 hours and a ~4% energy density gain, addressing the previously flagged "core collapse" manufacturing bottleneck, per The Limiting Factor.
- Tesla delivered its first customer vehicles in Estonia; freight carrier Bali Express Services completed a positive 3-week Tesla Semi trial.

## Market & Competition
- Subaru is spending roughly 3x its gas-car ad budget per vehicle on EV marketing while paying ~$9,000–$10,000 per incentivized EV sold, which hosts frame as a product problem, not an advertising one.
- GM and SAIC extended their China joint venture 20 years to 2047, with stated (and host-doubted) ambitions to export beyond China.
- MIT researchers found EVs emit 40–60% fewer lifecycle emissions than gas vehicles even on dirtier grids.
