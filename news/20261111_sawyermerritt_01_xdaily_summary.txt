## Executive Summary
Today's digest is split between a milestone claim in Tesla's robotaxi registration race with Waymo in Texas and a cluster of SpaceX/Starlink hardware and spectrum news (Starlink Mini 2 launch, FCC spectrum acquisition, solar cell manufacturing deal). On the Tesla EV side, Cybertruck continues to shrink its lineup and push out delivery dates, signaling fading demand/production rationalization.

## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Tesla edges ahead of Waymo in Texas robotaxi registrations:** Per the poster, Tesla has surpassed Waymo in total robotaxis registered to date in Texas for the first time — Tesla at 1,302 vs. Waymo at 1,297 — after Tesla added 20 more Model Ys. Note: these are registration counts, not active/operational fleet figures (distinct from the RobotaxiTracker.com operational fleet data, which remains stale at 839 total/135 Austin as of 2026-08-07). A registration count does not establish operational deployment scale.

2. **SpaceX Starlink Mini 2 launch and spectrum expansion:** SpaceX unveiled the Starlink Mini 2, its first Mini with a built-in, quick-swap battery offering up to 4.5 hours of connectivity (weight 3.53 lbs/1.6 kg including battery). Pricing starts at $999, bundled with one year of Roam Unlimited (normally $1,500–$2,100/year) and 30-day international travel windows; U.S. deliveries begin next month (December 2026). Separately, the FCC accepted SpaceX's application to acquire 800 MHz spectrum from Grain Management (546 paired licenses covering ~100% of the U.S., per the post), described as a major step toward Starlink Mobile becoming a major U.S. carrier. SpaceX also signed a manufacturing deal with SINGULUS for high-efficiency solar cells for its Starmind satellites at a Bastrop, Texas facility, with the first production line expected in H1 2027.

3. **Cybertruck lineup contraction continues:** Tesla has stopped taking orders for the Cybertruck Premium AWD trim, leaving Cyberbeast as the only orderable trim, and raised the Cyberbeast starting price by $7,500 (7.3%) to $109,990 (from $102,490). Separately, Cyberbeast estimated delivery dates have slipped further — now showing March–June 2027, up from January–March 2027 just days earlier and December 2026–January 2027 before that — leading the poster to speculate Tesla may soon halt orders for all Cybertruck trims (unconfirmed/speculative).

4. **Tesla pushes NHTSA on lighting regulation modernization:** Tesla submitted a letter to NHTSA urging updated vehicle lighting rules, arguing current regulations were built for traditional headlights rather than modern camera-assisted matrix LED systems.

5. **Elon Musk reportedly joining Trump's China delegation:** Per the post (reported, not confirmed), Musk and other business leaders may join President Trump's delegation to China next week for the APEC summit — not a scheduled interview/appearance, but worth noting for potential China-related Tesla/SpaceX commentary.

6. **Tesla Semi service center opening in Laredo, TX:** Tesla is hosting an event for its first Tesla Semi service center in Texas, located in Laredo, including a site tour and ride-along, signaling continued Semi network buildout.
