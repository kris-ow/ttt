## Robotaxi
- **Real bottleneck confirmed as operational, not safety:** Elon stated explicitly that what limits wider robotaxi deployment is not accidents but "convenience issues where the car gets paranoid and gets stuck" — specifically infinite routing loops and excessive caution in construction zones. Zero accidental injuries have occurred across the entire deployment history, per Elon and Ashok.
- **FSD 14.3 series is the unlocking version:** Elon characterized 14.3 as a "major architectural update" sufficient to enable unsupervised deployment. Reasoning capabilities introduced in 14.3.1 are expected to be extended to additional edge cases (railroad crossings, prolonged red lights) in subsequent point releases — no need to wait for version 15.
- **~14 production Cybercabs spotted leaving Giga Texas:** Per drone video coverage cited by the Cern Basher hosts, newly produced Cybercabs are moving off the lot rather than accumulating. Hosts note that Cybercabs without steering wheels have no QA-fleet use case and would logically feed directly into robotaxi expansion in Austin, Dallas, and Houston.
- **Revenue guidance unquantified:** Elon said unsupervised FSD/robotaxi revenue will "not be super material this year" but will be "material, probably in a significant way" in 2027. Vibob confirmed fleet grew quarter-over-quarter and will keep ramping into new geographies — without specifying targets. A speculative host estimate of 10,000 robotaxis by year-end was explicitly flagged as unconfirmed.
- **36,000 sq ft automated Cybercab car wash permitted in Las Vegas:** The facility is designed for robotic operation with minimal staffing, cited as evidence Tesla is building physical infrastructure for scaled operations ahead of volume deployment.

## Autonomous Driving
- **FSD version 15: "complete overhaul," targets AI 4 hardware, due by early 2027:** Elon described v15 as a full software architecture rebuild — clarifying AI 5 is not required — and said it arrives "hopefully later this year, but certainly by early next year." It is framed as a further safety leap beyond what 14.3 already provides, not the prerequisite for unsupervised operation.
- **Global unsupervised FSD ambition stated:** Elon said Tesla has "a whole pipeline of major improvements" and expects unsupervised FSD to be available "anywhere in the world that it is legal to do so," with certain regions reachable by end of 2026.
- **FSD China approval flagged as highest near-term catalyst:** During Elon's May 13 Beijing trip alongside President Trump, his stated priority was Chinese regulatory approval for FSD. Approval would open Tesla's software stack to its largest single vehicle market, where FSD currently cannot operate.

## Electric Vehicles
- **Model Y price increases — first in two years:** Tesla raised prices on three US Model Y trims: Premium RWD to $45,990 (+$1,000), Premium AWD to $49,990 (+$1,000), and Performance AWD to $57,990 (+$500). Base RWD ($39,990) and base AWD ($41,990) are unchanged. Per Electrek's Fred Lambert, the selective increases target high-margin trims while protecting the entry-level price point.
- **Model Y dominant in British Columbia:** The Model Y was the top-selling vehicle overall — not just top EV — in British Columbia for March and April 2026, with over 4,000 units sold in the first four months of the year.
- **Tesla positioned to capture Canada's Chinese EV quota:** Canada has established a 49,000-vehicle/year low-tariff import quota for Chinese-made EVs (CAD $35,000 import threshold, running through 2030). Tesla's existing Canadian showroom, service, and logistics infrastructure gives it a structural first-mover advantage over BYD, Xpeng, and Geely, which are still building out local presence. The Canadian government is reportedly debating per-manufacturer caps out of concern Tesla will take the majority of the quota.
- **Tesla Semi enters production — nine years after announcement:** The first vehicle off a completed Nevada production line shipped in April 2026, versus a 2019 delivery promise. Cited economics: ~35 cents/mile savings vs. diesel, ~$60,000–$80,000 annual fuel savings per truck on a Megacharger, implying a 3–5 year payback. Early orders include a 340-unit example. The truck is described as "autonomy ready."

## Energy
- **Multiple large Megapack contracts already signed:** Georgia Power ($2.7B, 3+ GW); Scotland (1 GWh); France (240 MW, described as one of the country's largest); China ($556M). A new Megablock product is ramping later in 2026. The host flagged Tesla Energy's backlog as a key item to watch at the next earnings call.

## Humanoid Bots
- **Analyst models: 50,000 Optimus units in 2027, 500,000 in 2028:** These are analyst projections, not Tesla guidance. Elon's stated long-term aspiration is 1 million units per year per production line. Optimus is currently being tested inside Tesla factories but is not yet doing autonomous production work.

## Financials
- **Automotive gross margins fell from 25%+ to below 18%** between early 2023 and mid-2025 amid sustained Model Y price cuts. The Q1 2026 delivery report showed a ~50,000-vehicle inventory build and missed expectations. Full-year 2025 deliveries totaled 1.636 million, below the 2023 peak. The new Model Y price increases signal a margin recovery attempt on higher-trim configurations.

## Market & Competition

Issues cited include ~1,000 incidents per month, vehicles stopping unexpectedly, and frequent remote operator interventions. Noted as context: Tesla's own robotaxi program is also currently limited in scale.
- **Newport, KY police fleet: $148,311 in projected five-year savings** across four additional Model Y additions, with Police Chief citing Tesla as both cheaper and a better product — a continued pattern of municipal TCO validation.
