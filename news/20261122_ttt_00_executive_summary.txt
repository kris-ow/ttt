## Autonomous Driving
- FSD v15 began rolling out Nov 21, starting with build 15.0.1 on AI4 cars in the US and Canada. Tesla says it updates every part of the end-to-end network. Ashok Elluswamy called it the first time the car in your garage runs the same software generation as the robotaxis. Musk had earlier flagged a wider release for December.
- Tesla claims v15 improves the critical intervention rate by 25x. A Tesla Autopilot lead said the gain is a fleet-wide measure that shows up over millions of miles, so a single drive may feel much like v14. The figure is Tesla's own and not independently verified.
- Release notes cite an updated visual encoder shared by FSD, robotaxi, and Optimus, trained on millions of online videos. Musk said v15 has a completely new architecture and meets the bar for removing a human from the driver's seat.
- The new Reality View renders the car's surroundings from the same end-to-end network that drives. Musk said it is real-time AI video generation with no game engine, running on half of one FSD AI accelerator. Elluswamy said it uses less compute than the old visualization. Users can tap the screen to set a drop-off or parking spot.
- Early user clips show parking-spot selection, with arrows, marked spots, and the planned path on screen. One clip shows the car aborting and retrying around a fallen tree. Another shows it handling fog. Sawyer Merritt said it "nailed parking" in his testing, and these are single-user impressions.
- Other v15 notes: navigation learned from fleet route preferences, better handling of potholes, deep puddles, debris and school zones, and slowing for speed cameras. A more tolerant driver-attention camera and quieter turn signals are also listed. Musk said HOV lane and late lane-change issues should be fixed.
- Per Herbert Ong, FSD growth now hinges on approvals, including an EU vote around December 3 or 6 and possible China expansion.

## Robotaxi
- Tesla's robotaxi account said Nov 12 that service now runs 24/7 in every city where it operates, eight by the host's count. Elluswamy had tied 24/7 operation to the v15-based update.
- Per Elluswamy on the Q3 call, the first customer cars could join the robotaxi fleet around Q2 next year. Musk told an Austin Uber driver that testing of sending a Model Y to work could begin next month if all goes well.
- After riding Cybercab in Dallas, some riders say they don't want to use any other robotaxi despite competitors' discounts, per Sawyer Merritt. This is anecdotal, based on a media clip.
- Herbert Ong says robotaxi becomes a real stock catalyst at around 5,000 vehicles with visible quarterly earnings growth.

## Electric Vehicles
- About 1.7M active FSD subscriptions at the end of Q3, up 67% year over year, per the host of one video. Over 75% of North American deliveries carried paid FSD, up from 55% a quarter earlier. Another host cites Elluswamy and CFO Vaibhav Taneja at 77% adoption on new cars, up from 50% two quarters ago.
- Tesla added an 8-year / 80,000-mile Emissions Performance Warranty for 2027 Model 3 and Model Y in the US and Canada. A software update (2026.44.+) improves turning radius on model year 2027 Cybertrucks.
- Tesla opened Model 3/Y ordering in Argentina, its fourth South American country, with Musk joining by video call.

## Humanoid Bots
- Per Herbert Ong, Musk's 2027 plan is 5,000-20,000 Optimus robots for internal use, with no third-party sales until 2028. Tesla's Q3 call guided to external Optimus deliveries around the end of 2027. Herbert's best case is 40,000-50,000 robots by the end of 2027, with no significant revenue within 24 months.

## Energy
- Tesla's Power Core product targets transformers, described as a major bottleneck for energy buildout. Musk said Tesla has planned the business for about three years, with launch in 2027.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute
- Anthropic is reportedly SpaceX's largest customer at about a third of last quarter's revenue. Notes from its analyst meetings reportedly said it might not renew after mid-next year. After the initial fixed periods end at the start of next year, either side can terminate on 90 days' notice.
- Musk said on DealBook that Anthropic tells him it will most likely keep leasing and wants about another 10 gigawatts. Anthropic has not confirmed that figure. Musk also said SpaceX expects zero sales to OpenAI and is adding about half a gigawatt per month. Dario Amodei said the future can't be known but that Anthropic loves working with SpaceX.
- CFO Bret Johnsen said SpaceX can fund much of the expansion from operating cash flow, with hosting deals paying back in under a year. SpaceX issued investment-grade bonds in June and has a $40B credit line with Apollo and Goldman Sachs for GPUs.
- Johnsen outlined orbital compute satellites flying in 2027, constellation scaling in 2028, and significant computing in orbit in 2029.
- Cameron County commissioners voted 3-2 against tax breaks for SpaceX's $24B Brownsville data center. SpaceX had sought 10 years of 100% abatement. It did receive an emissions permit for the power plant, with 35 gas turbines and about 750 MW in the initial phase.
- Satya Nadella said Microsoft would rent SpaceX compute if it has capacity, per the host.

## SpaceX — Starship
- The recent catch of the Starship upper stage was called the last big element of full reusability and a key enabler of orbital compute. The Super Heavy booster aborted its catch this time. Per Herbert Ong, shares fell slightly anyway, as investors wait for a product that sells.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
- Musk hopes to receive the first test chip from Terafab, the chip factory SpaceX is building with Tesla, around June next year.
- The host of one video argues a Tesla–SpaceX merger is the dominant revaluation catalyst, since index providers could reclassify the combined company as technology. He cites Alexandra Merz on consumer ETF assets of about $23B versus $110-120B for technology, and expects this around September or October 2027. Guest Herbert said index classification is outside his expertise.
- Per Herbert, SpaceX's market cap is already $1.8T.
