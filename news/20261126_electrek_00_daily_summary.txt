Electrek's only Tesla-relevant piece today covers Tesla's Thanksgiving Supercharger preparations, pairing a small mobile-stall deployment with a much larger permanent buildout.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Tesla deploys mobile Superchargers for Thanksgiving:** Electrek reports Tesla says 29 temporary stalls are live at four California and Arizona sites (Firebaugh +10, Bakersfield Olive Dr +8, Bakersfield Copus Rd +4, Holbrook +7), on top of 1,027 permanent US stalls opened across 91 sites in the last 56 days. Tesla also cites on-site attendants, improved uptime and service dispatch, and a virtual waitlist now at 300 sites (launched at five in May). Electrek calculates roughly 18 stalls a day versus about 14 ports a day in Q3, citing Paren data (1,287 Tesla ports; IONNA 378; Walmart 344), while noting the methodologies differ. Tesla did not say whether the mobile stalls run on grid or battery power. Fred Lambert's take: this looks like planned capacity rather than damage control, the 29 mobile stalls matter little next to the permanent build, and he says Tesla appears to be recovering the deployment pace lost after the 2024 charging-team layoffs, with no US rival building at that rate. [Read on Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/26/tesla-deploys-mobile-superchargers-thanksgiving-travel/)

## Editorial Stance
Electrek's view is largely positive here: it credits Tesla with proactive holiday planning and a recovering Supercharger rollout, while restating its earlier criticism of Musk's 2024 firing of the charging team.

Notable quotes and data:
- 29 mobile stalls live at four sites; 1,027 permanent US stalls opened across 91 sites in 56 days (Tesla, per Electrek).
- Virtual waitlist live at 300 sites, up from a five-site pilot in May (Electrek).
- Thanksgiving 2025 context: more than 2 million sessions and over 70 GWh delivered, with 0.76% of sessions involving a wait (Tesla, per Electrek).
- Paren data cited by Electrek: Tesla added 1,287 US fast-charging ports in Q3; IONNA 378; Walmart 344.
