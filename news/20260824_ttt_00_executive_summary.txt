## Brief

- Tesla confirms Cybercab Robotaxi launch, September 3, Austin
- Nevada approves Tesla for up to 5,000 robotaxis statewide
- FSD v14 Lite HW3 car drives over log without braking
- Viral Robotaxi routing failures: 66-min quote for 2.3-mile trip
- TSLA closed $348.95, down 3.83% on China recall news
- Germany's Brandenburg in talks with Tesla on rural autonomous shuttles

## Robotaxi
- Cybercab officially joins Austin Robotaxi service September 3rd, roughly 23 months after its October 2024 unveiling; event is invite-only via rider sweepstakes, winners announced August 26, RSVP deadline August 30, livestreamed. Electrek frames it as a "car swap dressed up as a milestone" since Tesla's unsupervised fleet has held near 20–30 vehicles for over a year despite the new body style.
- Nevada Transportation Authority approved Tesla for up to 5,000 robotaxis statewide (Model Y initially, not Cybercab); Tesla reportedly told the board it would be "extremely happy" with 2,500 deployed by next year. Waymo got separate Nevada approval for up to 1,000 vehicles over 12 months.
- Active Robotaxi fleet: 839 per RobotaxiTracker.com, unchanged since August 7 (Bay Area 657, Austin 135, Dallas 22, Houston 25) — treat as stale. Qualitative reports describe improving Austin/Houston service reliability and Cybercabs multiplying in Austin, Dallas, and Houston lots ahead of the launch.
- Viral service failures: a Tesla Ambassador's 2.3-mile ride was quoted at 66 minutes with no reroute option, refunded only after 630,000+ views; a repeat Dallas rider documented multiple missed drop-offs, a 3-mile stranding, and a manual-drive intervention, prompting him to say he's "done taking these rides."
- Texas driverless robotaxi registrations reportedly grew from ~33 at end of May to over 100 now, per analyst James Stephenson; Florida has been added to the driverless list, while California still requires an in-seat safety monitor.
- Giga Texas plastics section reportedly shows growing quantities of Cybercab structural panels, read by one Tesla-watcher as evidence of a coming production ramp.

## Autonomous Driving
- A Hardware 3 Tesla running FSD v14.1 Lite drove over a log across a wet road at 32 mph without braking, per an X video with 800,000+ views; Electrek reports HW3 units running v14 Lite have also shown thermal faults above 90°C (up to 96°C) and phantom braking/hesitation issues.
- Mainstream press turning more positive: WSJ's Dan Neil called FSD "the best thing about the new Tesla Model Y" and said he was "bewitched" after two hours of testing; Edmunds separately cited FSD as a reason to buy the Model Y.
- China's roughly 3 million-vehicle Tesla recall (part of a 5.7 million-vehicle industry-wide action effective September 25) is a software/label fix — OTA update plus warning labels for post-crash window release and driver monitoring — not a hardware repair.
- Brandenburg, Germany's transport ministry confirmed talks with Tesla on deploying driverless shuttles for underserved rural bus routes, modeled on an existing Eifel-region pilot; legal approvals still pending.

## Financials
- TSLA closed $348.95, down 3.83%, versus the Nasdaq-100's 0.97% decline; one host called the drop disproportionate to the China recall's estimated ~$30 million earnings impact (~1% of earnings).
- Tesla's year-to-date revenue through Q2 already exceeds the equivalent period in 2023 (its prior record year), per analyst James Stephenson, who expects a full-year revenue record in 2026 despite last year's retooling disruption.
- FSD subscriptions added ~170,000 net-new payers in Q1 with continued Q2 growth at $99/month, framed as near-pure-margin revenue since the software already runs on-car.
- Regulatory/ZEV credit revenue has fallen to an estimated ~$100 million per quarter, roughly a quarter of prior levels, due to expired U.S. tax credit programs.

## Market & Competition
- Waymo reportedly cut robotaxi hardware cost from $115,000 to $20,000 per vehicle via a new custom ~1,000 TOPS chip; one commentator was skeptical of the figure's comparability to Tesla's cost structure. Waymo runs roughly 3,000 driverless vehicles and 500,000+ paid weekly trips versus Tesla's ~380,000 cumulative unsupervised miles since January.

## SpaceX — Starship
- Starship Flight 13 recovered near Christmas Island after 24 days adrift at sea; it will not be reflown, with inspection focused on heat-shield tile damage. Flight 14 is expected in early September.
- Musk said SpaceX expects to catch a Starship ship with the tower within a few months, with first ship reflight targeted for late this year or early next year.

## SpaceX — Starlink
- Starlink constellation surpassed 11,000 satellites; SpaceX has filed with the FCC for approval of up to 100,000 satellites in a third-generation proposal, alongside a separate India filing covering nearly 30,000 satellites with direct-to-device capability.

## SpaceX — Launch Business
- SpaceX completed its 100th launch of the year, carrying 24 more Starlink satellites; Musk said SpaceX now launches over 80% of the world's mass to orbit, targeting ~2.25 million tons/year by 2032.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute

- Nvidia disclosed a roughly $21 billion stake in SpaceX earlier this month.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation
