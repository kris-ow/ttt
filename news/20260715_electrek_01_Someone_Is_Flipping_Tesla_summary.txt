A New Jersey dealer, J&S Autohaus, is listing a low-mileage Tesla Model S Signature (VIN #71, 297 miles) for $259,995 plus a $495 documentation fee, roughly $100,000 above Tesla's original price for the car.

## Electric Vehicles
1. **Listing details and pricing gap:** The Ewing, NJ dealer's listing totals $260,490 for a one-owner, accident-free Model S Signature with Garnet Red paint, gold brake calipers on carbon-ceramic brakes, and 21-inch wheels. Tesla priced the Model S Signature around $155,000 and the Model X Signature at $159,420; Tesla's final Model S Plaid inventory sold for $124,900, meaning the listing asks more than double a standard Plaid. The same dealer has other 2026 Model S Plaids listed from $149,490–$155,490 and base Model S units from $110,490.

2. **Scarcity and production context:** Tesla built only 250 Signature Model S sedans, each numbered 1/250 through 250/250, as part of an invite-only 350-vehicle farewell run for the Model S and Model X. Per the article's earlier reporting (April), Tesla ended Model S and Model X production, with the Fremont line being converted to build Optimus humanoid robots. Access to the Signature run was limited to select existing owners via email invite.

3. **Historical parallel:** When the Model S launched in 2012, the first roughly 2,000 units sold were Signature editions requiring a $40,000 deposit and costing nearly $100,000; author Fred Lambert states he still owns one of these original Signatures.

## Editorial Stance
Lambert is skeptical the $260,000 asking price will be met, arguing that the 2012 Signature editions — genuinely historic as the first 2,000 Model S units ever made — did not appreciate into six-figure collector items, and he doubts his own original Signature could fetch $50,000 today. He praises the Model S design as "timeless" and laments that Tesla ended the line without upgrading it (no new battery cells, faster charging, steer-by-wire, or range gains since the 2021 refresh), calling the price premium a bet on "badge appreciation" rather than genuine value.
