Notable quotes and data:
- OEM Ego battery: 3.46 kg, 14S4P configuration (56 total Samsung 18650 cells, 2.5Ah each, 20A max continuous discharge), potted BMS, ultrasonic-welded terminals, 12 gauge wiring.
- Cheap replacement pack: 2.45 kg, 14S3P configuration (42 cells from a Chinese supplier called "Len," rated 2.5Ah with 30A discharge but measured lower in practice), unpotted BMS, soldered joints, 16 gauge wiring.
- A second replacement pack with a different color scheme shared the identical part number and construction as the first but used unbranded cells the presenter measured at roughly 2.2Ah each, yielding an estimated total pack capacity around 6.6Ah despite marketing claims of 10Ah.
