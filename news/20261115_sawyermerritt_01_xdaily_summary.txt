## Key Posts / Themes

1. **SpaceX catches Starship upper stage for the first time:** SpaceX successfully caught its Starship rocket (upper stage) at 4:20 PM ET on 2026-11-14, which Sawyer Merritt describes as "the first recovery of a conventional orbital rocket upper stage in history." This generated the day's highest engagement (32.2K likes on the main post). A separate post notes the booster was NOT caught, though the stated reason wasn't detailed in the available text. SpaceX's shop has released a "ship catch" T-shirt ($35) and mission patch ($15) to commemorate the milestone. This is a first-of-its-kind reusability achievement directly relevant to the Starship/launch-cost thesis.

2. **Cybercab expansion and pricing details:** Elon Musk said Tesla will gradually expand sales of the $29,990 Cybercab to the general public across the U.S. for personal use (not just robotaxi fleet), with the current $299/month "Autonomy Subscription" eventually dropping to roughly $115/month over time, per Musk's comments (unconfirmed timeline). The $299/month subscription is described as bundling FSD Unsupervised within Tesla's robotaxi geofences, tire service/replacement, inspections/rotations, premium connectivity, and windshield replacement. Separately, Tesla has reportedly invited YouTuber Marques (MKBHD) to order a Cybercab for personal use, and Merritt notes "first public Cybercab rides in Dallas" appear imminent — both signals of expanding access beyond the initial invite-only robotaxi rollout. Merritt frames the car (no steering wheel/pedals, $29,990 price) as a real, delivered unsupervised self-driving vehicle launching next month, pushing back on skepticism about the invite-only limitation.

3. **Tesla FSD marketing push:** Tesla launched its first Times Square billboard ad for FSD, running through the rest of November 2026, citing "7x safer than a human when engaged" and "15+ billion miles driven globally" — these are Tesla's own marketing claims as displayed on the billboard, not independently verified figures in this post.

4. **Starlink V3 satellite deployment:** SpaceX deployed Starlink V3 satellites into orbit on 2026-11-14, per a brief post with no further detail on quantities or specifics.
