Electrek's Jameson Dow reports that Elon Musk said pothole avoidance for FSD is "coming soon" in a weekend tweet reply, and notes Musk made an essentially identical promise in 2019 that never materialized.

## Autonomous Driving
1. **Musk's new pothole-avoidance promise:** Responding to a Twitter user who asked for FSD to avoid potholes, Musk replied "coming soon." The article says this came the same weekend Musk made broader predictions about AI capabilities beginning next year, and notes an unnamed outlet claimed the announcement was responsible for a roughly 5% TSLA stock move that day (a claim the article reports but does not itself verify).
2. **Same promise made in 2019:** Electrek reported in 2019 that Tesla was working on pothole avoidance after a similar fan exchange with Musk on Twitter; the article states that capability still has not shipped seven years later, despite Tesla's vision-only system theoretically being able to detect potholes the way humans do.
3. **Current FSD state:** The article characterizes FSD as having improved over the years — handling highways, surface streets, and parking — and notes Tesla topped a large-scale Chinese real-world test of driver-assist software last year, though it also made unspecified "strange errors" in that test.

## Robotaxi
1. **Cybercab launch timing:** The article notes Tesla is launching its Cybercab — a geofenced, steering-wheel-free autonomous robotaxi — later in the week the article was published, operating without a driver present, and raises the concern that these vehicles may launch without the ability to avoid road hazards like potholes.
2. **Robotaxi service status:** The article states Tesla's robotaxi service has completed fully autonomous miles with no driver present but describes the system as still very limited and, in the author's characterization, not as capable as Waymo — a gap the author asserts Tesla itself recognizes even though Musk does not.

## Editorial Stance
The author is skeptical of Musk's "coming soon" claims generally, framing the pothole-avoidance promise as another example of Musk's history of missed self-driving deadlines, and expresses concern that Cybercab's imminent driverless launch could be undermined if the vehicles still cannot handle basic road hazards like potholes.

Notable quotes and data:
- Musk's reply to a fan asking about pothole avoidance: "coming soon"
- Electrek previously reported in 2019 on Tesla's then-upcoming pothole-avoidance capability, following a similar Twitter exchange
- Musk's separate weekend prediction: AI will be "superhuman at all digital tasks by the end of next year" (per the article, not necessarily including physical tasks)
