Electrek reports, per Bloomberg, that SpaceX is weighing a potential merger with either Tesla or xAI ahead of an anticipated IPO, with the author arguing this self-dealing structure would disadvantage Tesla shareholders.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation / Financials
1. **Merger options reported by Bloomberg:** SpaceX is reportedly weighing two paths ahead of an anticipated IPO later in 2026: a merger with Tesla, or a merger with xAI (which already owns X/Twitter). Bloomberg reports some investors are pushing for a SpaceX-Tesla combination, while Reuters separately reported SpaceX and xAI are exploring a tie-up in which xAI shares would be exchanged for SpaceX stock.
2. **IPO timing and valuation:** SpaceX is expected to go public by mid-2026 at a valuation potentially exceeding $1 trillion.
3. **Recent related transactions cited by the article:** The article recaps that xAI acquired X (Twitter) in 2025 for $33 billion, after the investment had reportedly fallen in value from $44 billion to roughly $9 billion for private investors; and that Tesla invested $2 billion in xAI last week (relative to publication), a move shareholders are suing over for alleged breach of fiduciary duty. The article also notes xAI has reportedly told investors it will build AI for Tesla's Optimus robots.

## Editorial Stance
The author (Fred Lambert) argues this is a pattern of Musk self-dealing — using Tesla shareholder money or favorable self-negotiated deal terms to rescue his private investors in xAI and SpaceX — and contends that because Musk owns a much larger stake in SpaceX and xAI than in Tesla, any Tesla-SpaceX merger would structurally favor his private holdings at Tesla shareholders' expense. The author states they doubt a Tesla merger actually happens, viewing a SpaceX-xAI combination as the easier path for Musk. A cited top reader comment speculates the merger strategy could be a way to inflate Tesla's market cap without product innovation.
