## Brief

- WSJ reports Tesla weighing China sale; Musk calls it "absurdly fake news"
- NHTSA caps Zoox at 2,500 robotaxis/year, no cap reported on Cybercab
- SpaceX stock down ~44-49% from post-IPO high on unlock fears

## Market & Competition

- WSJ reported Tesla was weighing a China business sale to clear the way for a SpaceX merger; Musk and Tesla China both denied it, with Musk stating "this has never even come up in the discussion ever." Commentators (Herbert/Goldberg, Merz) argue the sale framing is likely false but say underlying China-merger negotiation dynamics are real; Merz pegs actual divestiture probability at 10-15%.
- NHTSA/SAE launched a 3-year, $5 million initiative for first national AV performance standards; NHTSA separately granted Zoox a temporary exemption capped at 2,500 robotaxis/year for two years, a cap one host attributes to Zoox's failed self-certification crash testing versus Tesla's Cybercab, which he says already passed self-certification with no stated deployment cap (per Lars Moravy).
- July was described as the worst month in ~30 years for momentum-stock drawdowns, driven by forced liquidation of a leveraged hedge fund ("Situational Awareness," ~$45B peak assets) absorbed by Citadel; Tesla fell in lockstep with broader market contagion, not on company-specific news, per Jo Bhakdi.
- China's CXMT memory-chip IPO jumped 466% to a ~$500B valuation, adding competitive pressure that reinforces the strategic case for Tesla/SpaceX's Terafab chip initiative, per one host.

## Financials

- Q2 revenue hit $28.2B (+26% YoY), beating estimates; EPS of $0.33 missed the $0.49 expected, which one host attributes to heavy reinvestment (capex $5.79B, up 142%) rather than underlying weakness.
- Services revenue reached $4.58B (+50%, record margins); energy deployment hit 13.5 GWh (+40% YoY) — segments one host argues the market still undervalues as "car company" revenue rather than distinct high-margin businesses.
- Tesla trades near 156x forward earnings, a valuation one host concedes is a legitimate bear-case argument.
- Several large individual Tesla investors reportedly sold roughly a third of their holdings after the recent earnings call, per one host's client conversations.

## Robotaxi

- Robotaxi rides continue running in Austin, Dallas, Houston, and three Florida cities; no dedicated robotaxi revenue line exists yet in financial disclosures.
- One host says Cybercab won't move Tesla's earnings needle for nine months unless the fleet scales to thousands of unsupervised units, but states he "strongly believes" Tesla reaches 1,000 robotaxis this year; scaling depends on service centers coming online.
- A separate host argues Musk currently gives Robotaxi less personal attention because it's in a steady optimization phase requiring less direct input than Optimus did over the past year.

## Autonomous Driving

- FSD subscriptions reached ~1.5 million, up 56% YoY, cited as a growing recurring-revenue stream.
- A Detroit News piece profiled seniors, including a 78-year-old retired GM engineer, using FSD extensively to maintain independent mobility; hosts shared personal anecdotes of FSD streaks over 500 miles and usage rates up to 98%.

## Humanoid Bots

- One host believes Optimus V3 will likely impress when revealed but doubts a reveal before December.
- Musk reportedly overhauled the Optimus team over the past year after calling the program "a mess," with one host now characterizing it as back on track.

## SpaceX — Corporate & Valuation

- SpaceX stock has fallen roughly 44-49% from its post-IPO high (from a $135 IPO price, ~$160 debut, to ~$113-114), which hosts attribute mainly to an imminent stockholder unlock expanding float from ~5% to 25-35%, plus Starship uncertainty — not deteriorating fundamentals.
- SpaceX earnings are scheduled for August 4, with a share-lockup unlock event around August 6 expected to bring some guaranteed employee selling.
- Wall Street's average 12-month price target sits at $230 (from 30 of 32 covering institutions), with a high estimate around $800; one host urges skepticism given Wall Street targets were "nearly worthless" for Tesla 2017-2020.
- Merz speculates (own estimate, unconfirmed) that Musk may have acquired the dormant "X" stock ticker, though she doesn't believe it implies a new holding-company structure.

## SpaceX — AI & Compute

- One host argues Colossus (terrestrial compute buildout) — not Starlink or Starship — will be the key driver of SpaceX's upcoming earnings reaction, and plans a detailed follow-up analysis before the August 4 call.

## SpaceX — Starship

- Starship remains not yet orbital; one host expects it to reach orbit and cut costs by ~2027-2028 but not meaningfully affect earnings before then. Falcon 9 Block 5 was cited as having the best reliability record of any rocket in history, in contrast to Starship's ongoing development issues.
