## Robotaxi

1. **Cybercab production versus autonomous capability gap:** Jo Bhakdi notes the apparent contradiction at the center of the current Tesla debate — Cybercabs are being produced in volume and are vehicles that can only effectively operate without a driver, yet Tesla has not yet demonstrated the ability to run them unsupervised at scale. He describes this as a pivotal and unresolved tension for investors, referencing broader debate over whether the earnings call signaled a slowdown in robotaxi deployment timelines.

2. **Scaling threshold for stock repricing:** Jo Bhakdi reiterates his existing thesis that reaching approximately 1,800 unsupervised robotaxis in operation represents a key inflection point — what he calls a "forcing function." He believes that scaling into the thousands or tens of thousands of robotaxis would trigger a stock repricing that could take Tesla to $1,000. He emphasizes, however, that Tesla does not need to reach this threshold to return to all-time highs.
