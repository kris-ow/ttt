## Key Posts / Themes

1. **Next-gen Tesla Roadster unveiled with SpaceX-derived thruster tech:** Tesla officially unveiled its next-gen Roadster, a two-seater with steer-by-wire, with production slated to start in 2028. The headline feature is a compressed-gas thruster system (described by Tesla as using COPV tanks) intended to aid acceleration/handling, with the company targeting a sub-30-minute refill time for a single-use ~5-second thrust burst. Tesla brought a SpaceX Raptor V3 engine to the event for a test-fire, which Sawyer Merritt called the first-ever collaboration between a carmaker and a rocket company on a car. Trim levels: Performance ($50k deposit), Thrust ($250k deposit), and Rocket (invite-only); final/total pricing is TBD. 0-60 mph times given: Performance under 1.6s, Thrust under 0.9s, Rocket under 0.59s (Tesla also stated a 0.81s figure at one point during the event). The car uses a bespoke 4680 battery cell described as Tesla's fastest-charging (15-80% in 10 minutes), and a drive unit said to be one-third more powerful than the Model S Plaid's at half the mass. Merritt claims the event/demos (including a mid-air "flight" demo) surpassed 10 billion views across social platforms. The Roadster will be manufactured at a dedicated new building at Giga Texas called "RFTX" (Roadster Factory Texas). Top Gear got early access with Franz von Holzhausen and Lars Moravy. Merritt also claimed the slowest Roadster trim would outpace the current fastest Rimac Nevera R (1.66s 0-60) — a competitive performance-EV comparison, not independently verified here.

2. **FSD Supervised approved in Slovakia, 9th EU country:** Tesla's FSD Supervised (rebranded "Tesla Assisted Driving" in Europe) received approval in Slovakia, becoming the 9th European country to approve the system, joining Belgium, Netherlands, Denmark, Lithuania, Estonia, Slovenia, Czechia, and Croatia. This continues Tesla's steady regulatory expansion of supervised autonomy across the EU.

3. **Q3 earnings dates confirmed for both companies:** SpaceX will report Q3 earnings on October 20, 2026, and Tesla will report Q3 earnings on October 21, 2026 — both within the next week of this digest, making them the next major scheduled catalysts for investors in both theses.
